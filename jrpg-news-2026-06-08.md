# JRPG News — June 8, 2026

## Top Story: Xbox Games Showcase Delivers — Persona 6 Is Real

Yesterday's Xbox Games Showcase (June 7) delivered on its biggest promise: **Persona 6** received its world premiere after nearly a decade since Persona 5's release. Atlus dropped a cryptic teaser trailer — a slow pan through a graveyard of headstones ending on the Persona 6 logo — effectively confirming the leaked concept art and footage that prompted SEGA's DMCA takedowns earlier this year.

**What's confirmed:**
- Platforms: PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC
- Day One on Xbox Game Pass
- No release window given; leakers speculate **September 2027**, unconfirmed
- Fresh standalone story and new cast of characters; Atlus says more details will be shared "in the near future"

The reveal was light on gameplay — Atlus is clearly playing this one slowly — but the official confirmation closes the loop on months of insider reports and leaked material.

## Persona 4 Revival: February 18, 2027 — and a Livestream Coming June 18

**Persona 4 Revival** got a firm release date at the showcase: **February 18, 2027** — landing it in the same "winter launch" window as Persona 3 Reload, and squarely within Sega Sammy's stated FY2027 window. Platforms are PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC, and Nintendo Switch 2, with Day One Game Pass and Play Anywhere support.

Additionally, Atlus announced a **Persona 4 Revival Broadcast Livestream** on **June 18 at 4 PM PT / 7 PM ET**, hosted by P-Studio Director Kazuhisa Wada. Expect deeper dives into gameplay changes, the updated Inaba setting, and likely a look at the new social link system.

Sources: [oprainfall — Atlus showcase recap](https://operationrainfall.com/2026/06/07/xbox-games-showcase-atlus-persona-announcements/) · [The Gamer](https://www.thegamer.com/persona-6-revealed-during-xbox-games-showcase-june-2026/)

## Wo Long 2: Wings of Ember — Team Ninja Returns to Three Kingdoms

Koei Tecmo and **Team Ninja** revealed **Wo Long 2: Wings of Ember** at the showcase. Set in the late Eastern Han period as warlords splinter and the Three Kingdoms era begins, the sequel evolves the Chinese martial arts combat of the original — the trailer highlighted high-flying dragon fights and a visually expanded battlefield compared to Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty.

- Platforms: PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC
- Day One on Xbox Game Pass
- Release window: **Early 2027** (early February indicated during the show)

While not a traditional JRPG, Team Ninja's action RPGs have a strong crossover audience and this is one of the bigger RPG reveals from the showcase.

Sources: [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/149509/wo-long-2-wings-of-ember-xbox-games-showcase-2026-reveal) · [DayOne](https://playday.one/2026/06/07/wo-long-2-wings-of-ember-announced-at-xboxs-showcase/)

With the Persona 4 Revival broadcast also landing June 18, it's shaping up to be a notable day for the genre.
