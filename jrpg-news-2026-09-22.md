## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Near-Universal Acclaim — 89 Metacritic

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems**' **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2** and has been met with the strongest critical reception in the series since *Three Houses*.

Aggregating 53 scored reviews, the game holds an **89 on Metacritic** with 13 perfect scores. **IGN**, **VGC**, and multiple other outlets awarded it **10/10**, with reviewers praising the four interconnected campaigns, the tournament-bracket narrative structure, the tactical grid combat, and an orchestral score described by Game Informer as "the series' finest". GameSpot called it "the new high point to beat for future entries."

**Famitsu** published its review scores in the September 20 issue, continuing a strong domestic reception. Nintendo Life gave it **8/10**, one of the more conservative scores, citing the lengthy runtime as the primary reservation.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Is Out Now

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — the full 3D remake of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC* — launched worldwide on **September 17, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**.

The game picks up directly after 1st Chapter's ending, continuing Estelle Bright's journey. Combat blends real-time exploration with opt-in turn-based battles, retaining the orbment/Quartz system, Crafts, and S-Crafts from the remake. A **10% Steam launch discount** runs through **September 23**.

In Japan, **NIS America** announced the **Ouroboros Box** physical collector's edition, priced at **¥13,860**. It includes the physical game, an arranged music album spanning the Sky saga, sheep-themed in-game cosmetics, a comprehensive character book, and a **Blu-ray of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky The Animation***.

Sources: [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-worldwide-release-september-17-2026/) · [Operation Rainfall](https://operationrainfall.com/2026/09/18/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-out-now-for-ps5-nintendo-switch-2-nintendo-switch-steam/) · [Gematsu (releases.com)](https://www.releases.com/p/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter)

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation TGS Demo Confirms April 8, 2027 Launch

**Square Enix** headlined its **Tokyo Game Show 2026** presence with a playable floor demo for **Final Fantasy VII Revelation**, confirming the long-rumored **April 8, 2027** release date for the final chapter of the FFVII Remake trilogy.

The TGS demo builds on prior gamescom reveals (Typhon Summon, FITS jobs, Highwind traversal) by placing players directly into a boss encounter against **Jenova Deathmourner**, followed by a Sephiroth confrontation that bridges into a sequence showing Cloud and Aerith's psychic connection. The demo also showcased **Cloud and Vincent's Synergy Ability** and **Vincent's Gates of Hell Limit Break** — confirming Vincent Valentine as a full party member with his own Limit system.

The game releases on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC** via Steam, Epic Games Store, and Xbox on PC.

## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Announced — February 2027

**Square Enix** and developer **xeen Inc.** (the studio behind *Trials of Mana* and *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven*) announced **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** during the **Nintendo Direct on September 9, 2026**.

The game is a full **3D remake** of the **1995 Super Famicom RPG** *Romancing SaGa 3*. Players choose from **eight protagonists**, each with their own story and motivation, and can recruit more than **20 allies** across a non-linear adventure. The world is built around a recurring disaster called the **Rise of Morastrum**, which claims everyone born under the Star of Death every 300 years — except a single survivor known as the Child of Destiny.

New additions include all-new 3D graphics, overhauled combat and other systems, multiple difficulty options, and fully voiced cutscenes in **Japanese and English**.

The game launches **February 16, 2027**, for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/20/lufia-i-ii-the-sinistrals-saga-announced-for-pc-ps5-switch-and-switch-2/) · [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469133/lufia-i-amp-ii-the-sinistrals-saga-announced-for-switch-2-ps5-switch-and-pc/)

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World — TGS Gameplay Trailer and Demo Live Now

**Square Enix** released a **TGS 2026 gameplay trailer** for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** explaining core systems ahead of its **December 3, 2026** launch.

The trailer walks through the **monster scouting** system (recruiting enemies encountered in the wild), the returning **Synthesis** mechanic for combining monsters, and the new **Joint Battle** feature — a co-op combat mechanic in which players coordinate tactics alongside their monster companions, departing from the series' traditional solo turn-based structure.

A **playable demo** is now available on all platforms. Progress carries over to the full game: players can transfer up to eight monsters from their main party and reserves when the full release arrives.

The game launches **December 3, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC** via Steam and Windows.

Sources: [Gematsu (demo)](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-demo-now-available) · [Noisy Pixel (gameplay trailer)](https://noisypixel.net/dragon-quest-monsters-withered-world-gameplay-trailer/) · [Enduins (TGS trailer)](https://www.enduins.com/news/square-enix-debuts-dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-tgs-2026-trailer) · [Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-trailer-explains-scouting-and-joint-battles/)

## Edge of Memories Gets November 5 Launch Date — Will Ship as Midgar Studio's Final Game

In a development few expected after **Midgar Studio's court-ordered liquidation** in July, publisher **Nacon** confirmed that **Edge of Memories will launch on November 5, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC**. A **48-hour early access window opens November 3** for Deluxe Edition owners.

Nacon stated the launch plan is unaffected by the studio closure, as the game was near completion at the time. Edge of Memories will now serve as the **final release from Midgar Studio**, the 18-year-old Montpellier developer that was liquidated following Nacon's own insolvency proceedings. The game is set in the universe of Midgar's earlier title *Edge of Eternity*.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/edge-of-memories-launches-november-5) · [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Edge-of-Memories-gets-official-release-date.1405780.0.html) · [Play Asia (TGS)](https://play.asia/blog/2026/09/17/tgs-2026-edge-of-memories-launches-on-november-5/) · [Games Press](https://www.gamespress.com/EDGE-OF-MEMORIES-ANNOUNCES-ITS-RELEASE-DATE-AND-PRE-ORDERS-NOW-OPEN)

## Professor Layton and the New World of Steam Gets December 10 Release Date

**Level-5** revealed during the **Nintendo Direct on September 9, 2026** that **Professor Layton and the New World of Steam** will launch **December 10, 2026** — the first mainline Layton game in over a decade.

The game features what Level-5 is calling the **"biggest collection of puzzles in series history"** and releases simultaneously on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**. A separate trailer released the following day from Level-5 expanded on story details; notably, the September 9 Nintendo Direct also confirmed a PS5 release alongside the Nintendo versions — the first time the franchise has launched on a PlayStation platform.

## DECAPOLICE Delayed Again — Now 2027, Playable at TGS

**Level-5** announced that its cyberpunk detective RPG **DECAPOLICE** has been delayed **from 2026 to 2027**, marking the title's third delay (originally targeting 2023, then 2024, then 2026). A new gameplay trailer was shared alongside the announcement, and the game was **playable on the TGS 2026 floor**.

The game targets **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** and continues to look visually ambitious; Level-5 cited the need for more development time without specifying a more precise 2027 window.
