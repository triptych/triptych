## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct Reveals True Protagonist, September 17 Release

Nintendo held a dedicated **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct** on **August 4, 2026**, running roughly 20 minutes and revealing substantial new story and gameplay details for the Switch 2 exclusive ahead of its now-confirmed **September 17** launch.

The most significant reveal is **Eshmel**, a fifth protagonist who exists apart from the four Heroic Games competitors — Cai, Dietrich, Theodora, and Leda — previously shown. Set five years after those events, in **1454**, the kingdom of Dagda faces the apocalyptic threat of the demon god **Balor**. The four heroes of legend have disappeared, and Eshmel wields a unique power: the blessing of the goddess **Fortuna**, which allows travel back to **1449** to alter the fates of those four heroes before the cataclysm unfolds. This temporal structure gives the game a dual-timeline design — players can experience both eras and carry consequences forward.

The Direct also clarified that Eshmel's non-linear campaign interweaves with each hero's arc, meaning the four-protagonist mode and Eshmel's route are not separate games but a unified narrative that can be approached in different orders.

Sources: [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/nintendo-direct/2026/08/04/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-direct-reveals-true-protagonist-and-plot) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-direct-august-2000721176) · [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/nintendo-direct-august-2026-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave/)

## Final Fantasy XIV Lands on Switch 2 — Reviews Are Strong

**Final Fantasy XIV Online** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**, marking the first time the critically acclaimed MMORPG has appeared on a Nintendo platform. All current expansions are included, and Square Enix waived the first month of subscription fees for new Switch 2 players.

Review reception has been positive. Nintendo Life awarded the port a **9/10**, calling it a landmark arrival for Eorzea and noting that the Switch 2 holds its own technically against PS5 and PC — with modest visual trade-offs in exchange for true hybrid portability. CGMagazine's performance review highlighted stable framerates in the overworld and in most duty content, flagging only crowded trial and raid encounters as areas where performance occasionally dips. COGconnected described the combat and content library as fully intact, while warning that certain UI-heavy systems like macro setup and chat management benefit significantly from using a keyboard via the Switch 2's USB-C port.

The install size is approximately **117 GB**. A Free Trial is available for new players who want to try the game through the end of *A Realm Reborn* before committing.

Sources: [Nintendo Life Review](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/final-fantasy-xiv-online) · [CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/final-fantasy-xiv-nsw2/) · [COGconnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/final-fantasy-xiv-switch-2-review/) · [RPGamer Deep Look](https://rpgamer.com/2026/08/final-fantasy-xiv-switch-2-deep-look/)

## Suikoden: Star Leap Launches in Japan on iOS and Android

**Konami**'s free-to-play mobile RPG **Suikoden: Star Leap**, developed by **MYTHRIL**, officially launched in Japan on **iOS and Android on August 7, 2026**, following a pre-launch livestream the evening before.

Set in **SY 453** — a few years before the original *Suikoden* — the story follows an unnamed protagonist from a lakeside village east of the Scarlet Moon Empire. After mysterious enemies destroy the village, they set off alongside three companions — **Hisui**, **Shirin**, and **Shapur** — on a journey of restoration. The game maintains the franchise's signature **108 Stars of Destiny** mechanic, building toward a massive roster of recruitable allies. Konami has confirmed a future **PC (Steam)** release alongside a global mobile rollout, though dates for both have not yet been announced.

## Kingdom Hearts IV — D23 2026 "Coco" Trailer Confirms Late 2027 Window

**Square Enix** and **Disney** debuted an extended **Kingdom Hearts IV** trailer during the **D23 2026** fan convention, revealing **Coco** as the next confirmed Disney/Pixar world in the game. Sora visits the Land of the Dead, with the trailer depicting colourful environments and a sequence involving Ernesto de la Cruz's mansion.

The presentation, hosted during a "Deep Dive into Kingdom Hearts" panel with co-director **Tai Yasue** and series creator **Tetsuya Nomura**, confirmed a **late 2027 release window** for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC** via Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store. An extended version of the trailer was released simultaneously on YouTube.

Beyond the Coco world, the trailer includes sequences featuring **Donald and Goofy as playable characters** and a brief segment following **King Mickey** as he searches for clues to Sora's whereabouts — suggesting a wider playable roster than prior numbered entries.

## Steins;Gate Re:Boot Launches on Steam and Japanese Consoles

**5pb. / Spike Chunsoft**'s **Steins;Gate Re:Boot** — a remake of the 2009 science-fiction visual novel — released on **August 20, 2026** for **PC via Steam** (with English text from day one) and for **PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2** in Japan.

The remake uses updated **E-mote** expressive sprite technology (similar to *Anonymous;Code*) and adds new story content to the original narrative. A **10% launch discount** is active on Steam during the first two weeks, and players who purchase before **November 30, 2026** receive **Steins;Gate: Octet of Shifting Space** as a free bonus.

Western console players on Switch and Switch 2 will need to wait: the North American and European console release is scheduled for **October 29, 2026**. A Digital Deluxe Edition is available on both platforms, bundling a digital art book and soundtrack alongside the game.
