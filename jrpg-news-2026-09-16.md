## Level-5 Vision 2026 II "Dream" Reveals Professor Layton Date, Yo-kai Watch 2 Remake, Curious Village Surprise

**Level-5** held its **LEVEL5 VISION 2026 II "Dream"** online showcase on **September 10** at 21:00 JST, delivering a string of announcements for the company's upcoming lineup across Switch 2, Switch, PS5, and PC.

The headline news was a **December 10 release date for Professor Layton and the New World of Steam** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**. The showcase also included an extended trailer and a behind-the-scenes look at the game's theme song, "The New World." The game follows Layton on a new investigation set in a steampunk-era world.

The showcase's other major reveal was **Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain**, an enhanced remake of the 3DS original announced for **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam** with no release date yet. Level-5 described the project as a full graphics overhaul while preserving the original game's structure, with added story content and new Yo-kai to be revealed later.

The showcase closed with a surprise: **Professor Layton and the Curious Village Remake**, a ground-up re-creation of the first Layton entry, was announced for **Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam** in 2027, with full HD widescreen animations and enhanced exploration.

## Dragon Quest Heroes: Torneko's Mystery Dungeon -Classic HD- Shadow-Drops for First Official English Release

**Square Enix** shadow-dropped **Dragon Quest Heroes: Torneko's Mystery Dungeon -Classic HD-** on **September 9** simultaneously on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**, with mobile versions (iOS, Android, Google Play Games) arriving alongside.

The release marks the **first time the 1993 original has been officially localized into English**. The game is a roguelike dungeon-crawler following **Torneko**, the weapons merchant from *Dragon Quest IV*, as he explores procedurally generated dungeons to stock the greatest item shop in the world. It is historically significant as the title that launched the Mystery Dungeon genre in Japan.

The HD remaster adds widescreen support, quality-of-life improvements, and a new Standard and Special Edition release structure. Square Enix also released a free demo for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** (releasing December 3) in tandem with the Torneko announcement.

## Another Eden Begins Launches — Reviews Call It a Faithful Gacha-Free Adaptation

**Wright Flyer Studios** launched **Another Eden Begins** on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** on **September 16–17** (PC first, consoles a day later), completing a port of the acclaimed mobile JRPG *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space* with all predatory monetization removed.

The game retains the script by **Masato Kato** — the writer behind *Chrono Trigger*, *Final Fantasy VII*, and *Xenogears* — and its time-travel narrative spanning multiple eras. The console and PC version bundles the main story and major arcs, removes gacha banners and pull mechanics entirely, includes **18 playable party members**, a reworked progression system, and **over 10 different endings** unlockable through New Game+.

Critical reception is mixed-to-positive. Reviews consistently praise the writing and its nostalgic throwback feel, describing it as reminiscent of a rediscovered PSP-era classic. Several outlets note that the game's pacing and some legacy systems feel dated by modern console standards, though Kato's storytelling and the combat system compensate.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches Tomorrow — Reviews Already Live

**Nihon Falcom** and **NIS America** are launching **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** tomorrow, **September 17**, on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The game is a full modern remake of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC*, the sequel to last year's *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter* remake (September 2025). The story continues with **Estelle Bright** setting out across the Liberl Kingdom to find **Joshua** after he disappears amid a growing continental conspiracy, and her eventual confrontation with the Enforcers of Ouroboros.

Like its predecessor, 2nd Chapter features rebuilt HD graphics, tweaked combat systems, and additional side content not present in the original 3DS-era release. Embargo reviews went live today, calling it a superb sequel that strengthens the emotional payoff from the first game.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Launch Day Tomorrow

**Intelligent Systems** and **Nintendo**'s **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** — the **eighteenth mainline entry** in the series and a **Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive** — launches tomorrow, **September 17**, at **midnight local time** (technically available on the West Coast from 9 PM PT tonight, September 16).

The game is set in the same world as *Fire Emblem: Three Houses* and features a **four-route structure**, one per protagonist: **Cai, Theodora, Dietrich**, and **Leda**, each leading a distinct narrative path through the same central conflict.

The signature new mechanic is **Blaze Arts**: powerful signature moves that cost HP to use and fill a **Blaze Gauge**. Filling the gauge halfway grants a power boost, but filling it completely triggers **Burst**, which halves the character's maximum HP and ends the bonus — creating a risk-reward tradeoff around aggressive play.

## Culdcept The First Saturn Tribute Arrives on Switch — Genre Hybrid Classic Now in English

**OmiyaSoft** and **Clear River Games** released **Culdcept The First Saturn Tribute** on **Nintendo Switch** (and PS5) on **September 8** at **$29.99**, with a simultaneous physical edition from Clear River. The PC version had launched on Steam on July 30.

Culdcept is a card-game/board-game hybrid where players summon creatures onto board spaces in a style reminiscent of Magic: The Gathering played on a Monopoly-style map. *The First Saturn Tribute* is a remaster of the 1997 Saturn original, the game that launched the franchise.

The Switch version includes modern quality-of-life additions: checkpoint rewind (usable at any Fort or Castle), Quick Save and Load at any point, and updated visuals. This is the first time the Saturn original has received a wide Western physical release.
