## Persona 6 Domain Updated — Fans Speculate TGA 2026 Reveal

The Japanese **Persona 6** website received a new SSL certificate on **November 8, 2026** — the first renewal since June — reigniting speculation that **Atlus** is preparing a trailer for **The Game Awards 2026**, scheduled for **December 10** at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles.

The precedent driving the speculation: the Persona 4 Revival domain saw a similar certificate refresh roughly one month before its second major gameplay trailer debuted at the Xbox Games Showcase in June 2026. Community members who track Atlus certificate records noted the timing aligns with TGA's typical announcement window.

No confirmation of a Persona 6 showing has come from Atlus or SEGA. The game was first announced at the Xbox Games Showcase in June 2026 and has not received a release date.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo**, **Gust**, and **Akatsuki Games** will shut down the Japanese version of **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, four days from now.

The closure was announced on **August 27** with the development team citing an inability to continue delivering content at a satisfactory standard. New paid currency purchases were halted immediately after the announcement, though gems already owned can be spent until shutdown. Unused paid gems are eligible for refunds from **November 26 through February 24, 2027**, and the app itself stops being downloadable on February 24, 2027.

The global version had already ended on March 28, 2025, making the Japanese shutdown the final chapter for the title across all regions. *Atelier Resleriana* launched in Japan in January 2024, putting its total lifespan at under three years.

## The Blood of Dawnwalker — Patch 1.2 Adds New Game Plus

**Rebel Wolves** released **Patch 1.2** for **The Blood of Dawnwalker** on **November 17, 2026** across PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S, adding the game's first New Game Plus mode.

NG+ unlocks after completing the epilogue, after which a new option appears in the main menu allowing players to restart with their existing levels, skills, and items intact. In NG+ all enemies scale to match the player's level, removing the original game's soft area progression order.

Additional patch contents:
- NG+ item variants with new stat profiles
- Balance adjustments to enemy health and special-ability frequency for enemies in levels 17–30 of the base game
- New tracks added to the in-game soundtrack

The patch is free for all owners of the base game. Rebel Wolves has not confirmed whether the game's 30-day in-game calendar resets in NG+.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio — Switch 2 Version Now Available

**Atlus**'s acclaimed fantasy RPG **Metaphor: ReFantazio** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026**. A free demo released ahead of launch allowed players to transfer saves into the full game.

The Switch 2 release was announced earlier this year as part of Atlus's broader multiplatform strategy following the game's October 2025 debut on PS5 and PC. The Switch 2 version runs at **60 FPS handheld and docked** with enhanced texture filtering via the Switch 2's hardware.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 3 Switch 2 Edition — December 3 Details

**Nintendo** and **Monolith Soft** are set to launch **Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** on **December 3, 2026**. A **$9.99 upgrade pack** is available for existing owners of the original Switch version.

**Game Informer** reported in September that the edition introduces two notable new additions:
- A **survivors-like mode**, adding roguelite replayability to the base game's combat systems
- **Shimmer**, a new hero character who joins the roster alongside the base game's existing cast

Technical upgrades mirror those of the XC2 Switch 2 Edition: 4K docked / 1080p handheld and 60 FPS in both modes.

Sources: [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/nintendo-direct/2026/09/09/xenoblade-chronicles-3-switch-2-edition-adds-a-survivors-like-mode-and) · [Nintendo Store](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/xenoblade-chronicles-3-nintendo-switch-2-edition-switch-2/)
