## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Expansion Pass — Wave 1 Live Now

Nintendo announced the [Expansion Pass for Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-expansion-pass-announced), with Wave 1 DLC available now. The pass costs $34.99 and includes three waves of content:

- **Wave 1 (now):** Four new relic-inspired weapons
- **Wave 2 (Holiday 2026):** New god allies
- **Wave 3 (2027):** New story content featuring characters Bertrand, Talimun, Orchel, and Anatolia, plus new allies and classes

A bundle of the base game + Expansion Pass is available for $105. Version 1.1.0 of the game also launched alongside the announcement.

**Platform:** Nintendo Switch 2

## Resonance of Fate / End of Eternity Enhanced Remaster — Now on Steam

The [enhanced remaster of Resonance of Fate / End of Eternity](https://jrpgpursuit.com/) launched on Steam this week. The classic tri-Ace JRPG features stylish gun-based combat and a distinctive clockwork city setting.

**Platform:** PC (Steam)

## Fate/EXTRA Record — January 28, 2027 Release Date Confirmed

[Fate/EXTRA Record has a confirmed release date of January 28, 2027](https://gematsu.com/2026/09/fate-extra-record-launches-january-28-2027-for-ps5-switch-2-ps4-switch-and-pc) for Switch 2, Switch, and PC. A new [showcase trailer](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21297-fateex-record-showcase-trailer-explains-remakes-gameplay-systems-in-more-detail) covering the remake's gameplay systems in depth is now available. A character promotional video for Saber was also released.

This remake of the 2010 PSP RPG is published by XSEED Games in North America and Marvelous Europe in Europe.

- Standard edition: $59.99 (consoles), $69.99 (Switch 2), PC pricing TBA
- A demo was playable at Tokyo Game Show 2026

**Platforms:** Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC (Steam)

## Metaphor: ReFantazio — Coming to Switch 2 and Steam November 12

[Metaphor: ReFantazio arrives on Nintendo Switch 2 and Steam on November 12, 2026](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/16349-the-rpgs-2026-every-rpg-their-release-dates). ## Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga — Classic SNES JRPGs Return in 2027

[Taito and Clear River Games announced Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/lufia-i-ii-the-sinistrals-saga-announced-for-ps5-switch-2-switch-and-pc) in September, bringing both 1993's *Lufia & the Fortress of Doom* and its 1995 prequel *Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals* to modern platforms. The collection is "enhanced for modern play." No specific release date has been announced beyond 2027.

**Platforms:** Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC (Steam)
