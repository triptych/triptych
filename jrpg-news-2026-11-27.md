## Xenoblade Chronicles 3 — Switch 2 Edition Overview Trailer Reveals Heroes' Vault and New Hero Shimmer

Nintendo and **Monolith Soft** released the overview trailer for **Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition**, launching **December 3, 2026** digitally. The digital edition is **$69.99**; owners of the original Switch version can upgrade for **$9.99**.

The trailer's headline reveal is **Heroes' Vault**, an all-new roguelike battle mode exclusive to the Switch 2 Edition. The mode is wave-based: each defeated enemy fills a gauge that players spend on upgraded abilities or additional allies, with a tougher boss waiting at the end of the run. Sources describe the overhead presentation as similar to *Hades* and *Vampire Survivors*.

The edition also adds **Shimmer** as a new Hero — a speedy attacker built around rapid-fire combos and a Foresight power that lets her briefly anticipate enemy moves. Additional improvements include **4K resolution docked / 1080p handheld, 60 FPS in both modes**, and event scenes that are now fully voiced in both English and Japanese (the original release had many unvoiced scenes).

## Persona 4 Revival — Yasogami High Social Links Revealed With New Abilities

**Atlus** published details this week on the Yasogami High Social Links in **Persona 4 Revival**, including three newly confirmed abilities that unlock at **Rank 3** of each relationship.

- **Workaholic** (Ryotaro Dojima's Social Link): Allows the protagonist to continue home activities — studying, building model kits — after returning from a dungeon run, rather than automatically going to sleep. This is comparable to Sadayo Kawakami's signature ability in *Persona 5*, and fans have already nicknamed it "Dojima's Special Massage."
- **The Shape of Kindness** (Ai Ebihara's Social Link): Accelerates growth of the Expression and Understanding social stats.
- **Let's Speak Up** (Kou Ichijo or Daisuke Nagase's Social Link): When an ally guards the protagonist in battle, the protagonist has a chance to gain an Attack buff for 3 turns.

The reveal was accompanied by introductions to six new Yasogami High students. **Persona 4 Revival** launches **February 18, 2027** for PC via Steam; a **Nintendo Switch 2 version** follows on **May 20, 2027**.

## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United — Release Date Moved Up, New Companions and Systems Detailed

**Square Enix** announced that **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** has been moved to **February 4, 2027** (February 5 on Steam), over a week earlier than the original February 16 date. The **Digital Deluxe** version offers early access from **February 1** (February 2 on Steam).

One important caveat for players who already pre-ordered the Nintendo Switch or Switch 2 digital version: Square Enix has confirmed those pre-orders have been **cancelled** due to the date change and must be re-placed.

A new batch of companion and system details accompanied the announcement:

- **New traveling companions:** Fullbright, a merchant heir from Wilmington and descendant of one of the twelve sacred knights of the Matriarch; Ward; Undine; Herman; and Boston, a Lobster Risk member living on the island at the edge of the world.
- **Business minigame:** Unlocked through Fullbright, players purchase and develop properties across five industry categories, compete in bidding wars, and collect funds that fluctuate with the in-game economic forecast.
- **Zweig Tournament:** The tournament held in the northern bastion of Zweig has been expanded from the original game.
- **Combat updates:** Synthesized Arts work differently in the remake, and new battle formations such as Triple Incisors and Rampaging Mammoth have been added alongside the returning Glimmer technique-learning system.

The game is set for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

## Digimon Alysion — Pre-Registration Open, App Store Lists December 23

**Bandai Namco**'s free-to-play mobile card game **Digimon Alysion** has opened global pre-registration across 49 countries for iOS and Android. The app was designed as a card-game competitor in the same space as *Pokémon TCG Pocket* and features an original Digimon story alongside TCG battles. No official launch date has been confirmed by Bandai; a U.S. App Store listing shows **December 23, 2026**, though Bandai has not announced that date directly.
