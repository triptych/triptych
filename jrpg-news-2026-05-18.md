# JRPG News — May 18, 2026

## Starbites Gets Last-Minute Switch 2 Delay; Launches May 21 on Other Platforms

**STARBITES**, the sci-fi mech RPG from **IKINAGAMES** published by **NIS America**, launches **May 21** on **Nintendo Switch, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam** — but a last-minute delay has pushed the **Nintendo Switch 2** version to an unspecified later date.

The game follows salvager Lukida and her slingshot-focused Motorbot as she works to save the desert planet of Bitter. Combat is turn-based with a "Drivers High" gauge system that lets players interrupt the normal turn order by dealing or absorbing damage. Early Switch 1 reviews have flagged technical problems including fuzzy visuals, frame stutters, crashes, missing combat effects, and voice acting issues, though the overall narrative and characters have drawn praise.

## Zero Parades: For Dead Spies Pre-Launch Showcase Drops Ahead of May 21 Release

**ZA/UM** — the studio behind *Disco Elysium* — posted an **11-minute gameplay deep dive** on May 15 for their upcoming espionage RPG **Zero Parades: For Dead Spies**, which launches **May 21 on PC** (Steam, GOG, Epic Games Store) for **$39.99**. A PS5 version is planned to follow later in the year.

Players take the role of operant **Hershel Wilk** (alias CASCADE), a brilliant but haunted spy recalled after a catastrophic past mission for one final assignment. The showcase revealed skills-driven dialogue, an intricately realized spy-world setting, and the game's fashion collection and companion pen-and-paper adventure. Early critics have called it "an endlessly captivating narrative RPG" that stands in *Disco Elysium*'s shadow but earns its own identity.

## Persona 4 Revival Leak Points to February 2027 Launch; Xbox Showcase May Bring Full Reveal

A well-regarded Atlus/SEGA leaker has claimed that **Persona 4 Revival** will release in **February 2027** — the same month *Persona 3 Reload* launched — following the franchise's established pattern. Development is expected to wrap in August 2026, with localization work filling the remaining window.

**Sega** has officially confirmed only that the game will not release before April 2026 (i.e., within their FY2027 window), leaving the precise date unannounced. The likeliest venue for a full reveal is the **Xbox Games Showcase on June 7, 2026**, where Atlus is widely expected to appear following the *Persona 3 Reload* precedent.

---

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Dated September 17

**Nihon Falcom** and **GungHo Online Entertainment** have confirmed that **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — the full 3D remake of the classic PSP RPG *Trails in the Sky SC* — will launch globally on **September 17, 2026** for **PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

The game continues Estelle's search for Joshua and her confrontation with the Ouroboros Enforcers, rebuilt from the ground up with modern 3D visuals and refined gameplay. This is the second in Falcom's ongoing series of remakes following *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter*. Pre-orders are live now; the standard edition is priced at **$59.99** (Switch/PS5/Steam) and **$64.99** (Switch 2 Edition). A **Golden Wings Collector's Edition** is also available.
