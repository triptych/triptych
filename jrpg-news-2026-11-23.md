## Metaphor: ReFantazio — Switch 2 Version Launches, Price Cut to $39.99 Everywhere

**ATLUS** launched **Metaphor: ReFantazio** on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026**. The Switch 2 version adds three graphics options — **Performance**, **Balanced**, and **Graphics** modes — giving players flexibility between frame rate and visual fidelity in both docked and handheld play. A free demo with save-data transfer is available on the Nintendo eShop.

Alongside the Switch 2 release, ATLUS permanently reduced the base game's price to **$39.99 on all platforms** (3,990 yen in Japan). A **SteelBook Edition** physical release is priced at $49.99.

Metaphor: ReFantazio originally launched in October 2024 and went on to sell **one million copies on launch day**, making it ATLUS's fastest-selling game at release. The Switch 2 edition marks its first appearance on a Nintendo platform.

## Terminus Historia — Former AliceSoft Director's Dark Fairy Tale RPG Hits Steam Early Access

**Terminus Historia**, a dark fairy tale pixel-art RPG developed by **IMYUIC** and directed by a **former AliceSoft director**, launched on **Steam Early Access on November 15, 2026**.

The game is a free-scenario, turn-based RPG in which weapons and magic grow stronger and unlock new effects the more they are used. The Early Access build includes all **four story routes** to completion, with each running approximately **7–15 hours**, and supports **Japanese, English, and Simplified Chinese** text. The game originally released in 2023 on DLsite before this Steam expansion.

The developers plan to remain in Early Access for approximately one year, using the period to refine the UI, improve balance, and strengthen the localization. A **30% launch discount** runs through November 28.

## Danganronpa 2×2 — Akimbo Consensus System Detailed in Systems Trailer

**Spike Chunsoft** released a **systems trailer** for **Danganronpa 2×2** on **November 16, 2026**, revealing the full structure of **Class Trials in Slayhem Mode**.

The headline mechanic is **Akimbo Consensus**, an entirely new addition exclusive to Slayhem Mode. Rather than the traditional push-and-pull of Nonstop Debates, Akimbo Consensus lets players **endorse or debunk** competing theories during the trial, steering the debate's momentum. **Debate Scrum**, first introduced in *Danganronpa V3: Killing Harmony*, also returns in this mode.

Slayhem Mode operates at a fixed difficulty described as harder than the base game's Hard setting, emphasizing the mode's design as a more chaotic, unpredictable experience alongside the standard narrative path. Prior coverage had confirmed Slayhem Mode as an alternate-victims-and-culprits mode where the killing game unfolds differently each run.

**Danganronpa 2×2** launches **January 14, 2027** in North America and Europe for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**.

## Fate/EXTRA Record — Dan Faction Trailer Released, Dan Faction Joins Rivalry Lineup Ahead of January Launch

**Aniplex**, **TYPE-MOON**, and **Meteorise** have been rolling out a series of faction-focused character trailers for **Fate/EXTRA Record** as it approaches its **January 28, 2027** worldwide launch. The most recent trailer, released in mid-November, spotlights the **Dan Faction** — Dan Blackmore, a retired sniper who opposes injustice, and his Servant **Robin Hood (Archer)**, voiced by **Kohsuke Toriumi**. Dan himself is voiced by **Mugihito**.

Previous trailers this fall covered the **Shinji Faction** (October) and **Julius Faction** (early November), rounding out the roster of rival Masters the player faces across the Moon Cell Holy Grail War. The game is a full remake of the original *Fate/EXTRA* (2010 PSP), rebuilt in a new engine with an updated deck-building Dramatic Command Battle system, expanded story content, and new characters including **Ciel** as the shopkeeper and **Arcueid Brunestud** as an enemy Servant.

**Fate/EXTRA Record** launches **January 28, 2027** for **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** simultaneously worldwide.

## Mercenaries Grace: Black Feather and the Golden Right Arm — Japan PS5 Launch November 25

**RideonJapan**'s **Mercenaries Grace: Black Feather and the Golden Right Arm** launches for **PlayStation 5 in Japan on November 25, 2026**, priced at **3,850 yen**. A **Nintendo Switch** version follows in **December 2026**, and **PC via Steam** in **early 2027**. Western release timing has not been announced.

The game is the **eighth entry in the Mercenaries Saga series** and marks a notable mechanical shift for the franchise. Rather than the traditional simultaneous-phase-based turns of earlier titles, *Mercenaries Grace* uses a **chess/shogi-style turn order** in which only one unit per side acts at a time, emphasizing positional play and moment-to-moment tactical decisions.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** are shutting down the Japanese servers for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026** at 16:00 JST. In their developer letter, the team cited an inability to continue providing a service that would satisfy players as the reason for closure.

The game launched in Japan in October 2024. Its **global version** (mobile and PC) had already closed in **March 2025**, making the Japanese shutdown the final end of the game's service. The final story content — main scenario Chapter 33, "Our Future" — was added on August 27, 2026. Refund applications for unspent premium currency will be accepted through February 24, 2027.

## Previously Uncovered: Terranigma and Lufia I & II Return to Modern Platforms

Two beloved SNES-era JRPGs were announced for modern platforms between the last daily doc (July 27) and now.

**Terranigma** (1995, Enix / Quintet): **Clear River Games** announced a re-release on **August 24, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam, GOG)** in **early 2027**. This marks the first time the game will receive an official **North American release** — the original was never published in the US despite receiving European and Australian localizations. The re-release preserves the original game with a CRT filter option and an updated port.

**Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga**: **Taito** and publisher **Clear River Games** announced this two-game collection at **Tokyo Game Show in September 2026**. It bundles *Lufia & the Fortress of Doom* and *Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals* for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC (Steam)** in **2027**. The collection will mark the first European release of the original *Lufia* and will include Spanish text localization, among other languages.
