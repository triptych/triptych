## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Near-Universal Acclaim — 89 Metacritic

**Intelligent Systems** and **Nintendo**'s **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 17, 2026**, to reviews that have already established it as one of the year's most acclaimed titles.

The game earned an **89 on Metacritic** from 53 scored reviews, with 13 outlets awarding perfect scores. IGN gave it a 10/10. **Famitsu** awarded **9/9/9/9**, calling the unit control in turn-based battles "the most comfortable in this genre." GameSpot described it as "a sprawling masterpiece of a strategy RPG" and placed it at the top of the series.

Fortune's Weave features **four interconnected storylines** that reviewers praised for their depth and scope. The main criticism across outlets was pacing — GamesRadar+ noted "grand scope and ambition" weighed down by busywork in later chapters. Despite that, the consensus places Fortune's Weave as one of 2026's best games and a career-high for the Fire Emblem franchise.

## Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen Arrives October 9 — New Region, 12 Lost Rite Dungeons, Switch 2 Port

**Capcom**'s **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** expansion launches **October 9, 2026** on **PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch 2** (new base + expansion bundle at $49.99), and PlayStation 5 / Xbox Series X|S (DLC at $29.99 for existing owners).

The expansion adds **Norgan**, a desolate, snow-covered region once part of the kingdom of Verendum, abandoned centuries ago under mysterious circumstances. The zone is tuned for experienced Arisens and their pawns, with 15–20 hours of new story content. Scattered across both the Norgan region and the main map are **12 "Lost Rite" dungeons** — self-contained challenge areas lasting 30–60 minutes each, reminiscent of the original game's Everfall.

The centerpiece new enemy is **Finbul**, a frost dragon exclusive to Norgan. The expansion also brings back the **Evil Eye** in an enhanced form and introduces a **Hard Mode** toggle that rebalances enemy stats and AI aggression across the entire base game.

TGS 2026 hands-on impressions described Norgan as "genuinely eerie" and praised the Lost Rite structure for giving endgame players a focused reason to revisit the game. The **Nintendo Switch 2 port** of the base game is included in the $49.99 bundle and marks the game's first appearance on a Nintendo platform.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Launches October 16 — TOSE Handles Remaster, Graphics/SFX Toggle Included

**Bandai Namco** has confirmed **Tales of Eternia Remastered** launches **October 16, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**.

The remaster is developed by **TOSE**, the studio behind the well-received *Tales of Graces F Remastered*. It brings the 2001 PS1 original — released in North America as *Tales of Destiny II* — to modern platforms with enhanced visuals and a new quality-of-life suite.

Key new features include:
- **Graphics Mode and SFX Mode**: freely toggle between remastered visuals/sound and the original versions at any time
- **Boost feature**: adjust experience gain rate and random encounter frequency independently
- **Destination icons**: toggleable minimap markers guiding players to objectives
- **High-speed battle mode**: accelerates Auto-battle sequences

The game follows **Reid Hershel** and companions across two parallel worlds as they seek the power of the Greater Craymel to prevent the "Grand Fall," the cataclysmic collision of both worlds. It was notably absent from Western releases for over 20 years, making this the first time European players can play an official localization.

## Final Fantasy Resonance Demo Draws Strong Impressions — 4-Hour Preview, Progress Carries Over

The free demo for **Final Fantasy Resonance** — which launches **October 22, 2026** on Switch, Switch 2, PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S — has been available for several weeks and reviewers are largely enthusiastic.

The demo spans approximately **four hours**, covers the full first chapter, includes multiple boss fights, and transfers save progress to the full game. Standout observations from early coverage:

- **Combat**: Turn-based command combat returns, anchored by a **stagger gauge** separate from the HP bar. Breaking an enemy's stagger prevents their next action and leaves them temporarily vulnerable, adding a layer of resource management beyond raw damage.
- **Visuals**: The HD-2D art style is noted for dynamically blending pixel art, animated cutscenes, stop-motion effects, and brief 3D sequences in ways that shift during gameplay. RPG Site ran a technical comparison across Switch 2, PS5 Pro, Steam Deck, and ROG Ally — all versions were reported as stable with no major performance issues.
- **Story**: The demo covers the prologue from *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius*, retelling Rain, Lasswell, and Fina's origin story as a standalone single-player narrative. Critics describe the tone as "classic Final Fantasy" — crystals, a falling kingdom, and light-versus-dark themes.
- **PC performance**: Lords of Gaming and Fuzzfinger both reported 120 fps at 1080p with no crashes on mid-range hardware, including Steam Deck.
