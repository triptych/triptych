## Final Fantasy Resonance Launches to Strong Reviews

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy Resonance** launched **October 22** on Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam (and PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S). Early reviews are mostly positive.

**Famitsu** gave the game **36/40** — four reviewers each scoring a 9 — putting the main story at **20–30 hours** and 70+ hours with extra content. Aggregated scores reported by various outlets include an 85 for Switch 2 (unverified against Metacritic directly). Reviewers praised the **HD-2D visuals** and **turn-based combat** while noting mixed reception on world-building and writing compared to series highlights.

The game was previously announced with its pricing and platform details; this week marks its full release.

## One Piece: Grand Gourmet Launches Today on Switch and PC

**Bandai Namco** and **Kairosoft** launched **One Piece: Grand Gourmet** today, **October 23**, on Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam, iOS, and Android at **$19.99**.

Players build and manage **Baratie Number 2**, a floating restaurant run alongside Sanji and the Straw Hat Crew. The game features over **400 One Piece characters** as staff and customers rendered in pixel art, and over 200 furniture and decorative items for restaurant customization. The Switch 2 version supports Joy-Con 2 mouse controls. In Japan, the Switch and Switch 2 versions launched a day early on October 22; Steam and mobile remain October 23 worldwide.

## Vixen 357 — Classic Sega Mega Drive Strategy RPG Gets First English Digital Release

**Ratalaika Games** published **Vixen 357** digitally today, **October 23**, on Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam (and PlayStation platforms) for **$5.99**. It is the first official English-language digital release of the 1992 Sega Mega Drive tactical RPG.

Originally developed by **Masaya** and released only in Japan over three decades ago, Vixen 357 is a mech-piloting strategy RPG often compared to early Fire Emblem entries — **pilot deaths are permanent**, and players can assign VECTOR units to any character. The re-release adds modern conveniences including **rewind, save states, screen filters, turbo, and cheats**, plus an in-game gallery.

The English fan translation community had previously produced unofficial patches; this release marks the game's first appearance on a storefront with official English support.

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Launches October 29 in the West on Switch and Switch 2

**Spike Chunsoft** will release **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** in North America and Europe on **October 29, 2026** for Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation 5 (and PC). The Xbox Series X and PC versions launched in North America on **August 20**, the same day as Japan.

The remake is built on the scenario from **STEINS;GATE ELITE** and adds a brand-new **Gamma Worldline** story arc not present in the original 2009 visual novel. Standard and **Steelbook physical editions** are planned. The Switch 2 version is listed at **$59.99** on Target with pre-orders open; the Nintendo UK listing notes an **English, Japanese, and Chinese** language option, PEGI 16 rating, and a 7.8 GB download.

## Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga Announced for Switch, Switch 2, and PC in 2027

**Taito** and **Clear River Games** announced **Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga** in September 2026 at the PC Gaming Show Tokyo Direct. The collection pairs **Lufia & the Fortress of Doom** (1993) and **Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals** (1995) and targets **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam** in **2027** — marking the first time either title has had an official PC release in over thirty years.

Physical editions are planned for Switch, Switch 2, and PS5, though pricing and timing have not been announced. The announcement trailer shows CRT scanline effects; whether these appear as selectable in-game filters has not been confirmed. The Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, and DS entries in the series are not included.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/lufia-i-ii-the-sinistrals-saga-announced-for-ps5-switch-2-switch-and-pc) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21439-lufia-i-ii-the-sinistrals-saga-pc-ps5-switch-2-steam-release) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/09/20/lufia-returns-in-2027-with-lufia-i-ii-the-sinistrals-saga/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/20/lufia-i-ii-the-sinistrals-saga-announced-for-pc-ps5-switch-and-switch-2/)
