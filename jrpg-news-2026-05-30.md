# JRPG News — May 30, 2026

## LumenTale: Memories of Trey Launches on Steam and Switch

**Beehive Studios** and publisher **Team17** released **LumenTale: Memories of Trey** on **May 26** for **PC (Steam)** and **Nintendo Switch**.

LumenTale is a creature-collecting RPG that steers the monster-catcher formula toward traditional JRPG territory, emphasizing worldbuilding and a protagonist with his own defined personality. Players control **Trey**, a cyborg found unconscious in the Scarlet Woods with no memory of his past, who sets out to recover his lost memories by befriending creatures called **Animon**. The game features **140 capturable Animon**, tactical **4v4 battles** (alongside standard 1v1 duels), plus cooking, crafting, and home-furnishing via a customizable **Anispace**. A launch discount of 10% is active on Steam.

Early user reception is **Mostly Positive** on Steam (72% of ~393 reviews).

## Echo Generation 2 Is Out Now on PC, Xbox, and Game Pass

**Cococucumber** launched **Echo Generation 2** on **May 27** for **PC and Xbox Series X|S**, with a **day-one Game Pass** release. The game is the sequel to the studio's 2021 cult-hit and overhauls its predecessor's combat by replacing straightforward turn-based battles with a **deckbuilding system**.

Players take control of **Jack**, an ordinary dad who stumbles into a dimension beyond his hometown of **Maple Town** while fighting extra-terrestrial enemies with a cast of quirky allies. The deck is built from **150+ collectible cards** spread across six playable characters, each with a distinct combat style — cards can be chained into combos, used to summon allies, and combined to trigger powerful (or chaotic) effects. The pre-release demo had earned a **92% positive rating** on Steam ahead of launch.

## Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered Now Available on PC

**Aquaplus** released **Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered** on **May 27** for **PC worldwide** (PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2 in Japan only). The game continues the story of **Oshtor** and his allies after they return as heroes to the empire of **Yamato**, set prior to the events of *Utawarerumono: Mask of Deception* and serving as a sequel to *Monochrome Mobius: Rights and Wrongs Forgotten*.

The PC version supports English, Japanese, and Chinese. A launch bundle pairing the game with *Monochrome Mobius* is available at a discount through **June 18**. Current Steam user rating sits at **77% positive**.

## Persona 5: The Phantom X Version 4.1 — Persona 3 Reload Crossover Live

**Atlus** and developer **Black Wings Game Studio** launched **Version 4.1** of *Persona 5: The Phantom X*, bringing a full **Persona 3 Reload crossover** story event to the global version of the game. The update introduces **Makoto Yuki** (the protagonist of *Persona 3 Reload*) as a playable **5-Star Fire-attribute Assassin** focused on offense.

The crossover features familiar *P3R* locations including **Paulownia Mall** on Tatsumi Port Island. Makoto can be obtained through the **"Most Wanted Phantom Thief Contract: When Moon Reaches Stars"** banner, which runs through **June 25, 2026**.

## RuneSmith: Another Indie Quest 2026 Reveal

A game not highlighted in yesterday's Indie Quest roundup: **Terahard Studios** (13 years old, Athens-based) premiered **RuneSmith**, a dwarven **turn-based tactical RPG** for PC. Players are a dwarf blacksmith hunting an all-powerful summoner — not to save the world, but because the villain **still owes them money**.

The core hook is a **rune-smithing system** where players forge weapons, armor, and trinkets by combining magical runes. Rune effects chain together during combat, producing powerful combos or, just as often, catastrophic accidents. Outside battle, players recruit a crew of dubious adventurers, upgrade their forge, brawl in taverns, and explore dungeons. A release date has not yet been announced. The Steam page is live.
