## Final Fantasy XIV — Switch 2 Version Now Live

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy XIV** officially arrived on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**, following the Berlin Fan Festival announcement covered last month. The launch was smooth, with Square Enix's subscription waiver covering approximately one month of free play for new Switch 2 registrations.

The Switch 2 version supports full **cross-play** with all other platforms — PC, PlayStation, and Xbox — meaning existing characters transfer seamlessly and players can party with friends regardless of platform. **Mouse-and-keyboard input** works via the USB-C port, and both portable and docked modes support the complete suite of game features.

Four purchase tiers are available, ranging from **$19.99 to $97.99** depending on which expansions are included. Players who already subscribe on another platform receive a **50% discount** on the Switch 2 subscription fee. Square Enix also briefly offered a **30% sale** on Complete Editions through August 13 to mark the launch week.

The **Evercold** expansion, revealed at the Berlin festival, remains on track for **January 2027** and will be the first expansion Switch 2 players can purchase after the platform launch.

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Launches on Switch 2 and PC

**MAGES. Inc.** released **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** on **August 20, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. The visual novel is a full remake of the 2009 science-fiction thriller, rebuilt from the ground up with redrawn character art by original designer **huke**, re-recorded voice lines, and dynamic **E-mote character animations** replacing the static sprites of the original.

The remake adds several features not present in the source material:

- **New story routes** including an additional ending not found in the 2009 release
- A redesigned "**easier to read**" narrative flow intended to help new players follow the time-travel thriller's dense plot
- A **10% launch discount** on Steam during the first two weeks, applied to both the Standard and Digital Deluxe editions

Crucially for Western fans, the **Steam version launched with English text on day one**, ending the historical gap between Japanese and localized releases that defined the original Visual Novel's rollout. The game is canon to the **Science Adventure** series that includes Chaos;Head, Chaos;Child, and Robotics;Notes.

## Brigandine: Abyss Launches — First New Entry in Six Years

**Matrix Corporation** and **Happinet Games** released **BRIGANDINE ABYSS** on **August 26 (PC via Steam)** and **August 27 (Nintendo Switch 2)**. It is the fourth game in the long-running *Brigandine* series and the first since *Brigandine: The Legend of Runersia* in 2020.

The game is set in **Meltitea**, a brand-new continent where the **Abyssloa Empire** — stopped centuries ago by the power of the arcane Brigandine — rises again to conquer the world. The structure follows the series tradition of campaign-based grand-strategy: players choose from **six different story campaigns**, each telling the conflict from a distinct faction's perspective as they struggle to resist or serve the empire's ambitions.

A **Missions Mode** rounds out the content, letting players command one of **24 factions**, each with unique win conditions separate from the main campaigns.

Early reviews have been positive, with *Cat with Monocle* calling it a worthy successor that builds on *Runersia*'s foundation while delivering a fresher setting and tighter mission structure.

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — "The Planet's Crisis" Gameplay Trailer Debuts at Gamescom

**Square Enix** used **Gamescom Opening Night Live** (August 25) to premiere the most substantial gameplay footage yet for **Final Fantasy VII Revelation**, the concluding chapter of the *Final Fantasy VII* remake trilogy.

The trailer — titled **"The Planet's Crisis"** — reveals a scope significantly broader than either *Remake* or *Rebirth*:

- **Vincent Valentine** and **Cid Highwind** are confirmed as **fully playable characters**, completing the original game's main party roster in the remake format
- Combat now spans three distinct modes: **ground battles**, **aerial dogfights**, and **underwater combat**
- New traversal tools include a **grappling hook** and **parachute** for open-world exploration
- **Chocobo mounts** return, with new movement options tied to the open world
- **Summon Typhon** is revealed as a new addition to the game's summon roster
- The **FITS system** (shown previously with job-based outfits for Cloud) appears again, this time demonstrating how each party member gets their own FITS loadout with class-appropriate abilities

The trailer's narrative climax shows **Sephiroth summoning Meteor** and the **Weapons** appearing across the Planet — framed as trying to protect the world but causing mass destruction indiscriminately.

*Final Fantasy VII Revelation* targets **Spring 2027** for **PC, Switch 2, and other platforms**.

## Persona 4 Revival — Story Trailer Shown at Gamescom

**Atlus** debuted a new story trailer for **Persona 4 Revival** during Gamescom 2026, offering the most comprehensive look yet at how the remake retells the original's central mystery. The roughly two-minute trailer walks through the **Midnight Channel** premise — victims being tossed into the Shadow world — the formation of the **Investigation Team**, and key story beats familiar to fans of the 2008 RPG.

P-STUDIO director **Kazuhisa Wada** also shared new development commentary alongside the trailer, emphasizing that the remake is designed to serve both returning fans and newcomers to the Persona series. Confirmed features include upgraded visuals with modern presentation, full Persona fusion and All-Out Attacks, and quality-of-life improvements across the board — though Wada stressed that the team is committed to preserving the original's tone and structure rather than overhauling its design.

The game remains **PC via Steam** exclusive among platform options accessible without a console subscription, launching **February 18, 2027**. It will be available on **Xbox Game Pass** from day one.

**Capcom** delivered a concentrated promotional campaign for **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** throughout August as the game's **September 4 release date** approaches:

- The **"Severing Fates"** trailer (August 6) introduces a pivotal scene at a Kyoto shrine known for cutting the threads of fate — specifically **Yasui Konpiragu Shrine** — where Musashi discovers a malevolent presence preying on wishmakers. The trailer underscores the game's commitment to staging battles at actual historical Kyoto landmarks.
- A **gameplay trailer** (also August 6) highlights a boss fight against **Rasho-gan**, described by *Game Informer* as one of the game's most treacherous encounters.

*Onimusha: Way of the Sword* is the **first new Onimusha title in twenty years**, bringing **Miyamoto Musashi** to early Edo-period Kyoto. It launches **September 4, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2 and PC via Steam**.

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World — New Characters and Waterwing Can Mechanic Revealed

**Square Enix** and **TOSE** published a substantial new update for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** on August 20–21, 2026, via both a press release and the game's official Japanese website.

The update introduces:

- **The game's antagonist**, whose identity and faction remain partially obscured — tied to the forces of darkness spreading through the formerly lush **Kingdom of Witherwood**
- **New allies** encountered during the protagonists' journey: child versions of **Bianca** and **Nera** from *Dragon Quest V* remain the core duo, but new supporting characters were detailed for the first time
- **Newly revealed regions**, including deep canyons, arid wastelands, and areas of the Witherwood that have already succumbed to the encroaching darkness
- The **Waterwing Can** (Japanese: "Holy Bird's Watering Can") mechanic — a key item that allows players to restore **roots and buds** found across the overworld, which in turn activates fast-travel shortcuts, passive stat buffs, and **field synthesis** points for monster breeding

The game was described as taking place in Witherwood, a kingdom once protected by the **Archeagles** — mythical bird creatures that kept the land fertile — whose power has now been subverted by the darkness.

*Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World* launches **December 3, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

## Neptunia Unlimited Launches in Japan

**Idea Factory** and **Compile Heart**'s **Neptunia Unlimited** — the first new mainline *Hyperdimension Neptunia* entry in over a decade — launched in Japan on **August 27, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch 2, and Nintendo Switch**. A Western release window has not been announced.

The Japan launch was accompanied by a limited edition (¥19,800) including a special art box illustrated by series character designer **Tsunako**, a premium art book, a soundtrack CD, the Season Pass, and a Divine Cross promotional card. The standard edition is priced at ¥8,580.

*Neptunia Unlimited* is the series' first flight-heavy mainline entry, with aerial combat replacing the series' previous ground-based battles. New characters join the returning CPU goddesses, and the title is built around a new storyline independent of the prior numbered games.
