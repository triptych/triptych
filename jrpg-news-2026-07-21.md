## Persona 4 Revival — Yosuke Hanamura and Chie Satonaka Character Trailers Released

**Atlus** has released two new character trailers for **Persona 4 Revival** since the last roundup, spotlighting two key members of the Investigation Team.

The **Yosuke Hanamura trailer** dropped on **July 10** and showcases the protagonist's outspoken best friend and self-proclaimed advisor. Yosuke is voiced by **Paul Castro Jr.** in English and **Showtaro Morikubo** in Japanese. His Persona is **Jiraiya**.

The **Chie Satonaka trailer** followed on **July 20**, introducing the energetic kung-fu enthusiast and self-described protector of justice. Chie is voiced by **Anne Yatco** in English and **Yui Horie** in Japanese. Her Persona is **Tomoe**.

Atlus appears to be releasing one character spotlight per week in the lead-up to the game's **February 18, 2027** launch on PC via Steam and the Microsoft Store.

## Disgaea Mayhem Demo Live — Action RPG Spin-Off Launches July 23

**NIS America** has released a playable demo for **Disgaea Mayhem**, the newest entry in the long-running franchise — now available on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The demo covers three stages and lets players try all **seven weapon types** featured in the full game, each with distinct playstyles and combo potential.

*Disgaea Mayhem* departs sharply from the series' traditional grid-based tactical gameplay in favor of fast-paced real-time action. Players control **N.A.**, a demon mercenary hired to help the sweet-toothed Princess Tichelle satisfy her craving for flan. Combat involves pushing through rooms packed with large enemy mobs, building combo strings, and facing bosses in an arcade-style loop.

The game launches on **July 23** in the West. It has been available in Japan and Asia since January 29, 2026.

## .hack//Z.E.R.O. Team Discusses Self-Publishing Strategy at Anime Expo 2026

**CyberConnect2** Vice President **Taichiro Miyazaki** hosted a panel at **Anime Expo 2026** titled *"The Tale of .hack//Z.E.R.O.: From Licensed IP to Unprecedented Self-Publishing"*, offering the most detailed public look yet at the upcoming action RPG.

The presentation traced CyberConnect2's 30-year development history and laid out why the studio chose to step into self-publishing for this project. **Bandai Namco Entertainment** — which has published every prior *.hack* title — granted permission for CyberConnect2 to independently handle planning, development, and release of *.hack//Z.E.R.O.*, marking the first time the studio has taken full ownership of a major franchise title.

*.hack//Z.E.R.O.* is described as part of a larger cross-media project. The game retains the series' signature duality between a fictional MMO game world and the real world, but is set ten years in the future and is not directly connected to prior entries. Composer **Taro Hakase** is writing the score.

No platforms or release window have been announced.

## Culdcept The First: Saturn Tribute Hits PC July 30, Consoles September 8

**CityConnection** will release **Culdcept The First: Saturn Tribute** on **PC via Steam on July 30**, with a Western console release following on **September 8** across PS5, PS4, Nintendo Switch, and Xbox Series — handled by **Clear River Games**.

This marks the **first English-language release** of the original 1997 SEGA Saturn card-and-board-game RPG, which blends deck-building with a Monopoly-style board traversal system. The tribute version adds modern quality-of-life features including **rewind**, **quick save**, and a **reveal hands** option. Notably, progress from the available demo — including cards obtained — carries over to the full game.

## Tales of Series Producer Confirms New Game in Development, Apologizes for Long Wait

**Bandai Namco** producer **Yusuke Tomizawa** has issued a public statement acknowledging the growing gap since the last mainline *Tales of* release, calling it the franchise's longest stretch without a new entry.

Tomizawa confirmed development of a new *Tales of* game is actively underway, citing the demands of modern console specifications as a factor in the extended timeline. No title, platforms, or release window were shared. The series' last mainline entry, *Tales of Arise*, released in 2021.

The statement arrives alongside *Tales of Eternia Remastered*, announced for an October release, which is handling the series' near-term roadmap through back-catalogue rather than new IP.
