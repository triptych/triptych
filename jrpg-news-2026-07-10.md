## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Launches with Strong Reviews and Series-First Crossplay

**Cygames** and **Arc System Works** released the **Endless Ragnarok** expansion for **Granblue Fantasy: Relink** on July 9 across **PS5, PS4, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC**. The expansion introduces crossplay multiplayer across all supported platforms — a first for the franchise.

Reviews landed the expansion in roughly the **86th percentile on OpenCritic**, with consistent praise for the roguelite endgame mode called **the Conflux**, six new playable characters, an overhauled summon system, and new Master Traits that add meaningful build depth. Critics cautioned that the story presentation is thin — the expansion functions primarily as a high-difficulty content gauntlet — and that the experience is firmly aimed at players who have already cleared the base game's endgame.

## Veritas Tales: Witch of the Dark Castle Launches on Steam — A Vanillaware Veteran's Six-Year Solo Project

**Digitalis Publishing** released **Veritas Tales: Witch of the Dark Castle** on **Steam** on July 9, the debut game from studio founder **Yoshio Nishimura** — a 30-year industry veteran whose previous credits include art work on **13 Sentinels: Aegis Rim**, **Dragon's Crown**, and **Odin Sphere** at Vanillaware, as well as earlier work at Capcom.

Nishimura spent six years developing the game solo in a remote Japanese mountain village. The result is a digital fantasy gamebook blending visual novel presentation with RPG systems: players roll dice, make branching story choices, face enemies, and navigate a handcrafted narrative with genuine replayability. All **300+ illustrations** were drawn by hand — no generative AI was used at any stage — and the game runs approximately **20+ hours**.

The title is inspired by classic Western gamebooks such as *Fighting Fantasy* and *GrailQuest*, which Nishimura has credited as the reason he learned to read as a child.

Sources: [Automaton West](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/capcom-and-vanillaware-veteran-spent-6-years-in-a-remote-mountain-village-developing-veritas-tales-witch-of-the-dark-castle-a-gamebook-inspired-rpg-with-over-300-hand-d/) · [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20875-veritas-tales-witch-dark-castle-review) · [RPG Site Interview](https://www.rpgsite.net/interview/20797-yoshio-nishimura-interview-veritas-tales-witch-of-the-dark-castle-vanillaware-capcom-george-kamitani-gamebook-rpg) · [Shacknews Preview](https://www.shacknews.com/article/149749/veritas-tales-preview-steam-next-fest) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4508570/Veritas_Tales_Witch_of_the_Dark_Castle/)

## Digimon Story: Time Stranger Launches on Switch and Switch 2 with Substantial Free Update

**Bandai Namco**'s **Digimon Story: Time Stranger** — developed by **Media.Vision** — launched today on **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2**, completing its platform rollout following the October 2025 PS5/Xbox/PC release. The game has now sold over **one million copies** since that original launch, the fastest-selling entry in franchise history.

Alongside the new platform versions, **Update 1.2.1** released for all platforms, adding:
- **Playable Terriermon Assistant** — a new partner character joining the party
- **Photo Mode**
- **Graphics Mode toggle** on Switch 2: choose between 4K/30fps (Quality Mode) or 1080p/60fps (Performance Mode)

Switch 2 reviews published today are broadly positive, with particular praise for the handheld performance in Performance Mode and the strength of the game's narrative relative to previous series entries.

## Echoes of Aincrad Launches on PC to Mixed Reviews

**Bandai Namco**'s **Echoes of Aincrad** — a new action RPG set in the *Sword Art Online* universe — launched today on **PC (Steam)**. The game puts players in the role of a custom-created character navigating Aincrad's floating castle, with a **Death Game Mode** that raises the stakes for players who want a more punishing run.

Launch reviews have been mixed. The game sits at roughly **Metacritic 60** from early critics, reflecting two camps: franchise fans calling it the best *Sword Art Online* game in years, and action-RPG players critical of the repetitive quest design and sparse exploration. The combat system, the decision to move away from Kirito as the sole protagonist, and the game's attention to the dread and claustrophobia of the death game premise all drew genuine praise. Hollow enemy variety and MMO-like busywork drew consistent criticism.

Only the first two floors of Aincrad are available at launch.

Sources: [TechTimes Review](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320001/20260709/echoes-aincrad-review-repetition-grinds-down-saos-best-game-design-yet.htm) · [Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/echoes-of-aincrad-1744963/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/07/05/rpgs-coming-this-week-july-5-11-2026/)

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition Details Confirmed for July 30

**Nintendo** has detailed the full feature set for **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Nintendo Switch 2 Edition**, launching **July 30** digitally (physical on October 1).

The Switch 2 Edition upgrades the original game to **4K/60fps in TV mode** and **1080p/60fps in handheld mode**, and adds:
- A new **Rare Blade** and accompanying **Blade Quest**
- New **equipment designs** for Pyra and Mythra
- A new **battle mode** allowing Blades to be played as characters in **Mercenary Squad battles**

An **upgrade pack** is available for owners of the original Switch version. Additionally, **Xenoblade Chronicles 2: Torna — The Golden Country** will receive a **free update** on the same date, bringing 4K/60fps (TV) and 1080p/60fps (handheld) performance to Switch 2 owners.

Sources: [Nintendo Official](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/xenoblade-chronicles-2-nintendo-switch-2-edition-switch-2/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/xenoblade-chronicles-1-2-and-3-nintendo-switch-2-editions-announced/) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/xenoblade-chronicles-series-nintendo-switch-2-editions-announced) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/06/xenoblade-chronicles-1-2-and-3-are-all-getting-nintendo-switch-2-editions)
