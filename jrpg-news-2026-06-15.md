## Steam Next Fest June 2026 Is Now Live — Nearly 5,000 Demos Available

Steam Next Fest June 2026 opened today at 10 AM PT, and it is the largest edition of the event in history: **4,931 free demos** from unreleased games are now live through June 22. That surpasses the previous record of ~3,500 demos set at February 2026's Next Fest by a wide margin.

For JRPG fans specifically, the event features **80+ entries across action RPG, traditional turn-based, and hybrid styles.** For the first 48 hours, Valve runs a broadly randomized display, giving smaller demos equal exposure alongside highly wishlisted titles.

Notable JRPG-adjacent demos available from day one:

- **Edge of Memories** (see story below) — the marquee JRPG demo of the fest
- **Legends of Starkadia** — solo dev Doom Turtle's Paper Mario-style timing JRPG (first chapter free standalone regardless of full game launch)
- More than 80 additional RPG demos discoverable via the Steam Next Fest page

The fest runs through **June 22** and leads directly into the Steam Summer Sale. Demos remain available for the duration of the fest window but typically disappear shortly after it ends.

## Edge of Memories Demo — Now Playable on Steam

The **Edge of Memories** demo went live today alongside Steam Next Fest. It is the most technically ambitious JRPG demo of the fest and worth covering in its own right now that players can actually try it.

**What the two-hour demo includes:**

- The complete **first chapter** of the full game, starting as protagonist **Eline** awakens on the Estfalian Coast and encounters **Ysoris**, a mysterious traveler with unusual prosthetics.
- **Real-time tactical combat** — Edge of Memories is set in the same world as *Edge of Eternity* and blends active positioning with strategic skill use, a step up from the predecessor's combat feel.
- **Mount-based exploration** across the opening region.

**The pedigree is considerable.** The game was developed in collaboration with:
- **Raita Kazama** (character designer, *Xenoblade Chronicles X*)
- **Sawako Natori** (writer, *NieR* series)
- **Yasunori Mitsuda** (composer, *Chrono Trigger* / *Xenogears*)
- **Mariam Abounnasr** (composer, *Xenoblade Chronicles 3* / *ONINAKI*)

The demo chapter will remain available as a free standalone release even after the full game ships. Full game targets a **late 2026 release** on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.

Sources: [Worthplaying — Demo Trailer](https://worthplaying.com/article/2026/5/29/news/150002-edge-of-memories-pc-demo-coming-in-june-as-part-of-steam-next-fest-trailer/) · [AnimeBlurayUK — Release Date](https://animeblurayuk.wordpress.com/2026/06/02/edge-of-memories-demo-set-to-release-on-steam-for-windows-pc-on-15th-june-2026/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20482-edge-of-memories-steam-demo-release-date) · [AllKeyShop](https://www.allkeyshop.com/blog/edge-of-memories-demo-steam-june-news-r/)

## Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales — Famitsu Score and Final Pre-Launch Coverage

**Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales** launches in **three days** (June 18) and review scores are beginning to surface ahead of embargo lifts.

**Famitsu** published its review in the June 13 issue, scoring it **8/8/9/8 (33/40)** — a strong result for a new IP from Team Asano, consistent with the scores the team earned for *Triangle Strategy* and *Octopath Traveler 2*.

**Final preview consensus:**

- Nintendo Life: "The title's classic Zelda-style formula is in safe hands" — describes it as "overflowing with mystery, puzzles, and adventure possibilities."
- Press descriptions consistently reference the feel of **A Link to the Past** with **Octopath Traveler** visuals — time-travel dungeons, action combat, and level progression layered on top.
- This is Team Asano's **first action RPG entry**, a notable genre step for a team known for pure turn-based strategy and HD-2D RPGs.

**Platforms:** PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, PC. **Price:** Previously previewed; full reviews expected at launch on June 18.

Sources: [Nintendo Everything — Famitsu Score](https://nintendoeverything.com/famitsu-review-scores-june-14-2026-first-score-for-the-adventures-of-elliot-the-millennium-tales/) · [Nintendo Life — Final Previews Roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/05/round-up-the-final-previews-are-in-for-the-adventures-of-elliot-the-millennium-tales) · [Mobalytics — Demo Impressions](https://mobalytics.gg/news/guides/the-adventures-of-elliot-demo-impressions) · [RPGFan — Week of June 14](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/14/rpgs-coming-this-week-june-14-20-2026/)

## Tales of Eternia Remastered — October 16, 2026 Release Confirmed

*(Announced at the June 9 Nintendo Direct — covering here if not yet documented.)*

Bandai Namco confirmed **Tales of Eternia Remastered** at the June 9 Nintendo Direct with a global launch set for **October 16, 2026.** It arrives on PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, Switch, and PC via Steam.

*Tales of Eternia* (originally released on PlayStation in 2000, later PSP) has been largely inaccessible on modern platforms, making this remaster a significant return for one of the more beloved entries in the series. The game follows Reid, Farah, and Meredy as two worlds are set on a collision course.

**New features in the remaster:**

- **Graphics Mode / SFX Mode toggles** — switch freely between remastered and original visuals and sound effects at any time
- **Auto-battle high-speed mode**
- **Toggleable random encounters**
- **New unlockable difficulty level**

The October 16 date puts it between *Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave* (September 17) and *Metaphor: ReFantazio Switch 2* (November 12) in a crowded JRPG fall window.

## The Art of Final Fantasy Tactics — Artbook Announced for November 2026

Square Enix announced **The Art of Final Fantasy Tactics**, a 128-page English-language hardcover artbook releasing **November 10, 2026**, following *Final Fantasy Tactics: The Ivalice Chronicles* surpassing one million sales worldwide.

**Contents:**

- Artwork spanning the **original 1997 PS1 release**, the **War of the Lions PSP remake**, and the **Ivalice Chronicles** remaster
- Concept sketches, rough map designs, package art, and **never-before-seen character and job class illustrations**
- Highlights work from **Hiroshi Minagawa** and **Akihiko Yoshida**, the artists whose designs defined the Ivalice aesthetic

**Pricing:** $27.99 physical (hardcover, archival-quality stock, 8¼ × 11⅝ in.) / $14.99 digital. Pre-orders are live on the Square Enix store and major retailers.

Game director **Kazutoyo Maehiro** stated the team is "taking the positive reception as motivation to continue considering future updates to make the game more enjoyable and accessible to a wider audience" — a note that stopped short of confirming new content but left the door open.
