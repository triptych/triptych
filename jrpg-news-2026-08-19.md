## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct — True Protagonist, Time-Travel Structure, and Class Details

Nintendo held a **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct** on **August 4** that ran roughly 20 minutes and fundamentally reframed the game's premise.

The previous four-protagonist framing — Cai, Dietrich, Theodora, and Leda — turns out to be a prologue structure. The Direct revealed that *Fortune's Weave*'s actual story begins **five years in the future**, where the demon god **Balor** has already returned and destroyed the world. The progenitor god **Sothis** has summoned **a nameless hero clad in white** — the game's true main character — and tasked them with traveling to the past to influence the lives of all four champions and forge them into the team capable of standing against Balor in the future. Players control the white-clad hero directly, moving between the four protagonists' timelines and making choices that alter each of their fates.

The Direct also detailed the class system. *Fortune's Weave* features a new **Fortune Board**, a grid of tiles the player fills by completing battles and paralogues, unlocking promotions and hybrid class paths without a strict advancement tree. **Multiplayer Battle Simulations** return in a structured mode that lets players test armies against friends' teams locally or online.

The game launches **September 17, 2026** exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2**.

## Final Fantasy XIV Lands on Switch 2 — Reception Roundup

**Final Fantasy XIV Online** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **August 4** following subscription waivers for approximately the first month to ease the onboarding surge.

The port marks the first time the MMO has ever appeared on a Nintendo platform. Across multiple reviews, the general verdict has been that **the port succeeded** at bringing the full FFXIV experience to handheld without major feature cuts. Nintendo Life called it *"an expansive port that demands your commitment"*, noting some longer load times and a controller layout that required adjustment, while Sportskeeda called it *"a remarkable port to the handheld console."* RPGamer published an extended *Deep Look* piece noting the Switch 2's mouse-and-keyboard support via the USB-C port significantly eases navigation for MMO veterans.

The **Complete Edition** (which includes all expansions through *Dawntrail*) was available at 30% off until August 13.

## Suikoden: STAR LEAP Launches in Japan — First New Series Entry in 14 Years

**Konami** and developer **MYTHRIL** launched **Suikoden: STAR LEAP** for **iOS and Android in Japan on August 7, 2026** — making it the first completely new entry in the *Suikoden* series in 14 years.

Set in **Solar Year 453** (four years before the events of the original *Suikoden*), the game follows an unnamed protagonist from a lakeside village to the east of the Scarlet Moon Empire. After mysterious enemies destroy the village, the hero and three companions — **Hisui, Shirin, and Shapur** — set out to discover what happened and restore what was lost.

The game is free-to-play and developed by a new studio, but it features classic Suikoden elements including the series' signature 108 Stars of Destiny recruitment system. Konami confirmed that a **global release on PC via Steam**, iOS, and Android is planned for a later date, with no announcement on timing yet.

A pre-launch livestream — **Suikoden Live Vol. 7** — aired on August 6 and offered a detailed look at the game's first chapters and systems.

## Expeditions: Samurai Launches in Early Access at THQ Nordic Showcase

The **THQ Nordic Digital Showcase 2026** aired on **August 7**, and the headline launch for RPG fans was **Expeditions: Samurai**, which entered **Steam Early Access** the same day at a **34% launch discount**.

Developed by **Campfire Cabal** — the Copenhagen studio behind *Expeditions: Viking* and *Expeditions: Rome* — *Expeditions: Samurai* is a turn-based RPG set in **Japan in the year 1600**, dropping players into the final chapter of the Sengoku civil war aboard the Dutch frigate *De Albatros* as the newly elected leader of a crew of privateers drawn into the conflict.

The game blends stealth, environmental combat interactions, pirate and Japanese weaponry, and a companion system supporting friendship, rivalry, and romance arcs across eight party members. The Early Access build covers the opening portion of the campaign; the full planned release targets approximately **60 hours of content** including full voice acting and a co-op mode.

Early player impressions have been mixed, with some noting the Early Access content feels thin relative to previous Expeditions launches.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — New Promotional Trailer Released

**GungHo Online Entertainment** and **Nihon Falcom** released a three-minute **promotional trailer** for **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** on **August 15**, focusing on story and combat scenes ahead of the game's **September 17, 2026** worldwide release.

*2nd Chapter* is a full 3D remake of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC*, immediately following the cliffhanger ending of *1st Chapter*. Senior Bracer **Estelle Bright** sets out across the kingdom of Liberl to find her missing adoptive brother **Joshua**, whose disappearance is entangled with the machinations of the secret society **Ouroboros** and its Enforcers.

The trailer highlights several new allies — both returning faces and characters new to this chapter — alongside expanded mini-games and a new layer of combat abilities layered on top of the first chapter's systems. Players who carry a clear save from *1st Chapter* will receive bonus in-game items.

The game releases simultaneously worldwide on **September 17** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**. A **Golden Wings Edition** collector's edition is available for pre-order on the game's official site.

## Entropy Enters Early Access — Dread Delusion Studio's Turn-Based RPG

**Lovely Hellplace** and publisher **DreadXP** launched **Entropy** into **Steam Early Access on August 18** for PC. It is the studio's follow-up to the critically praised low-poly dark fantasy explorer *Dread Delusion*, and marks a significant genre shift: *Entropy* is a party-based **tactical turn-based RPG** rather than an exploration-first experience.

The game is set in a world that has not collapsed dramatically but has simply decayed — scattered settlements surrounded by barren land, creatures, and rulers more focused on control than survival. Players assemble a party of up to **six mercenaries** and must hold back a demonic invasion through tactical battles that emphasize skills, equipment synergies, and positioning.

The Early Access launch includes **Act 1 (Gloaming Isles)**, representing roughly 25% of the planned final content: two towns and three dungeons. Lovely Hellplace plans to keep the game in Early Access for approximately one year, expanding through three acts with each adding new towns, quests, dungeons, and characters.

**RPGFan** published an Early Access review on August 18.

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Launches Tomorrow on Switch, Switch 2, and PC

**MAGES.** and **Spike Chunsoft** launch **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** on **August 20** — tomorrow — for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** worldwide, alongside a simultaneous release in Japan for **PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5**.

*RE:BOOT* is a remake of the landmark 2009 time-travel visual novel, building on the streamlined script introduced in *STEINS;GATE ELITE* and adding newly redrawn sprites, CGs, and backgrounds, re-recorded voice acting, and **E-mote** integration for fluid character expressions. The headline new addition is a **brand-new worldline and ending scenario** — a story never before seen in any existing version of the game. Estimated playtime is approximately **30–50 hours**.

Nintendo Life noted that the game targets *"Switch 1 and 2 this August"*, making it one of the few visual novel remakes to launch simultaneously on both Nintendo platforms. RPGFan has already tagged it among their most anticipated titles of the month.

## Brigandine: Abyss — Full Details Ahead of August 26 PC / August 27 Switch 2 Launch

**Happinet** launches **Brigandine: Abyss** next week — **August 26 on PC via Steam** and **August 27 on Nintendo Switch 2** — with the game set in an entirely new world and designed as an accessible entry point for players who have never touched the series.

The game retains the *Brigandine* formula of large-scale hexagonal-grid strategy built around **two phases**: an Organization phase for resource management, unit recruitment, and monster binding, and an Attack phase for tactical battles that account for skills, elemental affinities, and the monsters bound to each officer. Six story campaigns let players experience the central conflict from different perspectives and factions.

Beyond the story, **Mission Mode** offers a freeform sandbox where players select from **24 distinct factions**, each with unique win conditions, and attempt to conquer the continent. A two-campaign **demo is available on the Switch 2 eShop now**.

A **Deluxe Edition** is available for Switch 2, bundling a mini art book and digital soundtrack with the base game.
