## Metaphor: ReFantazio Lands on Switch 2 at a New Lower Price

**Atlus** launched **Metaphor: ReFantazio** on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026**, completing the game's platform rollout more than a year after its original release on PS5 and PC. The Switch 2 version is priced at **$39.99**, and all other platforms were simultaneously reduced to that same base price — a significant cut from the original $69.99 launch price.

The Switch 2 version offers three performance options: **Performance**, **Balanced**, and **Graphics** modes. A free **Prologue Demo** is available on the Nintendo eShop, with save data carrying forward to the full game. Players who link their Nintendo Account to a SEGA Account receive a **Travel Gift Set** of in-game items at no additional charge.

A **SteelBook Edition** physical release is available at **$49.99**. Progress from Atlus's October demo transfers to the Switch 2 version.

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World — Opening Hours Showcased Ahead of December 3 Launch

**Square Enix** released a new batch of screenshots and details this week for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World**, spotlighting the game's **opening hours** ahead of its **December 3, 2026** launch on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC** (also PS5).

The images detail the early sections of the game's main story, including the initial monster-recruiting quests and the game's tutorial presentation of the **Synthesis system**: two monsters at Level 10 or higher can be fused into a new, more powerful monster that inherits traits from both parents. This batch follows prior screenshot releases covering the online battle system and the **Wrangler's Gauntlet** roguelike mode.

The game's pre-release demo with the **Wrangler's Bundle** in-game bonus remains available.

## Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle — New Mobile RPG Announced for 2027

**Square Enix** revealed a brand-new entry in the Dragon Quest series: **Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle**, a **free-to-play turn-based RPG** for **iOS and Android** scheduled for a global launch in **2027**.

The game is set on the floating isle of **Monbelusha** during its annual **Ringing Feast**, where the player character becomes the **Warden of the World Tree**. Gameplay keeps the classic Dragon Quest party structure and turn-based battles while adding **fast strikes** and follow-up attacks to battles.

A **closed beta** is scheduled to run **December 8–15, 2026**, with 25,000 participants. Applications closed November 23. Beta progress will not carry over to the full release. Minimum device requirements include an **iPhone 11** or a device with a **Snapdragon 855** chipset.

## Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom — Story Trailer Live, Pre-Orders Open, Releases February 25, 2027

**Koei Tecmo** and developer **Gust** released the **Story Trailer** for **Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories** this week, alongside opening **digital pre-orders**. The game launches on **February 25, 2027** for **Nintendo Switch 2** and **PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Xbox Series).

The trailer introduces two new characters not previously revealed: **Noelk Schreiber** and **Celes**, who join amnesiac alchemist Karia on her investigation of **Noctrem**, an underworld beneath the continent of Aladiss. The trailer also confirms **returning characters from Atelier Yumia**.

Pricing and editions:
- **Standard Edition:** $69.99
- **Digital Deluxe Edition:** Includes costume DLC and an exclusive equipment item
- **Premium Box (physical):** Includes the game, an artbook, a soundtrack CD with extra tracks, nine A4 posters, and costume DLC — available through the NIS America online store

**Pre-order bonus** (available until February 24, 2027): a set of limited poses and photo frames for the game's Photo Mode. Players with **Atelier Yumia** save data receive additional in-game bonuses.

Over 40 new screenshots were released alongside the trailer.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Version Ends Service November 25

**Koei Tecmo**'s mobile RPG **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** will shut down its **Japanese service on November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, ending the game's run in its home region.

Premium item and currency purchases were suspended on **August 27, 2026**. Refund applications for unspent premium currency will be accepted between **November 26, 2026 and February 24, 2027**, after which the application itself will no longer be accessible.

The developers cited "difficulty continuing to provide a service that would satisfy users" as the reason for shutdown. The global version of Atelier Resleriana had already ended service in **March 2025** after approximately 14 months of operation.

## Terminus Historia — Former AliceSoft Director's Dark Fairy Tale RPG Hits Steam Early Access

**IMYUIC**, the solo development label of former **AliceSoft** director **IMAYUI**, launched **Terminus Historia (境界戦役)** in **Steam Early Access on November 15, 2026**. A 30% launch discount brought the price to approximately **¥1,309** (regular: ¥1,870).

The game is a dark-fantasy, free-scenario turn-based RPG originally released on DLsite in 2023. The Steam Early Access release adds two additional story routes — bringing the total to three or four depending on source — alongside English and Simplified Chinese localization support, with ongoing balance and UI improvements planned for the Early Access period. Each route takes approximately 7–15 hours to complete.

IMAYUI worked at AliceSoft for a number of years before leaving to develop the game independently over five years. The Steam release marks its widest availability yet.

Sources: [Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/en/indie-games/former-alicesoft-directors-dark-fairy-tale-rpg-terminus-historia-gets-steam-early-access-release) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21770-terminus-historia-launches-for-pc-via-steam-early-access)
