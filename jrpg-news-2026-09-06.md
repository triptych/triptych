## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Launches to Critical Acclaim — 86 on Metacritic, IGN 10/10

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched **September 4, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam**, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, marking the franchise's first new mainline entry in over two decades.

The game has earned strong critical reception, landing an **86 on Metacritic** and **86 on OpenCritic** (rated "Mighty," 95th percentile, with approximately 95% of critics recommending). Notably, **IGN awarded the game a 10/10** — a rare perfect score from the outlet. The Switch 2 version holds the highest platform score at **89/100**, ahead of the PS5 version at 86, PC at 83, and Xbox at 82.

Reviewers widely praised the deep and satisfying sword combat system (centered on the "Issen" counter-slash mechanic), memorable boss encounters, and modernized dark-fantasy presentation. Common criticisms include a roughly **20-hour campaign** that some felt was padded, relatively linear level design, and limited post-game content.

The franchise revival appears to have landed exactly where longtime fans hoped.

## Rev. NOiR — Konami and ILCA's New JRPG Gets Story and Gameplay Trailers, 2027 Date Confirmed

**Konami** and developer **ILCA** debuted two new trailers for **Rev. NOiR** during the **PlayStation State of Play September 2026** event — a story trailer and the first proper gameplay footage — confirming the game is targeting a **2027 release** on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**.

The JRPG is produced by **Nobuki Kadoi** (formerly of Square Enix, worked on Drakengard 2 and Lord of Vermillion). Its premise centers on a world protected by a divine entity called **the Determinant**, whose astral ordinances maintain peace — but a growing catastrophe called **Lightfall** is bringing death to all it touches. Players follow **Asch**, a young man given a celestial ordinance to "save many people," alongside companions **Fine** and her guard **Diana**.

The new gameplay trailer showcases **"strategy action combat shaped by player choice"**: real-time battles with tactical positioning elements, switching between party members mid-combat, and a skill system built around player-defined builds. Konami has described Rev. NOiR as returning the company to the classic JRPG roots of meeting comrades, experiencing growth, and living through heartfelt story moments.

This is the game's second major public appearance; it was first revealed at a State of Play in February 2026.

## Another Eden Begins — Demo Now Live Ahead of September 17 Launch

**Wright Flyer Studios**' time-traveling JRPG **Another Eden Begins** has released a demo on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**, covering the first six chapters of the main story. Save data and character stats from the demo carry over to the full game.

The full release arrives **September 17, 2026** on **Switch 2 and Switch** (digital worldwide; physical edition launches same day in Japan, with a western physical release from **Aksys Games** planned for 2027). The game is directed by **Masato Kato** (Chrono Trigger, Xenogears) with a soundtrack by **Yasunori Mitsuda** (Chrono Cross, Xenogears), giving it a strong pedigree from classic JRPG royalty.

The Nintendo Switch 2 Edition features up to **4K output** and **60 FPS performance**, with enhanced graphics over the base version. Supported languages include English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

## Infinite Alliance — Free Crossover RPG Featuring 17 Indie JRPGs Revealed for Switch and PC

**Infinite Alliance** is a free-to-play crossover RPG pulling heroes and villains from **17 independent JRPGs** into a single original story, confirmed for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation and Xbox).

The featured roster includes characters from **Chained Echoes**, **CrossCode**, **Jack Move**, **8-Bit Adventures 2**, **Beloved Rapture**, **Quartet**, and more. The premise strands characters from across their home worlds on a "dead world," where a party of nine heroes must band together to redeem a fallen hero and stop a dark force.

Battles are **turn-based with a party of nine**, letting players mix and match characters from across the franchises. The game is free, with optional premium purchases on console where **all proceeds go to charity**.

A 2026 release window was confirmed at announcement, though a specific launch date has not yet been set.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — September 17 Launch Details

**Nihon Falcom** and **Nihon Falcom** publisher **NIS America**'s remake of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC** (**Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter**) launches **September 17, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5), as a simultaneous worldwide release.

The game continues directly from the first chapter remake (released September 2025), following senior bracer **Estelle Bright** as she searches for her adopted brother Joshua while confronting the schemes of the secret society **Ouroboros**. New English voice cast additions for this entry include **Cassandra Lee Morris** (Anelace), **Ray Chase** (Kevin Graham), **Patrick Seitz** (Mueller Vander), **Liam O'Brien** (Georg Weissmann), and **Gabriella Certo** (Julia Schwarz), among others.

The remake brings full English and Japanese voice acting, updated visuals, reorchestrated music, and new quality-of-life additions including fishing, cooking, poker, and a new **Viewer Mode** for posing and photographing characters. Save data from the 1st Chapter remake unlocks bonus items and special costumes.
