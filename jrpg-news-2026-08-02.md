## Super Robot Wars Z II: Ruination Remastered Announced — First Global English Release

**Bandai Namco** revealed **Super Robot Wars Z II: Ruination Remastered** at a **35th Anniversary Super Robot Wars** broadcast stream on **August 1, 2026**. This is the first time any entry in the *Super Robot Wars Z2* sub-series has received an official English localization.

The original *Dai-2-ji Super Robot Taisen Z: Hakai-hen* (literally "Destruction Chapter") was released exclusively in Japan for **PlayStation Portable in 2011**, continuing the alternate timeline established in *Super Robot Wars Z* (2008). The remaster will use the updated engine from **Super Robot Wars Y**, carrying over its modernized UI, animations, and quality-of-life features.

Producer **Kota Toma** confirmed that the team will finish work on *SRW Y* first before production on the remaster fully ramps up, but that a **worldwide release including English** is planned. Platforms and a release window were not announced.

*Super Robot Wars Z II* features a crossover roster spanning **Gundam 00, Eureka Seven, Code Geass, Macross Frontier, Gurren Lagann, Votoms**, and others. Its direct sequel, *Hakai-hen's* companion title *Saisei-hen* ("Regeneration Chapter"), has not yet been mentioned for similar treatment.

## Blue Reflection Quartet — Launch Reviews In

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust's** **Blue Reflection Quartet** launched on **July 30, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5) at **$49.99**. The collection bundles:

- **Blue Reflection** (original 2017 PS4/PC release)
- **Blue Reflection: Second Light** (2021)
- The **Blue Reflection Ray** anime series in a recap-slideshow format
- The **Blue Reflection Sun** mobile game

Critical reception is mixed. **RPG Site** praised the value proposition and called it "the most complete way to experience the Blue Reflection saga." **Shacknews** was less enthusiastic, titling its review "More like a Trio and a Half," primarily criticizing the Ray recap implementation as a "weird recap slideshow gimmick" that poorly represents the anime. **Anime News Network** offered a middling game review while noting the strongest material remains the two mainline RPGs. **Steam user reviews** stand at approximately **50% positive** based on early ratings.

Consensus: the two mainline RPGs are well worth playing, particularly *Second Light*, but the collection's framing of the anime and mobile content leaves some reviewers cold.

## Brigandine Abyss — Console Demo Now Live, $99.99 Limited Edition Revealed

**NIS America** released the **Brigandine: Abyss** demo on **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S on July 30, 2026**, matching the Steam demo that had been available since earlier this year. The console demo covers the first two seasons of the **Gran Dragnica** and **Scarlet Will** story campaigns; save data does not carry over to the full release.

The limited edition was also detailed: available to pre-order through the NIS America online store for **Nintendo Switch 2 and PlayStation 5** at **$99.99**, it includes:

- A copy of the game
- Collector's box
- Hardcover art book
- Original CD soundtrack
- Set of acrylic character stands
- Faction symbol pin set
- Cloth map of Meltitea

**Brigandine: Abyss** is the fourth game in Matrix Corporation's grand-strategy RPG series, set on the new continent of **Meltitea** across six playable faction routes totaling **200+ hours** of content. The game releases on **PC via Steam on August 26** and **Switch 2 on August 27**.

## The Duskbloods — Closed Network Test August 21–24, Sign-Ups Closed

**FromSoftware** and **Nintendo** have confirmed that the **Closed Network Test** for their Nintendo Switch 2-exclusive **The Duskbloods** will run from **August 21 to August 24, 2026**. Sign-ups were open from **July 22 to July 28** via the game's official website, and participants will be notified of their selection status on **August 7**.

Taking part requires a **Nintendo Switch 2** and an active **Nintendo Switch Online** membership. The test is designed to evaluate server load, balance, and overall multiplayer stability across the game's **PvPvE sessions for up to eight players**.

*The Duskbloods* casts players as the **Bloodsworn** — humans who have transcended their limits through a special blood transformation — and stages online encounters across gothic environments. FromSoftware has described it as distinct from the *Souls* formula, leaning more heavily into the online-centric design.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Switch 2 Edition Launch Impressions

**Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** launched digitally on **July 30** and early impressions are in. **RPG Site** published a detailed upgrade comparison noting that the jump to **4K/60fps in docked mode and 1080p/60fps handheld** is the most substantial technical leap of any Switch 2 upgrade so far, with Monolith Soft going beyond the typical resolution/framerate bump to re-render cutscenes and improve draw distances.

The **MOMO Rare Blade** — the first canonical crossover between *Xenosaga* and *Xenoblade* — is earning particular attention. Her Blade Quest runs approximately four hours and includes voiced story content acknowledging her origins in the Xenosaga universe, though handled in a way that doesn't require familiarity with that series.

The **Merc Assault** expansion (now supporting six Blades per mission) has been highlighted as a welcome quality-of-life improvement for completionists. Owners of the original Switch version who purchased the **Torna – The Golden Country** expansion can buy a separate upgrade pack to bring Torna up to 4K/60fps as well.

## Luminary — Co-op Fantasy ARPG-Lite Launches Steam Early Access August 11

**Refractive Entertainment**, a two-developer studio, will launch **Luminary** into **Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026** at **$19.99**. The game has built a following from its demo — currently rated **90% Very Positive** on Steam — and has crossed **50,000 wishlists** ahead of launch.

Described as an "ARPG-lite," *Luminary* is built around seamless **drop-in/drop-out online co-op for up to three players** with fully shared progression. The Early Access build includes a **2 km² hand-crafted open map**, six fantasy races, climbing and gliding traversal, resource gathering (mining and fishing), and a town-building system. An endgame loop with tiered difficulty content is also present from launch.

The full 1.0 release is targeting approximately one year after Early Access begins. Reviewers have drawn comparisons to *Fable* and *Breath of the Wild* in terms of tone — a relaxed, exploratory fantasy world rather than a punishing one.
