## Star Ocean: The Second Story R Drops on Nintendo Switch 2 Without Warning

**Square Enix** released **Star Ocean: The Second Story R** for **Nintendo Switch 2** on **July 16, 2026** with no prior announcement — the game simply appeared on the eShop alongside the week's scheduled releases.

The Switch 2 version is a technical step up from the original Switch release: it runs at **1080p resolution in both docked and handheld mode** at **60 frames per second**, compared to the significantly lower resolution and 30 fps of the Switch 1 version. Additional improvements include higher-resolution textures, more environmental objects, and improved shadow quality.

The game is priced at **$49.99**. Square Enix confirmed there is **no upgrade path** from the Switch 1 version, and **save data does not transfer** between the two versions. The Switch 2 edition is a clean standalone purchase.

*Star Ocean: The Second Story R* is the 2.5D remake of the 1998 RPG, originally released on the current generation in November 2023. It was previously available on Switch 1, PS5, PS4, and PC via Steam.

## Digimon UP Launches on Mobile — 1 Million Pre-Registrations at Launch

**Bandai Namco** released **Digimon UP** on **iOS and Android on July 15, 2026**. The free-to-play monster-raising RPG had surpassed one million pre-registrations by launch day, and all players receive **Taichi Yagami & Agumon** as a launch bonus.

The game is built around the concept of "a Digimon journey that moves with your life" — players feed and train their Digimon partner through idle mechanics, then push toward Digivolution all the way to Mega Digivolution. Core systems include Digimon Cards, Digivices, and pixel-art-style battle visuals drawn from classic series iconography.

*Digimon UP* is separate from the *Digimon Story* console RPG line (*Cyber Sleuth*, *Time Stranger*) and targets a more casual mobile audience.

## Disgaea Mayhem Demo Now Live on Switch and Switch 2 Ahead of July 23 Launch

**NIS America** has released a **free demo for Disgaea Mayhem** on the **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 eShops**, giving players a preview ahead of the game's **July 23 launch** across PS5, Switch 2, Switch, and Steam.

*Disgaea Mayhem* is an action RPG spinoff of the long-running strategy series. Players control **N.A.**, a veteran mercenary hired by Overlord Tichelle to recover a stolen pudding (as tradition demands). Seven weapon classes — fists, swords, spears, axes, bows, guns, and staffs — each play differently against crowds of enemies. Classic Disgaea systems return: **Item World** is back for powering up equipment, and **Reincarnation** allows players to reset and rebuild for higher base stats. The game also includes Magichange, Debate Assembly, and Chara World.

The Switch 2 version is sold physically as a **game-key card** at retail. Pricing is $49.99 across all platforms.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Switch 2 Edition Details Confirmed for July 30

**Nintendo** has outlined what's new in the **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Nintendo Switch 2 Edition**, arriving digitally on **July 30, 2026** ($49.99). A physical version follows on **October 1, 2026**.

Technical upgrades bring the game to **4K resolution at 60 fps in TV mode** and **1080p at 60 fps in handheld mode**, with sharper cutscenes throughout. A separate upgrade pack is available for players who already own the original Switch version.

New content includes:
- **Mercenary Battles mode** — a new challenge mode where Blades themselves become playable fighters, squaring off against special enemies outside the main story
- A new **Rare Blade and associated Blade Quest**
- New **equipment designs for Pyra and Mythra**
- **Amiibo support**

**Xenoblade Chronicles 2: Torna – The Golden Country** also receives a **free update on July 30** adding 4K/60fps in TV mode and 1080p/60fps in handheld mode on Switch 2 hardware — no separate purchase required.

## Final Fantasy XIV Patch 7.55 Previewed — Arrives July 28

**Square Enix** has previewed **Final Fantasy XIV Patch 7.55**, the next sub-patch in the "Trail to the Heavens" update cycle, launching on **July 28, 2026**.

Key content:
- **North Horn** — a new large zone expanding the **Occult Crescent** adventuring foray
- **Forked Tower: Magic** — a new large-scale raid instance
- **Eight new Phantom Jobs** added to the Phantom Job system
- **Knowledge Level cap raised to 40**
- **Phantom Weapons final upgrade stage** — the capstone of the enhancement chain

## Zenless Zone Zero Version 3.1 "The Long Goodbye" — July 29, Second Anniversary Rewards

**HoYoverse** has detailed **Zenless Zone Zero Version 3.1 "The Long Goodbye"**, launching **July 29, 2026**, alongside the game's **second anniversary** celebration.

New agents in 3.1:
- **Remielle Dan** — the game's first **Lumiflux attribute Agent**, S-Rank Anomaly. Her Attribute Flux mechanism syncs her damage output's attribute with the next Agent in the squad, letting players swap team compositions to exploit enemy weaknesses. Her banner runs the full version.
- **Sigrid** — S-Rank Ice Attack Agent, debuting in the second half of Version 3.1.

For the anniversary, all players receive a **free limited S-Rank Agent of their choice**, a matching **exclusive W-Engine**, **1,600 Polychromes**, and **20 Signal Searches**.

A new permanent mode, **"Enigma of the Labyrinth: Operation Bagel"**, launches alongside the update. The **Xbox PC version** of Zenless Zone Zero also officially launches with Version 3.1.
