## Xenoblade Chronicles 3 — Switch 2 Edition Overview Trailer Reveals Heroes' Vault and New Hero Shimmer

Nintendo and **Monolith Soft** released an overview trailer for **Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** ahead of its **December 3 digital and physical launch**.

The trailer highlights new content exclusive to the Switch 2 edition:

- **Heroes' Vault** is a new game mode that unlocks after clearing the main story, letting players replay any Hero's recruitment quest in an isolated challenge mode and earn cosmetic rewards for completing bonus objectives.
- **Shimmer** joins as a new Hero, bringing rapid-fire combo-based attacks and a unique class ability called **Foresight** that briefly extends team-based Chain Attack windows.
- Technical upgrades include **4K docked / 1080p handheld** at **60 FPS**, with improved draw distances and enhanced lighting in cutscenes.

Owners of the original Switch version can purchase an **upgrade pack** for $9.99 (or regional equivalent). The digital launch is **December 3**; a physical edition releases simultaneously, unlike the staggered approach used for the Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition.

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World — Final Pre-Launch Trailer Released

**Square Enix** released a final pre-launch trailer for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** as the game approaches its **December 3 global launch** on Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam (Steam launches December 4).

The game's major features include:

- A sprawling overworld split across multiple withered kingdoms, each with its own biome and roster of native monsters to recruit.
- **Wrangler's Gauntlet**, a roguelite endgame mode in which players build a squad of monsters under random restrictions and push through escalating challenge floors.
- Over 500 recruitable monsters with full synthesis chains; the synthesis system has been expanded relative to prior entries, with new hybrid trait inheritance mechanics.
- **Thespidor**, the game's signature new original monster, features prominently in the trailer's story beats.

The two young monster-wrangling protagonists are confirmed as the narrative focus, with the story set across a series of interconnected withered kingdoms that players must restore.

## Mercenaries Grace Launches in Japan on PS5 — Switch 2 and PC Dates Confirmed

**RideonJapan**'s strategy RPG **Mercenaries Grace: Black Feather and the Golden Right Arm** launched in Japan for PlayStation 5 on **November 25**. The Nintendo Switch version follows in **December 2026**, and a PC via Steam release is planned for **early 2027**.

The game is the eighth entry in the long-running Mercenaries Grace series, and its defining gameplay change is a chess/shogi-inspired turn order: **only one unit per side may act per turn**, creating a tighter tactical flow than the series' prior systems. Players take the role of a veteran mercenary company employed by a young overlord navigating political and military conflict.

RideonJapan confirmed to Gematsu that an **English localization is in preparation**, though no western release date has been announced. The Japan PS5 launch price is **3,850 yen**.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Server Shutdown Complete — Refund Window Now Open

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** closed the Japan server for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25 at 16:00 JST**, ending the game's run after the developers determined they could no longer "provide a service that would satisfy users." Paid currency sales and premium gacha had already ceased on **August 27, 2026**, with a final anniversary event running in the weeks since.

The **refund window for unused paid currency opens today (November 26)** and remains open through **February 24, 2027**, when the app also stops being available for download. Players who have unused premium currency must apply for refunds before that deadline.

This applies to the Japan service only. The global (English-language) version had already ended on **March 28, 2025**, making this the final closure of the game in any region.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/atelier-resleriana-to-end-service-in-japan/) · [Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/jrpg/one-of-the-oldest-ongoing-jrpg-series-adds-its-divisive-gacha-spinoff-to-the-live-service-graveyard-after-barely-a-year/)

## Persona 4 Revival — Atlus Clarifies Aika Nakamura Is a "Location Episode Character," Not a Social Link

**Atlus** has issued a correction regarding **Persona 4 Revival** character **Aika Nakamura**, the fan-favorite food delivery girl from *Persona 4: The Animation*. When Atlus first announced her inclusion in a September 2026 broadcast, the presentation described her as a new **Social Link**, prompting speculation about entirely new Arcana routes and fusion chains.

Atlus clarified this week that Aika is instead a **"Location Episode Character"** — a new category for *Revival* that offers self-contained, multi-part character storylines unlocked by visiting specific locations at certain times, rather than a traditional numbered Social Link tied to an Arcana and fusion benefit. Atlus apologized for the confusion in the original broadcast materials.

The distinction matters for endgame planning: Location Episode Characters will not appear in fusion recommendations or max-link checklists, but their storylines do yield gameplay rewards and are part of the game's expanded content exclusive to the remake.

**Persona 4 Revival** launches **February 18, 2027** on PC and Xbox Series (day one Game Pass).

## Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle — Closed Beta Wraps Up

The closed beta for **Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle** concluded on **November 23**, with **25,000 testers** selected by lottery from applicants. Beta data does not carry over to the final release, and the game is targeting a **2027 global launch** on iOS and Android.

The title was announced on **November 10, 2026**. It is a classic turn-based Dragon Quest game developed by **WFS** for mobile, set on the floating isle of **Monbelusha** during the annual **Ringing Feast** celebration. Gameplay features an isometric overworld, turn-based party combat, and an upgradable home hub with buildable shops and facilities. Music is composed by **Koichi Sugiyama**. A simultaneous global release is planned, excluding certain regions.

The title is designed as a standalone experience rather than a gacha game, though monetization specifics for the final release have not been detailed yet.

## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United — Traveling Companions and Battle Systems Detailed

**Square Enix** released an extended batch of details for **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United**, the full 3D remake of the 1995 Super Famicom RPG, covering traveling companions, the formation system, the **Zwei Tournament**, and equipment crafting.

Key details:

- **Five traveling companions** introduced: **The Minstrel, Tatyana, Nora, Muse**, and **Charl**. Each has a distinct recruitment path and personal quest tied to the game's open-ended protagonist structure.
- The **Formation System** returns, letting players arrange party members whose position in formation affects targeting probability and grants passive buffs.
- **Zwei Tournament** is a new side-mode offering optional combat challenges outside the main story.
- **Renown** accumulates as players complete quests and affects how NPCs and faction leaders respond, feeding into the game's multi-route narrative branches.
- A **forging system** lets players upgrade and customize equipment using materials gathered from exploration.

The game also features full Japanese and English voice acting and new musical arrangements alongside the original score. Developer is listed as **Xeen** (the studio behind the *Trials of Mana* remake); **Square Enix** publishes.

**Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** launches **February 16, 2027** on Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam.
