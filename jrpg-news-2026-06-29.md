## Persona 4 Revival — June 28 Broadcast Deep-Dives Story, Social System Overhaul, and Remixed Soundtrack

Atlus released a **22-minute Persona 4 Revival broadcast** on **June 28, 2026**, covering story, characters, battle, and systems in the most comprehensive look at the game yet.

**Social System — Human Parameters:**
- A new **"Human Parameters"** system expands on traditional social stat tracking, directly influencing which interactions, jobs, and story events become available.
- Every daily activity — school life, after-school events, studying, and part-time jobs — feeds into character growth in a more granular way than the original's Knowledge/Courage/Expression/Diligence/Understanding stats.
- All Social Link interactions across both main and side content have been **fully voiced** under the newly recorded English cast.

**Protagonist English VA:**
- The protagonist is voiced by **Nazeeh Tarsha**, known for his roles in *Zenless Zone Zero*.

**Music:**
- All vocal and battle tracks have been **newly rearranged into full-length versions**.
- Persona 4 Revival also features **brand-new compositions** including the opening theme and new battle music — not simply remixes of the original, but original pieces created for the remake.

**Story:**
- The broadcast confirmed the story centers on the **Inaba murder mystery** and the **Midnight Channel** from the original game, faithfully recreated with the new engine and cast.
- The updated battle system (Guard, Send Flying, Baton Pass, Prime Time) works alongside the expanded social content to make Revival a meaningfully different experience from both the 2008 original and the 2012 Golden re-release.

Persona 4 Revival launches **February 18, 2027** on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam/Microsoft Store).

## Tales of Eternia Remastered — New Gameplay Detail Articles Cover High-Speed Mode and Full Technical Specs

Following the June 9 announcement, Bandai Namco and NoisyPixel published follow-up coverage this week detailing systems not highlighted in the original reveal.

**High-Speed Mode:**
- A **high-speed movement mode** can be toggled on the field for faster traversal.
- In battle, **high-speed mode** activates automatically during Auto mode, dramatically cutting the length of longer encounters.

**New English Dub and QoL Details:**
- The game features a **completely new English voice cast** — the original western PS1 release (2001) had no English dub; the PS Vita version *Mugen no Frontier × Rebirth* (2012) had partial voicing limited to battle grunts and some skits. This is the first time Eternia has full English dialogue.
- Graphics Mode and SFX Mode allow free switching between **remastered and original visuals/sound effects** at any time.
- **Boost feature**: adjusts experience gain multiplier and encounter frequency.
- **Toggleable destination markers** for players who want guidance without hand-holding.
- **Battle Retry and Quick Recovery** available on death.
- Difficulty options: **Extreme** (accessible from the start) and a new **Unknown** difficulty unlocked after completing the game.

**Technical Specs:**
- **60fps** on all platforms.
- Up to **4K resolution** on Nintendo Switch 2.

Tales of Eternia Remastered launches **October 16, 2026** on PS4, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam.

## Arc System Works Teases New Unannounced Anime-Style Title for Anime Expo 2026 Panel

**Arc System Works** announced it will reveal a **new, previously unannounced anime-style title** at its developer panel at **Anime Expo 2026** (Day 3 — **July 4, 10:00–10:50 a.m. PDT**).

Panel attendees include:
- **Ken Miyauchi** (producer, *Guilty Gear -Strive-*)
- **Sohei Niikawa** (producer, *Demons' Night Fever*)
- **Satoshi Hirayae** (game director/designer, *Crymachina*, *Sword Art Online: Last Recollection*) — credited as leading the unannounced project

The announcement is notable because Hirayae's background is in JRPGs and action-RPG adjacent games (*Crymachina* was one of FuRyu's better-received single-player RPGs of recent years), suggesting the unannounced title could fall in that space. No other details have been shared.

## Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 — Second Teaser Trailer and Fall 2026 Release Window Confirmed

**CD Projekt Red** and **Studio Trigger** released a **second teaser trailer** for *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2* on **June 29, 2026**, ahead of the anime's dedicated panel at Anime Expo 2026 (July 3, 7:30 p.m. PT at Crypto.com Arena).

Key details:
- **Fall 2026** release window confirmed for Netflix; 10-episode season.
- The trailer introduces **Talia Young**, the fourth confirmed major character — a corporate-tower native who chooses the mercenary life over corporate advancement.
- Director **Kai Ikarashi**, showrunner **Bartosz Sztybor**, and executive producer **Saya Elder** are attending the AX panel, where the first full-length trailer is expected to debut.

*Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2* is a standalone story set in Night City, separate from the protagonists of Season 1. The original series remains one of the most prominent anime tie-ins with a JRPG-adjacent video game (*Cyberpunk 2077*), and both CP Red and Trigger have cited the original's unexpected popularity as the driver for the sequel's greenlight.
