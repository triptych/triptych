## Beast of Reincarnation Launches to Strong Reviews — Game Freak's First Non-Pokémon AAA

**Game Freak** officially entered the non-Pokémon AAA space yesterday with the global launch of **Beast of Reincarnation** (published by **Fictions**) on **August 4, 2026**, for **PC via Steam**, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

The game follows **Emma the Sealer** and her dog companion **Koo** in a post-apocalyptic Japan set in the year **4026**, where the pair must battle corruption threatening to consume the land. The combat system has received widespread praise for its inventive **dual-pace parry mechanic** that links Emma's slower human strikes to Koo's faster, canine-timed maneuvers — creating a push-and-pull rhythm critics describe as unlike anything currently on the market.

Review aggregators currently place the game at **76 on OpenCritic** across 45 critics (rated "Strong") and **73 on Metacritic** across 49 PlayStation 5 reviews. Outlets including **GamesRadar+** and **But Why Tho?** awarded high marks for the game's inventive premise, confident mood, and directorial vision from **Kota Furushima** (a Pokémon battle system veteran). Recurring criticisms point to story pacing issues, thin enemy variety in later hours, and notable PC performance instability at launch.

The game is directed by **Kota Furushima**, who previously worked on battle system design and sound across multiple Pokémon titles.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct Airs — New Story and Gameplay Details Ahead of September 17

**Nintendo** held a dedicated **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Nintendo Direct** on **August 4**, streaming approximately **20 minutes** of new story and gameplay footage ahead of the game's **September 17 Nintendo Switch 2** launch.

The Direct expanded on the four-protagonist structure announced earlier this year. The story is set in the same world as *Fire Emblem: Three Houses* and centers on the **Heroic Games** — a combat tournament where the winner has their wish granted by the world's ruler. The four protagonists — **Cai, Theodora, Leda**, and **Dietrich** — each enter with a deeply personal motivation. Similar to *Three Houses*' route system, players follow one protagonist's narrative path, with each route offering a distinct story perspective on the same tournament events.

The Direct also revealed new details on the **Engage-adjacent mechanics** available across routes, confirmation that **classic permadeath and casual modes** both return, and a closer look at the continent's faction map.

## Super Robot Wars Y — Anniversary Expansion Pack DLC Launches Today

**Bandai Namco**'s **Super Robot Wars Y** celebrated two milestones simultaneously today with the release of its **Anniversary Expansion Pack** paid DLC and a free **Version 1.4.0 update**, both available on **August 5** for **Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The DLC marks the series' **35th anniversary** and the franchise crossing **20 million copies sold worldwide**. Its contents include:

- **33 new area missions** with original scenarios
- **12 new weapons** and **5 new playable units**
- **1 new assist crew member**
- A **special bonus mission**

The free 1.4.0 update adds a new **"BOOST" game mode**, raises the pilot level cap, introduces a sales milestone commemorative mission and a 35th anniversary mission, and includes stability improvements accessible to all owners at no cost.

## Final Fantasy XIV Switch 2 Edition Goes Live — Subscription Details and Launch Specifics

**Final Fantasy XIV** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2** yesterday (**August 4, 2026**) at **7:00 PM JST / 3:00 AM PDT**. The launch is worldwide and simultaneous, with full cross-play across Switch 2, PC, and PlayStation confirmed at launch.

The Switch 2 version ships in four editions priced from **$19.99 to $97.99** and requires a **117 GB download**. Notably, **existing PC or PlayStation subscribers** who want to also play on Switch 2 must purchase a separate Switch 2 subscription, though **Square Enix is offering a 50% discount** on that secondary subscription. **New players** receive approximately **one free month** of subscription time to help stabilize server onboarding.

The base Switch 2 edition includes *A Realm Reborn* and every expansion through **Dawntrail**. Switch 2-specific features include **mouse-and-keyboard input via the USB-C port**, making it a viable docked platform for players who prefer that control scheme.

## Brigandine: Abyss Dated for August 26–27 — Six-Faction Grand Strategy Returns

**NIS America** and developer **Happinet** have set firm dates for **Brigandine: Abyss**, the sequel to *Brigandine: The Legend of Runersia*. The game launches **August 26 on PC via Steam** and **August 27 on Nintendo Switch 2** (and PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S).

Set in an all-new world, *Abyss* builds on the franchise's signature grand strategy RPG structure with a **three-phase turn system** — Organization (manage territory, troops, resources), Attack (plan operations), and Invasion (hexagonal grid battles). A new **Rally mechanic** allows players to pull monsters from squads to the leader position for passive buffs, while monsters can now move independently from their squad leaders for the first time.

The **Story Mode** features **six playable factions**, each with their own campaign narrative working to stop the Abyssola Empire. The newly added **Mission Mode** expands this to **24 factions**, each with a unique win condition.

## Crimson Moon Confirmed for September 1 — Gothic Action RPG from ProbablyMonsters

**ProbablyMonsters** has confirmed that its gothic action RPG **Crimson Moon** will launch on **September 1, 2026**, for **PC via Steam and Epic Games Store**, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, priced at **$19.99**. A **Deluxe Edition** ($29.99) includes a Ceremonial Armor Set, the Axe of the Gods weapon cosmetic, and access to a future expansion.

Players control a **Nephilim** — a half-human, half-angel warrior and Knight of the Crimson Moon — tasked with reclaiming the fallen city of **Gildenarch** from the **Infernal Legion** district by district. The game supports **optional two-player co-op** and emphasizes replayable missions with rotating objectives and permanent progression. Between sorties, players return to a **Church headquarters** to upgrade, interact with NPCs, and plan future operations from a War Table.

Original metal compositions for boss encounters are being contributed by **HEALTH**, **Jared Dines**, **Misha Mansoor**, and **Ricky Armellino**.

## Expeditions: Samurai Enters Steam Early Access August 7 — Ahead of THQ Nordic Showcase

**Campfire Cabal** and **THQ Nordic**'s party-based RPG **Expeditions: Samurai** enters **Steam Early Access on August 7** — the same day as the THQ Nordic Digital Showcase where further details are expected. The full release is currently targeting **Q1 2027**.

Set in Japan around **1600**, the game casts players as **Captain Flynn**, an outsider navigator who takes the historical role that English navigator William Adams occupied in reality — becoming one of the first European samurai. The studio describes the tone as a revisionist historical RPG where stealth, diplomacy, and violence are all viable approaches to navigating Sengoku-era politics.

The Early Access version includes **eight companions**, each with personal quest chains, and nearly all of the game's planned major systems. Three additional major content updates are planned before the 1.0 release.
