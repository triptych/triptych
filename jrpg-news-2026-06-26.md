## Dragon Quest X Online Ver. 8.0 "Stray Children of Space and Time" Launches in Japan

**Dragon Quest X Online: Stray Children of Space and Time**, the long-running MMORPG's version 8.0 expansion, launched in Japan yesterday (June 25) on PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, and PC for **¥4,950**. An **All-in-One Package (Versions 1–8)** collecting the entire game and all expansions to date is also available at **¥5,940**.

The new expansion introduces a fresh story chapter, the new **Storm Kaiser vocation**, new weapons and shields, and additional content further detailed at the Dragon Quest X Spring Festival in March. A detailed community summary by Japanese blogger たっちゃん outlines the version 8.0 feature set in depth, noting the story is titled "Lost Children of Time and Space" in the direct Japanese translation.

Dragon Quest X Online remains Japan-exclusive — no Western localization has ever been announced, and this expansion is no exception — but it continues to receive substantial ongoing support from Square Enix as the only mainline numbered Dragon Quest entry in active live-service operation.

## Dragon's Dogma II: Dark Arisen — Capcom Details Story, Relic Expedition Loop, and Free August Update

Capcom held a **Capcom Spotlight** event and followed up with new screenshots and written details for **Dragon's Dogma II: Dark Arisen**, the standalone expansion/enhanced edition arriving **October 9, 2026** ($49.99 new / $29.99 as DLC for base game owners on PS5, Xbox Series, and PC; Switch 2 version is new-platform).

Key story and gameplay details now confirmed:

- **Setting**: The frigid region of **Norgan**, a new area not in the base game.
- **Story**: Players encounter **Eir**, a mysterious pursuer tracking the Fallen Dragon through Norgan. The narrative ties Eir's hunt to the secrets of the Fallen Dragon itself.
- **Relic Expedition Cycle**: The core gameplay loop — explore dungeons to **discover relics**, **appraise them** to unlock powerful new weapons and skills, then use those tools to delve deeper. The loop compounds over multiple runs.
- **12 new dungeons** of escalating challenge.
- **New character customization options**: additional hairstyles and tattoos for the Arisen and Pawn.

Additionally, a **free update arriving in late August** will benefit all players regardless of Dark Arisen ownership: weapon skill slots increase from **four to six**, save slot count increases, and performance improvements roll out across all platforms.

## S.O.L School of Labyrinth — Japanese Indie Team Sushi Lab Opens Steam Page for 50-Floor School Dungeon Crawler

Japanese indie developer **Sushi Lab** has opened a **Steam page** for **S.O.L School of Labyrinth**, a classic-style dungeon crawler RPG set beneath a mysterious old school building. No release window has been announced.

The game's premise: you awaken in an otherworldly place called the **Nameless Auditorium** with no memory of who you are. Guided by a cryptic "Teacher," you descend through a **50-floor dungeon** beneath the school to find a way back to ordinary life.

Gameplay systems confirmed:
- **Four-student party** with roles tied to each character's school affiliation.
- **Turn-based command battles** with classic HP and MP tracking.
- **SAN (Sanity) management** alongside HP/MP — a nod to Lovecraftian horror dungeon crawlers like *Wizardry* and *Demon Gaze*.
- Treasure-hunting with chests distributed across floors.

A notable caveat: Sushi Lab disclosed on the Steam page that **generative AI was used** for both visual and audio assets as well as English translation. This disclosure is likely to draw scrutiny from the dungeon crawler community, where handcrafted art has historically been a selling point.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20792-indie-dungeon-crawler-sol-school-of-labyrinth-coming-to-pc-via-steam) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4736830/SOL_School_Of_Labyrinth/)

## MIRESI: Invisible Future Opens Pre-Registration Ahead of Anime Expo 2026 Playable Demo

**Smilegate** has opened **pre-registration** for **MIRESI: Invisible Future**, a collectible RPG from the South Korean publisher, ahead of the game's **North American playable demo debut at Anime Expo 2026** (July 2–5, Los Angeles Convention Center). This marks the first time Western players will be able to try the game hands-on.

Further details on platforms, release window, and gameplay systems have not yet been revealed by Smilegate beyond the pre-registration opening and AX demo announcement. The collectible RPG space (gacha-adjacent or full-purchase collector mechanics) is increasingly competitive, and Smilegate's presence at AX — alongside the already-confirmed SEGA/Atlus panel — makes the convention a significant destination for JRPG announcements in the next two weeks.
