## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct — Blaze Arts, Class System, and Dagdan Collection Revealed

Nintendo held a dedicated **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct** on **August 4, 2026**, providing a ~20-minute deep dive into new mechanics for the **September 17 Switch 2 exclusive**.

The most notable mechanical additions revealed:

- **Blaze Arts** — special high-damage abilities that cost the using unit's own HP rather than weapon durability. They function as a risk/reward alternative to Combat Arts and are exclusive to the four main protagonists — Cai, Theodora, Dietrich, and Leda.
- **Class System** — three tiers: Beginning, Specialty, and Advanced. Units take **Certification Exams** to access higher-tier classes, with specific stat and weapon-proficiency prerequisites per class. This is described as more structured than Three Houses' free-form certification.
- **Fortuna's Blessing** — a time-rewind mechanic that lets players undo and replay previous turns. It functions similarly to the Divine Pulse from *Three Houses*.
- The Direct also confirmed that the **unnamed soul** sent back in time by goddess Sothis is the true fifth protagonist — a player-avatar insert character who interacts with and influences all four heroes.

The **Dagdan Collection** limited edition — which includes a Steelbook, character art cards, a map of Dagda, and a 200-page artbook — was confirmed to ship alongside the standard edition on **September 17**.

## Final Fantasy XIV Switch 2 Launch — Positive Reception, Minor Server Hiccups

**Final Fantasy XIV** officially launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**, and reviews across the specialty press have landed strongly positive.

Several outlets praised the port's technical execution in handheld mode, with the game running smoothly enough for sustained dungeon sessions and casual daily-content gameplay. Docked performance drew more mixed commentary — most reviews agreed that the resolution targets feel conservative for a modern TV experience given the Switch 2's capabilities. The mouse-and-keyboard support via USB-C, however, was highlighted as a genuine differentiator that makes certain content (crafting menus, chat, Macros) meaningfully more accessible than playing on console.

The launch came with approximately **one free month of subscription time**, as Square Enix promised before release to ease server load during onboarding. Despite some early disconnects — described by reviewers as noticeably better than *Endwalker*'s 2021 launch queues — the Switch 2 servers were generally stable within the first 48 hours.

The **Complete Edition was offered at 30% off** through **August 13** as a launch promotion.

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Launches on PC; Switch/Switch 2 Version Coming October 29

**Spike Chunsoft**'s full remake of the 2009 visual novel classic **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** launched on **PC via Steam on August 20, 2026**, with a **10% launch discount** running through the first two weeks.

The remake is a ground-up rebuild: all character art, backgrounds, and event CGs have been redrawn from scratch; all voice performances have been re-recorded; and composer **Takeshi Abo** has remade the entire soundtrack. The story has also been restructured for pacing, and a **brand-new worldline and ending scenario** not present in the original have been added.

The **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 versions** are confirmed for a separate **October 29, 2026** worldwide release. A Digital Deluxe Edition is also available, including additional soundtrack and art content.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Demo Live on Switch, PC; Progress Carries Over

**GungHo Online Entertainment** and **Nihon Falcom** released a **free playable demo** for **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** on **August 19, 2026**, covering the game's prologue chapter on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**.

The demo is structured as a stand-alone download with **full save-data carryover** to the full game at launch — progress, items, and party levels all transfer. The release also came with a new **opening movie** for the title.

*Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter* is a full 3D remake of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC*, continuing Estelle Bright's journey after Joshua disappears amid Ouroboros's rising influence. The full game launches globally on **September 17, 2026**, with pricing at **$59.99 (Switch/PC) and $64.99 (Switch 2 Edition)**. Progress from this demo carries forward.

## Neptunia Unlimited Launches in Japan August 27 — Western Release Still Unannounced

**Compile Heart**'s **Neptunia Unlimited** — the first new mainline *Hyperdimension Neptunia* entry in over a decade — launches in Japan in **three days, on August 27, 2026**, for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PlayStation 4/5**. The Switch 2 version is priced at **¥8,580**; a limited edition is available for **¥19,800**, including the game, a special box with cover art by character designer **Tsunako**, a premium artbook, a soundtrack CD, the Season Pass, and a Divine Cross promotional card.

The game introduces **aerial movement and large-scale sky battles** as its primary gameplay innovation, alongside the original four CPU Goddesses — Neptune, Noire, Blanc, and Vert — joined by new characters Rouge (Red Heart), Ciel (Blue Heart), and the pair Anna and Lot (Eclipse Heart). The story centers on a world-ending meteor event and Neptune's newly acquired **"Retry" ability** to rewind catastrophic outcomes.

The PlayStation 4 physical version will be **digital-only** in Japan. No Western localization date has been announced; Compile Heart has acknowledged international interest but has not confirmed a publisher.

## Dragon Quest XI S Switch 2 Edition: No Upgrade Path, No Save Transfer Confirmed

Following earlier reports about performance and resolution improvements, **Square Enix** has formally confirmed that the **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition** for **Nintendo Switch 2** (releasing **September 24, 2026** for **$39.99**) will offer **no upgrade option** for existing Switch owners, and that **save data cannot be transferred** between the original Switch version and the Switch 2 edition.

The Switch 2 version does add technical improvements: higher resolution output, smoother performance, and it retains the full Definitive Edition feature set including 2D/3D mode toggling and the orchestral score option. The new story chapters adding party-member-focused scenarios are also included.

Square Enix's eShop listing explicitly states: *"There are no plans to sell an upgrade pack."* This has drawn criticism from fans who own the original Switch version, particularly given the relatively short gap between the two releases.
