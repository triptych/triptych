## Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III] Launches Tomorrow on Switch 2 (Natively)

**Square Enix** releases the **KINGDOM HEARTS Collection [I~III]** tomorrow, **October 8, 2026**, for **Nintendo Switch 2**, **PlayStation 5**, and **Xbox Series X|S**.

The major headline for Switch owners is that these are fully **native ports** — a significant upgrade over the original Switch releases, which were cloud-streaming-only versions. The collection bundles all three compilations:

- **KINGDOM HEARTS -HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX-**
- **KINGDOM HEARTS HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue**
- **KINGDOM HEARTS III + Re Mind (DLC)**

Each volume can also be purchased separately. Players who own the previous digital Switch (Cloud), PS4, or Xbox One editions are eligible for a **50% discount upgrade** to the Switch 2, PS5, or Xbox Series versions.

A **free demo of Kingdom Hearts III** is available now on Switch 2, covering the start of the game through to Olympus; save data from the demo carries into the full release.

## Final Fantasy Resonance — Demo Live, Releases October 22

**Square Enix**'s HD-2D RPG **Final Fantasy Resonance** is two weeks out, launching **October 22, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam** at **$49.99**.

A **playable demo** is live now, letting players experience the full first chapter with save data carrying over to the full release. The game draws from the now-defunct mobile title *Final Fantasy: Brave Exvius* but functions as a standalone story; it was confirmed at TGS 2026 that **Sephiroth** makes an appearance. The TGS demo build included a mid-game dungeon and an arena not available in the public demo.

The game features strategic turn-based combat, voiced characters, Chocobo and airship travel, and the characteristic deep-focus HD-2D visual style.

## Oni Zero: Revival Reboot — Konami Announces Switch Title, Seven Classic ONI Games Also Coming

Heian-era RPG **Oni Zero: Revival** is being rebooted for **Nintendo Switch** by **Mebius** under **Konami** publishing. The original *Oni Zero: Revival* (known in Japan as *ONI零～復活～*) was a PS1 RPG released in Japan in March 2001; the reboot reworks it for modern hardware with a playable demo shown at **Konami's TGS 2026 booth**. Story is written by original creator **Takiya Iijima**; a **spring to early summer 2027** release window is expected. An official English page has been published, though a western localization has not yet been confirmed.

In a related announcement, **D3 Publisher** will port **seven classic ONI RPG titles** from the **Game Boy and Super Famicom eras** to Nintendo Switch on **January 28, 2027 in Japan**.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21221-oni-zero-revival-reboot-will-have-playable-switch-demo-at-konami-booth-in-tgs-2026) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/seven-games-in-the-oni-series-coming-to-switch-on-january-28-2027-in-japan) · [RPG Site (seven titles)](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21355-seven-oni-rpg-entries-from-game-boy-super-famicom-eras-are-coming-to-nintendo-switch-on-january-28-2027) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/oni-game-boy-and-super-famicom-titles-coming-to-switch-in-japan/)

## Arknights: Endfield — Steam Version Confirmed, "Sanctuary of Ink" Update October 15

**Hypergryph** and **GRYPHLINE** have confirmed that **Arknights: Endfield** is coming to **Steam**, with the store page live and a wishlist milestone event active since **August 13, 2026**. Reports from Gematsu indicate a version update called **"Sanctuary of Ink"** is launching on **October 15**, coinciding with the Steam release.

*Arknights: Endfield* is an open-world action RPG set in the *Arknights* universe, currently available on PC via its own client as well as iOS and Android.

## Danganronpa 2×2 — New Release Date January 14, 2027; TGS 2026 Photo Spot

**Spike Chunsoft** and developer **Gemdrops** (the team behind *Star Ocean: The Second Story R*) have given **Danganronpa 2×2** a new release date of **January 14, 2027** (previously delayed from 2026), for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Steam, and Epic Games Store**.

The game was present at **TGS 2026** via a **Monokuma cosplayer photo spot** at the Happinet booth. *2×2* includes two modes: the original scenario with refreshed visuals (**Original Mode**) and a completely new scenario (**Kyoki Mode**) in which the same setting and characters experience different victims, culprits, and tricks.

Sources: [Spike Chunsoft](https://www.spike-chunsoft.com/?p=9074) · [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/comments/18482) · [RPGfan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2025/09/12/danganronpa-2x2-next-year/)
