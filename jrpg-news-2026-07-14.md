## Final Fantasy VII: Ever Crisis Announces End of Service on October 6

**Square Enix** has announced that **Final Fantasy VII: Ever Crisis** will shut down on **October 6, 2026** at 11:00 PM PDT (October 7 in certain regions). The mobile and Steam title has operated for approximately three years since its September 2023 launch.

In-app purchase sales ended on **July 7**; remaining Red Crystals can still be spent until the shutdown date. The team has committed to continuing content through the end — most notably, the long-delayed **Before Crisis: Final Fantasy VII** storyline will finally be told across three releases in July and August, with a concluding chapter on **September 6**. No offline mode has been announced. All original Sephiroth backstory material, seasonal scenes, exclusive costumes, and weapons created exclusively for *Ever Crisis* are expected to disappear permanently once servers close.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/final-fantasy-vii-ever-crisis-to-end-service-on-october-7) · [Square Enix](https://en.ffviiec.com/news/end-of-service/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20865-final-fantasy-vii-ever-crisis-shut-down-date) · [Collider](https://collider.com/final-fantasy-vii-spin-off-ever-crisis-ending-service-october-6-2026/)

## Persona 4 Revival: Yosuke Dialogue Changes Confirmed; New Marie Scene Revealed in Footage

**Persona Team General Producer Kazuhisa Wada** has publicly confirmed that **Persona 4 Revival** modifies some of **Yosuke Hanamura's** lines to fit the present day while leaving the overall story and character arcs intact. Wada indicated the changes are "mostly with Yosuke," pointing to the character's occasional insensitivity toward those he perceives as outsiders — particularly in scenes involving Kanji. "We wanted to lighten that up and make it a little bit more fitting for the world we live in now."

Separately, newly released gameplay footage contains a scene with **Marie** on the night of **April 17** — an event that has no equivalent in *Persona 4 Golden*. The added content has generated speculation that Marie's role will be more meaningfully woven into the main narrative compared to her *Golden* integration.

*Persona 4 Revival* launches **February 18, 2027** for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC.

Sources: [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/persona/persona-4-revival-changes-some-of-yosukes-controversial-scenes-we-wanted-to-lighten-that-up-and-make-it-a-little-bit-more-fitting-for-the-world-we-live-in-now/) · [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/persona-4-revival-changes-to-yosuke/) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/persona-4-revival-yosuke-romance-homophobia-changes-2000713780) · [Yahoo/Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/persona-4-revival-seemingly-features-114831608.html)

## Project Phoenix Breaks Seven Years of Silence — Developer Targets 2031 Completion

**Project Phoenix** — the 2013 Kickstarter campaign that raised over **$1 million** for a JRPG-meets-RTS from director **Hiroaki Yura** — has resurfaced with its first update in seven years. In a private message dispatched to backers this month, Yura issued an extended apology and financial accounting alongside a new production target of **end of 2031**.

Yura attributed the long silence to accumulated project scope and personal miscalculation: "I overestimated my ability to deliver." Rather than exhaust remaining Kickstarter funds and return empty-handed, he built a separate business whose profits have been quietly directed toward *Project Phoenix*'s continued development. He states the total investment in the game now "far exceeds what the Kickstarter ever raised." No gameplay footage accompanied the update.

The project's original 2015–2016 estimate has shifted by more than 15 years. Development continues with an end-of-2031 target.

Sources: [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/jrpg/13-years-after-raising-usd1-million-on-kickstarter-dev-of-infamous-jrpg-breaks-silence-to-say-work-will-continue-through-2031/) · [Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2026/07/i-overestimated-my-ability-to-deliver-infamous-kickstarter-project-phoenix-is-alive-but-it-might-not-be-out-until-2031) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/rpg-that-earned-over-a-million-dollars-on-kickstarter-in-2013-provides-first-update-in-seven-years-aiming-to-finish-production-at-the-end-of-2031-2000713325) · [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/crowd-funded-project-phoenix-which-went-silent-for-seven-years-has-rose-from-the-ashes/)

## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Reviews: 86th Percentile, First-Ever Crossplay

**Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok** launched on **July 9** across PlayStation 4/5, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC. The expansion has landed in the **86th percentile on OpenCritic** based on pre-release reviews.

The expansion adds six new playable characters, a roguelite endgame mode called **The Conflux**, an overhauled summon system, **Master Traits** for deeper build customisation, and **Challenging Chaos** difficulty quests. The most significant technical change is the introduction of **full cross-platform multiplayer** — the first time *Relink* has offered crossplay across all four platforms. The upgrade kit is priced at **$29.99**; a complete bundle for newcomers is **$59.99**.

Critical consensus: essential for players who cleared the base game and want more; a questionable entry point for newcomers given its endgame focus.

Sources: [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20857-granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-review) · [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-dlc-review/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319920/20260708/granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-launches-tomorrow-first-ever-crossplay.htm) · [wccftech](https://wccftech.com/review/granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-review-an-solid-expansion-well-worth-its-price/)

## Echoes of Aincrad Opens to Mixed Reviews — Metacritic 60

**Echoes of Aincrad**, developed by **Game Studio Inc.** and published by **Bandai Namco**, launched on **July 10** for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC, arriving to a **60 out of 100 on Metacritic** from eight outlets.

Reviewers consistently praise the functional action combat, the decision to let players create a custom protagonist rather than play as Kirito, and the high-stakes optional **Death Game Mode**. Criticisms cluster around heavily recycled enemy and boss designs, a slow-moving story, and a quest structure that draws comparisons to an offline MMO grind. Franchise fans report stronger satisfaction than the critical aggregate suggests, calling it the best original *Sword Art Online* game in over a decade.

Sources: [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/echoes-of-aincrad/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/08/echoes-of-aincrad-sword-art-online-review/) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/echoes-of-aincrad-review/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320001/20260709/echoes-aincrad-review-repetition-grinds-down-saos-best-game-design-yet.htm)

## Lunarium Confirms July 29 PC Release — Hand-Drawn Dual-Protagonist ARPG

**Lunarium**, an atmospheric hand-drawn isometric action RPG from **Lunarium Game Studio** and publisher **Imperfect Games**, has set a release date of **July 29** on PC via Steam, priced at **$17.99**. Supported languages include English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

The game follows two protagonists whose paths gradually converge: **Ave**, a swordmaster whose combat rewards precisely timed parries and dodges, and **Lune**, a sorceress who assists in both exploration and battle. Players accumulate **Resonance Points** during fights to coordinate attacks and weaken enemies; passive build customisation uses equippable charms called **Embroideries**.
