# JRPG News — May 20, 2026

## The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales — Preview Blitz and New Demo Live

With **The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales** (Square Enix / Team Asano / Claytechworks) arriving **June 18** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**, hands-on preview coverage dropped simultaneously across major outlets on May 18 — and a **free demo** is now available on all platforms.

The consensus is enthusiastic. RPGFan called it "something more" than the Zelda comparisons suggest, praising the HD-2D art style's lighting and depth effects as uniquely suited to a top-down action RPG. Noisy Pixel flagged the "thrill of discovery" as its standout quality, while The Outerhaven leaned into the *Chrono Trigger*-meets-*Secret of Mana* vibe. A PlayStation Blog feature from Square Enix focused on the game's central hook: Elliot and his companion Faie traversing a world across **four distinct time periods**, with each age reshaping both the environment and its inhabitants.

Key details from the preview wave:
- **Seven weapon types** — swords, chains and sickles, and others — each customizable via magicite
- **Two-player co-op** lets a second player control Faie while the first plays as Elliot
- Non-linear quest design with branching paths that permanently lock out some content based on choices
- Developers confirmed the time-travel mechanic affects puzzle solutions, NPC storylines, and world geography

Nintendo Life rounded up the final previews, calling the response "unusually consistent for a non-sequel" and noting that the demo is substantial enough to give a real feel for both combat and exploration.

## Echo Generation 2 Launches May 27 on Xbox and PC via Game Pass

**Cococucumber**'s sci-fi deckbuilder RPG **Echo Generation 2** hits **Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, and PC** on **May 27** — and will be available **day one on Xbox Game Pass**. A free demo is available now on all digital storefronts.

The sequel follows ordinary dad **Jack** beyond the dimensional borders of Maple Town, pulling in a team of six playable characters — Jack, Bulder, Sister M, Noliva, Strix, and Annata Z — each with their own dedicated deck and playstyle. Combat is built around **over 150 cards** with deck-building between fights; Windows Central called the recently revealed combat synergy trailer "the clearest look yet at how the game rewards building smart rather than just strong."

The story is structured into character-focused chapters that gradually introduce each party member's mechanics, giving the game a more paced tutorial flow than traditional RPGs. The game's Windows Central preview described it as "the long-awaited sequel to one of my favorite indie Xbox RPGs," setting expectations high for longtime fans of the original.

## STARDUST: Wish of Witch Drops Launch Trailer — Releases May 28 on Steam

**Kniv Studio Co., Ltd.** released a launch trailer for **STARDUST: Wish of Witch**, its pixel art tactical SRPG, ahead of a **May 28 release on PC via Steam** (with demo available now).

Players follow tomboyish warrior **Star** and her mage childhood friend **Yu** on a journey centered around stars and fate. The grid-based combat uses **card and combo systems** — a notable departure from traditional SRPG design, layering deck-building flexibility onto positional tactics. The full launch expands significantly on the demo: completed story through the final chapter, increased difficulty, broader enemy and boss variety, and enhanced background art.

The game ships with support for nine languages at launch: English, French, German, Spanish, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.

## Classic NES JRPG Toujinmakyouden: Hercules no Eikou Arrives on Switch 2 and PS5 May 21

**HAMSTER Corporation** is adding **Toujinmakyouden: Hercules no Eikou** to its **Console Archives** series on **May 21**, bringing the 1987 **Data East** NES RPG to **Nintendo Switch 2 and PS5** for **$7.99**.

The game casts players as a hero tasked with defeating the Demon King Hades and rescuing the goddess Venus from the underworld — a classic mythological RPG framework largely unknown outside Japan, as the game was never officially localized. The Console Archives release includes an **English UI and digital manual**, though the in-game dialogue and story text remain in Japanese. For JRPG history enthusiasts, it represents a rare chance to access a piece of late-'80s RPG history through an official digital storefront.

## Final Fantasy VI Hits Game Pass June 2 — Metaphor and Persona 4 Golden Leaving May 31

**Xbox Game Pass** subscribers have an important date to mark: **Metaphor: ReFantazio** and **Persona 4 Golden** will both leave the service on **May 31**, while **Final Fantasy VI Pixel Remaster** arrives on **June 2** — continuing the classic Square Enix run that brought Final Fantasy V to the service earlier this month.

For subscribers who haven't finished (or started) either Atlus title, the window is closing. Metaphor: ReFantazio — one of the most acclaimed JRPGs of 2024 — and Persona 4 Golden have both been on the service for several months. The FFVI addition marks the sixth mainline Final Fantasy to appear on Game Pass overall, and follows Microsoft and Square Enix's recent pattern of rolling out the Pixel Remaster titles in sequence.
