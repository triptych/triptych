# JRPG News — May 1, 2026

## Dragon Quest 40th Anniversary: DQ Day Hype Builds

Dragon Quest Day falls on **May 27** this year — and for 2026, it marks the **40th anniversary** of the franchise (the original launched on Famicom on May 27, 1986). Square Enix has been promising "various announcements" since revealing the official anniversary logo in January, and creator **Yuji Horii** reiterated: "I think we'll have a lot to announce this year." Industry insiders expect the heaviest news to land on DQ Day itself, with the most anticipated reveal being **Dragon Quest XII**, which has been conspicuously absent from any public showing.

Other 40th-anniversary activity in the lead-up reportedly includes:

- A franchise-wide limited-time in-game event called **"40-Year Quest"** across all active Dragon Quest titles
- An anticipated **Dragon Quest VIII 4K HDR remaster** with updated UI and a classic mode option (unconfirmed but widely expected)

---

## Final Fantasy IX: Vivi Picture Book Arrives May 19

Square Enix is releasing **Final Fantasy IX: Vivi and Grandpa's Memories for the Sky**, a picture book serving as a prequel story for the beloved Black Mage Vivi. It launches **May 19** and is priced at **$17.99 USD**. The release is tied to the broader FF9 anniversary and follows recent momentum around the title after years of fan demand for a remake or revisit of the game.

---

## Indie Quest 2026 Showcase Set for May 28

The indie JRPG showcase **Indie Quest** returns for its second year on **May 28 at 5 PM Pacific / 8 PM Eastern**, live on The Gaming Shelf YouTube channel. This year's event promises:

- **35+ new trailers** for upcoming indie JRPGs
- **6 world premiere** game reveals

The teaser has already confirmed appearances from *Alabaster Dawn*, *SacriFire*, *Edge of Memories*, *Spark Ignites*, and *Infinite Alliance*, with several unannounced titles also in the mix. The showcase is organized by Taylor Hoyt (The Gaming Shelf) and co-organized by RetroVibe Games, with other JRPG YouTube personalities presenting segments throughout.

---

## Alabaster Dawn Early Access: One Week Out

*Alabaster Dawn* — the action RPG from Radical Fish Games (*CrossCode*) — launches on **Steam Early Access in six days, on May 7**. The EA build covers approximately the first half of the game's second story chapter with around **10 hours of content**. The full release targets seven main story chapters and roughly 40 hours of playtime after an estimated two or more years in Early Access.
