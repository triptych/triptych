## Chronoscript: The Endless End Reveals Bayonetta's Mari Shimazaki and Mitsuda as Creative Team

**DeskWorks**, the studio formed by Sony veterans, has revealed that **Bayonetta** character designer **Mari Shimazaki** is serving as 3D character designer and modeling director on their hand-drawn action-adventure **Chronoscript: The Endless End**. Celebrated composer **Yasunori Mitsuda** (*Chrono Trigger*, *Xenogears*, *Xenoblade Chronicles*) is leading music and sound production alongside **Procyon Studio** composers Shunsuke Tsuchiya and Mariam Abounnasr, with **Manami Kiyota** contributing as guest composer.

The game places players inside a manuscript where they must explore hand-drawn worlds and a mysterious 3D manor spanning countless eras to uncover a thousand-year story that continues past its own ending. The project targets a **Fall 2026** release on **PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam**.

Sources: [Automaton West](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/bayonetta-designer-chrono-trigger-composer-revealed-as-creative-team-members-for-sony-veterans-hand-drawn-metroidvania-chronoscript-the-endless-end/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/chronoscript-the-endless-end-reveals-character-designer-composers/) · [Worthplaying](https://worthplaying.com/article/2026/7/2/news/150363-chronoscript-the-endless-end-recruits-bayonettas-mari-shimazaki-as-character-designer-and-chrono-triggerxenoblades-yasunori-mitsuda-as-composer/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/06/07/chronoscript-the-endless-end-fall-2026-launch-prologue-trailer/)

## Digimon UP Launches Globally on Mobile Today

**Bandai Namco** has released **Digimon UP** worldwide on **iOS and Android** today, July 15, 2026. ## NTE: Neverness to Everness Launched on Steam July 8 Alongside Version 1.2

**NTE: Neverness to Everness** arrived on **Steam** on **July 8** — earlier than the previously announced July 16 date — alongside the **Version 1.2 update**, which introduced the new **999 Nights** game mode. The supernatural urban open-world RPG, developed by **Hotta Studio** (*Tower of Fantasy*), is free-to-play with full cross-play and cross-progression: players can link an existing universal account to transfer all characters, inventory, and story progress directly from the PC client or mobile versions.

## Mamon King Gets Dedicated Switch 2 Version on October 8

**UUUM** and **LiTMUS** have confirmed a dedicated **Nintendo Switch 2** version of monster-raising RPG **Mamon King**, releasing **October 8, 2026**, in both digital and physical formats. The physical edition includes a clear pouch with four die-cut stickers of the game's key monsters for early purchasers. Note that save data is **not transferable** from the Nintendo Switch version due to a revamped save system in the Switch 2 build.

Inspired by *Monster Rancher*, the game has players raising and battling creatures called Mamon in one-on-one command-based fights, with the goal of winning tournaments and becoming the Mamon King. The game is currently available on PC, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/mamon-king-coming-to-switch-2-on-october-8) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/mamon-king-switch-2-release-set-for-october/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20814-mamon-king-nintendo-switch-2-release-date) · [Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/mamon-king-gets-switch-2-version-in-october/)

## Monster Fantasy Kickstarter Delayed to September

Developer **Jotoyo** has delayed the **Monster Fantasy** demo and Kickstarter campaign from the previously announced **July 15** date to **September 2026**. The studio cited a need for the playtest to more accurately reflect the core hunting and life-simulation mechanics before seeking crowdfunding backing, noting that hands-on experience is critical for a hybrid title of this type. Full development is still targeting **Summer 2027** for a simultaneous PC and console release via Steam.

## Lunarium Sets July 29 PC Release Date

**Lunarium Game Studio** and publisher **Imperfect Games** have confirmed **July 29, 2026** as the release date for hand-drawn isometric action RPG **Lunarium**, coming to **PC via Steam** at **$17.99**. The game follows dual protagonists **Ave** (sword) and **Lune** (magic) as they set out to save their dying homeland. Players can build Resonance Points during battle to weaken opponents and combine the pair's attacks, and customize Ave's fighting style through cloaks, an embroidery system, and a skill tree. Two endings are included. Supported languages: English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/lunarium-launches-july-29) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/lunarium-releasing-this-month/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20915-fantasy-action-rpg-lunarium-launches-for-pc-via-steam-on-july-29) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/lunarium-hand-drawn-action-rpg-steam-release-date/)

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition Details: Mercenary Battles, New Rare Blade, 4K/60fps

**Nintendo** has detailed the new content included in **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Nintendo Switch 2 Edition**, launching digitally on **July 30** (physical October 1). The edition adds a new **Mercenary Battles mode** where players pit their Blade roster against special challenge enemies outside the main story, a new **Rare Blade and Blade Quest**, and new equipment designs for **Pyra and Mythra**. Performance is upgraded to **4K resolution and up to 60fps**, with the same enhancements extending to *Torna ~ The Golden Country* — which receives a **free Switch 2 upgrade** for existing owners. Players who already own the Nintendo Switch version can purchase an **upgrade pack for $9.99**.

A playable **PS5 demo** for **Disgaea Mayhem** (*Kyouran Makaism*) is now available ahead of the game's **July 23** release on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC**. ## Village in the Shade Launches in Japan July 30; Western Limited Edition Up for Pre-Order

**Nippon Ichi Software**'s horror-tinged farming sim **Village in the Shade** (*Honogurashi no Niwa*) releases in Japan and Asia on **July 30, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC (Steam)**. The Western release is confirmed for **Fall 2026** via **NIS America**. Set in the eerie mountain village of Kagatsu, the game centers on cultivating crops and building community bonds under the shadow of three ominous rules: don't talk to strangers, don't leave the village, and don't go out at night.

The **Limited Edition** ($89.99) is available for pre-order from the NIS America store and includes the game, a collector's box, art book, original soundtrack, acrylic block, and metal pins.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/03/village-in-the-shade-coming-west-this-fall) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/19958-village-in-the-shade-switch-2-ps5-pc-steam-english-release-nis-america) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/04/07/village-in-the-shade-fall-2026/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/village-in-the-shade-announced-for-western-release-on-nintendo-switch-2-switch/)
