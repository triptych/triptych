## Metaphor: ReFantazio Launches on Nintendo Switch 2

**Atlus**'s acclaimed 2024 RPG **Metaphor: ReFantazio** arrived on **Nintendo Switch 2 yesterday, November 12**, priced at **$39.99 / £34.99** for the standard edition. The port is a Switch 2-only release — the original Switch does not receive a version.

A limited **SteelBook Edition** is available at launch. The physical release ships on a game-key card. A **free demo** covering the game's opening hours is also available on the Switch 2 eShop, with save data carrying over to the full game.

Switch 2-specific features confirmed at launch: **touch screen and swipe inputs** are supported; **Joy-Con 2 mouse controls** are not. Early reviews from outlets including **Final Weapon** (November 10) describe the port as a solid technical achievement, with performance that varies depending on the area, but generally holding up well visually. The base game previously sold one million units on its original 2024 launch day, making it Atlus's fastest-selling title.

## Prinny Party: Going Overboard! Launches for Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**NIS America** released **Prinny Party: Going Overboard!** on **November 11** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**, priced at **$29.99**. (The Japanese version launched one day later, on November 12, under the title *Minna de Poitto! Prinny Sugoroku*.)

The game is a **4-player party title** that blends command-based RPG elements with sugoroku (board-game) mechanics, featuring characters from the Disgaea franchise. Players fight, outsmart, or cooperate with rivals depending on the board state.

Multiplayer support includes **GameShare (Switch 2)** and **Remote Play Together (Steam)**, so friends without a copy can still join. A **free demo** is live across all platforms.

## Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle Announced for Mobile

**Square Enix** and developer **Wright Flyer Studios (WFS)** unveiled **Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle** on **November 6**, a new entry in the franchise designed as a classic turn-based RPG for **iOS and Android**, launching globally in **2027** (excluding certain regions).

The story is set on the floating isle of **Monbelusha**, where the annual festival is interrupted by the fiend **Zaahl**, who corrupts the world tree Yggdrasil into a corrupted form called **Grimvein**. Standard Dragon Quest party and turn-based battle systems are present, supplemented by new **fast strike** moves. Between missions, players return to a home base with crafting facilities and NPCs.

A **closed beta** runs with applications through **November 23**, with the test itself scheduled for **December 8–15** (25,000 participants selected). Series creator **Yuji Horii** serves as general director; character design is by **Akira Toriyama**.

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World — New Screenshots Detail Quick Scouting and Joint Battles

**Square Enix** and **TOSE** released a new batch of screenshots for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** this week, ahead of its **December 3 launch** on Switch 2, Switch, PS5, and PC. The images showcase two systems not previously detailed:

- **Quick Scouting** and **Scout Run** — streamlined mechanics for recruiting wild monsters during exploration
- **Joint Battles** — encounters where multiple monster parties cooperate, requiring players to coordinate across groups
- A new character, **Almira**, is also introduced, along with several additional monsters new to the series

The game features over 500 monsters, with Bianca and Nera from *Dragon Quest V* as dual protagonists. Four DLC packs are confirmed to launch alongside the base game on December 3. A playable demo is available now.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** are shutting down the Japanese version of **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026** at 16:00 JST, marking a full end to the game's operations in all regions. (The global version had already ended service on March 28, 2025.)

In-game purchases stopped on **August 27**. Refund applications for unused paid currency run from **November 26, 2026 through February 24, 2027**. The developers cited difficulty in continuing to provide service that would satisfy users, without giving a specific reason.

The closure follows the global version's earlier shutdown after approximately 14 months of operation — a brief life for a live-service title in a long-running series.
