## Deltarune Chapter 5 Hits 291K Steam Peak; Toby Fox Confirms Chapter 6 Targeting 2027

One day after **Deltarune Chapter 5** launched, the numbers are in: the chapter drove **291,816 peak concurrent players on Steam** according to SteamDB — a new all-time peak for the game by a wide margin. The overnight reception has been universally enthusiastic, with the Steam cumulative review count now above 104,000 and the game maintaining its **Overwhelmingly Positive** status.

More notably, Toby Fox quietly updated the game's **official Steam FAQ** in the hours following launch to confirm that **Chapter 6 is "aiming for a 2027 release."** The FAQ also reiterates that Chapter 5 is not the end of the story. This is a significant timeline signal given that earlier communication had left Chapter 6 dating entirely open-ended. Fox's pre-launch statement that the team is already moving on to "the next chapter" is now backed by a concrete year target.

Professional outlet reviews are starting to populate. Early scores on aggregators are tracking strongly, consistent with the user reception. Game8's first-impressions review called it "A Field of Pink Trees and Golden Moments" and praised the tone balance between lighthearted character work and rising stakes.

## Citizen Sleeper and Citizen Sleeper 2: Starward Vector Launch on Switch 2 Today

Both **Citizen Sleeper** and **Citizen Sleeper 2: Starward Vector** released their **Nintendo Switch 2 Editions today, June 25**, as promised at the Story-Rich Showcase hosted by Fellow Traveller earlier this month.

The Switch 2 upgrades deliver **4K resolution in TV mode**, **1080p in tabletop and handheld modes**, and **native 60fps** across all modes. Existing Switch owners of either game receive the Switch 2 edition as a **free upgrade** — no repurchase required.

Developer Gareth Damian Martin (under studio name Jump Over The Age) announced the ports at Summer Game Fest. The original *Citizen Sleeper* and its sequel *Starward Vector* are both narrative-driven sci-fi RPGs built around dice mechanics and body-horror survival; *Starward Vector* was one of 2025's most critically acclaimed RPGs. The Switch 2 editions mark the pair's first handheld-native run at high fidelity.

## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow — Arc System Works and UnitePlus Reveal Tactical Counter-Timeline RPG for September 24

Announced yesterday at the **ARC System Works Showcase 2026**, **Qliphah in Providence's Shadow** is a new **"tactical counter-timeline RPG"** from developer **UnitePlus**, published by Arc System Works, coming to **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and PC (Steam/Epic Games Store) on September 24, 2026** for **$24.99 / £19.99 / €24.99 / ¥2,970**. A Japan physical edition is also confirmed at ¥3,520.

The premise: Tokyo has collapsed under an Aberration incursion — grotesque entities that erode reality itself. Delivery worker **Souma Hoshigami** is attacked and left in a "Half-turned" state, partially fused with the monster that attacked him. The setting draws comparisons to urban paranormal RPGs like *Shin Megami Tensei* and *Tokyo Xanadu*.

The defining mechanical hook is time manipulation during combat: players can **slow or pause combat at any moment** to read tactical forecasts, track ability cooldowns, and queue precise counters. The "counter-timeline" framing suggests time reversal or branching timeline mechanics play into the broader progression, though full details were not shown in the announcement trailer.

## Neptunia Unlimited Second Trailer Shows Aerial Combat; August 27 Japan Release Date Confirmed

**Compile Heart** released the second official trailer for **Neptunia Unlimited** (also styled as Hyperdimension Neptunia ∞), the first new mainline Neptunia entry in over a decade, showcasing its full suite of gameplay systems: aerial traversal, vertical combat, **Apocalypse encounters** (large-scale field threats), and combo mechanics.

The trailer confirms the **Japan release date of August 27, 2026** for PS4, PS5, Nintendo Switch, and Switch 2. A Western localization date has not been announced.

The standout departure from the classic Neptunia formula is the shift to **fully 3D environments with free flight**: goddesses can now soar through the sky rather than being confined to ground-level dungeon crawling. The story follows Neptune alone after a catastrophic meteor event, with a time-loop / "retry" mechanic at its core — she must loop between endings to reunite with allies and uncover the truth behind Gamindustri's apocalyptic threat.

## The Necromancer's Tale Comes to Consoles This Week

**The Necromancer's Tale**, the narrative-heavy gothic CRPG from **Psychic Software** that launched on PC via Steam on **July 17, 2025**, arrives on consoles this week (the June 21–27 window). The exact console platforms were not specified in the RPGFan weekly roundup, but the game's prior PC release targeted a broad audience with its text-driven, choice-heavy structure set in a dark fantasy world.

The game was praised at PC launch for its atmospheric writing and branching choices tied to necromantic power progression. The console port brings that experience to players who missed the PC debut.
