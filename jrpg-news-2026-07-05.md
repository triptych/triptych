## Persona 4 Revival: Rise Kujikawa Trailer Released; MAPPA Confirmed for All In-Game Anime Cutscenes

Building on the AX 2026 panel coverage from July 4, **ATLUS** and **SEGA** released two distinct pieces of news that emerged from the Petree Hall event.

The first is a **Rise Kujikawa character trailer**, introducing the idol and first-year Yasogami High student whose stage name is "Risette." Rise is voiced in English by **Abby Trott** and in Japanese by **Rie Kugimiya**. The trailer highlights her outgoing public persona, her grandmother's tofu shop in Inaba as a grounding anchor for the character, and her Persona **Himiko** in combat.

The second and arguably larger reveal: **Studio MAPPA** — known for *Jujutsu Kaisen*, *Chainsaw Man*, and *Vinland Saga* — will produce every in-game anime cutscene in *Persona 4 Revival*. The animation director stated publicly that they played every mainline Persona title before beginning production. MAPPA previewed one completed cutscene at the panel, showing protagonist Yu Narukami confronting a Hablerie shadow. This marks only the second time MAPPA has animated the full cutscene suite for a game, following *Inazuma Eleven: Victory Road* in 2025.

**Persona 4 Revival** launches **February 18, 2027** on **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC**, with day-one Game Pass availability.

## Code Vein II: Mask of Idris Expansion Announced for Summer 2026

**Bandai Namco** has announced an expansion for **Code Vein II** titled **Mask of Idris**, targeting a **Summer 2026** release on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**. A specific date has not been set.

The expansion is post-game content, accessed after players clear the **"Luna Fraterna" ending** of the main campaign and complete the sub-mission **"Valentin's Request."** The narrative transports players to the **Forsaken World**, a setting built from the converged remnants of timelines that were erased by temporal distortions — a new storyline where multiple colliding realities determine the fate of existence itself.

The central new character is **Liv Voda**, described as an alluring, combative guardian of time and space.

A **free Version 2.0 update** releases simultaneously with the expansion, adding post-game exploration features and quality-of-life changes. Mask of Idris is included in the **Deluxe and Ultimate editions** of *Code Vein II* and will also be sold separately.

*Code Vein II* launched on **January 30, 2026**.

## Blue Reflection Quartet Confirms Minimal Legacy DLC at July 30 Launch

**Koei Tecmo** has clarified that **Blue Reflection Quartet** — the four-in-one collection launching **July 30, 2026** on **PS5, Switch 2, Switch, and PC via Steam** — will carry forward only two legacy DLC items from its constituent titles:

- **School Swimsuits** from *Blue Reflection* (2017)
- **Ao's Summer Bikini** from *Blue Reflection: Second Light* (2021)

No other DLC from either prior game is included, and Koei Tecmo stated that new DLC for *Quartet* is not currently planned.

Pre-orders placed before **July 29** include a set of four in-game photo frames for use in *Second Light*'s photo mode. A free limited-time bonus **Quartet Special Photo Frame** for *Second Light* is available through **August 12, 2026**.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20847-blue-reflection-quartet-will-not-include-most-dlc-from-original-second-light) · [Koei Tecmo America](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/games/blue-reflection-quartet/)

## Ova Magica Arrives on Consoles July 21

**Playtonic Friends** and developer **Fabian Rastorfer** confirmed that **Ova Magica** — the blob-farming, monster-taming life-sim RPG — launches on **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, and Nintendo Switch** on **July 21, 2026**, approximately two months after the game's full **PC (Steam)** release on May 28.

*Ova Magica* is set in the rural village of Clover Town, where the player inherits an overgrown farmstead, raises and battles egg-hatching creatures called Blobs, and explores a world built around their lifecycle. Inspirations cited by the developer include *Stardew Valley*, *Slime Rancher*, *Story of Seasons*, *Pokémon*, and *Jade Cocoon*. The game has been in development since 2020.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/05/ova-magica-now-available-for-pc-coming-to-ps5-xbox-series-ps4-xbox-one-and-switch-on-july-21) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/ova-magica-shares-nintendo-switch-release-date-new-trailer/)
