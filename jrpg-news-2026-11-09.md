## Metaphor: ReFantazio Launches on Nintendo Switch 2 This Thursday

**Atlus**'s critically acclaimed RPG **Metaphor: ReFantazio** arrives on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026** — just three days away. The port was announced during the **June 9, 2026 Nintendo Direct**.

The Switch 2 version is priced at **$39.99 / £34.99**, a notable reduction from the original's $59.99 launch price. It includes all previously released DLC and ships with a limited-time **Steelbook Edition** for physical collectors; packaged copies contain a game key card rather than a cartridge. A **free demo** covering the opening hours of the game is currently available on the Nintendo eShop, with save data carrying over to the full release.

Sources have differed on whether the port contains any Switch 2-exclusive features — RPGSite reported no additional content while Turn Based Lovers noted the specifics remain unconfirmed. The original game holds a Metacritic score of **94** and won Best RPG, Best Narrative, and Best Art Direction at The Game Awards.

## AURORA REQUIEM: The Lullaby of Calvaris — Late-90s-Style JRPG Launches November 16 on PC

**AURORA REQUIEM: The Lullaby of Calvaris** is set to launch on **PC via Steam on November 16, 2026**, presenting itself as a loving homage to late-1990s CRTs-era JRPGs.

The game is set in **Calvaris**, a city built inside the skeleton of a colossal sea creature, where lighthouses burn memories instead of oil. It features fixed-camera perspectives over pre-rendered backgrounds, a **MIDI-orchestra soundtrack**, and **nine playable characters** (three in battle at a time). The story follows a mechanic named **Zero Solace**, hired by a rebel crew to plant a bomb in a lighthouse — a premise described by the developers as three-disc in scope.

The Steam listing suggests a time-gauge battle system, keeping it in line with the classic ATB/CTB mechanics that defined the genre during the PSOne era.

Sources: [Steam page (app 5340240)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5340240/)

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ending November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** have confirmed that the Japanese servers for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** will shut down on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, citing difficulty in continuing to provide a service that would satisfy users.

The closure marks the end of the game's run in its home territory. The global version had already shut down on **March 28, 2025**, just fourteen months after launch, following the suspension of in-game purchases in January 2025. With the Japanese server closing, Resleriana will be fully retired across all regions.

Games Radar noted the game's short run with the headline "One of the oldest ongoing JRPG series adds its divisive gacha spinoff to the live service graveyard after barely a year."

## Kitase: Square Enix Likely to Focus on New IP After FFVII Revelation

In a **November 4 interview**, **Final Fantasy VII Remake** producer **Yoshinori Kitase** stated that once **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** ships — completing the trilogy — **Square Enix is more likely to invest in new titles and original games than continue making JRPG remakes**. He called the FF7 Remake project a special case that warranted its scale of commitment, and declined to name any specific follow-up project.

The comments signal a potential shift in direction for the publisher, which has leaned heavily on nostalgia-driven projects in recent years. Revelation is currently in a **Spring 2027** launch window. Square Enix has separately described a broader "three-year reboot" strategy aimed at restoring health to its lineup and committing to simultaneous multiplatform releases.

Sources: [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/final-fantasy/after-final-fantasy-7-revelation-series-lead-says-square-enix-is-more-likely-to-invest-in-new-titles-and-new-games-than-more-jrpg-remakes/)

## Harvestella Creator Announces Everbrink, a 4X Roguelike Deckbuilder for 2027

**Daisuke Taka**, the Square Enix producer behind **Harvestella**, has founded a new independent studio called **Impachi** and announced its debut title: **Everbrink**, a **4X roguelike deckbuilder** targeting **Steam Early Access in 2027**.

The game is set on floating islands made up of hexagonal tiles that **crumble away after every turn**, forcing players to continuously expand to new land. Players use cards to summon units (including elves and Cerberus), place structures, and cast spells — with all 4X elements expressed through the deck. Impachi was established in January 2026, and Taka described Everbrink as a survival strategy experience built around the tension of land that collapses beneath you at each turn.

A demo is described as coming soon; pricing is currently undecided.

Sources: [Automaton Media (English)](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/harvestella-creator-daisuke-takas-new-4x-roguelike-deckbuilder-everbrink-to-get-2027-early-access-release-survival-on-land-that-collapses-beneath-you-at-each-turn/)
