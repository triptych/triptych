## Blue Reflection Quartet — Launch Reviews: Mixed Reception for Four-Game Collection

**Gust** and **Koei Tecmo**'s **Blue Reflection Quartet** launched today on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam** at **$49.99**, bringing all four entries of the series into a single package.

The collection bundles:
- **Blue Reflection** (2017, originally PS4/Vita/PC)
- **Blue Reflection: Ray** (the 2021 anime adaptation, 24 episodes)
- The mobile game that bridges *Ray* to *Second Light*
- **Blue Reflection: Second Light** (2021, PS4/Switch/PC)

Critical reception is mixed. Metacritic scores hover in the **68–70 range** across platforms. Reviewers consistently praise **Hayato Asano's music** and the collection's **emotional, character-driven storytelling**, while criticism centers on stiff animations, sloppy cutscene transitions, and the mobile-era adaptations feeling thin. Game8 called it "a half-baked collection that fails to elevate the series further" but acknowledged fans of the genre would find value in the 100+ hours of content. COGconnected found it "a fantastic way to jump into Gust's underrated JRPG series." Noisy Pixel offered the harshest take, calling it "a hollow compilation that fails the series' legacy."

RPG Site has published a multi-platform technical comparison confirming **Steam Deck Verified** status from day one.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition — Launch Day Performance Impressions

**Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** went live digitally today. After the anticipation built by last week's overview trailer, RPG Site has published detailed launch-day impressions comparing performance to the original Switch release.

The headline finding: **60 fps is targeted but not locked**. Due to the v-sync implementation, the game drops to 30 fps when it cannot sustain 60, then climbs back up — rather than gracefully capping in between. In practice this is reported as less disruptive than the same issue in *Xenoblade Chronicles: Definitive Edition*'s Switch 2 version, and the improvement over the original release's notoriously unstable performance in combat is described as substantial. Docked mode targets **4K**, handheld targets **1080p**, both at 60 fps.

The **Momo** Rare Blade (from *Xenosaga*), the expanded **Merc Assault** mode allowing up to six Blades per mission, and the **new Pyra and Mythra outfits** are all confirmed present and working at launch. Existing Switch owners can access the Switch 2 Edition via a paid upgrade priced at **$9.99**.

Physical edition releases **October 1**.

## Dungeon Antiqua Launches on Switch Today; Sequel Dated for August 20

**AMATA K.K.**'s retro dungeon-crawler **Dungeon Antiqua**, developed by **Shiromofu Factory**, launched today on **Nintendo Switch** (alongside PS5) for **$8.99**. The game originally released on Steam in October 2024 and earned a "Very Positive" overall rating there. It is a first-person dungeon-crawler RPG inspired by 1980s and 1990s classics, featuring hand-crafted pixel art and original chiptune music.

In a surprise announcement made alongside today's launch, AMATA K.K. confirmed that **Dungeon Antiqua 2** — the direct sequel, already available on PC via Steam since January 2026 — is also coming to **Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S on August 20, 2026**, priced at **$10.99**. The sequel features SNES-style pixel art and takes place hundreds of years after the events of the original game.

## Tensei Game: Karma x Cross — Dragon Quest's Yuji Horii and Blue Lock's Muneyuki Kaneshiro Launch August 6 in Japan

An unusual collaboration launches next week in Japan: **Tensei Game: Karma x Cross**, a **Nintendo Switch** exclusive sugoroku-style board game from **Square Enix**, is set for a **Nintendo eShop release on August 6** with no western date yet announced.

The game is advised by **Yuji Horii**, creator of *Dragon Quest*, and conceived by **Muneyuki Kaneshiro**, the manga writer behind *Blue Lock* and *As the Gods Will* — whose work is known for high-stakes survival game scenarios. In keeping with that sensibility, *Tensei Game* casts players as someone who dies in a fatal accident and must compete in a supernatural board game to earn reincarnation. Four players participate but only three can be reincarnated; players spend **22 turns** (split into two halves of 11) collecting "Karma" points to purchase a suitable "Soul Vessel."

The game supports **one to four players**. Horii and Kaneshiro have described the game's inspirations as including Ryunosuke Akutagawa's short story "The Spider's Thread," a classic Japanese parable about karma and selfishness.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/tensei-game-karma-x-cross-launches-august-6-in-japan) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/from-the-creators-of-dragon-quest-and-blue-lock-tensei-game-karma-x-cross-gets-new-details-and-first-trailer/) · [Automaton West](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/dragon-quest-creator-yuji-horii-and-blue-lock-author-muneyuki-kaneshiro-say-their-upcoming-karmic-death-game-style-switch-title-plays-similarly-to-akutagawa/)
