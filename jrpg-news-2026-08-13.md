## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct Reveals True Protagonist, Demon God Balor, Full Combat Systems

Nintendo aired a dedicated **[Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct](https://www.nintendo.com/us/nintendo-direct/8-4-2026/)** on **August 4, 2026**, running approximately 20 minutes and providing the deepest look at the game yet.

The most significant reveal is the game's hidden narrative layer. While the four champions — **Cai** (a boy trying to free his imprisoned father), **Dietrich** (a swordsman who craves stronger opponents), **Theodora** (a queen fulfilling her homeland's dream), and **Leda** (a musician driven by revenge) — each enter the **Heroic Games** arena tournament with personal wishes, the true story unfolds **five years in the future**. The demon god **Balor** has already destroyed the world. A nameless hero clad in white, sent back through time by the progenitor god **Sothis**, is the game's actual central character — and the player must influence all four champions in the past to stand a chance against Balor in the future.

The Direct also fully detailed the combat systems for the first time:
- **Unleash Combat Arts** deal amplified damage at the cost of weapon durability
- **Blaze Arts** trigger special moves that consume the user's own HP
- Weapons span swords, gauntlets, spears, axes, bows, and magic, each with unique behaviors
- Classic Fire Emblem elements return: weapon triangle–style matchups, terrain bonuses, and adjacent-ally positioning advantages

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launches exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2 on September 17, 2026**.

## Expeditions: Samurai Enters Steam Early Access — Sengoku RPG With Co-op

**Campfire Cabal**'s historical party-based RPG **[Expeditions: Samurai](https://expeditionssamurai.thqnordic.com/)** launched into **Early Access on PC via Steam on August 7, 2026**, published by **THQ Nordic** and revealed at the studio's Digital Showcase the same day.

Set in early-1600s Japan during the twilight of the Sengoku period, players control a customizable protagonist (with first-name choice and four class options) who arrives in Japan before William Adams — the historical figure — and assumes the role of Japan's first European samurai. Eight companions are present at launch, including Antje, Johan, Tora-Hime, and the priest Étienne Durand.

The Early Access build includes the game's full **Act 1**: five main-story quests and three side quests. Three major updates are planned post-launch, shaped by community feedback. Notably, *Samurai* is the **first Expeditions game to support drop-in, drop-out online co-op**. Full release targets **2027**.

## Persona 4 Revival New Features Trailer: Send Flying, Full Voice Acting, New Music

**Atlus** released a **[New Features Trailer](https://personacentral.com/p4r-new-features/)** for **Persona 4 Revival** on **August 11, 2026**, narrated by **Paul Castro Jr.** (English voice of Yosuke Hanamura). The two-minute video spotlights five additions new to the remake:

- **Fully Voiced Events** — newly recorded voice acting for all daily life scenes in Inaba, covering both main story and the expanded Social Links
- **Send Flying** — landing a normal attack on a status-afflicted Shadow can knock it into nearby enemies, dealing damage and spreading the ailment to those hit
- **Baton Pass** — passing a turn to another party member after a successful hit continues the offensive chain
- **Prime Time Meter** — a mechanic previously teased, shown in fuller detail
- **New Music** — original compositions by **Ryota Kozuka** alongside remixed Persona 4 classics

**Persona 4 Revival** launches on **February 18, 2027** for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox PC (Game Pass), and PC via Steam.

## NIKKE × Persona 30th Anniversary Crossover Launches Today

**Shift Up** launched the **[Goddess of Victory: NIKKE × Persona](https://personacentral.com/persona-x-nikke/)** crossover event **today, August 13**, running through **September 10, 2026** — commemorating the **30th anniversary of the Persona franchise**.

The collaboration adds three playable units spanning the modern Persona trilogy:

| Unit | Source | Rarity | How to Obtain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aigis** | Persona 3 Reload | SR (SMG) | Free — daily login bonus |
| **Queen (Makoto Niijima)** | Persona 5 Royal | SSR (SG, Phantom Thief costume) | Gacha |
| **Yukiko Amagi** | Persona 4 Golden | Machine Gun (Yasogami uniform) | Gacha |

A new mini-game, **MIDNIGHT PHANTOM RUNNER**, runs throughout the event with themed gameplay and reward tracks. Additional cosmetic outfits include two free costumes for Yukiko and Queen, plus sleepwear variants available through an event pass. A new collaboration costume for existing unit **Phantom** is also available in the costume gacha.
