## Kingdom Hearts IV — D23 2026 Reveals Coco World, Late 2027 Release Window

**Square Enix** and **Disney** debuted a new extended trailer for **Kingdom Hearts IV** at **D23 2026**, confirming a **late 2027 release window** and unveiling the game's first fully revealed Disney world: a collaboration with **Pixar's *Coco***.

The Coco segment shows **Sora** fighting Heartless in **Land of the Dead** settings alongside **Miguel** and **Héctor** during Día de Muertos. Papa Héctor delivers an in-world exposition line mirroring the film's central theme: those forgotten by the living vanish from the afterlife in what he calls "The Final Death." The trailer also shows **King Mickey** navigating environments in search of clues to Sora's whereabouts, and brief action sequences playable as **Donald** and **Goofy**.

Kingdom Hearts IV is confirmed for **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC**. An extended cut of the trailer ran on the RPG Site and Gematsu within hours of the D23 broadcast.

## Final Fantasy XIV Launches on Nintendo Switch 2

**Final Fantasy XIV** officially launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**, completing the rollout announced at the Berlin Fan Festival in late July. The Switch 2 version carries editions priced from **$19.99 to $97.99** and requires a **117 GB** download. Square Enix provided approximately one free month of play time for new Switch 2 accounts to ease server onboarding.

Notably, the Switch 2 version does not require a Nintendo Switch Online membership, though a separate Square Enix subscription is still needed. Players subscribed on other platforms receive a **50% discount** on that subscription for Switch 2. The version supports mouse-and-keyboard input via the USB-C port and runs in full docked and handheld modes.

## Suikoden STAR LEAP Launches in Japan on Mobile

**Konami** released **Suikoden STAR LEAP** on **iOS and Android in Japan on August 7, 2026**, marking the **first entirely new Suikoden title in 14 years**. A pre-launch live stream, "Suikoden Live Vol. 7," aired on August 6 at 20:00 JST.

The game is developed by **MYTHRIL**, is free-to-play, and is set in **Solar Year 453** — four years before the events of the original Suikoden. Players take the role of an unnamed protagonist from a lakeside village who sets out with three companions — **Hisui**, **Shirin**, and **Shapur** — to restore their home after a surprise attack by mysterious enemies.

An international release and a **Steam (PC)** release are confirmed but dates remain unannounced, with the rollout order being Japan mobile → global mobile → Steam.

## Phantom Blade Zero — Dedicated State of Play Deep-Dives Into Combat and the Wulin

**Sony** broadcast a 20-minute **dedicated State of Play for Phantom Blade Zero** on **August 17, 2026**, led by **Donnie Yen**, who has served as creative consultant on the project since 2023. The presentation was the most detailed look yet at S-Game's action RPG ahead of its **October 29 launch on PlayStation 5 and PC**.

Key details revealed:

- **World structure:** The Wulin spans **8 interconnected regions**. The game is not open-world but is non-linear; players navigate using **Bells** for fast travel and an ink-drawn stylised map.
- **Weapons:** Over **30 main weapon categories** and **25 secondary weapon types** confirmed; the presentation showed many for the first time.
- **Combat:** The system emphasises character build customisation through the weapon loadout and skill tree, with dynamic encounters against bosses and secret encounters tucked throughout the handcrafted levels.
- **Story:** Protagonist **Soul**, a warrior framed for his leader's murder, is revived by a figure called the **Flesh Sculptor** but given only **66 days to live**. He travels the rotting martial world of the Wulin searching for a cure while uncovering the conspiracy behind his betrayal.

Pre-orders for Phantom Blade Zero went live alongside the State of Play announcement. An 11-minute trailer was also released ahead of the stream.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/08/phantom-blade-zero-pre-orders-now-available-11-minute-trailer-dedicated-state-of-play-set-for-august-17) · [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/08/17/phantom-blade-zero-state-of-play-dives-deep-into-combat-and-the-wulin-world/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/08/phantom-blade-zero-state-of-play-shows-gameplay-story/) · [Beebom](https://beebom.com/phantom-blade-zero-state-of-play-reveals-combat-system-weapons-and-a-massive-world/)

## Blue Reflection Quartet — Mixed Launch Reviews

**BLUE REFLECTION Quartet** launched on **July 30, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** at **$49.99**, bundling *BLUE REFLECTION*, *BLUE REFLECTION: Second Light*, the *BLUE REFLECTION RAY* anime, and the *BLUE REFLECTION SUN* mobile game. Reviews have since come in with a mixed but generally positive consensus.

- **Final Weapon** awarded it **4/5**, calling it a "magical girl JRPG collection focused on friendship, personal growth, and heartfelt storytelling," with *Second Light* as the standout.
- **The Outerhaven** described it as fitting four solid experiences into one package, though criticised uneven quality across the included titles.
- **Game8** titled its review "Half-Baked Collection That Fails to Elevate the Series Further," citing missed opportunities in remastering the included content.
- **Phenixx Gaming**'s Switch 2-specific review noted the portability benefits for what are primarily story-driven games.

The collection has been positioned as an accessible starting point for players new to the series.

## Brigandine: Abyss — Releases August 26–27

**Happinet**'s strategy RPG **Brigandine: Abyss** is set to release **August 26 on PC via Steam**, followed by **August 27 on Nintendo Switch 2 and PlayStation 5**. The game is set in an entirely new world separate from previous Brigandine entries and is designed to be accessible without prior series knowledge.

Players choose from **six story campaigns**, each offering a different perspective on a central conflict, or take control of any of **24 nations** in the sandbox **Mission Mode**. Gameplay alternates between an **Organisation phase** — resource management, unit recruitment, and positioning — and an **Attack phase** of hexagonal-grid tactical combat that factors skills, elemental attributes, and the monsters bound to each officer.

A **Deluxe Edition** for Switch 2 and PS5 includes a mini art book and digital soundtrack alongside the base game.

## Angeline Era — Arriving on Consoles September 2

**Analgesic Productions**' non-linear action RPG **Angeline Era** is coming to **PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2 on September 2, 2026** at **$24.99**. Xbox console versions were also shown in the announcement trailer, though dates for those remain unconfirmed.

The game originally released on PC and Mac via Steam in December 2025. It follows ex-soldier **Tets Kinoshta**, who allies with the angel **Arkas Gemini** to uncover the secrets of the dormant mothership **Throne** hidden behind a fierce storm in a world populated by humans, fae, and angels. The "Bumpslash" combat system handles encounters by automatically attacking on contact with enemies. Runs last **20–30 hours**.

## The Severed Gods — Turn-Based Tactical Roguelite Enters Early Access

**Topebox**'s **The Severed Gods** launched in **Early Access on Steam on August 10, 2026**. The game blends turn-based tactical RPG combat with roguelite progression in a dark fantasy world overrun by colossal **Titans**, following a band of heroes marked by fate on an endless cycle of death and rebirth as they seek to slay the gods.

Mechanics include **height-based tactical positioning**, severing giant bosses for parts and rewards, and a **Karma system** shaped by player choices that alters NPC interactions and relic availability across runs. The full game will add additional worlds and meta-progression in a **Realm of Spirits**. Both a free demo and the Early Access build are currently available on Steam; current user reviews are **mixed (66% positive)**.

Available on **PC (Windows)** only at Early Access launch.

Sources: [Steam Store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3755930/The_Severed_Gods/) · [Steam News](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3755930/view/689765153413006182)
