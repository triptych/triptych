## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Is the Best-Reviewed Game of 2026

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — a full remake of the 2006 second entry in the Liberl arc — launched on **September 17, 2026** for **PC via Steam, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2**, and PlayStation 5, and has since overtaken every other 2026 release on aggregate review trackers.

The game currently holds a **92 on OpenCritic** with **98% of critics recommending it**, pushing past *Resident Evil Requiem*, *Pokémon Pokopia*, and *Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave* to claim the top spot among 2026 releases.

Individual highlights from critics:

- **DualShockers** — 10/10: *"one of the finest titles the genre has to offer today"*
- **Game Informer** — 9.5/10: *"one of the best RPGs of the year"*
- **IGN** — 9/10
- **TechRadar** described it as *"yet another triumphant remake"*
- **RPGFan** praised the combat depth, visual flair, and quality-of-life updates

The remake substantially overhauls the 2006 original with updated visuals, a new fully voiced English and Japanese cast, and meaningful gameplay additions while preserving the story and structure that made the Liberl arc a series touchstone. The game is a direct sequel to the *Trails in the Sky FC* remake released in 2025.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Near-Universal Acclaim

**Intelligent Systems** and **Nintendo**'s **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched on **September 17, 2026** exclusively for **Nintendo Switch 2** and is generating some of the strongest critical reception the series has ever seen.

Multiple major outlets awarded it perfect or near-perfect scores:

- **IGN** — 10/10: *"Fortune's Weave builds upon its strategic arts in ways I couldn't have anticipated. It's never satisfied to make me grow only as a battlefield tactician, or only in my ability to manage my time between skirmishes wisely, but both in perfect harmony."*
- **Video Games Chronicle** — 5/5: *"Nintendo's most ambitious RPG yet is an incredible triumph"*
- **Eurogamer** — 5/5: *"Fortune's Weave represents the pinnacle of Intelligent Systems' achievements. It plaits a tapestry from all of the series' various highest highs, somehow managing to entwine the storytelling prowess of Three Houses with the clean, satisfying combat of Engage."*

Set roughly **100 years after** *Three Houses*, the game follows **four new protagonists** across largely distinct storylines that converge in the late game. Famitsu reviewed it the week of September 20 as part of a substantial scores update. Critics uniformly called it the longest campaign in the mainline series and praised how the four-route structure avoids the padding that hurt earlier multi-route games.

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure PS5 / Switch 2 Versions Reviewed

**NIS America** and porting house **PH3** (with **Durante** leading technical work) launched **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** for **PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 10, 2026**. A free patch simultaneously updated the existing Nintendo Switch and PC versions with compatibility improvements.

The new versions add:

- **4K resolution / 120 FPS** on PS5 and Switch 2
- **Mouse-and-keyboard input** support on Switch 2 via USB-C
- **Save data transfer** from Switch and PC versions

Critical reception has been warm, with RPG Site calling the ports *"superb"* and praising flawless performance on both base PS5 and PS5 Pro at 4K 120fps. Loot Level Chill described the package as *"some of the very best options if you're looking for a way to spend dozens and dozens of hours on Switch 2."* The Crossbell games have been frequently cited by Falcom fans as essential prerequisites before the upcoming *Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter* and the wider Cold Steel arc.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Switch 2 Edition Physical Version Out Today

**Nintendo**'s **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** gets its physical cartridge release today, **October 1, 2026**, following the digital-only launch on **July 30**. The physical edition includes all Switch 2 Edition content on-cart with no download required.

As a reminder for those picking up the box copy, the Switch 2 Edition adds:

- **Momo** from *Xenosaga* as a new Rare Blade (Light/Dark affinity) — the first canonical crossover between the two Monolith Soft franchises
- **New Pyra and Mythra outfits** illustrated by character designer **Masatsugu Saito**
- Expanded **Merc Assault** supporting up to six Blades per mission
- **4K docked / 1080p handheld at 60 FPS**

Existing Switch owners can purchase an upgrade pack on the eShop rather than buying the full physical edition. A free version 3.0.0 patch went out to all original Switch owners in July for compatibility preparation.

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — April 8, 2027 Release Date Confirmed at PlayStation State of Play

**Square Enix** used the **PlayStation State of Play in September 2026** to confirm **April 8, 2027** as the release date for **Final Fantasy VII Revelation**, the third and final entry in the *Final Fantasy VII Remake* project. The presentation included a lengthy gameplay showcase covering new mechanics and systems.

Highlights from the showcase:

- **Highwind airship** is fully explorable as a hub, with players able to roam the airship freely between story sequences
- **Roaming Weapons** can now be encountered as mounted encounters in the open world, dramatically changing their encounter design versus the scripted sequences in *Rebirth*
- **Chocobos** can be used as mounts during combat for the first time in the trilogy
- The FITS job system and Queen's Blood card game (previously detailed) were shown in extended gameplay

The game releases on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC**. Square Enix confirmed the Switch 2 version runs at 1080p handheld / 4K docked with no content cuts versus other platforms.

Sources: [Tom's Guide State of Play recap](https://www.tomsguide.com/news/live/ps5-state-of-play-september-2026-live-updates) · [Press Start State of Play roundup](https://press-start.com.au/features/2026/09/03/everything-announced-at-the-september-2026-playstation-state-of-play/) · [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/playstation-state-of-play-september-2026-all-the-biggest-announcements-games-and-trailers/)
