## EXSTETRA HD Remaster — 2013 Japan-Only Vita RPG Gets First English Release July 30

**FuRyu Corporation** is bringing **EXSTETRA HD Remaster** to **PC via Steam on July 30, 2026** — the first time the cult 2013 JRPG has been released in English anywhere in the world, thirteen years after its original Japan-only debut on PlayStation Vita and Nintendo 3DS.

The game follows high schooler **Ryoma**, who is transported to a fantasy dimension fused with a distorted version of modern-day Tokyo. As the chosen "Prisma," Ryoma has the power to awaken allies into **Prisma Knights** through kissing — a mechanic that seeds EXSTETRA's distinctive reputation in Japanese RPG circles. Combat is turn-based; players collect EXS energy from defeated enemies and transfer it to strengthen their Prisma Knight companions.

The HD Remaster includes updated visuals and runs with support for English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese. **Console versions for PS5, Switch, and Switch 2 are in development** but carry no release date yet, making the Steam launch the only way to play in the West at the moment.

## Signy & Mino: Against All Gods — Tag-Team Combo RPG Launches July 30 on PC

**Armor Games Studios**' **Signy & Mino: Against All Gods** (previously titled *Designated Demigod*) arrives on **PC via Steam on July 30, 2026**, with console versions unannounced.

The side-scrolling battle RPG follows **Signy** investigating her parents' deaths alongside her canine companion **Mino**. What begins as a personal quest expands when a vengeful mountain god is resurrected, drawing Signy into an alliance with fallen gods and washed-up warriors. Combat is turn-based and heavily combo-focused, rewarding players who chain attacks and abilities across the dual-protagonist team.

## Culdcept The First — 1997 Sega Saturn Card-Board Hybrid Comes West July 30 on PC, September 8 on Switch

**CityConnection** is releasing **Culdcept The First** — an enhanced version of the 1997 Sega Saturn classic — on **PC via Steam on July 30, 2026**. Console versions (PS5, PS4, Switch, and Xbox Series) follow on **September 8** in the West via publisher **Clear River Games**.

The game blends board game movement (dice-driven traversal across maps) with a 50-card deckbuilding system: players summon creatures and cast spells on the spaces where they land, competing to accumulate gold from rivals who trespass on occupied territories. The campaign battles AI-controlled Cepters across a lengthy adventure, all scored by **Yuzo Koshiro**. The remaster packs in over 300 cards, quality-of-life features standard to modern rereleases (rewind, quick save/load, boosted battle rewards), and carries over save data from the Steam demo already available.

## PRELUDE: Dark Pain — FFT-Inspired Tactical RPG Enters Steam Early Access

**PRELUDE: Dark Pain**, a dark fantasy tactical RPG developed by Spanish indie studio **Quickfire Games** (published by **Firesquid**), entered **Steam Early Access on July 27, 2026** at **$19.99** (10% launch discount through August 10).

The game casts players as **Soren**, a retired warrior-turned-blacksmith pulled back into conflict against the brutal **Order of the Ashen Crusade**. Battles play out on grid maps with elevation and environmental interactions in a style clearly indebted to *Final Fantasy Tactics*. Players build squads of up to five heroes, manage a resistance settlement, and face choices that affect both the story and which characters can be recruited.

The Early Access launch includes the complete first act — approximately **15 hours of content**, nine recruitable heroes, and 63 unlockable skills. Quickfire expects the Early Access period to last around one year; the full release will add multiple additional acts, more than 20 playable heroes across eight class types, 20 factions, roughly 70 enemy types, and equipment crafting.

## The Relic: First Guardian — Korean Mythology Action RPG Launches July 31 on PC, Switch 2 to Follow

**The Relic: First Guardian**, an action RPG set in an apocalyptic world drawn from **Korean mythology**, launches **July 31, 2026 on PC via Steam**. A **Nintendo Switch 2 version** is confirmed for later in summer 2026 (no exact date yet).

The world of **Arsiltus** was once prosperous until the destruction of a great Relic swallowed it into the void. Players take on the role of the last Guardian tasked with recovering the scattered Relic pieces to close the void. The combat system makes a notable design choice: **stamina is reserved exclusively for defensive actions** (dodges and blocks), removing resource constraints from standard attacks. Character builds are shaped by over **70 collectible Relic passive effects** rather than traditional level-ups.

A pre-launch gameplay trailer released July 23 gave the deepest look yet at these systems ahead of launch. A physical PS5 version is planned for **September 4**.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/the-relic-first-guardian-launches-july-31-for-ps5-and-pc-later-this-summer-for-xbox-series-and-switch-2) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/the-relic-first-guardian-gets-pre-launch-gameplay-trailer/) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/the-relic-first-guardian-release-date-ps5-pc-switch-2/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/06/15/the-relic-first-guardian-release-date-pc-ps5/)

## Beast of Reincarnation — Game Freak's First Non-Pokémon AAA Launches August 4 on PC

**Game Freak**, the studio behind every mainline Pokémon game, releases its first original AAA title outside the franchise — **Beast of Reincarnation** — on **August 4, 2026 via PC (Steam)**, with day-one availability on **PC Game Pass**. (No Nintendo version is currently planned.)

Set in post-apocalyptic Japan in the year **4026**, the game follows **Emma**, a Sealer, and her dog **Koo** as they navigate a land consumed by corruption. Built in **Unreal Engine 5**, it is the studio's most technically ambitious project outside of Pokémon and represents a notable pivot toward mature action-RPG design. A *Game Informer* preview published July 13 highlighted that parrying directly benefits Koo in combat — building the dog's abilities into the core moment-to-moment loop.
