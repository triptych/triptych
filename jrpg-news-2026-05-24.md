# JRPG News — May 24, 2026

## Persona 5: The Phantom X Version 4.1 — Persona 3 Reload Crossover Is Live

**Sega**, **Atlus**, and developer **Black Wings Game Studio** launched **Version 4.1** of *Persona5: The Phantom X* on May 20, kicking off a major crossover with *Persona 3 Reload*. The update introduces the event **"Met by Moonlight Part 1"**, in which the Phantom Thieves stumble into a dimension resembling Tartarus and fight alongside **S.E.E.S.** from P3R.

The headline addition is **Makoto Yuki** — the protagonist of *Persona 3 Reload* — as a new **5-Star Fire-attribute Assassin**. He builds **Moon Phase stacks** via his own skills and ally synergies, enabling burst damage along with party-wide buffing. The banner runs until **June 25, 2026 at 1:59 AM UTC**. A new **Roguelike Mode: Tartarus** also ships with the update. The event is live now on mobile and PC.

## LumenTale: Memories of Trey Launches Tuesday on PC and Switch

**Team17** and developer **Beehive Studios** are releasing **LumenTale: Memories of Trey** on **May 26** for PC (Steam) and Nintendo Switch. The game is a creature-collecting JRPG set in **Talea**, an Italian-inspired region shaped by old imperial rivalries and the memory of war.

Players follow **Trey**, a boy piecing together his lost memories while crossing the continent. Roughly **140 Animon** across 13 elemental types can be caught and battled. Combat supports both 1v1 encounters and **4v4 party formations** — an unusual twist on the genre — alongside cooking and crafting systems. The world design leans into proper JRPG storytelling rather than purely mechanized creature-collecting.

## Like a Dragon: Stranger Than Heaven — Full Story and Setting Revealed

A dedicated **Xbox Presents** broadcast on **May 6** gave the deepest look yet at **RGG Studio**'s upcoming prequel JRPG, *Like a Dragon: Stranger Than Heaven*. The game spans **five historical eras** — 1915, 1929, 1943, 1951, and 1965 — across five distinct Japanese cities.

The story follows **Makoto Daito**, a young boy of mixed American-Japanese heritage who loses both parents and, facing racial persecution, stows away on a ship in San Francisco in 1915 hoping to find belonging in Japan. The broadcast also teased the full cast, music, and combat systems. *Stranger Than Heaven* is an **Xbox Play Anywhere** title coming to Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Xbox Cloud Gaming — and will be included in **Xbox Game Pass** at launch. A release window has not yet been confirmed.

## Tales of Festival 2026 — Hanako Oku and DEEN Confirmed as Performers

**Bandai Namco** has revealed the first two musical acts for **Tales of Festival 2026**, set for **July 4–5** at the **Yokohama BUNTAI**. Vocalist **Hanako Oku** — who contributed theme songs to *Tales of Phantasia: Narikiri Dungeon X* — and rock band **DEEN** — who have appeared across multiple series entries — will both perform live. DEEN is confirmed for the second day, **July 5**. The event continues the celebration of the **Tales series' 30th anniversary**, and additional performers and details are expected in future announcements.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Summer Game Fest the Likely Stage for Release Date

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave**, the eighteenth mainline entry in Nintendo and Intelligent Systems' strategy RPG series, has had no new public showing since its initial reveal at a Nintendo Direct in September 2025. With the game still slated for 2026 on **Nintendo Switch 2**, the community is looking to **Summer Game Fest 2026** as the likely venue for a release date announcement.

Fortune's Weave is set around a gladiatorial tournament called the **Heroic Games**, hosted by a figure known as the **Divine Sovereign** who promises to grant the wishes of the victors. The game appears to be set in the same world as the Fódlan trilogy (*Three Houses* / *Three Hopes*) but in a different era, with a character resembling an older Sothis spotted in the reveal trailer. A dedicated GamesRadar feature and community prediction trackers both point to SGF as the most probable window.

## Chronosphere Entering Early Access Monday

Turn-based RPG **Chronosphere** enters **Steam Early Access on May 25**. The developers have indicated they're targeting a 1.0 release roughly six months after the EA launch. Details on the game's systems remain sparse, but it's worth flagging for genre fans watching the week's release slate.
