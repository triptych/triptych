## Kyoto Xanadu — New Promotional Trailer and Theme Song Ahead of July 16 Japan Launch

**Nihon Falcom** released a new promotional trailer and second TV commercial for **Kyoto Xanadu -the Blooming Phantom-** on July 1, showcasing fresh gameplay footage alongside the game's theme song, **"From 'i'"**, performed by in-game virtual idol **LinoN**. The theme song is now available on streaming services including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, mora, and YouTube Music.

**Kyoto Xanadu** is an action RPG set in an alternate-history version of modern Japan in which Kyoto remained the nation's capital. A second world inhabited by monsters is bleeding into reality, and the game follows **Rei**, a high school student with newly awakened supernatural abilities, who enrolls at **Hirasaka Academy** — a school for students gifted with similar powers — to conquer a dungeon called **Xanadu**. The game mixes 2D side-scrolling traversal with fully realized 3D environments and a combat system that evolves via a "Soul-Device" upgrade tree.

The game launches **July 16 in Japan and Asia** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. Pricing in Japan is **¥7,920** (PS5/Switch/PC) and **¥8,070** (Switch 2, digital only); Switch owners can upgrade for **¥150**. A **western release is confirmed** and is currently being coordinated with a publishing partner, with a date to follow once finalized.

Falcom has been on an upswing in 2026 following the strong reception of the Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure PS5 and Switch 2 ports (out September 10). Kyoto Xanadu represents the studio's first original IP built around a contemporary Japanese setting rather than a fantasy world, a meaningful pivot that many have been watching closely.

## Persona 4 Revival — Protagonist Trailer Spotlights Yu Narukami

Atlus released a dedicated **protagonist sizzle trailer** for **Persona 4 Revival** on July 2, timed ahead of the game's **Anime Expo 2026 panel** (July 4). The trailer focuses on **Yu Narukami**, spending over a minute on event scenes, cinematic sequences, and combat highlights, including:

- Yu entering the **TV World** for the first time alongside Chie Satonaka and Yosuke Hanamura.
- His first **Persona summoning** — calling forth **Izanagi**.
- A callback to the *Persona 4 Golden* addition of the **scooter license** subplot.
- A brief final-second line of English dialogue, the first time the silent protagonist is heard: **Nazeeh Tarsha** voices him in English, while **Daisuke Namikawa** returns for the Japanese track.

Persona 4 Revival launches **February 18, 2027** on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (day-one on Game Pass). The AX panel on July 4 will feature character designer **Shigenori Soejima**, general producer **Kazuhisa Wada**, and business producer **Atsushi Nomura**.

## Square Enix Manga & Books Announces Eight Manga and Two Books at Anime Expo 2026

**Square Enix Manga & Books** held its annual industry panel at Anime Expo on July 2 and announced **eight new manga series** and **two new game tie-in books** for English-language publication in **Spring 2027**.

The headline gaming book is **Final Fantasy VII Rebirth: Dear Destiny** — the sequel novel to *Final Fantasy VII Remake: Traces of Two Pasts* — confirmed for **January 2027** at **$26.99 USD**. Like its predecessor, the novel is structured around two female leads: **Aerith Gainsborough** struggling to find purpose selling flowers in Midgar and evading the Turks, and **Tifa Lockhart** managing Seventh Heaven while carrying painful memories of Nibelheim's destruction. The story ends with Aerith's departure from the church and her reunion with Cloud in the Sector 7 slums — the moment that opens *Final Fantasy VII Rebirth*. Voice actors **Briana White** (Aerith) and **Britt Baron** (Tifa) debuted the announcement in a video message at the panel. Pre-orders opened via Penguin Random House and other major booksellers on July 3.

The eight manga titles and the second book were not all named in initial coverage; the full announcement list is expected from Square Enix Manga & Books' official channels shortly.

## Dungeon Antiqua Coming to Consoles July 30

**AMATA Games** and developer **Shiromofu Factory** announced on July 2 that **Dungeon Antiqua** — a retro dungeon-crawler RPG that launched on Steam in October 2024 — is coming to **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch** on **July 30, 2026**, priced at **$8.99** (with a 15% launch discount for the first two weeks).

The game takes inspiration from 1980s and 1990s classic RPGs: top-down dungeon exploration, auto-generated maps, and a profession-based party-building system with a wide variety of classes. The map renders only the area currently visible to the party, creating a genuine old-school fog-of-war feel. Supported languages on console are English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese.

## PlayStation Plus July 2026 Monthly Games Include CrossCode

Sony announced the **PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for July 2026** on July 1. All three titles are available to claim from **July 7 through August 3**:

- **CrossCode** (PS5, PS4) — Radical Fish Games' acclaimed 2D action RPG with 16-bit aesthetics, a deep real-time combat and puzzle system, and a science-fiction story set inside a futuristic MMO. CrossCode is widely regarded as a modern classic of the genre.
- **For the King II** (PS5, PS4) — A roguelite/tabletop hybrid RPG playable solo or in up to four-player co-op.
- **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III** (PS5, PS4) — Not an RPG, but included in the lineup.

CrossCode's addition to PS Plus is the standout for JRPG-adjacent fans. The game has been available on PC since 2018 and on consoles since 2020 but has never previously appeared on PS Plus, making this the broadest audience it has reached on PlayStation.
