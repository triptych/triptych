## Persona 5 Royal Returns to Xbox Game Pass June 9

**Persona 5 Royal** is joining Xbox Game Pass on **June 9** — available on Cloud, Console, and PC (Ultimate, Premium, and PC Game Pass tiers). This is a return visit: the game was previously on Game Pass until it was removed on **October 31, 2023**.

For new subscribers or anyone who missed it the first time, Persona 5 Royal is the expanded 2020 version of the 2016 JRPG, adding a third chapter and additional content. It routinely clocks in at over 100 hours. The timing is notable: with Persona 6 rumors reaching a fever pitch ahead of the Xbox Games Showcase (June 7–8), dropping the series' most acclaimed entry back onto Game Pass reads as deliberate positioning by Atlus and Xbox.

## Like a Dragon: Stranger Than Heaven — What We Know

**SEGA** and **RGG Studio** have confirmed a **Winter 2026** release window for **Like a Dragon: Stranger Than Heaven**, a prequel to the entire Like a Dragon franchise that explores the origins of the Tojo Clan.

Key details gathered from the studio's deep-dive presentation earlier this year:

**Story:** Set across **five distinct time periods** spanning 1915 to 1965 across five Japanese cities. The protagonist is **Makoto Daito**, a young man of mixed American-Japanese heritage who stows away on a ship to Japan after losing his parents and facing persecution in the United States.

**Combat:** A significant mechanical departure from the franchise's turn-based Ichiban era. Players control **Makoto's left and right sides independently** using the bumpers and triggers — enabling jab-then-crush combos, one-hand block while countering with the other, and ground assault positioning. RGG describes it as more physical and instinctive than either the brawler Kiryu games or the turn-based Ichiban entries.

**Platform/Access:** Confirmed **Xbox Game Pass on Day 1** and an **Xbox Play Anywhere** title (one purchase covers Xbox and PC).

The Winter 2026 window puts it anywhere from October through January 2027. RGG has not committed to a specific month.

## Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales — Demo Live, June 18 Launch

**Square Enix** and **Claytechworks** (the team behind Bravely Default and Octopath Traveler) have a free demo live now for **The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales**, ahead of its **June 18, 2026** launch on Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam.

**Save data carries over** from the demo to the full game.

**What it is:** An action RPG set in Philabieldia, a continent overrun by beast tribes. Elliot and his fairy companion Faie discover a "Doorway of Time" artifact in ancient ruins — the story then spans multiple eras. Unlike Claytechworks' previous HD-2D titles, this one uses **real-time combat** (not turn-based) with **seven weapon types** including swords, bows, chains, and sickles. A second player can control Faie in **local co-op**.

The shift to action combat from Claytechworks is the most-discussed element — the demo gives players a direct answer before committing $60+.

## SGF Eve: Women-Led Games Showcase Airs Tonight

The **Women-Led Games Showcase** goes live tonight, **June 4 at 7:00 p.m. ET**, as part of Summer Game Fest week. The showcase spotlights games created, directed, or led by women developers.

Historically these satellite events have surfaced indie and mid-tier titles that fly under the radar of the main shows — worth a watch for JRPG and RPG fans hunting for overlooked titles.

Tomorrow's **SGF Main Show** (June 5) and the **Xbox Games Showcase** (June 7–8) remain the week's marquee events for major announcements.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — SGF Watch

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** is one of Nintendo's most-anticipated near-term titles and a strong candidate for Summer Game Fest coverage. The game was announced late last year with trailers suggesting a narrative link to *Fire Emblem: Three Houses* — a prominent appearance by **Sothis** has fans theorizing about timeline and world connections.

Gameplay appears to return to **turn-based tactical** combat. No confirmed release window beyond 2026 has been given. Nintendo has not confirmed a direct SGF slot, but given the timing and the franchise's momentum, a trailer or update this week is widely expected.
