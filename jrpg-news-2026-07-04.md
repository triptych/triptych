## Persona 4 Revival AX 2026 Panel — Anime Director's Presence Ignites Adaptation Speculation

The **Atlus AX 2026 panel** for **Persona 4 Revival** took place today at **Petree Hall** (11:30 AM – 12:20 PM PT), featuring general producer **Kazuhisa Wada**, character designer **Shigenori Soejima**, business producer **Atsushi Nomura** — and, notably, anime director **Yoshiyuki Asai**.

Atlus has not officially confirmed what Asai's role in the project entails beyond his panel participation. His résumé speaks for itself: directorial credits include **Charlotte** (P.A. Works), **Fate/Apocrypha** (A-1 Pictures), **The Day I Became a God** (P.A. Works), and **Buddy Daddies** (P.A. Works), the latter of which won the **Best Original Anime award at the Crunchyroll Anime Awards 2024**. His presence alongside the game's core producer and character designer has generated significant speculation in the fan community that a **Persona 4 Revival anime adaptation** is in development. Atlus has a history of using events to seed announcement groundwork — a formal reveal could follow in the months ahead.

The panel shared never-before-seen gameplay footage and character-related content ahead of **Persona 4 Revival**'s **February 18, 2027** launch on **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC** (day-one on **Game Pass**). The attendee experience also included an immersive SEGA booth area themed around the TV World and a Junes-inspired café experience with Persona-protagonist boba tea drinks in partnership with Thirsty Waters Premium Tea House, timed to Persona's **30th Anniversary**.

## Danganronpa 2x2 Delayed to Early 2027; Slayhem Mode Revealed

**Spike Chunsoft** announced at its **Anime Expo 2026** panel on July 3 that **Danganronpa 2x2** has been delayed from its previously planned 2026 release window to **early 2027**, with a specific date to be confirmed at a later time. Alongside the delay, Spike Chunsoft unveiled the name and details of the game's second playable scenario.

**Slayhem Mode** is a new scenario built on the foundation of *Danganronpa 2: Goodbye Despair* but with a completely different chain of events — a specific triggering incident causes different victims, culprits, and murder tricks compared to the original game's narrative. Spike Chunsoft estimates **Slayhem is approximately 20% larger** than the base "Original Mode," making the full package run to roughly **35 hours** of content versus the original game's approximately 29 hours.

Additional changes shown at the panel include:

- A **3D World Map** replacing the original game's universally criticized side-scrolling island navigation, giving players a clearer sense of Jabberwock Island's layout and removing the friction of the old traversal system.
- **Upgraded backgrounds, character sprites, and overworld visuals**, shown in side-by-side comparisons with the original.

**Danganronpa 2x2** launches on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

## Demons' Night Fever — Disgaea Creator's New SRPG Gets Gameplay Showcase

**Arc System Works** and developer **Drecom**, in partnership with Disgaea creator **Sohei Niikawa**'s one-person company **SuperNiche**, revealed gameplay footage for **Demons' Night Fever** at the Arc System Works Showcase 2026 last week, confirming a **2026 release** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and PC**.

The game stars **Giga Super Death**, the most wicked devil in existence — who was one step from killing God before abruptly regressing into a helpless infant. Restored to power gradually, he must build up his forces through the game's signature **Peon Exploitation System**: deliberately sacrificing allied units in combat generates "evil misdeeds" that fuel stat growth and unlock new abilities. The mechanic inverts the protective instincts typical of the strategy RPG genre and is designed to reward cold-blooded tactical decision-making.

The creative team is a notable assembly of JRPG veterans:

- **Producer/Scenario**: Sohei Niikawa (founder of SuperNiche; former CEO of Nippon Ichi Software, creator of the *Disgaea* franchise), writing the scenario under his pen name **Roman Kitayama**
- **Character Design**: **Yuji Himukai**, known for *Etrian Odyssey* and *Persona Q*
- **Original Soundtrack**: **Tatsuya Yano**, known for the *Atelier* series

**Demons' Night Fever** is published by Arc System Works and carries a 2026 target window, though a specific release date has not yet been set.

## Solo Leveling: KARMA — AX 2026 Panel Shows New Characters and Gameplay

**Netmarble** held the **"Solo Leveling: KARMA — Expanding Universe"** panel at Anime Expo 2026 today (July 4, 10:30 AM – 11:20 AM PDT, JW Marriott Diamond Ballroom), sharing fresh content for its roguelite action RPG adaptation of the *Solo Leveling* manhwa.

Highlights from the panel included:

- **New gameplay footage** not shown in prior reveals, expanding on how Sung Jinwoo's powers evolve through the story content set after he enters the dimensional gap.
- **Previously unannounced characters** revealed for the first time.
- **Live script readings** by **Aleks Le**, the English voice actor for protagonist **Sung Jinwoo**, alongside June Sung Kim and other franchise representatives.
- **Behind-the-scenes development context** from the **Netmarble Neo** development team.

**Solo Leveling: KARMA** is developed by **Netmarble Neo** and published by **Netmarble**, targeting **PC, iOS, and Android**. The game expands the story beyond the source material's ending, placing Jinwoo in a new dimensional conflict. No specific release date was given during the panel.
