# JRPG News — May 3, 2026

## Tales of Eternia Remastered: PEGI Leak Points to Imminent Announcement

Bandai Namco appears to be gearing up for an official reveal of **Tales of Eternia Remastered** after a PEGI rating listing briefly surfaced before being swiftly taken down. The classification appeared on April 16, assigning the title a **PEGI 12** rating for moderate violence and language, with the rating summary describing Reid, Farah, Keele, and the mysterious Meredy — confirming the story follows the original 2000 PlayStation game closely.

Key details from the leak:
- Platform confirmed so far: **Nintendo Switch** (though an exclusive seems unlikely given Bandai Namco's recent remaster strategy)
- The original *Tales of Eternia* was a PlayStation exclusive in North America released under the name *Tales of Destiny II*; it later received a PSP port in 2005
- No release window has been announced; the swift removal of the PEGI listing suggests an official announcement is close

With *Tales of Graces f Remastered* (2025) still fresh, this would be the second rapid-turnaround remaster in the series and suggests Bandai Namco is continuing its push to bring classic *Tales* entries to modern hardware.

## Granblue Fantasy: Relink – Endless Ragnarok Gears Up for July 9 Launch

**Granblue Fantasy: Relink – Endless Ragnarok**, the major expansion to Cygames' 2024 action RPG, is confirmed for **July 9, 2026** on PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam. An open beta ran April 24–27, offering a preview of the new systems.

What Endless Ragnarok adds:
- **New story** set after the base game's ending
- **New characters**, co-op quest tiers, and additional bosses
- **The Conflux** — a new solo mode with roguelite-style unpredictable challenges
- **Master traits** to push character builds further
- **Cross-platform multiplayer** across all platforms

Pricing: **$29.99 upgrade kit** for existing *Relink* owners; **$59.99** standalone Digital Standard Edition. A Special Edition upgrade kit is available for $54.99.

## Another Eden Begins: Pre-Launch Coverage Grows

Following its February 2026 Nintendo Direct Partner Showcase reveal, **Another Eden Begins** — Wright Flyer Studios' ground-up console/PC reimagining of the mobile *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space* — continues to generate coverage ahead of its **2026 summer** PC and Switch/Switch 2 release.

Notable details surfacing from developer interviews:
- The entire game has been redesigned from scratch — exploration, combat, and the character development system are all new builds, not ports of mobile systems
- **10 distinct endings** and New Game+ are confirmed
- **Masato Katō** (scenario and direction) and **Yasunori Mitsuda** (main theme composer), both of *Chrono Trigger* and *Chrono Cross* fame, are returning from the original mobile title
- Full voice acting throughout

This is one of the more ambitious console-to-JRPG pipeline stories of 2026, essentially using the mobile IP as a foundation for an entirely new game.
