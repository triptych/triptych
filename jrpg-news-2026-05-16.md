# JRPG News — May 16, 2026

## Where Winds Meet Announces "Imperial Palace" Second Major Expansion

**Everstone Studio** and **NetEase Games** revealed *Where Winds Meet*'s second major expansion — **Imperial Palace** — arriving in late May alongside update **1.7**. The new zone is described as a 1-million-square-meter royal court with over **3,000 interactive NPCs** and a conspiracy-laden story set inside the royal court of Kaifeng.

The update also introduces:
- A **homestead system** for player housing
- A new **companion system**
- **Gauntlet** weapons as a new combat type

A limited-time Panda cosmetic launches May 19 for 60 Echo Beads (~$0.99). The reveal was made during the game's half-anniversary celebration alongside a roadmap of planned content through the rest of 2026.

---

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Coming to PS5 and Switch 2 This Fall

**NIS America** and **Nihon Falcom** confirmed that **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** — the fourth and fifth entries in the Trails series — are coming to **PlayStation 5** and **Nintendo Switch 2** in **Fall 2026**.

The enhanced ports include:
- Up to **4K resolution** and **120 FPS**
- Toggle between **original and HD-upscaled textures**
- **Mouse input support** on Switch 2

Both titles are available separately digitally or bundled together as a physical collection. This marks the first time these two games — previously most accessible on Switch and PC — will be available on Sony hardware with meaningful visual upgrades.

## Mina the Hollower Launching May 29

**Yacht Club Games** (creators of *Shovel Knight*) confirmed **Mina the Hollower** launches on **May 29, 2026** for PS5, Nintendo Switch 1 & 2, Xbox Series X|S, PC, Mac, and Linux at **$19.99**.

The game is an action-adventure inspired by the visual look of Game Boy Color titles. Players control Mina, an inventor and "Hollower," who uses her whip **Nightstar** in four directions and can burrow underground for invincible traversal. The project launched via Kickstarter in early 2022, raising over $1.2 million from 21,000+ backers, and was delayed from its original October 2025 target.

## Another Eden Begins Confirmed for September 17, 2026

**Studio PRISMA**'s standalone offline JRPG **Another Eden Begins** has a locked release date of **September 17, 2026** on Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam. A physical edition is also planned.

Separate from the ongoing *Another Eden* mobile game, this is a new premium single-player title featuring a time-traveling story with command-based battles. The script is penned by **Masato Kato**, writer of *Chrono Trigger*, *Chrono Cross*, and portions of *Xenogears*. The game supports English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese at launch.

---

## Kyoto Xanadu Heads West After July Japan Launch

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Kyoto Xanadu** has a confirmed Japan and Asia release date of **July 16, 2026** on PS5, Nintendo Switch 1 & 2, and PC via Steam. A Western release is confirmed to be in the works, coordinated with a publishing partner whose announcement is pending.

The title is a spiritual successor to *Tokyo Xanadu* (2015), relocating the action RPG formula to a supernatural version of Kyoto. New character profiles and combat details have been trickling out ahead of the Japan launch, with Western fans expected to receive a release window announcement separately once the partner's deal is finalized.
