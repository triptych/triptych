## Metaphor: ReFantazio Arrives on Switch 2 This Wednesday

Atlus's award-winning fantasy RPG **Metaphor: ReFantazio** comes to **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12**, four days from now. The launch price is **$39.99** (3,990 yen in Japan / €39.99 in Europe), significantly below the original 2024 release price, making it the most affordable entry point for new players. A **35th Digital Anniversary Edition** is 5,990 yen in Japan.

A **SteelBook Edition** will be available for a limited time at launch through select retailers. The game ships on a game-key card for the physical version. An October demo that covered the opening hours carried save data forward to the full release.

This is the game's Nintendo debut; the original 2024 release was on PS5, PS4, Xbox Series, and PC. It won Best RPG, Best Narrative, and Best Art Direction at The Game Awards 2024.

## Persona 1 Remake Footage Surfaces Online — Atlus Silent

Roughly two minutes of footage that appears to show an unannounced **Persona 1 remake** circulated online on **October 31, 2026**, under the title **"Persona: Be Your True Mind."**

The clip, initially posted to GamingLeaksAndRumours, shows dungeon exploration, combat, and a dialogue scene rendered in a visual style consistent with Atlus's current-generation work. Fan analysis points to a shared summoning animation with *Persona 4 Revival* as a possible sign of authenticity, though Atlus has not commented.

Atlus producer **Kazuhisa Wada** discussed the possibility of Persona 1 and Persona 2 remakes in August 2026, adding context to the timing. Outlets are split on how credible the footage is, with some urging caution and others finding it convincing given the evident production effort.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** will shut down the Japanese server for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026**. A paid-currency refund window follows the shutdown.

The game launched in Japan for iOS and Android in September 2023, with a PC version and global release following in early 2024. The global version closed in March 2025 after roughly one year of service. The November 25 closure completes the game's worldwide end of service.

Koei Tecmo cited difficulty maintaining a service that would satisfy players. The shutdown does not affect the upcoming offline title **Atelier Resleriana: The Red Alchemist & The White Guardian**.

## Resonance of Fate Enhanced Remaster Launches on PC

**Tri-Ace** and **Sega** quietly released **Resonance of Fate ENHANCED REMASTER** on **PC via Steam on October 29, 2026** as a stealth launch. The remaster offers native **4K, HDR, and 120fps** support and adds a **2× game speed** option.

On PC, existing owners of the *Resonance of Fate 4K/HD Edition* can purchase the enhanced upgrade as a **$9.99 DLC add-on**. New players purchase both together. The original game received no prior announcement or marketing for the remaster, making this one of the more surprising JRPG launches of the year.

Notably, the character **Leanne's** official X gimmick account — dormant for eight years — reactivated alongside the announcement.

## Dungeon Antiqua ZERO Announced — SaGa-Inspired Prequel Targets 2027

The **Dungeon Antiqua** series returns with a prequel, **Dungeon Antiqua ZERO**, announced by developer **Shiromofu Factory** for a 2027 PC launch. The studio describes the new game as inspired by **Final Fantasy II** and **Romancing SaGa** — a shift in tone and mechanical design from the earlier entries.

The original *Dungeon Antiqua* launched on PC in October 2024 drawing on NES-era dungeon design. *Dungeon Antiqua 2* followed in 2026 with an SNES-era JRPG look (console versions arrived August 20). ZERO takes place before either game and features more detailed sprites in the late-SNES Squaresoft style.

Sources: [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/dungeon-antiqua-series-coming-back-with-ff2-and-romancing-saga-inspired-prequel-dungeon-antiqua-zero-set-to-launch-in-2027/)
