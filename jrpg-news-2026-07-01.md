## Final Fantasy X "Eternal Calm" Vinyl Set Launches Today

Square Enix's **FINAL FANTASY X LP Vinyl Set -Eternal Calm-** went on sale today, **July 1, 2026**, timed to Final Fantasy X's 25th anniversary.

**Details:**
- A **2LP set on clear blue colored vinyl**, each record featuring 10 tracks from the game's soundtrack split across two sides — 20 songs total.
- The sleeve features **new artwork by character designer Tetsuya Nomura**, created specifically for the anniversary.
- Purchasers receive a **download code** for Final Fantasy X's full official soundtrack.
- Available through Black Screen Records and select retailers.

The vinyl is one of several FFX 25th anniversary products Square Enix has rolled out, alongside an all-Zanarkand compilation album, art books, plush dolls, and the Dissidia Duellum in-game celebration that kicked off June 30.

## Suikoden Live Vol. 6 Broadcast Airs Today, Covers STAR LEAP Release Plan

Konami's **Suikoden Live Vol. 6** broadcast aired today, **July 1, 2026**, at 4:00 a.m. PT / 7:00 a.m. ET on YouTube, running approximately one hour. The show featured voice actors **Mikoi Sasaki**, **Aimi**, and **Ayasa Itou** as guests.

**Staggered launch plan confirmed:**
Konami reiterated its previously announced platform rollout for **Suikoden STAR LEAP**:
1. **iOS and Android — Japan** (first)
2. **iOS and Android — Global**
3. **Steam — Global**

Development is described as having reached its final stages, and a specific release date was flagged as coming soon. The broadcast is the sixth in Konami's ongoing Suikoden Live series, which has accompanied the franchise's revival alongside the *Suikoden I & II HD Remaster*.

## Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster Comes to Nintendo Switch 2 on July 23

**Square Enix** has confirmed that **Final Fantasy X & X-2 HD Remaster** will launch on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **July 23, 2026** via Nintendo eShop, priced at **$49.99**.

**Key details:**
- A **physical game-key card edition** launches in Japan on **August 27**, priced at 7,480 yen, with a special sleeve featuring the FFX 25th Anniversary logo artwork.
- **Save data from the Nintendo Switch version is not compatible** with the Switch 2 release; an upgrade path between versions has not been announced.
- The package includes both full games — over 100 combined hours of content.
- Pre-orders are now live on the Nintendo eShop.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20611-final-fantasy-x-x-2-hd-remaster-switch-2-release-date) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/final-fantasy-x-x-2-hd-remaster-coming-to-switch-2-on-july-23) · [Nova Crystallis](https://novacrystallis.com/2026/06/final-fantasy-x-x-2-hd-remaster-comes-to-nintendo-switch-2-on-july-23-2026/) · [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/61636-final-fantasy-x-x-2-hd-remaster-heads-to-switch-2-july-23rd-2026)

## Dark Deity III Announced as the Trilogy's Final Chapter

Publisher **indie.io** and developer **Sword & Axe** have announced **Dark Deity III**, the concluding chapter of the Dark Deity tactical RPG trilogy, for **PC via Steam**. A release date has not been set.

**Story:**
The game follows **Gwyn** and **Arthur Sildairan**, heirs to the Eternal Order, leading separate campaigns — Gwyn commanding a military force hunting death cults spreading across Terrazael, and Arthur leading scholars into the afterlife to uncover the truth behind the gods themselves.

**Gameplay:**
- **28 recruitable units**, **74 classes**, **100+ weapons**, and **60+ build-defining rings**
- Colorful, impressively animated **pixel art** in the style of Game Boy Advance Fire Emblem entries
- Extensive replayability through an "RPG sandbox" structure with meaningful army-building choices

The game was revealed at the IGN Live Indie Loop showcase.

Sources: [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/16/strategy-rpg-dark-deity-3-announced/) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/dark-deity-iii-announced-for-pc) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/dark-deity-3-announced-pc-steam/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20580-dark-deity-3-announced-for-pc-steam)

## Expeditions: Samurai Announced for Summer 2026 Early Access on PC

**THQ Nordic** and developer **Campfire Cabal** have announced **Expeditions: Samurai**, a new entry in the Expeditions series of historical turn-based tactical RPGs, targeting a **Summer 2026 Early Access launch on PC**. A full release is planned for Q1 2027.

**Setting and story:**
- Feudal Japan, **1600 A.D.** — warlords clash, alliances crumble, and players arrive as leader of a crew of privateers aboard the Dutch frigate *De Albatros*.
- A branching story shaped by life-or-death decisions, with **eight recruitable companions** whose relationships can develop into friendship, rivalry, or romance.

**Gameplay features:**
- Turn-based combat blending simultaneous actions, stealth, gadgets, ambushes, and a mixed arsenal of pirate and Japanese weaponry
- New series additions: stealth, environmental interactions, dynamic character portraits, full controller support, and **co-op multiplayer**

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20574-expeditions-samurai-announced-for-summer-2026-early-access-release-on-pc) · [THQ Nordic](https://thqnordic.com/news/can-you-samurai-renowned-expeditions-series-heads-to-feudal-japan-in-a-new-turn-based-rpg) · [Hermit Gamer](https://www.hermitgamer.com/thq-nordic-announces-expeditions-samurai-a-tactical-rpg-set-in-feudal-japan/)
