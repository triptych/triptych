# JRPG News — May 26, 2026

## LumenTale: Memories of Trey Launches Today on PC and Nintendo Switch

**Team17** and developer **Beehive Studios** release their creature-collector JRPG **LumenTale: Memories of Trey** today on **Steam** and **Nintendo Switch**. The game invites players to explore the Italian-inspired region of Talea, catching over **140 Animon** across 13 elemental types. LumenTale differentiates itself from genre peers by steering into traditional JRPG territory: the protagonist has their own defined personality and story goals rather than being a silent cipher.

Combat supports both **1v1** and **4v4** party battles, and the game layers in cooking, crafting, and home furnishing alongside the core monster-catching loop. The project originated as a **Kickstarter campaign** in early 2023, raising €160,948. Early reviews from outlets including Dual Shockers and Boss Rush Net describe it as "mostly the monster collector you need," praising its worldbuilding depth while noting some rough edges. It is available now on Steam and the Nintendo Switch eShop.

## Persona 5: The Phantom X Version 4.1 — Persona 3 Reload Crossover Now Live

**Atlus** and **Black Wings Game Studio** launched **Persona 5: The Phantom X Version 4.1** on May 20, bringing a full story crossover with **Persona 3 Reload** to the gacha RPG. **Makoto Yuki** — the protagonist of Persona 3 Reload — makes his debut in the game as a top-tier **5-Star Fire-attribute Assassin** focused on offense, available through the Most Wanted Phantom Thief Contract event running until **June 25, 2026**.

The crossover incorporates familiar Persona 3 locations such as **Paulownia Mall in Tatsumi Port Island**, and a special paid costume for Makoto Yuki is available alongside additional digital items. A 1st Anniversary countdown campaign also launched simultaneously: log in for 7 days during the event period to earn **5 Platinum Tickets** and **5 Platinum Milicoins**. The global version of the update includes some character adjustments relative to the original Chinese release, detailed by Siliconera.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Gets Encouraging Development Update

Nintendo has provided a positive update on **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave**, the upcoming **Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive** strategy RPG and the eighteenth mainline entry in the series. While a specific release date remains unconfirmed within the 2026 window, the update signals that development is progressing well — welcome news given how little has been shown since the game's September 2025 announcement.

The game is set in an arid region centered on a massive colosseum, where a gladiatorial event called the **Heroic Games** is hosted by a figure called the **Divine Sovereign**, who promises to grant the wishes of victors. The reveal trailer featured a character resembling an older Sothis from *Three Houses*, fueling speculation about how the game connects to the Fódlan continuity. Fortune's Weave is currently one of the most-anticipated Switch 2 titles.
