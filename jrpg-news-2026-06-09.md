# JRPG News — June 9, 2026

## Top Story: Nintendo Direct June 2026 — JRPG Overload

Today's Nintendo Direct was an exceptional show for JRPG fans, delivering release dates, a massive new Xenoblade reveal, and the most substantial Kingdom Hearts 4 footage in years. Highlights below.

---

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — September 17, 2026

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** locked in a release date of **September 17, 2026**, exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2. The Direct also revealed a special edition, the **Dagdan Collection**, which includes an artbook, map poster, art cards, and a steelbook case alongside the game card.

The game is the eighteenth mainline Fire Emblem entry. Trailers have shown Sothis from *Three Houses* appearing in a seemingly connective role, though Intelligent Systems has not confirmed a direct story link.

## Xenoblade Chronicles Genesis — 2027, Switch 2 Exclusive

Monolith Soft revealed **Xenoblade Chronicles Genesis**, a full mainline entry set in an entirely new world called **Leukos** with a new cast and a fresh lore framework. The game centers on an academy where students called **Vesselai** can channel elemental Anima powers (water, fire, wind) through objects called Anima Crystones. Nintendo showed the opening minutes of the game in-Direct — open-world vistas, mounts, and real-time/strategy-hybrid combat all briefly visible.

Genesis is a deliberate clean break from the Klaus Trilogy narrative concluded in Xenoblade 3, positioning Switch 2 as the start of a new Xenoblade era.

**Also announced:** Switch 2 editions of the original trilogy are rolling out — **Xenoblade Chronicles 1** Switch 2 edition is available now (today), **Xenoblade 2** arrives July 30, and **Xenoblade 3** on December 3. All three run at 60fps / 4K in TV mode, full HD in handheld.

Sources: [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/xenoblade-chronicles/xenoblade-chronicles-genesis-is-coming-in-2027-and-it-looks-very-different-to-monolith-softs-last-jrpg-trilogy/) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/xenoblade-genesis-marks-a-new-beginning-for-xenoblade-chronicles-2000704371) · [NintendoEverything](https://nintendoeverything.com/xenoblade-genesis-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2/) · [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/xenoblade-genesis-revealed-as-switch-2s-next-major-rpg-for-2027-3374013/)

## Kingdom Hearts 4 — Switch 2 Confirmed, Gameplay Revealed

**Kingdom Hearts 4** re-emerged at the Nintendo Direct with the most gameplay footage since the game's 2022 tease. The new trailer shows Sora in a rain-soaked modern urban setting resembling Tokyo's Shibuya district, fighting Kaiju-scale enemies alongside Donald, Goofy, and characters from Organization XIII — the first time supporting cast have been seen in-engine.

Square Enix confirmed the game is coming to **Nintendo Switch 2** at launch (no release date given). Alongside it, proper native Switch 2 ports of the Kingdom Hearts back catalog were announced, replacing the original Switch's cloud-only streaming versions. A free demo of **Kingdom Hearts III**'s Toy Story world is available on the eShop now.

Sources: [The Gamer](https://www.thegamer.com/kingdom-hearts-4-nintendo-direct-2026-reveal-trailer-announcement/) · [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/kingdom-hearts-4-gets-a-surprise-look-at-nintendo-direct-2026/) · [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/kingdom-hearts-4-trailer-nintendo-direct-june-2026/) · [Fantasy Land News](https://www.fantasylandnews.com/2026/06/09/kingdom-hearts-4-nintendo-switch-2-gameplay-reveal/)

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World — December 3, 2026

The Nintendo Direct confirmed **December 3, 2026** as the release date for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World**, coming to PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch, Switch 2, and PC. The game stars **Bianca and Nera**, the dual love interests from *Dragon Quest V: Hand of the Heavenly Bride*, as dual protagonists — a compelling hook for series fans. Dragon Quest creator Yuji Horii has stated he hopes to ship it before Dragon Quest XII.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio — Coming to Switch 2 November 12, 2026

Atlus confirmed **Metaphor: ReFantazio** is launching on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **November 12, 2026**, giving Switch 2 owners access to one of the best-reviewed RPGs of the past two years. The packaged version will ship with a game key card rather than a traditional cartridge.
