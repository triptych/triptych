## Atlus Says Persona 6 Is Not an Endpoint, Commits to Shorter Gaps Between Persona Games

In a developer interview published November 22, **Atlus** addressed fan concerns about the long wait between mainline *Persona* entries. Studio leadership confirmed that **Persona 6** is not intended to be a capstone for the series and that the team plans to reduce the gap between future Persona releases following lessons learned from the extended development cycle of *Persona 5* and beyond.

The statement stops short of a specific timeline, and further details on *Persona 6* have been kept under wraps pending the February 2027 launch of **Persona 4 Revival** — Atlus P-Studio director Kazuhisa Wada previously said the studio would not share more *Persona 6* information until after *P4 Revival* ships.

## Xenoblade Chronicles Genesis Reaches New High on Famitsu's Most Wanted Chart

**Xenoblade Chronicles Genesis** climbed to its highest position yet on **Famitsu's weekly reader most-wanted chart** as of the week ending November 23, 2026. The Switch 2-exclusive entry from **Monolith Soft** has been a consistent presence on the chart since debuting at #24 in early July (99 votes), rising to #8 in August with 204 votes. This week's report marks its strongest showing to date.

No new gameplay details or release window update accompanied the chart movement. *Xenoblade Chronicles Genesis* remains slated for **Nintendo Switch 2 in 2027**.

## Professor Layton and the New World of Steam Launches December 10

**Level-5** confirmed during the **September 9 Nintendo Direct** that **Professor Layton and the New World of Steam** will release on **December 10, 2026**, for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC**. The game had been announced back in February 2023 and was previously planned for 2025 before being pushed back.

This is the first mainline *Professor Layton* game since *Layton's Mystery Journey: Katrielle and the Millionaires' Conspiracy* in 2017. Level-5 has not detailed whether the Switch 2 version includes technical enhancements beyond those present on base Switch.

Sources: [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-09-09/professor-layton-and-the-new-world-of-steam-game-trailer-reveals-december-10-release/.241569) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/09/09/professor-layton-and-the-new-world-of-steam-arrives-this-december) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/professor-layton-and-the-new-world-of-steam-everything-we-know-so-far) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/09/11/professor-layton-new-world-dec-10th/)

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Servers Shut Down Tomorrow — Full Series Now Offline

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** will shut down the Japanese servers for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, one day away. With the global version having already ended service in March 2025, tomorrow marks the complete shutdown of the game worldwide.

The title launched in Japan in September 2023 and globally in January 2024. The global version closed after just 14 months. The Japanese server ran approximately three years before closing ahead of its third anniversary. Players wishing to preserve saves or screenshots should do so before the cutoff.

Sources: [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25) · [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/jrpg/one-of-the-oldest-ongoing-jrpg-series-adds-its-divisive-gacha-spinoff-to-the-live-service-graveyard-after-barely-a-year/)

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World — Preview Roundup Ahead of December 3 Launch

**Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** launches in nine days on **December 3, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. A roundup of recent hands-on previews paints an encouraging but familiar picture:

- **RPG Site** found the game charming and distinctly *Dragon Quest* in feel, but felt it offers little beyond what other monster-collecting games already do. Verdict: a solid choice for DQ fans wanting Pokémon-style gameplay in the series' aesthetic.
- **Gamer Guides** praised the monster synthesis system as "incredibly robust," noting the main caveat is the field-exploration perspective, which won't suit everyone.
- **Nintendo World Report** highlighted a visual upgrade over prior entries and found the four active / four reserve party system more intuitive than earlier *Monsters* games.
- **Shacknews** (from their Gamescom preview) described the narrative as a "tale of hope and despair" and found the world-building more developed than expected for the sub-series.

The standard edition is priced at **$59.99**. No day-one patch details have been announced.

Sources: [RPG Site preview](https://www.rpgsite.net/preview/21317-dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-offers-up-a-blend-of-classic-charm-and-familiar-gameplay) · [Nintendo World Report](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/preview/77099) · [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150638/dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-gamescom-2026-preview) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/preview/2026/09/29/as-the-world-turns)

## Xenoblade Chronicles 3 Switch 2 Edition Launches December 3 — New Hero, 4K/60fps, $9.99 Upgrade

**Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** arrives on the same day as Dragon Quest Monsters — **December 3, 2026** — exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2. Monolith Soft has detailed what the edition adds beyond technical improvements:

- **4K resolution** in TV mode / improved performance at **60 fps** in both modes
- A new **defensive-battle mode** (details on mechanics not yet disclosed)
- A **new Hero and accompanying Hero Quest** exclusive to the Switch 2 Edition

Owners of the original Nintendo Switch version of *Xenoblade Chronicles 3* can purchase an **upgrade pack for $9.99 / £7.99**. The full standalone edition is priced at **$69.99**. No review embargo information has been released yet.

## Dual Orb and Dual Orb II Coming to Switch as Mebius Dual Orb Collection

**Mebius Dual Orb Collection** was announced for **Nintendo Switch**, bringing two obscure **Super Famicom JRPGs** — *Dual Orb* (1992) and *Dual Orb II* (1994) — to modern hardware for the first time. The collection is set for a **March 18, 2027** launch in Japan.

Both games were originally published by I'MAX and never received Western releases. No localization announcement has been made alongside the collection reveal. This follows a broader trend of Japanese publishers reviving SFC-era RPGs for Switch.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/genres/rpg/jrpg) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/)
