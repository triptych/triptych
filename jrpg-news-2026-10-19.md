## Final Fantasy Resonance Launches Thursday — Chapter 1 Demo Available Now

**Square Enix** and co-developer **Lancarse** release **Final Fantasy Resonance** on **October 22, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Xbox Series). The game is priced at **$49.99** for the standard edition.

Resonance is the series' first full **HD-2D** entry and adapts the opening story arc of the mobile title *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* into a standalone turn-based RPG set in the world of **Lapis**. A **free Chapter 1 demo** has been available since early September, and save data from the demo carries over to the full game.

Square Enix is offering **early purchase bonus items** for players who buy by November 5, 2026. A **four-CD original soundtrack release** is planned for October 28 in Japan, with pre-orders open there now.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Expansion Pass Announced, Wave 1 Out Now

Nintendo revealed the **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Expansion Pass** on **October 14–15, 2026**, priced at **$34.99** (a bundle with the base game runs $104.99 on the Nintendo eShop). The three waves will not be sold separately.

**Wave 1** is available immediately and adds four **prototype weapons modeled after the Heroes' Relics from *Fire Emblem: Three Houses***:
- **Cai** receives Dimitri's spear
- **Theodora** receives Claude's bow and arrow
- **Leda** receives Edelgard's axe
- **Dietrich** receives Byleth's sword

**Wave 2** arrives **Holiday 2026** and adds new outfits for the four main protagonists plus divine aid from three new allies: **Aurora, Smyrnos, and Jurah**.

**Wave 3** (2027, date TBA) brings new stories featuring **Bertrand, Talimun, Orchel, and Anatolia**, along with new allies and classes. A free **Version 1.1.0 patch** accompanying the announcement adds Expansion Pass support to the base game.

## Kingdom Hearts HD Collections Now Live Natively on Switch 2

The **Kingdom Hearts HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX**, **HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue**, and **Kingdom Hearts III + Re Mind** collections launched **natively on Nintendo Switch 2 on October 8, 2026**. The three titles are available individually or as the **Collection [I~III]** bundle at **$74.99 USD**.

This marks the first time the Kingdom Hearts series has run locally on a Nintendo platform — the previous Switch versions were cloud-streamed and will stop working on **June 9, 2027** (they are no longer sold on the eShop). Players who owned the cloud versions are eligible for a **50% discount** on the native editions; eligibility may vary by region.

Square Enix confirmed that **Kingdom Hearts 4** will also launch on Switch 2, though no release date has been announced.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Reviews: Metacritic 76 / OpenCritic 77

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** launched **October 15–16, 2026** for Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam (also PS4, PS5, and Xbox Series), receiving a broadly positive critical reception with a **Metacritic score of 76** and an **OpenCritic average of 77** ("Strong").

Standout highlights from reviews:
- **Noisy Pixel** gave it **8.5/10**, praising the combat and presentation.
- **Hardcore Gamer** called it an easy recommendation for JRPG fans and specifically noted that this is the **first official English release of the game to include the camping skits**, which were removed from prior localizations.
- **Siliconera** described it as a much-needed, well-handled remaster.

Common criticisms center on **mandatory minigames** (particularly a mandatory raft-controlling segment early in the game) and pacing habits that feel dated by modern JRPG standards.

Sources: [RPGFan Review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/tales-of-eternia-remastered/) · [Noisy Pixel Review](https://noisypixel.net/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review/) · [Shacknews Review](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151003/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review-score) · [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/21435/tales-of-eternia-remastered) · [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/tales-of-eternia-remastered/)

## Kotaro Uchikoshi Founds New Studio CRASH KEYS After 18-Month Project Cancellation

**Kōtarō Uchikoshi** — creator of the *Zero Escape* series and the *AI: The Somnium Files* series — publicly announced his new game studio, **CRASH KEYS, Inc.**, on **October 15, 2026**. The company was formally established in **March 2025** and is headquartered in Shibuya, Tokyo.

Uchikoshi revealed the studio earlier than originally planned (he had targeted a 2027 announcement) because **an unnamed game his team had spent approximately 18 months developing was suddenly cancelled** by its publisher. He did not name the project, the publisher, or the reason behind the cancellation.

Founding members include writers **Akira Mine** (*The Hundred Line: Last Defense Academy*, *Danganronpa 2×2*) and **Muri Sasaki**. CRASH KEYS' business scope spans **game development, anime series composition, and scriptwriting**. The studio is currently seeking investment partners, and no projects have been announced yet.

## Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga Announced for Switch 2, Switch, and PC in 2027

**Taito** and Western publisher **Clear River Games** announced **Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga** during the **PC Gaming Show Tokyo Direct at TGS 2026** in September. The collection bundles both SNES-era *Lufia* games and is planned for **2027** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (no Xbox version announced).
