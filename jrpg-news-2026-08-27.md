## Brigandine: Abyss Launches on Switch 2 and PC

**Matrix Corporation**'s **Brigandine: Abyss** is out today — **August 27** — on **Nintendo Switch 2** (and PC via Steam, which went live August 26). Published in the West by **NIS America**, the game is a full-blown successor to *Brigandine: The Legend of Runersia* (2020) and the original PlayStation strategy-RPG series from the late 1990s.

The tactical RPG features **six full story campaigns** — one for each of the game's major nations — alongside a standalone **Missions Mode** that lets players experience all **twenty-four factions** without committing to a full campaign. Each faction fields different units, commanders, and magical creatures, with the typical Brigandine loop of capturing and holding regions using armies of monsters and knights persisting from earlier entries.

A physical **Deluxe Edition** in North America and Europe for Switch 2 includes a mini art book and digital soundtrack.

## Neptunia Unlimited Launches in Japan Today

**Compile Heart**'s **Neptunia Unlimited** — the franchise's **first new mainline entry in over a decade** — releases today in Japan across **Nintendo Switch**, **Nintendo Switch 2**, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5. A Western release has not yet been formally announced, though Compile Heart has confirmed global interest.

The game introduces brand-new characters alongside the returning CPU goddesses and leans into aerial combat as a core mechanic — a significant departure from the series' grid-based and turn-based systems. The tone retains the series' trademark absurdist humor while introducing a more serious narrative thread around the game's new antagonists.

Multiple physical editions were produced for the Japan launch, including a premium **Galaxy Edition**.

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — Gamescom "The Planet's Crisis" Trailer Confirms Spring 2027 Window

**Square Enix** brought the **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** gameplay trailer to **Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026** on August 25, and director **Naoki Hamaguchi** appeared live to introduce it. The trailer — titled **"The Planet's Crisis"** — is the most expansive look yet at the concluding chapter of the *FF VII Remake* trilogy.

Key new details from the trailer:

- **The entire Planet is explorable** via the **Highwind airship**. Players can drop into any region by parachute, with open-world traversal spanning previously unseen continents and sea zones.
- **Three Weapon battles** are shown — **Ruby**, **Emerald**, and **Ultima Weapons** — each with distinct arena types (land, underwater, aerial).
- **Typhon** is confirmed as a new summon.
- **Cloud and Tifa** are shown in their new **FITS**, the power-amplifying combat suits teased since the earlier FFVII Revelation reveal.
- A **grappling hook** and **Chocobo mounts** appear as traversal tools.

The game is confirmed for **Spring 2027** on **Nintendo Switch 2**, PC, and other platforms. No specific release date was given.

## The End of Allegoria Announced — Tactical JRPG Sequel for Switch 2 and PC in 2027

**Button Factory Games** and publisher **CobraTekku Games** revealed **The End of Allegoria** at **Gamescom 2026**, confirming it for **Nintendo Switch 2** and **PC via Steam** in **2027**. It is a direct sequel to 2024's *The Edge of Allegoria*, a turn-based RPG.

The sequel shifts the original's combat into a **full party-based tactical RPG** system. Key features announced:

- A **class system with 50+ classes** spread across three tiers
- The **Mastery System** from the original returns, now expanded to work with the new tactical framework
- **300+ new levels** spread across a revamped open world built on familiar geography from the original game, visually overhauled and newly interconnected
- **A new story** set many years after *The Edge of Allegoria*, depicting a Wasteland deity-triggered collapse that gives all humans supernatural abilities, fracturing society into violent factions

Playable demos are available at Gamescom 2026 in Cologne through August 30.

## HoYoverse Reveals Nodusfall — Dark Fantasy Co-Op RPG at Gamescom

**HoYoverse** surprised Gamescom Opening Night Live with the world premiere of **Nodusfall**, a dark fantasy co-op RPG developed in **Unreal Engine 5** — a deliberate departure from the anime-inflected visual style of *Genshin Impact*, *Honkai: Star Rail*, and *Zenless Zone Zero*.

In Nodusfall, players are **Weavers** — warriors operating in a world where divine authority has collapsed. The setting draws from mythology across both Eastern and Western traditions. Gameplay is built around **team-based boss encounters**, with Weavers wielding medieval weaponry (greatswords, bows, one-handed swords) alongside **supernatural thread-like abilities** used to grapple enemies, close distances rapidly, and coordinate group attacks against massive creatures.

No platforms or release date were announced. **Pre-registration is now open** on the official site.
