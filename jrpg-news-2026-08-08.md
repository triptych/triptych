## Suikoden: STAR LEAP Launches in Japan — First New Suikoden in 14 Years

**Konami** released **Suikoden: STAR LEAP** on iOS and Android in Japan on **August 7, 2026**, marking the first all-new title in the *Suikoden* franchise in 14 years.

STAR LEAP is a **free-to-play mobile tactical turn-based JRPG** set in Solar Year 453, slotting into the series timeline between *Suikoden V* and the original *Suikoden I*. The story opens in a village east of the Scarlet Moon Empire, where the hero — the chief's son — returns from his first hunt to find the village under attack. He sets out with allies **Hisui**, **Shirin**, and **Shapur** to restore peace. The game features **108 new Stars of Destiny** to recruit over the course of the adventure, honoring the franchise's signature mechanic.

A global release on **PC (Steam), iOS, and Android** is planned for a later date, though no window has been announced.

## Pokémon Pokopia — Version 2.0.0 and Bubbly Basin DLC Arrive

**The Pokémon Company** launched both the free **Version 2.0.0 update** and the first paid chapter of the **Expansion Pass** for **Pokémon Pokopia** on **August 5, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2**.

The free 2.0.0 update adds **Dive**, a new overworld ability that lets all players explore underwater environments without purchasing DLC. To unlock Dive, players must complete the "Raise the environment level!" request at Bleak Beach and already know both Jump and Surf.

The paid **Bubbly Basin DLC** (Part 1 of the Expansion Pass, priced at **$34.99** for all three parts) expands on Dive by opening the new **Bubbly Basin** town and adding many previously unavailable Pokémon to the game. Abilities such as Rollout, Rototiller, Rock Smash, and Leafage can now be used underwater. Parts 2 and 3 are expected in "late 2026" and 2027, respectively.

## Final Fantasy XIV Now Live on Nintendo Switch 2

**Final Fantasy XIV** officially launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**, completing the MMO's long-awaited console expansion to Nintendo hardware.

The Switch 2 version launched simultaneously worldwide at **3:00 AM PDT / 10:00 AM UTC** and carries a **117 GB download size**. Four editions are available: the **Starter Edition** ($19.99), **Complete Edition** ($59.99, pre-order price $41.99), and **Complete Collector's Edition** ($139.99). A **Nintendo Life extended gameplay showcase video** went live the same week, walking new players through the Switch 2-specific interface and Joy-Con controls.

A **one-month subscription waiver** is in effect for new Switch 2 accounts to support server stability during the launch period. The Switch 2 version runs on a **separate subscription** — existing PC or PlayStation subscribers must purchase a new platform licence, though a **50% discount** applies to existing active accounts. **Nintendo Switch Online is not required** to play. Full cross-play is supported across Switch 2, PC, PlayStation, and other platforms.

## Beast of Reincarnation — Game Freak's First Non-Pokémon JRPG Launches

**Game Freak** released its debut non-Pokémon JRPG, **Beast of Reincarnation**, on **August 4, 2026** for **PC via Steam** (as well as PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series). The game is published by **Fictions** and directed by **Kota Furushima**, a veteran of multiple Pokémon titles who previously worked on the series' battle system and audio.

*Beast of Reincarnation* is described as a **"one-person, one-dog action RPG"** set in post-apocalyptic Japan in the year **4026**, where humanity is on the brink of extinction and the world is ruled by monsters. Players control **Emma**, who wields blight-controlling sword abilities, alongside her dog **Koo**, whose commands provide additional tactical options in real-time combat.

The game launched at **00:00 UTC on August 4** simultaneously worldwide, and the PlayStation.Blog published a hands-on preview just ahead of launch highlighting the game's **parry-centric combat** and Emma and Koo's bond as its emotional core.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword — "Severing Fates" Trailer Highlights Shrine Location and New Boss

**Capcom** released a new pre-launch trailer for **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** this week, titled **"Severing Fates."** The two-minute video spotlights one of the game's real-world locations: **Yasui Konpiragu Shrine** in Kyoto, known in reality as a sacred site for severing unwanted ties. In the game, the shrine has been corrupted by a **Genma called Rasho-gan**, who twists pilgrims' deepest wishes and physically warps their bodies to harvest for his own purposes.

A **playable demo** is currently available on all confirmed platforms except Nintendo Switch 2.

*Onimusha: Way of the Sword* releases on **September 4, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, Microsoft Store)**, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series.

## Blue Reflection Quartet — Reviews Round-Up After July 30 Launch

**KOEI TECMO** and **Gust**'s **BLUE REFLECTION Quartet** launched on **July 30, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC (Steam)** (also PlayStation 5). The collection bundles all four entries in the franchise: the original **BLUE REFLECTION**, **BLUE REFLECTION: Second Light**, the anime series **BLUE REFLECTION RAY**, and the mobile game **BLUE REFLECTION SUN**.

Critical reception has been generally favorable. Based on **8 critic reviews**, the game holds a **77 on Metacritic**. Final Weapon awarded it **4/5**, calling it "a magical girl JRPG collection focused on friendship, personal growth, and heartfelt storytelling." RPG Site noted that the collection "offers tremendous value and a great way to start the series, but some parts leave a lot to be desired." The most cited weakness across reviews is BLUE REFLECTION SUN, the mobile entry, with Cubed3 summing up the consensus: "Three amazing games and one poor game to drag it down."

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Promotional Trailer Released Ahead of September 17 Launch

**Nihon Falcom** and publisher **GungHo Online Entertainment America** released a new **promotional trailer** for **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** on **August 7, 2026**, ahead of the game's worldwide release on **September 17**.

The three-minute trailer showcases the scope of the full 3D remake of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC*, following Senior Bracer **Estelle Bright** across the Kingdom of Liberl as she searches for her adoptive brother **Joshua** while confronting the machinations of the secret society **Ouroboros**. The game picks up immediately after the events of *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter*.

The remake features updated 3D visuals, reorchestrated music, quality-of-life improvements, and **full English and Japanese voice acting**. New cast additions include **Cassandra Lee Morris** (Anelace Elfead), **Ray Chase** (Kevin Graham), **Liam O'Brien** (Georg Weissmann), **Patrick Seitz** (Mueller Vander), and **Stephen Fu** (Leonhardt). Players with *1st Chapter* clear data receive bonus in-game items at the start. Mini-games including fishing, cooking, and poker carry over from the first remake.

*Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter* launches September 17, 2026 on **PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2**, and PlayStation 5. Pricing is **$59.99** (standard) / **$64.99** (Switch 2).

## Nioh 3: Hell Rising DLC Launches August 19

**Koei Tecmo** and **Team Ninja** announced that **Nioh 3**'s first paid DLC, titled **"Hell Rising,"** will release on **August 19, 2026** for **PC (Steam)** and PlayStation 5.

The DLC sends protagonist **Takechiyo** to a new stage set in **1651 Edo (Keian era)**, where the Crucible has taken hold. He is joined by one-eyed swordsman **Yagyu Jubei** and Spanish envoy **Rodrigo** as they battle new Yokai and uncover fresh mysteries. New additions include **hoko** and **shield** weapon types — the first new weapon classes added to the game since launch — plus a new **"Enlightened One's Journey"** difficulty tier, customisable challenge battles, and additional skills.

Hell Rising is available separately or as part of the **Season Pass**, which also includes a second DLC currently expected in **February 2027** under the title **"Bloody Insurrection."**
