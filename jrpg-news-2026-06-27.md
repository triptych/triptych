## Monster Hunter Stories 3: Twisted Reflection — "Additional Side Story: Rudy" DLC and Free Royal Monsters Update Out Now

Capcom released two pieces of new content for **Monster Hunter Stories 3: Twisted Reflection** on June 26, available now on Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam.

**Additional Side Story: Rudy** ($9.99 / included in Deluxe and Premium Deluxe Editions):
- Rudy, whose family has served as **Azurian Royal Palicoes** for generations, stumbles into a strange situation that brings him face-to-face with **Navirou** — the legendary Felyne who saved the world in the original *Monster Hunter Stories*.
- The DLC explores the **origins of the Royal Felyne lineage** and the ancestors who came before Rudy.
- Players will face **Nergigante** and other Elder Dragons as part of the side story.

**Free "Royal Monsters" Update** (available to all players):
- Adds a higher difficulty tier to the **Final Battle**; clearing it on Hard unlocks **Royal Monster** variants of nearly every creature in the game, including Invasive Monsters and Calamitous Elder Dragons.
- Royal Monsters represent the highest-challenge post-game loop for players who have finished the main campaign.

Monster Hunter Stories 3 launched earlier in 2026 to strong critical reception. This DLC deepens the game's lore by bridging the Azurian Royal Palico mythology with the cast of the original Stories title in a way the base game only gestured at.

## Muramasa: Revenant Blades — Vanillaware and Marvelous Announce First-Ever PC Release

**Vanillaware** and **Marvelous** announced **Muramasa: Revenant Blades** for **PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** in **early 2027** during the Nintendo Direct earlier this month (June 9). This is a landmark moment for the beloved Japanese studio: Vanillaware, founded in 2002 and responsible for *Odin Sphere*, *Dragon's Crown*, and *13 Sentinels: Aegis Rim*, has **never released a game on PC** in its 23-year history.

Muramasa: Revenant Blades is an expanded and remastered edition of the original **Muramasa: The Demon Blade** (Wii, 2009) and its enhanced port **Muramasa Rebirth** (PS Vita, 2013), which added four DLC character stories. Key changes for the new version:

- Full **4K resolution** support.
- **New combat and weapon systems** not present in prior versions.
- Additional gameplay modes.
- **Full English voice acting** for the first time across any version — plus new localizations in multiple languages.
- All previously released DLC content (the four character stories from Rebirth) expected to be included, though Marvelous has not yet explicitly confirmed the full scope of bundled content.

**Pricing**: Standard edition on PS5 and Nintendo Switch at **$49.99**; Nintendo Switch 2 at **$59.99**. PC pricing has not yet been announced. Physical pre-orders are available through the Marvelous USA Online Store and participating retailers.

Muramasa's two protagonists — **Kisuke**, a wandering swordsman with no memory, and **Momohime**, a princess possessed by a swordsman's vengeful spirit — journey through a mythologized Edo-period Japan rendered in Vanillaware's signature hand-painted 2D art style. The combat is fast, flowing, and sword-heavy, built around a system of spirit blades that heat up and shatter with overuse.

## Inazuma Eleven: Victory Road — Sixth Free Update "Contenders for Glory" Launches July 3

Level-5 has detailed **Contenders for Glory**, the sixth free major update for **Inazuma Eleven: Victory Road**, launching **July 3, 2026**.

Content confirmed for the update:

- **The Victory Road Tournament**: a new competitive event running from **July 3 through July 23**.
- **New difficulty tier** added to Chronicle matches.
- A new trailer detailing the update was released alongside the announcement.

Inazuma Eleven: Victory Road is the latest entry in Level-5's long-running soccer RPG series, currently available on Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam. The game has continued to receive substantial free content post-launch; six major updates is a strong cadence for a single-player-adjacent RPG with online competitive features. The July 3–23 tournament window aligns almost perfectly with Anime Expo week, suggesting Level-5 may be timing player engagement around the convention season.

## Wuthering Waves Version 3.5 and Xbox Series X|S Launch Set for July 10

Kuro Games confirmed that **Wuthering Waves Version 3.5 "Blade of Past Resounds, Lingering Dream Hymns"** launches **July 10, 2026**, simultaneously marking the game's **native launch on Xbox Series X|S** (plus the Microsoft Store on PC).

Major content in Version 3.5:

- **New Region — Mengzhou: Land of Xuanfang**: A large new explorable area divided into four distinct zones — Xuanfang Hold, Xuan Peaks, Fang Peaks, and Yuan Hills — described as shaped by ancient melodies, gathering storms, and lingering echoes.
- **Two new playable Resonators**: **Yangyang: Xuanling** and **Suisui**, both debuting as banner characters.
- **Electro Rover** as a playable option.
- **Denia Hologram** challenge content.
- Three new Echo sets.
- **Reverb Convene rerun banner** for returning characters.
- Summer-themed skins for **Lynae** and **Chisa**.
- **Escape from Duckov** bike collaboration event.
- Events calendar headlined by **Virtual Crisis** and **Tacet Crisis**.

The Xbox Series launch gives the game its final major platform footprint — it was already available on PC (Epic Games Store), iOS, and Android, with a PS5 version having launched in an earlier update. With the Xbox version now live alongside 3.5, Wuthering Waves reaches its full cross-platform target audience ahead of what is shaping up to be a content-heavy second half of 2026.

## Echoes of Aincrad — Second Story Trailer Confirms Iconic SAO Companions as Recruitable Partners

Bandai Namco released a **second story trailer** for **Sword Art Online: Echoes of Aincrad** on June 26, approximately two weeks ahead of the game's **July 10 launch** on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam.

Key reveals from the trailer:

- Players can **recruit established Sword Art Online series characters** as party partners, with four confirmed by name: **Asuna, Silica, Agil, and Lisbeth**.
- The story trailer focuses on mounting tension between **ordinary rookie players and beta testers**, specifically referencing **Diabel's death** and accusations that testers withheld crucial boss attack patterns — a direct adaptation of the anime/light novel's early arc.
- The trailer's dialogue sequence is set to **"Live to Survive" by Aimer**.

The companion reveal is significant: Echoes of Aincrad stars an original protagonist navigating the Aincrad tower dungeon, and the ability to recruit fan-favorite characters from the source material addresses a concern some SAO fans had about playing as an unknown lead. The game's Aincrad Edition (Bandai Namco Store exclusive) is priced at **$149.99** and includes physical and digital extras; the standard edition is **$69.99**.
