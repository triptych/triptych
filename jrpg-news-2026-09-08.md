## Onimusha: Way of the Sword — Reviews Land at 86 Metacritic, Switch 2 Version Scores Highest

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched on **September 4** for Switch 2, PC, PS5, and Xbox Series, and critic reviews are now in.

The game sits at **86 on Metacritic** ("Generally Favorable") and **86 on OpenCritic**, with roughly 95% of critics recommending it. The **Nintendo Switch 2 version scores highest at 89/100** on Metacritic, ahead of the PS5 (86) and PC (83) versions. **IGN** awarded the game a perfect **10/10** — the first such score for the franchise — while **Nintendo Life**, **Nintendo World Report**, and **Nintendo Insider** each gave it **9/10**. **Game Informer**, **GameSpot**, and **PCGamesN** each landed at **8/10**.

The near-universal praise centers on the parry-driven combat and boss fight design, which reviewers call the best the series has ever produced. The sharpest criticism is that the campaign is padded with repetitive side content. Campaign length runs **12 to 25 hours** depending on play style.

## Final Fantasy Resonance Demo Available Now — Chapter 1 (~4–5 Hours), Sephiroth Revealed

**Square Enix** dropped the **Final Fantasy Resonance** demo on **September 3**, available free on **Nintendo Switch**, **Nintendo Switch 2**, and **PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Microsoft Store). The demo covers the opening through the end of **Chapter 1**, with Square Enix estimating **three to five hours** of content. Save data carries over to the full release on **October 22, 2026**.

The September State of Play also confirmed **Sephiroth** appears in the game — how or why has not been revealed, as it is treated as a story beat rather than a crossover gimmick.

**Final Fantasy Resonance** is built around the concept of pushing high-definition pixel art in the HD-2D style to its limits, with a fully command-driven turn-based battle system and a modernized take on the classic job system. Early player impressions have been strongly positive, calling the Chapter 1 demo a compelling opening to what appears to be one of 2026's longest JRPGs.

Sources: [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/09/03/final-fantasy-resonance-ps5-demo-available-today-sephiroth-revealed/) · [Lords of Gaming PC Demo Impressions](https://lordsofgaming.net/2026/09/final-fantasy-resonance-pc-demo-impressions/) · [Guru Gamer](https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/final-fantasy-resonance-demo-gives-classic-turn-based-combat-a-welcome-return-27262) · [Netto's Game Room](https://www.nettosgameroom.com/2026/09/final-fantasy-resonance-demo-available.html)

## Konami Announces Rhapsody in Scarlet — 1920s New York Magick Action-RPG

At the September 2026 **PlayStation State of Play**, **Konami** announced **Rhapsody in Scarlet**, an original action-adventure game coming to **PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Xbox Series; no release date announced).

The game is set in **vibrant 1920s New York City** and follows **Ellie Myers**, a young magus with a love of jazz who comes to New York to pursue a singing career and finds herself drawn into a world of organized magi. Ellie works alongside **Ilya**, leader of an elite organization called the Seekers, and fights accompanied by powerful magical beasts — Sköll, Balhut, and Thunderbird — in real-time combo-driven combat. Gameplay footage shows Ellie unleashing large-scale magical attacks in concert with her beast companions.

More details are expected at a **State of Play Japan** event in the coming weeks.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Final Details Ahead of September 17 Launch

**Nihon Falcom** and **GungHo Online Entertainment America** have released the full English voice cast and a gameplay trailer for **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter**, which launches **September 17** on **Nintendo Switch 2** ($64.99), **Nintendo Switch** ($59.99), **PC via Steam** ($59.99), and PlayStation 5.

The modern rebuild of *Trails in the Sky SC* picks up immediately after the first remake — Estelle searches for Joshua against the backdrop of the looming **Ouroboros** threat. The hybrid combat system returns with new **Crafts**, **S-Crafts**, and the new **Brave Rush** mechanic. Side activities including fishing, poker, and cooking return in full.

A **Golden Wings Collector's Edition** is available for **$274.99** and includes a physical art book, soundtrack, and additional extras.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Final Pre-Release Previews Published, Launches September 17

**Nintendo**'s **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launches **September 17** on **Nintendo Switch 2**, and **Nintendo Life** has published a round-up of the final pre-release preview coverage.

The strategy RPG features **four selectable protagonists** — **Cai, Theodora, Dietrich, and Leda** — each leading a separate route through a story centered on the **Heroic Games**, a tournament run by the enigmatic **Divine Sovereign**. Win the tournament and one wish is granted. Preview coverage has praised the route structure and the way each protagonist recontextualizes the same events from a different vantage point.

The **prologue manga** by **Kazurō Kyō** began running in **Saikyō Jump** and **Jump+** on September 4. A **Special Edition** is available including art cards, a map poster, an art book, a steelbook case, and the game card.

Sources: [Nintendo Life Preview Round-Up](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/08/round-up-the-final-previews-are-in-for-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-on-switch-2) · [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78596-fire-emblem-fortune-s-weave-reveals-release-date-and-introduces-its-main-characters) · [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-guide/)

## Dragon Quest XI S: Definitive Edition — Switch 2 Version Pricing and Details Confirmed

**Square Enix** will release **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition** for **Nintendo Switch 2 on September 24** at **$39.99**. The digital version is available on the Nintendo eShop; the physical edition ships on a **game-key card**.

The Switch 2 version features new optimization modes (prioritize graphics or performance), orchestral score support, 3D/2D toggle, and new story chapters spotlighting party members — features carried over from the original Switch Definitive Edition. File size is **23.4 GB**, significantly larger than the original Switch version's 9.4 GB.

**Save data from the Switch version is not compatible** with the Switch 2 edition, and **no upgrade path or upgrade pack** is planned. Players who own the original Switch version must purchase the Switch 2 edition separately at full price.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/05/dragon-quest-xi-s-echoes-of-an-elusive-age-definitive-edition-coming-to-switch-2-on-september-24) · [Nova Crystallis](https://novacrystallis.com/2026/05/dragon-quest-xi-s-releases-for-nintendo-switch-2-on-september-24-2026/) · [The Sixth Axis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/05/27/dragon-quest-xi-s-is-coming-to-switch-2-in-september-but-theres-no-upgrades-from-switch-1/) · [Nintendo Official Site](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/dragon-quest-xi-s-echoes-of-an-elusive-age-definitive-edition-switch-2/)
