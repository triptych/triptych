## Octopath Traveler & Octopath Traveler II — Switch 2 Editions Launch October 1

**Square Enix** released both **Octopath Traveler** and **Octopath Traveler II** as Nintendo Switch 2 upgrades on **October 1, 2026**.

Both editions run at **1080p and 60 FPS** — a significant leap from the original Switch versions, which targeted 720p and 30fps. The HD-2D visuals benefit noticeably from the sharper resolution, bringing them in line with the PC and other current-gen versions. Nintendo World Report gave the original *Octopath Traveler* a 7.5 and the sequel an 8 in brief review coverage, describing both as "the definitive way to experience these two modern JRPG classics."

One notable caveat: **there is no upgrade path for existing Switch 1 owners**. Players who already own either game on the original Switch must purchase the Switch 2 editions at full price, with no discount or save data transfer supported.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Launches to Near-Universal Praise

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched on **September 17, 2026** exclusively for **Nintendo Switch 2** and has become one of the most acclaimed titles of the year.

The game holds an **89 on Metacritic** across 53 scored reviews, with **13 outlets awarding a perfect score**. IGN gave it a 10/10, writing that it "builds upon its strategic arts in ways I couldn't have anticipated." GameSpot called it "a sprawling masterpiece of a strategy RPG." Gamereactor described it as "undeniably one of the best titles of the year." 25 Years Later praised it as "one war worth fighting four times," a reference to the game's four-protagonist structure that lets players switch between storylines at will.

The game functions as a proper follow-up to *Three Houses*, retaining the social simulation elements between battles alongside a deeply tactical combat system emphasizing flexibility and player expression.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Out Now on Switch and PC

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** launched worldwide on **September 17, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**.

The game adapts the events of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC*, picking up immediately after the first chapter as **Estelle Bright** resumes her search for Joshua. New content includes expanded minigames (fishing, poker), cooking with companions, and additional fan-favorite characters joining as playable party members.

Japanese pre-orders for the Switch and Switch 2 versions included an HD remaster of the original *Trails in the Sky*, a notable bonus for players new to the series.

## Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III] — Native Switch 2 Versions Arrive October 8

**Square Enix** releases **Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III]** for **Nintendo Switch 2** on **October 8, 2026** — marking the first time Nintendo players can run the series natively rather than via cloud streaming.

The bundle packages **Kingdom Hearts HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX**, **Kingdom Hearts HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue**, and **Kingdom Hearts III + Re Mind DLC** in a single release. Save data transfers from the cloud-based Switch 1 versions are supported, letting existing players carry their progress forward.

**Kingdom Hearts 4** has separately been confirmed as a future Switch 2 title.

Sources: [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/06/kingdom-hearts-collection-iiii-releasing-in-october/) · [KH13](https://www.kh13.com/news/kingdom-hearts-collection-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2/) · [My Nintendo News](https://mynintendonews.com/2026/06/09/kingdom-hearts-collection-coming-to-nintendo-switch-2-this-october/) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/games/nintendo-switch-2/kingdom-hearts-collection-iiii) · [Nintendo Official](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/kingdom-hearts-collection-i-iii-switch-2/)

## Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen — New Region, 12 Dungeons, Switch 2 Launch October 9

**Capcom** launches **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** on **October 9, 2026** for **PC via Steam and Nintendo Switch 2** (alongside PS5).

The expansion adds the **Norgan region**, a snowy, abandoned area with **12 new dungeon challenges** spread across the base game's map, along with new character-creation options including additional hairstyles and tattoos. The **Relic Expedition Cycle** mechanic from the original *Dragon's Dogma: Dark Arisen* returns.

The **Switch 2 version** ($49.99) bundles the base *Dragon's Dogma 2* with all Dark Arisen expansion content in a single package. On other platforms the Dark Arisen content is a **$29.99 DLC** for existing owners.

## Final Fantasy Resonance — Chapter 1 Demo Available, Full Game October 22

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy Resonance** hits **Switch, Switch 2, and PC via Steam** on **October 22, 2026** (priced at $49.99). A **free playable demo covering Chapter 1** has been available since early September, with save data carrying over to the full release.

The game is the first mainline **HD-2D Final Fantasy** title, adapting the opening season of *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* into a standalone turn-based RPG. Set in the world of **Lapis**, it follows Rain, Lasswell, and Fina through classic turn-based combat featuring a visible action timeline, stagger mechanics, Limit Bursts, and Espers. Guest appearances from **Cloud Strife** (FFVII) and **Tidus** (FFX) are among the confirmed legacy character "Visions."
