## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Launches With First-Ever Crossplay, Reviews Positive

**Cygames** released **Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok** on July 9 for **PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**, introducing **crossplay** to the franchise for the first time across all platforms.

The expansion adds **The Conflux**, a roguelite mode in which players move through portals of varying battles and minigames; several new playable characters; new quests; and endgame content. The story is minimal by design, functioning primarily as a setup for the new gameplay systems.

Reviews landed in advance of release and placed the expansion in the **86th percentile on OpenCritic**. The consensus is consistent across outlets: Endless Ragnarok rewards dedicated fans of the original with substantial content and a meaningful new loop, while offering limited draw for anyone who bounced off the base game. The crossplay feature drew particular praise for finally unifying the playerbase across hardware.

Sources: [RPGSite Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20857-granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-review) · [Noisy Pixel Review](https://noisypixel.net/granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-review/) · [TechTimes (crossplay)](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319920/20260708/granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-launches-tomorrow-first-ever-crossplay.htm) · [DayOne Review](https://playday.one/2026/07/08/granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-review/)

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation: Highwind Airship, Parachutes, and Submarine Detailed

**Square Enix** revealed the world-traversal systems for **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** on July 10, showing how players will explore the full planet surface in the trilogy's final chapter.

The centrepiece is the **Highwind** airship, now fully pilotable and usable to reach any location on the overworld. To transition from air to ground, party members parachute directly from the deck onto the map in a seamless animation — a first for the Remake trilogy. The game also adds **submarine traversal** for underwater areas, and new **Chocobo mechanics** that integrate with overland movement.

Previously announced locations including **Wutai** and **Rocket Town** were confirmed reachable via the Highwind. The full globe is explorable from the start of the late-game segment. **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** is targeting **Spring 2027** for simultaneous release on PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam and Epic Games Store).

Sources: [My Nintendo News](https://mynintendonews.com/2026/07/10/square-enix-reveals-traversal-methods-in-final-fantasy-vii-revelation/) · [Kotaku — 7 Things We Learned](https://kotaku.com/airships-fresh-fits-and-queens-blood-7-things-we-learned-about-final-fantasy-vii-revelation-2000707110) · [Square Enix Press Hub](https://press.na.square-enix.com/FINAL-FANTASY-VII-REVELATION-ANNOUNCED-EXPERIENCE-THE-JOURNEYS-END-IN-)

## Disgaea Mayhem Launches July 23 — Action RPG Spinoff Ditches Turn-Based Roots

**NIS America** has confirmed **Disgaea Mayhem** for **July 23, 2026** on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. A physical Limited Edition priced at **$99.99** is available for pre-order via NIS America's storefront for Switch, Switch 2, and PS5.

Mayhem is an action RPG spinoff that replaces the series' signature grid-based tactical combat with real-time brawling. Players control **N.A.**, a mercenary hired to recover a flan for Princess Tichelle, switching between **seven weapon styles** on the fly while fighting through hordes of enemies. The shift to action gameplay is a deliberate break from the *Disgaea* formula, with the game developed as a standalone entry rather than a numbered sequel.

## Shironeko Project INFINITY Announced for Switch 2 and Switch

**COLOPL** announced **Shironeko Project INFINITY**, an original console action game based on its long-running *Shironeko Project* mobile game, on July 11. The game is targeting a **2027 release in Japan** for **Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch**, with no Western date announced.

The game adapts the mobile title's cast into a dedicated console action experience themed around "exhilarating action × explosive Character Links that expand infinitely in the midst of battle." A standout system is the **Choice mechanic**, which lets players mid-battle decide to either add a new ally to the party or strengthen an existing one, with different mechanical benefits for each path. **More than 50 playable characters** from the mobile game appear, alongside an original character created exclusively for Infinity.

## Shangri-La Frontier: The Seven Colossi Shows New Trailer on ShanFro Day

**Netmarble** and developer **Netmarble Nexus** broadcast a new trailer for **Shangri-La Frontier: The Seven Colossi** on July 7 as part of a special stream held for **ShanFro Day**, the series' annual fan holiday.

## Arknights: Endfield Version 1.4 "Homecoming" Arrives July 16

**GRYPHLINE** will release **Version 1.4 "Homecoming"** for **Arknights: Endfield** on **July 16** on PC, iOS, and Android, marking the game's **six-month anniversary**. The update is the largest content drop since launch.

Story-wise, Homecoming closes out the **Wuling arc** across two new maps — **Yinglung Pass** and the **North Wuling Exclusion Zone** — with themes centred on return and sacrifice. Two new Operators join the roster: **Arcane**, a six-star featured Operator whose playstyle shifts based on the balance between her Intellect and Will stats (allowing builds as a damage caster or elemental support), and **Liino**, an Electric supporter who cycles between two combat stances to buff allies and heal.

The **AIC factory system** gains a third production state — **Gas** — alongside solid and liquid, enabling new manufacturing chains and new resources including Inergen and Xiragen. A new permanent combat mode, **Echoes of War**, adds rotating stages with rankings and seasonal titles. Anniversary login rewards include **3,000 Oroberyls** and five Basic HH Permits across the first campaign.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/arknights-endfield-version-update-homecoming-launches-july-16) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/arknights-endfield-homecoming-update-july-2026/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/10/arknights-endfield-homecoming-update-announcement-details/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20897-arknights-endfield-homecoming-expansion-launches-on-july-16)

## HorrorVale Coming to Switch and Switch 2

**Electric Airship** will publish **BatWorks Software**'s horror RPG **HorrorVale** on **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2** (alongside PS5, PS4, and Xbox). The PC version launched on Steam in October 2024; the console ports add enhanced visuals, better performance, and new battles.

HorrorVale follows **Alice** as she journeys through an underworld to rescue her zombie dog Scruffy from monsters that have overrun the once-peaceful town of HorrorVale. The game is a traditional turn-based RPG across four acts, featuring **over 30 recruitable party members**, more than 50 hours of content, and puzzle elements throughout.

No release date has been announced for the console versions.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/spooky-rpg-horrorvale-coming-to-ps5-xbox-series-ps4-xbox-one-and-switch) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/rpg-adventure-game-horrorvale-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2-switch/)
