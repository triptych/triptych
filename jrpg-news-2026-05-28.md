# JRPG News — May 28, 2026

## Dragon Quest XII Renamed "Beyond Dreams," Development Restarted

The Dragon Quest 40th Anniversary livestream that aired yesterday delivered the long-awaited update on **Dragon Quest XII** — but it came with a significant caveat. The mainline entry, first announced in 2021 as *Dragon Quest XII: The Flames of Fate*, has been retitled **Dragon Quest XII: Beyond Dreams** following a full development restart under a restructured team. Executive producer **Yosuke Saito** stated that the original version encountered "numerous hurdles" and that the rebuild means the game "will take longer to reach players." Creator **Yuji Horii** accompanied the announcement with in-development footage and a refreshed logo. No release date or window was given.

---

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World Announced

Also revealed during the 40th anniversary stream: **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** (titled *Dragon Quest Monsters 4* in Japan), a new entry in the monster-collecting spin-off series. The game stars dual protagonists **Bianca** and **Nera** from *Dragon Quest V: Hand of the Heavenly Bride*, tasked with restoring life to a desolate world. It is coming to **Nintendo Switch**, **Nintendo Switch 2**, **PlayStation 5**, **Xbox Series X|S**, and **PC** (Steam and Microsoft Store). No release date was set, though Horii noted the team hopes to ship it before *Dragon Quest XII: Beyond Dreams*.

---

## Dragon Quest XI S Gets a Switch 2 Port This September

Rounding out the anniversary announcements, **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age** is coming to **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 24, 2026**. The port brings the acclaimed 2019 RPG to Nintendo's new hardware, giving lapsed players and newcomers a fresh entry point ahead of the new mainline and spin-off releases.

---

## Persona 6 Character Designs and Release Window Leak

Images purportedly sourced from an outsourcing studio have surfaced online showing early character designs for **Persona 6**, sparking intense discussion in the Atlus community. The leaked art, shared by known leaker **Lolilolailo** on Chinese social platform Xiaohongshu (RedNote), depicts what appears to be a **blonde male protagonist** — a series first — alongside a dark-haired female character identified in sheet labels as **Kugabe Neiko / Yasuko**. Additional imagery suggests a mascot companion named **Nilfer** (or Nilfar) and hints that the game is set in **Yokohama**.

The same leaker previously claimed *Persona 6* is feature-complete but has been pushed internally to **2027**, with a **September 2027** launch now being cited across multiple outlets. Atlus has not commented. A separate report notes that a potential *Persona 4* revival project may be contributing to any delay in a formal *Persona 6* announcement.

---

## Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered Launches on Console in Japan

The **PlayStation 5** and **Nintendo Switch 2** versions of **Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered** launched today in Japan, one day after the PC (Steam) global release. Developed by Aquaplus, the game is a turn-based JRPG sequel set prior to *Mask of Deception*. Western console releases have not been dated; the PC version is available now with English, Japanese, and Chinese support.

---

## Stardust: Wish of Witch Releases Today

**Stardust: Wish of Witch**, a classically styled JRPG featuring anime visuals and old-school grid-based combat, launched today. The game leans into traditional JRPG design conventions and is available now on PC.
