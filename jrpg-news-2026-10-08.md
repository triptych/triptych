## Kingdom Hearts HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue — Launches Today on Switch 2, PS5, and Xbox Series

**Square Enix** released **Kingdom Hearts HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue** today, **October 8, 2026**, on **Nintendo Switch 2** (alongside PS5 and Xbox Series X|S). This is the first native — non-cloud — Switch release for this collection.

The package bundles three titles from the lead-up to *Kingdom Hearts III*:

- **Dream Drop Distance HD** — A full HD remaster in which Sora and Riku sit the Mark of Mastery exam in preparation for their confrontation with Master Xehanort.
- **Kingdom Hearts χ Back Cover** — An HD movie exploring the mysterious story of the Foretellers from the series' earliest era.
- **0.2 Birth by Sleep – A fragmentary passage** — A short playable episode that connects directly to *Kingdom Hearts III*.

Owners of the older **Switch cloud version** are eligible for a **50% discount** on the Switch 2 native edition. The cloud versions will remain playable until **June 9, 2027**.

With 2.8 out today, all three Kingdom Hearts HD collections are now available on Switch 2 ahead of the **Kingdom Hearts 4** window expected in 2027.

## Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen — Expansion and Switch 2 Bundle Launch Tomorrow

**Capcom** launches **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** on **October 9, 2026**. The expansion arrives as a **$29.99 DLC** for existing PC, PS5, and Xbox Series owners, and as a bundled **$49.99 Switch 2 edition** that includes the full base game.

New content in the expansion:

- **Norgan region** — An abandoned northern territory where players ally with the enigmatic **Eir** to unravel the secrets of an undying Fallen Dragon.
- **Relic Expedition** — Players unearth and appraise forgotten relics to unlock new weapons and skills.
- **12 Lost Rites Dungeon Challenges** — New structured dungeon runs for endgame players.
- **Character creation additions** — New tattoo and hair options.

A pre-launch patch also introduced a **reusable Eternal Ferrystone** for easier fast travel, additional save slots, and general frame rate improvements across all platforms.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered — Preview Highlights Retranslation and QoL Options

**Bandai Namco** releases **Tales of Eternia Remastered** on **October 16, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2**, and **PC via Steam** (also PS4, PS5). Ahead of launch, RPG Site published a hands-on preview.

The preview praised the **redone localization** as a significant upgrade over the original PSX-era translation, paired with improved font and text-box readability. The game's visual approach is faithful: assets remain largely original, with output resolution increased for modern displays. Players can switch between the remastered and original visual and audio modes at will.

Quality-of-life additions confirmed for the release:

- **Destination markers** to reduce navigation friction
- **Encounter toggle** to disable random enemies
- **High-speed mode** during auto-battle

Tales of Eternia (released as *Tales of Destiny 2* in North America in 2002) follows Reid Hershel and Farah Oersted, who discover Meredy — a girl from the parallel world of Celestia — and must prevent the collision of their two worlds. It is the third mainline Tales game and one of the most-requested for remaster treatment.

Sources: [Bandai Namco Europe](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/tales-of/news/journey-between-two-worlds-begins-tales-of-eternia-remastered-october) · [RPG Site preview](https://www.rpgsite.net/preview/21490-tales-eternia-remastered-seems-to-understand-assignment)

## Final Fantasy Resonance — Last Trailer at TGS; October 22 Launch Confirmed

**Square Enix** showed the final pre-launch trailer for **Final Fantasy Resonance** at **Tokyo Game Show 2026** (September 17–21), confirming the **October 22, 2026** release date on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch**, and **PC via Steam** (also PS5, Xbox Series; Steam version may release October 23 depending on region).

*Final Fantasy Resonance* is the first HD-2D game in the Final Fantasy franchise. It adapts the opening story arc of *Final Fantasy: Brave Exvius* — with all gacha and mobile mechanics removed — into a full-length console RPG. The principal party consists of **Rain, Lasswell, Lid, and Nichol**. The TGS trailer showcased world traversal, airship travel, Chocobo riding, and turn-based combat with what outlets described as Persona-style presentation layered over classic Final Fantasy structure.

The game was announced during the June 2026 Nintendo Direct. A playable demo was available on the TGS show floor.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20619-final-fantasy-resonance-a-new-hd-2d-ff-game-launches-october-22) · [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/final-fantasy-resonance-continues-to-impress-with-new-trailer/) · [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/final-fantasy-resonance-shows-off-its-stunning-visuals-in-new-trailer/)

## Tokyo Game Show 2026 — Notable JRPG Highlights

**Tokyo Game Show 2026** ran **September 17–21** at Makuhari Messe. Key JRPG-relevant announcements and reveals from the show floor:

- **Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories** (Koei Tecmo) — A new trailer confirmed that **Rutger and Lenja** from *Atelier Yumia* appear in the game. It was playable on the booth, with a stage event. Platforms: PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PC.
