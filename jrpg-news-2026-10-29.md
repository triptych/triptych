## Phantom Blade Zero Launches to Strong Reviews and 431K Steam Peak

**S-GAME**'s long-awaited wuxia action RPG **Phantom Blade Zero** launched today, **October 29, 2026**, on **PS5 and PC via Steam**. The game's PC debut hit a staggering **431,541 concurrent players within three hours of launch**, earning a **"Very Positive"** Steam user rating in short order.

Critical response has been broadly strong:

- **Metacritic: ~88** (33+ reviews aggregated)
- **Game Informer: 9.5/10** — "one of the best action games I've ever played"
- **TechRadar:** "one of the year's best action games"
- **Insider Gaming:** "a standout experience"
- **Eurogamer: 6/10** — called out balance and quality issues

The most consistent praise across reviews targets the **weapon design and combat system**. Common criticisms include storytelling, performance issues on high-end hardware (stutter, missing textures, crashes), level design, and boss durability. Critics are split on voice acting. Despite the technical complaints, several outlets are naming it a **GOTY contender**.

Sources: [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/phantom-blade-zero-fourth-best-launch-of-2026/) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/review/phantom-blade-zero/wuxian-wonder-abounds) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/phantom-blade-zero-review) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/phantom-blade-zero-reviews-call-it-an-incredibly-well-tuned-adventure-with-brilliant-weapon-design-2000749708) · [Gaming Bible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/phantom-blade-zero-goty-contender-despite-technical-issues-565260-20261026) · [The Shortcut (roundup)](https://www.theshortcut.com/p/phantom-blade-zero-review-roundup)

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Out Today in North America

**Spike Chunsoft** released **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** in North America today, **October 29, 2026**, for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S**. The PC version launched in Japan on **August 20** via Steam; the Western Steam release is also live today.

The game is a full rework of *Steins;Gate Elite*, featuring smoother pacing, new dialogue, and a **brand-new worldline and ending** not present in any prior release. Voice acting is Japanese-only; text is available in English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.

**Pricing:**
- Standard Edition (physical and digital): **$59.99**
- SteelBook Edition and Digital Deluxe Edition are priced higher

**UK/EU note:** Digital editions are live today in all regions, but **physical Switch 2 and PS5 copies in the UK and EU have been delayed to November 3, 2026** due to a logistics issue.

## Final Fantasy Resonance Launches to Near-Universal Critical Acclaim

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy Resonance** launched on **October 22, 2026** across **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S** at **$49.99** (Digital Deluxe Edition $59.99). The HD-2D title — a reimagining of the narrative from the defunct mobile game *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* — is pulling some of the best review scores of 2026.

Aggregate scores:
- **Metacritic:** 85 (PS5) / 88 (PC) / 82 (Switch 2)
- **Steam user reviews: 97% positive** at launch

Notable individual scores:
- **Famitsu: 36/40** (9/9/9/9)
- **Game Informer: 9/10**
- **Nintendo Life: 8/10** — "a strong return to an older style of Final Fantasy"
- **GameSpot: 7/10**
- **Pocket Tactics:** "a deep, story-rich JRPG"

A free demo remains available on all platforms. Reviewers most frequently highlight the turn-based combat and the HD-2D presentation; mixed reactions to the source narrative are the main point of dissent.

Sources: [Nintendo Life (review)](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/10/the-first-review-for-final-fantasy-resonance-is-in) · [Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/10/final-fantasy-resonance-is-launching-on-xbox-to-extremely-impressive-reviews) · [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/final-fantasy-resonance/review) · [ixbt.games (review roundup)](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/10/21/final-fantasy-resonance-polucila-odni-iz-lucsix-ocenok-v-2026-godu.amp.html) · [PCGamesHardware (Steam reviews)](https://www.pcgameshardware.de/Final-Fantasy-Resonance-Spiel-78908/News/97-Prozent-positive-Bewertungen-1557700/)

## Demons' Night Fever Launches from Disgaea Creator Sohei Niikawa

**Arc System Works** and **Drecom** (published under the **SuperNiche** label) released **Demons' Night Fever** on **October 22, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, PS5, and PC via Steam** — a simultaneous worldwide launch. Creator **Sohei Niikawa**, best known as the father of the Disgaea series, reportedly took out a personal loan to fund the game's completion.

The SRPG's signature **Peon Exploitation System** inverts standard tactics-game logic: rather than protecting your expendable units, players deliberately detonate them to farm "Evil Misdeeds" and grow stronger. God has been deliberately written as a scumbag so players feel no guilt doing evil things, according to developer commentary.

No aggregate review score was available at time of writing, but pre-launch demo impressions were positive about the sacrifice mechanic.

Sources: [Inven Global (launch)](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27565/demons-night-fever-from-disgaea-creator-sohei-niikawa-launches-on-ps5-switch-and-steam) · [Gematsu (date announcement)](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/demons-night-fever-launches-october-22) · [RPG Site (date/pre-orders)](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21009-demons-night-fever-releases-simultaneously-worldwide-on-october-22-pre-orders-available-now) · [Automaton (design commentary)](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/disgaea-creators-new-dark-comedy-srpg-demons-night-fever-turns-god-into-a-scumbag-so-players-dont-feel-guilty-about-doing-evil-things/) · [Noisy Pixel (preview)](https://noisypixel.net/sohei-niikawa-demons-night-fever-preview/)

## Queen's Domain Launches Tomorrow — King's Field-Inspired Retro RPG

**Freshly Baked Games** and publisher **Future Friends Games** release **Queen's Domain** tomorrow, **October 30, 2026**, on **Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S** for **$19.99**.

The game is a retro-fantasy action RPG directly inspired by **From Software's King's Field**, set on the Isle of Neasied. The player wields a holy sword called **Endu** that functions as both weapon and traversal tool. The game features over 100 enemies and bosses and is single-player only. A PC demo is available on Steam ahead of launch.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio Heads to Switch 2 on November 12 — Orchestra Tour Announced

**Atlus** and **Sega** have confirmed that **Metaphor: ReFantazio** arrives on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026**, making it the game's Nintendo debut. The Switch 2 version is a straight port of the PS4/PS5/Xbox Series/PC release, launching at a lower price point than the original (£34.99 in the UK). A **Steelbook Edition** will be available for a limited time at launch. Pre-orders are open on the Nintendo eShop.

Alongside the platform launch, Atlus announced an **orchestra concert world tour** spanning 3 continents, 6 countries, and 8 cities globally, timed to the Switch 2 launch window.
