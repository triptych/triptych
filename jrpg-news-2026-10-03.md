## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Critical Acclaim — 89 Metacritic

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems** released **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** on **September 17, 2026**, exclusively for Nintendo Switch 2. The game has landed as one of the year's most acclaimed titles, earning an **89 on Metacritic** from 53 scored reviews, with 13 outlets awarding a perfect score.

**IGN** gave the game a **10/10**, writing that "Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave builds upon its strategic arts in ways I couldn't have anticipated." **Video Games Chronicle** also awarded a perfect score, calling it "an essential game for fans of Fire Emblem and SRPGs." **Famitsu** scored it **9/9/9/9**, and **Gamereactor** described it as "undeniably one of the best titles of the year."

Set approximately 100 years after *Fire Emblem: Three Houses*, Fortune's Weave features four new protagonists with largely distinct storylines that converge in the final act — making it reportedly the longest campaign in the series to date.

## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United — Full 3D Remake Announced for February 2027

**Square Enix** announced **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** during a **Nintendo Direct presentation on September 9, 2026** — a full 3D remake of the 1995 Super Famicom RPG, following the same approach as *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven*.

The game releases on **February 16, 2027** for Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Like its source material, *Destinies United* features **eight selectable protagonists**, each pursuing the story of the Child of Destiny from their own perspective.

Square Enix brought a **playable demo to Tokyo Game Show 2026** featuring two sections: "Palace of the Archfiends" from the early game and "Gwayne's Den" from the mid-game. Preview coverage from Dual Shockers described it as "a comfy and familiar JRPG" that retains the open-ended exploration the original was known for.

The game also won the **Japan Game Awards 2026 Future Division** at TGS alongside Persona 4 Revival, Final Fantasy VII Revelation, and Stranger Than Heaven.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21322-romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-release-date) · [Dual Shockers TGS Preview](https://www.dualshockers.com/tgs-2026-romancing-saga-3-destinies-united/)

## Kingdom Hearts Collection [I–III] Launches October 8 on Switch 2

**Square Enix** releases **Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III]** on **October 8, 2026** for Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, priced at **$74.99 USD** digitally.

## Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen Launches October 9 — New Region, 12 Dungeons, Switch 2 Port

**Capcom** releases **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** on **October 9, 2026** across PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC via Steam, and Nintendo Switch 2.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Arrives October 16 — Feature Overview

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** launches on **October 16, 2026** for Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, and Xbox Series X|S (PC version unlocks October 15 at 6:00 PM ET).

The remaster — developed by **TOSE**, who also handled *Tales of Graces F Remastered* — adds:

- **Graphics Mode / SFX Mode** toggles to switch between remastered and original visuals or sound effects
- **Boost features** to adjust EXP gain and encounter frequency
- **High-speed mode**, **Battle Retry**, and **Quick Recovery**
- Toggleable destination icons
- A new **Unknown** difficulty level

The base game remains the full original *Tales of Eternia* (released in North America as *Tales of Destiny II* in 2001), following Reid, Farah, and Keele as they race to prevent a cosmic catastrophe threatening two intertwined worlds.

## Final Fantasy Resonance — TGS Preview Strong; October 22 Launch

**Final Fantasy Resonance** — Square Enix's first HD-2D *Final Fantasy* — launches **October 22, 2026** for Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam.

At **Tokyo Game Show 2026**, the game was playable in a mid-game dungeon and arena stage not previously available in earlier demos. Dual Shockers called it "Square Enix firing on all cylinders," and Game Informer had earlier described it as "a little more than just nostalgia."

*Resonance* is a single-player turn-based JRPG set in the world of **Lapis**, adapted from the narrative foundation of *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius*. Combat uses a classic command-based system (no ATB) featuring timeline displays, stagger gauges, and a **Vision Resonance** mechanic that calls in echoes of classic Final Fantasy heroes.

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Console Launch October 29

**Spike Chunsoft**'s **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** arrives on consoles — Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S — on **October 29, 2026** (the PC version via Steam launched August 20).

Built from scratch, *RE:BOOT* completely redraws all character artwork, backgrounds, and event CGs — roughly **doubling the number of event stills** compared to the original Xbox 360 release and increasing background visuals by approximately 20%. All voice performances are re-recorded, and composer **Takeshi Abo** has remade the entire score.

The scenario is based on *STEINS;GATE ELITE* but re-paced for a more even flow, and the release adds a **new worldline and ending scenario** not present in any prior version. Characters use the **E-mote** animation system for subtle real-time expression and breathing.

Sources: [Official Site](https://steinsgate.jp/reboot/en-us/) · [Spike Chunsoft pre-orders](https://www.spike-chunsoft.com/news/steins-gate-re-boot-pre-orders-available-now/) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2025/11/steinsgate-reboot-confirmed-for-pc-coming-west-in-2026) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/05/steinsgate-reboot-is-coming-west-later-this-year)

## Phantom Blade Zero Launches October 29 on PS5 and PC

**S-GAME**'s wuxia action RPG **Phantom Blade Zero** releases on **October 29, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam and the Epic Games Store — pushed back from a September 9 date for additional polish.

Players control **Soul**, an elite assassin framed for the murder of his organization's patriarch and left with only days to live following a fatal wound kept at bay by a tainted heart replacement. Hong Kong action star **Donnie Yen** served as a creative consultant on the combat system, which draws from traditional Chinese martial arts choreography.

The game is built in **Unreal Engine 5** and has no current Nintendo or Xbox release window.
