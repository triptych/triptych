## Midgar Studio Liquidated; Edge of Memories' Future Uncertain

A French commercial court ordered the liquidation of **Midgar Studio** on **July 20, 2026**, ending the Montpellier-area developer's 18-year run after **NACON** failed to find a buyer following its own insolvency filing.

Midgar, known for *Edge of Eternity* (2021), had entered judicial reorganization proceedings in late May 2026. No acquirer stepped forward in time, and the court dissolved the studio. This is the second permanent closure attributable to Nacon's financial collapse, following the earlier liquidation of *GreedFall* and *Steelrising* developer **Spiders**.

*Edge of Memories* — an action RPG showcased as recently as this year's Nacon Connect — had been targeting a 2026 release for PC and consoles. No publisher has announced plans to rescue or continue the project, and its fate is currently unknown.

## Star Ocean: The Second Story R Drops as Surprise Switch 2 Launch

**Square Enix** and developer **Gemdrops** released **Star Ocean: The Second Story R** on Nintendo Switch 2 on **July 16, 2026**, with no pre-announced release date. The port is priced at **$49.99** and is a separate purchase — there is no upgrade path for owners of the original Switch version, and save data does not carry over.

The Switch 2 version targets **1080p resolution in both docked and handheld modes** at **60 FPS**, a meaningful improvement over the Switch 1 edition's lower resolution and 30 FPS target. Additional improvements include higher-resolution textures, extra environmental geometry, and better shadow quality.

*The Second Story R* is the 2023 remake of *Star Ocean: The Second Story* (1998), combining 2D character sprites with 3D environments, fully voiced dialogue, new anime cutscenes, and added story scenes. It was critically well-received on its original platforms.

## Culdcept BEGINS Launches to Strong Reviews; Earns Famitsu Gold Hall of Fame

**NEOS Corporation** released **Culdcept BEGINS** on **July 16, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2**, marking the first new entry in the card-and-board RPG series in approximately ten years. A **PC via Steam** release is planned for Q4 2026.

The game earned a **33/40** from Weekly Famitsu (9/8/8/8), netting the magazine's **Gold Hall of Fame** award. On OpenCritic, it aggregates to an **82** ("Strong") across 12 critics at launch.

The Culdcept series blends board game traversal — rolling dice, claiming territories — with collectible card dueling. Players build a custom 40-card deck and battle rivals for control of the board. *BEGINS* is both a series entry point and a full new title, designed to be accessible while retaining the depth series veterans expect.

## Disgaea Mayhem Launches Tomorrow; Reviews Arrive Mixed

**NIS America** releases **Disgaea Mayhem** on **July 23, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**. A free demo covering three stages and all seven weapon types has been available on all platforms since last week.

The game is a real-time action RPG spinoff of the long-running tactical series, trading turn-based strategy for direct character control. Players switch instantly between seven weapon types — Fists, Swords, Spears, Axes, Bows, Guns, and Staves — each with distinct combat feel. Core Disgaea staples like the **Level 9,999 cap** and the **Item World** return intact.

Reviews are mostly in the **6/10 range**. Critics consistently praise the kinetic, responsive combat but cite the brevity of the main campaign — most reviewers finished in **under 10 hours** — and the absence of the strategic depth that defines the franchise's tactical peers. Dual Shockers called it "uninspired... dedicated only to Disgaea fans"; RPGFan and CogConnected were kinder, with the latter praising "incredible combat." The consensus: solid for series loyalists who plan to grind, thin for everyone else.

A physical Limited Edition including an art book, soundtrack CD, and acrylic figures is available for pre-order.

## Langrisser: Sea of Sword Unveils First Gameplay Trailer

**BlackJack Studio** released the first gameplay trailer for **Langrisser: Sea of Sword** on **July 21, 2026**, alongside the opening of a **Steam page**. The title is targeting **PC via Steam** and **mobile platforms**; no release date has been announced.

The trailer reveals a **dual-hero combat system** as the game's central innovation: each squad is jointly led by two heroes, and players can switch between them mid-battle to change available abilities and tactical roles on the fly. Troops from both heroes can be merged into custom formations, and specific hero pairings unlock exclusive **Hero Unity Skills**. The game also features free-roaming exploration and destructible environments — a departure from traditional Langrisser's fixed-stage structure.

The story centers on an amnesiac protagonist known only as the Nameless, traveling alongside a white-haired girl named Eta across a continent gripped by political conflict. Returning characters from earlier series entries appear as well.

Pre-registration is open at the game's official website.

## Prinny Party: Going Overboard! Announced for Switch, Switch 2, PS5, and PC

**NIS America** announced **Prinny Party: Going Overboard!** on **July 16, 2026**, a Mario Party-style board game spinoff set in the *Disgaea* universe. A Japan release is set for **November 12, 2026**, with a worldwide release to follow in 2026.

Up to four players traverse a board, battling demons, leveling up, and buying equipment in a race to defeat the Supreme Overboard. Players can grab and throw items — and each other. Landing on build spaces lets players construct and upgrade facilities such as hospitals and armories, which opponents can then visit and spend at. The game includes **GameShare** support, so only one copy is needed for local multiplayer.

Platforms: **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

## Häxeri — Grid-Based Dungeon Crawler Inspired by Etrian Odyssey Announced for PC

Developer **Tarpit Games** has announced **Häxeri**, a grid-based first-person dungeon crawler RPG drawing inspiration from **Etrian Odyssey**, **Shin Megami Tensei**, and **Wizardry**. It is planned for **PC** with a **2027** release window; a Steam page and wishlist are live now.

The premise: an evil monster's soul has been sealed within a labyrinthine dungeon beneath a fantasy kingdom. When the sealed gate is reopened and a cult attempts to resurrect the being, the king dispatches adventurers to investigate. Players assemble their own party from **six character classes** and develop them through attribute points, skill trees, and equipment as they map the dungeon floor by floor.

Sources: [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/dungeon-crawler-haxeri-releasing-next-year/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3296970/Hxeri/)
