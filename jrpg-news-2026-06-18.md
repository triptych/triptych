## Persona 4 Revival Broadcast — Full Recap

The **Persona 4 Revival** information broadcast hosted by P-STUDIO director **Kazuhisa Wada** aired today at 4 PM PT on the Atlus West YouTube channel and Steam. It was the most comprehensive look at the remake to date, ahead of its **February 18, 2027** release on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (day-one Game Pass).

**Gameplay mechanics shown in footage:**
- **Airborne Collision** — Striking a Shadow afflicted with a status ailment launches it into adjacent enemies, spreading the ailment on contact. Demonstrated chaining across groups in Inaba dungeon corridors.
- **Prime Time** — A dedicated gauge fills through combat actions; when triggered it grants consecutive turns, free skill costs, ignored resistances, and free Baton Pass use, capped by a party-wide finishing attack.

**English voice cast (five confirmed roles):**

| Character | VA |
|---|---|
| Protagonist (Yu Narukami) | Nazeeh Tarsha |
| Yosuke Hanamura | Paul Castro Jr. |
| Chie Satonaka | Anne Yatco |
| Yukiko Amagi | Brianna Knickerbocker |
| Marie | Ari Thrash |

The remaining Investigation Team members have not yet been publicly confirmed by Atlus, though individual actors have been sharing credits via social media.

**Music:** **Ryota Kozuka** (composer of the original *Persona 4*) is returning to arrange new tracks alongside Shoji Meguro's original score, with vocalist **Shiori Sasaki** performing the vocal pieces.

**Social Links:** All Social Link scenes are now fully voiced — a significant upgrade over *Persona 4 Golden*, which left many Social Link scenes without voice acting.

## Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales — Out Now

**The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales** launched today worldwide on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC at **$59.99** (Digital Deluxe $69.99). The Collector's Edition ($229.99, Square Enix store exclusive) ships with the game, a Digital Deluxe upgrade code, the original soundtrack, and a "Faie and the Door of Time" desktop clock.

Launch-day coverage adds a Vice review to the critical pile: *"Square Enix's Best Original RPG in Years"* — a stronger headline than most outlets managed. The Metacritic aggregate (82 PS5 / 79 Switch 2 / 79 PC, tracked yesterday) holds steady with 81 reviews logged. Reviewer consensus consistently singles out the curved rolling-background effect on the HD-2D overworld and real-time weapon swapping as the game's most immediately distinctive qualities.

## Steam Next Fest — Stories of Somnia Emerges as Day 4 Standout

Four days into Steam Next Fest (June 15–22), **Stories of Somnia** (developer Lydian Games, publisher Monster Theater) has picked up word-of-mouth traction as a JRPG/Metroidvania hybrid worth watching. Previously unremarked upon in this series, it warrants a closer look:

- Players follow **Lumia** through a dream world called Somnia, shaped by memory, grief, and a corrupting fog called **Gloam**.
- Three major regions, dungeon discovery, platforming-based spells called **Arts**, and memory-born party members called **Dreamkin** that synergize with Lumia in turn-based combat.
- Runtime: approximately 20–30 hours.
- Release: **September 16, 2026**, PC (Steam). Demo is live through June 22.

The game has drawn comparisons to *Yoku's Island Express* for its genre blending and to *Omori* for its emotional tone around grief and rest. Still a small profile title — no console ports announced — but the Next Fest demo is generating earnest fan enthusiasm on social media.

Sources: [Stories of Somnia on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3635640/Stories_of_Somnia/) · [RPGamer — Launch Announcement](https://rpgamer.com/2025/11/stories-of-somnia-launching-in-2026/) · [Turn Based Lovers — Next Fest Picks](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/steam-next-fest-june-2026-turn-based-rpg-and-strategy-demos-to-try/) · [GAMES.GG — 3 Demos Not to Miss](https://games.gg/news/steam-next-fest-june-2026-3-demos-to-play/)
