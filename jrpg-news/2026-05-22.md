# JRPG News — May 22, 2026

## Tales of Arise: Beyond the Dawn Edition Launches on Switch 2 Today

**Bandai Namco**'s critically acclaimed action JRPG **Tales of Arise** arrives on **Nintendo Switch 2 today (May 22)** as the **Beyond the Dawn Edition**, bundling the full base game with the **Beyond the Dawn** expansion (20+ hours of additional content). It is priced at **$49.99** and comes on a Game-Key Card. Technically, the port runs at a stable **1080p / 60fps in cutscenes** and **30fps during exploration and combat**. A launch trailer dropped on May 20 ahead of today's release.

Sources: [Bandai Namco America](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/tales-of-arise-beyond-the-dawn-edition-coming-to-nintendo-switch-2-this-may) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/tales-of-arise-beyond-the-dawn-edition) · [My Nintendo News](https://mynintendonews.com/2026/05/20/tales-of-arise-beyond-the-dawn-edition-coming-to-switch-2-this-week/)

## Persona 5: The Phantom X Version 4.1 — Persona 3 Reload Crossover Now Live

**Persona 5: The Phantom X** (developed by Black Wings Game Studio, published by Atlus/Sega) launched its **Version 4.1 update on May 20–21**, bringing a full crossover with **Persona 3 Reload**. The event story transports the Phantom Thieves into a labyrinth tied to the **Dark Hour**, visiting Tartarus and Paulownia Mall alongside S.E.E.S. members. **Makoto Yuki** — the protagonist of Persona 3 Reload — makes his global debut as a **5-Star Fire-attribute Assassin**, available via the limited "When Moon Reaches Stars" banner running through **June 25, 2026**. A discounted Butler Makoto Yuki cosmetic set is available in the in-game shop for 2,880 Meta Jewels.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20405-persona-5-the-phantom-x-persona-3-reload-crossover-story-launches-with-makoto-yukis-addition) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/press-release/2026-05-20/persona5-the-phantom-x-makoto-yuki-from-persona-3-reload-makes-his-debut-ver-4.1-update-now-live/.237549) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/05/20/persona5-the-phantom-x-version-4-1-makoto-yuki-persona-3-reload-event/)

## Persona 4 Golden Leaving Xbox Game Pass May 31

**Persona 4 Golden** is among the titles departing **Xbox Game Pass on May 31, 2026**, as part of the service's late-May removal wave. The enhanced edition of the beloved Atlus JRPG has been a marquee RPG offering on the platform. Subscribers have until the end of the month to play or purchase it at a discounted member price before it leaves.

Sources: [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/xbox-game-pass-may-2026-leaving-soon-games-wave-2-list/)
