# JRPG News — October 20, 2026

## Final Fantasy Resonance Launches This Week — First-Ever HD-2D Final Fantasy

**Square Enix** and developer **Lancarse** release **Final Fantasy Resonance** on **October 22** for Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC via Steam (as well as PS5 and Xbox Series X|S). It is the first HD-2D entry in the Final Fantasy franchise, adapting the original story arc of *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* into a standalone offline RPG. A free demo covering Chapter 1 has been available since early September, with save data carrying over to the full game. Pricing is expected to be around $49.99–$59.99 for the standard edition.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Arrives on Switch and PC

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** launched on **October 15/16** worldwide for Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC via Steam (plus PS4, PS5, and Xbox). The game is the latest entry in Bandai Namco's Tales Remastered Project, developed by TOSE. Quality-of-life additions include a Graphics Mode and SFX Mode to toggle between original and remastered visuals and audio, two new difficulty settings (including an extreme mode available from the start), and a Digital Deluxe Edition that bundles a digital art book, soundtrack, and item DLC.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/tales-of-eternia-remastered-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-ps4-switch-and-pc) · [RPG Fan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/10/tales-of-eternia-remastered-october/) · [RPG Site](https://dev.rpgsite.net/news/20605-tales-of-eternia-remastered-launches-on-october-1516-for-ps5-ps4-xbox-switch-switch-2-and-steam)

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Now Out, Reviews Round-Up

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** released on **September 17** for Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC via Steam (also PS5). The remake brings fully 3D visuals and refined gameplay to the beloved Liberl arc. Nintendo World Report awarded the Switch 2 version **8.5/10**, praising the story and redesigned environments. RPG Site's multi-platform comparison notes the PC/Steam Deck version is the strongest technically — the Switch 2 Edition runs below native resolution and at lower graphical fidelity than the lowest PC settings. Pricing is **$59.99** for Switch/PC/PS5 and **$64.99** for the Switch 2 Edition.

## Demons' Night Fever Releasing This Week — Disgaea Creator's New SRPG

**Arc System Works**, **Drecom**, and **SuperNiche** release **Demons' Night Fever** this week (October 21–22) for Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam. The grid-based tactical RPG is produced by **Sohei Niikawa**, creator of the Disgaea series, who also writes the scenario under the pen name Roman Kitayama. The game's core hook: sacrificing your own allies in battle is the primary way to grow stronger, farming "Evil Misdeeds" to power up. Character designs come from **Yuji Himukai** (Etrian Odyssey) and the soundtrack is by **Tatsuya Yano** (former Gust/Atelier composer).

Sources: [RPG Site](https://rpgsite.net/news/20774-demons-night-fever-gameplay-trailer-showcases-strategy-rpg-gameplay) · [Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/disgaea-creator-sohei-niikawas-new-devil-training-srpg-lets-you-detonate-your-own-allies-to-farm-evil-misdeeds-and-grow-stronger/) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2025/06/arc-system-works-drecom-and-superniche-announce-raising-speedrunning-simulation-rpg-demons-night-fever)

## Vixen 357 Gets First English Release on Switch

**Vixen 357**, a 1992 SEGA Mega Drive strategy RPG that never left Japan, receives its first-ever English release on **October 23** on Nintendo Switch. A Steam release is also expected later this year. The game is a mech-based SRPG originally developed for the Mega Drive and is now playable in English for the first time.

Source: [RPG Fan Weekly Releases](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/10/18/rpgs-coming-this-week-october-18-24-2026/)

## Jun Maeda, Writer of CLANNAD and AIR, in Palliative Care

**Jun Maeda**, the acclaimed visual novel writer and composer behind *CLANNAD*, *Kanon*, *AIR*, and *Angel Beats!*, has been placed on palliative care for pharyngeal cancer, as reported October 19 by JRPG Pursuit. Maeda is a foundational figure in the visual novel and narrative RPG genre, with his works influencing countless JRPGs and anime adaptations.

Source: [JRPG Pursuit](https://jrpgpursuit.com/)
