# JRPG News — May 4, 2026

## FFXIV 8.0 Expansion Named "Evercold" — January 2027 Launch Confirmed

The FFXIV Fan Festival 2026 keynote (which ran April 24–25 in Anaheim) delivered on every expectation: Naoki Yoshida officially unveiled **Final Fantasy XIV: Evercold** as the 8.0 expansion, targeting a **January 2027** release. Key reveals include a level cap raise from 100 to 110, two brand-new original jobs (a tank and a physical ranged DPS — details on the first job come at the Berlin Fan Festival on July 25–26), and the headline system addition: **Reborn Mode vs. Evolved Mode** for all existing jobs. Reborn Mode preserves current rotations, while Evolved Mode overhauls actions with fewer required inputs and a stronger focus on job identity. Square Enix also confirmed the expansion will launch simultaneously on PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, PC (Windows/Mac/Steam) — the Switch 2 version being a new platform addition for the franchise. An Evangelion-themed alliance raid was also teased.

## FFXIV Patch 7.5 "Trail to the Heavens" and Ys Memoire Now Live

Both titles that were on last week's radar have now launched. **FFXIV Patch 7.5: Trail to the Heavens** went live on **April 28**, delivering Part 1 of the final Dawntrail main scenario quest alongside new content — with Part 2 arriving in early September 2026. Separately, **Ys Memoire: Revelations in Celceta** also launched on **Nintendo Switch** on April 28 as scheduled, priced at $29.99 digitally (15% discount active through today, May 4). The physical Day One Edition remains available at $59.99.

## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Dated for July 9

Cygames has confirmed that the major standalone expansion **Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok** launches on **July 9, 2026** for PC (Steam), PS5, PS4, and Nintendo Switch 2. Existing Relink owners can upgrade for **$29.99** (Standard) or **$54.99** (Special Edition). New additions include the solo roguelike mode **The Conflux**, new co-op quest tiers, additional bosses, master traits, and summons. Two new playable characters were detailed: **Beatrix** (contractor of the seal weapon Embrasque) and **Eustace** (marksman, contractor of Flamek Thunder), plus Society agents Gallanze and Maglielle. An open beta test has already taken place with hands-on impressions circulating.

## Indie Quest 2026 Showcase Announced for May 28

Taylor Hoyt (The Gaming Shelf) has announced that **Indie Quest 2026** returns on **May 28 at 5 PM Pacific / 8 PM Eastern**, streaming live on YouTube. The showcase will feature **35+ new trailers** and **6 world premiere game reveals** focused entirely on indie JRPGs. Confirmed titles appearing in teasers include **Alabaster Dawn**, **SacriFire**, **Edge of Memories**, **Spark Ignites**, and **Infinite Alliance**, with Hoyt hinting at "maybe a shadow drop or two." ## Falcom: Trails Sequel Plot Complete, Final Game in 2031–2032

Nihon Falcom president Toshihiro Kondo shared significant long-term franchise news: the **plot for the sequel to Trails beyond the Horizon is already written**, and the ending for the entire Trails series has been decided. The final entry in the saga is planned to be **announced at Falcom's 50th anniversary in 2031** and **released in 2032**, giving newcomers roughly six years to work through the series' 1,000+ hours of content. Additionally, Falcom confirmed they are working on a **Nintendo Switch 2 port** of an unannounced existing title, and have **six unannounced games** currently in development across the Trails, Ys, and new IP categories — with a second 2026 title beyond Kyoto Xanadu still to be revealed.

Sources: [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/03/11/falcom-trails-beyond-the-horizon-sequel-plot-complete-final-game-status/) · [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/jrpg/the-final-trails-game-will-be-announced-in-2031-and-released-in-2032-falcom-president-confirms-so-you-have-6-years-to-catch-up-on-1000-hours-of-jrpgs/) · [Automaton West](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/trails-developer-nihon-falcom-to-shift-focus-on-increasing-number-of-released-titles-per-year-and-publishing-more-switch-2-ports-six-unannounced-titles-currently-in-development/)
