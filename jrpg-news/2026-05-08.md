# JRPG News — May 8, 2026

## Alabaster Dawn Early Access Is Live — First Impressions Positive

**Alabaster Dawn** from Radical Fish Games (creators of CrossCode) officially launched in **Steam Early Access on May 7** at **$24.99**. The 2.5D action RPG — which blends pixel art with 3D environments — is **Steam Deck Verified** and follows protagonist Juno on a quest to save her world from a spreading blight. The current Early Access build covers through the midpoint of Chapter 2 (out of seven planned), offering roughly **10 hours of gameplay**. The full release is targeting **~40 hours** across all seven chapters, with the studio planning to remain in Early Access for approximately two years. Early reviews describe it as a confident, well-executed love letter to SNES-era JRPGs that "sticks to the basics and does all of them really, really well." A free demo remains available on Steam.

## Triple-I Initiative Showcase Recap — JRPG Highlights

The **Triple-I Initiative indie showcase** aired on **May 7**, featuring **40 games and 8 world premieres**. Key JRPG and genre-adjacent highlights:

- **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse** — Konami and Evil Empire's new Castlevania entry (first announced Feb 2026) received extended new footage. Set in **15th-century Paris**, players control Trevor Belmont's successor wielding the Vampire Killer whip through an action-exploration structure reminiscent of *Symphony of the Night*. Targets PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC in **2026**.
- **Neverway** — The narrative RPG narrowed its release window to **October 2026** for PC and Switch.
- **Dead as Disco** — Action-rhythm RPG received a new gameplay trailer.
- **Don't Starve Elsewhere** — Survival RPG spin-off introduces co-op play, base crafting, and a new "Fog" environmental hazard.

## Die in the Dungeon Exits Early Access with Version 1.0

**Die in the Dungeon** — the dice-building roguelite RPG from Alarts — released its full **1.0 version on May 1**. The 1.0 launch adds a significant amount of content over the Early Access build: a new playable character named **Nera**, a new **D8 difficulty mode**, an overhauled progression system with up to **10 character levels**, new boss challenges with special rewards, and a **true ending with a final boss**. The game currently holds a **93% positive rating** across over 1,650 Steam reviews (90% positive in the last 30 days). The color-coded dice combat system — red for damage, blue for defense, green for healing — has been well-praised for its tactical depth.

Sources: [InGameNews 1.0 Report](https://www.ingamenews.com/2026/05/die-in-dungeon-10-official-release-ken.html) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2026820/Die_in_the_Dungeon/) · [Game8 Release Date](https://game8.co/articles/release-dates/die-in-the-dungeon-release-date-and-time)
