# JRPG News — May 6, 2026

## Stranger Than Heaven Xbox Showcase Airs Today — Story, World, and Characters Detailed

RGG Studio and Xbox's dedicated **"Xbox Presents: A Special Look at Stranger Than Heaven"** broadcast aired today (May 6) at 4:00 PM PT / 7:00 PM ET. The roughly 30-minute showcase revealed deep-dive details on the game's story, world, and core cast of characters directly from the developers. The game spans **five distinct eras of modern Japan** — 1915, 1929, 1943, 1951, and 1965 — each set in a different city, with the 1965 era taking place in Kamurocho (the iconic fictional Tokyo district from the Like a Dragon franchise). Stranger Than Heaven is an Xbox Play Anywhere title, day one on **Xbox Game Pass**, and also coming to **PS5 and Steam**. A release date has not yet been announced.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Leaked via PEGI Rating

Bandai Namco's next remaster appears to have been revealed early: a **PEGI 12 rating** for **Tales of Eternia Remastered** was quietly published and then deleted from the European classification board's website. The listing cited **Nintendo Switch** as the platform, though whether other platforms are included awaits official confirmation. Tales of Eternia (2000, PS1) — released in North America as *Tales of Destiny II* — is a beloved but rarely revisited entry in the series, later ported to PSP in 2006. No official announcement from Bandai Namco has been made yet.

## Persona 4 Revival Targeting Early 2027 — Development Wrapping Late August

New reports have narrowed down **Persona 4 Revival**'s release window: current internal projections point to **early 2027**, with development completion expected by **end of August 2026**. Atlus confirmed last year that Persona 4 Revival (a full remake of the 2008 PS2 classic) would not launch before April 1, 2026, and Sega Sammy's investor materials had listed it as releasing within fiscal year 2027 (April 2026–March 2027). The early 2027 projection would put it just outside that window. The game targets **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC**.

## Alabaster Dawn Launches on Steam Early Access Tomorrow (May 7)

The 2.5D JRPG **Alabaster Dawn** — described as a love letter to SNES and Genesis-era JRPGs from an indie studio — launches in **Steam Early Access on May 7** as part of the **Triple-I Initiative showcase**. The showcase (running May 7) will feature new trailers for JRPG and RPG adjacent titles, including new boss fights, story sequences, and locations for Alabaster Dawn. The game has a free demo available on Steam now. The full Triple-I showcase will include 40 games total with eight world premieres; other notable genre-adjacent titles shown include a Castlevania return (**Castlevania: Belmont's Curse**).

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Confirmed for September 17, 2026

Nihon Falcom and NIS America have locked in **September 17, 2026** as the global simultaneous release date for **The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter**. The game will launch on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC (Steam)** all on the same day — a notable departure from Falcom's historically Japan-first releases. A full voice cast reveal and new trailer accompanied the announcement.
