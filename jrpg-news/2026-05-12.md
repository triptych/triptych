# JRPG News — May 12, 2026

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Coming to PS5 & Switch 2 This Fall

NIS America and Nihon Falcom announced that **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** — the Crossbell duology following Lloyd Bannings and his Special Support Section — are coming to **PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 in Fall 2026**. Both games support **4K resolution and 120 FPS**, with the option to toggle between original and upscaled textures. The Switch 2 versions additionally support **mouse controls**. High-Speed Mode carries over from prior releases. Each title can be purchased separately digitally, or together as a physical collection. NIS America originally brought the Crossbell games to the West in 2022 (Zero) and 2023 (Azure) for PS4, Switch, and PC.

Sources: [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/05/08/trails-from-zero-trails-to-azure-ps5-switch-2/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20306-the-legend-of-heroes-trails-from-zero-to-azure-ps5-switch-2-port-4k-120fps) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/05/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-from-zero-and-the-legend-of-heroes-trails-to-azure-coming-to-ps5-switch-2-this-fall)

## Mina the Hollower Gets May 29 Launch Date

**Yacht Club Games** (creators of Shovel Knight) confirmed that **Mina the Hollower** launches **May 29, 2026** for **PS5, Switch, Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam / Humble Store), Linux, and macOS** at **$19.99**. The game had previously been delayed from its original Halloween 2025 target. Players control Mina, a skilled inventor and "Hollower" who travels to the mysterious Tenebrous Isle to investigate why her Spark Generators have gone dark. The game draws inspiration from top-down Zelda and Castlevania with an 8-bit aesthetic evoking Game Boy Color titles.

Sources: [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/05/08/mina-the-hollower-launches-may-29th/) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/05/06/mina-the-hollower-finally-launches-later-this-month) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/mina-the-hollower-release-date-falls-in-may-2026/)

## Wardrum Launches — "Fire Emblem Meets Parappa the Rapper"

**Wardrum**, the tactical turn-based roguelite from **Mopeful Games** (published by Team17), launched on **May 7 on PC (Steam)** at **$19.99** (currently 20% off at launch). Players lead a five-warrior tribal warband across a fantasy world corrupted by "off-beat magic," with the core hook being rhythm-timed combat — attacks and abilities deal more damage when executed in sync with a pounding tribal drumbeat. The game features over 35 enemy types requiring tactical planning to overcome. Early Steam reception sits at **88% positive**. It has been likened to "Octopath Traveler meets Fire Emblem with sick beats."

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/04/wardrum-launches-may-7) · [Saving Content](https://www.savingcontent.com/2026/05/07/rhythmic-tactical-turn-based-roguelite-wardrum-launches-today-on-steam/) · [Hey Poor Player Review](https://www.heypoorplayer.com/2026/05/07/wardrum-review-pc/)

## Never Second in Rome Exits Early Access

**Never Second in Rome**, the text-heavy historical RPG-management hybrid from solo developer Alessandro Roberti, reached **full 1.0 release on May 5**. Players take on the role of a Roman centurion serving under Julius Caesar, managing both their character and their unit through turn-based combat and strategic decisions. The 1.0 launch adds an **optional Ironman mode** with meta-progression and permadeath, unlocked after completing the game once in Standard mode. The game currently holds **81% positive** across over 520 Steam reviews.

Sources: [Steam Store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3169580/Never_Second_in_Rome/) · [arobertigames.com](https://www.arobertigames.com/never-second-in-rome/)
