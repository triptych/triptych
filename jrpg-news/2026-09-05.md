## Final Fantasy Resonance Demo Live Now — Full Game October 22

**Square Enix** surprised fans on September 3 during PlayStation's State of Play by dropping a free demo for **Final Fantasy Resonance**, their long-awaited return to fully turn-based FF gameplay. The demo covers the entire opening chapter (roughly 3–5 hours) and save data transfers to the full release. In the same broadcast, **Sephiroth** was revealed as a major presence in the story, sending the internet into a frenzy.

The full game launches **October 22, 2026** on Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC (Steam).

Sources: [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/final-fantasy-resonance-demo-released/) · [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/final-fantasy/final-fantasy-resonance-the-square-enix-jrpg-ive-wanted-for-nearly-a-decade-gets-a-surprise-demo-today/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/09/04/final-fantasy-resonance-demo-consoles-pc/)

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches in 12 Days (September 17)

Nihon Falcom's **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — the full 3D remake of *Trails in the Sky SC* — is just 12 days away. It releases **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** (worldwide simultaneous release), picking up right where Estelle's story left off as Joshua disappears amid a growing continental conspiracy.

Both physical and digital editions are available for Switch and Switch 2 in the West. A demo released on August 20 with save data carryover.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/19218-falcom-reveals-trails-in-sky-2nd-chapter-set-to-release-in-fall-2026-worldwide-for-playstation-5-switch-2-switch-pc) · [NintendoSoup](https://nintendosoup.com/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-launches-september-17th-2026/) · [Famiboards](https://famiboards.com/threads/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-announced-for-switch-switch-2-ps5-steam-coming-sep-17-demo-coming-on-20th-august.15601/)

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Also Drops September 17 on Switch 2

Nintendo and Intelligent Systems confirmed **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** for a **September 17, 2026** launch exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2**. Set in the same world as *Three Houses*, the game offers four distinct protagonists — Cai, Theodora, Dietrich, and Leda — each with their own story route, echoing the multi-path structure fans loved.

A **Dagdan Collection** limited edition is available, including the game, steel case, artbook, character art cards, and a map of Dagda. Developer Intelligent Systems has already added it to their completed works page.

Sources: [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/games/nintendo-switch-2/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave) · [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-gets-a-september-2026-release-date-and-collection-edition/) · [Nintendo UK](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-2-games/Fire-Emblem-Fortune-s-Weave-2915498.html)

## Stories of Somnia Demo Available on Steam

Indie developer Lydian Games released a free demo for **Stories of Somnia** on Steam on **September 2**. It's an exploration-first JRPG fused with Metroidvania-style design — protagonist **Lumia** wakes aboard a dream train called the *Lullaby Express* and must uncover her connection to a mysterious world called Somnia while battling a corrupting fog called the Gloam.

Spells double as traversal tools, letting you grapple across gaps and reshape combat. The creature-collecting system features companions called **Dreamkin**. The full game targets **November 2, 2026** on PC.

Sources: [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/09/exploration-first-jrpg-with-creature-collecting-stories-of-somnia-gets-a-first-demo/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3635640/Stories_of_Somnia/) · [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/steam-games-like-paper-mario-metroid-stories-somnia/)

## Konami's Rev. NOiR Targeting 2027 — PC Version Confirmed

Konami's in-house action RPG **Rev. NOiR** (developed by ILCA) received a 2027 release window and new story details this week. The game follows **Asch**, a young man given an ordinance to "save many people" in a world under threat from a phenomenon called **Lightfall**. Combat mixes combo-driven swordplay with customizable **Astral Sigil** runes and **Kardieos** augmentations.

A **PC (Steam)** version was confirmed alongside other platforms. No release date beyond 2027 has been announced.

Sources: [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/rev-noir-release-window-set-for-2027/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/rev-noir-releasing-next-year/) · [Konami](https://www.konami.com/games/us/en/topics/3099/)
