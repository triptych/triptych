## Final Fantasy Resonance Launches to Strong Reviews

**Square Enix**'s **[Final Fantasy Resonance](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/the-final-fantasy-resonance-game-is-out-now/)** officially launched on **October 22, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC (Steam)**, earning an aggregated critic score of **86–87** from 45+ reviews. The HD-2D action-JRPG retells the first story arc of the mobile game *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* as a standalone single-player experience with no gacha mechanics. Famitsu estimates **20–30 hours** for the main story, up to **70 hours** with all extra content. A free demo covering Chapter 1 launched alongside the game with save data that carries over to the full release.

Pocket Tactics calls it "a deep, story-rich JRPG with lovable characters and engaging combat," while critics broadly praise the presentation as Square's signature HD-2D visual style at its most polished. The Nintendo Switch 2 version is available from the [Nintendo eShop](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/final-fantasy-resonance-switch/).

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Is Out Now on Switch, Switch 2, and PC

**Bandai Namco** and **TOSE** released **[Tales of Eternia Remastered](https://finalweapon.net/2026/06/09/tales-of-eternia-remastered-officially-revealed-launches-october-16/)** worldwide on **October 16, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC via Steam** (also PS4/PS5 and Xbox). The remaster is the next entry in the *Tales Remastered Project* and brings quality-of-life improvements including **destination markers**, an **encounter-off toggle**, and a **fast battle mode**. Players can also toggle between remastered and original visuals and sound.

A note for Switch owners: the Switch 1 and Switch 2 editions are **separate purchases with no upgrade path**, and save data does not carry between the two versions.

Sources: [Final Weapon announcement](https://finalweapon.net/2026/06/09/tales-of-eternia-remastered-officially-revealed-launches-october-16/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/10/tales-of-eternia-remastered-october/) · [RPGSite platforms & frame rate](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20638-tales-of-eternia-remastered-resolution-frame-rate-ps5-nintendo-switch-2-pc-xbox-ps4-pro-upgrade)

## Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III] Arrives Natively on Switch 2

The **[KINGDOM HEARTS Collection \[I~III\]](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/06/09/the-full-kingdom-hearts-saga-is-coming-to-switch-2-ps5-and-xbox-series-xs/)** launched **October 8, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2** at **$74.99**, replacing the discontinued cloud versions with full native ports. The bundle includes:

- **HD 1.5 + 2.5 ReMIX** (KH1, Re:Chain of Memories, 358/2 Days, KH2, Birth by Sleep, Re:coded)
- **HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue** (Dream Drop Distance HD, Back Cover, 0.2 Birth by Sleep)
- **KINGDOM HEARTS III + Re Mind**

Owners of the previous Kingdom Hearts Integrum Masterpiece for Cloud or all individual KH Cloud titles are eligible for a **50% discount** on the collection. Switch 2 buyers also receive an exclusive Keyblade: **Long Night**. A demo is available covering the opening through Olympus, with save data carrying to the full release.

Additionally, **Kingdom Hearts IV** was confirmed for Nintendo Switch 2 during the June 2026 Nintendo Direct, though no release date has been set.

## Octopath Traveler I & II Land on Nintendo Switch 2

Both **[Octopath Traveler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopath_Traveler_II)** and **Octopath Traveler II** received native **Nintendo Switch 2** versions on **October 1, 2026**, with improved resolution and frame rates over the original Switch releases. These are separate purchases — there is no upgrade path for existing Switch owners and save data does not carry between versions. The Japan release of both Switch 2 versions was in July 2026, with the worldwide release following in October.
