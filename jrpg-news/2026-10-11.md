# JRPG News — October 11, 2026

## Kingdom Hearts Collection Launches on Switch 2

**Square Enix** released the full **Kingdom Hearts** saga natively on **Nintendo Switch 2 on October 8**, replacing the cloud-only versions that had been available on the original Switch. The collection is sold as the bundled **Kingdom Hearts Collection [I–III]** ($74.99 USD on eShop) or as three separate packages:

- **Kingdom Hearts -HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX-**
- **Kingdom Hearts HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue**
- **Kingdom Hearts III + ReMind**

Save data from the old Switch cloud versions transfers to the Switch 2 editions. The cloud versions will stop being playable on June 9, 2027, and are no longer sold on the eShop. **Kingdom Hearts 4** was also confirmed for Switch 2 but has no release window yet.

Sources: [The Sixth Axis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/06/09/the-full-kingdom-hearts-saga-is-coming-to-switch-2-ps5-and-xbox-series-xs/) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/kingdom-hearts-4-switch-2-appears-at-nintendo-direct/) · [FeedBagel](https://feedbagel.com/post/kingdom-hearts-collection-switch-2-estimated-file-size-revealed)

## Octopath Traveler I & II Come to Switch 2

**Square Enix** released native **Switch 2** versions of both **Octopath Traveler** and **Octopath Traveler II** on **October 1**. Both titles run at improved resolution and frame rates compared to their Switch 1 counterparts. Note: these are separate Switch 2 editions with no upgrade path — existing Switch saves are not compatible with the new versions.

Sources: [Nintendo World Report](http://www.nintendoworldreport.com/news/77569/nintendo-downloads-october-8-2026)

## Persona 4 Revival Gets "Shadow" Trailer

**Atlus** released a new **"Shadow" trailer** for the upcoming **Persona 4 Revival** (October 3, 2026), spotlighting the Shadow boss encounters and revealing the Shadow forms of party members. The trailer also includes a new OST track that weaves in elements from the original *Persona 4*'s "The Backside of the TV."

**Persona 4 Revival** launches **February 18, 2027** on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam/Microsoft Store), with a **Switch 2** version following on **May 20, 2027**.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/persona-4-revival-shadow-trailer) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21513-persona-4-revival-shadow-trailer-released) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/03/persona-4-revival-shadow-trailer/)

## Coming This Week: Tales of Eternia Remastered (Oct 16) & Castlevania: Belmont's Curse (Oct 15)

**Tales of Eternia Remastered** (**Bandai Namco** / TOSE) launches **October 16** on **Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC (Steam)** — the same developer that handled *Tales of Graces F Remastered*. Key features include:
- A **Graphics Mode** to toggle between remastered and original visuals at any time
- An **SFX Mode** to switch between remastered and original sound effects
- Quality-of-life additions: destination markers, encounter toggle, and high-speed auto-battle
- A **Digital Deluxe Edition** with digital art book, OST, and bonus DLC items
- Steam Deck Verified on PC

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20605-tales-of-eternia-remastered-launches-on-october-16-for-ps5-ps4-xbox-switch-switch-2-and-steam) · [Bandai Namco](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/tales-of-eternia-remastered-coming-this-october-2026) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/tales-of-eternia-remastered-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-ps4-switch-and-pc)

**Castlevania: Belmont's Curse** (Konami / Evil Empire / Motion Twin) launches **October 15** on **PC (Steam)**, with a **Nintendo Switch** release also in October 2026 (exact date unconfirmed — check Konami's site or the eShop). Standard and Midnight editions are available for pre-order.

Sources: [Gematsu (Switch date)](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/castlevania-belmonts-curse-for-switch-launches-october-15) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20591-castlevania-belmonts-curse-launches-on-october-15-for-ps5-xbox-and-pc-with-switch-coming-later)
