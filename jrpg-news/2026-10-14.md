# JRPG News — October 14, 2026

## Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III] Now Available on Switch 2 and PC

**Square Enix** released **Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III]** on **October 8, 2026**, bringing the complete trilogy to **Nintendo Switch 2** natively for the first time, as well as **PC via Microsoft Store**. The bundle includes all three major releases: *KINGDOM HEARTS -HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX-*, *KINGDOM HEARTS HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue*, and *KINGDOM HEARTS III + Re Mind (DLC)*. Players who previously purchased the cloud-streaming version for Nintendo Switch can transfer their save data to the native Switch 2 client.

## Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen Launches on Switch 2 and PC

**Capcom** launched the **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** expansion on **October 9, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2** (bundled with the base game, $49.99) and **PC via Steam** (DLC for $29.99 for existing owners). The expansion sends the Arisen to the northern region of **Norgan** alongside a new companion named Eir, and adds twelve **Lost Rites** dungeon challenges that reward rare equipment from past Arisen. Character creation also receives new hairstyles and tattoos. A free update tied to launch added the reusable Eternal Ferrystone for streamlined fast travel, and a second update planned for later adds improved frame rate performance, extra save slots, and additional quality-of-life improvements.

The Switch 2 version uses **FSR frame interpolation** — a first for Capcom's output on that platform.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20617-dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-release-date) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/06/dragons-dogma-2-switch-release-dark-arisen-expansion-announced/)

## Octopath Traveler 1 & 2 Arrive on Switch 2 — No Save Transfer

**Square Enix** brought both **Octopath Traveler** and **Octopath Traveler II** to **Nintendo Switch 2** on **October 1, 2026**, timed to the series' eighth anniversary. Each title is priced at **$59.99**, and a digital bundle of both is available for **$74.99**. Physical copies ship on Game-Key Cards.

The Switch 2 versions target **1080p / 60 fps**, matching the PS4 versions' performance settings. Existing owners of the original Nintendo Switch versions do **not** receive a discount or free upgrade, and save data does **not** carry over — purchases start fresh. A digital-only Japan release launched ahead of the worldwide retail date.

Sources: [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/octopath-traveler-and-its-sequel-are-coming-to-nintendo-switch-2-this-october/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/octopath-traveler-octopath-traveler-ii-switch-2-versions-announced/) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/07/13/the-first-two-octopath-traveler-games-are-coming-to-switch-2-in-october-but-theres-no)

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation Playable Demo at Brasil Game Show

**Square Enix** offered the first playable demo of **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** — the conclusion of the *FF7 Remake* trilogy — at the **Brasil Game Show (BGS) 2026**, which ran October 9–12 in São Paulo. ### Tales of Eternia Remastered (October 16)

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** lands on **October 16, 2026** worldwide (October 15 in Japan) for **Switch**, **Switch 2**, **PC (Steam)**, PS5, and PS4. Developed by **TOSE** (who also handled *Tales of Graces f Remastered*), the package allows players to toggle between the original and remastered visuals and sound effects on the fly. New quality-of-life options include destination map markers, a combat high-speed auto-battle mode, and a **Boost** slider for adjusting XP gain and encounter frequency. Two new difficulty settings are added: a hard mode available at the start and the ultra-challenging **Unknown** mode unlocked in New Game+. A **Digital Deluxe Edition** is available, including a digital art book, original soundtrack, and bonus DLC items.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20605-tales-of-eternia-remastered-launches-on-october-16-for-ps5-ps4-xbox-switch-switch-2-and-steam) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/tales-of-eternia-remastered-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-ps4-switch-and-pc) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/10/tales-of-eternia-remastered-october/)
