## Disgaea Mayhem Launches as Action RPG Spin-Off — Out Now on Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**NIS America** released **Disgaea Mayhem** on **July 23, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**. The game is the western release of *Makaism: Frenzy of the Netherworld*, which launched earlier in Japan.

The spin-off abandons the franchise's long-running grid-based, turn-based strategy format entirely in favor of fast-paced, real-time action combat. Players control mercenary **N.A.**, hired by overlord **Tichelle** to track down the thief who stole the **First Radiance Pudding** — a confection created by Tichelle's late father.

Despite the genre shift, Disgaea DNA remains intact: the level cap still sits at **9,999**, single hits can reach **one million damage**, and long-term character growth relies on the series-staple **reincarnation** and **Item World** upgrade systems. Seven weapons — including sword, bow, and others — allow for different playstyles. A pre-launch demo covering three stages remains available on Switch 2, Switch, and Steam.

A **Limited Edition** priced at $99.99 is available from the NIS America store and includes an art book, soundtrack, acrylic stand, and keychain.

Sources: [GoNintendo – Switch 2 date/Limited Edition](https://www.gonintendo.com/contents/60226-disgaea-mayhem-to-launch-july-23rd-2026-on-switch-switch-2-limited-edition-detailed) · [Nintendo Everything – Switch 2 review](https://nintendoeverything.com/disgaea-mayhem-review-for-nintendo-switch-2-and-switch/) · [Final Weapon – review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/16/disgaea-mayhem-review-nintendo-switch-2/) · [Anime News Network – launch](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/press-release/2026-07-23/disgaea-mayhem-is-out-now-on-switch-2-switch-playstation-5-and-steam/.239917)

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition Overview Trailer Reveals MOMO, Merc Assault Mode

**Nintendo** and **Monolith Soft** released a new overview trailer for **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** today (July 24), detailing the additional content exclusive to the enhanced version ahead of its **July 30 digital launch**.

Key additions revealed:

- **MOMO** — the 100-Series Observational Realian from the *Xenosaga* trilogy — joins the Blade roster as the third Xenosaga crossover character alongside KOS-MOS Re: and T-elos Re:. MOMO is a new Rare Blade who can swap between light and dark forms.
- **Merc Assault** — a new gameplay mode letting players directly control up to six Blades in real-time squad combat, distinct from the base game's battle system.
- **New costumes** for Pyra and Mythra, visible in both gameplay and cinematics.
- Full **4K / 60fps** performance in TV mode; 1080p / 60fps in handheld. Cutscenes have also been remastered. These enhancements carry over to the expansion *Torna ~ The Golden Country*.

Existing *Xenoblade Chronicles 2* owners can upgrade for **$9.99 / £7.99**. A free **Ver. 3.0.0 update** has already been applied to the base game for compatibility. The physical Switch 2 Edition arrives **October 1**.

## Culdcept BEGINS Launches — First New Entry in the Series in Ten Years

**NEOS Corporation** released **Culdcept BEGINS** on **July 16, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch (digital only), and PC via Steam**. This is the first brand-new entry in the *Culdcept* series in approximately ten years, following *Culdcept Revolt* (2016).

The game is a hybrid of board game and card-battling RPG: players roll dice, traverse a board, claim territories as property, and defend or challenge spaces using a custom 40-card deck of around **400 total cards** across Creature, Item, and Spell categories. An **Online Versus** mode supports worldwide matchmaking, including Quick Match and custom Versus Rooms.

The **Nintendo Switch 2 physical edition** comes in two variants: Standard ($54.99, includes game and booklet) and **Limited SteelBook Edition** ($89.99, adds a folding box, SteelBook case, soundtrack CD, and 10 creature tablet-style cards). The **Famitsu Gold Hall of Fame Award** was awarded to the game around launch.

## Häxeri Announced — Old-School Dungeon Crawler for PC in 2027

**Tarpit Games** has announced **Häxeri**, a first-person, grid-based dungeon-crawling RPG coming to **PC** in **2027**, now available to wishlist on Steam.

The game is openly inspired by **Etrian Odyssey**, **Shin Megami Tensei**, and **Wizardry**. Set in a fantasy kingdom where an ancient monster's soul has been sealed beneath a labyrinthine dungeon, the game tasks players with investigating a cult attempting to resurrect the creature after the dungeon gates are reopened.

Players create a custom party from six available classes and improve them through attribute points, skill trees, and gear. Navigation is first-person with a player-drawn or automapping option. Combat uses a turn-based **Press Turn**-style system where exploiting enemy weaknesses grants extra actions, while misses or ineffective attacks reduce available turns.

Sources: [RPGamer – announcement](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/dungeon-crawler-haxeri-releasing-next-year/) · [Niche Gamer – details](https://nichegamer.com/old-school-dungeon-crawler-haxeri-announced-for-pc/)

## Final Fantasy XIV Patch 7.55 Previewed — Occult Crescent: North Horn Arrives July 28

**Square Enix** has previewed **Final Fantasy XIV** patch **7.55**, set to release **July 28, 2026** on **PC and PlayStation 5**.

The patch expands the **Occult Crescent** large-scale exploration zone with the new **North Horn** area, raising the **Knowledge Level** cap to 40 and adding **eight new Phantom Jobs**. Other highlights include the **final Phantom Weapon upgrade stage**, the concluding chapter of the **Hildibrand** questline for Dawntrail, and **Allied Society capstone quests** that require completing all three Dawntrail allied society storylines. A new raid, **Forked Tower: Magic**, arrives in both Normal and Extreme difficulties.

The patch does *not* include the Dawntrail Main Scenario finale or the Beastmaster limited job — both are held for **Patch 7.56** (September 6). A **Letter from the Producer LIVE** on July 25 will preview Beastmaster gameplay ahead of that release.

Additionally, a **Nintendo Switch 2 beta** for *Final Fantasy XIV* is planned to begin in August.

## The Relic: First Guardian Pre-Launch Gameplay Trailer — Launches July 31 on PC

**Perp Games** and developer **Project Cloud Games** have released a pre-launch gameplay trailer for **The Relic: First Guardian**, ahead of its **July 31 release on PC via Steam** (and PlayStation 5). Switch 2 and Xbox Series versions are planned for later in 2026.

The dark fantasy action RPG draws from **Asian mythology and folklore** and aims to carve out a distinct niche within the soulslike genre. Its stamina system is reserved strictly for **defense and dodging** — standard attacks are unrestricted. Instead of traditional level-ups, the game uses a system of collectible **Relics** offering over **70 distinct passive effects** for build customization.

A **physical edition** from Perp Games is set for **September 4, 2026**.
