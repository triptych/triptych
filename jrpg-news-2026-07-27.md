## Final Fantasy XIV: Evercold — Berlin Fan Festival Reveals Bastion Job, FFVII Raid, and Switch 2 Date

**Square Enix** held the **Final Fantasy XIV Fan Festival in Berlin** this past weekend, where producer and director **Naoki Yoshida** delivered the expansion deep dive for **Evercold**, still on track for **January 2027**.

The headline reveal is **Bastion**, the expansion's first new job. It is a main tank that wields a pair of greatshields called **skyltborg** — making it the first tank in the game built around a two-shield combat style rather than sword-and-board or magitek arms.

The eight-player raid series announced for Evercold is titled **"Beyond the Lifestream"** and draws directly from the *Final Fantasy VII* universe. The 24-player alliance raid is a formal **Neon Genesis Evangelion** collaboration titled **"Ghosts of Desire"**, with original series episode director **Mahiro Maeda** joining as a guest artist. Evercold also introduces narrative structure changes that give players more freedom to choose the order in which they progress through the main scenario, with enemy difficulty scaling dynamically to the player's current level.

On the platform side, **Final Fantasy XIV** will launch on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**. Square Enix confirmed approximately **one free month of subscription time** for Switch 2 players at launch to ensure server stability during onboarding. The Switch 2 version supports mouse-and-keyboard input via the USB-C port.

## Midgar Studio Liquidated — Edge of Memories Future Uncertain

A French commercial court ordered the **liquidation of Midgar Studio** on **July 20, 2026**, ending the Montpellier-based developer's **18-year run** after no buyer was found before the court's deadline.

Midgar is the second permanent studio casualty of **Nacon's ongoing financial collapse** — Nacon, which had published the studio's work, filed for insolvency in February 2026. **Spiders** (the *GreedFall* developer) was liquidated in April under the same circumstances.

The principal concern for players is the fate of **Edge of Memories**, the action RPG Midgar had been actively developing toward a late-2026 release. The game was still being demoed as recently as Nacon Connect in May 2026, and was reportedly in the final stages of production at the time of closure. Because Nacon itself lacks the resources to fund completion of an unfinished game without a studio, **cancellation is considered the most likely outcome** unless a third party acquires the IP.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Switch 2 Edition Overview Trailer Reveals Momo Blade, New Outfits

Nintendo and **Monolith Soft** released a detailed overview trailer for **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** this week ahead of its **July 30 digital launch** (physical version arrives **October 1**).

The trailer confirms several new additions exclusive to the Switch 2 edition:

- **Momo**, the android companion from **Xenosaga**, joins as a new Rare Blade with Light and Dark elemental affinities. This is the first canonical crossover between the two Monolith Soft franchises.
- **New outfits for Pyra and Mythra** illustrated by character designer **Masatsugu Saito** appear in both cutscenes and combat.
- **Merc Assault** has been expanded: players can now deploy up to six Blades per mission rather than being limited to the Core Crystal party.
- Technical upgrades include **4K resolution docked / 1080p handheld, 60 FPS in both modes**, and enhanced cutscene detail and framerate.

A free **version 3.0.0 patch** was released on **July 22** to all existing owners of the original Switch version, adding Switch 2 compatibility flags ahead of the edition's launch.

## Disgaea Mayhem Launches on Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**Nippon Ichi Software**'s **Disgaea Mayhem** launched on **July 23, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5). The game carries a **$59.99 price** and is a significant genre departure for the franchise.

Rather than the series' trademark strategy-RPG grid combat, *Mayhem* is an **action RPG** giving players direct real-time control. The protagonist is **N.A.**, a veteran mercenary hired by overlord **Tichelle** of the Superlative Netherworld, whose motivation is equal parts professional obligation and a princess's affinity for flan. The game retains the Disgaea hallmarks of absurdist humor, stacked stat numbers, and a cycle of death, reincarnation, and power escalation.

## Chaos Zero Nightmare Revamps Act 1, Adds Combatant Fei; Season 4 Begins July 28

**Smilegate**'s dark fantasy roguelite RPG **Chaos Zero Nightmare** received a major content update this week that overhauls the first five chapters of Act 1. The revamped story is fully re-recorded with new voice acting and significantly expands world-building and character backstories beyond the original release versions.

The update also introduces **Fei** (paired with companion **Ruixiang**) as a new playable Combatant. Fei was previously available in the Chinese version at launch where she quickly became a fan favourite; she now arrives in the Korean and global clients. A "Monsters' Arena" event accompanies the update, letting players battle using Monster Cards to earn rewards.

**Season 4 – "Shattered Light and Claw"** is set to begin on **July 28**, making this an active week for the game's live-service roadmap. RPGamer published an interview this week with **game director and producer Hyungseok Kim** and **art director So Su-been** covering the studio's long-term vision for the title.
