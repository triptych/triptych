## Trails from Zero & Trails to Azure — Crossbell Duology Gets September 10 Date for PS5 and Switch 2

**NIS America** and **Nihon Falcom** confirmed on June 19 that the **Crossbell Duology** — *The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero* and *The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure* — will launch simultaneously on **PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 on September 10, 2026**.

Both games were originally released in Japan in 2010 and 2011, with English localizations arriving on PC and legacy consoles in 2022. The new-generation ports are not simple re-releases: they feature **4K resolution**, **120 frames per second**, the ability to toggle between **original and upscaled textures**, and **High-Speed Mode**. The Switch 2 version specifically gains **mouse input support**.

**Pricing:**
- Digital (each title separately): **$39.99**
- Physical bundle (both games): **$69.99**
- Limited Edition (NIS America online store only): **$109.99** — includes both games and physical extras

The Crossbell arc follows **Lloyd Banning** and his colleagues in the Special Support Section of the Crossbell Police Department, and is considered essential context for the later *Trails of Cold Steel* and *Trails into Reverie* arcs. September 10 puts both games on shelves before *Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave* (September 17) and the *Dragon Quest XI S Switch 2 Edition* (September 24), marking a dense month for JRPG fans.

## NONEWORLD — Monochromatic Turn-Based RPG Launches on Steam

**NONEWORLD** launched on Steam on **June 21** from first-time studio **After Dark Entertainment** at **$8.99** (with a 10% launch discount through June 28).

The game is a short-form turn-based RPG adventure set in a strange, dying universe. You play as a **Princess from another world** who awakens in a forgotten castle and teams up with a small party of "fruity colored" companions to fight through it. A **Cheshire Cat** joins the party and the tone is described as trippy and surreal, drawing comparison to *Omori* and *In Stars and Time* in its hand-drawn monochromatic art.

**Scope:** The full experience runs roughly **3+ hours**, with 40+ abilities and skills, four party members each with unique moves and combo potential, and several boss encounters. RPGFan's weekly roundup flagged it as one of five notable RPGs releasing this week (June 21–27), noting the game has a "creepy vibe" alongside a necromancy theme. This is the studio's debut release.

Sources: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3365680/NONEWORLD/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/21/rpgs-coming-this-week-june-21-27-2026/)

## NTE: Neverness to Everness — Hotta Studio's Supernatural Urban RPG Hits Steam July 16

**Hotta Studio** (the developer behind *Tower of Fantasy*) has set **July 16, 2026** as the Steam and Epic Games Store launch date for **NTE: Neverness to Everness**, the studio's free-to-play supernatural urban open-world RPG.

NTE has been available on PC via its own standalone client and on **iOS, macOS, iPadOS, Android, and PS5**, but the Steam/Epic launch marks its first appearance on the major PC storefronts. The game supports **full cross-play** across all platforms.

**Setting:** The city of **Hethereau**, where you play as the city's first "unlicensed" **Anomaly Hunter**, working out of a rundown antique shop called **Eibon**. The game blends neon-lit urban open-world exploration with a cast of supernatural companions, promising a mix of action combat, urban mystery, and slice-of-life storytelling.

The Steam page is already live for wishlisting ahead of the July 16 debut.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20422-neverness-to-everness-nte-steam-release-date-july-epic) · [Gamecom](https://gamecom.website/blog/nte-neverness-to-everness-steam-release-date-july-2026) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4508340/NTE_Neverness_to_Everness/)

## Steam Next Fest June 2026 — Final Day

**Steam Next Fest June 2026** closes today at **1 PM ET / 10 AM PT**, capping an event that launched with over **4,300 demos** — up from roughly 2,400 at the equivalent event in June 2025. The JRPG-adjacent demos that drew the most attention during the two-week run were **Echoes of Aincrad** (Bandai Namco, consistently in the top-5 overall) and **Stories of Somnia**, with **SoulStream Saga: Chapter 1** and **Embers of the Uncrowned** also generating notable community discussion. All demos go offline at close; the next Steam Next Fest is expected in October 2026.
