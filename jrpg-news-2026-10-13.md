## Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III] Launches on Switch 2 — Reviews Note KH III Frame Rate Issues

**Square Enix** and **Disney** released the **Kingdom Hearts Collection [I~III]** for **Nintendo Switch 2** on **October 8, 2026**, priced at **$74.99**. The package combines *KINGDOM HEARTS – HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX*, *KINGDOM HEARTS HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue*, and *KINGDOM HEARTS III + Re Mind DLC* in three separate downloads totalling approximately **131 GB**.

A notable distinction from earlier handheld-adjacent releases is that this collection runs natively — no internet connection is required and there is no cloud streaming.

Reviews are split along generational lines. Scores range from 4.5/10 to 9.5/10 across outlets. The older games in *1.5+2.5* draw strong marks — reviewers cite stable 60 fps and clean visuals — while *Kingdom Hearts III* and *0.2 Birth by Sleep – A Fragmentary Passage* are the weak points, with frame rate drops in docked mode and stuttering in handheld. RPG Site called the collection "a hard recommendation to make without caveats on Switch 2" if *Kingdom Hearts III* is the priority, but an easy one for players whose interest stops at *1.5+2.5*.

## Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen Launches October 9 — First Dragon's Dogma on Nintendo Hardware

**Capcom** released **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** on **October 9, 2026**, marking the franchise's first-ever appearance on a Nintendo platform. Switch 2 players receive a **$49.99 bundle** containing the base game plus the expansion; existing PC owners can purchase the expansion standalone for **$29.99**.

The *Dark Arisen* expansion introduces **Norgan**, a snow-covered northern region, along with new vocations, a new ice dragon, relic digging and appraisal mechanics, and **12 Lost Rites** dungeon challenges. The expansion also brings new character creator options.

Two pre-release patches (June and late August) improved frame rate, added quality-of-life changes, and increased save slot counts for all platforms ahead of the expansion's arrival.

## Octopath Traveler 1 & 2 Now Available on Switch 2 — No Upgrade Path for Existing Owners

Both **Octopath Traveler** and **Octopath Traveler II** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **October 1, 2026**, timed to the series' eighth anniversary. Each game is priced at **$59.99** individually; a digital bundle costs **$74.99**. Physical editions use Game-Key Cards.

The Switch 2 versions target 1080p/60fps, matching the PS4 editions' visual profile. However, **Square Enix confirmed no free upgrade path** for existing Switch owners, and **save data does not carry over** from the Switch versions to the Switch 2 editions — a decision that drew criticism from players who already own either title.

## Final Fantasy Resonance — Final Trailer Released, Demo Available Ahead of October 22 Launch

**Square Enix** released the **final trailer** for **Final Fantasy Resonance** this week ahead of the game's **October 22, 2026 launch** on Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PC via Steam (October 23 on Steam), and also PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series. The trailer features the theme song **"INSIDE"** performed by **WurtS** and runs approximately four minutes.

A **free Chapter 1 demo** is currently available with **save data carry-over** to the full game. Resonance is an **HD-2D standalone RPG** built from the first chapter of the defunct mobile title *Brave Exvius*, rebuilt without gacha mechanics and with traditional Final Fantasy structure. The game was first revealed at the June 2026 Nintendo Direct.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Arrives October 16 with Encounter Toggle and Auto-Battle

**Bandai Namco** and developer **TOSE** release **Tales of Eternia Remastered** on **October 16, 2026** for Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PC via Steam (and also PS5 and PS4). The game received a **PEGI 12** rating ahead of launch.

The remaster adds several modern conveniences absent from the original 2001 PS1 release: **destination markers**, an option to **disable random encounters**, and an **accelerated auto-battle mode**. Players can also switch between a new graphics presentation and the original one, and between new and original sound-effect sets.

## Shadow of the Road Pushed to March 10, 2027

**Owlcat Games** and **Another Angle Games** have delayed turn-based RPG **Shadow of the Road** from its **November 12, 2026** window to **March 10, 2027** on PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, and GOG). The announcement was posted to Steam on October 9, 2026.

The studio cited two reasons: additional time to rework tactical encounter design so that positioning, initiative, and ability choices carry more weight in every fight, and further tuning of progression systems to give players stronger incentives to experiment with skills. The move also sidesteps the late-2026 release calendar, which includes several large releases. A free demo has been on Steam since July 2025. The developers said additional trailers and world-building details will release during the delay period.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/shadow-of-the-road-delayed-to-march-10-2027) · [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/news/shadow-of-the-road-pushed-to-march-2027) · [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/thank-goodness-one-of-my-top-wishlishted-crpgs-is-dodging-world-of-warcraft-forever-and-grand-theft-auto-6s-warpath-with-a-new-release-date) · [PlayNews](https://www.playnews.gg/en/release-dates/shadow-of-the-road-release-date-pushed-to-march-10-2027-on-steam-epic-and-gog)
