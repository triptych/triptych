# JRPG News — May 17, 2026

## Dragon Quest 40th Anniversary Livestream Set for May 27

**Yuji Horii** has confirmed a special livestream event for **Dragon Quest**'s 40th anniversary on **May 27, 2026**, promising the reveal of the series' **next game** alongside "various other things besides the next game." The long-anticipated **Dragon Quest XII: The Flames of Fate** — first announced during the 35th anniversary in 2021 — remains the most likely subject, though Horii has not confirmed the title by name. Fans are interpreting this as the end of a prolonged silence on the mainline series' future.

---

## LumenTale: Memories of Trey Launches May 26

**LumenTale: Memories of Trey**, a creature-collector JRPG from indie developer **Team Trey**, arrives May 26 on **Nintendo Switch** and **PC via Steam**.

The game follows Trey, a boy recovering lost memories while traveling the world of Talea. Players collect roughly **140 Animon** — mystical beings tied to an emotion-influencing force called Anivis — across **13 elemental types**, with battles supporting both **1v1** and **4v4 party formats**. The game also features cooking, crafting at Fountain hubs, and a dedicated training space called the Anispace.

Reviews describe it as sitting between a traditional creature collector and a story-driven JRPG, with elemental matchups and party composition as the combat backbone.

## The Remake of the End of the Greatest RPG of All Time — May 28 on PC

A uniquely meta indie title is launching May 28 on **PC via Steam**: **The Remake of the End of the Greatest RPG of All Time**, developed by **Team Azule** and **Coin Drop Games** and published by **Glootschke Games**.

The premise: players start at **level 99** in the final hour of a legendary JRPG that never existed. Armed with a digital manual, director's commentary, and archival footage, you experience story climaxes for characters you never met and heroic sacrifices for side characters whose arcs you never saw — all while the game blurs the line between fiction and reality. The experience runs **4–6 hours**.

## Octopath Traveler TTRPG Rulebook Coming West in October

**Square Enix** and **Dark Horse Comics** have confirmed an English localization of the **Octopath Traveler Tabletop RPG Rulebook**, launching **October 6, 2026** in the West for **$59.99** (hardcover).

The TTRPG has been available in Japan since February 2022 and is built around the world and systems of the original *Octopath Traveler*. This marks its first official Western release.

---

## Fate/EXTRA Record Delayed Indefinitely, Bandai Namco Steps Down as Publisher

**TYPE-MOON** has confirmed that **Fate/EXTRA Record** — the full remake of *Fate/EXTRA* — has been delayed past its previously planned **Spring 2026** window with no new release date given. Simultaneously, **Bandai Namco** has stepped down as the title's publisher. Development is continuing and a new publisher and date will be announced once determined.

The remake, targeting **PS5, PS4, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**, has been in development for several years and was originally announced as a remake of the 2010 PSP RPG with significantly expanded content and modernized production values.
