## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct — True Protagonist Revealed, Manga Adaptation Announced

Nintendo broadcast a dedicated **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct** on **August 4** running approximately 20 minutes, delivering the game's most significant story revelations since its initial announcement.

The Direct confirmed that **Cai, Dietrich, Theodora, and Leda** — the four previously showcased protagonists who compete in the Heroic Games tournament — are not the game's true lead. The actual protagonist is a **nameless hero clad in white**, summoned five years in the future by the progenitor goddess **Sothis** after the demon god **Balor** has already destroyed the world. Unable to face Balor alone, the white-clad hero travels to the past to influence the fates of the four champions and forge them into allies for the final confrontation. Players can pursue the four storylines in any order, with each champion's progress saved independently.

The Direct also detailed the full **class system**, non-combat village activities, and how the two timelines eventually converge. One illustrated highlight: the Theodora route grants the game's cleric character a firearm, which the broader gaming press quickly seized on.

Following the Direct, Nintendo and Shueisha separately confirmed a **manga adaptation** of *Fortune's Weave* launching in the **October 2026 issue of Saikyo Jump** (on sale in September), with the prologue chapter simultaneously posted on the **Shonen Jump+** digital platform.

*Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave* launches on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 17, 2026**.

## Suikoden STAR LEAP Launches in Japan — First New Suikoden Entry in 14 Years

**Konami** released **Suikoden STAR LEAP** on **iOS and Android in Japan on August 7**, the franchise's first all-new entry in fourteen years. The game was developed by **MYTHRIL** and is set within the Suikoden universe, drawing on the series' signature 108 Stars of Destiny framework while building an original storyline.

Konami aired a **Suikoden Live stream on August 6** — the evening before launch — featuring character introductions, system deep dives, and voice actor appearances. A **PC release via Steam** has been confirmed for a later date; neither a global mobile launch window nor a Western release date has been announced.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition — Reviews Round-Up

**Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** launched digitally on **July 30** and reviews have now arrived in full. The critical consensus praises the technical upgrade as a genuine leap while noting the core content additions beyond the new Momo Blade crossover and costumes are limited.

- **CGMagazine — 9/10:** Calls the visual presentation a near-remaster-level improvement, highlighting a stable 4K/60fps docked experience as the headline upgrade
- **Digital Foundry (via Nintendo Life):** Confirms 4K upscaled from an internal 1080p render in docked mode, stable 60fps across both handheld and docked; cuts that were previously sub-30fps on Switch 1 now run smoothly
- **RPGFan:** "Worthwhile upgrade" — recommends the edition for both returning players and newcomers, noting the Momo Blade crossover content from *Xenosaga* as a meaningful addition for series fans
- **RPG Site:** In-depth Switch 1 vs. Switch 2 comparison; textures, cutscene quality, and environmental detail are substantially improved; Merc Assault cap increase (four to six Blades per mission) is the most notable gameplay change
- **TheSixthAxis:** "The ultimate form of this classic JRPG" — praises the edition as removing the visual rough edges that divided players on the original release

The game is priced at **$59.99** for new buyers. A physical edition releases **October 1, 2026**.

## Expeditions: Samurai — Enters Steam Early Access

**Expeditions: Samurai** launched into **Steam Early Access on August 7** at a **34% launch discount valid through August 14**. The game is developed by **Campfire Cabal** — a studio founded by veterans of **Logic Artists**, creators of *Expeditions: Conquistador*, *Viking*, and *Rome* — and is published by **THQ Nordic**.

Set in **Sengoku-era Japan circa 1600**, the party-based RPG casts players as navigators of a war-divided country, resolving conflicts through stealth, diplomacy, or combat. The Early Access build contains nearly all of the game's major feature systems alongside a substantial content slice. The developer has published a **three-update roadmap** covering the Early Access period, with the full release targeting **Q1 2027**.

## Titan Quest II — Spirit Mastery and Crafting System Now Live on PC

**THQ Nordic** and developer **Grimlore Games** released the **Spirit Mastery & Crafting System** update for **Titan Quest II** (PC Early Access) on **August 7**, the game's second-largest content push since launch. The update follows directly after the conclusion of the **Wild Lands** chapter (June 18 – August 7).

**Spirit Mastery** is a new summoner-style class built around channeling ancestral spirits and raising undead, filling the summoner archetype that had been absent from the Early Access class roster. The new **Crafting System** introduces a material-gathering and item-forging loop as a parallel progression path alongside loot drops, giving players more control over gear quality and build direction.
