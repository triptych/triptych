## Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle — Full Reveal Today

**Square Enix** has announced a brand-new **Dragon Quest** mobile RPG titled **Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle** (Japanese: *DQ RPG 魔界樹と導かれし島*). The announcement trailer premieres today, **November 10 at 20:00 JST**.

The game is a turn-based RPG for **iOS and Android**, currently targeting a **2027 release**. Square Enix is running a **closed beta from December 8–15**, with sign-ups open now through November 23. The game will be free-to-play with in-app purchases.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio – Switch 2 Edition Launches November 12

**Atlus** and **Sega**'s award-winning JRPG **Metaphor: ReFantazio** comes to **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12**, two days from today, priced at **$39.99** — a lower entry point than its original console release.

A limited physical **Steelbook Edition** ships alongside the digital launch. Reviewers who played early builds noted the port holds up well for a large-scale Atlus RPG, though an **RPG Site preview** flagged uneven framerate in busy areas, suggesting Atlus may deliver a day-one patch.

## Alleged Persona 1 Remake Footage Surfaces Under Title "Persona: Be Your True Mind"

Roughly **two minutes of unverified footage** purporting to show an in-development **Persona 1 remake** appeared online around **October 31, 2026**. The clip — initially posted to YouTube under the account name *P1REMAKE* — spread quickly across Reddit and file-hosting sites before takedowns began.

The footage reportedly shows dungeon exploration, combat in the modern *Persona* style, and a dialogue scene. Some outlets note that a **Persona 2 protagonist model** appears as a placeholder during battle, hinting the footage is an internal build. The possible title **"Persona: Be Your True Mind"** was spotted in the clip by several outlets; others describe the footage as very low resolution, raising questions about authenticity.

**Atlus has not confirmed a Persona 1 remake.** The leak arrives weeks after Atlus publicly stated it was "considering more JRPG revivals" following the success of *Persona 4 Revival*. Treat this as an unverified rumor.

Sources: [Games Radar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/persona/two-minutes-of-persona-1-remake-footage-seemingly-leaks-online-weeks-after-atlus-said-it-was-considering-more-jrpg-revivals/) · [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/rpg/footage-of-what-sure-looks-like-a-persona-1-remake-has-leaked) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/31/rumor-persona-1-remake-gameplay-footage-gets-leaked-online/) · [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/persona-1-remake-apparently-revealed-through-leaked-trailer/)

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and developer **Gust** will shut down the Japanese server for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, closing the book on the Atelier franchise's first major gacha spinoff.

The global (English) version had already shut down in **March 2025** after just 14 months, citing an inability to maintain content quality for players. The Japan-side service lasted somewhat longer but now follows suit. No reason was given for the Japan closure; the development team issued a brief thank-you statement to players.

Sources: [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25) · [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/atelier-resleriana/end-of-service-announcement) · [Game8](https://game8.co/articles/latest/atelier-resleriana-and-soul-tide-both-announce-end-of-service)

## Edge of Memories Launches Despite Studio Liquidation

In a surprise outcome, **Midgar Studio**'s action RPG **Edge of Memories** launched on **November 5, 2026 for PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series**. The game's release was considered in doubt after a French commercial court ordered **Midgar Studio's liquidation in July 2026** — the second studio casualty of publisher **Nacon**'s financial collapse.

Apparently the game was complete enough at the time of liquidation that Nacon (or a surviving entity) was able to ship it. Deluxe Edition owners received early access on November 3. The game is a sequel in the *Edge of Eternity* universe, with the original title having passed one million players. No post-launch support roadmap has been announced.

Sources: [Gematsu launch date](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/edge-of-memories-launches-november-5) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/edge-of-memories-releasing-in-november/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21448-edge-of-memories-release-date)

## Fate/EXTRA Record Confirmed for January 28, 2027 — Combat Completely Redesigned

**Aniplex**, **Meteorise**, and **TYPE-MOON**'s long-delayed **Fate/EXTRA Record** finally has a firm date: **January 28, 2027**, releasing on **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. Western publishing falls to **XSEED Games** (North America) and **Marvelous Europe**.

The game made its first public playable appearance at **Tokyo Game Show 2026**. Hands-on reports describe a dramatic departure from the original game's "rock-paper-scissors" combat: the remake uses a **deck-building, turn-based command system** where cards enter the player's hand each turn and Masters set traps for opposing players to navigate. RPG Site's preview called the new system "pretty good so far for a game caught in development hell." Automaton published an extended Q&A with Aniplex addressing the development handover from Bandai Namco and the departure of the project's original director.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://gematsu.com/2026/09/fate-extra-record-launches-january-28-2027-for-ps5-switch-2-ps4-switch-and-pc) · [RPG Site preview](https://www.rpgsite.net/preview/21523-fateextra-record-feels-pretty-good-so-far-for-game-that-was-caught-in-development-hell) · [RPGamer preview](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/fate-extra-record-preview/) · [Automaton developer Q&A](https://automaton-media.com/en/interviews/fate-extra-record-showcase-and-publisher-qa-aniplex-explains-modern-jrpg-overhaul-and-new-systems-addresses-concerns-about-development-handover/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/04/fate-extra-record-tgs-2026-playable-demo/)
