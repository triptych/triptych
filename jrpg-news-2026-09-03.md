## State of Play September 3 — Final Fantasy VII Revelation Gets April 8, 2027 Release Date, Extended Wutai Gameplay Revealed

**Square Enix** closed today's **PlayStation State of Play** with the most substantial look at **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** yet: a **10-minute extended gameplay segment** focused on the **Wutai region**, confirming an **April 8, 2027 simultaneous worldwide release** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, Microsoft Store)**, and PlayStation 5.

The gameplay segment confirms that Wutai plays a much larger role in *Revelation* than it did in the original *Final Fantasy VII*, with Yuffie as a co-lead alongside Cloud for the Wutai chapters. The segment showed the **open-world structure** of Wutai's territory — a multi-island chain with verticality achieved through chocobos, wire traversal, and a water-skimming mechanic tied to Yuffie's Windstorm ability. The **FITS job system** (shown previously) allows Cloud to equip Yuffie's ninjutsu abilities mid-combat through a new cross-character Materia slot.

Square Enix also confirmed that *Revelation* will include **two story expansion passes**: one focused on **Vincent Valentine**, and a second described only as "an untold encounter in the Planet's past."

## Konami Announces Rhapsody in Scarlet — Jazz-Age NYC Action-RPG from ILCA

Also revealed during today's State of Play and its accompanying **State of Play Japan** broadcast, **Konami** and developer **ILCA** unveiled **Rhapsody in Scarlet**, a new action-RPG set in **1920s New York City** during the jazz age.

Players control **Ellie Myers**, a mage who fights alongside three magical beasts — **Sköll**, **Balhut**, and **Thunderbird** — using a "highly flexible combo system" that weaves long-range spellcasting with beast-summon attacks. The reveal trailer showed Ellie unleashing large screen-spanning magical attacks while co-op beast abilities layer on top. Producer **Takanori Murayama** elaborated during State of Play Japan that the jazz soundtrack was composed to capture the era's atmosphere, and that the Prohibition-era setting informs the game's tone of danger lurking beneath glittering surfaces.

Murayama compared the game's ambition to *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* in terms of "European-influenced aesthetic with Japanese RPG DNA" — though Rhapsody grounds itself in a distinctly New York mythology. **No release window** was announced; platforms confirmed so far are **PC** only (additional platforms TBA).

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Launches Tonight — First New Entry in 20+ Years

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** unlocks on **PC via Steam at 9:00 PM PDT tonight (September 3)**, which is 4:00 AM UTC on September 4 — and on **Nintendo Switch 2** at local midnight going into September 4. This marks the return of the Onimusha franchise for the first time in over two decades.

The game stars **Miyamoto Musashi**, whose likeness is modeled after classic film icon **Toshiro Mifune**, as he battles supernatural forces with a demonic gauntlet in feudal Japan. Capcom originally had the game slated for **September 25**, but moved the date forward by three weeks earlier this summer.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches September 17 on Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**Nihon Falcom** and **GungHo Online Entertainment** have locked September 17, 2026 as the worldwide release for **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter**, the full remake of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC*, arriving on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** simultaneously.

The remake picks up immediately after last year's *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter* remake — following Estelle's search for Joshua as the shadow organization Ouroboros tightens its grip. The hybrid combat system returns with new **Crafts**, **S-Crafts**, and the addition of the **Brave Rush** mechanic, and the game retains the series' dense web of NPC dialogue that shifts with each plot beat.

Both physical and digital editions will be available for Switch 2 and Switch in the West; Japan gets physical releases limited to PS5 and Switch 2. Japanese pre-orders come with a remastered version of the original 2006 PC game as a bonus.

## Another Eden Begins Launches September 17 — Kato and Mitsuda's Offline JRPG

**Wright Flyer Studios** / **Studio Prisma** will release **Another Eden Begins** on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** — a standalone, fully offline JRPG with a complete beginning-to-end narrative, distinct from the ongoing mobile game *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space*.

The creative team is a significant draw: **Masato Kato** (story and scenario writer on *Chrono Trigger* and *Xenogears*) directs, while **Yasunori Mitsuda** (*Xenogears*, *Chrono Cross*) composes the soundtrack. The game is a time-traveling turn-based RPG, and Studio Prisma has described it as everything they wanted to do in the mobile game without the constraints of a live-service format.

A Western physical release is planned for **2027 via Aksys Games** for players who prefer a boxed copy.

## Final Fantasy XIV Switch 2 — Free Subscription Period Ends Today; Port Praised by Critics

**Final Fantasy XIV**'s **Nintendo Switch 2** version launched on **August 4**, and its **free early-access subscription period ends today (September 4)**. Square Enix waived subscription fees for the first month to manage server load from the influx of new and returning players, and the servers held up better than many MMO launches of its scale.

Critical reception has been strong: reviewers highlighted that the game runs at a stable framerate in both docked and handheld modes, with Nintendo Life calling it "an expansive port that demands your commitment" and noting that portable play is "the Switch 2 version's greatest strength." MMORPG.com reported being "blown away" by what Square Enix achieved in terms of visual quality on the platform.

The Switch 2 version supports mouse-and-keyboard input via USB-C, uses a separate subscription from Switch Online, and does not require Nintendo Switch Online. A free trial accessible via the Nintendo eShop (covering the base game and *Heavensward* up to level 60) launched alongside the paid version.

## Dragon Quest XI S Switch 2 Edition — No Upgrade Path from Switch Version

With its **September 24 release** approaching, **Square Enix** has confirmed several details for **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition** on **Nintendo Switch 2** that prospective players should know:

- **No upgrade pack** is available for existing Switch owners — the Switch 2 edition must be purchased separately at full price
- **Save data does not transfer** from the Switch version
- The physical release uses a **Game Key Card** (no ROM chip)
- New content includes additional story chapters starring party members, new optimization modes for graphics vs. performance prioritization, and an orchestral score toggle

The Switch 2 edition includes all previously released DLC and the ability to toggle between the game's 3D and retro 2D modes, as well as both Japanese and English voice acting.
