## Final Fantasy Resonance — Critical Reception: 87 Average, 97% Positive on Steam

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy Resonance** launched on **October 22, 2026** for PC (Steam), Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and consoles. The HD-2D turn-based RPG is drawing strong critical praise across the board.

Review scores gathered this week:

- **Famitsu**: 36/40 (four reviewers, each scoring 9)
- **Game Informer**: 9/10
- **COGconnected**: 9.5/10
- **Windows Central**: 4.5/5
- **Nintendo Life**: 8/10
- **IGN**: 8/10
- **GameSpot**: 7/10
- **Metacritic aggregate** (per secondary sources): ~87/100 across 45 critics

On **Steam**, the game holds a **Very Positive** rating with **97% positive reviews** from over 1,300 players at time of writing. Nintendo Life confirmed the main story runs approximately **20–30 hours**, with a full completionist run around **70 hours**.

Critics praised the HD-2D visuals and turn-based combat depth while flagging a predictable story and uneven pacing as the main weaknesses. The game continues Square Enix's strategy of releasing the HD-2D format simultaneously across PC and Nintendo platforms on day one.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered — Mixed Critical Reception

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** launched on **October 15–16, 2026** for PC, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and consoles. The remaster of the beloved 2000 PS1 RPG (later released on PSP) is drawing a divided critical response.

**OpenCritic aggregate**: ~75/100 (64% critics recommend)

Selected scores:
- **PlayStation Universe**: 8/10
- **Gamefa**: 8.2/10
- **Shacknews**: 5/10 — sharply critical of the AI video upscaling applied to anime cutscenes
- **Push Square**: 7/10
- **CGMagazine**: 7.5/10

Reviewers broadly praised the **restored skits** (previously cut from Western releases), **new English voice acting**, **retranslated script**, and **quality-of-life improvements** including difficulty and accessibility options. The consistent criticism across outlets targets the **dated visuals and world map design**, the age of the underlying game's structural feel, and the uneven remaster work compared to other recent Tales of entries.

The game marks the first Western release of *Tales of Eternia* since the 2006 PSP version.

Sources: [OpenCritic listing](https://opencritic.com/game/21435/tales-of-eternia-remastered) · [Playnews review roundup](https://www.playnews.gg/en/reviews/tales-of-eternia-remastered-how-good-are-the-reviews-scores-from-6-5-to-8-5-10) · [Shacknews review](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151003/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review-score) · [NoobFeed review](https://www.noobfeed.com/reviews/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review) · [Gematsu (Europe rating)](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/04/tales-of-eternia-remastered-rated-in-europe)

## Demons' Night Fever Has Launched — Disgaea Creator's New SRPG Now Available

**Demons' Night Fever**, the dark comedy strategy-RPG from **Disgaea** creator **Sohei Niikawa** and developer **Drecom/SuperNiche**, published by **Arc System Works**, launched on **October 22, 2026** for **PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch, and PS5**.

The game is priced at **€39.99 / ~$39.99** and is single-player only. Rather than a pure tactics game, it is a stat-raising SRPG built around villain management: players sacrifice and detonate their own allied Peons mid-battle, farming "Evil Misdeeds" to grow stronger — the core loop of the **Peon Exploitation System**. The narrative is deliberately designed to make God an overt scumbag so players don't feel guilty about committing atrocities.

No aggregated review scores were available at the time of writing. The Inven Global launch-day article confirmed the game was available on all announced platforms without delay.

Sources: [Inven Global (launch)](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27565/demons-night-fever-from-disgaea-creator-sohei-niikawa-launches-on-ps5-switch-and-steam) · [Automaton (gameplay details)](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/disgaea-creator-sohei-niikawas-new-devil-training-srpg-lets-you-detonate-your-own-allies-to-farm-evil-misdeeds-and-grow-stronger/) · [RPG Site (announcement)](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/17820-arc-system-works-announces-demons-night-fever-a-simulation-rpg-produced-by-the-creator-of-disgaea) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/game/demons-night-fever/)

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Launches This Week — New Worldline, Fully Re-Recorded Cast

**STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT**, the full remake of the 2009 visual novel developed by **MAGES** and published by **Spike Chunsoft**, launches **October 29, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PS5** in North America. It previously launched in Japan on August 20, 2026.

The remake is not a straight remaster: it adds a **brand-new worldline and ending scenario** not present in the original, and **all in-game voice lines have been newly recorded** in Japanese. Visuals are rebuilt in 4K with updated character art.

**North American physical and digital editions** are unaffected and ship October 29. However, **UK and EU physical editions** (both Standard and SteelBook) have been delayed to **November 3, 2026** due to unforeseen logistics circumstances; UK/EU digital is unaffected.

**Edition pricing:**
- Standard Edition (physical/digital): $59.99
- SteelBook Edition: $69.99

Pre-orders are currently open for physical editions via Spike Chunsoft's online store.

## Phantom Blade Zero — Two Days From Launch, Pre-Orders Have Exceeded Expectations

**S-Game**'s wuxia action RPG **Phantom Blade Zero** launches **October 29, 2026** for **PC (Steam)** (also PS5). After an earlier delay from its original September 9 window, the developer has confirmed the October 29 date is firm.

**Editions and pricing:**
- Standard Edition: $60
- Deluxe Edition: $70

The game emphasizes **parrying-based combat** across 30+ main weapons and 25 secondary "Phantom Edge" weapon types, with three difficulty modes. A **State of Play appearance** earlier this year detailed the weapon variety and the Wuxia Qi energy system underpinning combat.

The producer stated pre-orders have **"far exceeded expectations"** since they opened. A **CES 2026 hands-on preview** of a near-final build called the game well-optimized and close to release-ready; no review embargo lift date has been confirmed as of October 27.

Sources: [Seasoned Gaming (State of Play / pre-order trailer)](https://seasonedgaming.com/2026/08/11/phantom-blade-zero-pre-order-trailer-and-state-of-play/) · [The Outer Haven (preview)](https://www.theouterhaven.net/phantom-blade-zero-preview-hands-on-with-a-new-ces-2026-build-that-feels-nearly-final/) · [Exputer (pre-orders)](https://exputer.com/news/games/phantom-blade-zero-preorders-exceeded-expectations/) · [RPGamer (launch date)](https://rpgamer.com/2025/12/phantom-blade-zero-launching-in-september/)

## Villion: Code — Western Release Date Set for January 26, 2027

**Idea Factory International** confirmed this week that **Villion: Code**, Compile Heart's school action RPG, will launch in **North America and Europe on January 26, 2027**.

The game arrives on **PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PS5, and PS4** in the West. Physical editions are planned for **PS5, PS4, and Switch in North America**, and **PS5 only in Europe**. Pre-orders for physical copies are open now.
