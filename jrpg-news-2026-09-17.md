## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Critical Acclaim — Tied for Series' Best

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems** released **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** today, September 17, exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2**, and reviews are exceptional.

The game holds an **89 on Metacritic** based on early scores, with **IGN awarding a perfect 10/10** and 13 outlets matching that score. Critics repeatedly highlight the four interconnected protagonist stories set in the **Dagdan Empire**, the enormous strategic depth, and rich character progression. Gamereactor called it "undeniably one of the best titles of the year." The game is currently **tied with Fire Emblem: Three Houses as the highest-rated entry in the franchise** on Metacritic.

Some reviewers noted the game's slower pacing and sheer volume of content can be overwhelming, but that criticism was largely framed as a sign of the game's ambition rather than a flaw. Game Informer scored it 8.75/10 and GameSpot 9/10.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches — Best-Reviewed Game of 2026 So Far

**Nihon Falcom** and **NIS America**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** also released today on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC**, and it has surged to the top of 2026's review charts.

The PC version sits at a **93 on Metacritic** (10 reviews) and the PS5 version at **92** (13 reviews), making it the best-reviewed game of 2026, surpassing Resident Evil Requiem and Pokémon Pokopia. Following directly from last year's *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter* remake, the game continues Estelle Bright's story and is being praised as a definitive way to experience the classic arc.

Notable review excerpts:
- **Game Informer (9.5/10):** "*2nd Chapter* masterfully updates a timeless story, accentuating it with visual enhancements and phenomenal voice acting."
- **RPGFan:** "Easily the best of both worlds and a shining example of how a remake can offer something new while maintaining what made the original so special."
- **Dual Shockers:** "A flawless execution and a fitting conclusion to the Bracer duo's story."

Sources: [Shacknews review](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150733/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review-score) · [Game Informer review](https://gameinformer.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/a-superb-sequel) · [RPGFan review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/) · [Dual Shockers review](https://www.dualshockers.com/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review/) · [Nintendo Everything review](https://nintendoeverything.com/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review-for-nintendo-switch-2-and-switch/)

## Tokyo Game Show 2026 Opens — Square Enix Brings FFVII Revelation, KH4, FF Resonance Demos

**Tokyo Game Show 2026** opened today at Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan, running through September 21 — the event's 30th anniversary, with a five-day format for the first time under the theme *"The Longest Five Days of Nonstop Play."*

**Square Enix** has the most prominent JRPG presence, with playable demos at the venue for:

- **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** — Three photo spots themed around the *FFVII Remake* trilogy (the Scorpion Sentinel, the Forgotten Capital, and Diamond Weapon) accompany a playable build.
- **Final Fantasy Resonance** — A massive pixel art panel is on display alongside the playable HD-2D tactical RPG.
- **Dragon Quest VII Reimagined** — Playable from the beginning at the Family Game Park area, making it the highest-profile TGS showing for the February 2026 release to date.
- **Kingdom Hearts IV** — Present at the booth, but reports ahead of the show indicated Square Enix would not be revealing substantial new footage; the game's previously released trailers anchor its presence.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Launched September 4 — Surpasses 1 Million Sales

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched on September 4 for **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC**, and it crossed **1 million copies sold worldwide on launch day**.

The action RPG — the first mainline Onimusha in over 20 years — holds an **86 on Metacritic** and **86 on OpenCritic** (95% of critics recommend). **IGN awarded it a perfect 10/10**, the franchise's first from that outlet, and **Nintendo Life gave it 9/10**. Critics praised its engaging combat, rich visuals, and entertaining characters, with the deliberate samurai-styled gameplay drawing favorable comparisons. The main point of division among reviewers was whether the 25-hour campaign earns its length.

Sources: [Nintendo Life review roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-onimusha-way-of-the-sword-are-in) · [Giant Bomb review](https://giantbomb.com/reviews/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review) · [Forbes review](https://www.forbes.com/sites/games/2026/08/31/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review-more-inagaki-than-kurosawa/) · [Review roundup via GameMetaHub](https://www.gamemetahub.com/games/onimusha-way-of-the-sword/review-roundup-launch-verdicts)

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Now Available on Switch 2 and PS5

**NIS America** released the **Nintendo Switch 2 and PlayStation 5 versions** of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** on **September 10**. Both existing Switch 1 and PC versions were also patched on the same day for compatibility and quality-of-life improvements.

The new platform versions feature **4K resolution, 120 frames per second**, and mouse input support via the Switch 2's USB-C port. Pricing is **$39.99 per game digitally**, or **$69.99 for a physical Deluxe Edition** containing both titles. A limited edition available through the NIS America Online Store for $109.99 includes a box, art book, soundtrack CDs, holographic art cards, an acrylic stand, iron-on insignias, and a Mishelam Wonderland coaster set.

## Blue Reflection Quartet Launched July 30 — Mixed Reviews for Four-Game Bundle

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust**'s **Blue Reflection Quartet** launched on July 30 for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**, bundling all four titles in the franchise: the original *Blue Reflection*, *Blue Reflection: Second Light*, anime series *Blue Reflection Ray*, and mobile title *Blue Reflection Sun*.

The collection landed at **76 on Metacritic** (10 reviews). Reviewers praised the series' atmospheric storytelling and charm — Final Weapon gave it 4/5 calling it "magical girl JRPG at its most heartfelt" — but criticized the Ray and Sun adaptations specifically as "extremely lackluster." Gaming Respawn scored it 8/10; Game8 gave it 68, calling it "less than the sum of its parts."

Sources: [Koei Tecmo America press release](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/news/relive-the-emotional-enchanted-world-of-blue-reflection-in-its-entirety-with-blue-reflection-quartet-launching-july-30-2026/) · [RPG Site review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20992-blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/30/blue-reflection-quartet-launch-trailer-illustration/) · [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/21069/blue-reflection-quartet)

## Final Fantasy XIV Switch 2 — Positive Reception Since August 4 Launch

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy XIV Online** arrived on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4**, and the port has been received positively by critics. CGMagazine awarded it a **9/10** in its August 20 review, describing the launch as something that "seemed impossible, if not laughable" just years prior. The game runs at **30 FPS in towns** with improved performance during raids and instanced content; the Switch 2 version supports **mouse-and-keyboard input via USB-C** and delivers handheld MMORPG play that reviewers have called competitive with the Steam Deck version.

Sources: [CGMagazine review](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/final-fantasy-xiv-nsw2/) · [RPG Site impressions](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21052-final-fantasy-14-switch-2-port-review-vs-steam-deck-ps5-pro-xbox-pc-impressions) · [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/final-fantasy-xiv-on-switch-2-might-be-the-handheld-mmo-we-ve-been-waiting-for) · [Nintendo Life gameplay showcase](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/08/video-final-fantasy-xiv-online-switch-2-extended-gameplay-showcase)
