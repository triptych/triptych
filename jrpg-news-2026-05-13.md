# JRPG News — May 13, 2026

## Dragon Quest 40th Anniversary Stream Set for May 27 — Next Game Reveal Confirmed

Series creator **Yuji Horii** has confirmed a Dragon Quest anniversary livestream on **May 27, 2026** — the exact date the original Dragon Quest launched in Japan in 1986 — with an explicit promise to discuss the **next title in the franchise**.

While Horii stopped short of naming a game, **Dragon Quest 12: The Flames of Fate** remains the overwhelming fan expectation. First announced at the 35th anniversary stream in 2021, DQ12 has had an unusually quiet development period amid internal restructuring at Square Enix. Horii previously described the game as taking a "darker tone" with a revamped command-battle system.

Horii also noted the stream will cover "various other things besides the next title." Adding fuel to the fire, **Dragon Quest XI S** has reportedly been rated for Nintendo Switch 2, suggesting a potential enhanced re-release announcement may also be in the pipeline.

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Coming to PS5 and Switch 2 This Fall

**NIS America** and **Nihon Falcom** announced that both **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** are heading to PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 in **Fall 2026**.

The new versions come with meaningful enhancements:
- Up to **4K resolution** and **120 FPS** support
- Toggle between original and **HD-upscaled textures**
- **Nintendo Switch 2 mouse support**

Both games will be available separately on digital storefronts, or together as a **physical collection**. Note: the Switch 2 physical release will be a **Game-Key Card** (no full data on the cart).

The Crossbell arc — set between the Liberl arc (Trails in the Sky) and the Erebonia arc (Cold Steel) — is considered essential context for the broader Trails saga. These ports bring the duology to its first native PS5/Switch 2 versions at higher fidelity than any previous release.

## Mina the Hollower Gets May 29 Release Date

**Yacht Club Games** (Shovel Knight) has confirmed **Mina the Hollower** launches **May 29, 2026** on PS5, Nintendo Switch 1 & 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam / Humble Store) for **$19.99**.

Mina the Hollower is a top-down action RPG with a Victorian horror aesthetic, following a mouse engineer named Mina as she battles through a monster-infested island. The game has been in development since 2021, making this a long-awaited release for Yacht Club fans. The price point and multi-platform day-one launch align with Shovel Knight's original rollout strategy.

---

## Quartet Launches on Nintendo Switch

**Quartet**, the pixel-art turn-based RPG from **Something Classic Games**, arrived on Nintendo Switch on **May 12** for **$19.99**. The game originally launched on PC/Mac/Linux in 2025 and is now confirmed compatible with Switch 2 as well. PS4 and PS5 versions are planned to follow at a later date.

The game tells four interlocking stories — train robberies, a mysterious deck of cards, deadly airships, and accidental magic — that converge into a single larger plot. Combat fields **eight heroes** at once: four active fighters plus four in reserve who regenerate Action Points while waiting to be swapped in.
