## Final Fantasy Resonance — October 22, 2026 | HD-2D, All Platforms

Announced at yesterday's Nintendo Direct but deserving a full spotlight: **Final Fantasy Resonance** is the first ever HD-2D Final Fantasy title, launching **October 22, 2026** on Switch 2, Switch, PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Steam, and Microsoft Store at **$49.99**.

The game is a fully rebuilt console RPG based on *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* (mobile), not a port — Square Enix has described it as an "extensively rebuilt, console-quality RPG experience." It features turn-based combat with enemy stagger mechanics and cinematic "Resonance attacks," classic FF staples (airships, crystals, espers, moogles), and summoned helpers called Visions. The HD-2D aesthetic uses pixelized 3D models with dynamic camera angles, the same engine approach as *Octopath Traveler* and *Triangle Strategy* but applied to the FF brand for the first time.

## Echoes of Aincrad — July 10, 2026 | SAO Universe Action RPG

Bandai Namco has confirmed **Echoes of Aincrad**, a new standalone action RPG set in the *Sword Art Online* universe, launches **July 10, 2026** on PC (Steam), PS5, and Xbox Series X/S.

Developed by Game Studio Inc., the game puts players in a user-created hero's shoes rather than Kirito's — a first for the SAO franchise. The story is original, set on the 1st and 2nd floors of Aincrad alongside familiar characters Argo, Kirito, and Asuna. Combat is real-time with equipment upgrades and a partner system: each NPC companion has unique skills that deepen as your bond grows, unlocking new abilities. Pre-orders are live now.

## Threads of Time — Indie JRPG Revealed at Day of the Devs

**Threads of Time** was a standout at the Day of the Devs 2026 showcase (held alongside Summer Game Fest). Developed by Canadian indie studio **Riyo Games**, it's a turn-based JRPG with pixel art characters, 2.5D environments built in Unreal Engine 5, and an explicit love-letter tone drawing from *Chrono Trigger*, *Final Fantasy*, and *Xenogears*.

Players travel through history aboard a time ship called **The Needle** as part of a quest to restore the order of Time Knights. Combat pairs classic turn-based structure with time-bending abilities, multi-hit combo attacks, and cinematic team skills. A new trailer shown at the event highlighted The Needle and several historical-era environments.

No release date yet. Wishlist now on **Steam** and **Xbox Series X/S**.
