# JRPG News — June 3, 2026

## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth Launches on Nintendo Switch 2 and Xbox Today

The day's biggest JRPG release: **Final Fantasy VII Rebirth** is now available on **Nintendo Switch 2**, **Xbox Series X|S**, **Xbox on PC**, and **Xbox Cloud Gaming** as of June 3. Originally released on PS5 in February 2024 and Windows in January 2025, the second entry in Square Enix's FF7 Remake trilogy has now reached its final major platforms.

Key details:

- A **20% early-purchase discount** applies to the digital version through **June 10**
- The Switch 2 port scored an **86 on Metacritic** (42 reviews) — minor frame rate dips reported in open-world areas, but generally well received
- The release was announced during the **February 2026 Nintendo Direct Partner Showcase** alongside a launch trailer released in Japan
- Rebirth received **125+ perfect review scores** and **40 Game of the Year awards** during its original run — this brings that acclaim to a much wider audience

## Persona 6 Leak Round-Up: Protagonist, Setting, and SGF Speculation

Ahead of **Summer Game Fest** (main show June 5) and the **Xbox Games Showcase** (June 7–8), a wave of Persona 6 leaks has consumed JRPG fan forums this week. Here's a summary of the credible reports:

**Protagonist design** — Concept art leaked via a domestic/overseas outsourced animation team on Douyin (quickly deleted). A reliable SEGA/Atlus insider confirmed the images as genuine; the leaker was reportedly fined **100,000 RMB** for distributing them.

- The **male protagonist** has a short blonde bowl cut and green eyes
- A female character named **Yoko Kusakabe** (also romanized as Rinko) with short black-and-red hair appeared in the leak — initially thought to be a co-protagonist, she has since been identified as a supporting character
- The game **appears to be set in Yokohama**
- Insider lolilolailo claims Atlus is targeting a **September 2027** release window; a separate leaker reported the game is feature-complete but delayed internally
- The game is rumored to feature a **more melancholic tone** compared to recent entries

**Where will it be revealed?** SGF 2026 (June 5) and the Xbox Games Showcase are the two most-cited candidates. Atlus producer Kazuhisa Wada promised major franchise news in 2026, and 2026 marks the **30th anniversary of Persona** as a series. Atlus's existing relationship with Xbox has fueled speculation about a multiplatform Day 1 release.

Nothing has been confirmed by Atlus or SEGA.

## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Pre-Orders Open

**Cygames** has opened pre-orders for **Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok**, a standalone expanded release of the 2024 action RPG, launching **July 9, 2026** on Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, PS4, and PC via Steam.

New content highlights:

- **Six new playable characters** in total, including **Fraux** and **Fediel** — bringing the full roster to **29 characters**
- **Brand-new story** alongside additional co-op quest tiers and new bosses
- **The Conflux** — an all-new solo mode with procedurally unpredictable challenges
- **Master Traits** system to push characters beyond previous stat ceilings
- **Summons** added as a new tactical layer
- Full **crossplay** across all platforms

Pricing: Standard Edition **$59.99**, Special Edition **$79.99**. Upgrade kits for owners of the original Relink are available at **$29.99** (Standard) and **$54.99** (Special Edition). Physical editions are available for Switch 2 and PS5.

## Echoes of Aincrad: New Sword Art Online Action RPG Arriving July 10

**Bandai Namco** has announced that **Echoes of Aincrad**, a new action RPG set in the *Sword Art Online* universe, launches **July 10, 2026** on **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam)**.

Unlike SAO adaptations that follow Kirito, this title is a series first: **players create their own hero** and forge their own path through the floating castle of Aincrad. Key gameplay features:

- Real-time action combat with upgradeable equipment, weapons, attributes, and selectable special skills
- A customizable **NPC partner** whose abilities deepen as the relationship develops, unlocking new skills and more powerful combos
- Open exploration across diverse environments — cities, plains, and dungeons — with quests and rare treasure hunts

**Editions:**

| Edition | Price |
|---|---|
| Standard | $69.99 |
| Deluxe (includes Expansion DLC + early Death Game Mode unlock) | $89.99 |
| Ultimate | $109.99 |

The SAO expanded universe angle (original protagonist rather than Kirito) has generated cautious interest among both franchise fans and JRPG fans who had written off prior SAO game adaptations.
