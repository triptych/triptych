## Neptunia Unlimited Launches in Japan — First Mainline Entry in Eleven Years

**Compile Heart** and **Idea Factory**'s **Neptunia Unlimited** (full title: *Hyperdimension Neptunia ∞*) launched in Japan on **August 27** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4**, and **PlayStation 5**. Standard edition pricing is **8,580 yen**; the PS4 version is digital-only.

The game marks the first new mainline *Neptunia* entry since *Megadimension Neptunia VII* in 2015. The story follows **Neptune** after a catastrophic meteor strike levels Gamindustri, leaving her alone in a white featureless void. She awakens with a "retry" ability that lets her loop between timelines and rewrite outcomes — the mechanic structures the narrative around branching endings rather than a single linear path.

Despite the eleven-year gap, early hands-on impressions describe the game as distinctly on-brand: self-aware humor, heavy gaming industry satire, and the series' established CPU transformation and aerial combat systems. Each title in the franchise has an independent enough story that new players can start here without prior installments. Western release window has not been confirmed.

## Brigandine: Abyss Launches — Dense, Demanding SRPG Earns Solid Reviews

**Happinet** published **Brigandine: Abyss** on **August 27** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam**, and PlayStation 5. The sequel to *Brigandine: The Legend of Runersia* (2020) arrives six years after that game's cult revival.

Set on the continent of **Meltitea**, the story begins after the Kingdom of Solginat falls to a scheming adviser corrupted by a dark force called the **Abyss**. Players choose from **six story campaigns** tied to different factions, or tackle a **Missions Mode** where they command one of **24 factions** each with distinct win conditions — a scale considerably broader than the previous game's already-generous structure.

The core loop fuses grand-strategy territorial control with discrete tactical battle phases, a combination the series has always positioned between Fire Emblem and Dynasty Warriors Empires. Reviews land the game as a genuine must-buy for strategy-RPG enthusiasts willing to engage with its steep learning curve, though critics note it falters in presentation and pacing compared to its predecessors.

- **Famitsu**: 8/8/8/8 (32/40)
- **Cubed3** (Switch 2 version): 8/10
- **Game8**: 70/100 — "a good strategy game with a few too many problems"

The Switch 2 version targets 30 fps but is not a fully locked framerate; PC and other console versions offer more robust performance options.

## Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2 Out Now — MGS4's First Port in 18 Years

**Konami** released **Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2** on **August 27** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam**, and PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The collection is priced at **$49.99**.

The package includes:

- **Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots** — its first official port from PlayStation 3 since the game's original 2008 release, making this its debut on PC and Nintendo hardware
- **Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker** — HD version previously released on PS3/360/Vita
- **Metal Gear: Ghost Babel** (bonus title, Game Boy Color) — a standalone side story

MGS4's inclusion is the headline: the game has been PlayStation-exclusive hardware for 18 years due to technical complexity and licensing considerations. The Vol. 2 collection's PC and Switch 2 versions therefore represent the first time *Guns of the Patriots* has been accessible outside Sony platforms.

## HoYoverse Reveals Nodus Fall at Gamescom — Dark Fantasy Co-op RPG Built in Unreal Engine 5

**HoYoverse** unveiled a brand-new project called **Nodus Fall** during **Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026** (August 25), marking one of the more surprising announcements of the show. The game is a dramatic tonal departure from the studio's established lineup of *Genshin Impact*, *Honkai: Star Rail*, and *Zenless Zone Zero*.

Described as a **dark fantasy co-op action RPG**, *Nodus Fall* is built in **Unreal Engine 5** — the studio's first game developed outside Unity. The world draws on mythologies from across the globe and presents a gritty, realistic aesthetic bearing closer resemblance to *Elden Ring* than anything HoYoverse has released before.

Players take the role of **Weavers**, who must form squads to hunt corrupted mythic entities called the **Dreadnodi**. Defeating enemies lets players absorb **Divine Skills** drawn from the fallen foe's power, integrating a build-progression loop into the hunt. Host Geoff Keighley summarized the premise as "stand as one to defeat mythic beings using close coordination and sharp tactics."

No platforms or release window have been announced. The Gamescom trailer served as the game's world premiere.

## Steins;Gate Re:Boot Launched August 20 — Xbox Version Delayed to October

**MAGES.**'s **Steins;Gate Re:Boot** launched on **August 20** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5**, and **PC via Steam**. The **Xbox Series X|S** version was postponed and is now scheduled for **October 29, 2026**.

*Re:Boot* is a full remake of the landmark 2009 sci-fi visual novel, rebuilt with redrawn sprites using the **E-mote** animation system for fluid character movement, a rerecorded voice cast, revised pacing and expanded new endings, and enhanced animated presentation throughout. The opening theme remains Kanako Ito's *Skyclad Observer*, rerecorded for the new production.

The Western localization (North America and Europe) was confirmed by **Spike Chunsoft** in late 2025, though a specific Western release date has not been announced. Text support includes Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English in the Japanese version at launch.

## Final Fantasy XIV Launched on Switch 2 August 4 — Free Month, Cross-Play, 117 GB

**Final Fantasy XIV Online** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **August 4** at **3:00 a.m. PDT**, completing the MMORPG's presence across PC, PlayStation, and now Nintendo platforms for the first time. Switch Online is not required; the game uses its own subscription model.

Key launch details:

- **Complete Edition was 30% off** through **August 13** to celebrate the Switch 2 launch
- **Subscription fees were waived** for approximately one month post-launch to ensure server stability during platform onboarding
- Players already subscribed on other platforms receive a **50% discount** on the Switch 2 subscription
- The Switch 2 version supports **mouse-and-keyboard input via the USB-C port** and includes full **cross-play** with PC and PlayStation versions
- **File size**: approximately **117 GB**
- A **Free Trial** covering content through the end of *A Realm Reborn* also launched August 4
