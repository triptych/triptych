## Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales Reviews — Metacritic 79–82

The review embargo for **The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales** lifted today ahead of tomorrow's launch. With 81 critic reviews logged on Metacritic, the game sits at:

- **82 / PS5** (34 reviews)
- **79 / Switch 2** (35 reviews)
- **79 / PC** (14 reviews)

A selection of notable scores:

| Outlet | Score | Notable quote |
|---|---|---|
| The Gamer | 4.0 / 5 | "A Near-Perfect Zelda-Like" |
| GamesRadar | 4.0 / 5 | "Expansive without being intimidating, varied without being overwhelming, and familiar without being tired" |
| The Outerhaven | ★★★★½ | "A fantastic action RPG for veterans and newcomers alike" |
| Nintendo World Report | 7.5 / 10 | "As enjoyable as many experiences set in Hyrule" despite a shallow Magicite system |
| Game Informer | 7 / 10 | "Adequate and effective — always glad to pick it back up across roughly 25 hours" |

The consensus paints it as a confident action-RPG debut for Team Asano in the Zelda-like space — handsome HD-2D presentation, puzzle-filled dungeons, light RPG progression — without quite hitting the heights of their turn-based work. The PS5 version edges out Switch 2 in the aggregate, though no outlet has cited meaningful platform-specific differences in content.

## Persona 4 Revival Broadcast Preview — Airs Tomorrow, June 18 at 4 PM PT

Tomorrow's **Persona 4 Revival** information broadcast (Atlus YouTube, 4 PM PT / 7 PM ET) is expected to be the most comprehensive look yet at the remake ahead of its **February 18, 2027** release. Director Kazuhisa Wada will host.

Going into the broadcast, confirmed new mechanics include:

- **Airborne Collision** — Landing normal attacks on Shadows afflicted with status ailments sends them flying into adjacent enemies, spreading the ailment on contact.
- **Prime Time** — A new dedicated gauge that, when filled, triggers consecutive actions, free skill costs, ignored resistances, and free Baton Pass usage.

The English voice cast was confirmed yesterday (June 16) via Final Weapon and Noisy Pixel, though Atlus has not yet published the full list publicly — individual role confirmations have been appearing through actors' social media ahead of the broadcast.

## Signet City — RPGFan Spotlight on the Citizen Sleeper Creator's Fungalpunk RPG

RPGFan ran a dedicated reveal-trailer article today on **Signet City**, the next game from Jump Over The Age (solo developer Gareth Damian Martin, *Citizen Sleeper*), published under Fellow Traveller.

The game was technically shown at the PC Gaming Show in early June, but today's RPGFan piece digs into the design language: Signet City is a **first-person fungalpunk RPG** in which the player is a fungal parasite infecting the streets of a coastal city called Signet City. Players alternate between parasite and host, using tabletop RPG-influenced mechanics shaped by a host's emotional state to alter the city's history and trajectory.

Visually the game is defined by **black-and-white photography-inspired art**, screentone manga hatching, and pen-and-ink illustration — stark and high-contrast in a way that deliberately distances it from pixel-art nostalgia. The thematic inspiration is the turbulent 1980s UK and Northern industrial cities.

No release window beyond "coming soon to PC" has been given. A Steam page is live.

## Steam Next Fest Day 3 — Mid-Fest JRPG Standings

Halfway through Steam Next Fest (running June 15–22), wishlist momentum is now the primary discovery driver as the front-page randomized rotation has ended. Among JRPG-adjacent titles:

- **Echoes of Aincrad** remains the top-performing RPG demo and is tracking among the most-wishlisted titles across the full fest.
