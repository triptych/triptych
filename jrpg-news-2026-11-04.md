## Metaphor: ReFantazio Arrives on Nintendo Switch 2 in Eight Days

**Atlus** and **SEGA**'s award-winning fantasy RPG **Metaphor: ReFantazio** launches on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026** — just eight days away. The release was confirmed during a **June 2026 Nintendo Direct** and marks the game's debut on a Nintendo platform.

Key details for the Switch 2 release:

- **Price:** 3,990 yen in Japan / 39.99 € in Europe — lower than the original launch price on other platforms
- **Physical edition:** Ships as a game-key card in the West; a limited **SteelBook Edition** is available at launch
- **Demo:** A playable prologue demo is already available on the Nintendo eShop
- **Content:** A straight port with no platform-exclusive content

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ending November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** will end the Japanese server for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, closing the final regional service for the mobile JRPG spinoff.

The global English version had already shut down in March 2025. For Japanese players still active:

- **Premium purchases** ended on August 27, 2026
- **Refund applications** open from November 26, 2026 through **February 24, 2027**
- The app will no longer be available to download after service ends

Sources: [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/atelier-resleriana-to-end-service-in-japan/)

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World Demo Now Live on Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**Square Enix** has released a free playable demo for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** ahead of the game's **December 3, 2026** launch.

Up to **eight monsters recruited in the demo** can be transferred to the full game at launch. The game features over **500 monsters** to collect and battle, and stars **Bianca and Nera** from *Dragon Quest V* in a new story.

Four DLC packs will be available at launch: Mega Mole Hole, Monster Marathons, the Metallic Ring, and the Scout Run VIP Card — available individually or as a bundle.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21382-dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-demo-download-save-data-bonus-switch-2-ps5-xbox-pc-steam) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/17/dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-december-3rd/) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-details-characters-story-world-and-monsters) · [Gaming on Linux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2026/06/dragon-quest-monsters-the-withered-world-arrives-in-december)

## Fate/EXTRA Record Set for January 28, 2027 — TGS Preview Coverage Roundup

**Aniplex** and **TYPE-MOON**'s long-in-development remake **Fate/EXTRA Record** now has a confirmed worldwide release date of **January 28, 2027**, targeting **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

The game was playable at the **Aniplex booth at Tokyo Game Show 2026**, with hands-on previews published by multiple outlets. Coverage describes the combat as a hybrid of *Persona*-style structure and a *Slay the Spire*-style card system, overhauling the original PSP game's rock-paper-scissors battle design. **Game Informer**'s preview specifically highlighted the depth of the new card deck-building layer.

A public demo is also reportedly planned for **Paris Games Week** in November. Pricing is set at $59.99 on Switch and $69.99 on Switch 2.

## Resonance of Fate Enhanced Remaster Out Now on Steam

**Tri-Ace** and **SEGA** released the **Resonance of Fate Enhanced Remaster** on **Steam on October 30, 2026** as a paid upgrade DLC for owners of the existing *Resonance of Fate 4K/HD Edition*. The upgrade is priced at **$9.99**.

The Enhanced Remaster adds:

- **Native 4K support**, HDR, and **120 FPS** output
- A **2x battle speed** option to accelerate combat
- Improved graphical fidelity throughout

The remaster's announcement was accompanied by the reactivation of the **official Leanne character account on X** after eight years of silence, which generated notable fan attention online.
