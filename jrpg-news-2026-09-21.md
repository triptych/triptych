## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Critical Acclaim — Metacritic 89

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems**' **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 17** to strong critical reception, earning an **89 on Metacritic** from 53 scored reviews, with 13 outlets awarding perfect marks.

**IGN** and **VGC** both gave the game **10/10**. IGN wrote: *"Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave builds upon its strategic arts in ways I couldn't have anticipated."* VGC called it "an essential game for fans of Fire Emblem and SRPGs." **GameSpot** awarded **9/10** ("a sprawling masterpiece of a strategy RPG") and **Game Informer** gave **8.75/10**. Critics consistently praised its four interwoven campaigns, tactical depth, and scale — full playthroughs reportedly range from **70 to over 100 hours** depending on how many routes are completed.

The review embargo lifted September 16, one day before launch.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword — 86 Metacritic, One Million Day-One Sales

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword**, which launched **September 4** on **Switch 2** (and other platforms), has emerged as one of the year's bigger successes. The game holds an **86 on Metacritic**, with **IGN awarding 10/10** — the first perfect score the outlet has given the Onimusha franchise.

Capcom's investor announcement on September 7 confirmed **worldwide sales surpassed 1 million units on launch day**, pushing the Onimusha series past **10 million lifetime units** sold.

## Tokyo Game Show 2026 Wraps — Day 5 Cancelled Due to Typhoon Dujuan

**Tokyo Game Show 2026** ran **September 17–21** at Makuhari Messe, marking the show's **30th anniversary** and its first-ever five-day format. However, **Day 5 (September 21, a public holiday) was cancelled** as Typhoon Dujuan approached Japan, reducing the event to four days. The official TGS site posted a notice confirming the cancellation and directing attendees to safety.

Despite the shortened run, TGS 2026 featured a dense schedule of JRPG demos and announcements — the major ones are covered in the sections below.

## Level-5 at TGS 2026: DECAPOLICE Delayed to 2027, Snack World: Reloaded Set for Switch 2

**Level-5** used Tokyo Game Show to update fans on two long-in-development RPG projects:

**DECAPOLICE** — the detective mystery action RPG — has been officially **delayed to 2027** (platforms: **Switch 2, PS5, PC via Steam**). The game has been in development since 2023 and previously missed earlier windows. Despite the delay, a **playable public demo** ran at TGS for the full four days. RPGFan published a [hands-on preview](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/09/19/tgs-2026-decapolice-hands-on-preview/) from the show floor. The game follows young detective Harvard, who investigates crimes in a futuristic megalopolis, with suspects confronted Ace Attorney–style. An anime adaptation is planned to release alongside the game.

**Snack World: Reloaded**, the action RPG remake, is also confirmed for **Switch 2 in 2027** and was playable at TGS. Level-5 highlights a major visual upgrade described as "adventuring through a diorama."

Sources: [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/65071-level-5-shares-tgs-2026-trailers-for-snack-world-reloaded-decapolice-and-holy-horror) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/decapolice-snack-world-reloaded-resurface-with-new-trailers/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/10/snack-world-reloaded-launches-in-2027-playable-at-tgs-2026/) · [AllKeyShop](https://www.allkeyshop.com/blog/level-5-delays-decapolice-2027-tgs-demo-news-r/)

## Fate/EXTRA Record — January 28, 2027 Release Date Confirmed at State of Play

**Aniplex** and developer **Meteorise** (with **TYPE-MOON**) confirmed a **worldwide release date of January 28, 2027** for **Fate/EXTRA Record**, the remake of the original *Fate/EXTRA* (2010). The date was announced during **Sony's State of Play: September 3, 2026**.

The game launches on **PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, PS4, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. Physical editions are available for PS5, Switch 2, and Switch. A **playable demo was on the TGS 2026 show floor**, and *Shane the Gamer* published a hands-on preview noting the card-based combat system.

## Nintendo Direct September 2026: Romancing SaGa 3 Remake, Dragon Quest Heroes Shadow-Drop, and More

**Nintendo's September 2026 Direct** (held September 9) included several notable JRPG reveals:

**Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** — **Square Enix** announced a **full 3D remake** of the 1995 Super Famicom RPG *Romancing SaGa 3*, targeting **February 16, 2027**.

**Dragon Quest Heroes: Torneko's Mystery Dungeon Classic HD** — **Square Enix** shadow-dropped an HD remaster of the classic Mystery Dungeon spin-off on Nintendo consoles during the Direct.

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure — Now Live on PS5 and Switch 2

**NIS America** and **Nihon Falcom**'s enhanced ports of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** launched on **September 10** for **PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2**. Existing **Switch 1 and PC** versions were simultaneously patched with performance improvements.

## KAELIS: Seraph Protocol — Sci-Fantasy JRPG Announced with Kickstarter

Seoul-based **YoshiYoshi Studio** announced **KAELIS: Seraph Protocol**, a Japanese-inspired single-player science-fantasy RPG, on **September 18**. The Kickstarter campaign is live seeking **$27,000 USD**.

The game stars **Kaito**, an Executor of Serenos Academy who investigates "Noise incidents" with academy companions. Features include turn-based combat, academy life, an exploration system, character bonds, and dice-based story events that can shift the flow of each case. The PC (Steam) release is targeted for **2028**.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/character-driven-single-player-sci-fantasy-jrpg-kaelis-seraph-protocol-announced-for-pc) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/kaelis-seraph-protocol-jrpg-2028-kickstarter/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/18/kaelis-seraph-protocol-announced-for-pc-kickstarter-campaign-now-live/)
