## Metaphor: ReFantazio Launches Today on Nintendo Switch 2

**Atlus**'s acclaimed 2024 RPG **Metaphor: ReFantazio** makes its Nintendo debut today with its **Switch 2 version launching November 12**. The port was announced during a Nintendo Direct in June 2026.

The standard edition is priced at **$39.99 / £34.99**, a step below the original release price, and a physical **SteelBook Edition** is available at launch for a limited time. The game ships on a game-key card for the physical version. A **free demo** is available letting players experience the opening hours of the adventure.

The Switch 2 version is a straight port of the game that launched on PS4, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC in October 2024. It supports TV, tabletop, and handheld modes on Switch 2.

## Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle — New Mobile Title Announced, 2027 Worldwide

**Square Enix** officially unveiled **Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle** on November 10, 2026, a new turn-based RPG for **iOS and Android** targeting a **2027 worldwide release**.

The game is being developed by **Wright Flyer Studios (WFS)**, the team behind *Heaven Burns Red*. Series creator **Yuji Horii** penned a letter on the official site explaining that the "Dragon Quest RPG" branding is deliberate — the title is meant to be a classic, mainline-style Dragon Quest adventure rather than another spinoff, distinguishing it from other mobile entries like *Dragon Quest Smash/Grow*.

**Story:** The world tree **Yggdrasil** has been corrupted by the fiend **Zaahl** into the twisted **Grimvein**, and the player takes on the role of Warden of the World Tree and sets out to save the world.

**Closed beta:** Applications open **November 10–23**, with the test running **December 8–15**. 25,000 participants will be selected.

## Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom — Story Trailer and Digital Pre-Orders Live

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** released a new **Story Trailer** for **Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories** this week (around November 8–9), alongside opening **digital pre-orders**.

The trailer follows amnesiac alchemist **Karia** and her party as they descend into **Noctrem**, the underworld of the continent of Aladiss, in search of her lost memories.

**Release date confirmed:** The game launches **February 25, 2027** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam)**. A **physical Premium Box** is available exclusively through the NIS America online store and includes the base game, artbook, bonus CD tracks, nine A4 posters, and costume DLC.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** will shut down the Japan version of **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, marking the end of the game's global operations entirely (the Global version already ended on March 28, 2025).

The studio cited difficulty in continuing to provide a service that satisfies players. Paid **Lodestar gems** can be refunded from November 26, 2026 through **February 24, 2027** under Japan's Payment Services Act. Account data recovery closes at 16:00 on November 24, the day before service ends.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/atelier-resleriana-to-end-service-in-japan/) · [Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/08/27/atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-and-the-polar-night-liberator-ends-service-on-november-25-in-japan/)

## Alleged Persona 1 Remake Footage Surfaces Online

Nearly **two minutes of unverified footage** purporting to show a **Persona 1 remake** surfaced online on **October 31, 2026**, spreading quickly across Reddit's r/GamingLeaksAndRumours and file hosts after first appearing on a YouTube account named "P1REMAKE." Neither **Atlus** nor **Sega** has commented on the leak as of this writing.

Fan analysis points to a party member (believed to be **Eriko**) sharing a Persona-summoning animation with a character in *Persona 4 Revival*, suggesting possible shared development infrastructure. Others noted one character model appeared unfinished, possibly indicating work-in-progress footage.

Context: **P-Studio producer Kazuhisa Wada** discussed the possibility of Persona 1 and Persona 2 remakes in August 2026. This follows earlier merchandise listings that briefly hinted at Persona 1 and 2 re-releases before being quietly altered. Atlus has not confirmed any such project is in development.

## Shadow of the Road Delayed to March 10, 2027

**Another Angle Games** and publisher **Owlcat Games** announced on **October 9, 2026** that the feudal Japan turn-based RPG **Shadow of the Road** has been pushed from its previously announced November 12 date to **March 10, 2027** on **PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG)**.

The developers cited the need to refine tactical encounters, fine-tune combat balance, and rework progression systems to better encourage skill experimentation. A free demo remains available on Steam. The game blends feudal Japan with magic and steampunk-style machinery in a story-driven turn-based format.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/08/shadow-of-the-road-launches-november-12) · [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/shadow-of-the-road-sets-november-12-release-date-for-its-turn-based-journey-through-1868-japan/) · [PlayNews](https://www.playnews.gg/en/release-dates/shadow-of-the-road-release-date-pushed-to-march-10-2027-on-steam-epic-and-gog)

## Medabots Classics Plus Coming to Steam in Japan in December

**Imagineer** and **SUCCESS Corporation** will release both the **Kabuto Ver.** and **Kuwagata Ver.** of **Medabots Classics Plus** on **Steam in Japan** in **December 2026**, priced at **5,478 yen each**. The Steam pages will be region-locked to Japan.

The compilation includes five mainline *Medabots* entries that originally appeared on Game Boy and Game Boy Color, plus three additional GBA bonus titles. The Switch version launched in Japan back in November 2020; the Steam release brings the collection to PC for the first time.

No western release has been announced.
