## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Launches to Franchise-Best Reviews, Sells 1 Million on Day One

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched on **September 4, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, PC via Steam, and Xbox Series X|S** — the first mainline Onimusha in 20 years. The review embargo lifted on August 31 to a broadly enthusiastic reception.

**Metacritic: 86** (48 reviews) · **OpenCritic: 86 / "Mighty"** (63 outlets, ~95th percentile). IGN awarded the game its first-ever perfect **10/10** for the franchise. Giant Bomb scored it 4/5. The near-universal praise goes to the combat design and boss fights; the sharpest criticism is that the campaign is padded with repetitive side content.

Capcom confirmed the game sold **over 1 million units worldwide on launch day**.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Reviews In — Metacritic 89, Tied for Series Best

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2** and reviews are in. The game currently holds an **89 on Metacritic** (60 reviews), tied with *Fire Emblem: Three Houses* as the highest-rated entry in the franchise.

IGN and several other outlets awarded perfect scores. GameSpot gave it a 9/10, calling it "a sprawling masterpiece of a strategy RPG, building upon the successes of past Fire Emblem games with new interconnected elements, expertly designed combat puzzles, and an emphasis on flexibility and choice." Critics praised the four interconnected storylines and the deep tactical combat; some flagged the enormous runtime and overwhelming content as drawbacks, but even mixed reviews acknowledged the ambition.

Sources: [TechRadar Review](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-review) · [Nintendo Life Review Roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-are-in) · [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-reviews-scores/) · [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/review/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-switch-2-review/) · [GamesRadar Guide](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-guide/)

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches; Falcom Eyes More Remakes

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — a full remake of *Trails in the Sky SC* — launched globally on **September 17, 2026** for **PS5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. The game picks up immediately after the cliffhanger ending of the prior remake, following newly certified senior bracer **Estelle Bright** as she searches the Liberl Kingdom for her vanished partner Joshua while confronting the secret society Ouroboros.

Combat mixes real-time **Quick Battle** action with strategic turn-based **Command Battle** sequences, with a new **Brave Rush** system letting characters link attacks. The game is also being showcased within **Konami's partner area at TGS 2026**, timed to its global launch.

Falcom president **Toshihiro Kondo** told outlets that the studio is hoping to continue making remakes following this release.

Sources: [WCCFTech Review](https://wccftech.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review-falcom-delivers-a-faithful-remake-of-one-of-its-best-role-playing-games/) · [Final Weapon — Falcom Remakes](https://finalweapon.net/2026/08/23/falcom-hopes-to-make-more-remakes-after-trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/) · [Gungho Online — Release Details](https://gunghoonline.com/2026/04/17/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-launches-september-17-trailer-and-voice-cast-revealed/) · [Konami TGS](https://www.konami.com/games/tgs/en/)

## Another Eden Begins Out Now — Gacha-Free Standalone on Switch 2 and PC

**Wright Flyer Studios** (Studio Prisma) released **Another Eden Begins** on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. The game is a premium standalone reimagining of the free-to-play mobile RPG *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space*, stripping out all gacha mechanics and microtransactions in favor of a traditional offline JRPG experience with a **$39.99** price.

The story follows **Aldo** and his sister **Feinne**, who live in Baruoki until Feinne is taken by the Beast King — after a battle, Aldo is hurled 800 years into the future through a tear in spacetime. The game features 19 recruitable companions, full English and Japanese voice acting, and **10+ endings** unlocked through New Game+. Command-based battles let players build a gauge to interrupt enemy turns.

The game is led by RPG veteran **Masato Kato** (*Chrono Trigger*, *Xenogears*).

Sources: [RPGamer — Release](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/another-eden-begins-demo-available/) · [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Another-Eden-Begins-removes-gacha-for-Sept-17-single-player-launch.1393239.0.html) · [Steam Page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4163960/Another_Eden_Begins/) · [NewsBreak](https://www.newsbreak.com/news/4890441015418-chrono-trigger-creator-s-another-eden-begins-is-out-now-on-switch-2-and-steam)

## TGS 2026: Final Fantasy VII Revelation Demo Confirms April 8, 2027 PS5 Release, Vincent and Cid Playable

The **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** playable demo at **Tokyo Game Show 2026** is the most substantial look yet at the trilogy's finale. The demo is set in the **Whirlwind Maze** and puts players in control of Cloud alongside a full party — with **Vincent Valentine** and **Cid Highwind** confirmed as playable characters for the first time.

Vincent features **dual gunplay and a beast transformation** mechanic; his Chaos form drains HP over time as a balancing constraint. The FITS system (Function-Integrated Tactical Suitwear) — which lets the party switch between **Warrior, Knight, Black Mage, and Summoner** roles — returns, with the TGS demo's Cloud equipped in the Summoner FITS to summon Ifrit, Shiva, and Titan.

Story beats in the demo include the **Jenova Deathmourner** boss fight, a peculiar post-battle encounter with Sephiroth involving a hug, and Barret and Tifa witnessing the Nibelheim incident for themselves, including a first glimpse of Zack Fair.

Square Enix confirmed **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** will launch on **PlayStation 5 worldwide on April 8, 2027**. Platforms beyond PS5 have not yet been officially detailed for that specific date.

## TGS 2026: Atelier Karia Confirmed for February 2027 with New Alchemy and Exploration Details

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** showed **Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories** during the **Koei Tecmo Live! TGS 2026** stream (September 18) and confirmed a **February 2027 release window** for **PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC**.

The game follows **Karia**, an alchemist with no memories, as she uncovers the truth of her past. New details confirm that returning characters **Rutger** and **Lenja** assist her, that synthesized items now play active roles during exploration (not just in combat), and that ingredients can be consumed for unique field effects. Combat and alchemy systems are both described as significantly expanded relative to prior Atelier entries.

## Neptunia Unlimited Launches in Japan — Famitsu 31/40

**Idea Factory** and **Compile Heart** released **Neptunia Unlimited** in Japan on **August 27, 2026** for **PS4, PS5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2**.
