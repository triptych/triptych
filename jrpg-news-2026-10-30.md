## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT — Western Switch / Switch 2 / PS5 Launch (October 29)

**MAGES.** and **Spike Chunsoft** released **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** in North America on **October 29, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2**, and PC via Steam (the PC/Steam version launched globally on **August 20**). The Xbox Series version was delayed from that August date and also launched October 29 in the west.

RE:BOOT is a complete visual remake of the original *Steins;Gate* featuring overhauled art, a full HD re-recording of the Japanese voice track, and the brand-new **"Gamma Worldline"** route — a story branch not present in the 2009 original. Physical North American editions (Standard and SteelBook) are shipping as of today; UK/EU physical editions have been pushed to **November 3** (digital unaffected). Physical editions for Japan launched alongside the August 20 digital launch.

## Phantom Blade Zero — Launches on PC and PS5 (October 29)

**S-GAME**'s dark wuxia action RPG **Phantom Blade Zero** launched on **October 29, 2026** on **PC (Steam and Epic Games Store)** and PS5. The game was originally set for September 9 before a ~50-day delay to allow for additional polish.

The **Standard Edition is $59.99**; the **Digital Deluxe Edition is $69.99**. PC preload opened 48 hours before launch. The install size is approximately **145 GB**. An Xbox version has been confirmed but will not arrive before 2027.

Sources: [Keen Gamer — release confirmed](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/phantom-blade-zero-release-confirmed-for-october-29/) · [Keen Gamer — preload times](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/phantom-blade-zero-preload-and-global-release-times-revealed/) · [RPGSite — pre-orders/price/DLC](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21092-phantom-blade-zero-preorder-price-deluxe-dlc-trailer-new-gameplay) · [Shacknews — date announcement](https://shacknews.com/article/150341/phantom-blade-zero-release-date-state-of-play) · [Gematsu — pre-orders](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/08/phantom-blade-zero-pre-orders-open-august-11)

## Eternal Anima — Special Discussion Series Launches with Sakaguchi, Uematsu, Sakimoto

**Marvelous** released the first two episodes of the **"Special Discussion"** video series for the upcoming time-traveling JRPG **Eternal Anima** on **October 28, 2026**.

The videos feature:
- **Katsuhisa Higuchi** (director; *Final Fantasy* and *Chrono Trigger* veteran) in conversation with guest **Hironobu Sakaguchi** (creator of *Final Fantasy*, *Mistwalker*)
- Composers **Nobuo Uematsu** and **Hitoshi Sakimoto** discussing their approach to the game's score

Eternal Anima is built around a **"Future Sight"** combat system that lets players preview enemy actions and their outcomes before committing to a turn. The game was announced in a September Nintendo Direct and is set to launch **March 4, 2027** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PC**, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.

Sources: [Final Weapon — two episodes released](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/28/eternal-anima-first-two-episodes-of-special-discussion-series/) · [RPGamer — developer videos](https://rpgamer.com/2026/10/eternal-anima-gets-pair-of-developer-videos/) · [Gematsu — announcement](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/marvelous-announces-rpg-eternal-anima-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-and-pc) · [Turn Based Lovers — gameplay overview](https://turnbasedlovers.com/overview/eternal-anima-is-a-turn-based-jrpg-that-lets-you-predict-and-rewrite-enemy-turns/) · [Marvelous Games — official page](https://marvelousgames.com/games/eternal-anima) · [Inven Global — Uematsu/Sakimoto video](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27826/nobuo-uematsu-and-hitoshi-sakimoto-discuss-jrpg-music-in-eternal-anima-video)
