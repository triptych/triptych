## Unverified Footage of a Persona 1 Remake Surfaces Online

A YouTube channel called **P1REMAKE** uploaded just under two minutes of footage today claiming to show an in-development remake of **Persona: Be Your True Mind** — the original Persona game. The clip spread rapidly to Reddit and social platforms before the channel went quiet.

Neither **Atlus** nor parent company **Sega** has issued any statement acknowledging or denying the footage.

What's in the clip:
- Turn-based combat with redesigned UI elements consistent with the Persona 3 Reload engine's aesthetic
- **Eriko Kirishima** summoning her Persona using an animation that fans identified as near-identical to Yukiko Amagi's in **Persona 4 Revival**
- Visible **Unreal Engine** debug text — Atlus's adopted engine since Persona 3 Reload
- An appearance by **Tatsuya Suou**, a protagonist from *Persona 2*, which is raising doubts since he is not a character in the original game

Skeptics note the wave of AI-generated game trailers circulating this year and point to Tatsuya's inclusion as a credibility gap. Supporters point to the Unreal Engine artifacts as something difficult to fake convincingly. Persona series producer **Kazuhisa Wada** said in a recent interview that remakes of the first two Persona games are "probably eventually" happening, lending context but not confirmation.

## Final Fantasy Resonance Launches to Strong Reviews

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy Resonance** released on **October 22, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PC (Steam), and other platforms**, and landed to notably positive critical reception.

The game carries the distinction of being the **first HD-2D Final Fantasy title**, applying the engine and art style from *Octopath Traveler* and *Triangle Strategy* to an original story set in the Final Fantasy universe. Its Visions system — letting players collect and deploy characters from across the franchise's history — drew early skepticism as a retooled gacha concept, but reviewers largely found it earned its place in the design.

Scores at launch:
- **Metacritic:** 85 (PS5, 47 critics) · 88 (PC, 19 critics) · 82 (Switch 2)
- **Steam:** 97% positive from over 1,300 user reviews at launch

Consistent praise went to the **turn-based battle system** and the HD-2D presentation. Story was the main dividing point — some reviewers found it retreads *Brave Exvius* narrative beats, while others praised it as a solid original RPG that doesn't require franchise history.

Sources: [Nintendo Life (first review)](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/10/the-first-review-for-final-fantasy-resonance-is-in) · [Pure Xbox review roundup](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/10/final-fantasy-resonance-is-launching-on-xbox-to-extremely-impressive-reviews) · [DL Compare (controversy coverage)](https://www.dlcompare.com/gaming-news/final-fantasy-resonance-earns-high-praise-but-still-sparks-debate-86906)

## Fate/EXTRA Record Gets January 28, 2027 Release Date; Paris Games Week Demo Available

After multiple delays stretching back years, **TYPE-MOON Studio BB** and **Aniplex** confirmed a worldwide release date of **January 28, 2027** for **Fate/EXTRA Record** during a **September 3, 2026** broadcast. Bandai Namco had previously dropped publishing duties, but Aniplex stepped in to carry the title to release.

The game — a full remake of the 2010 PSP RPG *Fate/EXTRA* — will launch on **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. A physical Switch 2 edition was confirmed alongside the announcement.

At **Tokyo Game Show 2026**, press got hands-on time with the game; early impressions described the combat as a hybrid of **Persona's social-calendar structure** and **Slay the Spire's card-based battle loop**. A **Paris Games Week 2026** playable demo gives European players a chance to try it.

## Threads of Time Confirms Summer 2027 Window; Switch and Switch 2 Versions Announced

**Riyo Games** and **Balor Games** confirmed on **October 28** that **Threads of Time** — the 2.5D Chrono Trigger-inspired JRPG — is targeting a **Summer 2027** release, pushing the window back from prior TBA status.

The announcement also confirmed **Nintendo Switch** and **Nintendo Switch 2** versions alongside the previously announced **PC (Steam)** and **Xbox Series X|S** versions.

The time-travel RPG features multiple playable eras, with heroes recruited across different time periods forming the player's party. Development updates have continued steadily throughout 2026, including a trailer at Day of the Devs 2026.

Sources: [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/28/threads-of-time-summer-2027/) · [RPG Fan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/13/threads-of-time-day-of-the-devs-trailer/) · [Turn Based Lovers (release window trailer)](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/new-trailer-for-chrono-trigger-inspired-jrpg-threads-of-time-reveals-a-release-window/)

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** will end the Japanese server of **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST** — 24 days from today. The publisher cited an inability to maintain a satisfying service as the reason for the decision.

Key timeline:
- **August 27, 2026:** Sales of premium currency and gacha banners ceased
- **November 25, 2026:** Service ends at 16:00 JST; game becomes unplayable
- **November 26, 2026 – February 24, 2027:** Refund window for unused premium currency

The global version of the game shut down earlier on **March 28, 2025**, making this the end of the title's Japanese run as well.

Sources: [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/atelier-resleriana-to-end-service-in-japan/) · [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25)
