## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Best-Reviewed Game of 2026

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** launched on **September 17, 2026** for **PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2** in a simultaneous global release — the first time a mainline *Trails in the Sky* remake has shipped worldwide on day one.

The game is a full remake of the 2006 **Trails in the Sky SC**, continuing Estelle Bright's journey directly from where the 1st Chapter left off. It brings the original to modern standards with 3D graphics, full English and Japanese voice acting, a modernized UI, and rebuilt environments that expand on the original's locations.

Critical reception has been exceptional: the game holds **Metacritic 91 / OpenCritic 92 with a 97% critic recommendation**, making it a frontrunner for game-of-the-year discussions. Reviewers highlighted the remake's mastery of character development and emotional payoff — *Dual Shockers* called it "a flawless JRPG experience," while *Game Informer* labeled it "a superb sequel." Minor criticisms centered on sparse environmental detail and UI plainness inherited from the original's structure.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Reviews In, Series' New Peak

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched September 17 on **Nintendo Switch 2** and review scores confirm it as the highest-rated entry in the franchise since *Three Houses*.

*GameSpot* called it "the series' new peak," awarding **9/10**, while **IGN gave it a 10/10** — the highest score the site has awarded a Nintendo-published title since *Breath of the Wild*. *Nintendo Life* posted **8/10**, citing the game's exhausting length as the main drawback. *Kotaku*'s review roundup summarized the consensus as "dense but rewarding." Full playthroughs of all four routes range from 70 to well over 100 hours.

The four-protagonist structure — which Intelligent Systems outlined in pre-launch promotional materials — has been praised by critics as more than a structural novelty, with the four narratives growing richer in conversation with each other.

Sources: [GameSpot Review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-is-the-series-new-peak/) · [Nintendo Life Review Roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-are-in) · [Kotaku Roundup](https://kotaku.com/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-review-round-up-switch-2-2000735072) · [Checkpoint Review](https://checkpointgaming.net/reviews/2026/09/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-review-its-a-long-battle/)

## Dragon Quest XI S Definitive Edition — Switch 2 Launch Reviews

**Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition** launched for **Nintendo Switch 2 on September 24, 2026**. The port is a native Switch 2 release rather than a compatibility-mode upgrade, adding a **Performance / Quality graphics toggle** and running at **60 FPS in both docked and handheld modes**.

*CGMagazine* scored it **8/10**, noting that returning players will find limited new content beyond the technical improvements, while first-time players get the definitive way to experience one of the best JRPGs of the 2010s. *RPG Site* published a multiplatform version comparison confirming the Switch 2 port now stands alongside the PC version as the preferred way to play, surpassing the older PlayStation and Xbox versions in performance. The Definitive Edition's orchestral soundtrack, dual-language dub, and expanded post-game content remain intact.

## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow — Launched September 24

**Arc System Works**' tactical counter-timeline RPG **Qliphah in Providence's Shadow** launched as scheduled on **September 24, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, PC (Steam and Epic Games Store)**. The game follows investigator **Rena Hizuki** and protagonist **Soma Hoshigami** — left in a "Half-turned" state after an Anomaly attack — as they work within the Tokyo Metropolitan Police's Public Safety Bureau in a post-World Collapse Tokyo.

The turn-based combat system lets players pause combat at any time to read the battle information display, allowing prediction of enemy actions before committing to a move. The "counter-timeline" framing means combat outcomes ripple backward and forward through the battle timeline, rewarding careful reading of enemy patterns.

## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Announced — February 16, 2027

**Square Enix** revealed **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** during the **September 2026 Nintendo Direct**, with a playable demo on the show floor at **Tokyo Game Show 2026**. The game is a full **3D remake of the 1995 Super Famicom RPG** developed by **xeen Inc.** — the same studio behind *Trials of Mana* and *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven* — and launches **February 16, 2027** for **PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2**.

Key features confirmed:

- **Freeform Scenario System** returns: each of the eight selectable heroes enters the story from a different angle, and the order of events is shaped by which protagonist the player chooses and the decisions made during their run.
- **Timeline Battles** introduce a new twist on the original's combat, with **United Attacks** allowing coordinated strikes between party members.
- **War Mode** and **Business** minigames from the original return, expanded for the remake.
- Combat features the series' signature **Glimmers** system — skills learned mid-battle through combatant improvisation.
- Fully voiced cutscenes and selectable difficulty levels new to the remake.

The story centers on eight heroes bound by fate in a world threatened every 300 years by **Morastrum, the Star of Death**. After an insurrection in Loanne uncovers that the **Four Sinistrals** seek to reopen the gates to the Abyss, the heroes must unite to stop them.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/09/koei-tecmo-and-gust-reveal-the-apothecary-diaries-the-false-imperial-brother/) · [The Konn Network](https://thekonnetwork.com/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united/) · [Nintendo Direct Trailer (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F7TeiEhRViE)

## The Apothecary Diaries: The False Imperial Brother — Koei Tecmo/Gust Announce First Console Game

**Koei Tecmo** and developer **Gust** (in collaboration with **Toho Co., Ltd.**) announced **The Apothecary Diaries: The False Imperial Brother** — the first ever console game adaptation of the popular manga/anime franchise — at the **September 2026 Nintendo Direct** and expanded on it at **Tokyo Game Show 2026**.

The game releases in **early 2027** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC (Steam)**. It is a **mystery-solving adventure RPG** in which players follow **Maomao**, a pleasure-district apothecary working in the rear palace, as she investigates the Five Curses and uncovers the identity of a mysterious figure known as the "imperial younger brother." The story is an original scenario written in collaboration with series author **Hyuganatsu**.

Gameplay blends **exploration, evidence gathering, and deduction mechanics** with a medicine-crafting system where players gather apothecary ingredients to brew remedies that resolve incidents. TGS footage showcased the deduction board where collected clues are organized before Maomao draws a conclusion — a visual structure reminiscent of the *AI: The Somnium Files* series' investigation screens.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/koei-tecmo-and-gust-announce-the-apothecary-diaries-the-false-imperial-brother-for-ps5-switch-2-switch-and-pc) · [Noisy Pixel Gameplay Details](https://noisypixel.net/apothecary-diaries-false-imperial-brother-gameplay-crafting-deduction/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/09/koei-tecmo-and-gust-reveal-the-apothecary-diaries-the-false-imperial-brother/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/the-apothecary-diaries-the-false-imperial-brother-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2-switch/) · [Koei Tecmo Europe](https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/news/follow-maomao-in-a-new-exclusive-investigation-with-koei-tecmos-the-apothecary-diaries-the-false-imperial-brother/)
