## Nintendo Direct (September 9) — JRPG Highlights

Nintendo's September 9 Direct was heavy on RPG content. The major JRPG takeaways:

### Final Fantasy VII Revelation — April 8, 2027; Switch 2 Trailer Released

Square Enix confirmed **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** — the trilogy's conclusion — launches **April 8, 2027** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC**. The Nintendo Direct featured the first footage of the game running on Switch 2, which Square Enix says achieves parity with other versions. New details from the trailer: players gain access to the **Highwind airship** as a controllable vehicle, with the option to **parachute directly onto the world map** from altitude — a series first. Pre-orders are live with a bonus Summoning Materia: Mecha-Chocobo & Moogle.

### Crisis Core: Final Fantasy VII Reunion — Shadow Drop on Switch 2

**Crisis Core: Final Fantasy VII Reunion** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on September 9** as a same-day-as-Direct shadow drop, priced at **$49.99** with a **30% launch discount through September 24**. The Switch 2 port is built on the 2022 remaster (full voice acting, reworked DMW/Limit Break systems, new summon animations, re-arranged soundtrack). No upgrade path or save-data transfer exists from the original Switch version.

### Eternal Anima — Full Reveal; March 4, 2027

**Marvelous** fully unveiled **Eternal Anima** during the Direct — the game previously teased as *Project Life is RPG* in 2023. The title is a **legacy-driven time-travel JRPG** launching **March 4, 2027** simultaneously on **Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**.

The creative team is notably pedigreed: director **Katsuhisa Higuchi**, story concept writer **Keisuke Makino**, and scenario writer **Kazushige Nojima** (Final Fantasy VII, VIII, X; Kingdom Hearts). Players control Wade, who travels through history to prevent civilizations from collapsing, gaining **inherited abilities from ancestors** and using a **Future Sight** system in turn-based combat to preview enemy move orders before committing actions.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/marvelous-announces-rpg-eternal-anima-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-and-pc) · [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/64748-eternal-anima-coming-to-switch-2-on-march-4th-2027) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/eternal-anima-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2/)

### Dragon Quest Heroes: Torneko's Mystery Dungeon —Classic HD— Shadow Drops Globally

In another Direct surprise, **Square Enix** released **Dragon Quest Heroes: Torneko's Mystery Dungeon −Classic HD−** on **September 9** for **Switch, Switch 2, PS5, and PC**. ## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Launch on Switch 2 and PS5

The **PS5 and Switch 2 versions of The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure** launched **September 10** as planned, developed by **PH3** and published by **NIS America**. Reviewer reception has been very strong. Key enhancements:

- **4K resolution** (PS5 and Switch 2 docked) / **1440p @ 120Hz** on compatible displays in Switch 2 docked mode
- **60 FPS** across both platforms; **120 FPS** available on PS5 in performance mode
- **Mouse input** support on Switch 2
- Save data transfer from existing PC saves supported

RPG Site called the ports "nearly perfect" with the lone criticism being the **absence of a paid upgrade path** from the existing console versions. Each game is available separately digitally or together as a physical bundle.

Sources: [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21247-trails-from-zero-trails-to-azure-switch-2-ps5-review-new-features-save-transfer-durante-ph3) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/19/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-from-zero-and-trails-to-azure-lands-on-ps5-switch-2-on-september-10th/) · [Final Weapon – Azure Review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/03/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-to-azure-switch-2-review/)

## Fantasy Life i: The Girl Who Steals Time — "The Celestial Express" Paid DLC Announced

Announced during Level-5 Vision II, **The Celestial Express** is a major paid DLC expansion for **Fantasy Life i** (no release date yet). Content includes:

- Two new **Life Rank tiers**: God-in-Training and God — the highest ranks achievable, with unique challenges for every Life
- **God Crafting**: recipes that can generate items with special God Skills; visible appearance variants from a single recipe
- **God Crown Enemies**: a new enemy tier above Gold Crown
- A customizable **Wingmate** accessory that flies alongside the player and assists in combat
- A new **Mount** whose appearance and stats are altered by God-rank crafted parts
- New furniture sets, house designs, monsters, and gatherables

## HOSHiN ENGi Re:surrection Announced for PS5, Switch 2, and PC (2027)

**Shueisha Games** and developer **LASTWONDER** announced **HOSHiN ENGi Re:surrection**, a **roguelite action game** based on Ryu Fujisaki's 1996–2000 *Hoshin Engi* (*Soul Hunter*) manga. The announcement came September 9 during a Nintendo Direct segment.

The game is set **after the events of the original manga**, following a new hero named **Toshin** who descends through procedurally arranged dungeons. Combat is built around **Paope** — mythic treasures wielded by gods and immortals — which combine and evolve as they are upgraded. The art style animates Fujisaki's manga panels directly. Original creator **Ryu Fujisaki** designed the new protagonist and is personally overseeing development. Targeting **2027** on PS5, Switch 2, and PC.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/roguelite-action-game-hoshin-engi-resurrection-announced-for-ps5-switch-2-and-pc) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/hoshin-engi-resurrection-announced-for-2027/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/hoshin-engi-resurrection-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2/) · [Automaton West](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/hoshin-engi-resurrection-a-roguelite-hack-and-slash-adaptation-of-the-classic-shonen-jump-manga-launches-in-2027/)

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword — Reviews In (Metacritic 86)

**Onimusha: Way of the Sword** (Capcom) launched **September 4** on **Switch 2, PS5, and PC** and has been widely praised. Current scores: **Metacritic 86** (48 reviews), **OpenCritic 85** (63 outlets), ~95% recommend. The **Switch 2 version leads all platforms at 89/100**. IGN awarded the game a **perfect 10/10** — the franchise's first-ever.

The critical consensus praises the swordplay combat and boss encounters. The most common criticism is that side content becomes repetitive in the back half of the campaign.

Sources: [Nintendo Life Review Roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-onimusha-way-of-the-sword-are-in) · [Game Meta Hub](https://www.gamemetahub.com/games/onimusha-way-of-the-sword/review-roundup-launch-verdicts)

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Launches September 17; Final Previews Positive

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** is six days out. Nintendo published a **Fire Emblem Direct on August 4** with the full systems deep dive, and a final preview wave went up in late August — reception across outlets was strongly positive. A **developer interview publishes September 15** (Japanese) and an **Ask the Developer feature runs September 16** (English).
