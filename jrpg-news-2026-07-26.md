## FFXIV Fan Festival Berlin: Bastion Job Revealed, Switch 2 Launch August 4

The **Final Fantasy XIV Fan Festival 2026** ran July 25–26 at Messe Berlin, and producer/director **Naoki Yoshida** packed the keynote with *Evercold* expansion news.

**New job — Bastion:** The first job confirmed for *Evercold* is **Bastion**, a main tank that dual-wields a pair of greatshields called *skyltborg*. No further jobs were named during the keynote.

**World and setting:** A majority of *Evercold* takes place across floating regions called **Realmships** (also known as *The Five Firmaments*) hovering above the frozen wasteland of The Fourth.

**New raid series:** A fresh 8-player alliance raid series called **Beyond the Lifestream** is themed around *Final Fantasy VII*, joining the expanding cross-franchise raid tradition.

**Gold Saucer crossover:** The in-game Gold Saucer is receiving a *Final Fantasy VII* Golden Saucer makeover, including a new minigame called **Keybound Brawler**.

**Switch 2 launch date:** *Final Fantasy XIV* arrives on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**. *Evercold* itself is targeting a **January 2027** release.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition Overview Trailer Showcases Merc Assault and New Blade Momo

Nintendo dropped a full overview trailer for **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** on July 24, ahead of the game's **July 30 digital launch** (physical edition follows October 1).

Key upgrades and additions detailed in the trailer:

- **Performance:** 4K / 60 fps in TV mode; 1080p / 60 fps in handheld mode with improved cutscene quality
- **New Pyra & Mythra outfits:** New costume designs carry through into cutscenes and can be earned as rewards via the new Merc Assault mode
- **Merc Assault:** A new mode letting players directly control Blades in battle — send up to six Blades and switch between them mid-combat based on what the situation demands
- **Momo:** A new rare Blade with two distinct forms; switching between them changes both her appearance and elemental affinity (light ↔ dark). She arrives with a dedicated side quest

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/xenoblade-chronicles-2-nintendo-switch-2-edition-overview-trailer) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/07/xenoblade-chronicles-2-switch-2-edition-overview-reveals-new-pyra-and-mythra-outfits-merc-assault-and-momo) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/xenoblade-chronicles-2-highlights-switch-2-version-features/) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/xenoblade-chronicles-2-switch-2-edition-overview-trailer/)

## Star Ocean: The Second Story R Surprise-Launches on Switch 2

**Square Enix** dropped a surprise same-day release of **Star Ocean: The Second Story R** on Nintendo Switch 2 on **July 15, 2026**, with no prior announcement. The eShop price is **$49.99**.

The Switch 2 version is a technical step up from the original Switch release:

| | Switch | Switch 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Frame rate | 30 fps | 60 fps |
| Handheld resolution | 576p | 1080p |
| Docked resolution | 864p | 1080p |
| Other | — | Higher texture/shadow resolution, additional objects |

## Disgaea Mayhem Launches to Mixed Reviews — Great Combat, Thin Content

**NIS America's** action RPG spinoff **Disgaea Mayhem** released on **July 23** for Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC (Steam). It launched in Japan in January and was heavily anticipated as the franchise's first full action game.

Critical response has landed at roughly **Metacritic 60** across platforms. The recurring consensus across reviews: the action engine is genuinely strong — seven distinct weapon types each play differently and crowd control against large enemy groups feels satisfying — but the campaign clocks in at **under 10 hours** and level design is repetitive throughout.

Story follows veteran mercenary **N.A.**, hired by Netherworld overlord **Tichelle** to recover the stolen First Radiance Pudding created by her late father. A limited physical edition ($99.99 via NIS America's store) includes an art book, soundtrack digipak, acrylic stand, and keychain.

Sources: [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/disgaea-mayhem/) · [Nintendo Everything review](https://nintendoeverything.com/disgaea-mayhem-review-for-nintendo-switch-2-and-switch/) · [RPGamer review](https://rpgamer.com/review/disgaea-mayhem-review/) · [GameDaily roundup](https://gamedaily.com/games/disgaea-mayhem-review-roundup)

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World Details Battle System and Synthesis

**Square Enix** and **TOSE** published a new batch of details and screenshots for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** on July 23, focusing on its monster-battle and synthesis systems.

Key points:

- **Party size:** Up to four monsters in the active party, plus four in reserve, for a maximum of eight at once. Monster wranglers (the player character class) do not fight directly.
- **Synthesis:** The game's monster-breeding system lets players fuse creatures to create new ones with combined traits — a staple of the *Dragon Quest Monsters* series.
- **New monsters revealed:** **Slime Balls** (available in sour plum, veggie mix, and shrimp tempura varieties), **Bamboo Badboy**, and **Groovy Grouse** join previously shown classics like Golem and Mud Mannequin.

The game remains on track for its **December 3, 2026** release on Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

## Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster Switch 2 Reviews: Definitive Console Version

Reviews for the **Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster** on Nintendo Switch 2 (released **July 23**) have come in broadly positive, with most outlets calling it the best console version of the remaster to date.

The Switch 2 edition runs at up to **4K in TV mode** and **full HD in handheld**, and — most notably for returning players — brings several features previously exclusive to the **PC version**:

- **Auto-save / speed-up toggle**
- **Encounter rate toggle** (reduce or disable random encounters)
- **Max Gil / Max AP** shortcuts

These quality-of-life options were missing from prior console releases. Review outlets called the performance "runs like a dream" and praised the sharper textures and improved shadow rendering. Minor criticisms focused on the age of the underlying remaster assets (unchanged since 2013/2014) rather than the port work itself.

A **physical edition** (game-key card) is scheduled for **August 27 in Japan**; Western physical release details have not yet been announced.

Sources: [Worthplaying](https://worthplaying.com/article/2026/7/22/reviews/150526-nintendo-switch-2-review-final-fantasy-xx-2-hd-remaster/) · [The Outerhaven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/final-fantasy-x-x-2-hd-remaster-switch-2-port-review/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/22/final-fantasy-x-x-2-hd-remaster-switch-2-review/) · [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150099/final-fantasy-x-x2-hd-remaster-switch-2-impressions)

## Celestial Return: Cyberpunk Noir Narrative RPG Out Now on Switch and Switch 2

**Celestial Return** — a narrative RPG from developer **GoldenGratus** — released on **July 16** for Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 ($44.99 / $54.99 respectively; a $10 upgrade pack is available for Switch owners).

The game is set in the rain-soaked cyberpunk city of **Netherveil City** and follows detective **Howard**, who is assisted by a sentient rose as he investigates a string of suicides. Character and narrative design lean into a **manga/American comics hybrid art style** with a synth-heavy soundtrack.

The core mechanical hook is a **personality + dice system**: choices shape Howard's developing personality, which determines which dialogue options and actions remain available. Dice are a limited resource spent on unlocking conversation branches, bribing characters, and influencing high-stakes moments — spending them is permanent.

Sources: [RPGFan review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/celestial-return/) · [RPGamer round-up](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/new-release-round-up-july-16-2026/) · [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/new-turn-based-rpgs-to-play-july-18-2026/)

## Memoirium Sets August 13 Release Date, Gets New Trailer

**Memoirium**, a retro soulslike action RPG from developer **GoldenGratus** (published by **Outersloth**), has set a **PC/Steam release date of August 13, 2026** at **$14.99**. A new trailer was released alongside the date announcement, and a playable demo is currently available on Steam.

The game casts players as an amnesiac figure who awakens in a dreamlike liminal realm and must escape while fighting phantasms — drawing on PS1-era visual aesthetics and soulslike stamina-based combat. A second dev diary video has also gone live discussing inspirations and design philosophy.
