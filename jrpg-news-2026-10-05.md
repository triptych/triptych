## Persona 4 Revival — "Shadow" Trailer Drops Today, Confirms February 19 Release Date

**Atlus** released the **"Shadow" trailer** for **Persona 4 Revival** today, October 5, giving the most substantial look yet at the remake's story framing and its depiction of the **Midnight Channel's Shadow World**.

The 100-second trailer follows Inaba's serial kidnapping crisis and the Investigation Team's growing awareness that a distorted, fog-drenched television dimension lies at the heart of the murders. It shows revised Shadow battle sequences, updated UI, and brief glimpses of Social Link interactions that are now fully voiced — a first for the franchise at this depth of dialogue.

The trailer simultaneously pinned down the full release schedule: **Persona 4 Revival launches February 19, 2027 on PC (Steam) and Xbox Series X|S**, with a **Nintendo Switch 2 version following May 20, 2027**. The PC/Xbox versions had previously been announced for February but the exact date was unconfirmed; the Switch 2 date is new information. The Xbox version is day-one on Game Pass.

## Octopath Traveler I & II Launch on Nintendo Switch 2

**Square Enix** quietly launched both **Octopath Traveler** and **Octopath Traveler II** on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **October 1, 2026**, coinciding with the series' eighth anniversary.

Both ports are sold separately (no included bundle), with each priced at **$59.99 digitally and physically**. A digital-only bundle of both games is available for **$74.99**. Switch 2 enhancements include improved resolution and frame rates in both docked and handheld modes.

There is a notable caveat: **Square Enix confirmed no upgrade path** from the original Switch versions, and **save data is not compatible** between the Switch and Switch 2 editions. Players wishing to continue their existing saves must remain on the original Switch version or restart on the new edition.

## Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen Preview Roundup — Launches October 9

**Capcom**'s **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** expansion launches **October 9** on **PS5, Switch 2, and PC** (Xbox Series X|S also). Preview coverage from TGS 2026 and Gamescom has generated a strongly mixed-but-positive consensus heading into launch.

The expansion adds:
- **The frozen northern region of Norgan**, a high-level area with new story content, enemy types, and an environmental hazard loop built around sub-zero conditions
- **12 new "Last Rites" dungeons** accessible to all players regardless of expansion ownership, offering a randomized gear chase that can rapidly level up lower-level characters
- A **relic transformation system** where Norgan relics are converted into powerful new equipment at crafting stations

PC Gamer noted that the **Pawn interaction expansion** (the ability to trigger physical reactions from your Pawn companion, including a long-requested high-five) is among the more talked-about additions on the PC side. TechRadar came away cautiously optimistic after an hour of hands-on time. Game Rant's Gamescom preview was less convinced, calling the new region "an enigma" but not a reason to return yet. Gaming Bible described their hour with it as more impressive than several hours spent with Crimson Desert, which shipped the same month.

The standalone Switch 2 edition of Dark Arisen launches at the same time for players who have not purchased the base game.

Sources: [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisens-new-region-and-dungeons-sound-great-but-im-more-excited-to-finally-high-five-my-pawn-on-command/) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-is-what-i-hoped-itd-be-coming-from-the-original-but-after-an-hour-i-still-need-to-see-more) · [DualShockers TGS Preview](https://www.dualshockers.com/tgs-2026-dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen/) · [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-preview-early-impressions-gamescom/) · [Gaming Bible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-hands-on-preview-077809-20260917)

## Bloomwalker Confirmed as Ni No Kuni Spin-Off at TGS 2026

**Netmarble** confirmed at **Tokyo Game Show 2026** in September that its previously announced cozy survival title **Bloomwalker** is officially set in the **Ni no Kuni universe**.

A new TGS trailer revealed the game's world of **Luminoa**, a fallen land whose ecosystem has been poisoned. Players take the role of a Bloomwalker who can channel a power to restore life, traversing the world in a **moving house** while cleansing corrupted landscapes through crafting, base-building, and ecosystem restoration loops. The tone is a deliberate departure from the mainline Ni no Kuni RPGs, though the Studio Ghibli-adjacent aesthetic is retained throughout.

The TGS announcement simultaneously added **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PlayStation 5** to the game's platform list alongside the previously announced PC and Xbox Series X|S versions. A release window has not been announced.

## Decapolice Delayed to 2027 — Level-5 Offers Playable TGS Demo

**Level-5** announced during its **LEVEL-5 VISION 2026 II "Dream"** presentation on September 10 that its cyberpunk detective JRPG **Decapolice** has been delayed from its 2026 window to sometime in **2027**. This is the fourth release window the game has missed since its original 2023 announcement.

Level-5 did not provide a specific 2027 date or explain the reason for the latest delay. Despite the news, a **fully playable public demo** was on the floor at Tokyo Game Show 2026 (September 17–21), offering what appears to be a complete opening segment of the game. A three-minute gameplay trailer accompanying the demo showcased the game's **Detective Live Action system**, in which players investigate crime scenes in real-time and construct arrests using collected evidence before transitioning into turn-based battle sequences.

Decapolice is targeting **PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC** upon release.

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation Gets First Latin American Demo at Brasil Game Show

**Square Enix** will bring the first Latin American playable build of **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** to **Brasil Game Show 2026** (October 9–12, Distrito Anhembi, São Paulo). This follows TGS 2026, where a separate demo was already playable in Japan.

The TGS build let players explore the **Whirlwind Maze** and featured two newly playable characters: **Vincent Valentine** and **Cid Highwind**, both joining the roster for the first time in the trilogy. The demo also showcased **Jenova Deathmourner** as the boss encounter. Also on display: the **FITS job assignment system** applied to Vincent's Galian Beast and Chaos forms, with both forms gaining ability slots tied to equipped weapons.

**Final Fantasy VII Revelation** is confirmed for an **April 8, 2027** release on **PS5, Switch 2, and PC** (the Spring 2027 window now has a locked date). The BGS build also includes a separate **Final Fantasy Resonance** boss demo ahead of that game's October 22 launch.

Sources: [PSX Brasil](https://psxbrasil.com.br/final-fantasy-vii-revelation-gameplay-da-tgs-2026-level-up-automatico-e-tres-escolhas-de-nome/) · [Manuel dos Games](https://manualdosgames.com/final-fantasy-vii-tera-demo-jogavel-bgs-26/) · [Acid City On](https://www.acidadeon.com/tudoep/epgames/noticias/final-fantasy-vii-revelation-tera-primeira-demo-jogavel-da-america-latina-na-bgs-2026/)
