## Nintendo Direct 9.9.2026 — A Packed Showcase for JRPG Fans

Today's **Nintendo Direct** aired with roughly 45 minutes of announcements focused on Switch 2 and Switch titles. Several JRPG highlights stood out.

### Eternal Anima — New Marvelous RPG with HD-2D Style, March 4, 2027

The most surprising JRPG reveal of the show was **Eternal Anima**, a brand-new original RPG from **Marvelous** launching **March 4, 2027** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series). The game was written by **Kazushige Nojima** (of *Final Fantasy VII* and *Crisis Core* fame) with direction by **Katsuhisa Higuchi** and story concept from **Keisuke Makino**.

*Eternal Anima* is set in a city that has purged its own history, criminalizing all change and progress. Peacekeeper **Wade** questions this dogma and finds himself on an era-spanning journey through time, forging alliances across generations. Gameplay is turn-based, with Wade wielding a power called **Psychometry** that lets him inherit ancestral skills lost to history — including **Future Sight**, which lets players manipulate the battlefield by predicting enemy actions. The visual style is Marvelous's own take on HD-2D, using layered sprite characters against richly detailed 3D environments.

### Persona 4 Revival — Switch 2 Date Confirmed May 20, 2027; Persona 6 Confirmed for Switch 2

**Atlus** used the Direct to confirm that **Persona 4 Revival** will launch on **Nintendo Switch 2 on May 20, 2027** — three months after its PS5, Xbox Series, and PC release on February 18, 2027.

More significantly, **Persona 6** was formally confirmed for Switch 2. A release date has not been announced, but the confirmation ends speculation about whether the platform would be supported. This marks the first time both a numbered mainline *Persona* sequel and an ongoing remake have been simultaneously confirmed for a Nintendo platform.

### Xenoblade Chronicles 3 — Switch 2 Edition Adds Hero Shimmer and Survivors-Style Heroes' Vault Mode

**Monolith Soft** and Nintendo revealed fresh details for **Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition**, arriving **December 3, 2026**.

The Switch 2 edition introduces **Shimmer**, a new Hero character with rapid combo attacks and a **Foresight** ability that predicts incoming enemy moves. She integrates into the standard Hero system, bringing the total roster of recruitable Heroes above 30.

The headlining new feature is **Heroes' Vault**, a top-down roguelike mode that draws comparisons to the Survivors genre. Players pilot **Noah** from an overhead perspective, eliminating waves of enemies to fill a gauge that powers up their forces, and working toward a final boss encounter with a progressively assembled team. The mode is designed to be replayable without requiring familiarity with the full 100-hour main story.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword — Reviews Are In, and They're Strong

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched on **September 4** for Nintendo Switch 2, PC, and other platforms after a pre-embargo lifting on August 31. The game earned strong across-the-board critical reception:

- **Metacritic: 86** (overall), **89** on Switch 2 (highest platform score)
- **OpenCritic: 86** — "Mighty," 95th percentile, ~95% of critics recommend
- **IGN** awarded a **10/10** — the first perfect score the outlet has given the series

The consensus praises the combat design and boss fights as the series' best since its PlayStation 2 peak. The most common criticism is padding in the campaign's side content. The Switch 2 version's enhanced performance contributed to its platform-high score.

Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onimusha:_Way_of_the_Sword) · [ResetEra Review Thread](https://www.resetera.com/threads/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review-thread.1619431/) · [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-switch-2-review-2026/) · [Timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/blog/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review-roundup)
