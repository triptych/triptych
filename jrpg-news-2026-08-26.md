## Brigandine Abyss Launches August 26–27 on PC, Switch 2, and PS5

**Happinet**'s long-awaited tactical RPG **Brigandine Abyss** launched on **PC via Steam on August 26, 2026**, with the **Nintendo Switch 2 and PlayStation 5 versions arriving August 27**.

The game is set in an entirely new world separate from the settings of previous Brigandine entries, making it accessible to players coming in fresh. Six story campaigns each offer a distinct perspective on the game's central conflict, letting players explore the same events from different nations and characters. A Deluxe Edition bundles a mini art book and digital soundtrack for Switch 2 and PS5 buyers.

Brigandine Abyss is the follow-up to *Brigandine: The Legend of Runersia* (2020) and the first major new entry in the franchise since the PlayStation original in 1998.

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Launched August 20 on PC and Xbox Series

**MAGES.** released **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** on **PC via Steam and Xbox Series X|S on August 20, 2026**, with the **Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5 versions following on October 29**.

The remake rebuilds the landmark 2009 sci-fi visual novel with updated visuals, enhanced pacing, new endings, and expanded animated presentation. The PC/Steam release includes **English text from day one**, removing the usual localization wait for Western fans. The story follows Okabe Rintaro, a self-proclaimed mad scientist in Akihabara whose group accidentally creates a time-machine-adjacent device and becomes entangled in a conspiracy spanning timelines.

The staggered release means Switch and PlayStation players won't receive the game until late October, though the Steam version is fully playable now. An October console release will also be available for those who prefer to play on Switch 2.

## Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition Launches August 28 on Nintendo Switch 2

**FromSoftware** and **Bandai Namco**'s long-delayed **Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition** arrives on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 28, 2026**, priced at **$79.99**.

The Tarnished Edition bundles the base game with the **Shadow of the Erdtree** expansion, **two new starting classes**, expanded armor sets, and new customization options for Torrent. Digital pre-orders include exclusive in-game gestures (The Ring and Ring of Miquella), though both can also be earned during play. A gameplay showcase trailer released this week shows the port running at a stable 30 frames per second — a significant improvement over the 15fps build shown at Gamescom last year.

Players on other platforms can access the new classes and content via **"The Tarnished Pack" DLC**, also launching August 28 on PlayStation 5 and PC.

## Final Fantasy XIV on Switch 2 Reviewed: Strong Port Reception After August 4 Launch

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy XIV** launched on Nintendo Switch 2 on **August 4, 2026**, and reviews are in. The port has been received more positively than many anticipated, with critics noting that it stands as one of the system's most technically ambitious titles.

**Nintendo Life** gave it high marks, calling it "An Expansive Port That Demands Your Commitment," while **TheSixthAxis** and others praised the stable performance in both docked and handheld modes. The one-month complimentary subscription for Switch 2 players to ease server load during onboarding wrapped up in late August, with standard subscription fees now applying. A **30% launch discount** on Complete Edition bundles ran through August 13.

The Switch 2 version supports mouse-and-keyboard input via USB-C and includes access to all content up through the *Endwalker* expansion, with *Dawntrail* available separately. The upcoming *Evercold* expansion — announced at the Berlin Fan Festival last month — is confirmed for Switch 2 alongside PC and PlayStation when it releases in January 2027.

## Neptunia Unlimited Launches Tomorrow in Japan — First New Mainline Neptunia in 11 Years

**Compile Heart**'s **Hyperdimension Neptunia Unlimited** (also written as **Neptunia ∞**) launches in **Japan on August 27, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2** at 8,580 yen. A Western release date has not yet been announced.

The game marks the **first new mainline Neptunia RPG in over a decade**, following *Megadimension Neptunia VII* in 2015. The returning four Goddesses — Neptune, Noire, Blanc, and Vert — are joined by three new characters: **Rouge (Red Heart)**, **Ciel (Blue Heart)**, and the dual-character **Anna and Lot (Eclipse Heart)**. The story centers on a meteorite portending the world's destruction and Neptune's "Retry" mechanic that ties into the narrative.

Combat features fully aerial battles for the Goddess characters, including a multi-Goddess colossal boss encounter. The PS4 version is digital-only.

## Captain Tsubasa II: World Fighters Launches August 28 on Switch, PC, and More

**Bandai Namco**'s **Captain Tsubasa II: World Fighters** launches **worldwide on August 28, 2026** (August 27 in Japan) for **Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam**, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

The sequel to the 2021 soccer RPG features **22 national teams, 110 playable characters**, and an original story scenario set in the World Youth arc, supervised by series creator **Yoichi Takahashi**. The "Super Action Soccer" gameplay returns with new systems: **Max Actions** (high-risk power moves for offense and defense), a **Chain System** that builds momentum through dribbles and passes, and **Miracle Actions** — rare dramatic comeback moments that can shift a match's outcome.

Editions include Standard ($59.99), Deluxe ($79.99), and Ultimate ($89.99) digital versions.

## THQ Nordic Showcase Results: Expeditions: Samurai Into Early Access, Gothic 3 Classic for Switch 2

The **THQ Nordic Digital Showcase 2026** took place on **August 7** and included several RPG-adjacent reveals.

**Expeditions: Samurai** launched directly into **Steam Early Access** on the same day as the showcase. The game is the latest entry in THQ Nordic's exploration-focused RPG series (previously *Expeditions: Viking* and *Expeditions: Rome*), this time set in feudal Japan.

## Coming Soon: Another Eden Begins (September 17) and Emery Hearts (September 2)

Two notable PC and Switch RPGs are arriving in early September:

**Another Eden Begins** (September 17 — Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam) is a fully standalone premium version of the free-to-play mobile JRPG *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space*, rebuilt from the ground up for consoles and PC. Created by **Masato Kato** (*Chrono Trigger*, *Xenogears*) with music by **Yasunori Mitsuda** (*Xenogears*, *Chrono Cross*), the game follows Aldo on a time-traveling adventure after his sister is kidnapped by the Beast King. The standalone version features full English voice acting in the main story, over 18 companions, 10 different endings, and a New Game+ mode. A physical edition is also available.

**Emery Hearts** (September 2 — PC via Steam, Mac) is a retro-inspired action RPG set in a vibrant voxel world where Lug, a young villager, battles robots corrupted by an ancient virus to protect the last human colonies. Published by Shinyuden and developed by Indie Dev Hell, the game features semi-linear environments, real-time combat, and classic puzzle mechanics.
