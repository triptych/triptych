## Persona 4 Revival — New Mechanics Revealed + Chie Voice Recast Confirmed

Atlus dropped a batch of new **English screenshots and battle system details** for **Persona 4 Revival** ahead of next week's dedicated broadcast (June 18, 4 PM PT).

New combat mechanics revealed:
- **Airborne launches**: landing normal attacks on a status-afflicted enemy sends them flying into adjacent Shadows, spreading the ailment.
- **Prime Time**: a new gauge that, when filled, enables consecutive actions, free skill costs, ignored resistances, and free Baton Pass uses — effectively a high-risk-reward burst window.
- **Mid-battle party swap**: players can change party composition during combat, a first for this entry.

Separately, **Erin Fitzgerald**, who voiced Chie Satonaka in *Persona 4 Golden*, has confirmed via social media that she will **not** be reprising the role in P4 Revival. Atlus has not yet named her replacement. The studio has a history of recasting for remake projects, but Chie's fanbase reacted strongly, given her iconic "CHIE!" delivery.

P4 Revival remains on track for **February 18, 2027** (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC/Steam).

## Persona 6 — Full Announcement Breakdown

Atlus officially revealed **Persona 6** at the **Xbox Games Showcase on June 7**, confirming leaks that had circulated for months.

The brief teaser trailer opens in a graveyard lined with headstones and closes on the logo. The apparent color theme is **neon green** — a sharp departure from Persona 5's red or Persona 3's blue. No gameplay was shown; the reveal was atmosphere-first. The Xbox website describes the game as "a bold new standalone story blending heartfelt daily life and new characters with pulse-pounding supernatural adventure."

Platform details:
- Xbox Series X|S and PC via Microsoft Store — **day one on Xbox Game Pass / PC Game Pass**
- Xbox Play Anywhere certified
- PlayStation 5 and Steam also confirmed

No release window has been given. Leaker reports suggest a **late 2027** window, but Atlus has not confirmed this.

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World — Story & Characters Detailed

Square Enix published a full breakdown of **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World**'s story and cast ahead of its **December 3, 2026** launch (PS5, Xbox Series, Switch/Switch 2, PC).

The protagonists are **Bianca and Nera**, the two love interests from *Dragon Quest V: Hand of the Heavenly Bride* — a notable choice that roots the game firmly in DQ lore. Their mission is to journey through Witherwood, a dying realm, and restore it to life by befriending, raising, and synthesizing monsters.

The game features **500+ monsters** from across the DQ series. Monster synthesis — combining two monsters to create a more powerful offspring — returns as a core system. The combat pits player-raised monsters against wild ones in strategic turn-based battles.

## Kingdom Hearts — HD Collections Coming to Switch 2 on October 8

Square Enix confirmed that the full **Kingdom Hearts** back-catalog arrives on Nintendo Switch 2 on **October 8, 2026**:

- *Kingdom Hearts HD 1.5 + 2.5 ReMIX*
- *Kingdom Hearts HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue*
- *Kingdom Hearts III + Re Mind*

This makes Switch 2 the first Nintendo platform to receive native ports of the mainline KH series (prior entries appeared via cloud streaming on Switch 1).

**Kingdom Hearts 4** also showed a new trailer at the June 9 Nintendo Direct, featuring a look at Sora in the game's modern city setting, Heartless combat, and updated movement abilities. The game was confirmed for Switch 2 **at launch**, though no release date has been set — rumors continue to point toward 2027.
