## Netflix Developing Live-Action Persona Series With Stranger Things' 21 Laps

**Variety** reported on **June 29, 2026** that Netflix is developing a live-action television adaptation of Atlus/SEGA's **Persona** franchise — the first time the RPG series has been adapted into live-action.

**Creative team:**
- **Christopher Monfette** (*12 Monkeys*, *Star Trek: Picard*, *9-1-1*) is attached to write the series and serve as executive producer/showrunner.
- **Shawn Levy**, **Dan Levine**, and **Robert Atwood** of **21 Laps Entertainment** — the studio behind Netflix's *Stranger Things* — will executive produce.
- **Dmitri M. Johnson**, **Michael Lawrence Goldberg**, and **Timothy I. Stevenson** of **Story Kitchen** are also attached as executive producers.
- **Toru Nakahara** of SEGA will executive produce on behalf of the publisher.

**Details:**
- The report does not specify which mainline Persona game (or original story) the series will adapt, and Netflix declined to comment.
- The news lands the same week as Atlus's deep-dive broadcast for *Persona 4 Revival* and weeks after *Persona 6* was officially confirmed, underscoring the franchise's high profile heading into a packed 2026–2027.

Sources: [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/persona-live-action-tv-series-netflix-1236798581/) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/variety-persona-live-action-series-in-development-at-netflix) · [Persona Central](https://personacentral.com/persona-tv-series/) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-06-29/variety-netflix-developing-live-action-persona-series/.239114)

## Dissidia Duellum Final Fantasy Adds Tidus for Final Fantasy X's 25th Anniversary

**Square Enix** added **Tidus** to *Dissidia Duellum Final Fantasy* on **June 30, 2026**, as a limited-time Season Pass reward tied to *Final Fantasy X*'s 25th anniversary.

**Character details:**
- Tidus is an **Agile-type, Water-element** fighter.
- His character action, **Quick Hit**, dashes him forward into a multi-hit attack; his featured UR abilities are **Jecht Shot** and **Blitz Ace**.
- He's obtainable for free via the normal Season Pass track through **July 27**.

**FFX 25th Anniversary celebration:**
- Players can claim up to **100 free summons**, with 25 free pulls unlocking every Wednesday across four weeks starting **July 1**.
- The update adds a new Memoria, **"A Dream Fulfilled,"** and a new map, **Corrosion: Zanarkand**.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20793-dissidia-duellum-final-fantasy-tidus-release-date) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/dissidia-duellum-tidus-final-fantasy-10-update-roadmap/) · [Nova Crystallis](https://novacrystallis.com/2026/06/tidus-arrives-as-a-limited-time-fighter-in-dissidia-duellum-final-fantasy-on-june-30-2026/)

## Suikoden Live Vol. 6 Broadcast Set for July 1, Will Detail STAR LEAP Mobile Game

**Konami** announced a new **Suikoden Live** broadcast for **July 1, 2026** at 4:00 a.m. PT / 7:00 a.m. ET on YouTube, running roughly an hour.

**Focus and guests:**
- The broadcast will cover the latest news and updates for **Suikoden STAR LEAP**, the franchise's mobile game, ahead of its Japan-first launch on Android and iOS.
- A worldwide mobile release and a subsequent **Steam** release are planned to follow.
- Guests include voice actors **Mikoi Sasaki**, **Aimi**, and **Ayasa Itou**.

This is the sixth entry in Konami's recurring Suikoden Live broadcast series, which has been used throughout 2026 to drip-feed news on the franchise's revival efforts alongside the previously released *Suikoden I & II HD Remaster*.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/suikoden-live-broadcast-set-for-july-1) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/suikoden-live-broadcast-july-2026-star-leap/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/06/26/suikoden-live-broadcast-announced-for-july-1/)

## Ninjala 2: The Uncharted Planet Gets First Public Demo at Anime Expo 2026

**GungHo Online Entertainment America** detailed its full **Anime Expo 2026** (July 2–5, Los Angeles Convention Center) plans, headlined by the first public hands-on demo of **Ninjala 2: The Uncharted Planet** at West Hall booth #2316.

**Demo details:**
- Two playable courses: a **Planetary Exploration Course** showcasing combat, and a **Photography Course** for a sightseeing-focused experience across the open world.
- Everyone who plays receives an inflatable bat modeled after the game's **IPPON Katana** weapon.
- Prize giveaways include a Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller bundle, with winners choosing between *Ninjala 2*, *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter*, *LUNAR Remastered Collection*, or *GRANDIA HD Collection*.

**Game overview:**
- *Ninjala 2: The Uncharted Planet* evolves the original's PvP brawler formula into a **co-op open-world action game**, billed as the first chapter of a two-part saga.
- Launches exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2** in **Spring 2027**.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/gungho-online-entertainment-announces-anime-expo-2026-plans) · [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/62107-gungho-s-full-anime-expo-2026-plans-detailed-ninjala-2-demo-prizes-and-merch) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/06/22/gungho-online-entertainment-anime-expo-2026-lineup-ninjala-2-the-uncharted-planet/) · [Worthplaying](https://worthplaying.com/article/2026/6/22/news/150257-ninjala-2-the-uncharted-planet-heads-to-anime-expo-2026-with-playable-demo-prizes-and-merch/)
