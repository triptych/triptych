## Tales of Eternia Remastered — Launches Yesterday to Positive Reviews

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** launched worldwide on **October 16, 2026** for Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, PS4, and PC via Steam (October 15 in Japan). The remaster was developed by **TOSE**, the same studio behind Tales of Graces F Remastered.

Early reviews are broadly positive with some caveats about the game's age. **Final Boss** called it a story worth the friction — recommending it for JRPG fans who prioritize narrative, while noting the dated combat and environments prevent a universal recommendation. **CGM** praised the quality-of-life additions, saying they make the already-solid game feel far more accessible, and highlighted the restoration of **skits** that were absent from previous Western releases.

New features include:
- Resolution and sound toggles between original and remastered presentation
- Boost settings for experience gain and encounter rates
- High-speed mode
- New Game+, autosave, and **Nereid's Labyrinth** unlocked on a first playthrough
- The game is based on the enhanced **2005 PSP version**, meaning it includes content absent from the original PS1 release

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Expansion Pass Announced, Wave 1 Out Now

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems** announced an **Expansion Pass** for **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** on **October 14, 2026**, with the first content wave going live the same day on Nintendo Switch 2. The pass costs **$34.99**; a bundle of the base game and Expansion Pass is **$104.99** on the eShop.

The pass is structured in three waves:

- **Wave 1 (available now):** Four prototype weapons modeled on Heroes' Relics from *Fire Emblem: Three Houses*, one for each of the game's four protagonists.
- **Wave 2 (Holiday 2026):** Additional wearable outfits and three new allies — **Aurora**, **Smyrnos**, and **Jurah**.
- **Wave 3 (2027):** New story scenarios centered on **Bertrand**, **Talimun**, **Orchel**, and **Anatolia**, plus new allies and new classes.

A free **version 1.1.0** patch released alongside the announcement adds Expansion Pass support and a new DLC eShop link on the title screen.

## Octopath Traveler I & II — Switch 2 Editions Launch With No Upgrade Path or Save Transfer

Both **Octopath Traveler** and **Octopath Traveler II** are now available for **Nintendo Switch 2**, having launched on **October 1, 2026**. Each game costs **$59.99** individually; a digital bundle of both is **$74.99**. Physical releases use Nintendo Switch 2 Game-Key Cards, which require a download to play.

The Switch 2 versions target **60 FPS in both docked and handheld modes**, a significant improvement over the original Switch versions' 30 FPS cap, and Square Enix describes improved resolution across the board.

The major controversy surrounding both releases is **Square Enix's decision to offer no upgrade path and no save data transfer** from the original Switch versions. The official product listing states: *"there are no plans to sell an upgrade pack to upgrade the Nintendo Switch version to the Nintendo Switch 2 version."* Players who own either game on Switch and want the Switch 2 versions must purchase them again at full price and start new save files.

## Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga Announced for Switch 2, Switch, PS5, and PC

**Taito** and **Clear River Games** announced **Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga** on **September 20, 2026** at the **PC Gaming Show Tokyo Direct**. The collection brings together the original *Lufia & the Fortress of Doom* (1993) and the acclaimed prequel *Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals* (1995) to modern platforms for the first time — notably marking the **first official PC release** for either game.

The collection is planned for **2027** on Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam. Physical editions for Switch, Switch 2, and PS5 are planned; exact timing and pricing are to be announced. Clear River Games is the Western publisher.

Taito describes the collection as retaining the original pixel art while adding new gameplay functions suited to current hardware. No further specifics on those functions have been shared.

## Brigandine Abyss — Mixed Reviews After August Launch

**Happinet** and **Lancarse**'s **Brigandine Abyss** launched on PC via Steam on **August 26, 2026**, with console versions (Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series, and PC) following on **August 27**. The game costs **$49.99** and a free demo covering the first two seasons of the Gran Dragnica and Scarlet Will campaigns is available.

Critical reception has been mixed. **Niche Gamer** called it one of the more substantial SRPGs of the year on Switch 2, praising its depth once players commit to learning its systems — while noting it demands patience. **The Outer Haven** praised it as a return of a legend. **RPG Site**, however, found it a disappointing follow-up that falls short of its potential. **Siliconera** flagged a mouse sensitivity issue on Switch 2 severe enough to make that control method unusable, though praised the visual improvements over the previous game.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21237-brigandine-abyss-now-available-launch-trailer-switch-2-ps5-xbox-series-pc-steam) · [Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/reviews/brigandine-abyss-review/) · [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/brigandine-abyss-review-return-of-a-legend/) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/?p=1121839)
