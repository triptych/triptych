## Star Ocean: The Second Story R Surprise-Launches on Switch 2

**Square Enix** released a **Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** of **Star Ocean: The Second Story R** on **July 16, 2026**, without a prior announcement window — the version arrived the same day it was revealed to the public. The upgrade is priced at **$49.99** and carries several technical improvements over the original Switch release:

- Frame rate rises from **30 FPS to 60 FPS** in all modes
- Resolution jumps from **576p handheld / 864p docked** to **1080p in both modes**
- Additional texture detail, object density, and improved shadow resolution

The Switch 2 version does **not** support save data transfer from the Switch 1 version, and no paid upgrade path exists for existing Switch owners — it is a full-price standalone purchase. The release coincides with the **Star Ocean 30th Anniversary**, and Square Enix has not announced additional anniversary titles at this time.

## Culdcept BEGINS Launches — First New Entry in a Decade

**OmiyaSoft** released **Culdcept BEGINS** on **July 16, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2** and **Nintendo Switch** (digital only). A **PC via Steam** release is scheduled for **Q4 2026**. This is the first all-new title in the long-running **Culdcept** series in **ten years**.

The game features over **400 cards**, returning to the franchise's signature blend of board game movement and collectible card-based combat. **Famitsu** awarded it a **Gold Hall of Fame** distinction ahead of launch.

Two physical editions are available for Switch 2 via the NIS America Online Store:

- **Standard Edition — $54.99**: Base game and booklet
- **Limited SteelBook Edition — $89.99**: Folding box, SteelBook case, soundtrack CD, and a set of 10 creature tablet-style cards

## Disgaea Mayhem Demo Available Now on Switch, Switch 2, PS5, and PC

**NIS America** released a playable demo for **Disgaea Mayhem** on **July 9, 2026**, across **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**. The full game launches **July 23, 2026**.

The demo grants access to **three stages** — each populated with dozens of enemies and a boss — and lets players sample all **seven weapon classes**: swords, spears, fists, axes, bows, guns, and staves. Each weapon type has its own attack range and four unique special techniques.

Disgaea Mayhem is a real-time action spin-off of the series starring mercenary **N.A.**, swapping classic turn-based strategy for fast-paced combat. Classic Disgaea systems including **Item World**, **Reincarnation**, **Magichange**, **Debate Assembly**, and **Chara World** all return. A **Limited Edition ($99.99)** is available from the NIS America Online Store, including the game, art book, digipak soundtrack, acrylic stand, and acrylic keychain.

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation Story Expansion Pass Surfaces in Epic Games Store Database

Listings for multiple **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** DLC packages — including a **Story Expansion Pass** — have emerged from the **Epic Games Store** backend via EpicDB, a third-party tool that tracks changes to Epic's storefront database. The listings were discovered in early July and include a **Pre-Purchase Bonus**, a **Premium Edition**, a **Premium Plus Edition**, the **Story Expansion Pass**, and several standalone DLC entries.

No pricing, release dates, or content details are attached to any of the listings. Square Enix has not confirmed the DLC. The leak follows an established pattern in the trilogy: *Final Fantasy VII Remake* received the *Intergrade* / Yuffie episode as a PS5-exclusive chapter, and *Final Fantasy VII Rebirth* followed a similar content model.

**Final Fantasy VII Revelation** — the concluding chapter of the Remake trilogy — is scheduled to release in **Spring 2027** on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam, and the Epic Games Store**.

## Silver Palace Showcases at Anime Expo, Dichotomy Beta Registrations Close July 16

**Elementa Games** showcased open-world action RPG **Silver Palace** at **Anime Expo 2026** (July 2–5), marking the start of a year-long global convention tour. Registrations for the **Silver Palace: Dichotomy Beta Test** were open through **July 16**.

Silver Palace is set in **Silvernia**, a sprawling Victorian-era metropolis powered by a mysterious energy source called Silverium. Players take the role of a detective returning to the city after a three-year absence and investigate a conspiracy touching criminal syndicates, rival factions, and the royal court. Combat chains basic attacks to build **Activation Energy** for ranged skills, stuns enemies for high-damage executions, and generates **Reactor Energy** that enables switching between four party members to unleash **Tactical Assaults**.

Following Anime Expo, the convention tour continues at Japan Expo (Paris, July 9–12), Bilibili World (Shanghai, July 10–12), Firefly ACG EXPO (Guangzhou, July 17–20), Anime NYC (August 20–23), gamescom (Cologne, August 26–30), and gamescom Asia (Bangkok, October 29–November 1).

## Celestial Return Launches on PC — Cyberpunk Narrative RPG Draws Disco Elysium Comparisons

**Celestial Return** released on **PC via Steam on July 14, 2026**, priced at **$14.99** (with a 20% launch discount). A console release is targeted for early 2027.

The game is a narrative RPG set in the cyberpunk noir **Netherveil City**, following detective Howard — aided by a sentient rose — as he investigates a string of suicides. Player choices and dice rolls together determine how the investigation unfolds. The art is hand-drawn, blending human silhouettes with abstract mechanical constructs to render the city's world. **Steam early user reviews** sit at **82% positive** from initial players. Critics have drawn comparisons to *Disco Elysium*, with one outlet giving it **8.5/10**.
