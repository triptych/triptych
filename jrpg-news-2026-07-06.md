## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Release Date Moved Up to September 4

**Capcom** has announced that **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** is launching three weeks earlier than planned. The action RPG now releases on **September 4, 2026**, moved up from the previously announced **September 25** date. The revised date applies to all platforms: **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam, and PC via the Epic Games Store**.

No reason was given for the change. A pre-purchase discount remains active on Steam. The game is a full series revival set in the Sengoku period, developed by an internal Capcom team, and marks the franchise's first new mainline entry since *Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams* in 2005.

## Wild Wild Eden Officially Revealed — Shueisha Games' Open-World Survival Crafting RPG

**Shueisha Games** and developer **Magnus Games Studio** have formally unveiled **Wild Wild Eden**, an anime-style open-world survival crafting RPG first teased under the codename **Project Survival** at Anime Expo 2023.

The game is set on an untamed alien planet filled with floating landmasses, ancient ruins, and mysterious creatures. Core systems include base building, creature taming and riding, a deep monster breeding system to create unique companions, and online co-op. The studio reports the project underwent significant changes since its initial announcement, including a full engine switch, with both visuals and core gameplay evolving substantially.

**Wild Wild Eden** is targeting **Steam Early Access in Spring 2027**. Confirmed languages at launch are Japanese, English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and Korean.

## The Last Hour of an Epic TO THE MOON RPG Gets New Trailer; Arrives Summer 2027

**Freebird Games** has released a new trailer for **The Last Hour of an Epic TO THE MOON RPG**, confirming a **Summer 2027** release window. A playable demo is planned for **Steam Next Fest in October 2026**, running from approximately **October 19–26**.

The game is a standalone turn-based RPG conceived as the finale to the *To the Moon* series. The central conceit: players load a save file already through 99 hours of grinding and play only the final stretch — joining a fully levelled party of **Neil, Eva, Roxie, and Rob** as they ascend a Lighthouse-shaped dungeon to confront the series' ultimate antagonist. Puzzles and turn-based combat fill the tower's floors, with the game drawing on all previous entries in the series for narrative payoff.

The title follows *Just a To the Moon Series Beach Episode* (2024) and is intended to close out the story arc begun in the original *To the Moon* (2011).

## Digimon Story: Time Stranger Launches on Switch and Switch 2 on July 10

**Bandai Namco** has confirmed that **Digimon Story: Time Stranger** — developed by **Media.Vision** — arrives on **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 on July 10, 2026**. The date was announced during the **Nintendo Direct: Partner Showcase** in February 2026.

The game originally launched on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC** on **October 3, 2025**. The Switch 2 version is priced at **$10 less** than the PS5 and Xbox versions, making it the most affordable retail option at launch. A playable demo is currently available on both Switch eShops ahead of release.

*Time Stranger* is a traditional monster-collecting turn-based JRPG in the *Digimon Story* lineage (*Cyber Sleuth*, *Hacker's Memory*), and received broadly positive reviews upon its original release.

Sources: [Bandai Namco Europe](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/digimon/news/digimon-story-time-stranger-arrives-nintendo-switch-and-nintendo-switch-2-10-july) · [Nintendo Reporters](https://www.nintendoreporters.com/en/news/nintendo-switch-2/digimon-story-time-stranger-comes-to-nintendo-switch-and-nintendo-switch-2-on-july-10-2026/) · [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/digimon-story-time-stranger-switch-switch-2-physical-buying-guide/1100-6538012/) · [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/digimon-story-time-stranger/switch-release-date/)

## RPGamer Kicks Off #JRPGJuly Community Game-Along

**RPGamer** has launched **#JRPGJuly**, a month-long community game-along celebrating the genre throughout July. Staff members are publicly sharing their intended play lists for the event, and community members are encouraged to participate by playing and discussing JRPGs across the month under the hashtag.

The initiative accompanies RPGamer's weekly round-up coverage, which also includes a backlog diary segment and video reviews. The event has no formal competition element and is intended as a low-barrier community tradition.

Sources: [RPGamer Round-Up: June 28 – July 5](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/rpgamer-round-up-june-28-july-5-2/)
