## Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026 — FF7 Revelation New Trailer, ANANTA Gameplay, and More

Geoff Keighley's **Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026** aired tonight, August 25, delivering a two-hour showcase with several RPG-relevant moments.

**Final Fantasy VII Revelation** was the headline JRPG appearance. Director **Naoki Hamaguchi** took the stage to present a new extended trailer for the trilogy's conclusion, with sources indicating a **playable demo** has been made available on the Gamescom show floor. The trailer reportedly showcases the **Highwind airship** as a traversal hub, confirming that the entire planet is freely explorable from the air, with parachute drop-ins anywhere on the map creating seamless land-to-air transitions — a significant mechanical departure from *Rebirth*'s regional structure. *Final Fantasy VII Revelation* remains on track for **Spring 2027** simultaneously on **PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC** (Steam and Epic Games Store).

**ANANTA** (formerly *Project Mugen*), the urban open-world action RPG from **NetEase/Naked Rain**, also appeared with a "fresh look" after going nearly a year without public updates. The game is described as a party-based action RPG set in a modern city called **Nova City** featuring parkour traversal, mix-and-match team combat, and deep environmental interaction. Platforms at announcement remain **PC, PS5, iOS, and Android** (no Switch announced); a simultaneous global launch is planned with no release date yet set.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct — True Protagonist Revealed, Full Combat System Detailed

Nintendo held a dedicated **~20-minute Nintendo Direct for Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** on **August 4**, revealing story elements that go well beyond the previously announced four-protagonist structure.

The game's **true plot structure** was disclosed: the world is set five years in the future, where the demon god **Balor** has returned and destroyed civilisation. The goddess **Sothis** summons a nameless **"white-clad hero"** — the game's actual protagonist, not one of the four gladiatorial champions — and sends them back in time. The player's role is to influence the fates of **Cai, Dietrich, Theodora, and Leda** across the Heroic Games tournament so that all four survive and can stand together against Balor in the future. Unlike *Three Houses*, all four champions' storylines are experienced within **a single playthrough**.

The combat system was fully detailed:
- Weapons: **swords, gauntlets, spears, axes, bows, and magic** (magic has a **gun form** variant)
- **Combat Arts** deal amplified damage at the cost of **weapon durability**
- **Blaze Arts** trigger special moves at the cost of the **user's HP**

*Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave* launches **September 17, 2026** exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2**.

## Brigandine Abyss Launches This Week — Mixed Reviews Greet the Series' Return

**Brigandine Abyss**, the fourth entry in **Matrix Corporation / Happinet Games**' cult grand-strategy SRPG franchise, launches **August 26 on PC (Steam)** and **August 27 on Nintendo Switch 2 and PlayStation 5** (also Xbox Series X|S).

The game is set on a new continent called **Meltitea** and features **six story campaigns** driven by distinct factions, plus a freeform **24-faction Mission Mode** with unique win conditions per faction and no narrative obligations. Key mechanical additions include **battlefield verticality** and more granular **starting position** customisation before battles.

Reviews are mixed-to-positive, landing in the **7/10–7.5/10** range across major outlets. Critics praised the stronger narrative ambition compared to earlier entries and the tactically sharper combat, but flagged **easily exploitable enemy AI**, **limited variety**, and a tonal mismatch between the Unreal Engine 5 environments and character models. The RPGFan review described it as "best of the series, but still lacking," while Noisy Pixel called it "easy to love, hard to recommend."

## Elden Ring Tarnished Edition Arrives on Switch 2 August 28

**FromSoftware** and **Bandai Namco**'s **Elden Ring Tarnished Edition** launches on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 28, 2026**, priced at **$79.99**. The edition includes:

- The complete base game
- The **Shadow of the Erdtree** expansion
- Two new starting classes: **Heavy Knight** and **Idus Knight**
- New cosmetic armour and **Torrent** mount customisation options

Simultaneously, a **Tarnished Pack DLC** ($4.99) makes the two new classes and cosmetics available to existing owners on PlayStation and PC, so Switch 2 isn't the only way to access the new content.

Digital pre-orders are live on the Nintendo eShop. *Elden Ring* originally launched in 2022 and won multiple Game of the Year awards; the Switch 2 edition marks the game's first appearance on a Nintendo platform.

## Neptunia Unlimited Launches in Japan on August 27

**Compile Heart**'s **Neptunia Unlimited** — the first new mainline entry in the *Hyperdimension Neptunia* series in over a decade — launches in Japan on **August 27, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch 2, and Nintendo Switch**. Western and PC release dates have not yet been announced.

The game centres on Neptune gaining a **"retry" ability** following a catastrophic meteor event, allowing her to loop between endings and new beginnings across its flight-based action combat. Classic CPU Goddesses (Neptune, Blanc, Vert, Noir) return alongside new characters. A physical Galaxy Edition is available in addition to standard physical/digital options; the PS4 version is digital-only.

## Kitaria Fables 2 Closed Beta Opens September 7 — Spring 2027 Target

**PQube**, **Secret Level Studios**, and **Twin Hearts** announced a **two-week closed beta for Kitaria Fables 2** running **September 7–21, 2026 on PC (Steam)**. Applications close September 4; accepted participants will receive access to approximately **60% of the planned content**, estimated at around 10 hours of play.

The sequel to the 2021 action RPG is set after the events of the first game on the new **Arkadera Peninsula**. Players take control of two new heroes — Royal Army "Pathfinder" **Alice Skylar** and local guide **Dusty Tuftkin** — with distinct unlockable skill trees, material gathering, crafting, and farm management across solo and co-op (local and online) play.

The full game is targeting **Spring 2027** for **PC, Nintendo Switch 2, and PlayStation 5** (also Xbox Series X|S).

## Suikoden STAR LEAP Anime Screens in Japanese Theaters Starting August 21

**Konami**'s mobile JRPG **Suikoden STAR LEAP** launched in Japan on **August 7** (iOS/Android, free-to-play). To accompany that release, the **first three episodes of the Suikoden anime adaptation** began a two-week theatrical run at **10 cinemas across Japan on August 21**. The anime is scheduled to premiere on broadcast in **October 2026**.

The game is set in Solar Year 453, bridging the timelines of *Suikoden V* and *Suikoden I*. It features the franchise's signature 108 Stars of Destiny structure alongside gacha elements. A **global and PC (Steam) release date** has not yet been announced; Konami is conducting a staged Japan rollout to test server stability before widening the release.
