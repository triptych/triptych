## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Launches to Critical Acclaim — Metacritic 86, IGN Perfect Score

**Capcom**'s long-awaited revival **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched on **September 4, 2026** for Nintendo Switch 2 and PC (also PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S). The game has earned the franchise's best-ever critical reception.

The Switch 2 version leads with an **89/100 on Metacritic** (higher than the PS5 version's 86), placing the game firmly in "Universal Acclaim" territory. On OpenCritic, the title sits at **86/100 ("Mighty")** with roughly **95% of critics recommending it**. **IGN awarded a perfect 10/10** — the first perfect score the outlet has given the series. Nintendo Life and Nintendo World Report each awarded **9/10**, and Nintendo Insider similarly landed at 9/10 on a Switch 2 copy.

Set in feudal Japan, *Way of the Sword* follows a new protagonist through a story that reimagines the tone of the original trilogy with a modern action-RPG framework. Critics praised the combat depth and boss design; the most common criticism is that side content grows repetitive in the back half of the campaign.

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Now Available on Switch 2

**NIS America**'s upgraded ports of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** launched on **September 10, 2026** for Nintendo Switch 2 (also PlayStation 5).

The Switch 2 versions feature **4K resolution, 120 frames per second**, and **mouse input support** via the console's USB-C port. Each game is priced at **$39.99 digitally**, and a physical **Deluxe Edition** bundles both titles for **$69.99**, including a digital soundtrack and collector's card set. A premium **Limited Edition** ($109.99 via the NIS America Online Store) adds an art book, soundtrack CDs, holographic art cards, an acrylic stand, iron-on insignias, and a Mishelam Wonderland coaster set.

The two games adapt the *Crossbell arc* of Nihon Falcom's long-running *Trails* saga, first released in Japan in 2010–2011 and later localized for PC and the original PlayStation. The Switch 2 versions represent their first appearance on a Nintendo platform.

## Nintendo Direct September 2026 — Key JRPG Highlights

Nintendo held a **September 2026 Nintendo Direct** this week, featuring several notable JRPG announcements:

### Final Fantasy VII Revelation — New Trailer, April 8, 2027 Release Confirmed
**Square Enix** showed a new trailer for **Final Fantasy VII Revelation**, the conclusion to the *Final Fantasy VII Remake* trilogy. The game is confirmed for **April 8, 2027** on Nintendo Switch 2 (also PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC). Pre-orders went live digitally following the showcase. As an added bonus, **Crisis Core: Final Fantasy VII Reunion** also launched on Switch 2 on the same day as the Direct.

### Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United — Revealed for February 16, 2027
**Square Enix** and developer **Xeen** (known for the *Trials of Mana* and *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven* remakes) announced **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United**, a full 3D remake of the 1995 Super Famicom classic. It releases on **February 16, 2027** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**.

The remake retains the original's **Free Scenario System**, letting players choose one of eight protagonists — Ellen, Julian, Katarina, Khalid, Mikhail, Princess Monika, Sarah, or Thomas — and tackle main and side quests in any order. New features include **3D graphics, fully voiced cutscenes in Japanese and English, selectable difficulty levels**, and newly arranged compositions by original composer **Kenji Ito** alongside the classic score.

### Eternal Anima — Marvelous Announces HD-2D-Style JRPG for March 4, 2027
**Marvelous** unveiled **Eternal Anima**, an original JRPG with a visual style that evokes the HD-2D aesthetic, coming to **Nintendo Switch 2 and PC** on **March 4, 2027** (also PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S).

The game is set in Urvis Ael, a "false paradise" where yearning for a better future is a capital offense, built on a world whose four continents were sunk by an apocalyptic calamity. Protagonist **Wade** can relive memories of the past through a mysterious power. The development team includes director **Katsuhisa Higuchi**, scenario writer **Kazushige Nojima** (*Final Fantasy VII*, *X*, *XV*), and story concept writer **Keisuke Makino**. The Switch 2 physical edition is priced at **$59.99**; a Limited Edition is available for **$89.99**.

### The Apothecary Diaries: The False Imperial Brother — Teased for 2027
**KOEI TECMO** briefly teased **The Apothecary Diaries: The False Imperial Brother**, a new JRPG adventure based on the popular anime and manga property, targeting a 2027 release. Platforms and further details were not announced.

## Level-5 VISION 2026 II "Dream" Showcase — Yo-kai Watch 2, Layton Remake, Inazuma Eleven Sequel

**Level-5** held its **LEVEL5 VISION 2026 II "Dream"** online showcase on **September 10** at 21:00 JST, delivering updates and reveals across its upcoming lineup. English subtitles were provided live.

### Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain
An update on **Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain** was presented, confirming it is coming to **Nintendo Switch 2, and PC**. Level-5 also plans a **media expansion** tied to the title, including short animations and merchandise.

### Professor Layton and the New World of Steam — December 10, 2026
**Professor Layton and the New World of Steam** received updated footage. The game is confirmed for **December 10, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC** (also PlayStation 5), a date that was previously announced at Nintendo Direct.

### Professor Layton and the Curious Village Remake — Announced
As the showcase's surprise closer, Level-5 announced **Professor Layton and the Curious Village Remake**, a full remake of the original 2007 puzzle-adventure that started the franchise. No platforms or release window were detailed.

### Inazuma Eleven: Flames of Revolution — World Premiere
Level-5 debuted the first-ever look at **Inazuma Eleven: Flames of Revolution**, a direct sequel to *Inazuma Eleven Victory Road*. No platforms or release window were confirmed.

### Other Updates
Additional footage was shown for **Holy Horror Mansion** (PC, Switch 2, 2027; playable at TGS 2026), **Decapolice**, and **Snack World: Reloaded**. Level-5 also confirmed its lineup will be playable at **Tokyo Game Show 2026**.

## This Week in JRPG Demos — Psycho Casket Steam Demo

**Psycho Casket**, an indie JRPG from developer **ClockworkLink**, released its **Steam demo on September 8, 2026**. The game's hook is a literal one: the setting is a **sentient messaging application**, and protagonist **Casket** must navigate conversations and environmental puzzles to rescue her friends from an entity that seeks to consume everything in the world. The full release is targeting **Q4 2026 for PC**.
