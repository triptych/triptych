## Metaphor: ReFantazio — Switch 2 Port Launches to Mostly Positive Reviews

**Atlus**'s acclaimed **Metaphor: ReFantazio** arrived on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026**, priced at **$39.99** digitally. Physical game-key card editions are also available at retail.

Reviews landed ahead of launch and are broadly positive, with consistent caveats about performance. RPG Site rates the port highly but notes it still needs optimization. Nintendo Life and Final Weapon both call it an instant classic in handheld form. The game targets 60 fps in both docked and handheld modes but doesn't always sustain that target — the Performance mode holds up better than the Graphics mode, though frame rate variation is scene-dependent.

Notably, the port **does not support Joy-Con 2 mouse controls** but does support touchscreen input and swipe gestures, which suits inventory and menu navigation well.

A prologue demo was available on the eShop ahead of launch, with save data carrying forward to the full game.

## Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle — New Mobile Title Fully Revealed

**Square Enix** fully revealed **Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle** on **November 10, 2026**, targeting a **2027 global launch on iOS and Android** as a **free-to-play** title with in-app purchases.

The game is set on **Monbelusha**, a floating isle high above the world, where the annual Ringing Feast celebration is underway when trouble begins. The gameplay is designed around classic Dragon Quest pillars — exploration, command-based combat, and character growth — adapted for mobile play. The developer is **WFS** (also known as Armor Project handles planning and production for Square Enix).

A **closed beta test** is scheduled for **December 8–15, 2026**. Sign-ups are open until **November 23**, with 25,000 testers selected by lottery.

## Edge of Memories — Not Cancelled: Delayed to December 3 with New Demo

An update that corrects a prior report: **Edge of Memories is not cancelled**. Following the liquidation of developer **Midgar Studio** in July, it appeared the game's fate was uncertain. However, the action RPG has been given a new release date of **December 3, 2026** for **PC (Steam), and other platforms**.

Publisher **Nacon** announced the delay from the original November 5 date in mid-October, with the stated reason being to avoid a crowded point in the end-of-year release calendar. **Digital pre-orders carry over automatically** to the new date without any action required from customers.

An **updated demo** was made available on Steam in early November ahead of the December launch, offering a fresh look at the game's current state.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/edge-of-memories-delayed-to-december-3) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/10/edge-of-memories-delayed-to-december/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/15/edge-of-memories-delayed-to-december-3/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21570-edge-of-memories-new-release-date-delay-demo-steam-update) · [Gamer Fuzion](https://www.gamerfuzion.com/edge-of-memories-delayed-december-3-2026-demo-returns-november/)

## Fate/EXTRA Record — January 28, 2027 Release Date Confirmed; TGS Hands-On Praised

**Aniplex** and **Type-Moon Studio BB** have set **January 28, 2027** as the launch date for **Fate/EXTRA Record** on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The game received its first extended public hands-on at **Tokyo Game Show 2026** in September, where the Aniplex booth featured the demo across two Arena floors. RPG Site's preview found the game "pretty good so far, for a game that was caught in development hell," singling out the redesigned deck-building card combat and reworked dungeon exploration. Push Square noted that the boss encounters demand genuine strategy based on the hand of cards drawn.

Additional coverage from later events highlighted that **Fate/EXTRA Record** will be the first entry in the *Fate* franchise to receive **official localization in German, Spanish, French, and Italian** alongside the standard English and Japanese.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** will shut down the Japanese server of **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, ending the game's run in its home region. The game will remain playable until that time. No official reason was given for the shutdown.

The global (English) version of the game had already ended on **March 28, 2025**. The Japanese service thus lasted roughly two and a half years from its 2024 launch.

## Persona 1 Remake Footage Reportedly Leaks Online

Roughly **two minutes of alleged Persona 1 remake footage** surfaced online around **October 31, 2026**, spreading quickly across YouTube, Reddit's r/GamingLeaksAndRumours, and file-sharing sites before most copies were taken down.

The low-resolution clip reportedly shows dungeon exploration, real-time-to-command combat in a modern style reminiscent of *Persona 3 Reload*, and voiced dialogue. **Atlus and Sega have made no statement** confirming or denying the footage's authenticity.

The timing follows **P-Studio producer Kazuhisa Wada's comments in August 2026** that Atlus was actively considering remakes of *Persona 1* and *Persona 2*. Some outlets speculate the leaked material may be from an internal pitch reel rather than a full production build. No platforms or release window have been mentioned.

## Queen's Domain Hits 100K Sales in Five Days

**Queen's Domain**, a PC and Switch 2 action RPG inspired by FromSoftware's early *King's Field* series, launched **October 30, 2026** and reached **100,000 copies sold in under five days**, according to developer **Freshly Baked Games** and publisher **Future Friends Games**. As of November 10, Steam concurrent players had surpassed 11,000 — roughly double its launch-day peak.

The game is a **retro-styled first-person dungeon crawler** with a heavy atmosphere drawing from PS1-era FromSoftware aesthetics. The Switch 2 version ranked first on the Nintendo eShop's download chart in Japan in its launch week.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21696-queens-domain-sales-numbers-new-100-k-copies-sold) · [dlcompare](https://www.dlcompare.com/gaming-news/queen-s-domain-crosses-10k-concurrent-players-best-price-impressions-89345) · [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/this-new-retro-fantasy-indie-rpg-channels-dark-souls-and-king-s-field/) · [Game*Spark (JP)](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/11/10/174681.html)
