# JRPG News — May 15, 2026

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Reveals Japanese Voice Actor Changes, Locks September 17 Release

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** has confirmed several Japanese voice actor replacements alongside its worldwide release date of **September 17, 2026** on PS5, Nintendo Switch 1 & 2, and PC (Steam).

The updated Japanese cast includes notable changes from prior series entries:

| Character | Previous VA | New VA |
|---|---|---|
| Kevin Graham | Ryohei Nakao | Kazuyuki Okitsu |
| Muller Vander | Hiroshi Isobe | Ryuichi Kijima |
| Julia Schwarz | Yuka Komatsu | Eriko Matsui |
| Leonhardt | Hikaru Midorikawa | Satoshi Hino |

The direct sequel to the 2025 remake of Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter, this title continues to generate attention as one of the major Trails releases ahead of the Crossbell arc ports announced earlier this month.

## Digimon Story: Cyber Sleuth Series Surpasses 3 Million Copies Worldwide

**Bandai Namco** has announced that **Digimon Story: Cyber Sleuth** and its sequel **Hacker's Memory** have together surpassed **3 million units** shipped and sold digitally worldwide — up from 1.5 million back in 2020, representing a doubling of the series' install base over six years.

Both titles remain available together as the **Digimon Story: Cyber Sleuth Complete Edition** on PC and Nintendo Switch. While no new entry in the Cyber Sleuth line has been announced, the milestone keeps fan hopes alive for a follow-up, particularly given the ongoing momentum of the Digimon franchise.

## Nitro Gen Omega 1.0 Launches on PC and Consoles

**DESTINYbit**'s mecha anime RPG **NITRO GEN OMEGA** has officially exited Steam Early Access with its full **version 1.0 launch** across PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch, priced at **$29.99**.

Set in a post-apocalyptic future where humanity lost the war against machines and now survives on floating sky colonies, the game blends turn-based tactical combat with open-world sandbox systems. Players recruit pilots, customize and tune mechs, and lead them in cinematic anime-style battles. The game had been in Early Access since June 2025.

## Tales of ARISE – Beyond the Dawn Edition Confirmed for Switch 2, Arrives May 22

**Bandai Namco** confirmed **Tales of ARISE – Beyond the Dawn Edition** is launching on **Nintendo Switch 2 on May 22, 2026**. The complete package includes the full 2021 action RPG alongside the **Beyond the Dawn** expansion, which adds 20+ hours of additional story content.

The Switch 2 port joins existing versions on PS4, PS5, Xbox, and PC. No Switch 2-specific enhancements beyond the core package were announced. For those who missed the original run, the expansion-bundled edition makes this a solid first entry point for the title.

## Persona 4 Revival Remains on Track; Atlus Continues 30th Anniversary Rollout

**Atlus** continues to drip-feed content through its **Persona 30th anniversary** initiative in 2026, commemorating 30 years since the original *Revelations: Persona* (1996). The dedicated anniversary site — featuring artwork by series artist **Shigenori Soejima** — has quietly referenced "the next chapter for the series," fueling ongoing fan speculation about a potential **Persona 6** reveal.

More concretely, **Persona 4 Revival** — an enhanced reimagining of the beloved 2008 title — is confirmed to be releasing **sometime in 2026** (post-April), though no specific date has been set. The anniversary campaign is expected to continue delivering announcements throughout the year.
