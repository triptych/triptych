# JRPG News — June 1, 2026

## Summer Game Fest 2026 Begins — JRPG Community on High Alert

**Summer Game Fest 2026** officially kicks off today and runs through **June 8**, with the main stage presentation scheduled for **June 5** at the Dolby Theater in Los Angeles. The JRPG community is buzzing with anticipation, with fans and industry watchers eyeing the event for potential reveals of **Persona 6**, **Final Fantasy VII Remake Part 3**, and **Dragon Quest XII: Beyond Dreams**. GamesRadar highlighted **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** and **Fable** among the top RPGs to watch across the week's showcases.

---

## Persona 6 Leaks: Dual Protagonists, Green Theme, September 2027 Target

A wave of **Persona 6** leaks broke in the final days of May, with a SEGA/Atlus insider corroborating the images as authentic. Key details from the leaks:

- **Two protagonists**: a blond male lead with a more melancholic tone and a black-and-red-haired female lead
- **Setting**: Yokohama
- **Color theme**: green (following Persona 3's blue, 4's yellow, and 5's red)
- **Release window**: September 2027 is the reported internal target
- A separate leaker (Extas1s) claims the game is already **feature complete** but was pushed internally to 2027

Rumors point to a potential reveal at the **Xbox Games Showcase on June 7** during this week's Summer Game Fest events. Atlus has not confirmed anything.

---

## Dragon Quest XII Re-Announced as *Beyond Dreams* at 40th Anniversary Stream

On **May 27**, **Square Enix** held a Dragon Quest 40th Anniversary livestream and officially re-announced the troubled twelfth mainline entry. The game has been fully restarted under a new team and has a new title: **Dragon Quest XII: Beyond Dreams**.

The original *Dragon Quest XII: The Flames of Fate* hit insurmountable development hurdles, leading to a complete reset after discussions with series creator **Yuji Horii** about what a mainline Dragon Quest should be. Notable details about the re-announced version:

- A new "eternally sleepy" protagonist, hinting at dream-themed storytelling
- A brighter, more hopeful tone — the subtitle asks "What lies beyond dreams? Surely not a world of darkness, but a bright and exciting future"
- The world shows hints of advanced technology including robots and hover or flying vehicles
- **Akira Toriyama's character designs** and **Koichi Sugiyama's music** will be retained, as with all prior entries

Square Enix did not announce platforms or a release window, acknowledging the restart means a longer road ahead.

## Kyoto Xanadu Confirmed for July 16 in Japan — Rurui Character Trailer Out

**Nihon Falcom** has confirmed **Kyoto Xanadu -the Blooming Phantom-** launches **July 16** in Japan and Asia for **PlayStation 5**, **Nintendo Switch 2**, **Nintendo Switch**, and **PC (Steam)**. Pricing is 7,920 yen (7,870 yen for Switch 2 digital-only).

A new **character trailer for Rurui** was released this week. The game alternates between real-world Kyoto exploration — attending classes at Hirasaka Academy, building character abilities, interacting with NPCs — and dungeon crawling through the Labyrinth, which uses both **2D and 3D action gameplay**. The story follows Rei, a thief fleeing the criminal underworld when a mysterious otherworld suddenly manifests in the middle of Kyoto.

A **North American and European release** is planned, with Falcom coordinating a date with an unnamed publishing partner.

## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Launches July 9, Pre-Orders Live

**Cygames** has confirmed **Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok** releases **July 9, 2026** for **PC**, **PlayStation 4**, **PlayStation 5**, and **Nintendo Switch 2**. Pre-orders are now live.

Endless Ragnarok is a large-scale expansion that adds new story content continuing from the original Relink's ending, along with:

- **Six new playable characters** including **Fraux** and **Fediel**, bringing the total roster to **29 characters** when combined with returning Relink cast
- **Summons**: players can now call upon summons in battle
- **The Conflux**: a new **solo mode** featuring a gauntlet of randomly shuffled areas where players fight monsters, choose branching paths, and select power-ups that persist until exiting
- New co-op quest tiers and additional bosses
- **Multi-platform crossplay** for online multiplayer

## Final Fantasy VII Remake Part 3 Reveal "Steadily Being Prepared," Says Square Enix

In an update shared in **April**, **Naoki Hamaguchi** (director of the FF7 Remake trilogy) confirmed Square Enix is actively working toward an official announcement for **Final Fantasy VII Remake Part 3**:

> "We know many fans are eagerly waiting, and preparations toward the announcement are steadily underway. Please look forward to it just a little longer."

The game is expected to be **multi-platform from day one**, breaking from the timed PS5 exclusivity of *Remake* and *Rebirth*. Square Enix is reportedly targeting a **January/February 2027** release to coincide with the original Final Fantasy VII's **30th anniversary**. Confirmed returning elements include the Highwind airship, Rocket Town, and both **Vincent Valentine** and **Cid Highwind** as playable party members.

---

## Final Fantasy VI Arrives on Xbox Game Pass Tomorrow

The classic 1994 SNES RPG **Final Fantasy VI** makes its **Xbox Game Pass** debut **tomorrow, June 2**. The pixel remaster version of the game — featuring redrawn HD-2D sprites and rearranged music — is available on PC via Steam and will now be accessible to Game Pass subscribers on day two of the Summer Game Fest season.
