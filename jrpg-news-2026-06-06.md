# JRPG News — June 6, 2026

## Top Story: Final Fantasy VII Revelation Announced at Summer Game Fest

Square Enix closed out Summer Game Fest 2026 with the long-awaited reveal of **Final Fantasy VII Revelation**, the third and final chapter in the FF7 Remake trilogy. Key details:

- **Release:** Spring 2027, simultaneous across PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC, and Nintendo Switch 2
- **Open world:** The entire planet is freely explorable via the iconic Highwind airship; players can parachute in anywhere for seamless air-to-land transitions
- **Combat:** Builds on Rebirth's system — real-time action with a Tactical mode that now lets players queue commands for the entire party at once
- **Party:** Cid Highwind and Vincent Valentine officially join as full party members
- **Content:** Wutai region confirmed, snowboarding minigame returns, Queen's Blood card game expanded
- **Story focus:** Hunting down the planet's WEAPON monsters appears to be a major arc

## Dragon Quest XII Rebranded as "Beyond Dreams"

Announced on May 27 during Dragon Quest's 40th anniversary celebration, **Dragon Quest XII: The Flames of Fate** has been renamed **Dragon Quest XII: Beyond Dreams** after a full development restart from scratch. Creator Yuji Horii confirmed the game has gone in a "different direction" from the original vision, though it will still feature Akira Toriyama's character designs and Koichi Sugiyama's music. No release window or platform details were given.

Sources: [Square Enix Press](https://press.na.square-enix.com/DRAGON-QUEST-XII-BEYOND-DREAMS-REVEALED-AS-DRAGON-QUEST-CELEBRATES-ITS) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/05/27/dragon-quest-xiis-development-was-restarted-from-scratch-and-it-has-a-new-name-and) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/05/25/dragon-quest-yuji-horii-news-on-may-27th/)

## Summer Game Fest 2026 Wrap-up

The June 1–8 showcase season wrapped its main show on June 5. Beyond FF7 Revelation, the event featured **Resident Evil: Veronica** (a remake of Code Veronica) and spotlighted over 30 independent games. **Persona 6** was not at SGF — as insiders had predicted, it remains slotted for the **Xbox Games Showcase on June 7**, which has not yet aired as of this writing.
