## Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Launches Today on Nintendo Switch

**Evil Empire** and **Motion Twin**'s **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse** is out today, **October 15, 2026**, on **Nintendo Switch** (also PC). Set **23 years after Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse**, the game stars **Rose Belmont** in a classically structured 2D action-adventure. The Switch version is playable on Switch 2 with a free performance upgrade to **60 FPS**, up from the Switch's 30 FPS target, though Konami has not released a dedicated Switch 2 native version.

Notably, the game is **not releasing on Switch 2 as a standalone SKU** — a decision that puzzled commentators including a former Nintendo marketing lead, who described Konami's handling of the launch as "a little weird."

Reviews for the Switch version are now live. Nintendo Life published a full roundup as of this morning.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Reviews In — Metacritic 77 Ahead of Tomorrow's Launch

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** arrives on **October 16** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S**. Review embargoes lifted today and the game sits at a **Metacritic score of 77** based on eleven early critic reviews.

The remaster marks the first re-release of a 2D *Tales of* game in more than a decade. New features include:

- A new **English dub** covering most main story scenes and over 250 skits
- **Encounter rate toggle**, **New Game+**, and an option to **run by default**
- Quality-of-life options for graphics and sound

Critic reception is split. **Noisy Pixel** gave it **8.5/10**, praising the Craymel Cage system and combat. **Hardcore Gamer** called it an easy recommendation for JRPG fans. **Siliconera** highlighted the remaster's thoughtful QoL additions. **Shacknews** dissented at a **5/10**, objecting to certain visual changes, though the publication approved of the restored skits. Critics broadly flagged uneven minigames and dated dungeon-town design as weak points.

Sources: [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/tales-of-eternia-remastered/) · [Noisy Pixel Review](https://noisypixel.net/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review/) · [Siliconera Review](https://www.siliconera.com/review-tales-of-eternia-remastered-is-a-much-needed-remaster/) · [Shacknews Review](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151003/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review-score) · [Hardcore Gamer Review](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-tales-of-eternia-remastered/) · [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21563-tales-eternia-remastered-review)

## Kingdom Hearts HD Collections Launch Natively on Switch 2

**Square Enix** released **Kingdom Hearts HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX**, **Kingdom Hearts HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue**, and **Kingdom Hearts III + Re Mind** natively on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **October 8, 2026**. A bundled edition (**Kingdom Hearts Collection [I-III]**) containing all three packages is also available at **$74.99 USD**.

This is a significant upgrade from the original Nintendo Switch releases, which were cloud streaming versions due to the hardware's limitations. The Switch 2 versions run natively, and **Square Enix confirmed that save data from the cloud editions can be transferred** to the new versions. Owners of the cloud versions are eligible for a **50% discount** on the Switch 2 editions.

## Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen Switch 2 Reviews — Strong Port With Caveats

**Capcom**'s **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** arrived on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **October 9, 2026** at **$49.99**. The bundle includes the full base game, the **Norgan expansion** (which reviewers say feels seamlessly integrated), and both a hard mode and a new casual mode. **New Game+** remains present but without added challenge over the original.

Review scores have been high: outlets including Pocket Tactics gave it 90+ scores, calling it "simply excellent." **RPG Site** called the port impressive, saying it feels like "Capcom coming full circle." **Nintendo Everything** noted a frame rate choice between variable and a 30 FPS lock. Caveats include **longer load times**, occasional frame drops while docked, and **no cross-platform save transfer** — players start fresh on Switch 2.

Sources: [Nintendo Life Review](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen) · [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21520-dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-switch-2-review-impressions-frame-rate-resolution-visuals-frame-generation) · [Nintendo Everything Review](https://nintendoeverything.com/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-review-for-nintendo-switch-2/) · [Metacritic Switch 2](https://www.metacritic.com/game/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen/critic-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch-2)

## Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga Announced for Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**Taito** and **Clear River Games** announced **Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga** at the **PC Gaming Show Tokyo Direct** on approximately **September 19–20, 2026**. The collection bundles the 1993 SNES original **Lufia & the Fortress of Doom** and its 1995 prequel **Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals**, targeting a **2027 release** on **PC via Steam, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation 5**.

This marks the first time either game will receive an **official PC release**. Taito says the collection will preserve the original pixel art while adding quality-of-life features for current hardware; specifics have not yet been shared. Physical editions will be announced separately.

## Decapolice Delayed to 2027 by Level-5

**Level-5** CEO **Akihiro Hino** announced during the company's **Vision 2026 broadcast** on **September 10, 2026** that the detective RPG **Decapolice** has been delayed from its planned 2026 window into **2027**. Hino said the team needs more time, without providing a specific reason beyond quality. The game is targeting **PlayStation 5, PC, and Nintendo Switch 2**.

*Decapolice* has now been delayed three times since its 2023 announcement, previously pushed from 2023 to 2024, then to 2026. Despite the delay, **a playable demo was shown at Tokyo Game Show 2026** in September.

Sources: [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/09/promising-detective-rpg-decapolice-officially-delayed-into-2027) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/?p=1078071) · [PSU](https://www.psu.com/news/decapolice-has-been-delayed-to-2027-confirms-level-5/)
