## Anime Expo 2026 Opens: SEGA/Atlus Booth Is Live

**Anime Expo 2026** opened today at the Los Angeles Convention Center, running **July 2–5**, and the **SEGA/ATLUS booth** is among the most packed game presences on the floor. Key activities underway or confirmed for the event:

- **Metaphor: ReFantazio Switch 2 playable demo** — the first hands-on demo for the Switch 2 version of the game, playable at the booth throughout the event. Attendees receive a free exclusive postcard while supplies last.
- **Persona 4 Revival Immersive Experience** — a real-world themed area within the SEGA booth recreating the world of P4R, with a photo op and a surprise fan appreciation giveaway item.
- **String quartet live performance** — on **July 4 and 5**, a string quartet will perform live music from *Metaphor: ReFantazio* on the SEGA Stage, serving as a preview of the upcoming full orchestra world tour (details below).
- **Junes-inspired café** — ATLUS has partnered with **Thirsty Waters Premium Tea House** to offer a Junes café experience featuring Persona protagonist-inspired boba tea drinks, artisanal cookies, ice cream, and exclusive merch.
- **Soejima live drawing session** — character designer **Shigenori Soejima** will hold a live drawing session on the SEGA Stage.

## Persona 30th Anniversary x OMOCAT Collection Pre-Orders Live

**SEGA, ATLUS, and LA-based indie art studio OMOCAT** have launched a **Persona 30th Anniversary x OMOCAT** merchandise collection. Pre-orders went live on **July 1 at 5:00 PM PT** via the OMOCAT online shop, with limited quantities also available in person at Anime Expo 2026 (July 2–5).

**Collection items:**
- Zip hoodies, T-shirts, outerwear, accessories, skate decks, and plushies
- OMOCAT's signature color-forward visual style applied to each mainline Persona game's protagonist(s) and tone
- Plushies were specifically called out by OMOCAT via social media as a pre-order highlight

The collaboration marks one of the more notable third-party licensed partnerships in the Persona franchise's anniversary year.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio Orchestra World Tour Launches This Fall

Alongside the Anime Expo string quartet preview, **SEGA and ATLUS** have fully detailed the **Metaphor: ReFantazio Orchestra World Tour**, a live concert experience launching **October 2026**.

**Tour scope:**
- Spans **3 continents**, **6 countries**, and **8 cities** across the US, Asia, and Europe
- Features a **full live orchestra and choir**, original vocalists from the game, and synced gameplay footage
- Music by **Shoji Meguro** and the Atlus Sound Team

The Anime Expo string quartet performance on July 4–5 is explicitly described as a precursor to the full tour. Specific city dates and venues are listed on the official concert site.

Sources: [SEGA](https://www.sega.com/news/metaphor-refantazio-orchestra-world-tour-announcement) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/03/03/metaphor-refantazio-orchestra-concert-world-tour-kicks-off-this-fall) · [OtakuKart](https://otakukart.com/metaphor-refantazio-nintendo-switch-2-demo-confirmed-for-anime-expo-2026/) · [Crunchyroll News](https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/latest/2026/3/3/metaphor-refantazio-orchestra-concert-world-tour)

## Persona 4 Revival AX Panel: Yoshiyuki Asai Among Panelists, Anime Signals Likely

The **Persona 4 Revival** panel at Anime Expo is confirmed for **July 4, 2026**, at **Petree Hall**, running **11:30 AM – 12:20 PM PT**, and will be livestreamed.

**Confirmed panelists:**
- **Kazuhisa Wada** — General Producer
- **Shigenori Soejima** — Character Designer
- **Atsushi Nomura** — Business Producer
- **Yoshiyuki Asai** — Director/Animator (see below)

**Yoshiyuki Asai** is the notable addition. An animation director whose credits include *Fate/Apocrypha*, *Charlotte*, *The Day I Became a God*, and *Buddy Daddies* (winner of Crunchyroll Anime Awards 2024 Best Original Anime), Asai has no previously announced connection to the *Persona 4 Revival* game itself. His presence on the panel — alongside the game's producer and art director — strongly suggests an anime companion project or significantly upgraded animated content tied to the game's February 18, 2027 launch.

The panel promises "never-before-seen content and information" to be revealed live.

Sources: [ATLUS West (X)](https://x.com/Atlus_West/status/2067283678883930446) · [Persona Central](https://personacentral.com/anime-expo-2026/) · [OtakuKart](https://otakukart.com/atlus-brings-persona-4-revival-to-anime-expo-2026-panel-details-and-guest-lineup-revealed/) · [Anitrendz](https://anitrendz.net/news/2026/06/09/takash-iizuka-kazuhisa-wada-shigenori-soejima-atsushi-nomura-and-yoshiyuki-asai-as-guest-of-honor-in-anime-expo-2026/)

## Echoes of Aincrad: "Unanswered//butterfly" First Look at AX Today

**Bandai Namco** is hosting an *Echoes of Aincrad* — **"Unanswered//butterfly" First Look** panel at Anime Expo today, **July 2, at 12:30 PM PT**.

"Unanswered//butterfly" is a companion anime project bundled with the **Ultimate Edition** of *Echoes of Aincrad*, which launches **July 10** on PS5, Xbox Series, and PC. The Ultimate Edition also includes the digital soundtrack, digital artbook, and an in-game armor pack.

The companion anime reunites characters from the SAO universe inside Aincrad, with the panel representing the first extended public look at the project ahead of the game's release next week.

## Mistfall Hunter Confirmed for July 29, New Class and Combat Rework Detailed

**Bellring Games** and publisher **Skystone Games** have confirmed the **July 29, 2026** release date for **Mistfall Hunter**, a dark fantasy action extraction RPG, launching on **PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S** with crossplay and **Day One Xbox Game Pass** availability.

**New details from the open beta and roadmap announcement:**
- **Withered Knight** — a new class launching with the full game, featuring a new weapon archetype called **Black Arrow**
- **Hallowgrave** — a new map debuted during the open beta playtest
- **Sálmar** — the first look at a new **Mist Lord** boss
- **Combat overhaul** — the developers are moving from an energy-based combat system to a **stamina-based system**, described as more fluid while preserving match tension
- Three-player co-op with a PvPvE structure where other players can be encountered as threats or allies mid-extraction

## The Life and Suffering of Prince Jerian Launches July 20

**Schisma Games** and publisher **101XP** have confirmed that **The Life and Suffering of Prince Jerian** will release on **PC via Steam on July 20, 2026**. A new demo covering the first two chapters is available on Steam now.

The game is a narrative RPG and follow-up to Schisma's 2021 cult hit *The Life and Suffering of Sir Brante*, set in the same universe but telling a completely independent story. Players take the role of a crown prince of the Blessed Arknian Empire — a society where one's fate and status are literally determined at birth under divine law — beginning in childhood and charting a path to the imperial throne.

Sources: [RPGamer (release date)](https://rpgamer.com/2026/06/the-life-and-suffering-of-prince-jerian-releasing-in-july/) · [RPGamer (Kickstarter)](https://rpgamer.com/2026/02/the-life-and-suffering-of-prince-jerian-kickstarter-set-for-march/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2936290/The_Life_and_Suffering_of_Prince_Jerian/)
