## Kingdom Hearts IV — D23 2026 "Coco" Trailer Confirms Late 2027 Release, Anime Series

**Square Enix** and **Disney & Pixar Games** used the **D23 2026** ultimate fan event on **August 15** to give **Kingdom Hearts IV** its firmest release window yet: **late 2027**, on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC**. The extended trailer began circulating publicly on August 18 alongside the full panel recording.

The showcase's centerpiece is a formal world reveal for **Coco** — the first Pixar world confirmed for KH4. New gameplay features shown include action-packed sequences where the player takes direct control of **Donald and Goofy**, plus a dedicated exploration segment starring **King Mickey** as he searches for clues about the whereabouts of the missing Sora. Series director **Tetsuya Nomura** and the Square Enix creative team framed it as the deepest look yet at the game's structure.

Alongside the game, Square Enix and Disney announced an original **Kingdom Hearts** anime series (working title) in development for **Disney Channel and Disney+**, produced with Nomura's direct involvement.

## Steins;Gate RE:BOOT Launches Today on Steam, Switch 2 Version Dated October 29

**Spike Chunsoft**'s from-scratch remake **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** launched **today, August 20, 2026** on **PC via Steam** (as well as Xbox Series X|S). The Japanese physical launch also arrives on August 20. **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation 5** versions follow **October 29** worldwide.

RE:BOOT is a full ground-up remake of the 2009 visual novel: all character art, backgrounds, and event stills have been redrawn; all in-game voices have been re-recorded; and composer **Takeshi Abo** has re-scored the entire soundtrack. The remake also adds a **brand-new worldline** and an unseen ending scenario alongside the original story branches. RPGFan's early-access review calls the visual overhaul faithful but sharper, with the new route significant enough to justify a replay for series veterans.

## Starsand Island Leaves Early Access, Launches on Switch 2 and Steam

Seed Sparkle Lab's anime-styled life and farming simulation **Starsand Island** left Early Access on **August 18** with its full **1.0 launch** on **Nintendo Switch 2** and **PC (Steam)**. A physical Switch 2 edition ($39.99) is scheduled for **November 12**.

Inspired by traditional Chinese countryside aesthetics and Eastern philosophy, the game folds together **farming, crafting, ranching, fishing, and production** on a hand-drawn island, and supports **online co-op for up to four players**. The 1.0 release adds ancient-ruin dungeons with monster combat that were only partially implemented during Early Access, plus new island regions and expanded festival events. Nintendo Life and CGMagazine's launch coverage highlights the smooth handheld performance on Switch 2 as a particular strength.

## Entropy — Dread Delusion Studio's JRPG-Inspired CRPG Hits Steam Early Access

**Lovely Hellplace** — the studio behind cult first-person RPG *Dread Delusion* — launched its follow-up **Entropy** in **Steam Early Access on August 18**, published by DreadXP.

Entropy is a **tactical turn-based RPG** built around classic JRPG structure but preserving Lovely Hellplace's signature **late-1990s low-poly 3D aesthetic**. Set in a dying age where humanity survives in small tyrant-ruled enclaves between barren wastes, the player controls a member of a Draenog theatre troupe whose performance is interrupted by a demon attack. The Act 1 build launching today represents roughly **25% of the final planned content** — two towns and three dungeons — with Early Access expected to run **about a year** as Acts 2 and 3 are added. RPGFan's Early Access review flags the writing and world-building as immediate highlights but says the combat systems still need balance work.

## Fractured Worlds — Dark Fantasy ARPG Adds Switch Release for August 26

**Laser Guided Games** announced this week that its dark-fantasy action RPG **Fractured Worlds** will launch on **Nintendo Switch** alongside its existing PC (Steam/Epic) release on **August 26, 2026**. The game had previously been in Epic Games Store Early Access since October 2025.

Fractured Worlds is helmed by industry veteran **Matt Oelfke** (Gears of War, Unreal Tournament). The premise: a reality shattered by the ambition of its inhabitants, with players guided by a mysterious voice to explore fragmented realms — medieval dungeons, enchanted forests, volcanic wastelands, steampunk cities, a cosmic core — and confront those responsible for the cataclysm. The combat system is deliberately Souls-adjacent, with distinct weapon rhythms across two-handers, spears, and Aether-infused wands. A signature **Map Shard system** lets players share fragments of their own worlds with others, spawning co-op instances with randomized modifiers, plus optional PvP-style **invasion encounters**.

## Kernel Hearts — Magical-Girl Roguelike Locks September 17 Date on Switch 2 and PC

**Ephemera Games** and publisher **Whitethorn Games** locked a **September 17, 2026** release date for their magical-girl action roguelike **Kernel Hearts**, confirmed on August 17. Platforms are **PC (Steam & Epic)** and **Nintendo Switch 2**.

Up to **four players** cooperate as the android magical girls of the **M.A.H.O.U Unit** climbing the **Tower of Babel** to dethrone god. Runs mix and match attack, spell, and movement chips per unit, with **up to 256 KB of chips** to unlock across the game — a build-crafting metaphor with genuine breadth. Between runs, players return to a laboratory hub where each celestial domain unlocks unique allies to **befriend, deepen relationships with, and potentially romance**, borrowing more from *Persona* than typical roguelikes. A free demo is currently available on Steam and Epic.

## Brigandine: Abyss Confirms Switch 2 Launch Window

**Matrix Software** and **Happinet** finalized the launch window for the new SRPG **Brigandine: Abyss** this week: **Steam on August 26** and **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 27, 2026**. The Switch 2 version ships with a **Deluxe Edition** including a mini art book and digital soundtrack, and a **physical release on game-key card**.

The game is a fresh entry in the classic Happinet strategy franchise, set in a brand-new world designed to be approachable to franchise newcomers. Hundreds of years ago the Abyssloa Empire was stopped by the arcane power of the Brigandine; now a new Abyssloa Empire is rising, and the player picks one of **six story campaigns** to resist it. A **24-faction Mission Mode** offers post-story replayability.

## Suikoden: Star Leap Launches in Japan for iOS, Android, and Steam

**Konami**'s mobile-and-PC entry in its dormant JRPG franchise, **Suikoden: Star Leap**, launched in Japan on **August 7, 2026** for **iOS, Android, and PC via Steam**. Star Leap slots into the *Suikoden* timeline **before the first game** and **after Suikoden V**, and — unusually for a Konami mobile entry — features a **fully voiced main story**.

The game is a free-to-play recruit-and-command RPG that leans on the series' hallmark **108 Stars of Destiny** structure. A Western release has not been confirmed, but the Steam version supports Japanese and English text at launch.
