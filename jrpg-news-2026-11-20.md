## Metaphor: ReFantazio Launches on Switch 2 with Price Cut Across All Platforms

**Atlus** released **Metaphor: ReFantazio** on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12**, bringing the acclaimed 2024 fantasy RPG to the console with three graphics modes: **Performance**, **Balanced**, and **Graphics**. The Switch 2 version requires approximately **62 GB** of free storage.

Alongside the launch, Atlus permanently reduced the base game price to **$39.99** on all previously released platforms. A limited **SteelBook Edition** was offered at launch, and a **35th Digital Anniversary Edition** was available in Japan.

## Prinny Party: Going Overboard! Out Now on Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**NIS America** launched **Prinny Party: Going Overboard!** on **November 11** in the West (November 12 in Japan and Asia) for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5), priced at **$29.99**.

The game is a party RPG featuring characters from the Disgaea universe: up to four players travel a board, battle demons, and buy equipment to defeat the Supreme Overboard. It is rated **E10+** and supports both English and Japanese voice acting. The Switch 2 version supports **GameShare**, allowing friends to join without owning a copy (the Switch 2 download is 1.3 GB vs. 572 MB on Switch 1).

A free demo was available before launch. Switch 1 owners can upgrade to the Switch 2 edition via a free upgrade pack on the eShop.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21742-prinny-party-going-overboard-now-available-switch-2-playstation-5-pc-steam-online) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/prinny-party-going-overboard-launches-november-11-in-the-west) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-09-09/prinny-party-going-overboard-game-launches-on-november-11/.241589) · [GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/news/prinny-party-going-overboard-is-out-now-and-your-friendships-are-not-safe/)

## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Moves to February 4, 2027

**Square Enix** announced on **November 12** that **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** — the full remake of the 1995 Super Famicom title developed by the same team behind *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven* — will now launch **February 4, 2027**, nearly two weeks earlier than the previously announced February 16 date. The change applies to all platforms: PS5, Switch 2, Switch, and PC via Steam.

Pre-orders are live on Steam. Players who have *Romancing SaGa 2* save data on the same system will receive the **Leon's Crown** bonus item at launch.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21744-romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-new-release-date-early-february-4-square-enix) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/11/12/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-will-now-release-on-february-4-2027/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/?p=929537) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-release-date-bumped-up/)

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** will shut down the Japanese server for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25 at 16:00 JST**, closing the last remaining regional version of the gacha RPG (the global version had already ended in March 2025). The developer cited difficulty "providing a service that would satisfy users" as the reason.

Premium purchases and gacha banners were disabled as of **August 27, 2026**. Refund applications for unspent currency will be accepted from **November 26, 2026** through **February 24, 2027**.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25) · [Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/08/27/atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-and-the-polar-night-liberator-ends-service-on-november-25-in-japan/) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/atelier-resleriana-to-end-service-in-japan/)

## Fate/EXTRA Record Confirmed for January 28, 2027

**Aniplex** and developers **Meteorise** and **TYPE-MOON** have confirmed **Fate/EXTRA Record** — the full remake of the 2010 PSP RPG — will launch worldwide on **January 28, 2027** for **PS5, PS4, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

Pricing for North America is **$59.99** (standard) with a **$89.99** limited physical edition that includes a soundtrack and art book. The limited edition is **$99.99** on Switch 2. In Japan, the base price is **¥8,100** before tax across all platforms. European and Japanese Switch 2 physical copies will be game-key cards; **XSEED Games** is handling a full cartridge release for North America.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://gematsu.com/2026/09/fate-extra-record-launches-january-28-2027-for-ps5-switch-2-ps4-switch-and-pc) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21288-fate-extra-record-release-date-switch-2-physical-edition-ps5-physical) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/fate-extra-record-shares-nintendo-switch-2-release-date-new-trailer/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/fate-extra-record-releasing-worldwide-in-january-western-publishers-announced/)

## Tenshi no Uta Memorial Collection Coming to Switch Physically in Japan — March 11, 2027

**Edia** announced a physical Nintendo Switch release of **Tenshi no Uta Memorial Collection** in Japan for **March 11, 2027**. The bundle collects all three classic entries in the series originally released for PC Engine and Super Famicom, priced at **¥6,380** (standard) or **¥11,880** (special edition). The trilogy is currently available digitally on Switch; this marks the first physical package for the platform.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21771-tenshi-no-uta-memorial-collection-releases-for-nintendo-switch-on-march-11)
