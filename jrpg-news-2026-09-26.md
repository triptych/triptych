## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — 2026's Best-Reviewed Game at 91/92 on Metacritic/OpenCritic

[**Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4225980/Trails_in_the_Sky_2nd_Chapter/) — Nihon Falcom's modern rebuild of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC* — launched **September 17, 2026** on **PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2** simultaneously worldwide.

Reviews positioned it as the **highest-rated game of 2026**, with a **91 on Metacritic** (Universal Acclaim) and a **92 on OpenCritic** (97% of 36 critics recommending it). DualShockers and RPG Site awarded perfect 10/10 scores; Brazilian outlet PSX Brasil gave a flat 100/100; Game Informer called it 9.5/10 and "the Trails series' defining entry, and one of the best RPGs of the year"; IGN and Push Square each gave it 9/10, with Push Square describing it as "one of the best RPG sequels ever made."

The remake's recurring critique — where critics could find agreement — was minor: inconsistent voice acting, sparse environments, and a plain interface. Everything else drew high praise.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Reviews — 89 Metacritic, 13 Perfect Scores

**Intelligent Systems** and **Nintendo**'s **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched on **September 17** for **Nintendo Switch 2** and has drawn strong critical reception, landing an **89 on Metacritic** with **13 outlets awarding it a perfect score**, including IGN.

The four-protagonist structure — where players tackle the Heroic Games storylines in any order with difficulty scaling dynamically to the player's progression — earned consistent praise for its narrative depth and tactical depth. Critics also highlighted the orchestral score as exceptional. The main criticisms were pacing concerns in the second half and the sheer volume of content feeling overwhelming for newcomers to the series.

## Dragon Quest XI S Switch 2 Edition Launches at $39.99 — No Save Transfer

**Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition** arrived on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 24, 2026** at **$39.99**, shipped on a game-key card for physical copies.

The Switch 2 edition adds two display modes: **Graphics Priority Mode (1440p/30 fps)** and **Performance Priority Mode (1080p/60 fps)**. The Definitive Edition content (new story chapters spotlighting party members, orchestral score, 2D/3D switching, Japanese voice acting) carries over in full.

Square Enix confirmed that **save data from the original Switch version does not transfer** and there is **no paid upgrade path**, meaning existing owners must repurchase at full price.

## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow — Mixed Reviews at Launch

**Qliphah in Providence's Shadow**, the counter-timeline tactical mystery RPG from **Marvelous**, launched September 24 for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and PC (Epic Games Store)**.

The game follows delivery boy **Soma Hoshigami** and investigator **Rena Hizuki** through a distorted supernatural Tokyo. Its defining mechanic is a timing-based counter combat system — when an enemy attacks, players press the matching color button as an expanding circle overlaps a stationary one to counter. Reception was mixed: some reviewers found the system rewarding once mastered while others found it "poor execution" on "an interesting idea," with the mechanic becoming repetitive after the first few hours. Metacritic scores across outlets varied significantly.

Sources: [Noisy Pixel review](https://noisypixel.net/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [Final Weapon review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/24/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review-steam/) · [DualShockers review](https://www.dualshockers.com/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [RPGamer review](https://rpgamer.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/)

## Armed Fantasia Cancelled — 17,920 Kickstarter Backers Left Waiting

**Digital Bros** (505 Games' parent company) confirmed in its **FY2026 financial results** on **September 24** the cancellation of **Armed Fantasia: To the End of the Wilderness**, the spiritual successor to *Wild Arms* led by series creator **Akifumi Kaneko** and producer **Masato Kato** (*Chrono Trigger*, *Xenogears*).

The decision came despite the team having recently completed a vertical-slice ROM covering the main scenario and core gameplay systems. Digital Bros cited gameplay revisions, market conditions, and completion costs. The cancellation simultaneously affects **Directorate Novitiate**, a second unrelated title also under Digital Bros' umbrella.

Armed Fantasia had raised **¥379,328,385** across its 2022 Kickstarter campaign, leaving roughly **17,920 backers** without a clear path to refunds or communication from the publisher. The game was announced alongside the *Chrono Cross* composer's involvement and was widely anticipated as a revival of the old-school PlayStation JRPG era.

## TGS 2026 Roundup: Final Fantasy Resonance Trailer, Bloomwalker Enters Ni no Kuni Universe, Atelier Karia Update

**Tokyo Game Show 2026** (held September 17–20) brought several notable JRPG showcases.

**Final Fantasy Resonance** received its [TGS 2026 trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c_MKVqX7rU4) — a nearly four-minute video featuring new story scenes, gameplay footage, and the song **"INSIDE"** by Japanese artist **WurtS**. The HD-2D turn-based RPG, an adaptation of *Brave Exvius* featuring Visions of characters from across the Final Fantasy series, is due **October 22** on Switch 2, Switch, PC, and other platforms.

**Bloomwalker** — formerly known as "Project Bloomwalker" from **Netmarble Neo** — was officially confirmed as a title set in the **Ni no Kuni** universe. The cozy crafting game (not a traditional RPG) casts players as a world-purifier traveling across Luminoa with a moving house, working alongside Higgledies. **Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories** (Gust/Koei Tecmo) received a new character trailer at TGS featuring the return of **Rutger and Lenja** from *Atelier Yumia*.

**Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** was also highlighted at Konami's TGS 2026 showcase.
