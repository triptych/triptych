## Final Fantasy XVI Lands on PlayStation Plus Extra Today

**Final Fantasy XVI** is available now for PS Plus Extra and Premium subscribers on PS5 as of today, June 16. It headlines the June 2026 Game Catalog drop alongside Sonic X Shadow Generations, Kingdom Come: Deliverance, and Life is Strange: Double Exposure.

For JRPG subscribers, the notable additions are:

- **Final Fantasy XVI** — Clive Rosfield's action-RPG entry is now the anchor of the PS Plus library for Square Enix fans. It joins at no extra cost for Extra and Premium tiers.
- **Gitaroo Man** (PS Plus Premium only) — The 2002 PS2 rhythm-RPG cult classic arrives as a PS2 Classic, making it one of the rarest games to hit a subscription service in recent memory.

**One catch:** Sony is testing a **staggered regional delivery** this month. Subscribers in most of Europe, Australia, Canada, and other regions outside the US, UK, and Japan receive the full eight-game catalog today. US, UK, and Japan subscribers receive only five of the eight titles today; the remaining three (Farming Simulator 25, Blades of Fire, Black Desert) arrive in those regions on June 30.

## Steam Next Fest Day 2 — JRPG Demo Rankings and Spotlight: Lezarishia Stories

After 24 hours of Steam Next Fest, early download rankings are forming. Among JRPG-adjacent titles, **Echoes of Aincrad** is charting as the top-performing RPG demo and among the most-downloaded across the entire fest (alongside action titles Mistfall Hunter and EMPULSE).

The fest's randomized front-page rotation ends after the first 48 hours — from Tuesday onward, wishlist count will drive discovery, meaning the current window is the best chance for smaller JRPG demos to get seen.

**Spotlight: Lezarishia Stories**

One under-the-radar JRPG demo drawing attention is **Lezarishia Stories**, a classic command-based JRPG with a distinctive 2.5D visual style that blends pixel art with 3D environments. The story follows a quest to recover a stolen holy sword, and the combat system emphasizes studying enemy traits and choosing actions deliberately — a deliberate throwback to 1990s SNES-era pacing and design. The demo is available free for the duration of Next Fest via Steam.

## Deltarune Chapter 5 Subtitle Revealed: "The Field of Pink and Gold" — Chapter 6 Already in Production

While the June 24 release date for Deltarune Chapter 5 was announced during the June 9 Nintendo Direct, additional details have since emerged about what's coming next.

Chapter 5 is officially subtitled **"The Field of Pink and Gold"** and drops as a free update at **11:00 AM EDT on June 24** across Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PS4, PS5, PC, and Mac.

More notably: at the end of the Chapter 5 trailer, a single on-screen line reads **"Chapter 6 is waiting."** Toby Fox has since confirmed that Chapter 6 is **already in active production** and described it as "easier to make than the others." The team is also reportedly eyeing a possible **Chapter 7 production start before the end of 2026**.

Deltarune is planned as a seven-chapter series in total. Chapters 3 and 4 launched as a paid release on June 5, 2025; Chapter 5 onward follows Fox's April 2025 commitment to release remaining chapters at no additional cost.

## One Piece: Grand Gourmet — October 23, 2026 (Switch, Switch 2, PC, Mobile)

Bandai Namco has confirmed **One Piece: Grand Gourmet**, a new management sim developed by **Kairosoft** (Game Dev Story, Pocket Academy), launching **October 23, 2026** on Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PC via Steam, and mobile platforms.

Players helm the Baratie Number Two — the Straw Hat crew's floating restaurant — alongside Sanji. The core loop involves gathering ingredients, designing menus, decorating the restaurant interior, and managing staff across more than **400 characters from the One Piece roster**, rendered in a game-engine-inspired sprite art style.

The game is squarely a management simulation rather than a JRPG, but Kairosoft's JRPG-adjacent design language (stat progression, story events, level-gating) makes it a natural draw for the fanbase. Pre-orders are live on Steam and Nintendo eShop.
