# JRPG News — November 11, 2026

## Releases & Updates

### [Metaphor: ReFantazio — Nintendo Switch 2 Version](https://www.gamesradar.com/upcoming-rpg-games/)
Atlus's acclaimed turn-based RPG launches on Nintendo Switch 2 **tomorrow, November 12**. Early reviews note the port performs better than expected, though it still needs some optimization polish.

### [Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Switch 2 Patch](https://www.rpgsite.net/)
Nihon Falcom's Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter received a Switch 2 update adding a **30fps fixed mode** to graphics settings, giving players a smoother option for the handheld experience.

### [AURORA REQUIEM: The Lullaby of Calvaris](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/top-turn-based-rpgs-and-strategy-games-coming-in-november-2026/)
A new turn-based JRPG from ISEKAI Games, **AURORA REQUIEM** launches **November 16** on PC and Switch. It features classic party-based combat with an emphasis on narrative.

### [One Fenix Down — PC](https://jrpgpursuit.com/)
A PC JRPG releasing today, November 11. Worth keeping an eye on if you enjoy indie RPGs.

### [Castlebound — Switch, Switch 2 & PC](https://jrpgpursuit.com/)
Also launching today on Switch, Switch 2, and PC.

## Announcements & Trailers

### [Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle](https://www.cbr.com/dragon-quest-rpg-first-trailer-release/)
Square Enix revealed a brand-new **Dragon Quest mobile RPG** with an official trailer yesterday (November 10). The turn-based RPG is planned for iOS and Android with a **global 2027 release**. Notably, this is expected to be one of the final titles to feature work from the late **Akira Toriyama**.

### [Final Fantasy Resonance — Final Trailer](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/jrpg/)
Square Enix dropped a final trailer for *Final Fantasy Resonance*. The game was also playable at **Tokyo Game Show 2026** with new content not found in the current demo.

### [Fate/EXTRA Record — Rivals PV](https://www.gematsu.com/genres/rpg/jrpg)
Aniplex released a new "Rivals" promotional video for **Fate/EXTRA Record** and held a 90-minute Tokyo hands-on event on November 7–8.

### [Everbrink — Announced by Harvestella Team](https://rpgamer.com/2026/11/new-release-round-up-november-5-2026/)
The creators behind Square Enix's *Harvestella* announced **Everbrink**, a roguelike strategy RPG targeting a **2027 release**. Few details are available, but fans of the team's work are watching closely.

### [Dungeon Antiqua ZERO — SaGa-Inspired Retro JRPG](https://rpgamer.com/2026/11/new-release-round-up-november-5-2026/)
Shiromofu Factory announced **Dungeon Antiqua ZERO**, a retro JRPG inspired by classic *SaGa* entries. No release date yet.

## Rumor / Leak

### [Persona 1 Remake Footage Leaks Online](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/persona/two-minutes-of-persona-1-remake-footage-seemingly-leaks-online-weeks-after-atlus-said-it-was-considering-more-jrpg-revivals/)
Roughly two minutes of unconfirmed footage labeled **"Megami Ibunroku Persona Remake"** surfaced on Gofile on October 31. Atlus has not commented. The leak comes weeks after Atlus P-Studio producer **Kazuhisa Wada** publicly discussed considering remakes of *Persona 1* and *Persona 2* back in August. Treat platforms, release window, and build authenticity as unknown until Atlus makes an official announcement.

## Industry News

### [Square Enix Leans Toward New IP Over Remakes](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/jrpg/)
Following the completion of the *Final Fantasy 7* remake trilogy with **FF7 Revelation**, the series lead at Square Enix has stated the company is now "more likely to invest in new titles and new games" rather than pursuing further JRPG remakes. This could signal a shift in strategy for the publisher.

### [Tales of Destiny Hidden Character Mystery Solved After 29 Years](https://bleedingcool.com/tag/jrpg/)
Fans have discovered a method to recruit a **hidden character** in the original PS1 version of *Tales of Destiny*, finally cracking a mystery that has persisted since the game's 1997 release.

## Service Endings

### [Atelier Resleriana Shutting Down November 25](https://www.gematsu.com/genres/rpg/jrpg)
Koei Tecmo has confirmed that the mobile and PC service for **Atelier Resleriana** will end on **November 25, 2026**. A refund window for outstanding purchases will be available after shutdown.
