## Final Fantasy XIV Lands on Switch 2 — One Month Free, 117 GB, Mouse-and-Keyboard Confirmed

**Final Fantasy XIV** officially launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**, completing Square Enix's long-awaited push to bring the MMO to a Nintendo platform. The free trial launched simultaneously, and new Switch 2 accounts received approximately **one month of complimentary subscription time** to ease server onboarding during the launch window.

The Starter Edition is priced at **$19.99** and the Complete Edition (including all expansions through Dawntrail) at **$59.99**, which was discounted to **$41.99** during a pre-order sale that extended through August 13. A Complete Collector's Edition sits at **$139.99**. Players who already have active time on other platforms can subscribe to the Switch 2 client at **half price** for the overlap period.

The Switch 2 version carries a **117 GB install size** and supports **mouse-and-keyboard input via the USB-C port**, a feature Square Enix highlighted for players who prefer the PC control scheme while playing on a TV or at a desk. Cross-play is fully active across Switch 2, PC, PlayStation, and Xbox from day one.

## Blue Reflection Quartet Reviews — "Strong" 76 Average, Praised for Heart Over Technical Shine

**Blue Reflection Quartet** launched on **July 30** for Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC (PlayStation 5 also), and reviews have now settled into consensus. The collection holds a **76 OpenCritic average** from 24 critics, earning a "Strong" designation with a 67% recommendation rate.

The package compiles all four entries in the franchise: the first **Blue Reflection** JRPG, the **Ray** anime adaptation, the **Sun** mobile game (re-adapted for consoles), and **Blue Reflection: Second Light**. Priced at **$49.99**, it offers 30–40 hours each across its two main RPGs.

Critical reception is divided by component: the two RPGs are praised for their genuine emotional storytelling and focus on friendship, while the Ray and Sun adaptations are widely considered "extremely lackluster" by comparison. Final Weapon awarded the package a **4/5**, calling it a "magical girl JRPG collection focused on friendship, personal growth, and heartfelt storytelling." Game8 acknowledged the RPG halves while noting the collection overall is "less than the sum of its parts."

Sources: [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/21069/blue-reflection-quartet) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/30/blue-reflection-quartet-launch-trailer-illustration/) · [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20992-blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [Game8](https://game8.co/reviews/blue-reflection-quartet/blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/reviews/blue-reflection-quartet-review/)

## Brigandine: Abyss Launches on Switch 2, PS5, and PC

**Happinet Games** and **Matrix Corporation** released **Brigandine: Abyss** — a fully new entry in the cult classic strategy-RPG franchise — on **August 26 (Steam) and August 27 (Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5)**. The game is set in an entirely new world separate from the original Brigandine: The Legend of Forsena or Grand Edition.

The premise centres on the revived **Abyssloa Empire**, which once threatened the continent before being stopped by the power of the arcane Brigandine. Players can choose from **six story campaigns** representing different factions mounting resistance against the empire's resurgence. Beyond the main campaigns, **Mission Mode** offers 24 playable factions with distinct win conditions for extended replayability.

Brigandine: Abyss follows the franchise's signature approach of large-scale territorial conquest, monster-unit deployment, and multi-army maneuvering that made the original PlayStation titles cult classics. Publisher Happinet also brought the game to Xbox Series X|S.

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — Gamescom ONL Trailer Adds Chocobos, Grappling Hook, Summon Typhon

**Square Enix** brought **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** to **Gamescom Opening Night Live** (August 25, 2026) with a new gameplay-focused trailer that revealed several previously unannounced mechanics.

The trailer introduces:

- **Chocobo mounts** for open-world traversal, the first confirmed in-game detail about how large-scale field movement works in the new engine
- A **grappling hook** ability for traversal across vertical environments
- **Summon Typhon**, a new summon joining the roster alongside previously announced ones
- **Open-world exploration via parachute**, shown in a sequence where Cloud descends from a high-altitude location into a valley below

Cloud and Tifa are also shown in their new **FITS (Final Integrated Tactical Suit)** forms, the power-boosting outfit system previously announced, with expanded abilities on display. Square Enix confirmed the **Spring 2027** release window holds — no specific date was given — for **Nintendo Switch 2, PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S**.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Dagdan Empire Setting, Four Protagonist Routes Detailed

**Nintendo** released more information about the story and structure of **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave**, arriving on **September 17** exclusively for Nintendo Switch 2.

New details from RPGamer and official sources:

- The game is set on the **continent of Dagda**, home to the **Dagdan Empire** — far to the southwest of *Three Houses'* Fódlan, establishing a fresh narrative geography
- The story begins with the empire's **fall at the hands of the revived demon god Balor**, and the goddess **Sothis** sends the player's soul back **five years** to change the fates of four heroes who had disappeared
- Players choose between **four protagonists** — **Cai, Theodora, Dietrich, and Leda** — each anchoring their own individual route with distinct story perspectives
- A **Heroic Games** tournament frames the central competition, with participants seeking wishes granted by a mysterious **Divine Sovereign**
- Classic mechanics return: weapon and unit strengths and weaknesses, terrain modifiers, and adjacent-ally bonuses

A special edition includes **art cards, a map poster, an artbook, a steelbook case, and the game card**.

## Showa American Story — Gamescom Trailer Showcases Alternate-History Japan-Ruled USA

**2P Games** and **Nekcom Entertainment** unveiled a new trailer for **Showa American Story** during **Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026**, giving the game its highest-profile Western showcase yet.

The alternate-history action RPG is set in a 1980s America in which Japan won a cultural and economic invasion — and then a literal one. Players navigate this hybrid world as a young woman navigating J-pop aesthetics, zombie-filled highways, and Japanese corporate-colonial politics layered over American geography. The game draws visual influence from late Showa-era anime and vintage Japanese game aesthetics.

Showa American Story had previously been announced for PC but is now confirmed for **Nintendo Switch and Switch 2** as well, with a release window **TBA in 2026 or 2027**.
