## EXSTETRA HD Remaster Now Live on Steam — First English Release in 13 Years

**FuRyu**'s **EXSTETRA HD Remaster** launched on **Steam on July 30** as the first time this Japan-only JRPG has ever been officially available in English. The original game launched for PlayStation Vita and Nintendo 3DS in Japan in November 2013 but never received a western release.

Set in **Amazea** — a Tokyo fused with a parallel world and destined for destruction — the game follows high-schooler **Ryoma**, who awakens as a "Prisma," the world's chosen savior. The combat system is built around a unique **"Kiss" mechanic**: Ryoma can share magical energy through a kiss, activating party members' special abilities and forging bonds that strengthen them in battle. Up to four characters can participate in each turn-based encounter with front- and back-row placement affecting roles.

The HD Remaster adds widescreen support, revised character artwork, and English subtitle options (alongside Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese). A **20% launch discount** runs through August 12 on Steam.

**Console versions** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation 5** were confirmed simultaneously and are scheduled to launch on **September 3, 2026**.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition — Now Available, Torna Gets Free Upgrade Patch

**Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** is now available digitally following its **July 30 launch**. Existing Switch owners of the base game can purchase the **$9.99 Upgrade Pack** from the Nintendo eShop to access all Switch 2 Edition enhancements, which include 4K/60fps in docked mode, 1080p/60fps in handheld, enhanced cutscene detail, new Pyra and Mythra outfits, the Momo (Xenosaga) rare Blade, and the expanded six-Blade Merc Assault mode.

Early technical impressions from [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/20994-xenoblade-chronicles-2-nintendo-switch-2-edition-review-vs-switch-1-impressions-comparison-upgrade) note that the 60fps target is **not fully locked** — the game's v-sync implementation causes occasional drops to 30fps when the hardware is taxed. The publication found the upgrade "good in some ways, but lacking in others" for a full recommendation. Physical editions arrive **October 1**.

Separately, **Xenoblade Chronicles 2: Torna – The Golden Country** received a **free Switch 2 upgrade patch** on July 30 for standalone disc and digital owners, enabling 4K/60fps performance parity with the Switch 2 Edition. Expansion Pass holders who already own Torna receive the same patch at no additional cost.

## Blue Reflection Quartet — Mixed Reviews as Four-Game Collection Arrives

**Blue Reflection Quartet** launched July 30 across **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam** (and PlayStation 5) at **$49.99**, bundling all four entries in Gust's magical-girl JRPG series: the original *Blue Reflection*, the 2021 anime *Blue Reflection Ray* adaptation, the mobile rework *Blue Reflection Sun*, and the sequel *Blue Reflection: Second Light*.

Reviews at launch are mixed. **Game8** scored it **68/100**, calling it "a collection that is less than the sum of its parts," noting that the adaptations of *Ray* and *Sun* feel "extremely lackluster" and that the visual-novel–heavy approach leaves combat and progression systems feeling undercooked across most entries. **Niche Gamer** and **The Outerhaven** were more positive, praising the collection as a welcoming entry point to an underrated series and highlighting the audio design as a standout across all four games.

## Final Fantasy XIV Switch 2 — Pricing, Install Size, and Subscription Details Ahead of August 4 Launch

With **Final Fantasy XIV**'s Nintendo Switch 2 debut three days away (August 4), Square Enix has published the full pricing and infrastructure details for the launch.

The game is sold in **four editions** ranging from **$19.99** (Starter Edition, covering A Realm Reborn through Shadowbringers) to **$97.99** (Complete Edition including all expansions through Dawntrail), with Endwalker and Dawntrail standalone options in between. The Switch 2 version requires a substantial **117 GB install** and supports mouse-and-keyboard input via the USB-C port.

On subscriptions: the Switch 2 version **requires its own separate subscription** and cannot share an existing PC or PlayStation subscription. However, players who already maintain an active subscription on another platform receive a **50% discount** on the Switch 2 subscription fee. New Switch 2 players who have never played FFXIV also have access to the **free trial**, which allows unlimited play up to **level 80** (covering A Realm Reborn through Shadowbringers) with no time restrictions. The previously announced **free first month** for Switch 2 players applies to new paid subscription accounts.

## Dragon's Dogma 2 — August Update Detailed: Triple Save Slots, Performance Pass

**Capcom** has published patch notes for the upcoming **Dragon's Dogma 2 August update** alongside the release of **Title Update 3.1**, which is live now across PC, Nintendo Switch 2, and other platforms.

The August patch focuses on **performance optimization and quality-of-life improvements** heading into the *Dark Arisen* expansion window (October 9). Its most notable change expands the save system from **one slot to three**, with each slot holding three distinct save types: Autosave, Interim Save, and Last Inn Rest Save — a significant change for players who previously had no safety net against accidental overwriting.

Additional August patch items include framerate and GPU optimization passes, Pawn behavior adjustments, enemy balance tuning, and multiple bug fixes covering eyelash rendering, voice line triggers, and the ability to acquire multiple Eternal Ferrystones.

## Elements Destiny — JIRPG with German Voice Cast Targets Q4 2026 for Switch and PC

The game follows **Erika**, a young huntress whose prophetic nightmares draw her into a world-spanning conflict against demonic forces, alongside a stranger named Airic O'Fory who seeks descendants of the elemental Clans. Gameplay centers on **turn-based tactical battles** where players master elemental affinities, craft weapons, and brew remedies — a conscious throwback to 16-bit and early-32-bit JRPG mechanics rendered in retro-styled pixel graphics with hand-drawn artwork.

## Nioh 3: Hell Rising DLC — August 19 for PC and PS5

**Koei Tecmo** and **Team Ninja** have dated the first DLC for the action-RPG **Nioh 3**: **Hell Rising** arrives **August 19** for **PC via Steam** (and PlayStation 5), priced as part of the **$39.99 Season Pass** or the **$109.99 Digital Deluxe Edition**.

The expansion transports protagonist **Takechiyo** to **Edo in 1651** during the Keian era, where the death of the future shogun has plunged the city into chaos. A new open-field region joins the campaign, and two new allies accompany Takechiyo: one-eyed swordsman **Yagyu Jubei** and Spanish envoy **Rodrigo**. New weapon types include **hoko** (a long-bladed spear) and **shields**, both fully supported with new Martial Arts. Additional content includes new Ninjutsu, Guardian Spirits, Soul Cores, the **"Enlightened One's Journey"** difficulty tier, and the **Hundred Demon Realms Picture Scroll** — a new rare loot equipment category.

A second DLC, **Bloody Insurrection**, is targeted for **February 2027**.
