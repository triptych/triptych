## Final Fantasy Resonance Launches — Full Offline HD-2D RPG Is Out Now

**Square Enix** released **Final Fantasy Resonance** on **October 22, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store**.

What began as a mobile gacha title has been completely reimagined as a standalone, fully offline turn-based RPG in HD-2D — the same visual style used for *Octopath Traveler* and *Triangle Strategy*. The game features a new cast of characters and an original world, though familiar Final Fantasy jobs, summons, and elemental mechanics ground it in series tradition. There is no gacha, no live service, and no microtransactions.

RPGFan highlighted Resonance in its [October 18–24 week preview](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/10/18/rpgs-coming-this-week-october-18-24-2026/) as the headline release of the week. The game launches at $49.99 on all platforms.

## One Piece: Grand Gourmet Ships on Switch, Switch 2, PC, and Mobile

**Bandai Namco** and **Kairosoft** launched **One Piece: Grand Gourmet** on **October 23, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam**, iOS, and Android at **$19.99**.

The game is a restaurant management sim set aboard Baratie Number Two, with Sanji leading the kitchen. Players serve over 400 One Piece characters appearing in pixel art style, cook dishes inspired by the series, and build out the restaurant. On Switch 2, Joy-Con 2 mouse controls are supported for menu navigation.

A Japan-region quirk: Switch and Switch 2 versions launched there on **October 22**, one day ahead of the global Steam and mobile release.

## Vixen 357 Gets First English Digital Release on Switch and PC

**Shinyuden** and **Ratalaika Games** released the sci-fi mecha strategy RPG **Vixen 357** digitally in English for the first time on **October 23, 2026**, priced at **$5.99** on Nintendo Switch. A release on Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, and PC via Steam was also confirmed, with the Switch version leading the Western rollout.

Originally a **Sega Mega Drive / Genesis** title from 1992, Vixen 357 was previously available in English only via Shinyuden's limited physical cartridge run. The game features mecha-vs-mecha strategy combat with permadeath on a grid, set in a war-torn post-apocalyptic future.

Shinyuden had confirmed the digital release was coming following their physical cartridge which began shipping worldwide in **June 2026**.

## Villion: Code Western Release Set for January 26, 2027

**Idea Factory International** confirmed this week that **Villion: Code**, the action RPG from **Compile Heart**, will launch in North America and Europe on **January 26, 2027** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The game launched in Japan on June 25, 2026. The Western release includes English subtitles with Japanese voice acting across all platforms. Physical editions for PS5, PS4, and Switch are planned for North America, with a PS5 physical edition confirmed for Europe. Pre-orders for physical editions are open now.

The PC Steam release will support English, and Traditional and Simplified Chinese subtitles.

## Octopath Traveler TRPG Rulebook Arrives in English on October 27

**Dark Horse Comics** is publishing the English edition of the **Octopath Traveler TRPG Rulebook** on **October 27, 2026**, priced at **$59.99** in hardcover (328 pages).

The tabletop RPG was originally developed in Japan by **FEAR** (designers with ties to the Octopath franchise) and published by Square Enix in 2022. This is the first official English release. The system uses **2d6 resolution**, lets players create original characters or use the video game cast, and is designed for either pre-written adventures or GM-led custom stories set in Orsterra.

Dark Horse Direct is carrying the book, and Penguin Random House is handling broader distribution.

Sources: [Game Informer exclusive](https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive/2026/04/02/octopath-traveler-trpg-to-receive-english-release) · [Bell of Lost Souls](https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2026/04/square-enix-and-dark-horse-to-bring-octopath-traveler-to-tabletop-rpg-in-october.html) · [Dark Horse Direct](https://www.darkhorsedirect.com/products/octopath-traveler-trpg-rulebook-hc) · [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/827443/octopath-traveler-trpg-rulebook-by-octopath-traveler-roleplaying-game-designed-by-yura-kubota-and-fear/9781506753256)
