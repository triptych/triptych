## Metaphor: ReFantazio Launches on Nintendo Switch 2 — Reviews Round-Up

**Atlus** released **Metaphor: ReFantazio** on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026**. The port is priced at **$39.99 / £34.99**, notably lower than the original PS5 and PC release, and a **limited Steelbook Edition** was available at launch.

Reviews are largely positive with consistent performance caveats:

- **Nintendo Life** praises it as an extraordinary JRPG but notes poor optimization on Switch 2.
- **Final Weapon** calls it a solid port that holds up well visually, with performance varying by content.
- **RPG Site**'s pre-launch assessment found it better than expected but in need of further optimization, noting the Performance mode runs significantly better than Graphics mode without reaching a fully stable frame rate.
- **Pocket Tactics** says it is not completely flawless but more than impressive.

The Switch 2 version does **not support Joy-Con 2 mouse controls** but does support touchscreen inputs and swiping. Players who downloaded the demo ahead of launch can transfer saves into the full game.

Sources: [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21726-metaphor-refantazio-switch-2-review-full-game-vs-ps5-ps4-steam-deck-pc) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/metaphor-refantazio) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/11/10/metaphor-refantazio-nintendo-switch-2-review/) · [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/metaphor-refantazio/review)

## Falcom Financial Report Targets Trails beyond the Horizon Sequel for Fiscal Year Ending September 2027

**Nihon Falcom**'s annual financial report for the fiscal year ending September 2026 confirms the company's next title is a **sequel to The Legend of Heroes: Trails beyond the Horizon**, targeting a launch **by the end of September 2027** in Japan.

Falcom president **Toshihiro Kondo** has previously described the upcoming game as a concluding chapter for the current story arc. The company projects a decline in both revenue and profit for the coming fiscal year, as it plans to release **only one new title** alongside several ports.

No game title, platforms, or Western release window have been announced.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21732-trails-beyond-the-horizon-sequel-release-set-for-september-2027-in-japan-falcom-date) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-beyond-the-horizon-sequel-to-launch-by-september-2027-in-japan) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/11/10/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-beyond-the-horizon-sequel-september-2027-release-date/)

## Queen's Domain Surpasses 100K Copies in Under Five Days

**Queen's Domain**, a first-person action RPG inspired by FromSoftware's *King's Field* developed by two-person indie studio **Freshly Baked Games** and published by **Future Friends Games**, sold **100,000 copies in less than five days** following its **October 30, 2026 launch** on PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

The game features slow, methodical dungeon-crawling combat with heavy environmental storytelling, drawing direct comparisons to the early *King's Field* and *Shadow Tower* entries. The Switch 2 version reached **#1 on the UK eShop download-only chart**. Steam concurrent players peaked above 11,000.

Sources: [RPG Site — 100K milestone](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21696-queens-domain-sales-numbers-new-100-k-copies-sold) · [80.lv feature](https://80.lv/articles/this-new-retro-fantasy-indie-rpg-channels-dark-souls-and-king-s-field/) · [Game*Spark (JP)](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/11/10/174681.html)

## Fate/EXTRA Record Confirmed for January 28, 2027 — XSEED Publishing in the West

After years of delays and a mid-development publisher change, **Fate/EXTRA Record** — the full remake of the 2010 PSP RPG — now has a firm worldwide release date of **January 28, 2027**. It will launch on **PS5, PS4, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC (Steam)**.

**XSEED Games** will handle the North American release; **Marvelous Europe** covers Europe. The original PSP publisher **Bandai Namco Entertainment** departed the project; **Aniplex** stepped in as new publisher, confirmed August 31, 2026.

The hands-on demo at **Tokyo Game Show 2026** (September 17–21) confirmed the battle system has been significantly overhauled — the classic **command-card rock-paper-scissors** mechanic has been replaced with a **deck-building** system. Players chose from Saber, Archer, or Caster for the demo's first arena. Impressions were broadly positive despite the game's long development history.

Sources: [RPG Site — Release Date](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21288-fate-extra-record-release-date-switch-2-physical-edition-ps5-physical) · [Gematsu](https://gematsu.com/2026/09/fate-extra-record-launches-january-28-2027-for-ps5-switch-2-ps4-switch-and-pc) · [Automaton Media — TGS impressions](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/fate-extra-record-gets-january-28-release-date-alongside-new-trailer/) · [RPG Site — TGS preview](https://www.rpgsite.net/preview/21523-fateextra-record-feels-pretty-good-so-far-for-game-that-was-caught-in-development-hell)

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** will shut down the Japanese service of **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026 at 16:00 JST**, closing the last remaining regional version of the gacha-based Atelier spinoff. The global service had already closed on **March 28, 2025** after less than 14 months.

Premium item purchases stopped in Japan in August 2026. Refund applications for remaining paid currency will be accepted from **November 26, 2026 through February 24, 2027**.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25) · [Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/08/27/atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-and-the-polar-night-liberator-ends-service-on-november-25-in-japan/)
