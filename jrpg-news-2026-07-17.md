## Star Ocean: The Second Story R Launches on Switch 2 — No Upgrade Path from Switch Version

**Square Enix** and developer **Gemdrops** released **Star Ocean: The Second Story R** on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **July 16, 2026**, as a surprise same-day launch with no prior announcement. The Switch 2 version is priced at **$49.99** and brings enhanced visuals at higher resolutions compared to the original Switch release.

Notably, Square Enix has confirmed there is **no Nintendo Switch to Switch 2 upgrade path**, and **save data is not compatible** between the two versions. Players who already own the game on Switch must repurchase it at full price with no discount option. The situation drew immediate criticism from fans on social media.

*Star Ocean: The Second Story R* first launched on PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam in November 2023. It is a 2.5D remake of the beloved second installment in the *Star Ocean* series, featuring a dual-protagonist system, real-time combat, and a visually distinct hybrid art style blending 2D sprites against 3D environments.

## Culdcept BEGINS Launches July 16; First New Series Entry in a Decade Earns Famitsu Gold Hall of Fame

**Culdcept BEGINS** launched on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** on **July 16, 2026**, marking the first all-new entry in the **Culdcept** franchise in ten years. A Steam release is slated for **Q4 2026**.

The game preserves the series' signature formula — players roll dice to traverse a board, claim territories, and battle rivals using a custom **40-card deck** spanning Creature, Item, and Spell categories. Physical editions for Switch 2 come in two flavors: a **Standard Edition ($54.99)** and a **Limited SteelBook Edition ($89.99)** that includes a soundtrack CD and a set of ten creature tablet-style cards.

*Culdcept BEGINS* received a **Famitsu Gold Hall of Fame award** ahead of launch, one of the publication's highest honors. The series blends the strategy of card games with the luck of a board game in a fantasy setting and has historically been popular in Japan since its debut in 1997.

## Final Fantasy VII: Ever Crisis to End Service October 7

**Square Enix** announced on **July 7, 2026** that **Final Fantasy VII: Ever Crisis** will end service on **October 7, 2026** (October 6 at 11:00 p.m. PT). Sales of premium Red Crystals were halted immediately upon announcement. In-game events will continue to be updated until the shutdown date.

The gacha-style mobile RPG launched on iOS and Android in **September 2023** and came to **Steam in December 2023**, spanning roughly three years of service. The game retold stories from across the *Final Fantasy VII* extended universe — including the original *FF7*, *Crisis Core*, and *Dirge of Cerberus* — in a shared episodic format with a chapter-based structure and gacha weapon pulls.

Square Enix apologized for the abrupt nature of the announcement. No reason for the closure was given.

## Celestial Return Launches on Nintendo Switch and Switch 2

**Celestial Return**, a cyberpunk noir narrative RPG developed by **Cygnus Cross**, launched on **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 on July 16, 2026**. The Switch version is priced at **$44.99**; the Switch 2 version at **$54.99**, with a **$10 Upgrade Pack** available for Switch owners. A **PC via Steam** release is planned for later in 2026.

The game follows **Howard**, a detective aided by a sentient rose, as he investigates a string of suicides in **Netherveil City**, a stylized cyberpunk metropolis rendered in a visual blend of manga and American comic art. Player choices shape Howard's personality across four dimensions, which in turn gates which dialogue options and actions are available — the available choices are literally determined by **dice collected** throughout the adventure.

Early impressions note a strong atmosphere and unusual dice-driven personality system, though the $54.99 Switch 2 price has drawn some pushback.

Sources: [DualShockers review](https://www.dualshockers.com/celestial-return-review-a-cyberpunk-mystery-in-a-brutal-world/) · [Gaming Boulevard](https://gamingboulevard.com/2026/07/indie-corner-celestial-return/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2704090/Celestial_Return/)

## Robot Rhapsody Kickstarter Underway; Cyberpunk Pixel RPG Seeks $20K

Developer **Rapturous Studio** has launched a **Kickstarter** crowdfunding campaign for **Robot Rhapsody**, a turn-based pixel-art RPG set in a cyberpunk world on the edge of anarchic revolution. The campaign runs until **August 14, 2026** with a goal of **$20,000**; early tracking shows it approaching the target.

The game takes place in **Zoxxo City**, a metropolis partially reclaimed by nature following a tech prohibition, and follows **Selva Lux** — a former rogue-robot hunter who forms a gang of freedom-fighters to halt runaway AI development. The main story is estimated at **20–30 hours**, with 10+ additional hours of side content. A **$20 pledge** secures a digital PC copy.

*Robot Rhapsody* is targeting a **late 2027** PC release. A [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3758170/Robot_Rhapsody/) and [Kickstarter page](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/rapturousstudio/robot-rhapsody) are live.

## Emberville Sets October 27 Early Access Date; Reveals Star-Studded Voice Cast

**Emberville**, an action RPG/life-sim hybrid from indie studio **Cygnus Cross**, will launch in **Steam Early Access on October 27, 2026**, also available via **GOG**. A new gameplay trailer accompanies the date announcement.

The game sends players into the underground world of **Vitromotus** to fight through dungeons, gather resources, and funnel those resources into rebuilding a surface-level town called **Emberville**. Each expedition unlocks new NPCs, crafting recipes, character classes, and story branches above ground. Early Access is expected to last **approximately twelve months** before the 1.0 release.

The game features an acclaimed voice cast including **Matthew Mercer, Jennifer English, Ben Starr, Nick Apostolides, Jane Perry, Elias Toufexis, Anjali Bhimani, Briana White**, content creator **LilyPichu**, and DJ **Dillon Francis**.
