## Atelier Resleriana (Japan) Shuts Down November 25

Koei Tecmo and Gust will end the Japanese service for **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026** at 16:00 JST. The studio cited an inability to maintain a satisfying user experience as the reason for closure. Premium currency purchases were halted in August 2026. Players with remaining premium currency may apply for refunds between November 26, 2026 and February 24, 2027. The global version had already shut down in March 2025. The separate offline spinoff, *Atelier Resleriana: The Red Alchemist & the White Guardian*, is unaffected.

- [Automaton Media — Koei Tecmo's Atelier Resleriana to end services in Japan on November 25](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/)

## Fate/EXTRA Record Gets January 28, 2027 Release Date

After multiple delays, **Fate/EXTRA Record** finally has a firm launch date: **January 28, 2027**. The announcement was made during a State of Play broadcast on September 3, 2026. The game comes to PS5, PS4, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam. Developer TYPE-MOON Studio BB showed the game at Tokyo Game Show 2026, where press compared the card-combat system to a Persona-meets-Slay-the-Spire loop. A public playable demo was planned for Paris Games Week 2026.

## Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain Remake Coming to Switch 2 and PC

Level-5 announced **Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain** — a full remake of the 2014 3DS sequel — for Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam. The title was revealed at Level-5's September 2026 Online Showcase. The remake features rebuilt graphics, additional story content, and new Yo-kai. No release date has been announced. This marks the first Yo-kai Watch title with a confirmed Western release in over seven years.
