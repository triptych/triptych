## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Launches Today; Reviews Positive

**Cygames'** expanded edition of **Granblue Fantasy: Relink**, titled **[Endless Ragnarok](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20854-granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-final-launch-trailer)**, is out today — **July 9, 2026** — on **Nintendo Switch 2 and PC via Steam** (also PS4/PS5). This marks the game's first Nintendo platform release and introduces **cross-platform multiplayer for the first time** in the franchise's history.

The expansion adds substantial content over the original 2024 release:

- **Six new playable characters**: Beatrix, Eustace, Gallanza, Maglielle, Fraux, and Fediel
- **New story arc** continuing the base game's narrative
- **Roguelite endgame mode** for solo or co-op play
- **Overhauled summon system**
- **Local co-op** on Switch 2 for same-room play

**Pricing**: $29.99 upgrade kit for existing owners; $59.99 full bundle for new players.

Reviews landed at approximately **86th percentile on OpenCritic**. Critics praised the new characters, summon system, and the roguelite mode, though noted the Chaos difficulty reuses too many enemies and the story presentation is thin. The consensus is that it's an excellent proposition for fans of the base game, with a higher bar for newcomers.

## Moonlight Peaks Now Out on Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**[Moonlight Peaks](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/03/moonlight-peaks-launches-july-7-for-switch-2-switch-and-pc)**, a gothic farming and life-sim RPG from Dutch developer **Little Chicken**, launched **July 7, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam, and Google Play Games**.

The game casts players as a young vampire stepping out from Count Dracula's shadow. You inherit an overgrown family farm in a supernatural town populated by creatures of the night, with gameplay blending farming, crafting, relationship-building, and light RPG progression. The studio describes influences including *Stardew Valley* and classic vampire fiction.

**Pricing**: $39.99 on Switch 2; $34.99 on all other platforms. A **15% launch discount** is active through July 13. A free demo remains available on Steam and the Nintendo eShop.

The game was featured by **RPGFan** in their [RPGs Coming This Week, July 5–11](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/07/05/rpgs-coming-this-week-july-5-11-2026/) roundup.
