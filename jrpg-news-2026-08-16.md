## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct Reveals True Protagonist and Full Story Structure

Nintendo aired a dedicated **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct** on **August 4** (roughly 20 minutes), expanding far beyond the four-protagonist framework previously shown.

The biggest revelation: the game's *actual* protagonist is an unnamed hero clad in white. The world has already been destroyed by the demon god **Balor**, and the progenitor goddess **Sothis** has summoned this lone soul as humanity's last hope. Unable to face Balor alone, the white-clad hero travels back in time to guide the fates of the four arena champions — **Cai, Dietrich, Theodora, and Leda** — so they can be assembled in the future to fight. Players can switch between all four protagonists freely, with each character's progress saved independently.

Combat keeps traditional Fire Emblem grid placement for arenas, with a new weapon category: **guns**, which function differently from standard ranged units. Hands-on impressions from GamesRadar describe being ready to "sacrifice hundreds of hours" at the game's altar.

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** releases on **Nintendo Switch 2 on September 17, 2026**.

## Final Fantasy XIV Is Now Live on Nintendo Switch 2

**Final Fantasy XIV** officially launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**, completing the platform's biggest MMORPG addition since the console launched. The Switch 2 version arrived with cross-play across PC, PlayStation, and Xbox, mouse-and-keyboard support via USB-C, and a download size of approximately **117 GB**.

To smooth the rollout, Square Enix offered approximately **one free month of subscription time** for Switch 2 players at launch. The Complete Edition (base game through *Dawntrail*) was also discounted 30% through August 13 on the eShop. An extended gameplay showcase published by Nintendo Life walks through the Switch 2 version's interface and visual performance in handheld and docked modes.

## Final Fantasy XI × Wizardry Variants Daphne Crossover Is Live

A reciprocal collaboration between **Final Fantasy XI** and **Wizardry Variants Daphne** began on **July 30** and runs through **September 10, 2026**.

On the **Final Fantasy XI** side: the event adds new **Notorious Monsters** in Seekers of Adoulin areas that drop locked treasure boxes, and all adventurers who log in during the event period automatically receive an **Abyssal Mask** — headgear modeled on the signature look of Wizardry's Masked Adventurer.

On the **Wizardry Variants Daphne** side: a collaboration dungeon event called **"Fantasy Castle of Deepest Dark"** runs for the same period, featuring FFXI characters **Prishe, Iroha, and Zeid** with their original Japanese voice actors. Players can also unlock **Relic Weapons** (in base form via the Jeweler's Exchange) and upgrade them through reforging.

## Beast of Reincarnation Launches on PC with Mixed Reviews — Game Freak's Soulslike Debut

**Game Freak** (the Pokémon studio) released its first-ever PC title, **Beast of Reincarnation**, on **August 4** via Steam and other storefronts alongside console launches. The action RPG is set in **Japan in the year 4026**, where protagonist **Emma the Sealer** and her dog **Koo** navigate a corruption-ravaged post-apocalyptic landscape.

The game's combat centers on parrying and balancing real-time melee with a pseudo-turn-based command layer. Critical reception landed around a **73 Metacritic score on PC** — "boring world, beguiling combat" in Game Informer's framing; But Why Tho gave it a 9/10, calling it a well-designed action RPG with several unique, well-executed elements. Game Freak acknowledged in a statement that the game "did not provide a sufficiently enjoyable experience" for all players.

Sources: [PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/beast-of-reincarnation-review/) · [Game Informer review](https://gameinformer.com/review/beast-of-reincarnation/boring-world-beguiling-combat) · [Gaming Trend review](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/beast-of-reincarnation-review/) · [Lords of Gaming review (9/10)](https://lordsofgaming.net/2026/08/beast-of-reincarnation-review/)

## Genshin Impact 7.0 "Everwinter Without Mercy" Launches — Snezhnaya Finally Opens

**HoYoverse** released **Genshin Impact Version 7.0** on **August 12**, titled **"Everwinter Without Mercy"**. This update introduces **Snezhnaya**, the seventh and final nation of Teyvat, completing the game's original seven-nation world structure.

Two new 5-star playable characters make their debut: **Odette** and **Alyosha**. The update's signature exploration mechanic is the **Eye of Graeae**, which allows Travelers to construct Prime Ice Constructs in the open world for both navigation puzzles and asynchronous multiplayer — players can leave behind messages and materials visible to others in their worlds.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/genshin-impact-version-version-7-0-update-everwinter-without-mercy-launches-august-12) · [Genshin Impact Wiki 7.0](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Version/7.0) · [Sportskeeda overview](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/genshin-impact-7-0-update-new-region-characters-reactions)

## Kingdom Hearts IV Shows Coco World and Anime Series at D23 2026

At **Disney's D23 2026 event on August 15**, Square Enix unveiled an extended new trailer for **Kingdom Hearts IV** built around the game's first confirmed Disney world: **Pixar's Coco**. ## Suikoden: STAR LEAP Launches in Japan on Mobile

**Konami** soft-launched **Suikoden: STAR LEAP** on **iOS and Android in Japan on August 7**, marking the franchise's return to active release after years of dormancy. The free-to-play mobile RPG is developed by **MYTHRIL** and features 108-star recruitment, classic Suikoden political conflict storytelling, and the series' ensemble cast format adapted for a mobile structure.

A **global mobile release** and **Steam PC version** are both confirmed but have no announced dates yet.
