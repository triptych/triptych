## Blue Reflection Quartet — Mixed-to-Positive Reviews on Launch

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust**'s collection **Blue Reflection Quartet** launched **July 30** across **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam** at **$49.99**, bundling the original *Blue Reflection*, *Blue Reflection: Second Light*, the *Blue Reflection Ray* anime, and the *Blue Reflection SUN* mobile title.

Reviews landed in the mixed-to-positive range. **Game8** called it a "half-baked collection that fails to elevate the series further," noting the Ray and Sun adaptations feel underdeveloped. **Shacknews** (7/10) praised the two mainline games while similarly criticizing the anime and mobile inclusions. **Final Weapon** awarded 4/5, calling it a "magical girl JRPG collection focused on friendship, personal growth, and heartfelt storytelling." **Gaming Respawn** gave it an 8, highlighting strong narrative and character development across both main entries.

The consensus is that both *Blue Reflection* and *Second Light* remain compelling 30–40 hour experiences each, making the collection worthwhile for newcomers despite the weaker bonus content.

Sources: [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/30/blue-reflection-quartet-launch-trailer-illustration/) · [Shacknews Review](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150181/blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20992-blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [Game8 Review](https://game8.co/reviews/blue-reflection-quartet/blue-reflection-quartet-review)

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Switch 2 Edition Now Live; Physical Arrives October

**Monolith Soft**'s **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** launched digitally on **July 30** at **$69.99** (or **$9.99** as an upgrade from the original Switch version). The physical edition arrives **October 1**.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct — True Protagonist, Full Combat, Dagdan Collection Revealed

Nintendo held a **dedicated Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct on August 4**, running approximately 20 minutes and substantially expanding the picture of the game ahead of its **September 17 Switch 2 launch**.

The Direct revealed the game's true narrative structure: while four protagonists — **Cai, Dietrich, Theodora, and Leda** — participate in the Heroic Games arena tournament, the actual protagonist is a **nameless white-clad hero** summoned from five years in the future by the goddess Sothis to assemble those four champions and prevent the demon god **Balor** from destroying the world. Players can switch freely between the four champions' storylines at any point.

The combat deep dive detailed **Fortuna's Blessing**, a rewind mechanic letting players roll back to virtually any prior move without restarting the map — a significant evolution beyond the series' traditional Divine Pulse system. Units wield swords, gauntlets, spears, axes, bows, and magic, with some weapon types offering alternate forms (including a gun).

Nintendo also announced the **Dagdan Collection** limited edition, containing the game, a Steelbook case, protagonist art cards, a map of Dagda, and an artbook.

Sources: [Game Informer Direct Recap](https://gameinformer.com/nintendo-direct/2026/08/04/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-direct-reveals-true-protagonist-and-plot) · [RPGFan Report](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/08/06/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-direct-report/) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/08/nintendo-direct-for-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-announced) · [TheSixthAxis Preview](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/08/13/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-preview/)

## Suikoden: STAR LEAP — Launches in Japan August 7; Steam Version Forthcoming

**Konami** and developer **MYTHRIL** launched **Suikoden: STAR LEAP** on iOS and Android in Japan on **August 7**, marking the **first all-new entry in the Suikoden franchise in 14 years**. A Suikoden Live stream aired August 6 at 8 PM JST ahead of the release.

Set in **Solar Year 453** — canonically between *Suikoden V* and the original *Suikoden* — the story begins in a village near the Scarlet Moon Empire where the hero, son of the village chief, returns from his first hunt only to see his village attacked. He sets out with allies **Hisui, Shirin, and Shapur** to restore peace, and the game features **108 new Stars of Destiny** to recruit. The combat system is timeline-based, and visuals use upgraded pixel art with dynamic audio.

A **global release on PC via Steam, iOS, and Android** is planned but undated.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/08/suikoden-star-leap-launches-august-7-in-japan) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/08/suikoden-star-leap-releasing-in-japan-this-week/) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/suikoden-star-leap-japanese-mobile-release-date-set/) · [Saiga NAK](https://saiganak.com/release/suikoden-star-leap/)

## Nioh 3: Hell Rising DLC — Out August 19; Second DLC Named

**Koei Tecmo** and **Team Ninja** released **Nioh 3**'s first paid story expansion, **Hell Rising**, on **August 19** for **PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam**.

Set in **Keian-era Edo** (1651), *Hell Rising* sends protagonist Takechiyo to a new stage, reuniting him with legendary swordsman **Jubei Yagyu** to stop a conspiracy involving Spirit Stones. The expansion introduces two new weapon types (**Hoko & Shield** and **Ninja Hoko & Shield**), new Guardian Spirits, Soul Cores, Ninjutsu, Martial Arts, and a new difficulty called **Enlightened One's Journey** — where certain enemies receive random Curse buffs granting much harder fights in exchange for divine-rarity item drops.

The team also announced the second DLC: **Bloody Insurrection**, targeting **February 2027**.

Sources: [Koei Tecmo America](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/news/travel-to-the-keian-era-with-hell-rising-the-first-dlc-for-nioh-3-coming-aug-19-2026/) · [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150161/nioh-3-hell-rising-dlc-vol-1-release-date-first-look) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3681010/view/668371786222537334) · [Game Informer Interview](https://gameinformer.com/interview/2026/08/18/team-ninja-answers-our-burning-questions-about-nioh-3s-first-story-expansion)

## Steins;Gate Re:Boot — Out Now on Switch, Switch 2, and Steam

**MAGES Inc.**'s **Steins;Gate Re:Boot** launched worldwide on **August 20** — on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**, with **PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5** versions also available.

The remake of the landmark 2009 visual novel features fully updated visuals and streamlined pacing based on the scenario structure of *Steins;Gate Elite*. ## Bringer — New Fantasy Action RPG Announced for PS5, PC, and Mobile; Gamescom Demo

Chinese developer **YHKT Entertainment** revealed **Bringer**, a narrative-driven fantasy action RPG built in Unreal Engine 5, targeting **PlayStation 5, PC, iOS, and Android**. A release window has not been set.

The game is set on a world defined by the **"Thalassa"** and massive creatures called **Arkiloth**. Players control a traveler with a hidden secret, navigating competing factions and ancient mysteries. Combat allows real-time switching between party members with parries, dodges, and finishers woven into story sequences and exploration.

A **playable demo at Gamescom 2026** (August 26–30, Cologne) is the game's first public hands-on.

## Brigandine Abyss — Launches August 26–27 on PC and Switch 2

**Happinet**'s long-awaited **Brigandine Abyss** arrives **August 26 on PC via Steam** and **August 27 on Nintendo Switch 2** (PlayStation 5 also on August 27). A **Deluxe Edition** on Switch 2 and PS5 bundles a mini art book and digital soundtrack.

The new entry is set in a completely new world from prior *Brigandine* titles and is designed to be accessible to newcomers. A playable demo was made available on consoles ahead of launch.

## Neptunia Unlimited — Japan Launches August 27

**Idea Factory** and **Compile Heart**'s **Neptunia Unlimited** — the first new mainline *Hyperdimension Neptunia* entry in over a decade — launches in Japan on **August 27** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5** (PS4 digital-only) at **8,580 yen standard / 19,800 yen limited edition**.

The limited edition includes the game, a special box with art by **Tsunako**, a premium art book, soundtrack CD, Season Pass, and a Divine Cross promotional card. Returning goddesses **Neptune (Purple Heart), Noire (Black Heart), Blanc (White Heart), and Vert (Green Heart)** are joined by newcomers **Rouge (Red Heart), Ciel (Blue Heart), and dual-pilots Anna and Lot (Eclipse Heart)**. The game's central threat is a world-ending meteorite, and Neptune's story-central ability is called **Retry**. Combat shifts toward an action-oriented style drawing comparisons to the *Tales* series.

A western release date has not been announced.

## Another Eden Begins — September 17 Worldwide Launch; Physical Edition Announced

**Wright Flyer Studios / Studio Prisma**'s standalone console RPG **Another Eden Begins** launches **September 17 worldwide** on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. A **physical edition** was also announced (details via RPGamer).

The game is a reworked premium release of the free-to-play mobile *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space*. The story follows **Aldo** and his sister **Feinne**, who are torn apart by the Beast King; after a battle, Aldo is thrown **800 years into the future** through a spacetime rift. Features include **18 companions**, full English voiceovers for the main story, and command-based battles with a gauge system that lets players halt enemy actions.

Veteran scenario director **Masato Kato** (Chrono Trigger, Xenogears) leads at Studio Prisma, with composer **Yasunori Mitsuda** contributing the score.
