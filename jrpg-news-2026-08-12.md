## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct — True Protagonist, Manga Adaptation Announced

Nintendo aired a **20-minute Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct** on **August 4, 2026**, delivering the deepest look yet at the strategy RPG arriving **September 17** on Nintendo Switch 2.

The presentation's biggest reveal is the game's narrative twist: what appeared to be a four-protagonist story set around the gladiatorial Heroic Games is actually a framing device. The **true protagonist** is a **nameless hero clad in white**, summoned by the progenitor god Sothis from a future five years hence — a future in which the demon god Balor has already destroyed the world. This time-displaced hero travels into the past to guide the four champions — **Cai, Dietrich, Theodora, and Leda** — toward destinies capable of averting the apocalypse. Players can switch freely between all four champions at any time, with each character's storyline progressing independently.

Gameplay details confirmed in the Direct:
- Units wield swords, gauntlets, spears, axes, bows, and magic, plus some unconventional options including a firearms-type weapon; each has distinct properties and forms
- **Combat Arts** deal increased damage at the cost of weapon durability
- **Blaze Arts** are bespoke special moves that drain HP instead
- Non-combat activities and class mechanics were shown but not fully detailed

Following the Direct, Nintendo announced that **Fortune's Weave is getting a manga adaptation**. The first prologue chapter will appear in the **October 2026 issue of Shueisha's Saikyo Jump** (on shelves in September). The artist is **Kazurou Kyou**, who previously illustrated the Fire Emblem Engage manga.

## BlazBlue: Entropy Effect X — Switch 2 Edition Launches Tomorrow, Platinum Update Hits All Platforms

The Switch 2 Edition features sharper visuals, smoother performance, and **GameShare support**. ## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT — Xbox Series Release Delayed to October 29, Steam Launch Unchanged

**Spike Chunsoft** announced on **August 10** that the **Xbox Series X|S version of STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** has been delayed from its originally scheduled **August 20** release to **October 29, 2026**. In an official statement, Spike Chunsoft said the delay is to "align with the western console release date."

The launch schedule now stands as follows:

- **August 20** — Japan and global release on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, and PC via Steam**
- **August 20** — PC (Steam) in the west launches on the same day as Japan
- **October 29** — Western console release for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation 5** (Xbox Series X|S now joins this date)

Developed by **MAGES**, *RE:BOOT* is a full remake of the original visual novel built on the *Steins;Gate Elite* scenario, with entirely new character art, backgrounds, and event illustrations. The remake adds a **brand-new worldline and ending scenario**, significantly more story text than prior versions, and revised pacing throughout. The new story content is exclusive to *RE:BOOT* and was not present in any previous release.

## Final Fantasy XIV Launches on Switch 2 — Post-Launch Subscription Clarification, Separate Fee Confirmed

**Final Fantasy XIV** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4, 2026**, bringing all current expansions — Heavensward, Stormblood, Shadowbringers, Endwalker, and Dawntrail — to the platform simultaneously. The Switch 2 version supports mouse-and-keyboard input via the USB-C port.

Key details confirmed at and after launch:

- **Four editions** are available, priced from **$19.99 to $97.99**
- The digital Complete Edition (and its upgrade path) is **30% off until August 13**
- The install footprint is **117 GB**
- **Free trial** is available on Switch 2, allowing new players to reach level 60 and complete *A Realm Reborn* and *Heavensward* at no cost
- Subscription fees are waived for approximately **one month post-launch** for server stability purposes
- **Existing subscribers** on PC or PlayStation must pay a **separate Switch 2 subscription fee** — the platforms do not share a subscription; however, a **50% discount** applies to existing subscribers adding the Switch 2 platform

## Neptunia Unlimited — Combat Trailer Released, Tokyo Hands-On Event August 8

**Compile Heart** released a new **gameplay trailer** this week for **Neptunia Unlimited**, the first mainline entry in the Neptunia franchise in eleven years, showcasing its combat system ahead of the **August 27 Japan launch** on PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch 2, and Nintendo Switch.

The trailer demonstrates the game's signature combo chains, aerial maneuvers, field movement, and **Apocalypse encounters** — large-scale confrontations against powerful enemies that appear to be the game's equivalent of boss fights. The returning goddesses (**Neptune, Noire, Blanc, and Vert**) are joined by three new characters: **Rouge, Ciel, and Anna** and **Lot**.

Compile Heart also held an **advance sale and hands-on play event on August 8** at Sofmap AKIBA PC & Digital Building in Tokyo. New **Sweets Time merchandise** was announced for the event, including acrylic stands (¥2,530), character badges (¥605), and a lidded mug (¥2,420) featuring the four main goddesses.

A western release date has not yet been announced.

## Octopath Traveler Producer Teases Third Entry, Cites High Expectations

**Tomoya Asano**, the series producer of the **Octopath Traveler** franchise, addressed the question of a third mainline game in a recent interview, making the clearest acknowledgment to date that it is on his radar — while carefully stopping short of an announcement.

Asano stated he is "well aware that expectations for 3 are the highest," before cutting himself off and apologizing for being unable to share further details "at this time." He also pointed toward the franchise's upcoming **10th anniversary** as a meaningful milestone, though declined to elaborate on whether or how it might factor into any announcement plans.

The original *Octopath Traveler* launched in 2018; its sequel, *Octopath Traveler II*, arrived in 2023. A 10th anniversary for the series would fall in **2028**. **Square Enix** has not made any formal announcement, but Asano's comments — the most direct he has been on the subject — have intensified fan speculation about a reveal within the next two years.
