# JRPG News — May 31, 2026

## Nihon Falcom Reports 1,227% Profit Growth; Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Dated September 17

**Nihon Falcom** released its latest financial results, revealing operating profit surged **1,227.4%** year-on-year while revenue climbed **153.9%**, driven by the continued commercial success of **Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter** — both in direct sales and overseas licensing, which alone grew 218.3%.

In the same announcement, Falcom confirmed that **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** will launch on **September 17, 2026** as a simultaneous global release on **PC (Steam)**, **PlayStation 5**, and **Nintendo Switch**. The company noted it is being conservative with its full-year forecast due to uncertainty around **Kyoto Xanadu**, which is expected to release in the second half of the fiscal year and whose sales are harder to predict.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio and Persona 4 Golden Leave Xbox Game Pass Today

Five titles exit **Xbox Game Pass** today (May 31), and two of them are major RPGs:

- **Metaphor: ReFantazio** — Atlus's critically acclaimed fantasy RPG, which landed on Game Pass on day one in October 2024.
- **Persona 4 Golden** — the enhanced remaster of the JRPG, which had been on the service since August 2025 (its second stint after an earlier run in 2023–2024).

Also departing today: *Against the Storm*, *Crypt Custodian*, and *Spray Paint Simulator*. Players who want to continue either RPG will need to purchase them outright.

## The Remake of the End of the Greatest RPG of All Time — Out Now on Steam

Developer **Imaginary Game Studio** released **The Remake of the End of the Greatest RPG of All Time** on **May 28** for **PC (Steam)**. The game is a meta puzzle experience built entirely around a *fictional*, never-released 1990s JRPG of the same name.

Players must complete the final hour of this legendary lost game — then dig into the original manual, director's commentary tracks, and footage from an unreleased amateur documentary to uncover the secret at the heart of it all. The deduction layer resembles *Tunic*'s approach to hidden knowledge, but applied to JRPG nostalgia. Visually the game pairs crisp pixel art with 3D environments and dynamic lighting.

## STARBITES — Sci-Fi Mecha JRPG from NIS America — Out Now

**IKINAGAMES** and publisher **NIS America** released **STARBITES** on **May 21** for **PC (Steam)**, **PlayStation 5**, **Nintendo Switch**, and **Xbox Series X|S** (priced at $49.99). The **Nintendo Switch 2** version was delayed at the last minute to an unannounced window.

Set on **Bitter**, a desert planet scarred by interstellar war, the game follows **Lukida**, a young salvager sifting through sand for parts and relics whose life changes after an encounter tied to the mysterious **Oracle**. Combat is classic turn-based, with each character piloting a personal **Motorbot** that is central to both battle and character progression.

## Birushana: Winds of Fate — Otome Fan Disc Now on Switch

**Idea Factory International** released **Birushana: Winds of Fate** on **May 26** for **Nintendo Switch** in North America and Europe digitally (physical European release pushed to June 26). The game is a fan disc follow-up to *Birushana: Rising Flower of Genpei*, developed by **Otomate** and **RED**.

*Winds of Fate* picks up after the love endings of the first game and offers **9 routes total** — epilogues showing life after the war with returning love interests, plus four brand-new love interests introduced for the first time. The Outerhaven called it "a heartfelt fan disc that rewards returning players with satisfying epilogues and strong new romance routes."
