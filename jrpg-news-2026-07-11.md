## Disgaea Mayhem Demo Live on All Platforms Ahead of July 23 Launch

**NIS America** and developer **Nippon Ichi Software** have released a playable demo for **Disgaea Mayhem** on **Nintendo Switch**, **Nintendo Switch 2**, **PlayStation 5**, and **PC via Steam**. The demo offers three stages using any of the game's seven weapon types — swords, bows, guns, fists, and axes — each ending with a boss fight.

*Disgaea Mayhem* is an action RPG spinoff that trades the series' traditional grid-based tactics for direct real-time action. Players control **N.A.**, a mercenary hunting monsters for money and the affections of a princess with an obsessive love of flan. Deep Disgaea systems remain intact: Item World dungeon diving, **Dark Chocolate Assembly** bill-passing for stat bonuses, equipment upgrades, and reincarnation to raise damage caps into the series' signature absurd numbers.

A **Limited Edition** is available for **$99.99**, including the artbook *The Art of Mayhem*, the original soundtrack on disc, an N.A. and Tichelle acrylic stand, and *The Flan Fanatic* acrylic keychain.

The full game launches **July 23** on Switch, Switch 2, PS5, and PC.

## Cygames Confirms Project Awakening, Lost Order, and Garnet Arena: Mages of Magicary Still in Development

In a **Weekly Famitsu** anniversary interview, **Cygames** president **Koichi Watanabe** confirmed that three long-awaited titles remain in active development — and shared exclusive new footage with Famitsu editors that has not been released publicly.

- **Project Awakening** — the dark fantasy action RPG first shown in 2018 — has evolved from its original reveal, with the world and visual style changed somewhat though the overall direction remains largely intact. Watanabe says development is "really starting to take shape" and the team is working "with confidence that things are coming together."
- **Lost Order** — a real-time tactics game that ran a closed smartphone beta in 2017 — has been rebuilt from scratch, including all character models. Watanabe describes it as "now entering the final stretch," and Famitsu editors who viewed new footage called the results "astonishing quality — a bit unbelievable that this is possible as a smartphone app."
- **Garnet Arena: Mages of Magicary** — a fantasy action game — is progressing, with Watanabe saying the team "will be able to deliver something unlike our other titles."

No release windows were announced for any of the three.

## Honkai: Star Rail Version 4.4 Launches July 15 with Himeko Nova and Fate Collab Chapter 2

**HoYoverse** will release **Honkai: Star Rail** version **4.4 — "In Ravages Does the Whistle Sound"** on **July 15**, continuing the Planarcadia story arc.

New character **Himeko Nova** debuts as a **5-star Fire / Erudition** unit who fights alongside a mech called **Starblazer**, deployed through a Territory mechanic that grants Assist Skills to the full party.

The second phase of the **Fate/stay night [Unlimited Blade Works]** collaboration begins **July 24**, adding **Gilgamesh** and **Rin Tohsaka** (5-star Quantum / Erudition — burns Skill Points to generate Gem Energy, with damage scaling to her Gem Energy level). ## Arknights: Endfield "Homecoming" Update Launches July 16

**GRYPHLINE** and developer **Mountain Contour** will release version update **"Homecoming"** for **Arknights: Endfield** on **July 16**, marking the game's six-month anniversary. The update concludes the current Wuling story arc while adding the series' largest main-story map expansion since launch. New content includes:

- **Two story maps** — *Yinglung Pass*, gateway into a sealed territory introducing the Feranmut Proxy Si, and *North Wuling Exclusion Zone*, a Blight Miasma-covered homeland where players must suppress corruption to open a path for returning survivors
- **Two new Operators** — **Arcane** (6-star; her Intellect/Will balance lets her be built as a damage-focused caster or elemental support) and **Liino** (Electric supporter with two stances that buff allies and provide healing)
- **AIC factory system expansion** — adds **Gas** as a third production state alongside solids and liquids, new resources including Inergen and Xiragen, and new industrial facilities such as the Xiranflow Cloudseeder Station
- **Echoes of War** — a new permanent seasonal combat mode with rotating stages, multiple difficulty tiers, rankings, and seasonal titles

Half-year celebration rewards include 3,000 Oroberyls, five Basic HH Permits, the free 6-star weapon **Farsight**, and two seven-day login campaigns each granting five limited HH Permits.

## Brigandine: Abyss Console Demo July 30; Limited Edition Announced

**NIS America** has announced that the console demo for strategy RPG **Brigandine: Abyss** will release on **July 30** for **PlayStation 5**, **Xbox Series X|S**, and **Nintendo Switch 2**. The demo lets players complete the first two seasons of both the Gran Dragnica and Scarlet Will campaign routes, though save data cannot be transferred to the full game.

A **Limited Edition** at **$99.99** is available to pre-order now (PS5 disc and Switch 2 game-key card) through the NIS America Online Store. Contents: collector's box, hardcover art book, original CD soundtrack, acrylic character stands, a faction symbol pin set, and a cloth map.

The full game launches worldwide on **PC via Steam on August 26** (August 27 in Japan) and **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2 on August 27, 2026**. The Steam demo is already available, and NIS America confirmed the PC version will run at 60 FPS.

## Moonlight Peaks Launches on Switch, PC, and Mobile

**XSEED Games** published **Moonlight Peaks**, a gothic vampire life sim, on **July 7** for **Nintendo Switch**, **Nintendo Switch 2**, **PC**, **Android**, and **macOS**.

Players step into the role of a young vampire stepping out from the shadow of Count Dracula to build a new life in the magical mountain town of Moonlight Peaks. Because vampires cannot work in sunlight, all activity — farming, foraging, fishing, socializing, and exploration — takes place at night; when the sun rises, the player is automatically returned to their coffin. Systems include potion-crafting, shapeshift abilities for traversal, witchcraft spells, flower arranging, and more than two dozen romanceable characters.

Critical reception has been broadly positive, with review scores ranging from 7 to 9.5. The game is priced at **$39.99** on Nintendo Switch 2 and **$34.99** on all other platforms. A 15% launch discount is active through **July 13**.
