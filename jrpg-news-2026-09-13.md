## Nintendo Direct September 9 — Major JRPG Showcase Recap

Nintendo's September 9, 2026 Nintendo Direct was a standout showing for JRPG fans, featuring multiple announcements and release date confirmations across Square Enix, Koei Tecmo, and Level-5.

**Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** — Square Enix announced a full 3D remake of the 1995 Super Famicom RPG *Romancing SaGa 3*. Subtitled *Destinies United*, the game features all eight original protagonists (Mikhail, Monika, Katarina, Khalid, Julian, Thomas, Sarah, and Ellen), 20 recruitable companions, fully voiced cutscenes, upgraded visuals, overhauled combat, and selectable difficulty levels. It releases **February 16, 2027** for Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PS5, Xbox Series, and PC.

**The Apothecary Diaries: The False Imperial Brother** — Koei Tecmo revealed the first-ever console game based on *The Apothecary Diaries*, featuring original story content written by series creator Natsu Hyuga. Players control Maomao in a 3D world as she investigates the "Five Mysteries and Five Curses" plaguing the rear palace, pleasure district, and capital. The game features real-time 3D exploration for ingredient gathering alongside point-and-click mystery investigation sequences. It targets **early 2027** on Switch 2, Switch, PS5, and PC via Steam.

**Final Fantasy VII Revelation** — Digital pre-orders opened for the final entry in the Final Fantasy VII Remake series. The game's Spring 2027 release date is unchanged; the Direct offered a new trailer and confirmed the digital pre-order bonus: the **Buster Sword Legacy** weapon skin for Cloud.

## Level-5 Vision 2026 II "Dream" — Yo-kai Watch 2 on PC/PS5, Inazuma Eleven Sequel, and More

Level-5 held its **VISION 2026 II Dream** online showcase on **September 10 at 21:00 JST**, broadcast with English and Traditional Chinese subtitles on the Level5Ch YouTube channel.

Key announcements:

- **Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain** — Previously announced for Switch 2, the remake of the second *Yo-kai Watch* entry is now also coming to **PC and PlayStation 5**. Level-5 confirmed enhanced graphics and additional content beyond the original 3DS release. A release window was not specified.
- **Inazuma Eleven: Bold Revolution** — A direct sequel to *Inazuma Eleven: Victory Road* was announced, targeting **2027**. Few gameplay details were shared; Level-5 described it as continuing the story of the soccer RPG series with a "larger scale" than its predecessor.
- **Holy Horror Mansion** — A new horror-themed title from Level-5 targeting **PS5, Switch 2, and PC in 2027**. The brief showcase segment positioned it as a haunted-house adventure with puzzle elements.
- **Professor Layton and the New World of Steam** release date confirmed (see story below).
- Updates on **Decapolice**, **Snack World: Reloaded**, and the **Professor Layton and the Curious Village Remake** were also shown.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21291-level-5-vision-2026-ii-dream-presentation-to-be-broadcast-on-september-10) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/level-5-provides-game-updates/) · [ResetEra Roundup](https://www.resetera.com/threads/all-announcements-at-level-5-vision-2026-ii-dream.1629889/) · [NuclearCoffee](https://nuclearcoffee.org/round-up-everything-announced-in-the-level-5-vision-2026-ii-showcase/)

## Professor Layton and the New World of Steam Gets December 10 Release Date

Following its reveal at the Level-5 Vision 2026 II showcase on September 10, **Professor Layton and the New World of Steam** has been confirmed for **December 10, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The release date arrives more than three years after the game was first announced in February 2023, with multiple delays along the way. The December 10 date marks the franchise's debut on PlayStation hardware. The game follows a new mystery in an industrial steampunk city and stars a young Layton and Luke in a prequel timeline.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Reviews — 86 Metacritic, IGN 10/10

**Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched on **September 4, 2026** for Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC, marking the franchise's first mainline entry in 20 years. Review scores have settled with a broadly enthusiastic reception:

- **Metacritic: 86** (48 critic reviews)
- **OpenCritic: 85** (63 outlets)
- **IGN: 10/10** — the franchise's first perfect score from the outlet
- **Nintendo Life: 9/10** — "a successful revival of Capcom's samurai franchise that pushes its gameplay in an exciting new direction"

Critics are near-unanimous in praising the combat and visual presentation, though the 25-hour campaign's pacing drew more divided opinions, with some reviewers feeling it overstays its welcome in the final act. The game follows a new protagonist in feudal Japan dealing with demonic forces and the Oni gauntlet power system.

Sources: [Nintendo Life Review Roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-onimusha-way-of-the-sword-are-in) · [PC Gamer Review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review/) · [Giant Bomb Review](https://giantbomb.com/reviews/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review) · [GameMetaHub Roundup](https://www.gamemetahub.com/games/onimusha-way-of-the-sword/review-roundup-launch-verdicts)

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Launch for PS5 and Switch 2 — Strong Reception

**The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** launched on **September 10, 2026** for **PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2**, developed by PH3 and published by NIS America.

The new ports have been well received, with RPG Site calling them "amazing ports" in an early technical assessment. Technical highlights include:

- **PS5:** 4K 120fps on both base PS5 and PS5 Pro; praised as the best TV experience for the games
- **Switch 2 docked:** Multiple graphics modes, reaching up to 4K
- **Switch 2 handheld:** 1080p with 30fps, 60fps, or 120fps options
- **Mouse support** on Switch 2 via the USB-C port

Both titles are available digitally separately or as a physical bundle. A save data transfer feature allows players who already own the PC or original console versions to carry progress forward.

Sources: [RPG Site Review Feature](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21247-trails-from-zero-trails-to-azure-switch-2-ps5-review-new-features-save-transfer-durante-ph3) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/19/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-from-zero-and-trails-to-azure-lands-on-ps5-switch-2-on-september-10th/) · [Gamersyde Switch 2 Gameplay](https://www.gamersyde.com/news_our_switch_2_videos_of_trails_from_zero_and_trails_to_azure-24371_en.html)

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Four Days Away, Merchandise Announced

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** releases in **four days on September 17, 2026** for Nintendo Switch 2. Developer **Intelligent Systems** has added the title to its official "Completed Works" page, signaling the game is finished and gold.

**Merchandise** tied to the game's four protagonists — Cai, Theodora, Dietrich, and Leda — will go on sale simultaneously with the game on September 17 through the Nintendo online store. The collection includes acrylic standees, a tapestry, and an artbook.

The game is set in the world of *Three Houses* and features four separate routes that diverge based on protagonist choice, with each route running approximately 30–40 hours.

Sources: [Nintendo Life – IS Works Page](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/fire-emblem-dev-intelligent-systems-adds-fortunes-weave-to-its-completed-works) · [Siliconera – Merchandise](https://www.siliconera.com/first-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-merchandise-announced/) · [Turn Based Lovers – Release Info](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-gets-a-september-2026-release-date-and-collection-edition/)

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Also September 17

**The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** launches on **September 17, 2026** for **PC, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2**, published by NIS America.

The HD remake continues directly from *1st Chapter*, following Senior Bracer Estelle Bright as she searches for her missing adoptive brother Joshua across the kingdom of Liberl, with the secret society Ouroboros emerging as the central antagonist. Save data transferred from *1st Chapter* unlocks bonus items.

New mechanics introduced in *2nd Chapter* include **Brave Rush** — a quick-battle ability that chains characters' attacks together — alongside expanded Crafts and S-Crafts and new Art orbment options.

Sources: [RPGamer – Release Announcement](https://rpgamer.com/2026/04/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-launching-in-september/) · [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-gets-a-release-date-for-september-2026/) · [Steam Page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4225980/Trails_in_the_Sky_2nd_Chapter/)
