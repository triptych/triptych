# JRPG News — May 7, 2026

## Alabaster Dawn Launches in Steam Early Access Today

**Alabaster Dawn** — the CrossCode follow-up from Radical Fish Games — is now live in Steam Early Access as of today, May 7. The launch version includes the story through the middle of Chapter 2, totaling roughly **10 hours of content**, plus an optional roguelite side mode for added replayability. The full game will span 7 chapters (~40 hours), with Early Access expected to run at least 2 years before a 1.0 release targeting PC and consoles.

The game is **Steam Deck Verified**, launches with a 15% discount, and a CrossCode owner bundle adds another 5% off on top of that.

Story setup: the shadow of Nyx has warped the world into a wasteland and erased a god and their people. Player character Juno, an Outcast Chosen, must rebuild what was lost — puzzle-heavy exploration, gem-socketed weapon builds, and a Palate Level cooking system carry forward the design DNA of CrossCode.

## FF7 Rebirth Switch 2 Demo: Resolution and Performance Breakdown

Now that Square Enix has released the **free Switch 2 demo** for Final Fantasy VII Rebirth (45.1 GB, covering the first two chapters), technical analysis is in:

- **Handheld internal resolution:** 1344×756 (max) to 672×380 (min), with DLSS doing aggressive upscaling
- **Docked internal resolution:** 1920×1080 (max) to 960×540 (min)
- **Frame rate target:** 30 fps — frequently dips into the low-to-mid 20s in open-world areas, with a recorded low of 19 fps in stress cases
- **Known issues:** texture pop-in, micro-stutters between scenes, visibly less-populated areas vs. PS5

Side-by-side comparison video from Nintendo Life pits Switch 2 against Xbox Series S and PS5; the Switch 2 version holds up better than many expected in enclosed environments, though open-world traversal remains the weak point. Impressions from preview outlets generally land as cautiously positive — the ambition of the port is acknowledged even where the execution has rough edges.

The full game launches on Switch 2 (and Xbox) on **June 3, 2026**.

## FF7 Rebirth Switch 2 Pre-Orders "Extremely Well" — Director Cites Emotional Weight

In a separate GoNintendo interview, **Final Fantasy VII Rebirth** director Naoki Hamaguchi described pre-order reception for the Switch 2 version as going "extremely well" and spoke to the emotional significance of bringing the trilogy to Nintendo fans for the first time. He also detailed the technical challenges of the port: the DLSS pipeline required significant engine work given Rebirth's heavy use of Unreal Engine 5's Lumen lighting.

Hamaguchi confirmed the game has earned **over 125 perfect review scores** and **40 Game of the Year awards** since its original PS5 launch in February 2024, framing the Switch 2 port as a chance for a second wave of players to experience it.

## The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales — New Story and Gameplay Details

With six weeks to go before its **June 18, 2026** launch, Square Enix and Team Asano have shared an expanded look at *The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales* — the studio's first real-time action RPG after Octopath Traveler and the Bravely Default series.

New details revealed:
- Story follows **Elliot**, a traveling merchant drawn into a world-spanning conflict tied to ancient memory and a mysterious clock called the Millennium
- Party system allows three-character co-op-style switching in combat with distinct weapon archetypes
- **Quest system** is non-linear, with branching paths that permanently lock out some content depending on player choices
- Developed jointly by **Team Asano** and **Claytechworks** (the Monster Hunter Stories studio) under Square Enix Creative Studio 5
- Platforms: Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC

This is shaping up as the studio's most ambitious release yet in terms of scope; the Octopath pedigree combined with Claytechworks' action experience makes it one of the higher-profile JRPG releases of the summer.
