## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct — True Protagonist Revealed, Manga Adaptation Announced

Nintendo aired a roughly **20-minute Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct** on **August 4, 2026**, delivering the largest single dump of story and systems information since the game's initial announcement.

The headline reveal is the game's **true protagonist**. Fortune's Weave is structured around four heroes — **Cai, Dietrich, Theodora, and Leda** — competing in the Heroic Games, a gladiatorial tournament where the winner's wish is granted. But the Direct confirmed that the framing story takes place **five years in the future**, where the demon god **Balor** has returned and destroyed the world. The progenitor god **Sothis** summons a nameless hero clad in white — the player-named character, defaulting to **Eshmel** — as the only soul capable of stopping Balor. Eshmel can either attempt to face Balor alone (described as deliberately near-impossible) or use the time-traveling powers of the goddess **Fortuna** to travel back to 1449 and shape the fates of the four tournament champions so they're ready to stand against Balor in the future.

The four champion storylines run in parallel and can be played in any order, with progress on each saved independently. Systems detailed in the Direct include **class branching tied to each champion's background**, a **Bond Weave** relationship system that unlocks passive abilities in battle, and a **Foresight** mechanic that lets Eshmel briefly see two turns ahead once per chapter.

Following the Direct, Nintendo and Shueisha announced a **manga adaptation** of Fortune's Weave. **Kazuro Kyou** — who handled the Fire Emblem: Engage manga adaptation — will draw the series. The **prologue chapter (Chapter 0)** will appear in the October 2026 issue of Shueisha's **Saikyo Jump**, due out **September 4**, and will also run on Shonen Jump+.

Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave launches **September 17, 2026** exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2**.

## Suikoden: STAR LEAP Launches in Japan — First New Entry in 14 Years

**Konami** released **Suikoden: STAR LEAP** on **iOS and Android in Japan on August 7, 2026**, making it the first entirely new entry in the Suikoden franchise in 14 years. A Suikoden Live broadcast aired on August 6 (the day before launch) to preview the launch build and first in-game event details.

STAR LEAP is set in **Solar Year 453** — four years before the events of the original *Suikoden* — in an entirely new story featuring a new cast of **108 Stars of Destiny** and new runes. Konami bills it as a standalone entry accessible to players with no prior series knowledge, while longtime fans will find historical threads connecting to the world they know.

The game is **free-to-play** with optional purchases and launches Japan-exclusive for now. Konami confirmed a **staggered global rollout**: Japan mobile is first, an international mobile release follows, and the **Steam PC version** comes last. Specific dates for the global and PC releases have not been announced, but the Steam page is live and marked "Coming Soon" with language support for English, French, German, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Promotional Trailer Out, September 17 Launch Confirmed

**Falcom** and **GungHo Online Entertainment** released a full **promotional trailer** for **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** in early August, providing the most complete look yet at the remake's updated visuals, story pacing, and voice work ahead of its **September 17, 2026** global launch.

The game picks up immediately after *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter*, following **Estelle Bright** as she pursues **Joshua**, who vanished in the aftermath of the first game amid a growing continental conspiracy. The remake uses **fully 3D visuals** and refined gameplay while preserving the script and pacing of the original. Voice acting has been updated in both English and Japanese for the new platform versions.

GungHo also confirmed the **English voice cast** now stands at **15 named characters**, with seven new additions revealed this month.

*Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter* launches **September 17** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. Text language support varies by platform: Steam supports Japanese, English, German, French, and Spanish; console versions support English, German, French, and Spanish.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword — "Severing Fates" Gameplay Trailer

**Capcom** released a new gameplay trailer titled **"Severing Fates"** for **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** on **August 6**, three weeks before the game's **September 4** launch — the first new Onimusha in 20 years.

The trailer focuses on **Miyamoto Musashi** visiting **Yasui Konpiragu Shrine** in Kyoto, a real-world landmark historically associated with severing bonds of fate. In the game, a twisted supernatural presence has corrupted the shrine, preying on wishmakers. The sequence demonstrates how the game opens up after its early chapters, with missions flowing into wider locations that offer new challenges and a growing arsenal of demon-arm abilities to unlock.

Capcom has described the game as using a **"broad linear structure"** — a series of connected corridors and areas rather than a fully open world, but with significantly more lateral space than a traditional linear action game. A **demo is currently available** for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC; the Switch 2 demo has not yet been released.

*Onimusha: Way of the Sword* launches **September 4, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PC (Steam, Epic, Microsoft Store)**.

## Brigandine: Abyss — Launches August 26 on PC, August 27 on Switch 2

**Happinet**'s strategy RPG **Brigandine: Abyss** is set to release this week: **Steam on August 26**, followed by **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series on August 27**. The Switch 2 physical edition uses a **game-key card** rather than a standard cartridge.

Set in an entirely new world disconnected from prior *Brigandine* entries, *Abyss* is designed to be approachable for newcomers. The game features a **Story Mode** centered on six competing nations and a standalone **Mission Mode** that expands the conflict to a **24-faction war** — one of the largest faction counts in the franchise's history.

Combat plays out on a **hexagonal grid** during Attack Phases, demanding players account for skills, elemental attributes, and the development of **Monsters bound to officers**. A **Deluxe Edition** is available for Switch 2 and PS5 bundling a mini art book and digital soundtrack.

## Entropy — JRPG-Inspired Dark Fantasy Enters Steam Early Access Tomorrow

**Lovely Hellplace**'s dark fantasy turn-based RPG **Entropy** launches on **Steam Early Access on August 18** — tomorrow from today's date. The game carries the late-90s 3D aesthetic of developer Lovely Hellplace's previous title *Dread Delusion* and draws its mechanical DNA from classic JRPGs.

Players control a member of a theatre troupe in the town of **Draenog** whose performance is interrupted by a demon attack. The group gathers itself and embarks on an adventure to stop an encroaching demonic force. The Early Access version covers **Act 1 of three** planned acts; developer and publisher **DreadXP** estimate approximately one year in Early Access.

A **demo** is currently available on Steam.

## Captain Tsubasa II: World Fighters — August 28 Global Launch on Switch and PC

**Bandai Namco**'s **Captain Tsubasa II: World Fighters** launches **August 27 in Japan and August 28 worldwide** on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (Xbox Series also confirmed).

## Expeditions: Samurai Enters Steam Early Access

**THQ Nordic** and developer **Campfire Cabal** launched **Expeditions: Samurai** into **Steam Early Access on August 7** alongside the THQ Nordic Digital Showcase. The game is planned for a full **Q1 2027** release.

Set in **feudal Japan around 1600** — in an alternate history where the player character arrives before William Adams, preventing Adams from becoming Japan's first European samurai — *Expeditions: Samurai* is a party-based tactical RPG featuring **turn-based combat, stealth, diplomacy, and full two-player co-op**. Campfire Cabal is founded by veterans of Logic Artists, developers of the previous *Expeditions* entries (*Conquistador, Viking, Rome*).

Early Access reviews describe the game as a hybrid of *Expeditions: Rome*'s narrative structure with tactical elements reminiscent of *Jagged Alliance 3* and the stealth of *Shadow Tactics: Blades of the Shogun*.
