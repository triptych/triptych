## Onimusha: Way of the Sword — Reviews Land Ahead of Thursday Launch; Metacritic 86, IGN 10/10

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launches in two days on **September 4**, and review embargoes have lifted early this week, revealing a strong critical response for the franchise's first new entry in over two decades.

The game holds a **Metacritic score of 86** across 48 critic reviews and an **OpenCritic score of 85** from 63 outlets. **IGN** awarded the game a rare **10/10**, calling it "the most satisfying swordplay I can remember in a video game" and marking the first time the publication has given the franchise a perfect score. **Nintendo Life** gave it **9/10**, calling it "a successful revival of Capcom's samurai franchise that pushes its gameplay in an exciting new direction while still retaining its core identity." **Forbes** (9/10) and **RPG Site** (9/10) also praised the title, while **GameRant** and **Siliconera** were more measured at **7/10**, noting that the combat's brilliance is somewhat offset by uneven progression and exploration.

**The Gamer** remarked that it "completes Capcom's trilogy of phenomenal releases in 2026, and is arguably the best of the lot." The title launches on **Nintendo Switch 2** and **PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Xbox Series X|S) this Thursday.

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure — Switch 2 and PC Versions Eight Days Out

**NIS America**'s enhanced ports of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** for **Nintendo Switch 2** and **PC via Steam** arrive on **September 10** — eight days from now — and the publisher has finalized all edition details.

The new versions run at **4K resolution with 120 FPS** and include **Nintendo Switch 2 mouse support** for menu navigation. Both titles are available digitally at **$39.99 each**, while a physical **Deluxe Edition** bundling both games retails for **$69.99** and includes a digital soundtrack and a collector's card set. A premium **Limited Edition** at **$109.99** — available via NIS America directly — contains a collector's box, *Collected Works* art book, curated soundtrack selection, holographic collector's card set, Friends of the Force acrylic stand, S.S.S. iron-on insignias, and a Mishelam Wonderland coaster set.

The Crossbell duology bridges the gap between the *Liberl* arc (*Trails in the Sky*) and the *Erebonia* arc (*Trails of Cold Steel*) and has historically been the hardest entry point for Western fans due to its late localization. The Switch 2 and PC versions represent the most accessible and polished version of both games to date.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — September 17 Launch Confirmed for Switch, Switch 2, and Steam

**Nihon Falcom** and **Bandai Namco** have finalized details for the worldwide simultaneous release of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** on **September 17** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam** (also PS5).

The game is a ground-up remake of *Trails in the Sky SC* — the 2006 direct sequel to the original *Trails in the Sky* — and continues the story of **Estelle Bright**, now a senior bracer, who sets out across the kingdom of Liberl to find her adoptive brother Joshua and confront the machinations of the secret society Ouroboros. The remake carries over the overhauled combat and expanded side content introduced in *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter* (which launched September 2025 to mark the series' 20th anniversary) and adds new dialogue and scenes.

Physical and digital editions will be available in the West for Switch 2 and Switch; in Japan, physical copies are limited to Switch 2. A free demo carrying progress into the full game has been available on all platforms since **August 20**.

## Another Eden Begins — Masato Kato's Offline JRPG Launches September 17

**Wright Flyer Studios** / **Studio Prisma** have released final details on **Another Eden Begins**, the standalone offline JRPG based on the *Another Eden* universe, launching **September 17** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

The game is directed and written by **Masato Kato** — veteran of *Chrono Trigger*, *Xenogears*, and *Chrono Cross* — with the main theme composed by **Yasunori Mitsuda** (*Xenogears*, *Chrono Cross*) and additional music from **Procyon Studios**. Unlike the original *Another Eden*, which remains a free-to-play live-service game, *Begins* is a fully self-contained premium title with a defined beginning, middle, and end.

The story follows **Aldo** and his younger sister **Feinne** in the village of Baruoki. After Feinne is abducted by the Beast King, Aldo is pulled through a rift in spacetime during battle and transported 800 years into the future. The campaign features **18 companions**, each with individual backstories and side chapters, and **over 10 different endings** based on player choices. The game is fully voice-acted throughout.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Intelligent Systems Marks Game Complete; September 17 Launch Two Weeks Away

With **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** now two weeks from its **September 17** launch on **Nintendo Switch 2**, developer **Intelligent Systems** has updated its official website to list the game under its completed works — a low-key signal that development has fully wrapped and the game has gone gold.

The game is set in the **Dagdan Empire** in the year 1454, on the brink of ruin following the resurrection of **Balor**, a demon god. The goddess **Fortuna** — blessed with the power to move through time — offers the player's soul the chance to alter the fates of four heroes drawn from the empire's past and lead them to fight Balor in the present. The four protagonists are:

- **Cai** — a young boy seeking to free his imprisoned father
- **Theodora** — a queen fighting to protect her homeland
- **Dietrich** — a swordsman in search of stronger opponents
- **Leda** — a musician driven by revenge

Each character leads their own distinct route through the game. Combat centers on the **Heroic Games**, gladiatorial arena battles that serve as the story's political backdrop. A **Special Collection Edition** including an art book, map poster, art cards, and steelbook launches alongside the standard edition.

## Dragon Quest XI S Switch 2 Definitive Edition — New Story Chapters, $39.99, No Save Transfer

**Square Enix**'s **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age — Nintendo Switch 2 Definitive Edition** arrives **September 24** at **$39.99** (digital), and this week Square Enix confirmed the port's full scope. The release is a new standalone purchase — a **save data transfer from the original Switch version is not supported**, and the publisher has confirmed there are no plans to sell an upgrade path from the Switch version.

The Definitive Edition adds **new story chapters** placing supporting party members front and center, beyond the content included in the original *XI S*. Technical upgrades include support for both **"Graphics" and "Performance"** output modes and a significantly larger file size — **23.4 GB** versus the original Switch version's 14 GB. The game retains the ability to switch between full 3D and 2D pixel art presentation, as well as the full orchestral score added for *XI S*. The physical edition ships as a **Game Key Card**, meaning no game data is stored on the cartridge.
