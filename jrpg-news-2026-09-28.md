## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Reviews Land — Metacritic 89, Multiple Perfect Scores

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems**' **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** released on **September 17, 2026** exclusively for **Nintendo Switch 2**, and the critical reception has been overwhelming.

The game holds an **89 on Metacritic**, with **13 outlets awarding perfect scores** including **IGN** (10/10) and **VGC** (5/5). IGN wrote: *"Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave builds upon its strategic arts in ways I couldn't have anticipated."* GameSpot (9/10) called it *"a sprawling masterpiece of a strategy RPG."*

Reviewers consistently praise the four interconnected protagonists, the deep tactical combat, the orchestral score, and the game's enormous scope. It is widely considered one of the best titles in the series to date and one of 2026's strongest overall releases.

Sources: [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-reviews-scores/) · [Nintendo Life Review Roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-are-in) · [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Fire-Emblem-Fortune-s-Weave-is-a-must-grab-according-to-early-reviews.1402032.0.html) · [G2A News](https://www.g2a.com/news/game-spotlights/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-reviews/)

## Tokyo Game Show 2026 — Major JRPG Highlights

**Tokyo Game Show 2026** wrapped up this past week, bringing several notable announcements for the genre.

### Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Announced

**Square Enix** revealed **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United**, a full **3D remake** of the 1995 Super Famicom RPG. The remake features fully overhauled visuals and new voice acting in cutscenes while preserving the series' freeform structure. Players choose from **eight protagonists** and tackle quests in any order using the signature Glimmer system for ability acquisition. Combat uses a **turn-order timeline** and visible field encounters. The game launches **February 16, 2027** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PC, and PS5**.

## Dragon Quest XI S Switch 2 Edition Launches — Reviews Round Up

**Square Enix**'s **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 24, 2026**. The port runs at **4K/60fps docked** and brings the game in line with other console releases, with options to toggle between performance and quality modes.

Reviews are positive for newcomers — DualShockers' performance breakdown calls it "outstanding at 60 FPS" — though returning players note the upgrade is incremental. The Definitive Edition content (orchestral soundtrack, 2D mode, bonus story chapters, faster battle speed) is all present. **Nintendo World Report** published a detailed technical comparison alongside its review.

## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow — Mixed Reviews on Launch

**Qliphah in Providence's Shadow** (PS5, Nintendo Switch, PC) launched **September 24** to a polarized response. The game is a visual novel with an unusual counter-based RPG combat system set in a distorted supernatural Tokyo, following delivery courier **Soma Hoshigami** and investigator **Rena Hizuki**.

Critics generally find the combat concept intriguing but the execution uneven. **RPGFan** and **Noisy Pixel** praised the deep, experimental combat mechanics as enticing. **DualShockers** gave it a **5.5/10**, finding the early intrigue quickly giving way to repetition and dense lore dumps that crowd out storytelling. **Game8** called it "impressively innovative, weird, and interesting." The game currently holds a mixed score on **Metacritic**.

Sources: [RPGFan Review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow/) · [RPGamer Review](https://rpgamer.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [DualShockers Review](https://www.dualshockers.com/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [Noisy Pixel Review](https://noisypixel.net/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [Final Weapon Review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/24/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review-steam/)

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure — PS5/Switch 2 Launch Recap

**The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **Trails to Azure** launched for **PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 10, 2026**, published by **NIS America** with technical work by **PH3** (Durante's studio). The ports run at **4K / 120fps** and support mouse input on Switch 2, with new texture-style options and save transfer from the original Switch and PS4 versions.

Loot Level Chill described them as "two of the best JRPGs now with perfect Switch 2 versions." RPG Site's in-depth review praised the new features while flagging that there is no discounted upgrade path for existing owners — each game must be purchased at full price separately, though a physical bundle was released simultaneously.

Sources: [Gematsu Date Announcement](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-from-zero-and-the-legend-of-heroes-trails-to-azure-for-ps5-switch-2-launch-september-10) · [RPG Site Launch](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21353-trails-from-zero-to-azure-ps5-switch-2-out-now-pc-steam-switch-1-patch-new-september-2026) · [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21247-trails-from-zero-trails-to-azure-switch-2-ps5-review-new-features-save-transfer-durante-ph3) · [Loot Level Chill](https://lootlevelchill.com/reviews/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-from-zero-trails-to-azure-switch-2-review/)

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — April 8, 2027 Date Confirmed

**Square Enix** confirmed **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** will launch on **April 8, 2027** for **PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC** at PlayStation's State of Play on September 3. The announcement was accompanied by a new trailer and extended gameplay footage. Key details revealed:

- A **fully controllable airship (Highwind)** with the ability to parachute directly onto the open-world map — a first for the trilogy
- The **FITS (Function Integrated Tactical Suitwear)** system carries over from the previous reveal, letting characters equip special gear that unlocks classic Final Fantasy job skills
- Two **story DLC expansions** are planned post-launch: one focusing on **Vincent Valentine and the Turks** (Winter 2027) and one spotlighting **Sephiroth** (Spring 2028)
