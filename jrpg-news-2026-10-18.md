## Tales of Eternia Remastered Launches to Mixed-Positive Reviews

Bandai Namco's **Tales of Eternia Remastered** officially launched on **October 16** for Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam (also PS5, PS4, and Xbox Series X|S). The remaster of the classic 2000 action RPG opened to a **77 OpenCritic score** with 71% of critics recommending it.

Reviewers praised the real-time 2D combat, the story, the updated English script and voice acting, and new quality-of-life options including the ability to toggle between remastered and original visuals and audio. Criticisms focused on the dated world map and environments. [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/?p=1117297) called it "a much-needed remaster," while [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151003/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review-score) was harsher, criticizing the upscaling quality. [Final Boss](https://finalboss.io/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review-a-story-worth-the-friction) praised the new English script and voice acting and recommended it primarily for story-focused RPG fans.

The remaster adds a Graphics Mode and SFX Mode, two new difficulty levels including an extreme New Game+ mode, destination icons, and an option to disable random encounters.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20605-tales-of-eternia-remastered-launches-on-october-16-for-ps5-ps4-xbox-switch-switch-2-and-steam) · [Bandai Namco](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/tales-of/news/journey-between-two-worlds-begins-tales-of-eternia-remastered-october) · [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/21435/view)

## Eternal Anima — New Hands-On Preview and Producer Interview Published

Marvelous's time-travel JRPG **Eternal Anima** — developed with Final Fantasy veterans including director **Katsuhisa Higuchi** and scenario writer **Kazushige Nojima** — received substantial new coverage this week ahead of its **March 4, 2027 release** for Nintendo Switch 2 and PC via Steam (also PS5 and Xbox Series X|S).

[Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/eternal-anima-hands-on-gameplay-preview) published a hands-on gameplay preview alongside a [producer/director interview](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/eternal-anima-interview-with-producer-makoto-shioda-and-development-director-katsuhisa-higuchi-future-sight-battles-time-travel-and-more) with producer **Makoto Shioda** and development director Higuchi covering the **Future Sight** battle system and the game's world.

Key gameplay details:

- The **Future Sight** system reveals enemy turn order each round, letting players counter, delay, or cancel incoming actions before they execute.
- Protagonist **Wade** uses **Psychometry** — the power to read memories from ancient artifacts — to inherit the skills and classes of past heroes, with weapon types unlocking powers as the player invests in them.
- Story setup: Wade lives in **Urvis Ael**, humanity's last city after an apocalyptic flood drowned all four continents. The city outlaws hope for a better future as a capital offense.

[Pocket Tactics' preview](https://www.pockettactics.com/eternal-anima/preview) found the game solid, praising the weapon unlock system while noting the art style as the weakest element. Physical editions are priced at $49.99 (PS5) and $59.99 (Switch 2), with limited editions at $79.99 and $89.99 respectively.

## Edge of Memories Gets Official Release Date Despite Developer's Closure

**Nacon** has officially set **November 5, 2026** as the launch date for **Edge of Memories** on PC via Steam, with Deluxe Edition early access starting **November 3**. The announcement confirms the game will ship despite the July liquidation of its developer, **Midgar Studio**.

Nacon confirmed the game's content was finalized before Midgar's closure, with remaining work limited to final polish. This makes *Edge of Memories* Midgar's final release — a bittersweet capstone for the 18-year-old Montpellier studio. The game was demoed as recently as Nacon Connect in May 2026 and was in the final production stretch when the studio closed.

Note: one earlier source cited a December 3 delay date, but [RPGSite's coverage](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21448-edge-of-memories-release-date) citing Nacon directly gives November 5 as the current official date.

## Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga Announced for Switch and PC

**Taito** and publisher **Clear River Games** announced **Lufia I & II: The Sinistrals Saga** on **September 20** during the **PC Gaming Show Tokyo Direct**. The collection bundles the 1993 original *Lufia & the Fortress of Doom* and its 1995 prequel *Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals* in what will be the first official PC release for either title.

The collection is targeting **2027** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PS5). Physical editions are planned for Switch 2, Switch, and PS5, with details to follow. Taito confirmed the collection retains the original pixel art while adding new gameplay functions suited to modern hardware; specifics have not yet been announced. The trailer shows optional CRT scanlines.
