## Final Fantasy VII: Ever Crisis to End Service October 6, 2026

**Square Enix** announced on **July 7** that **Final Fantasy VII: Ever Crisis** will shut down on **October 6, 2026 at 11 p.m. PDT**, approximately three years after the mobile and PC title's launch.

Paid sales of **Red Crystals** (the game's premium currency) ended immediately with the announcement, though existing balances can still be spent in the in-game store until servers close. Refunds for unused currency are being offered only to players in **Taiwan**.

Producer **Yoshinori Kitase** cited the high cost of maintaining high-quality weapon and costume visuals, the volume of new equipment required to sustain the gacha economy, and the difficulty of preserving gameplay balance across a growing character roster as the primary reasons for ending service.

Before shutdown, Square Enix will add the previously unreleased **Before Crisis: Final Fantasy VII** story as playable content — its first-ever appearance in a game — alongside final weapon upgrades and seasonal quests tied to the game's third-anniversary celebration.

## NTE: Neverness to Everness Launched on Steam July 8 — A Week Ahead of Schedule

**NTE: Neverness to Everness**, the free-to-play supernatural urban open-world RPG by **Hotta Studio**, launched on **Steam and the Epic Games Store on July 8**, one week earlier than the July 16 date previously announced. The Steam debut arrived simultaneously with the **Version 1.2 Update**, which introduced the new **999 Nights** game mode.

The game was previously available on iOS and Android. The PC release includes full **cross-play and cross-progression**, allowing players to link existing mobile accounts and transfer all characters, inventory, and story progress directly.

Set in the city of Hethereau, players take on the role of an unlicensed anomaly hunter who teams up with a diverse cast of companions to unravel the city's mysteries, built in **Unreal Engine 5**.

## Disgaea Mayhem Demo Live on Switch and Switch 2

**NIS America** launched a free demo for **Disgaea Mayhem** on **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 on July 9**, paired with a new demo trailer. The demo is also available on **PlayStation 5** and **Steam**.

The demo includes **three stages** using all seven of the game's weapon types (fists, swords, spears, axes, bows, guns, and staves), each ending in a boss fight. Progress does not carry over to the full game.

*Disgaea Mayhem* is an action-focused spin-off that swaps the series' trademark grid-based strategy for real-time combat. It follows mercenary **N.A.**, summoned by overlord **Tichelle** to track down the thief who stole her late father's legendary First Radiance Pudding. The game launched in Japan in January 2026 and arrives in North America and Europe on **July 23, 2026** for **Switch, Switch 2, PS5, and PC via Steam**.

## Persona 4 Revival: Yosuke Character Trailer Released; Producer Confirms Scene Updates

**ATLUS** released a **Yosuke Hanamura character trailer** for **Persona 4 Revival** on **July 13**. Alongside the trailer, producer **Kazuhisa Wada** confirmed that several of Yosuke's scenes have been updated for the remake.

Wada described Yosuke as "a little bit insensitive in terms of how he treats outsiders sometimes" and said the team chose to "lighten that up and make it a little bit more fitting for the world we live in now." He clarified the changes are confined mostly to Yosuke's dialogue and do not affect the overall story, the core personalities of characters, or how they act overall.

One cited example: a line involving the protagonist being "good with his hands" has been replaced with "Actually, you do strike me as someone who'd be good at that."

## Game Freak Releases Beast of Reincarnation Combat Overview Trailer

**Fictions** (publisher) and **Game Freak** (developer) released a five-minute **Combat Overview trailer** for **Beast of Reincarnation** on **July 14**, giving the most detailed look yet at the action RPG's systems ahead of its **August 4** launch.

The game blends real-time swordplay with a command-based overlay. Protagonist **Emma** fights in real time, while her partner **Koo** uses **Bloom Arts** — techniques selected from a turn-slowing command menu powered by the **FP gauge**. Parrying enemy attacks is the primary method for building FP.

Every enemy displays two bars: a standard health gauge and a **Down Gauge** that fills from sustained pressure and parries. Filling the Down Gauge creates an opening for a finishing attack. A skill tree covers three branches: **Tracking Shot**, **Hair**, and **Entanglement Overdrive**.

*Beast of Reincarnation* launches **August 4** on **PC via Steam and the Microsoft Store**, and is available day one via **Xbox Game Pass**.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/beast-of-reincarnation-combat-overview-trailer) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/beast-of-reincarnation-gameplay-trailer-prioritizes-combat/) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/beast-of-reincarnation-combat-trailer-parries-bloom-arts-boss-battles/) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4sUz96KJHYA)

## Culdcept BEGINS Launches Today — First Original Entry in a Decade

**NEOS Corporation** released **Culdcept BEGINS** worldwide today (**July 16, 2026**) for **Nintendo Switch 2** (physical and digital) and **Nintendo Switch** (digital only). A **PC via Steam** release follows in **Q4 2026**.

*Culdcept BEGINS* is the first all-new title in the card-based board game RPG series in approximately ten years. ## Hamster Brings Glory of Heracles II to Switch 2 via Console Archives

**Hamster Corporation** has added **Hercules no Eikou II: Taitan no Metsubou** (**Glory of Heracles II: The Fall of the Titan**) to its **Console Archives** service today (**July 16**) on **Nintendo Switch 2** and **PlayStation 5**, priced at **$7.99**.

The game was originally released for the **Famicom in 1989** by **Data East**. Unlike the first *Glory of Heracles*, the sequel introduced a **party system** and notably featured a scenario written by **Kazushige Nojima** — who would later write *Final Fantasy VII*, *Final Fantasy X*, and many other Square Enix titles. Game content is in **Japanese only**; the UI and manual are available in English.

The first *Glory of Heracles* (1987) joined Console Archives on **May 21, 2026**.

## Silver Palace "Dichotomy" Beta Wraps Up; Victorian ARPG Highlighted at Anime Expo

**Elementa Games**' open-world action RPG **Silver Palace** concluded its **"Dichotomy" Beta Test** today (July 16), following a featured showcase at **Anime Expo 2026** (July 2–5) where it was named one of the best games at the show by multiple outlets.

Set in the prosperous metropolis of **Silvernia**, a city powered by an element called **Silverium**, players take on the role of a detective who returns after a three-year absence to find the city entangled in faction conspiracies, criminal syndicates, and a deadly royal secret. Combat involves building **Activation Energy** through basic attacks to fire ranged skills (including guns that stun enemies), then spending accumulated **Reactor Energy** to switch between a party of four characters and execute **Tactical Assault** abilities.

No release date has been announced. The game is targeting **PC and consoles**.
