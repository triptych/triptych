## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Launch Trailer and Early Reviews

[**Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok**](https://relink-ragnarok.granbluefantasy.com/en/) releases **tomorrow, July 9**, and **Cygames** dropped its [launch trailer](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/granblue-fantasy-relink-endless-ragnarok-launch-trailer) today alongside an early review from Nintendo World Report.

Endless Ragnarok is a full standalone release (not just DLC) priced at **$59.99** on Switch 2 and PC. Existing owners of Granblue Fantasy: Relink can purchase a cheaper **Upgrade Kit for $29.99**. The new version adds a new story, the **Conflux** solo mode, new co-op quest tiers, summon mechanics, master traits for all characters, and multi-platform **crossplay** — a first for the franchise. A free demo launched on June 18 and remains available on Steam and the Switch 2 eShop.

Reviews note strong improvements to the endgame loop and the crossplay implementation, though the core action RPG structure is unchanged from the 2024 original.

## Console Archives: Rhapsody: A Musical Adventure Out Tomorrow on Switch 2

[**Hamster Corporation**](https://www.consolearchives.com/en/title/csa-0021/) is bringing **Rhapsody: A Musical Adventure** to the Switch 2's Console Archives service on **July 9** for **$14.99 / ¥1,500**.

The game — originally released on Nintendo 64 in 1998 and developed by Nippon Ichi Software (the studio behind Disgaea) — is a musical-themed RPG following **Cornet**, a girl who can magically befriend puppets, on a quest to rescue a kidnapped prince. It's a short, whimsical RPG with sung musical numbers embedded in the story, and was ahead of its time as a genre experiment.

The Console Archives version includes customizable button mapping, rapid-fire settings, and multiple save slots. This marks the first time the original N64 version has been officially available on a modern platform.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/console-archives-rhapsody-a-musical-adventure-launches-july-9) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/rhapsody-a-musical-adventure-is-this-weeks-console-archives-game-on-nintendo-switch-2/) · [Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2026/07/this-disney-inspired-ps1-rpg-from-the-creator-of-disgaea-is-making-its-way-to-nintendo-switch-2-and-ps5) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/every-console-archives-game-on-nintendo-switch-2)

## Brigandine: Abyss Console Demo Dated for July 30

A playable demo for [**Brigandine: Abyss**](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/brigandine-abyss-demo-for-ps5-xbox-series-and-switch-2-launches-july-30) — the revival of the late-1990s fantasy war strategy RPG series — has been dated for **July 30, 2026** on Nintendo Switch 2. The demo was announced today.

The demo covers the first two seasons of both the **Gran Dragnica** and **Scarlet Will** story campaigns. Save data from the demo does **not** carry over to the full release. A PC demo has been available on Steam since May.

The full game launches **August 26** digitally and **August 27** physically. Bitjump Games is publishing the Switch 2 **Deluxe Edition**, which is available for pre-order now.

An RPGamer preview published this week calls it a faithful and modernized take on the strategy RPG formula, with a strong focus on multi-faction territorial conquest and Rune-based unit customization.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/brigandine-abyss-demo-for-ps5-xbox-series-and-switch-2-launches-july-30) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/brigandine-abyss-demo-on-the-way-to-nintendo-switch-2/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20868-brigandine-abyss-ps5-switch-2-demo-release-date-xbox) · [RPGamer Preview](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/brigandine-abyss-preview/)
