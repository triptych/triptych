## Another Eden Begins — Aksys Games Announces Western Physical Edition for Switch and Switch 2

**Aksys Games** revealed at **Anime Expo 2026** that it will publish a western physical edition of **Another Eden Begins** for **Nintendo Switch** and **Nintendo Switch 2** in **2027**. The announcement adds a physical option for western players on top of the game's previously confirmed digital release.

Digitally, **Another Eden Begins** launches **September 17, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** worldwide. A physical edition in Japan also ships September 17. The western physical arrives separately in 2027 and will be handled entirely by Aksys.

The game is developed by **Studio Prisma** and is a standalone time-traveling turn-based RPG penned by **Masato Kato** (scenario writer of *Chrono Trigger*, *Chrono Cross*, and the original *Another Eden*), with music composed by **Yasunori Mitsuda** and Procyon Studios. The main story is fully voiced, and a New Game+ mode unlocks after completion with over **ten distinct endings** shaped by player choices throughout the run.

The Aksys AX panel also announced western releases for several other Studio Prisma and Gotcha Gotcha Games titles, including visual novel games *Bounty Sisters*, *Illusion of Itehari -trail-*, and *Olympia Soirée -Catharsis-*.

## Cthulhu Mythos ADV: Lunatic Whispers & The Isle of Ubohoth — Coming to Switch in the West This Holiday

**Aksys Games** announced it will release **Cthulhu Mythos ADV: Lunatic Whispers** and **Cthulhu Mythos ADV: The Isle of Ubohoth** — a two-game collection developed by **Gotcha Gotcha Games** — for **Nintendo Switch** in the west during **Holiday 2026**. Both games are being sold together as a bundle; additional platforms beyond Switch are listed as to be determined.

The games are visual novel / tabletop RPG hybrids set in the Cthulhu Mythos:

- **Lunatic Whispers** is a dice-driven adventure in which characters' statistics, luck, and rolls determine how the story branches, following a protagonist drawn into an eldritch mystery.
- **The Isle of Ubohoth** takes a similar dice-roll mechanic but places the protagonist on a cursed island in the Seto Inland Sea, where uncovering the island's mysteries and breaking the curse depends entirely on the roll of the dice.

Both titles were developed by Gotcha Gotcha Games, the RPG Maker studio behind the *Cthulhu Mythos ADV* series. They originally launched for PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, and Nintendo Switch in **Japan on October 9, 2025**. The PC versions of *Lunatic Whispers* (2022) and *The Isle of Ubohoth* (2024) have been available on Steam in English for some time; this announcement brings the console versions to western players for the first time.

## Sea of Stars: Sunset Edition Now Available via Humble Choice July 2026

**Sea of Stars: Sunset Edition** — the expanded version of Sabotage Studio's critically acclaimed turn-based RPG — is one of the eight games in the **Humble Choice July 2026** bundle, available starting today, **July 7**, through **August 4, 2026**, for the standard subscription price of **$14.99**.

*Sea of Stars* is widely regarded as a modern high point for the JRPG genre: a turn-based RPG inspired by *Chrono Trigger* and *Super Mario RPG* that adds a lock-breaking mechanic to combat, seamless world traversal with no random encounters, and a story spanning multiple time periods. The **Sunset Edition** includes the main game plus the **Throes of the Watchmaker** paid DLC, which adds a full standalone story chapter set in a clockwork world. The base game previously appeared on the PlayStation Plus Essential tier in December 2023 and was included in Xbox Game Pass, but this is its first time surfacing in a Humble bundle.

The rest of the July 2026 Humble Choice lineup: **TUNIC**, **Neon White**, **DiceFolk**, **Police Simulator: Patrol Officers**, **Drop Duchy – Complete Edition**, **Sledders**, and **Our Adventurer Guild**.

## Outward 2 Early Access Delayed from Today (July 7) to 2027

**Nine Dots Studio** announced on **June 17** that **Outward 2** — the sequel to their open-world survival RPG — will not launch in **Steam Early Access** on its planned date of **July 7, 2026** (today), and will instead debut in Early Access sometime in **2027**.

The decision followed closed and open beta feedback that convinced the studio the game needed additional development time before charging players for an Early Access build. In a statement, CEO and Creative Director **Guillaume Boucher-Vidal** explained the reasoning: "The best we can do is take a hard, honest look at the game, and at our team, and ask: in a few weeks, will this be good enough to charge money for?" Nine Dots emphasized that they want the Early Access release to feel like a meaningful step up from the beta experience — enough of a gap that players feel the wait was justified.

No replacement date was given. *Outward 2* is a PC-exclusive (Steam) sequel to the original *Outward* (2019), which was a deliberate "unfair" survival RPG designed to punish players for poor preparation and decision-making — mechanics the sequel reportedly expands significantly.

## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Launch Trailer Released Ahead of Tomorrow's Launch

**Cygames** has released the official launch trailer for **Granblue Fantasy: Relink – Endless Ragnarok**, titled **"Not Forgotten Sky,"** ahead of the expansion's release tomorrow, **July 9, 2026**. A pre-launch developer livestream is also scheduled for **July 8 at 11:00 AM UTC** to walk through final balance adjustments.

*Endless Ragnarok* is a massive expansion to the 2024 action RPG *Granblue Fantasy: Relink*, adding six new playable characters, a new story arc continuing directly from the base game's events, new endgame modes including the **Conflux** (a solo challenge mode), new co-op quest tiers, and an uncapped Master level system that overhauls the late-game progression loop. The expansion costs **$29.99** on PC and existing consoles. Nintendo Switch 2 players receive the full package — base game plus the expansion — as a single release priced at **$49.99**.
