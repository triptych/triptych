# JRPG News — May 19, 2026

## Starbites Reviews Round-Up: Charming but Uneven

With **STARBITES** officially launching **May 21** on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, and PC, review embargoes have lifted and the critical picture is coming into focus. The sci-fi mech JRPG from IKINAGAMES earned praise for its engaging lead character, rewarding boss battles, and surprisingly rich worldbuilding — but reviewers noted repetitive dungeon design, shallow random encounters, and dated presentation hold it back. The consensus seems to be a modest but worthwhile experience for players craving a bite-sized (~25-hour) old-school JRPG that doesn't overstay its welcome. The previously reported Nintendo Switch 2 version remains delayed to an unspecified date.

## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Pre-Orders Live, New Features Detailed

**Cygames** opened pre-orders for **Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok** on **May 14**, ahead of its **July 9, 2026** launch on **Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, PS4, and PC via Steam**. The expansion-sized release layers two major new systems onto the original action RPG foundation:

- **The Conflux** — a new single-player roguelike mode with randomly shuffled areas, strategic power-up choices, and powerful Area Boss encounters.
- **Summon Equipping** — players can now call summons directly into battle or equip them for passive stat bonuses, adding a new layer to party customization.

The game supports cross-play between all platforms and is positioned as the definitive version for both returning skyfarers and newcomers.

## Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered Gets New Character and System Details

**Aquaplus** has been rolling out pre-launch details for **Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered**, which launches **May 28** for **PS5 and Switch 2 in Japan** and **PC via Steam worldwide** (with English, Japanese, and Chinese text). The sequel to *Monochrome Mobius: Rights and Wrongs Forgotten* follows Oshtor and his allies after their return as heroes to the empire of Yamato, set just before the events of *Utawarerumono: Mask of Deception*.

Combat retains the turn-based **Action Ring** system from its predecessor — characters move clockwise around a ring and act when they reach a trigger point, with Speed determining how quickly they advance. New details highlight **Area Conquer** mechanics where players clear zones by defeating required enemy counts, and face down powerful **Area Rulers** to fully secure each region.

## The Remake of the End of the Greatest RPG of All Time Launches May 28 on PC

One of the more unusual indie RPGs of the year, **The Remake of the End of the Greatest RPG of All Time** (from developers **Team Azule** and **Coin Drop Games**, published by **Glootschke Games**), hits **PC via Steam on May 28**. The concept is deliberately meta: you play the final hour of a legendary JRPG that — according to the fiction — was lost for decades and has only just resurfaced in remade form.

Players start at level 99, walking into the conclusion of story arcs they were never a part of, mourning side characters they never knew, and unraveling a meta-narrative about why this game vanished and why only its ending survives. The 4–6 hour experience blends in-game director's commentary, a digital manual, and archival footage to blur the line between real and fictional game history.

## LumenTale: Memories of Trey Launches May 26 on Switch and PC

**Beehive Studios** and **Team17** have set **May 26** as the release date for **LumenTale: Memories of Trey** on **Nintendo Switch** and **PC via Steam**. The monster-collecting JRPG follows amnesiac protagonist Trey as he traverses Talea — a world fractured by war and old imperial wounds — gathering **Animon**, elemental creatures tied to a spiritual force called Anivis.

The game features roughly **140 catchable Animon** across **13 elemental types**, party-based 4v4 battles alongside traditional solo duels, cooking and crafting via on-map Fountains, and a dedicated training hub called the **Anispace**. The creature-collecting mechanics are built around multiple trading methods and party composition depth, positioning it closer to traditional JRPG territory than the Pokémon-lite crowd.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Leaker Whiff, But September Window Rumors Persist

A prominent leaker's May 14 claim that a new **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** trailer would drop that day proved false — but the surrounding rumors have not gone away. Speculation now points to **September 17, 2026** as a possible release date for the Nintendo Switch 2 tactical RPG, coinciding with the same date recently confirmed for *Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter*. Nintendo has officially reaffirmed in recent financial disclosures only that the game is on track for a **2026 Switch 2 release** with no narrower window provided. A full reveal is widely anticipated at the **Nintendo Switch 2 Direct** or the **Xbox Games Showcase on June 7**.
