## Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster Now Available on Nintendo Switch 2

**Square Enix** has launched **Final Fantasy X | X-2 HD Remaster** on **Nintendo Switch 2** today, July 23, 2026, as a digital-exclusive package priced at **$49.99**. The physical edition (game-key card) follows on **August 27** in Japan and the West.

The Switch 2 port runs at **4K resolution in docked mode** and **1080p handheld**, with quality-of-life options including XP boosts and turbo speed carry over from prior versions. The package includes both the original *Final Fantasy X* and *Final Fantasy X-2*, with the full *Last Mission* content.

The launch is timed to **Final Fantasy X's 25th anniversary** — the original Japanese release was July 19, 2001 — and Square Enix published a dedicated anniversary trailer and special commemorative logo illustrated by **Tetsuya Nomura** to mark the occasion. Anniversary merchandise (apparel with CG art of Tidus and Yuna) is also available via Amazon.

## Disgaea Mayhem Launches Today on Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**NIS America** has released **Disgaea Mayhem** today on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. The game marks a significant genre departure for the long-running franchise, trading 23 years of grid-based turn-based strategy for a **fast-paced action RPG** with Musou-style crowd combat in 3D environments.

Players control **N.A. (Not Applicable)**, a sweet-toothed mercenary who arrives in the Super Duper Netherworld and is enlisted by Princess Tichelle to retrieve her family's stolen flan. A demo has been available on Switch and Switch 2 eShops ahead of today's launch.

Critical reception is **mixed**, with the Metacritic score sitting at **61** based on early reviews. Outlets generally praise the combat's energy and the series' trademark humor, but point to the main story wrapping up in under 10 hours and interchangeable level design as significant drawbacks. The consensus: best suited for existing Disgaea fans rather than newcomers.

## Midgar Studio Liquidated — Edge of Memories Future Uncertain

**Midgar Studio**, the French developer behind *Edge of Eternity* and the upcoming action RPG **Edge of Memories**, was ordered by a French court to **liquidate on July 20, 2026**, ending the studio's 18-year run. No buyer was found before the court's deadline.

The closure is the second permanent studio shutdown stemming from **Nacon's ongoing financial crisis**. Nacon filed for insolvency in February 2026 and has been unable to keep multiple of its subsidiary studios afloat. Studio founder **Jérémy Zeler-Maury** announced the closure on LinkedIn: *"Over the past few weeks, we worked until the very end to find a solution that could preserve both the company and the team. Despite every avenue explored and everyone who tried to help us, no solution could be completed within the timeframe of the process."*

Midgar was founded in **2008** and released *Hover* in 2017 and the turn-based RPG *Edge of Eternity* in 2021 before being acquired by Nacon in 2022. **Edge of Memories**, which had been revealed at Nacon Connect earlier this year with gameplay footage and a 2026 release window, is now considered in jeopardy with no publisher or developer confirmed to carry it forward.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Updated to Version 3.0.0 Ahead of Switch 2 Edition

**Nintendo** pushed **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 version 3.0.0** today, preparing the existing release for the **Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** launching on **July 30**. The update is a **30.7 GB** download and has no functional changes for players on original Switch hardware — its sole purpose is adding Switch 2 Edition support.

The Switch 2 Edition, confirmed separately, brings the following new content and technical upgrades:

- **Full 4K resolution** in docked mode and enhanced framerate up to **60 fps**
- A new **Rare Blade and accompanying Blade Quest**
- New equipment designs for **Pyra and Mythra**
- **Mercenary Battles Mode** — a new battle mode where Blades can be played directly
- **Amiibo support**
- All upgrades apply to **Torna ~ The Golden Country** as well

Existing owners can access the Switch 2 Edition via a **paid upgrade priced at $9.99 / £7.99**. The new version is also available as a standalone purchase. Physical edition arrives **October 1, 2026**.

## Blue Reflection Quartet — Full Collection Contents Detailed Ahead of July 30 Launch

**Koei Tecmo** has detailed the full contents of **Blue Reflection Quartet**, the series-spanning collection launching **July 30** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

The package includes:

- **BLUE REFLECTION** (original PS4/Vita game, now on Switch 2 for the first time)
- **BLUE REFLECTION: Second Light** (with graphical enhancements and faster combat)
- **BLUE REFLECTION RAY** (the anime series, presented as interactive story content)
- **BLUE REFLECTION SUN** (updated version of the mobile game)

New additions across the collection include: an **interactive character relationship chart** spanning the full series, a **series timeline**, a **term glossary**, and a gallery of existing and **20 entirely new illustrations** by series character designer and supervisor **Mel Kishida**.

Digital pre-orders before **July 29** unlock a set of four exclusive **photo frames** usable in *Second Light*'s photo mode.
