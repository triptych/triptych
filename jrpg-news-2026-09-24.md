## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Critical Acclaim — Metacritic 89

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems**' **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched on **September 17, 2026** exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2**, and reviews are in across the board.

The game holds an **89 on Metacritic** from 53 scored reviews, with **IGN awarding it a rare 10/10** and **GameSpot calling it "a sprawling masterpiece of a strategy RPG"** (9/10). Game Informer scored it **8.75/10**. The recurring praise is for the four-protagonist campaign structure (which the July deep dive previewed), the tactical grid combat, and the orchestral score. The main criticism across multiple outlets is pacing drag — the city management and overworld systems stretch the campaign longer than some critics feel is earned.

Sources: [Nintendo Life review roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-are-in) · [Famitsu scores](https://nintendoeverything.com/famitsu-review-scores-september-20-2026-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-more/) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/review/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave/let-the-games-begin) · [Game8](https://game8.co/reviews/fire-emblem-fortune-s-weave/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-review)

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches September 17

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — a full modern rebuild of the 2006 classic *Trails in the Sky SC* — launched simultaneously on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The game is a direct continuation of the 2025 *Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter* remake, returning to the Liberl Kingdom to follow Estelle and companions as they untangle the fallout from Joshua's disappearance. It preserves the series' turn-based combat while layering in the quality-of-life modernizations introduced in the first remake.

Sources: [Turn Based Lovers release date](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-gets-a-release-date-for-september-2026/) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-worldwide-release-september-17-2026/) · [Game8](https://game8.co/articles/release-dates/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-release-date-and-time)

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Arrive on PS5 and Switch 2 — 4K/120fps, Mouse Support

**NIS America** and **PH3**'s upgraded ports of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **Trails to Azure** launched on **September 10, 2026** for **PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2**, joining the existing PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, and PC releases.

The new versions run at **4K resolution and 120 frames per second** on both platforms, with **mouse input support** on Switch 2. A free patch simultaneously updated the existing Switch and PC versions. The two games are available separately digitally or as a **physical collection**; a limited edition ($109.99) includes an art book, soundtrack CDs, holographic art cards, an acrylic stand, iron-on insignias, and a Mishelam Wonderland coaster set.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-from-zero-and-the-legend-of-heroes-trails-to-azure-for-ps5-switch-2-launch-september-10) · [RPG Site (ports review)](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21247-trails-from-zero-trails-to-azure-switch-2-ps5-review-new-features-save-transfer-durante-ph3) · [RPG Site (out now)](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21353-trails-from-zero-to-azure-ps5-switch-2-out-now-pc-steam-switch-1-patch-new-september-2026) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/19/the-legend-of-heroes-trails-from-zero-and-trails-to-azure-lands-on-ps5-switch-2-on-september-10th/)

## Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age — Definitive Edition Launches on Switch 2 Today

**Square Enix**'s **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age — Definitive Edition** launched today, **September 24, 2026**, on **Nintendo Switch 2**.

The Switch 2 version brings visual and performance upgrades that bring the game's presentation in line with other current-generation releases, running at 60 FPS. ## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow Launches Today — Mixed Reviews

**Arc System Works**' **Qliphah in Providence's Shadow**, the post-apocalyptic sci-fi urban fantasy tactical RPG/visual novel hybrid, launched today, **September 24, 2026**, on **Nintendo Switch, PC (Steam)** (also PS5).

Critics are divided. Noisy Pixel praised it as "a game carried by its fun combat, solid visuals and two solid leads" and a worthwhile experience for its Tactical Counter Timeline battle system. Dual Shockers landed harder, writing that the execution is poor, the mechanics grow repetitive, and the script "cares far more about explaining technical terms and lore than actually telling a story." Final Weapon and RPGFan landed in the middle, noting the deep combat is enticing enough to recommend as a good budget title despite its narrative stumbles.

Sources: [RPGFan review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow/) · [RPGamer review](https://rpgamer.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [Dual Shockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/24/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review-steam/) · [Anime News Network review](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/review/game/pc/qliphah-in-providence-shadow/.242076)

## Persona Series Celebrates 30th Anniversary — Broadcast, Shadow Art, P5 Tops Metacritic

**Atlus** marked the **30th anniversary of the Persona series** on **September 20, 2026**, with a dedicated live broadcast featuring a talk show, live music, and merchandise showcase.

In the days leading up to the anniversary, Atlus released a four-day daily radio series (September 15–18) with series voice actors, followed by sequential releases of *Persona 5 The Animation* episodes on the official YouTube channel. The anniversary broadcast also debuted a "Shadow Version" of the series' anniversary video — newly commissioned artwork reimagining all seven main series protagonists as Shadow selves.

Separately, **Persona 5 Royal** was recognized by **Guinness World Records** as the highest-rated JRPG in history, holding an aggregate score of **95 on Metacritic**.

## Fate/Extra Record Gets January 28, 2027 Date — Aniplex Publishing, Switch 2 Version Added

**Fate/Extra Record**, Marvelous' long-in-development remake of the 2010 TYPE-MOON cult classic, finally has a firm release date: **January 28, 2027**, confirmed by new publisher **Aniplex**.

The announcement marks the completion of a chaotic publisher transition — Bandai Namco dropped the title in March 2026, and in August 2026 Aniplex stepped in alongside development support from **Studio Meteorise** (following a reorganization of Studio BB). The release now adds a **Nintendo Switch 2 version** to the planned lineup of PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, and PC (Steam). Physical editions include a full game-on-cart for North America on Switch 2; JP and EU Switch 2 physicals will use game-key cards.

Sources: [RPG Site (publisher change)](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21253-fateextra-record-now-being-published-by-aniplex-developed-by-meteorise-with-additional-switch-2-release) · [RPG Site (release date)](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21288-fate-extra-record-release-date-switch-2-physical-edition-ps5-physical) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/03/fate-extra-record-release-date-confirmed/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/fate-extra-record-shares-nintendo-switch-release-date-new-trailer/)

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Sells 1 Million Copies at Launch — Metacritic 86

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched on **September 4, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, PC, PlayStation 5** (also Xbox Series X|S), and sold **1 million copies on its first day**.

The revival of the dormant samurai action-RPG series landed at **86 on Metacritic** (95% critics recommend). IGN awarded it **10/10** — the first perfect score from that outlet for the Onimusha franchise. Forbes gave it **9/10**, Nintendo Life **9/10**. Praise is nearly universal for the combat and boss fights; the main criticism across reviewers is that the 25-hour campaign is padded with repetitive side content that dilutes the intensity.

Sources: [Nintendo Life review roundup](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-onimusha-way-of-the-sword-are-in) · [Forbes review](https://www.forbes.com/sites/games/2026/08/31/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review-more-inagaki-than-kurosawa/) · [GameMetaHub roundup](https://www.gamemetahub.com/games/onimusha-way-of-the-sword/review-roundup-launch-verdicts)
