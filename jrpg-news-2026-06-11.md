## Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales — Releases in One Week (June 18)

**The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales**, the new action RPG from **Team Asano** (creators of *Octopath Traveler* and *Bravely Default*), launches **June 18, 2026** on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch 2, and PC (Steam).

The game follows adventurer Elliot and his fairy companion Faie on a mission spanning four historical eras — crossing through a "Doorway of Time" to break a curse on Princess Heuria of Huther. Combat is action-based with real-time battles, strategic fairy support abilities, and puzzle elements woven throughout exploration. The HD-2D visual style carries over from Team Asano's previous titles.

Pricing: **$59.99** base / **$69.99** Digital Deluxe / **$229.99** physical Collector's Edition (Square Enix store exclusive).

## Deltarune Chapter 5 — June 24 Release Date Confirmed

**Deltarune Chapter 5** was confirmed at the June 9 Nintendo Direct with a release date of **June 24, 2026 at 11:00 AM EDT**, available on all platforms simultaneously as a **free update** for paid Deltarune owners.

Toby Fox's reveal trailer showed the chapter's Dark World features areas with a large flower stem to slide down, a plant-overgrown pond, and traditional Japanese-style buildings. The in-game summary describes it as "The Field of Pink and Gold. The vast garden is charred in an inferno of jealousy."

Sources: [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/deltarune-chapter-5-release-date-falls-in-june-2026/) · [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/61675-deltarune-chapter-5-launches-june-24th-2026/) · [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/deltarune-chapter-5-announced-during-june-nintendo-direct/) · [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78603-deltarune-chapter-5-gets-official-release-date-launches-as-free-update-in-june)

## Steam Next Fest — June 15–22 | 80+ JRPG Demos

**Steam Next Fest** runs **June 15–22** (17:00 UTC start) with over 80 JRPG demos in the action RPG, traditional, and related categories. Standouts highlighted by curators include:

- A **top-down 2.5D action RPG** from the creators of *CrossCode*, involving breaking a curse and guiding humanity's rebirth with stylish combos and multiple divine weapons.
- A **retro voxel action JRPG** starring a boy named Lug in a world of humans vs. machines, exploring vibrant voxel environments and battling mechanoids.
- A **narrative turn-based RPG** about two sisters and their spirit-mech fighting a fast food empire — described as "spicy" with community reputation systems and corpo fights.

The *Echoes of Aincrad* demo (above) also runs during this window.

## Nioh 3 Update 1.07.00 — Set Bonus Overhaul

**Team Ninja** dropped **Nioh 3 update 1.07.00** on June 10 for PS5 and PC. The patch focuses on tuning Grace set bonuses that were underperforming:

- **Stack Count Duration** (6-piece bonus): increased effectiveness
- **Effect Duration** (4-piece bonus): increased effects
- **Damage Bonus (Bow Damage)** (6-piece bonus): increased effects
- Bug fixes throughout

Sources: [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/title-updates-and-patches/nioh-3-update-1-070-brings-enhanced-set-bonuses-graces) · [Team Ninja](https://teamninja-studio.com/nioh3/us/update/)
