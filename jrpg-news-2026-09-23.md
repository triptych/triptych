## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Near-Universal Acclaim

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems**' **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2 on September 17, 2026**, and the reviews are in — it's one of the year's best-reviewed games.

The strategy RPG carries a **Metacritic score of 89** based on 53+ scored reviews, with **98% of OpenCritic critics recommending it**. Thirteen outlets awarded the game a perfect score, including **IGN (10/10)**. **GameSpot** gave it a 9/10, calling it "a sprawling masterpiece of a strategy RPG," while **Game Informer** scored it 8.75/10. Full playthroughs reportedly range from **70 to over 100 hours** to complete all routes.

Critics praised the game's narrative, four-protagonist structure, orchestral score, and the depth its Heroic Games tournament framing brings to the strategy-RPG formula.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches to GOTY-Level Reviews

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — the remake of *Trails in the Sky SC* — launched **September 17, 2026** on **PC, Switch, and Switch 2** (as well as PS5), and has emerged as a serious Game of the Year contender.

The game sits at a **91 on Metacritic (Universal Acclaim)** and a **92 on OpenCritic (Mighty)**, with **97% of critics recommending** it. **RPGFan** and **DualShockers** both awarded **10/10**, while **Game Informer** called it "the Trails series' defining entry, and one of the best RPGs of the year" at **9.5/10**. **IGN** and **Push Square** each gave it 9/10.

Minor criticisms center on inconsistent voice acting, sparse environments, and a plain interface — none enough to dampen the consensus.

Sources: [DualShockers review](https://www.dualshockers.com/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review/) · [RPGFan review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/) · [Game Informer review](https://gameinformer.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/a-superb-sequel) · [Final Weapon review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/16/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review/) · [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-reviews-goty-contender/)

## Another Eden Begins Launches as a Full Standalone JRPG — No Gacha

**Wright Flyer Studios** released **Another Eden Begins** on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. The game is a full standalone offline JRPG adapted from the mobile *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space*, with **all gacha mechanics and microtransactions removed**.

The story follows **Aldo**, whose sister **Feinne** is taken by the Beast King, sending Aldo 800 years into the future through a tear in spacetime. The game features **eighteen companions**, full English and Japanese voice acting for the main story, and a new reworked combat system.

The creative pedigree carries franchise veterans: director **Masato Kato** (*Chrono Trigger*, *Xenogears*) and composer **Yasunori Mitsuda** (*Chrono Cross*, *Xenogears*). The game is available now at $39.99.

## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow Launches Tomorrow — Final Details

**Arc System Works**' tactical RPG **Qliphah in Providence's Shadow** launches **September 24, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam, and PC via Epic Games Store** (also PlayStation 5). Both Standard and Deluxe Editions are available digitally and physically.

Set in a **post-apocalyptic Tokyo** where supernatural beings run rampant, the game follows **Soma Hoshigami**, a delivery worker who becomes a "half-turned" human/supernatural hybrid after a creature attack. The counter-timeline tactical combat system lets players predict and manipulate enemy timelines as a core mechanic.

Pre-orders have been open since early September, and a gameplay trailer released this week detailed the battle system ahead of tomorrow's launch.

## Dragon Quest XI S: Definitive Edition Launches on Switch 2 Tomorrow — No Upgrade Path

**Square Enix**'s **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** launches **September 24, 2026** for **$49.99**.

The Switch 2 Edition includes all content from the original Definitive Edition — new story chapters, 2D/3D mode switching, and the orchestral soundtrack — plus visual enhancements targeting the Switch 2's hardware. The game takes up **23.4 GB**, compared to 9.4 GB on Switch 1.

One significant caveat: **there is no upgrade path from the Switch 1 version**, and save transfers are not supported. The physical release uses Nintendo's **Game Key Card** format — a physical cartridge that redeems a digital copy, containing no game data on the card itself.

Sources: [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/61131-dragon-quest-xi-s-definitive-edition-switch-2-edition-launches-sept-24th-2026) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/05/dragon-quest-xi-gets-the-definitive-edition-treatment-again-for-switch-2) · [The Sixth Axis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/05/27/dragon-quest-xi-s-is-coming-to-switch-2-in-september-but-theres-no-upgrades-from-switch-1/) · [Nova Crystallis](https://novacrystallis.com/2026/05/dragon-quest-xi-s-releases-for-nintendo-switch-2-on-september-24-2026/)

## Eternal Anima Announced at Nintendo Direct — Kazushige Nojima, March 2027

**Marvelous** unveiled **Eternal Anima** at the **Nintendo Direct on September 9, 2026**, a brand-new JRPG coming to **Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam** (and PS5/Xbox Series) on **March 4, 2027**.

The game is positioned as a spiritual successor to late-1990s/early-2000s JRPGs, with an original time-traveling narrative and strategic twists on turn-based combat. Key creative staff:
- **Director**: Katsuhisa Higuchi
- **Story Concept Writer**: Keisuke Makino
- **Scenario Writer**: Kazushige Nojima (*Final Fantasy VII*, *XIII*, *XVI*)

Combat uses protagonist **Wade**'s **Psychometry** power to read past memories and inherit Abilities and Classes, while the **Future Sight** mechanic lets players predict enemy actions mid-battle and rewrite the outcome.

Pricing is **$49.99 (PS5) / $59.99 (Switch 2)**. A physical Limited Edition at **$89.99** includes a two-disc soundtrack, nine character art cards with a display stand, and an art book.

Sources: [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/64748-eternal-anima-coming-to-switch-2-on-march-4th-2027) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/eternal-anima-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2/) · [Cat with Monocle](https://catwithmonocle.com/news/2026/09/09/eternal-anima-announced-during-nintendo-direct/)
