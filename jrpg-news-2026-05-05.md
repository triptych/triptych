# JRPG News — May 5, 2026

## Final Fantasy VII Remake Part 3: Director Has Completed 40+ Full Playthroughs

Director **Naoki Hamaguchi** has confirmed in a recent interview that he has personally completed "over 40 full playthroughs" of the still-untitled Final Fantasy VII Remake trilogy conclusion. He stated development is "proceeding on time and on schedule" and urged fans to wait just a bit longer, noting that Square Enix is actively planning a public reveal.

Key takeaways:
- The game has an **official internal title** that has not yet been publicly disclosed
- The volume of playthroughs suggests extensive QA and balancing work at a late-stage level
- Community speculation is pointing toward a **summer 2026 reveal**, with a possible launch in early 2027
- No platforms officially confirmed beyond PS5, though Switch 2 support is widely anticipated

This is the first meaningful production update on Part 3 since Rebirth shipped in February 2024, and the tone suggests the team is well past crunch toward a polished final build.

## Final Fantasy IX: Vivi Prequel Book Arrives May 19

Square Enix is releasing **Final Fantasy 9: Vivi and Grandpa's Memories for the Sky** on **May 19, 2026** — a 48-page illustrated children's book that serves as an origin story for Vivi Orunitia, the beloved black mage from the 1999 PS1 classic.

Notable credentials behind the project:
- Written by **Kazuhiko Aoki**, a Square veteran involved with the franchise since 1990
- Art by **Toshiyuki Itahana**, the original character designer for Final Fantasy IX
- Priced at **$17.99 USD**
- The book ties in with a broader FF9 revival that includes an upcoming animated series, **Black Mages Legacy**, set after the events of the game

With the original creative team attached, this is being treated by fans as soft canon rather than a merchandising spin-off.

## Final Fantasy V Joins Xbox Game Pass

**Final Fantasy V** is now available on Xbox Game Pass across all three membership tiers, making it the first catalog addition to the service in May 2026 and the 73rd Xbox Game Pass arrival of the year. The game is the Pixel Remaster version, which launched on consoles in 2023.

## Alabaster Dawn Enters Early Access May 7

**Alabaster Dawn**, the follow-up to CrossCode from **Radical Fish Games**, launches in **Steam Early Access on May 7, 2026** — two days from now. The release date was confirmed at the April 2026 Triple-i Initiative showcase.

What to expect from Early Access:
- Action RPG with puzzle-heavy exploration in the CrossCode tradition
- Character build system centered on **gems socketed into weapons and cores** for enchantments ranging from stat boosts to situational buffs
- Cooking system that unlocks healing and temporary combat buffs, with a **Palate Level** that scales dish effectiveness over time
- Developers estimate **at least 2 years** of Early Access given the game's scope
- Full 1.0 launch will target PC and consoles; Switch 2 support has not been ruled out

CrossCode (2018) was a sleeper hit that earned a devoted following for its exceptionally tight action and elaborate puzzle design. Alabaster Dawn is built in the same engine and by the same small team.
