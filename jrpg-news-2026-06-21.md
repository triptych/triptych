## Final Fantasy Resonance — More Details Emerge on the HD-2D Entry

Following its June 9 Nintendo Direct reveal, **Square Enix** has shared additional specifics on **Final Fantasy Resonance** that flesh out what was only briefly shown in the announcement trailer.

**Story source:** The game is an adaptation of the first season of the mobile title *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius*, set in the crystal-focused world of **Lapis**. Players control Grandshelt knight **Rain**, his adoptive brother **Lasswell**, and the enigmatic maiden **Fina** as they chase down **Veritas of the Dark** and the Sworn Six of Paladia before the world's eight elemental crystals are destroyed. For players unfamiliar with the mobile source material, Square Enix is framing it as a standalone story.

**Gameplay:** The battle system is turn-based for parties of four, running on a timeline similar to the *Octopath Traveler* series — action choice affects how long before a character's next turn arrives. Recruitable **Visions** of classic FF characters — including **Cloud**, **Terra**, and **Clive** — can join battles and unleash special moves, serving a role similar to summons.

**Pricing tiers:**
- Standard: **$49.99**
- Digital Deluxe: **$59.99**
- Collector's Edition: **$209.99** (base game + digital bonuses + artbook + soundtrack + Final Fantasy Trading Card Game promo card)

Platforms: Switch 2, Switch, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam + Microsoft Store). October 22, 2026.

## Deltarune Chapter 5 — Toby Fox's Own Words on What's Coming

With **Chapter 5: The Field of Pink and Gold** three days away (June 24, 11 AM EDT), Toby Fox published a newsletter alongside the original Nintendo Direct reveal that spells out the chapter's emotional register more clearly than the brief trailer alone.

Fox wrote: *"In Chapter 4, we saw dark clouds on the horizon… That's why, let's not look there for now. Let's turn around and watch the sun, before it goes down completely. Let's smile again. Let's have one more fun adventure, okay?"*

The imagery — flowers, the "Field of Pink and Gold" subtitle, ties to **Asgore's flower shop Flower King** — points to a chapter designed as a breath of air before the series moves into its endgame. Fox added that a friend who played an early, unfinished build reported: *"I am still thinking about it a lot and want to play it more."*

Chapter 5 drops simultaneously on Switch, Switch 2, PS4, PS5, and PC (Steam / Mac) as a **free update** to all owners of the paid version. Chapter 6 is confirmed in production; Fox described it as "easier to make than the others," with the team eyeing a possible **Chapter 7 production start before the end of 2026**.

## Titan Quest II — "Wild Lands" Is the Largest Early Access Update Yet

**THQ Nordic** and **Grimlore Games** launched **Chapter 4: Wild Lands** for *Titan Quest II* on **June 18**, and by the studio's own accounting it is the biggest content drop the Early Access game has received since launch.

**What's new:**
- A full open-world region with six distinct biomes: deep canyons, arid dunes, crimson sands, deep forests, a lush oasis, and a decaying poisonous swamp
- **18 new dungeons**, 10 points of interest, three new bosses including **Nemesis, Goddess of Retribution**
- 5 Fated Quests, 13 World Quests, 20+ hidden events and secrets, 20+ randomized events
- **Level cap raised from 50 to 55**, new passive skills, and a long-requested **loot filter**

*Titan Quest II* remains in Early Access on PC (Steam and Epic Games Store). No 1.0 date has been announced.

## Full Circle — PS1-Era JRPG Announced by Chained Echoes Publisher

**Deck13 Spotlight** — the publisher behind the physical *Chained Echoes* Switch Edition — has announced **Full Circle**, a 2.5HD indie JRPG developed by solo developer **2ndPlayerGames**. The reveal came during the **Indie Quest 2026 Showcase** in late May, with coverage continuing to spread this week.

**Pitch:** A post-apocalyptic world where mutated beasts have driven the remnants of humanity into **flying cities**. Young heroes are trained and deployed into the dangerous wilds below to ensure the survival of these last refuges. The aesthetic draws explicitly from *Lunar*, *Breath of Fire*, *Final Fantasy*, and *Legend of Dragoon*.

**Combat:** Deck13 Spotlight describes a **"constant flow combat"** system — turn-based at its core, but built around timed inputs, chained attacks, combos, and party swaps to keep players actively engaged throughout each exchange.

**Platforms:** PC and consoles (specifics TBA). The game will be **playable at Gamescom** later this year. No release window yet.

## Threads of Time — 350K Wishlists and Day of the Devs Trailer

**Riyo Games** (Canada) showed a new developer-update trailer for **Threads of Time** during **Day of the Devs: Summer Game Fest Edition 2026**, and the studio announced the game has now surpassed **350,000 wishlists** on Steam.

The trailer's focus was **The Needle**, a massive timeship serving as the player's hub across eras. Players recruit characters from different historical periods to rebuild the ancient **Order of the Time Knights**, tasked with defending the timeline from an unspecified corrupting force. Combat is turn-based with time-themed abilities that shift turn order in battle.

Visually the game pairs detailed pixel art characters with **2.5D environments built in Unreal Engine 5**. Platforms confirmed: **PC and Xbox Series X|S**. No release date or window.

## Edge of Memories — Nacon's Action RPG Gets a Full Gameplay Reveal

**Nacon** and **Midgar Studio** released a four-minute **gameplay overview trailer** for **Edge of Memories** during Nacon Connect 2026, providing the most complete look yet at how the French action RPG plays.

**Setting:** The continent of **Avaris**, slowly succumbing to a spreading corruption called **the Corrosion**. Young Soul Whisperer **Eline** and her allies travel the continent searching for a cure.

**Combat:** Real-time, with strings of light and heavy combos and a **perfect dodge** mechanic. A successful dodge feeds into a supercharged "Corruption state" that temporarily unlocks faster attack strings for higher damage. Ally abilities have distinct roles: Eline's partner **Ysoris** can hack ancient technology to solve environmental puzzles, while other allies' elemental skills destroy obstacles in the world.

**Exploration:** Players ride a swift mount called a **Nekaroo** across the open world, with wall-climbing and platforming available. The traversal design suggests a world built around verticality as well as horizontal scale.

Platforms: PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam). 2026 release window; specific date not yet set.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/05/edge-of-memories-gameplay-overview-trailer) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/04/edge-of-memories-gameplay-trailer/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20324-edge-memories-shows-combat-exploration-progression-in-new-gameplay-overview)

## SoulStream Saga: Chapter 1 — Indie ATB JRPG in Steam Next Fest

**After1mage 1nteractive**, a three-person team, has a Next Fest demo running through June 22 for **SoulStream Saga: Chapter 1**, a pixel art JRPG built around a **Shared ATB** system inspired by late-90s/early-2000s JRPGs.

**Setting:** The capital city of **Alestria**, torn apart by a war between rival Mages and Alchemists. **Astarius Valcrest**, a former Mage who renounced his title to study alchemy, returns wielding **Alchaeon** — a blade he forged himself, infused with both disciplines.

**Full-release scope:** Three active combat characters drawn from seven total recruitable party members, unique skill trees for each character, and minigames including **treasure hunting**, **fishing**, and a **playable trading card game** embedded in the world.

The current Steam demo is a prequel scenario separate from the main story. No release date for the full game.

Sources: [SoulStream Saga Steam Page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4373550/SoulStream_Saga_Chapter_1/) · [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/overview/soulstream-saga-chapter-1-jrpg/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/03/soulstream-saga-chapter-1-announced/)
