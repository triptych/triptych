## Blue Reflection Quartet — Second Trailer Drops, Digital Pre-Orders Now Live Ahead of July 30

**Koei Tecmo** and developer **Gust** released the second official trailer for **Blue Reflection Quartet** on June 22 and simultaneously opened digital pre-orders. The game launches on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam on July 30, 2026**.

Blue Reflection Quartet is an all-in-one collection bundling all four entries in the Blue Reflection series:
- **Blue Reflection** (the original PS4/Vita game)
- **Blue Reflection: Ray** (anime adaptation)
- **Blue Reflection: Sun** (the mobile title)
- **Blue Reflection: Second Light** (the 2021 PS4/Switch sequel)

The second trailer runs over the series track **"OVERDOSE"** and highlights the school-life segments, Reflector combat sequences, and story moments across all four connected projects. New accessibility features and contextual notes are included to help newcomers follow the series' interconnected narrative.

**Pre-order bonus:** Players who pre-order before July 29 receive a set of four **in-game photo frames** for use in *Blue Reflection: Second Light*'s photo mode. A free limited-time bonus — the Quartet special photo frame — is available for anyone who purchases (not just pre-orders) through August 12.

## Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories — Next Mainline Atelier Announced for Early 2027

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust** announced **Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories** on June 9 during the Nintendo Direct, with a dedicated livestream on June 12 providing additional details. The game is targeting an **early 2027** release on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**.

Karia is the next mainline entry in the long-running Atelier series and continues the **Memories Trilogy** arc. The protagonist, **Karia**, is an adventurer and member of the Aladiss Pioneering Team who stumbles upon an undiscovered underground world while investigating ancient ruins. There she discovers a mysterious large cauldron and encounters alchemy for the first time.

The signature new mechanic blends traditional alchemy with **culinary exploration**: while gathering ingredients in the field, Karia can eat them on the spot to trigger immediate dynamic effects — for example, consuming animal-based ingredients sprouts animal ears and grants specialized traits. This eat-to-transform loop feeds directly into the alchemy crafting system.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/atelier-karia-the-night-kingdom-the-guide-of-memories-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-and-pc) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/13/atelier-karia-announced-early-2027/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/06/atelier-karia-the-night-kingdom-the-guide-of-memories-announced/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20623-atelier-karia-the-night-kingdom-guide-memories-coming-to-switch-2-ps5-xbox-series-x-pc)

## Deluge — Dark 16-Bit RPG About Serving a Necromancer Releases This Week on Steam

**Deluge** is a newly released indie RPG on Steam (part of the June 21–27 release window) from a solo or small team. It is a short-form, dark atmospheric **16-bit-styled RPG** focused on morality and memory.

Players are cast as a **resurrected skeleton warrior** forced to serve a tyrannical necromancer bent on destroying the village of **Figaro** — a community the necromancer already laid to waste once before. The character retains fragmented memories of their human life, and the central tension is whether to faithfully carry out the necromancer's destructive orders or find ways to protect the village's surviving inhabitants.

The game draws comparisons to cult narrative RPGs and is described as a "melancholic adventure with bendable storytelling" — player choices affect the fate of multiple NPCs and determine how Figaro's story ends. RPGFan flagged it in their June 21–27 weekly roundup alongside NONEWORLD as one of the notable smaller JRPG-adjacent releases of the week.

Sources: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3779440/Deluge/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/06/21/rpgs-coming-this-week-june-21-27-2026/)

## Aether Dawn — Anime Cyberpunk-Fantasy ARPG Announced by Ex-Genshin and Arknights Developers

Singapore-based indie studio **Jin Universe Studios** — founded by former developers from **Genshin Impact** and **Arknights** — announced their debut title **Aether Dawn** for **PC, iOS, and Android**. No release window was given.

Aether Dawn is a **third-person 3D hack-and-slash action RPG** set in an original **cyberpunk-fantasy** universe. Its core loop combines action combat with **roguelite dungeon-crawler** mechanics, and the studio is pitching it as a long-term live-service franchise targeting the global anime-styled game market.

The studio secured financial backing from **Arbitrum Gaming Ventures** and **BITKRAFT Ventures**, though the investment amount was not disclosed. Co-founder Tack Wei Ho described the project as meeting "the surging global demand for anime-styled games while evolving into a long-lasting franchise." The announcement generated interest specifically due to the Genshin/Arknights pedigree of the development team.

## Echo Generation 2 — Reviews Published; RPGFan Critical

**Echo Generation 2**, a prequel to the 2024 original, saw its review wave hit on June 23 with coverage from multiple outlets. The game primarily follows **Jack**, the missing father from the first game.

RPGFan's review by Jerry Williams was notably critical, describing the gameplay as "akin to biting into a chunky voxel burger that had no flavor whatsoever" and citing a 9-hour story that relies on heavy exposition and "forced relationships" — with prolonged dialogue that is "all tell and no show." Other outlets were more measured, with Use a Potion calling it "a sci-fi deck-building epic" and Cloud Dosage offering a positive take on the deck-building mechanics.

The game is available on **Xbox Series X|S** and **PC via Steam**. Metacritic and OpenCritic aggregates are currently accumulating.

Sources: [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/echo-generation-2/) · [Use a Potion](https://www.useapotion.com/2026/05/echo-generation-2-review-a-sci-fi-deck-building-epic/) · [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/echo-generation-2/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1115990/Echo_Generation_2/)

## Deltarune Chapter 5 — Global Unlock Times Published; Toby Fox Notes Chapter 7 Could Begin Before 2027

With **Deltarune Chapter 5: "The Field of Pink and Gold"** launching **tomorrow, June 24 at 11:00 AM EDT / 8:00 AM PT**, outlets have published global unlock times. The release is simultaneous worldwide across **Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, PS5, and PC via Steam** as a free update for existing owners.

One new piece of developer context emerged from pre-launch coverage: Toby Fox noted that Chapter 6 is "easier to make than the others" and that development has been moving quickly enough that the team **may be able to begin work on Chapter 7 before the end of 2026**. If accurate, this suggests the endgame of the Deltarune release schedule is accelerating — Chapters 5 and 6 were described as a connected pair at announcement, and Chapter 7 would begin the final stretch.

The trailer Fox released for Chapter 5 deliberately avoided showing "the most exciting moments" to preserve player surprises.

Sources: [OpenCritic unlock times](https://opencritic.com/news/33473/deltarune-chapter-5-global-unlock-times) · [Dual Shockers unlock times](https://www.dualshockers.com/deltarune-chapter-5-global-unlock-times/) · [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/61675-deltarune-chapter-5-launches-june-24th-2026) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/deltarune-chapter-5-launches-june-24)
