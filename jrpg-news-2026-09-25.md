## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — 2026's Highest-Rated Game at Launch

**Nihon Falcom** and **NIS America**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** launched on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**, and immediately became the year's best-reviewed title.

The game is a full modern rebuild of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC** (originally 2006 on PC-88 and PSP), continuing Estelle Bright's story directly after the cliffhanger ending of *1st Chapter*. Like that remake, *2nd Chapter* preserves the turn-based combat structure while adding quality-of-life features, expanded voice acting, and a complete visual overhaul.

Critical reception was outstanding. The game carries a **91 on Metacritic** (Universal Acclaim) and a **92 on OpenCritic** with 98% of critics recommending it — making it 2026's highest-reviewed release at time of writing, surpassing even *1st Chapter*'s already acclaimed reception by three points. **RPG Site** and **DualShockers** both awarded perfect 10/10s; **Game Informer** called it "the Trails series' defining entry, and one of the best RPGs of the year." The main criticism across reviews was inconsistent voice acting in peripheral scenes, sparse environments in some regions, and a functional but plain interface.

A **Golden Wings Edition** collector's release was also available at launch.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Reviews — 89 Metacritic, IGN 10/10

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** launched on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2** and received near-universal acclaim in reviews, earning an **89 on Metacritic** based on 53 scored reviews with a **98% recommendation rate** on OpenCritic. Thirteen outlets awarded perfect scores.

The game features **four interconnected campaigns** following separate protagonists competing in the Heroic Games tournament, each tackled in any order. Critics consistently praised the tactical grid combat, deep character progression, and orchestral score; **IGN** gave it a 10/10, writing: "Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave builds upon its strategic arts in ways I couldn't have anticipated." Full playthroughs range from 70 hours (single route) to well over 100 hours for all four routes, making it one of the franchise's most ambitious entries by scope.

## Dragon Quest XI S — Switch 2 Edition Out Now

**Square Enix** launched **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age — Definitive Edition** for **Nintendo Switch 2 on September 24, 2026**, priced at **$39.99**.

The Switch 2 port offers two graphics modes: **Graphics Priority** (1440p/30 fps) and **Performance Priority** (1080p/60 fps). All content from the Definitive Edition is included — expanded story chapters spotlighting party members, the 2D retro mode toggle, additional English and Japanese voice recording, and the orchestral soundtrack option.

**Notable caveats:** Save data is incompatible with the original Switch version, and there is no upgrade pricing for existing owners. The physical release uses a **Game Key Card** rather than a cartridge.

## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow — Mixed Reviews at Launch

**Qliphah in Providence's Shadow**, the tactical counter-timeline RPG from Japanese developer **Team Sinfonia**, launched September 24 for **Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC** (Steam and Epic Games Store) and received a mixed critical response.

The game is a post-apocalyptic sci-fi urban fantasy RPG/visual novel hybrid with two protagonists running parallel timelines to prevent catastrophe. Its **Counter Rush** combat — a timing-based system requiring precise reads of enemy patterns — drew consistent praise; critics who stuck with it found the interlocking mechanics and central mystery rewarding. The game is held back by an awkward English localization, limited quality-of-life options, and a slow opening section of several hours before its systems cohere. [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) called it "half-baked" for never realizing its best ideas; [CogConnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) called it "a brilliantly weird JRPG" worth the patience it demands.

Sources: [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/24/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review-steam/) · [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [CogConnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/)

## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Announced — February 2027

**Square Enix** announced **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** during the **September 2026 Nintendo Direct**, confirming a **February 16, 2027** release for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The game is a full **3D remake of the 1995 Super Famicom RPG Romancing SaGa 3**, developed by **xeen Inc.** — the studio behind *Trials of Mana* (2020) and *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven* (2024). Players choose from **eight protagonists**, each with their own entry into a world threatened by the Four Sinistrals and the recurring catastrophe known as the Rise of Morastrum. More than 20 allies can be recruited across playthroughs. The remake brings fully voiced cutscenes in Japanese and English, overhauled combat, difficulty options targeting both newcomers and series veterans, and all-new 3D graphics.

A TGS 2026 demo was playable at the show floor, with Square Enix sharing 15 minutes of gameplay details from it.

## Persona 4 Revival — Aika Nakamura Confirmed as Location Episode Character; Switch 2 Date Set

A **Persona 4 Revival** broadcast on **September 19** brought a flurry of new information ahead of the game's **February 18, 2027** PC/PS5 release.

**Aika Nakamura** — a fan-favourite character originally created for *Persona 4: The Animation* (2011) — was confirmed as a new addition to the game. Atlus initially described her as a Social Link, then issued a clarification: Aika is a **Location Episode Character**, a new category distinct from the standard Social Link system. She is the daughter of the Aiya restaurant owners and handles the delivery service, and has been close friends with Hanako Ohtani since childhood. Her English voice actress is **Judy Alice Lee** (*Melinoë* in *Hades II*, *Luna Snow* in *Marvel Rivals*).

The broadcast also confirmed **new voice casting**: Tohru Adachi, Ryotaro Dojima, and Nanako Dojima all received updated English cast announcements.

Separately, the **Nintendo Switch 2 version** of Persona 4 Revival was dated: **May 20, 2027**, three months after the PC/PS5 launch.

## Edge of Memories — November 5 Release Date Despite Midgar Studio Closure

In a reversal of the outcome previously considered most likely, **Nacon** announced at **TGS 2026** that **Edge of Memories** will release on **November 5, 2026** for **PC (Steam)** and PlayStation 5 — making it the final release from the now-liquidated **Midgar Studio**.

Midgar was ordered into liquidation by a French commercial court in July 2026 after no buyer was found during Nacon's insolvency proceedings. At the time, cancellation appeared probable since no functional studio remained to complete the game. Instead, Nacon has shepherded the near-finished action RPG through release — set in the world of Midgar's previous title *Edge of Eternity*, it follows an anime-styled cast through a new standalone story. A **Deluxe Edition** with 48-hour early access opens **November 3**.
