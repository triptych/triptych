## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Sells 1 Million on Launch Day, Series Tops 10 Million

**Capcom** announced that **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** sold over **1 million units on its launch day** of September 4, 2026, across **Nintendo Switch 2, PC (Steam, Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S**. The milestone pushed cumulative lifetime sales of the **Onimusha series past 10 million units** — the first time the franchise has crossed that threshold.

The game has been met with strong critical reception:

- **Metacritic:** 86 (PS5) / 89 (Switch 2)
- **IGN:** 10/10 — the outlet's first perfect score in the franchise's history
- **Nintendo Life:** 9/10
- **PC Gamer:** 83/100

Critics praised the deep sword-combat system and boss encounter design. Common criticisms include a linear campaign of roughly 20 hours and limited post-game content.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Manga Launches September 4, Intelligent Systems Marks Game Complete

Two pieces of news arrived for **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** ahead of its **September 17 launch** on **Nintendo Switch 2**:

**Manga adaptation:** A serialization has begun in *Saikyo Jump*. Illustrated by **Kazuro Kyou** (who previously handled the *Fire Emblem Engage* manga), Chapter 0 — a prologue — appeared in the October issue of the magazine, which went on sale **September 4, 2026**.

**Gold status:** Developer **Intelligent Systems** quietly added *Fortune's Weave* to its official list of completed works, confirming the game has gone gold and is fully in production. The addition was spotted by Nintendo Life this week.

The game is a Switch 2 exclusive JRPG-flavored strategy title set in the Dagdan Empire on the continent of Dagda. Players select one of four protagonists — **Cai, Theodora, Dietrich, or Leda** — each with their own route. Returning mechanics include Combat Arts, Gambits, and Weapon Durability. A Collector's Edition is available including an artbook, art cards, a map poster, and steelbook case.

Sources: [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/fire-emblem-dev-intelligent-systems-adds-fortunes-weave-to-its-completed-works) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/08/more-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-details-revealed/) · [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78596-fire-emblem-fortune-s-weave-reveals-release-date-and-introduces-its-main-characters)

## Another Eden Begins — September 17 on Switch 2, Switch, and PC

**Wright Flyer Studios** and **Studio Prisma** have confirmed that **Another Eden Begins** releases **September 17, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. No PlayStation or Xbox release has been announced.

The standalone premium JRPG is directed by **Masato Kato** — writer of *Chrono Trigger* and *Xenogears* — with music composed by **Yasunori Mitsuda** (*Xenogears, Chrono Cross*). The game features time-traveling mechanics, 18 companion characters, and a prominent **New Game+ mode** where a new party member joins and the story branches across **10 different endings**. Full English and Japanese voice acting is included in the main story, featuring the returning cast from *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space*.

Language support at launch: English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

Sources: [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/59604-another-eden-begins-gets-sept-17th-2026-release-date-new-trailer-shared) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20042-another-eden-begins-release-date-steam-switch-2-physical-edition-serial-codes) · [DSO Gaming](https://www.dsogaming.com/news/another-eden-begins-is-a-new-time-travelling-jrpg-from-the-writer-of-chrono-trigger-and-xenogears/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4163960/Another_Eden_Begins/)

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — September 17 Details Recap

**Nihon Falcom** and **GungHo Online Entertainment**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — a full remake of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC* — launches **September 17, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam, and PlayStation 5**. Physical editions are available in the west for Switch 2, Switch, and PS5.

The remake follows **Estelle Bright** as a senior bracer pursuing her adoptive brother Joshua and confronting the secret society Ouroboros. It features updated visuals, reorchestrated music, quality-of-life improvements, and full English and Japanese voice acting. In Japan, Switch 2 and PS5 pre-orders include a download code for a remastered version of the original *Trails in the Sky*.

## Dragon Quest XI S: Switch 2 Edition — New Story Chapters Confirmed

A new trailer for **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition – Switch 2 Edition** dropped this week ahead of its **September 24 launch** on **Nintendo Switch 2** at **$39.99**.

Beyond the resolution and performance upgrades (higher frame rate and output than Switch 1), the Switch 2 edition adds **new story chapters** that put individual party members front and center — expanding on content beyond the original Definitive Edition. The game uses the **2D/3D switching** feature and orchestral score from the Definitive Edition. File size is 23.4 GB (up from 9.4 GB on Switch). The physical version is a **game-key card**, not a traditional cartridge.

Important: **save data from the Nintendo Switch version is not compatible** with the Switch 2 edition, and no upgrade path from the Switch version is planned.

Sources: [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/64085-check-out-the-latest-trailer-for-dragon-quest-xi-s-definitive-edition-switch-2) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/05/dragon-quest-xi-s-echoes-of-an-elusive-age-definitive-edition-coming-to-switch-2-on-september-24) · [Nintendo Wire](https://nintendowire.com/news/2026/05/27/dragon-quest-xi-s-gets-the-switch-2-treatment-in-a-new-definitive-edition-port/)
