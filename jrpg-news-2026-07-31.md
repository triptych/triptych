## Pokémon Pokopia: "Bubbly Basin" Expansion Pass and Version 2.0 Revealed for August 5

**The Pokémon Company** announced on **July 29** that the first paid expansion for its Switch 2 life-sim RPG **Pokémon Pokopia** will arrive on **August 5, 2026**, alongside a free **Version 2.0** update.

**Bubbly Basin** is the first of three planned Expansion Pass parts. It adds an entirely new underwater area centered around **Bubblebottom**, a sunken city players can visit and develop. New Pokémon joining the roster include **Mudkip**, **Popplio**, and others native to the aquatic region. An Expansion Pass is now available for purchase; the second and third parts are expected in late 2026 and 2027 respectively.

The free **Version 2.0 update** introduces the move **Dive**, allowing **Ditto** — Pokopia's playable protagonist — to explore underwater environments for the first time. Version 2.0 also adds the Mythical Pokémon **Manaphy** and launches simultaneously with the paid DLC at no cost to all owners.

## The Relic: First Guardian Launches Today on PC; Switch 2 Version Coming This Summer

The dark fantasy action RPG **The Relic: First Guardian** released today, **July 31**, on **PC via Steam**. A **Nintendo Switch 2** version is confirmed for later in summer 2026.

Set in the apocalyptic world of **Arsiltus** — a formerly prosperous land destroyed when a great relic was shattered, leaving it swallowed by a void — the game tasks players with recovering the relic's pieces to seal the void. The world draws on **Korean mythology** for its lore, monsters, and aesthetics. Combat is Soulslike in structure, with deliberate dodge-and-counter mechanics and challenging bosses.

The game is available now on **PC** and **PS5** at $59.99. The **Switch 2** release follows later this summer with a date yet to be confirmed.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/06/the-relic-first-guardian-launches-july-31-for-ps5-and-pc-later-this-summer-for-xbox-series-and-switch-2) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/06/the-relic-first-guardian-hittin-pc-ps5-end-of-july/) · [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/the-relic-first-guardian-july-31-launch/)

## Exstetra HD Remaster — 13-Year Japan-Only JRPG Gets Its First English Release

**FuRyu**'s **Exstetra** — a fantasy turn-based JRPG that launched exclusively in Japan for **Nintendo 3DS** and **PlayStation Vita** in November 2013 — is now available worldwide for the first time via its **HD Remaster on PC (Steam)**, which launched on **July 30**.

The game carries a **$24.99** price with a **20% launch discount** (through August 12). For the first time, *Exstetra* is fully playable in **English**, alongside Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese. The remaster includes updated visuals and the full original content.

**Nintendo Switch 2** (and Switch) and **PS5** versions are confirmed for **September 3, 2026**.

Sources: [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/exstetra-hd-remaster-steam-launch-switch-2-ps5-release-date/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317148/20260525/exstetra-hd-remaster-hits-steam-july-30-13-year-japan-only-jrpg-gets-first-english-release.htm) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/05/exstetra-remaster-launching-in-july-console-versions-announced/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21002-exstetra-hd-remaster-steam-launch-discount-ps5-switch-2-release-date)

## Fuga: Melodies of Steel Goes Mobile; Sequels to Follow

**CyberConnect2**'s dark strategy RPG **Fuga: Melodies of Steel** launched on **iOS and Android on July 29**, marking the franchise's first mobile appearance. The port includes all bonus content from the original Deluxe Edition and all three Costume Packs.

The mobile version launched at a **20% discount** running through August 3. CyberConnect2 confirmed that **Fuga: Melodies of Steel 2** and **3** will also come to mobile in the future, with an announcement of new exclusive story adaptations planned for both sequels' mobile versions.

In *Fuga*, players command a group of children piloting a tank against an invading army, assigning each child to a unique gun turret with their own abilities and personality. The game is notable for its tonal balance of heartfelt character writing against brutal wartime stakes.

## Culdcept The First — 1997 Sega Saturn Classic Arrives on PC with First English Release

**Culdcept The First** launched on **PC via Steam on July 30**, bringing the 1997 **Sega Saturn** card-strategy RPG to the West for the first time with full **English language support**.

The release is priced at **$29.99**, with a **10% launch discount** available through **August 12**. The remaster adds quality-of-life features including **rewind**, **quick save**, and a **reveal hands** option. The game blends board game traversal with collectible-card-based combat — players build decks and use spells and creature cards to contest territory on a game board.

**Console versions** for Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PS5 are set for **September 8, 2026** in the West.

## Blue Reflection Quartet — Launch Reviews Mixed to Positive

**Koei Tecmo / Gust**'s **Blue Reflection Quartet** collection launched on **July 30** across Switch 2, Switch, and PC (also PS5) at **$49.99**. The bundle packages the two mainline entries — *Blue Reflection* and *Blue Reflection: Second Light* — alongside the *Blue Reflection Ray* anime adaptation and the mobile game *Blue Reflection Sun*.

Launch reviews have been mixed-to-positive, with critics praising the core RPG entries while flagging the ancillary content:

- **Final Weapon** — **4/5**: Praised as a "magical girl JRPG collection focused on friendship, personal growth, and heartfelt storytelling."
- **Shacknews** — **7/10**: Highlighted the two main games as "neat, compelling takes on the magical girl genre," though found the *Ray* anime adaptation awkward and *Sun* still a middling gacha experience.
- **Game8** — **68/100**: Described as "less than the sum of its parts," with the *Ray* and *Sun* adaptations rated as lackluster.
- **RPG Site**: Called it "tremendous value and a great way to start the series," with caveats about the weaker components.

Sources: [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/30/blue-reflection-quartet-launch-trailer-illustration/) · [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150181/blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [Game8](https://game8.co/reviews/blue-reflection-quartet/blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20992-blue-reflection-quartet-review)
