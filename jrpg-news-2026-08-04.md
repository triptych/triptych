## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct — Full Gameplay Showcase Aired Today

Nintendo aired a dedicated **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Direct** today, August 4, at 10:00 a.m. ET, delivering approximately **20 minutes** of new information about the September 17 release.

The presentation formally introduced the four playable protagonists and their English voice cast:

- **Cai** (voiced by **Zeno Robinson**) — a young boy fighting to free his imprisoned father
- **Dietrich** (voiced by **Xander Mobus**) — a wandering swordsman who craves ever-stronger opponents
- **Theodora** (voiced by **Shara Kirby**) — a queen striving to fulfill her homeland's generations-long dream
- **Leda** (voiced by **Katiana Sarkessian**) — a musician driven by a deeply personal vendetta

The story takes place **five years after the Heroic Games**, a continent-wide tournament that defines the social order of the Dagdan Empire. The demon god **Balor** has been revived, the four Heroes who won the last Heroic Games have vanished, and the progenitor god **Sothis** tasks a single chosen soul with unravelling the catastrophe—by travelling back in time through the Heroic Games themselves. Players choose one protagonist at the start but can **switch between all four narratives** throughout, experiencing their parallel journeys as the full truth gradually comes into focus.

The Direct detailed the revised class and combat systems. The traditional weapon-triangle is replaced with a **skill-affinity web** that rewards pairing specific unit archetypes. A new mechanic called **Favour** tracks relationship depth in battle: high-Favour pairs gain access to **Dual Arts**, a set of combo attacks unavailable to strangers. New special classes unique to Fortune's Weave include the **Blade Dancer** and the **Storm Knight**.

A **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Dagdan Collection** physical bundle—containing a steel game case, character art cards, a map of the continent of Dagda, and a hardcover artbook—will be available on September 17 alongside the standard edition. Pre-orders for both are live now on the Nintendo eShop.

## Final Fantasy XIV Is Now Live on Nintendo Switch 2

**Final Fantasy XIV** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2** earlier today, marking the MMORPG's debut on a Nintendo platform after more than a decade on PC and PlayStation.

**Editions and pricing:**

| Edition | Price |
|---|---|
| Starter Edition (A Realm Reborn through Shadowbringers — up to level 80) | $19.99 |
| Complete Edition (all expansions through Dawntrail) | $59.99 (pre-order: $41.99) |
| Complete Collector's Edition | $139.99 (pre-order: $97.99) |

The **free trial** — covering A Realm Reborn through Shadowbringers with the standard trial restrictions — launched simultaneously on the Nintendo eShop. Existing PC or PlayStation subscribers who want to play on Switch 2 must purchase a **separate Switch 2 subscription**, though a 50% discount applies for cross-platform subscribers during the early access period.

On the technical side, the Switch 2 version targets **30 FPS in both docked and handheld modes**—half the framerate of the PC and PS5 versions. The estimated install size is **117 GB**. Mouse-and-keyboard input is supported via the USB-C port in docked mode. Producer Naoki Yoshida acknowledged frame-rate variability in highly populated areas but expressed confidence in the overall stability of the version.

A free month of subscription time is included with new Switch 2 accounts to support the server onboarding period.

## Beast of Reincarnation — Game Freak's AAA Debut Launches Today to Mixed Reviews

**Game Freak** — the studio behind every mainline Pokémon game — shipped its first original AAA title outside the Pokémon franchise today: **Beast of Reincarnation**, an action RPG set in a post-apocalyptic Japan of the year 4026. The game is available now on **PC via Steam**, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. It is also included in Game Pass at launch.

Players control **Emma the Sealer** and her dog **Koo** as they navigate a world overtaken by a spreading corruption called the Crucible. Combat is built around Koo's abilities, with Emma directing and amplifying her companion's attacks rather than fighting directly — a structure reviewers widely praised as distinct and engaging.

**Critical reception** lands at approximately **73 on Metacritic**. Famitsu awarded it **35/40** (scores of 8/9/9/9), calling it a bold and heartfelt debut. CGMagazine gave it **8.5/10**, citing it as one of the year's best action games. VGC scored it **6/10**, praising the world and the Emma-Koo dynamic but calling the narrative undercooked. Early Steam user reviews are mixed — roughly 52% positive from over 700 reviews as of launch day.

The consensus picture: a compelling visual mood and inventive companion-led combat system, held back by uneven storytelling and some technical rough edges. For a studio whose entire commercial history is Pokémon, the ambition alone has drawn substantial attention.

## Blue Reflection Quartet — Reviews Are In for the July 30 Launch

**BLUE REFLECTION Quartet** launched July 30 on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam**, and PlayStation 5 at $49.99 — and the critical reception is divided.

The compilation bundles:

- **BLUE REFLECTION** (2017) and **BLUE REFLECTION: Second Light** (2021) as fully playable entries
- The **BLUE REFLECTION RAY** anime series
- The **BLUE REFLECTION SUN** mobile game in a playable standalone form

Reviewers who valued the package as a gateway to the series were generally positive. The **Outerhaven** described it as fitting four experiences into one collection and called it worthwhile for newcomers. **Cubed3** gave it **8/10**. **Final Weapon** awarded **4/5**, praising the magical-girl JRPG themes of friendship and personal growth.

Harsher takes centered on the weaker entries dragging down the compilation. **Noisy Pixel** headlined its review "A Hollow Compilation That Fails The Series Legacy," and **Game8** called it "Half-Baked." The adaptations of Ray and SUN in particular were flagged as thin compared to the two proper games.

**The Pokémon Company** will release the first chapter of the **Pokémon Pokopia Expansion Pass**, subtitled **"Bubbly Basin"**, on **August 5** for **Nintendo Switch 2**, alongside a free **Version 2.0.0** update for all Pokopia owners.

**Bubbly Basin** introduces an underwater town accessible from the main world. New gameplay additions include:

- **Watermelon cultivation** with the help of Water-specialty Pokémon
- **Bubble machines and underwater streetlights** powered by Generate-specialty Pokémon
- New outfits, furniture, and prepared foods including **smoothies**
- The move **Surf**, with a power-up unique to Bubbly Basin

The free 2.0.0 update — available to all Pokopia owners on August 5 — grants players the move **Dive** from Manaphy, enabling underwater swimming alongside Water-type Pokémon and underwater building in the main game.

## Brigandine: Abyss Demo Now Available; Game Launches August 26–27

**Matrix Corporation** and **Happinet Games** (published in the West by **NIS America**) have opened a playable demo for **Brigandine: Abyss** on consoles, ahead of its **August 26** PC launch and **August 27** launch on **Nintendo Switch 2** and PlayStation 5. A Deluxe Edition for Switch 2 and PS5 bundles a mini art book and digital soundtrack.

Set in the all-new continent of **Meltitea**, the grand strategy/TRPG offers **six independent story campaigns** — each representing a different faction's perspective on the resurgence of the **Abyssloa Empire**, a dark force sealed away centuries ago by the arcane Brigandine artifact. The game is explicitly designed to be newcomer-accessible.

Combat takes place on **hexagonal grids** with **monster-binding** as a core mechanic: officers summon and develop creatures whose elemental affinities and skills must be factored into every engagement. Between battles, an **Organization Phase** has players manage territory, resources, troop recruitment, and positioning across the continent map.

A preview from **Dual Shockers** called it "a natural and incredible evolution of the series' formula," while **RPGamer**'s preview highlighted the expanded campaign length and richer faction differentiation compared to *The Legend of Runersia*.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio Switch 2 Port — Technical Preview Raises Concerns About Framerate

**RPG Site** has published a technical preview of **Metaphor: ReFantazio**'s upcoming Switch 2 port, conducted in docked mode at a preview event. The game targets **60 FPS** but shows **noticeable dips in dungeon sections**, with the framerate described as hovering closer to stable 60 in open areas while dropping in denser environments.

The preview notes that **DLSS has not yet been implemented** in the build shown, and calls the port "frustratingly close to being an easy recommendation" — the foundation is there, but the framerate consistency and upscaling support need refinement before the November 12 launch.

The Switch 2 version is priced at approximately **¥2,990 / ~$25** — significantly below the original release — reflecting that it is a straight port of the 2024 Game Awards Game of the Year without exclusive additions.
