## Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster Arrives on Switch 2; Reviews Call It the Definitive Console Version

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster** launched digitally on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **July 23, 2026**, timed to the 25th anniversary of the original PlayStation 2 release of *Final Fantasy X*. The collection is priced at **$49.99**; a physical game-key card edition releases on **August 27** in Japan, with North American and European physical releases on the same date.

The Switch 2 version runs at **4K/60fps in TV Mode and 1080p/60fps in handheld** — a substantial improvement over the original Switch release. Quality-of-life options include toggleable XP boosters, random encounter settings, and the ability to switch between the original and the Masashi Hamauzu-arranged soundtracks at any time. Both *FFX* and *FFX-2* are included alongside all previously released bonus content.

Critical reception has been broadly positive. Reviewers across multiple outlets describe it as the **definitive console experience** for both games and highlight the noticeable image quality jump over the original Switch port. A technical comparison published by Nintendo Life confirms substantially sharper textures and character models in both docked and handheld configurations.

## Disgaea Mayhem Launches July 23; Reviews Praise Combat, Flag Short Campaign

**NIS America**'s **Disgaea Mayhem** — the western localization of *Kyouran Makaism: Frenzy of the Netherworld* — released on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam** on **July 23, 2026**.

The game abandons the series' traditional grid-based strategy format in favor of a **musou-style action RPG**, casting players as mercenary **N.A.** alongside Princess **Tichelle** in a demon-filled adventure themed around flan. Seven weapon classes are available, each with distinct move sets.

Critical consensus has quickly coalesced around two consistent points: **the combat is genuinely fun and mechanically strong**, but the campaign runs **under 10 hours** and the level design is repetitive throughout. Multiple outlets characterized it as a recommendable title for existing Disgaea fans looking for something lighter, but a weak proposition for newcomers or players expecting a full-length game. Review scores have clustered in the mid-to-upper-6 range.

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition Overview Trailer Reveals MOMO, New Mode, and Costumes

Nintendo released an **overview trailer** for **Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** on **July 24, 2026**, fully detailing the new content included with the upgrade just six days before its **July 30 digital launch**.

New additions confirmed by the trailer:

- **MOMO** — a new rare Blade who can toggle between light and dark combat forms. The character's design draws from the *Xenosaga* series (specifically KOS-MOS), making this a notable cross-series addition.
- **Merc Assault** — a new mode that lets players directly control up to six Blades in squad-based mercenary battles, expanding on the existing mercenary dispatch system.
- New alternate costume designs for **Pyra and Mythra**.
- **Amiibo support**.
- Technical improvements: **4K/60fps in TV Mode**, **1080p/60fps in handheld**, and smoothed cutscenes throughout.

The upgrade costs **$9.99 / £7.99** for existing Switch owners. The new digital version launches **July 30, 2026**; a physical cartridge release (not game-key card) follows on **October 1, 2026**.

## Midgar Studio Liquidated; NACON's Financial Collapse Claims Second RPG Developer

A French court ordered the **liquidation of Midgar Studio** on **July 21, 2026**, ending the Montpellier-based developer's 18-year run. The studio — known for open-world RPG **Edge of Eternity** (2021) and the in-development action RPG sequel **Edge of Memories** — was acquired by publisher **NACON** in 2022.

Founder **Jérémy Zeler-Maury** confirmed that the team pursued buyers and legal options up to the court-imposed deadline, but no acquisition materialized. The fate of **Edge of Memories**, which had been targeting a 2026 release on PC and consoles, is currently unknown.

This is the second major RPG developer lost in NACON's ongoing financial difficulties. Paris-based **Spiders** (*GreedFall*, *Steelrising*) was liquidated in **April 2026** after *GreedFall 2: The Dying World* underperformed in early access. Midgar's closure comes less than three months later.

## Langrisser: Sea of Sword Opens Steam Page; Yasunori Nishiki Composing

**BlackJack Studio** and **HongKong GameTree Limited** opened a **Steam page** and released a full **gameplay trailer** for **Langrisser: Sea of Sword** on **July 21, 2026**, following the title's initial reveal on July 15.

The game is billed as a **next-generation tactical RPG** in the long-running Langrisser series, targeting PC (Steam), iOS, and Android, with "other platforms" TBD. Story details: players control **"the Nameless,"** an amnesiac protagonist journeying with a white-haired woman named **Eta** across a continent shrouded in magical mist.

Mechanical highlights include a **Dual Hero system** — two heroes share a battlefield slot, and players can freely switch which acts as primary or secondary — and a departure from the series' traditional linear stage-by-stage structure toward integrated free exploration. The full original score is being composed by **Yasunori Nishiki** (*Octopath Traveler*, *Triangle Strategy*). Pre-registration is open on the official website.

## RPGamer Publishes .hack//Z.E.R.O. Interview — CyberConnect2 on Decade of Development

**RPGamer** published an interview with **CyberConnect2 Vice President Taichiro Miyazaki** about **.hack//Z.E.R.O.** during the July 12–19 period, following a panel the studio held at **Anime Expo 2026**.

Key points from the conversation: CyberConnect2 has been preparing the project **for approximately a decade**, with the studio's **30th anniversary** serving as the catalyst to finally push it forward. The game is self-planned and self-published with a license from IP holder **Bandai Namco**, which Miyazaki said gives the team complete creative control.

On design direction, Miyazaki drew a comparison to the **Persona series' evolution** from its early entries to *Persona 3* and *4* — a significant internal reinvention that kept the franchise's soul while modernizing every surface it touched. The .hack//Z.E.R.O. team is aiming for a similar result: an action RPG set **ten years in the future** of the .hack universe that is welcoming to newcomers while honoring the series' duality of game world and real world.

Platforms and a release window have not been announced.

Sources: [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/hack-z-e-r-o-interview/) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/hack-zero-interview-cyberconnect2-series-revival-modern-jrpg/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/19644-hackzero-seems-to-be-cyberconnect2s-answer-in-revitalizing-sustaining-dot-hack-into-future)

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Nintendo Today! Drops Second Wave of Character Details

**Nintendo Today!** and **Serenes Forest** published a second round of character reveals for **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** on **July 21, 2026**, building on the four-protagonist introductions covered earlier.

The update introduced supporting characters attached to each lead. A small thematic thread: each protagonist's closest advisor has **impaired vision** — Tialla wears glasses, Fabio has a lazy eye, Bonaventure wears an eyepatch, and Buccar has a burn scar that keeps one eye permanently closed. Mercenary **Mikaela** (known as "**Mikaela the Ironblood**") was also revealed — a retired warrior who came to the Dagdan Empire's capital to fund her daughter's education.

On the mechanical side, the update confirmed that **combat arts, gambits, and Crests** from *Three Houses* return, and that each of the four main characters has a **unique personal combat ability** (Cai's shown ability is a large-area dark magic burst).

**Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** releases exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 17, 2026**.
