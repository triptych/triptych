## Tales of Eternia Remastered Launches Today — Reviews Mixed but Favorable

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** is out today, **October 16, 2026**, on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PS4, PS5). The remaster of the 2001 PlayStation original carries a **Metacritic score of 76** and an **OpenCritic rating of 'Strong' (77)** based on 16 critics at launch.

The headline addition to the remaster is a **brand-new English dub** covering main story scenes and more than **250 skits** — the game's original Western release had minimal English voice work. Quality-of-life features include destination markers on the field map, an enemy encounter toggle, and high-speed auto-battle mode.

Reviewers broadly praised the story, cast chemistry, and the **Craymel Cage magic system**, while flagging the combat's age and uneven pacing as weaknesses by modern standards. Notable scores include **Noisy Pixel (85/100)**, which called the Craymel Cage system "surprisingly deep" and praised the game's brisk overall pace; **CGMagazine (7.5/10)**, which highlighted the restoration of content cut from prior North American releases; and **Siliconera**, which called it "a much needed and well-handled remaster." **Shacknews (5/10)** was the harshest, criticizing visual filtering applied to anime cutscenes and calling the package another disappointing entry in a franchise of inconsistent remasters.

Sources: [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/21435/tales-of-eternia-remastered) · [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/tales-of-eternia-remastered/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21563-tales-eternia-remastered-review) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-tales-of-eternia-remastered-is-a-much-needed-remaster/) · [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151003/tales-of-eternia-remastered-review-score) · [CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/tales-of-eternia-remastered-ps5/)

## Final Fantasy Resonance — Runtime Revealed, Free Demo Available Ahead of October 22 Launch

**Square Enix** confirmed this week that **Final Fantasy Resonance** will take **30 to 40 hours** to complete the main story, with full completion requiring **60 to 80 hours**. The game launches on **October 22, 2026** across **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** ($49.99).

A **free Chapter 1 demo** is currently available, and save data carries over to the full game. The demo goes beyond the earlier TGS build and includes more of the HD-2D world map.

Final Fantasy Resonance was revealed at the June 9 Nintendo Direct and represents the franchise's first mainline entry built in the HD-2D visual style. The runtime estimate comes from the development team via Square Enix's pre-launch materials.

## Dragon's Dogma II: Dark Arisen Review Roundup — Scores Strong Across the Board

**Capcom**'s **Dragon's Dogma II: Dark Arisen** expansion, which launched **October 9** for **Nintendo Switch 2 and PC** (also PS5), has collected a strong round of reviews over the past week.

The expansion adds the northern region **Norgen**, a new **Fallen Dragon** antagonist, **12 unique dungeon challenges** as part of the Relic Expedition Loop, and a Relic gear appraisal system. Content estimates put the expansion at roughly **25 additional hours**.

Critic scores clustered around 8/10, with standouts including **RPGFan (95/100)**, **Push Square (9/10)**, and **IGN (8/10)**; the most reserved was **RPGamer (3.5/5)**, whose reviewer noted that players who didn't click with the original will find little here to change their minds. COGconnected called it "a worthy return to the wild."

The Switch 2 version uses **FSR frame interpolation** to hit a smooth framerate — Capcom confirmed this at Tokyo Game Show as the studio's first use of the technique.

Sources: [Stevivor](https://stevivor.com/news/dragon-s-dogma-2-dark-arisen-expansion-earns-mostly-strong-reviews/) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/10/round-up-the-reviews-for-dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-are-in) · [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/21470/view) · [Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-adds-around-25-hours-of-new-content-for-players-1747293/) · [COGconnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/dragons-dogma-2-dark-arisen-review/)
