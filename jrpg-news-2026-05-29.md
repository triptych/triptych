# JRPG News — May 29, 2026

## Mina the Hollower Launches Today

**Yacht Club Games** releases **Mina the Hollower** today on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 1 & 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC** (Steam and Humble Store) for **$19.99**. The game was originally scheduled for Halloween 2025 before being pushed to this date.

Mina the Hollower is a top-down action-adventure with sprite art styled to evoke the look of a **Game Boy Color** title. Players control Mina, a researcher who uses a burrowing technique called "Hollowing" to dart underground, solve environmental puzzles, and navigate combat — a movement mechanic that recalls *Shovel Knight* while the overall design draws from **The Legend of Zelda** and **Dark Souls**. The island setting is under a curse, and Mina is there to lift it.

Early reviews have pushed the game to one of the **highest Metacritic scores of 2026** so far, with outlets praising the tight controls, handcrafted level design, and atmosphere.

## Indie Quest 2026 Showcases New Indie JRPGs

The **Indie Quest 2026** showcase aired on **May 28** (8 PM Eastern / 2 AM CEST), delivering over **30 trailers** and several world premieres across the indie JRPG space. The event was co-organized by **Retrovibe** and accompanied by a **Steam festival** with demos and discounts. Two standout announcements:

### Full Circle

Solo developer **2ndPlayerGames** (from Peru) and publisher **Deck13 Spotlight** — best known for publishing *Chained Echoes* — revealed **Full Circle**, a **2.5HD turn-based JRPG** inspired by the PlayStation 1 golden era. The developer cites **Lunar, Breath of Fire, early Final Fantasy,** and **The Legend of Dragoon** as primary reference points.

The setting is a post-apocalyptic world where mutated creatures have overrun the surface and humanity shelters in floating cities. A generation of young heroes is now being sent down to reclaim the ground below. Combat uses a "constant flow" system built on **timed inputs, chained attacks, combos, and party swaps**. The game is coming to **PC and consoles** and will be playable for the first time at **Gamescom** later this year.

### Colossus: Eternal Blight

Six-person Phoenix-based studio **Rustic Panda Games** premiered **Colossus: Eternal Blight** at the showcase and launched a **Kickstarter campaign** immediately after. The game is a pixel-art action RPG inspired by **Hyper Light Drifter** and **Chrono Trigger**, following a hero named **Lucian** who is trying to save his brother **Lance** from a corruption called the Blight that is spreading across the kingdom of **Eireos**.

Combat is built around **parries, dodges, and fluid combos**, and the narrative branches based on player choices — players can decide whether to aid or abandon struggling citizens as the Blight spreads. This is the studio's debut title.

Other games shown during the event include **Alabaster Dawn**, **SacriFire**, **Edge of Memories**, **Spark Ignites**, and **Infinite Alliance**.

## Story of Seasons: Grand Bazaar Arrives on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

**Marvelous** launched **Story of Seasons: Grand Bazaar** on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S** yesterday (**May 28**) alongside a free **Version 1.5 update** for all platforms. This marks the franchise's first appearance on PlayStation and Xbox hardware.

The game is a full 3D remake of the 2010 Nintendo DS title *Harvest Moon DS: Grand Bazaar*, set in the mountain farming community of **Zephyr Town**, whose once-famous weekly bazaar has fallen into disrepair. Players farm, raise animals, and craft goods to sell at stall each week, gradually reviving the bazaar. The remake features an expanded character roster, a larger world, and — a franchise first — **full voice acting** during story events.

Pricing: **$49.99** standard digital, **$59.99** Digital Deluxe (includes "Trunk of Transformation" cosmetic DLC), **$69.99** Super Digital Deluxe (adds digital artbook and soundtrack). A **$49.99 physical PS5 edition** is also available. The game was previously available on Nintendo Switch 1 & 2 and PC.
