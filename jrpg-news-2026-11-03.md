## Metaphor: ReFantazio Launches on Nintendo Switch 2 in Nine Days — Free Demo and SteelBook Confirmed

**ATLUS and SEGA** have confirmed all final details ahead of the **November 12 Nintendo Switch 2 launch** of [**Metaphor: ReFantazio**](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/19530-metaphor-refantazio-is-coming-to-nintendo-switch-2-on-november-12). The port arrives at a reduced price point — **$39.99 / €39.99** versus the original release's $59.99 — and a **free prologue demo** is already available on the Nintendo eShop, letting players begin their journey before the full game unlocks.

A limited **SteelBook Edition** will be available at launch in western regions. In Japan, ATLUS is offering a standard edition at ¥3,990 and a **¥5,990 Atlus 35th Digital Anniversary Edition** that bundles additional content celebrating the publisher's milestone year.

The Switch 2 version runs at **4K docked / 1080p handheld** and takes full advantage of the console's improved hardware. The port does not add exclusive story content but is otherwise a complete version of the award-winning 2024 JRPG. Mouse and touchpad support are available through Switch 2 accessory input.

## Atelier Resleriana Japan Service Ends November 25 — Paid Currency Refunds Open Through February

[**Koei Tecmo and Gust**](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) will shut down the Japanese server of **Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator** on **November 25, 2026, at 16:00 JST**, bringing an end to the mobile and PC live-service title in its home territory. Sales of premium currency and items were halted on **August 27**, and a paid-currency **refund window** will remain open from **November 26 through February 24, 2027**.

In a statement, the publisher cited difficulty continuing to provide a service that satisfies users. The global version had already shut down in March 2025.

Players should note that the console spin-off **Atelier Resleriana: The Red Alchemist & the White Guardian** (Nintendo Switch / Switch 2, PC via Steam) is a separate product and is **not affected** by the service closure.

Sources: [Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/koei-tecmos-atelier-resleriana-to-end-services-in-japan-on-november-25/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21219-atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-polar-night-liberator-shuts-down-in-japan-on-november-25) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/atelier-resleriana-to-end-service-in-japan/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/08/27/atelier-resleriana-forgotten-alchemy-and-the-polar-night-liberator-ends-service-on-november-25-in-japan/)

## AURORA REQUIEM: The Lullaby of Calvaris Launches November 16 on PC

[**AURORA REQUIEM: The Lullaby of Calvaris**](https://techwiser.com/upcoming-rpg-games/) is a new turn-based JRPG scheduled to launch on **PC via Steam on November 16, 2026**. The title draws on classic JRPG conventions — a party-based structure, narrative-driven combat, and a world built around the Calvaris mythology — while introducing its own take on the lullaby leitmotif as a gameplay mechanic. Full details about the combat system and story scope have been limited ahead of release, but it is one of the more notable original JRPG releases landing on PC this month.

Sources: [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/upcoming-rpg-games/) · [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/top-turn-based-rpgs-and-strategy-games-coming-in-november-2026/)

## Food Devils Enters Steam Early Access Today

[**Food Devils**](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/top-turn-based-rpgs-and-strategy-games-coming-in-november-2026/), a monster-collecting turn-based RPG where the creatures are personified foods, launches in **Steam Early Access today, November 3**. The game pulls from the monster-collecting tradition of mainline Pokémon and Digimon Story but leans into a more comedic tone, with an overworld built around food biomes and a strategic party-building system.

Early Access gives players access to the first major region and its associated content, with the developer planning regular updates before a full 1.0 release.

Sources: [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/top-turn-based-rpgs-and-strategy-games-coming-in-november-2026/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/upcoming-rpg-releases/)

## Threads of Time New Trailer Reveals Release Window

A new trailer for [**Threads of Time**](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/top-turn-based-rpgs-and-strategy-games-coming-in-november-2026/), the Chrono Trigger-inspired JRPG in development for **PC**, dropped this week and reportedly contains a release window — though current coverage has not published the specific window date. The game has been building wishlists steadily (it cleared 350K prior to the July 27 Dev Day demo) and its Chrono Trigger lineage is its primary pitch: time-travel mechanics, an ensemble cast with interlocking timelines, and a classic turn-based battle system.

The new trailer is the first substantial footage since the game's Day of the Devs appearance. More details are expected from the developer shortly.

## Persona 4 Revival Gets Nintendo Switch 2 Date — May 20, 2027

New reporting confirms that [**Persona 4 Revival**](https://www.notebookcheck.org/Persona-4-Revival-tendra-como-fecha-de-lanzamiento-febrero-de-2027.1403893.0.html), previously dated for **February 2027** on PC and other platforms, will arrive on **Nintendo Switch 2 on May 20, 2027** — approximately three months after the initial launch. The game is a full remake of Persona 4 Golden rather than a port of the original 2008 version.

This is the first confirmed Nintendo platform release date for the title. Switch 2 players will receive the same remake content as the initial release, with no exclusive additions confirmed at this time.

Sources: [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.org/Persona-4-Revival-tendra-como-fecha-de-lanzamiento-febrero-de-2027.1403893.0.html) · [Wikipedia — Persona 4 Revival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persona_4_Revival)
