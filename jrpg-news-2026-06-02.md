# JRPG News — June 2, 2026

## PlayStation State of Play: God of War Faye Spin-Off Officially Revealed

The big headline from today's **PlayStation State of Play** (2 PM PT) was the official reveal of a new **God of War** spin-off starring **Faye**, Kratos' second wife and mother of Atreus. Leaks from insider Tom Henderson had circulated ahead of the show, and Santa Monica Studio confirmed them in full.

Key details from the reveal:

- **Faye** is the playable lead, and the game is set after the events of *God of War: Ragnarök*
- Combat is described as faster and more stylish than the Norse trilogy — comparisons to **Devil May Cry** have been widely made
- The game ventures beyond Norse mythology to explore **Chinese, Japanese, and Mayan** mythological traditions
- **Kratos will appear**, reportedly as a boss encounter
- Targeted release: **first half of 2027**

No official title was given; the game is currently referred to by press and fans as *God of War: Faye*. While not a JRPG, the inclusion of Japanese mythology and the DMC-adjacent action style has made this one of the most-discussed announcements of the week among action RPG fans.

## State of Play: Marvel's Wolverine Confirmed for September 15

The show opened with an extended gameplay look at **Marvel's Wolverine** (Insomniac Games), confirming a **September 15, 2026** release for PlayStation 5. The extended demo showcased Wolverine's bone-claw variant, the berserker rage system, and a New Orleans-set open area. While not an RPG, it's a major PS5 title landing in the same fall window that fans are eyeing for potential JRPG releases.

---

## Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales — Demo Live, Playtime Revealed

**Square Enix** and Team Asano (the team behind *Octopath Traveler* and *Bravely Default*) have dropped a free **prologue demo** for **The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales** across all platforms — PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam. Save data carries over to the full game.

Nintendo Life also reported the full game's runtime estimates this week:

- **Standard playthrough**: ~30 hours
- **Full completion** (all eras explored): ~50 hours

The game casts protagonist Elliot and his fairy companion Faie across four historical ages in the continent of Philabieldia on a thousand-year mission. The game uses the series' signature **HD-2D** visual style blended with **action-based combat**. It launches **June 18, 2026**.

## Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World Announced (DQ Monsters 4)

Covered briefly in the May 27 Dragon Quest 40th Anniversary stream alongside *Beyond Dreams* (see [yesterday's roundup](jrpg-news-2026-06-01.md)), **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** — known as **Dragon Quest Monsters 4** in Japan — deserves a closer look:

- **Protagonists**: **Bianca** and **Nera**, both returning characters from *Dragon Quest V: Hand of the Heavenly Bride*
- The premise centers on restoring life to a "desolate, withered world"
- Platforms: **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC** (Steam / Microsoft Store)
- No release date, but series creator **Yuji Horii** noted the team hopes to release it **before Dragon Quest XII**
- Rated E10+ by the ESRB

The return of DQ5's beloved Bianca/Nera dynamic to a Monsters spinoff has generated significant fan enthusiasm, as DQ5 is widely regarded as one of the franchise's emotional peaks.

## Indie Quest 2026: Ten Turn-Based JRPGs Showcased

The dedicated indie JRPG showcase **Indie Quest 2026** (part of the Summer Game Fest satellite events) aired earlier this week, spotlighting over **30 projects** including 6 world premieres. Turn Based Lovers highlighted ten standout turn-based titles from the event. Notable announcements:

- **Full Circle** — A pixel art story-driven JRPG directly inspired by the PSX golden era of the genre (Final Fantasy VII–IX era aesthetics)
- **Gravastar** — A sci-fi fantasy Japanese-style RPG announced for PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC
- **Emery Hearts** — An action RPG launching on **PC via Steam on September 2**
- **Runesmith** — A dwarven tactical RPG blending "rune-forging, tavern brawls, and status-effect combinations"

Playable demos for several titles were made available through a Steam co-event hosted with Retrovibe.

## Follow-Up: Final Fantasy VI Lands on Xbox Game Pass Today

As previewed [yesterday](jrpg-news-2026-06-01.md), the pixel remaster of **Final Fantasy VI** is now live on **Xbox Game Pass** as of today, June 2. The remaster features redrawn HD-2D sprites and a rearranged score. It remains available on Steam as a standalone purchase.
