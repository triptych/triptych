## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure — PS5 and Switch 2 Versions Out Today

The **PS5 and Switch 2 editions** of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** are now available as of today, published by **NIS America**.

Both games feature **4K resolution, 120 FPS**, and mouse input support on Switch 2. Each title is available digitally for **$39.99**, while NIS America is also selling a **Deluxe Edition physical set** bundling both games for **$69.99**. A limited collector's edition at **$109.99** includes both games alongside an art book, soundtrack CDs, holographic art cards, an acrylic stand, iron-on insignias, and a Mishelam Wonderland coaster set.

## Level-5 Vision 2026 II "Dream" — Professor Layton Remake, DecaPolice Delay, and More

**Level-5** held its **LEVEL5 VISION 2026 II Dream** online broadcast today (September 10 at 21:00 JST), unveiling the latest news on its upcoming lineup. English subtitled streams ran simultaneously.

### Professor Layton and the Curious Village Remake Announced

The showcase closed with a surprise reveal: **Professor Layton and the Curious Village**, the original 2007 DS entry that started the puzzle-adventure series, is receiving a full **remake** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam in 2027**.

The remake shifts the visual style from 2D to **full 3D character models** with high-resolution widescreen animations adapted for a single screen. Level-5 confirmed that exploration mechanics have been expanded. For longtime fans it's a chance to revisit St. Mystere in a new form; for newcomers it's the complete beginning of the Professor and Luke's partnership.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/professor-layton-and-the-curious-village-remake-announced-for-ps5-switch-2-switch-and-pc) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-09-10/professor-layton-and-the-curious-village-game-gets-remake/.241627) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/professor-layton-curious-village-remake-2027/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/professor-layton-and-the-curious-village-remake-revealed-for-nintendo-switch-2-switch) · [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25802/professor-layton-and-the-curious-village-remake-revealed-at-level-5-vision-showcase)

### DecaPolice Delayed to 2027

Level-5 CEO **Akihiro Hino** announced during the broadcast that the crime-suspense RPG **DecaPolice** is being pushed from its 2026 window to **2027**, citing the title's "whole new approach to detective work with cases that unfold in a virtual world." A new trailer accompanied the announcement, and a **TGS 2026 demo** was confirmed for Tokyo Game Show.

## Eternal Anima Announced — Final Fantasy and Persona Veterans, March 2027

**Marvelous** unveiled **Eternal Anima**, a "legacy-driven time-travel RPG" in a HD-2D-esque visual style, during a **Nintendo Direct** presentation on September 9. The game was previously teased in 2023 as **PROJECT LIFE is RPG**.

The creative team behind Eternal Anima reads as a celebration of 1990s golden-era JRPGs:

- **Director:** Katsuhisa Higuchi (*Final Fantasy* series, *Chrono Trigger*)
- **Story Concept:** Keisuke Makino (*Persona 5*, *Metaphor: ReFantazio*)
- **Scenario Writer:** Kazushige Nojima (*Final Fantasy VII* Remake trilogy, and much of the mainline FF series)

The setting is a world covered almost entirely by ocean, and the story is built around time-travel as a central mechanic. Marvelous describes the game as "dedicated to JRPG fans" and explicitly inspired by the genre's golden era.

**Eternal Anima** launches **March 4, 2027** on **PC, Nintendo Switch 2**, and other platforms. Pricing is **$49.99** (PS5 standard), **$59.99** (Switch 2 full cartridge), and **$79.99/$89.99** for Limited Edition on the respective platforms.

## Muramasa: Revenant Blades Gets February 4, 2027 Release Date

**Vanillaware** and publisher **Marvelous** have set **February 4, 2027** as the global launch date for **Muramasa: Revenant Blades** on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**.

The game combines the original *Muramasa: The Demon Blade* (Wii/PS Vita) with all four **Genroku Legends** side-story DLC chapters into a single complete release. Set in Genroku-era Japan, the game features **six playable protagonists**, each with their own separate storyline that can be experienced in any order. New additions include **4K visuals, English voice acting**, and new gameplay systems not present in the Vita version.

Ten minutes of new gameplay was shared alongside the date announcement.

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — Hands-On Preview Coverage

Media hands-on impressions for **Final Fantasy VII Revelation**, the third and final chapter of the *Final Fantasy VII Remake* trilogy, have begun publishing today. The playable demo — reportedly shown at Gamescom in late August — allowed access to the **Highwind airship**, free open-world traversal, and sub-quests.

Key gameplay details from preview coverage:

- **Vincent Valentine and Cid Highwind** join as playable party members. Vincent is described as lightning-fast with high burst damage.
- A **grappling hook** mechanic (mapped to L2) adds a new traversal option for navigating terrain and structures.
- **Piko the Chocobo** is an upgradable party companion who can swim, scale walls, and learn skills such as **Chocojet** for gliding. Piko plays a story role, not just a utility one.
- The open world is described by multiple outlets as even more ambitious in scale than *Rebirth*'s.

Final Fantasy VII Revelation is scheduled for **Spring 2027** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PC**, and other platforms.

## The Legend of Fancy Realm Remake Launches on PC

**The Legend of Fancy Realm Remake** is now available on **PC via Steam**. The title is a ground-up remake of a 1998 Chinese tactical RPG returning after 28 years to international audiences. The game blends turn-based tactical combat with character-driven storytelling.

New features for the remake include **full voice acting** for the entire main story, updated character art, and modern combat cutscenes. The remake also ships with the **original 1998 version** playable from within the launcher. An official in-game mod editor and **Steam Workshop** support let players customize fonts, voices, BGM, and character visuals. Post-launch updates will add additional DLC storylines.

Localizations include English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21130-the-legend-fancy-realm-remake-releases-for-pc-via-steam-on-september-10) · [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25512/return-after-28-years-the-legend-of-fancy-realm-remake-launches-september-10)

## Under Mythos — New Studio KADATH Announces Dark Fantasy JRPG

**KADATH**, a newly established Tokyo-based game studio founded by producer **Yoichi Miyaji**, has announced both the studio's formation and its debut title: **Under Mythos**, a dark fantasy JRPG coming to **Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam in 2028**.

The team's credits span **Pokémon, Tales of the Abyss, GRANDIA**, and **LUNAR** — a pedigree spanning multiple genre classics. Under Mythos centers on a lone cat who wanders into another world in search of their mother and discovers a mysterious **hot spring inn**, where the player takes a job caring for the supernatural guests who come to rest and recuperate. Between shifts, dungeon expeditions gather food and resources as the world around the inn gradually descends into madness.

KADATH is currently preparing a vertical slice to demonstrate the intended quality level and is seeking development collaboration partners.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/dark-fantasy-rpg-under-mythos-announced-for-switch-pc) · [IndieBunny](https://indiebunny.com/news/warm-baths-slow-madness-this-jrpg-turns-cozy-innkeeping-into-a-descent-into-the-otherworld)

## Final Fantasy Resonance — Demo Available, Sephiroth Confirmed as Boss

Announced at PlayStation's **September 3 State of Play**, the free demo for **Final Fantasy Resonance** is now live on **PC, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2** (and other platforms). The demo covers the entire first chapter of the game, roughly **three to five hours** of play, with save data carrying over to the full release.

The State of Play trailer also confirmed **Sephiroth** will appear in the full game as a seekable, optional boss encounter with a special reward, joining the previously revealed **Cloud** cameo. The HD-2D turn-based RPG launches **October 22, 2026** at **$49.99**.
