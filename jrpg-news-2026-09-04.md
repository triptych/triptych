## Final Fantasy VII Revelation Gets April 8, 2027 Release Date

During Sony's **PlayStation State of Play** broadcast on **September 3**, **Square Enix** confirmed that **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** will launch worldwide on **April 8, 2027** for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. Pre-orders went live alongside the announcement.

The broadcast included an extended **10-minute gameplay deep dive** exploring the game's systems and world, and a **Collector's Edition** was revealed. Final Fantasy VII Revelation is the concluding chapter of the Final Fantasy VII Remake trilogy.

## Konami Announces Rhapsody in Scarlet — 1920s NYC Action-RPG

**Konami** unveiled a brand-new action-RPG called **Rhapsody in Scarlet** at the September 3 State of Play. The game is set in a jazz-filled, alternate **1920s New York City**, where players control **Ellie**, a young magus who battles alongside magical beast companions referred to in the trailer as **Sköll**, **Balhut**, and **Thunderbird**.

The reveal trailer showed Ellie chaining magical attacks together with her beast partners in fast-paced combat. Additional details were teased for the subsequent **State of Play Japan** segment.

Rhapsody in Scarlet is confirmed for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**. No release date has been announced.

Sources: [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/new-konami-action-rpg-called-rhapsody-in-scarlet/) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/state-of-play/2026/09/03/konami-announces-rhapsody-in-scarlet-bringing-urban-fantasy-action-to-nyc) · [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/09/03/rhapsody-in-scarlet-announced-for-ps5/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/03/rhapsody-in-scarlet-announced-by-konami/)

## Rev. NOiR — Story and Gameplay Trailers Debut at State of Play and Konami Press Start

**Konami**'s upcoming action-RPG **Rev. NOiR** received two new trailers on September 3. The **story trailer** aired during the State of Play broadcast; a separate **gameplay trailer** followed hours later at the **Konami Press Start** showcase. Both confirm the game's **2027 release window**.

The trailers flesh out the world and premise: Rev. NOiR is set in a realm threatened by **Lightfall**, a supernatural catastrophe that brings death to everything it touches. Players control **Asch**, a young man issued a divine ordinance to save as many lives as possible before Lightfall consumes the world.

The game is directed by **Nobuki Kadoi** (Drakengard 2, Lord of Vermillion) and is developed by **ILCA** (Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl).

Rev. NOiR is targeting a **2027** launch on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Launches to Strong Reviews

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched today on **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam** — the first wholly new entry in the Onimusha franchise in over two decades. The game stars **Miyamoto Musashi** as its protagonist, pitting the legendary swordsman against demonic Genma forces.

Critical reception has been strong:

| Outlet | Score |
|---|---|
| IGN | 10/10 |
| Nintendo Life | 9/10 |
| Forbes | 9/10 |
| Giant Bomb | 4/5 |

Aggregated scores stand at **85 on Metacritic** and **86 on OpenCritic**. The most consistent criticism across reviews is repetition in the game's later hours, though the combat system is broadly praised as skillful and approachable for series newcomers.

## Dragon Ball Xenoverse 3 — "Fight Your Own Way" Trailer Shows Character Creation and Android Race

**Bandai Namco** released a new **"Fight Your Own Way"** trailer for **Dragon Ball Xenoverse 3** on September 4, spotlighting the game's character creation system and debuting the **Android** playable race — the sixth available race in the title, and a first for the series.

Set during the unexplored **Age 1000** era of Dragon Ball's timeline, Xenoverse 3 builds a custom hero who fights alongside iconic characters from across the franchise's history. The game was initially announced in April 2026; this trailer marks its most detailed public showing to date.

Dragon Ball Xenoverse 3 targets a **2027 release** on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**.

## Another Eden Begins — Demo Live Ahead of September 17 Launch

**Wright Flyer Studios** / **Studio Prisma** has released a playable demo for **Another Eden Begins** ahead of the game's **September 17 worldwide launch** on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**.

The game is a standalone time-traveling turn-based JRPG developed under the creative direction of **Masato Kato** (Chrono Trigger, Xenogears) and featuring a soundtrack by **Yasunori Mitsuda** (Xenogears, Chrono Cross). Players control **Aldo**, whose sister **Feinne** is abducted by the Beast King, triggering a journey that sends Aldo 800 years into the future. The main party can include up to eighteen companions.

The demo is available now on all three platforms.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20042-another-eden-begins-release-date-steam-switch-2-physical-edition-serial-codes) · [GoNintendo (demo)](https://gonintendo.com/contents/64480-demo-now-available-for-another-eden-begins-new-trailer-shared) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/04/another-eden-begins-launches-september-17) · [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/new-jrpg-another-eden-begins-sets-september-17-launch-on-pc-switch-and-switch-2/)
