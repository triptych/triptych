## Octopath Traveler and Octopath Traveler II Launch on Switch 2 — West Gets October 1 Date

**Square Enix** has released **Octopath Traveler** and **Octopath Traveler II** on the Nintendo Switch 2 eShop in Japan today, July 13. Western players will be able to purchase both games digitally and physically starting **October 1, 2026**.

Pricing in the US is **$59.99 per game**, with a digital bundle available for **$74.99**. UK pricing is £49.99 each or £59.99 bundled. Japanese pricing is 5,478 yen each or 6,820 yen for the bundle. Physical releases will be **Game-Key Cards**. The Switch 2 versions feature improved resolution and frame rate over the original Switch releases.

Square Enix confirmed there is **no upgrade path** from the Switch 1 versions, and **save data does not transfer** between platforms.

## Rhapsody: A Musical Adventure Now Available via Console Archives on Switch 2

**Nippon Ichi Software** released **Console Archives Rhapsody: A Musical Adventure** for **Nintendo Switch 2** (and PS5) on **July 9**, priced at **$14.99 / 1,500 yen**.

Originally published in 1998 for PlayStation in Japan (2000 in North America), *Rhapsody* is a musical tactical RPG starring **Cornet**, a girl with the magical power to befriend puppets, who sets out to rescue a prince kidnapped by villains. The game is the first entry in the **Marl Kingdom** series, which serves as a spiritual precursor to *Disgaea*. The Console Archives release adds customizable button layouts, rapid-fire settings, and multiple save points.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/console-archives-rhapsody-a-musical-adventure-launches-july-9) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/rhapsody-a-musical-adventure-getting-ps5-switch-2-release/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/rhapsody-a-musical-adventure-is-this-weeks-console-archives-game-on-nintendo-switch-2/) · [Digitally Downloaded](https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/2026/07/console-archives-rhapsody-a-musical-adventure-is-now-available.html)

## Letalis 1.0 Exits Early Access — Game Boy-Style Gladiator RPG Now Fully Released

**Letalis** has graduated out of Steam Early Access and launched its full **1.0 version** this week. The game wears its inspirations openly: a monochromatic **Game Boy** visual style paired with mechanics drawn from classic **Pokémon** and **SaGa** (known in North America as *Final Fantasy Legend*).

The core loop is party building — players recruit from **over 1,000 gladiators**, then train and fuse fighters into new combinations before sending them into the colosseum. Beyond combat the game offers a world map to explore, puzzles, and secrets in a nonlinear structure.

Sources: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2396200/Letalis/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/07/12/rpgs-coming-this-week-july-12-18-2026/) · [Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2024/10/letalis-is-a-gladiator-style-rpg-which-gives-us-massive-og-pokemon-vibes)

## Lunarium Sets July 29 Release Date — Hand-Drawn Isometric Action RPG

**Lunarium Game Studio** and publisher **Imperfect Games** have confirmed that **Lunarium** launches on **PC via Steam on July 29** for **$17.99 / €17.99 / £15.99**.

The game is a hand-drawn isometric action RPG following **Ave**, a light-armored knight, and **Lune**, a mysterious Starfarer, as they travel a fragile world of bubble-like realms that rise from a chaotic sea toward distant stars, doomed to burst in an endless cycle of rebirth. Combat emphasizes precise timing — parries and dodges neutralize incoming attacks while **Resonance Points** built up with Lune unlock powerful combination strikes. A demo is available now on Steam.

Supported languages at launch: English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/lunarium-launches-july-29) · [GAMESTIC](https://www.gamestic.nl/2026/07/13/lunarium-locks-in-july-29-release-date/) · [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/468456/hand-drawn-isometric-action-rpg-lunarium-launches-july-29-for-pc/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2939790/Lunarium/)

## Prelude: Dark Pain Enters Steam Early Access July 27

**Quickfire Games** and publisher **Firesquid** have announced that **Prelude: Dark Pain**, a dark fantasy tactical RPG, enters **Steam Early Access on July 27**.

Players take the role of **Soren**, a local blacksmith who leads an uprising against the Order of the Ashen Crusade. The game features **20+ playable heroes** across 8 class types (Ranged DPS, Magic Support, Assassins, and more), each with two talent branches and a pool of 25 skills, with rosters of up to 5 heroes. The Early Access build covers the full first act with **15+ hours of gameplay** following 9 heroes. The full Early Access period is expected to last approximately one year.

## Project Phoenix Breaks 7-Year Silence — Targets End of 2031

The long-troubled Kickstarter JRPG **Project Phoenix** resurfaced this week when director and producer **Hiroaki Yura** issued a lengthy private update to backers on **July 7** and spoke publicly with outlets including VGC and Kotaku.

The project raised **$1,014,600** on Kickstarter in 2013, making it one of the higher-profile JRPG crowdfunding campaigns of that era — and has been largely silent for years since. Yura acknowledged the situation bluntly: *"I overestimated my ability to deliver. I had never led a game project prior to then."* He said he was forced to make an internal commitment: finish development **by end of 2031**. "I don't think I can pursue any more later than 2031 because, you know, I've made everybody wait too long."

No platforms were specified in this update.

Sources: [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/jrpg/13-years-after-raising-usd1-million-on-kickstarter-dev-of-infamous-jrpg-breaks-silence-to-say-work-will-continue-through-2031/) · [Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2026/07/i-overestimated-my-ability-to-deliver-infamous-kickstarter-project-phoenix-is-alive-but-it-might-not-be-out-until-2031) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/rpg-that-earned-over-a-million-dollars-on-kickstarter-in-2013-provides-first-update-in-seven-years-aiming-to-finish-production-at-the-end-of-2031-2000713325) · [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/features/project-phoenix/)
