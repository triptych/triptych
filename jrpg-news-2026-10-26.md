## Final Fantasy Resonance Launches to Strong Reviews

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy Resonance** launched on **October 22, 2026** for Switch, Switch 2, PC via Steam, and additional platforms. The HD-2D RPG — a standalone premium title built on the first major story arc of the free-to-play mobile game *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* — has landed to broadly positive critical reception.

**Famitsu** awarded it **36/40** (four reviewers scoring 9 each). Aggregate scores currently sit at approximately **88 on PC** and **82 on Switch 2**. Individual outlet scores include Game Informer's 9/10 ("a strong return to older JRPG design"), IGN's 8/10 (praising the HD-2D visuals and music while calling the story underwhelming), and Push Square's 8/10. COGconnected gave it a 9.5/10. The main critical divide is whether the game's heavy nostalgia callbacks produce a compelling identity: *Gaming Trend* argued it feels disposable precisely because it hews so close to classic Final Fantasy, while *Shacknews* called it a "vision of greatness." Campaign length is reported at approximately 20–30 hours for the main story and around 70 hours for full completion.

A **free demo** is available now on PC and Switch 2.

## Demons' Night Fever Is Out Now

**Demons' Night Fever**, the strategy RPG from **Sohei Niikawa** (creator of the *Disgaea* series) and developer **SuperNiche**, published by **Arc System Works**, launched simultaneously worldwide on **October 22, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam**.

The game's central mechanic is the **Peon Exploitation System**, a grid-based combat structure built around deliberately sacrificing allied units to power up the party. The game was self-funded by Niikawa, who reportedly took out a personal loan of approximately $127,000 to see it through to completion.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21009-demons-night-fever-releases-simultaneously-worldwide-on-october-22-pre-orders-available-now) · [Arc System Works](https://www.arcsystemworks.com/?p=15417) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/demons-night-fever-launches-october-22) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/06/24/demons-night-fever-is-coming-to-ps5-pc-and-switch/)

## Vixen 357 Gets First English Release on Switch

**Vixen 357**, a turn-based strategy RPG originally published in Japan by **Sega** in **1993** for the Mega Drive, received its **first official English localization** on **October 23, 2026**, released on **Nintendo Switch**. A PC version via Steam is expected to follow later this year.

The game is a mecha-themed strategy title set in a post-war future and is notable as one of the lesser-known entries in Sega's Genesis/Mega Drive library that never reached the West in its original run.

## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Launches in the West This Wednesday

**STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT**, the full remake of **Mages.**'s beloved visual novel, arrives in **North America and Europe on October 29, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2**. **Spike Chunsoft** is handling Western publishing.

The remake re-records all Japanese voice acting with the original cast and introduces an entirely new **Gamma Worldline** story route not present in the original game. Physical editions include a Standard and **Steelbook Edition**; the first-print run of both includes a bonus sticker sheet.

The Japan release launched earlier, on **August 20, 2026**, with English text included.

## Villion: Code Confirmed for January 26, 2027 in the West

**Idea Factory International** has confirmed a Western release date of **January 26, 2027** for **Villion: Code**, Compile Heart's action RPG. Platforms in the West will include **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**.

The game launched in Japan on **June 25, 2026** for PS5, Switch 2, PS4, and Switch. Tsukasa Masuko (*Shin Megami Tensei*, *Digital Devil Saga*) composed the soundtrack. The Western announcement follows an initial September 2026 confirmation that the title would come West "in 2027," with today's firm date pinning down the month.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio Switch 2 Launches in Three Weeks at Reduced Price

**Metaphor: ReFantazio** arrives on **Nintendo Switch 2 on November 12, 2026**, roughly two years after the original October 2024 release on other platforms. **ATLUS/SEGA** has positioned the Switch 2 port at a lower price point than earlier versions — the standard digital edition is priced at **$39.99 / £34.99**. A limited **SteelBook Edition** physical release is available at launch in the West; physical copies ship on a **game-key card**.

The original release holds a **94 Metacritic score** and won Best Role-Playing Game, Best Narrative, and Best Art Direction at The Game Awards 2024. Pre-orders for the Switch 2 version are open now.
