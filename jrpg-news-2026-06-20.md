## Persona 6 — Store Pages Live, and They Say More Than Expected

**ATLUS** quietly opened **Persona 6** wishlist pages across Steam, PlayStation Store, and the Xbox Store on **June 18–19**, and the combined listings contain more concrete detail than the initial Xbox Games Showcase reveal did.

**What the store pages confirm:**

- **Setting and structure:** Steam's short description reads "Live a Year Alongside Unforgettable Companions in Modern-Day Japan," locking in the franchise's signature calendar/school-year structure for the new entry. The game takes place in Japan but no specific city is named.
- **Self-contained entry:** Atlus's copy explicitly states Persona 6 "requires no prior Persona experience," reinforcing its standalone nature.
- **Content rating:** The Steam mature content descriptor lists *"Some Nudity or Sexual Content, Frequent Violence or Gore, General Mature Content."* The nudity flag is notable — Persona 5 Royal and Persona 4 Golden carried violence and suggestive themes but not explicit nudity ratings. GamesRadar published a short piece arguing the specificity of an ESRB/Steam rating process suggests the game is further into final production than the sparse marketing implies, with a headline noting it may "not be as far from launch as we all feared."
- **DRM:** The Steam page lists **Denuvo Anti-Tamper**, which has already generated discussion on ResetEra and among PC players.
- **Language support:** 15 languages confirmed for subtitles and UI; both **Japanese and English voice acting** confirmed.

The listed release date on all platforms reads "Coming Soon" with no window given.

## SEGA/Atlus at Anime Expo 2026 — Full Lineup Confirmed

**SEGA** and **Atlus** published their full schedule for **Anime Expo 2026**, running **July 2–5** at the Los Angeles Convention Center (South Hall Booth #1300). The lineup is heavier on JRPG content than previous years:

**Persona 4 Revival — Panel on July 4**
A dedicated P4 Revival panel will be held on **July 4** with developer guests including character designer **Shigenori Soejima**, general producer **Kazuhisa Wada**, and business producer **Atsushi Nomura**. SEGA's announcement describes "never-before-seen content and information" — the first promise of new disclosures since the NicoNico broadcast recap. Given the game's **February 18, 2027** release is still eight months out, this panel may include a look at dungeon structure, the extended Social Link system, or additional English cast members.

**Metaphor: ReFantazio — Playable Switch 2 Demo + String Quartet**
Attendees can go hands-on with the upcoming **Switch 2 port** (releasing November 12) on the show floor. Separately, a **string quartet** will perform music from *Metaphor: ReFantazio* on **July 4 and 5** — described as a preview of the full **Metaphor: ReFantazio Orchestra World Tour** launching in October 2026.

**Persona 5: The Phantom X — Velvet Room Photo-Op**
A Velvet Room activation themed around the mobile title offers attendees an exclusive item while supplies last.
