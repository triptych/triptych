## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to 89 Metacritic, Series High-Water Mark

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems** launched **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 17, 2026**, and review embargoes lifted one day prior on September 16.

The game earned an **89 on Metacritic** based on 53 scored reviews, with **13 outlets awarding a perfect score**. IGN, Eurogamer, and others gave it 10/10 and 5/5 respectively, while Game Informer scored it 8.75. The consensus praised its enormous campaign structure built around four interconnected storylines, flexible narrative progression, deep tactical combat, and an orchestral score. Common criticisms pointed to slower early pacing and an overwhelming volume of content.

Multiple critics called Fortune's Weave one of the best games in the series and a strong contender for 2026's best strategy-RPG.

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Returns After 20 Years, Scores 86 on Metacritic

**Capcom** released **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** on **September 4, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam** (and PlayStation 5), ending an 18-year mainline series absence since 2006's *Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams*.

The game scored **86 on Metacritic (PS5)** and **89 on Metacritic (Switch 2)**, with **94% of critics recommending** it on OpenCritic. IGN awarded it a rare **10/10**, the first perfect IGN score for the series. Critics called the sword combat deep and satisfying, with particular praise for boss encounters and the modernized dark-fantasy presentation.

The game sold **1 million copies on launch day**. Common criticisms noted a relatively linear 20-hour campaign, repetitive enemies, and limited post-game content.

Sources: [GameDaily roundup](https://gamedaily.com/games/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review-roundup) · [Gaming Trend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review/) · [Tech-Insider Switch 2 review](https://tech-insider.org/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-switch-2-review-2026/) · [4-platform launch](https://tech-insider.org/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-4-platform-launch-2026/) · [Timesaver score roundup](https://timesaver.gg/blog/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review-roundup)

## Final Fantasy XIV Switch 2 Launch: Strong Handheld Port, Docked Compromises Noted

**Final Fantasy XIV** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2** on **August 4, 2026**, with approximately one free month of subscription time offered to new players to ease server onboarding.

Reviews published through August generally praised the port as a technical achievement, with several outlets calling the handheld experience the best way to play the MMO. The game supports mouse-and-keyboard input via the USB-C port on Switch 2. Square Enix confirmed the game runs at approximately **30 fps in both docked and handheld modes**, which reviewers noted is below the standard for competing platforms — RPG Site specifically called out the docked performance as a missed opportunity given PlayStation 5 and PC run at higher frame rates. Nintendo Life, But Why Tho, and Digitally Downloaded all highlighted the handheld mode as the strongest use case. No subscription requires Nintendo Switch Online, though the FFXIV subscription fee still applies.

Square Enix confirmed that existing subscribers on other platforms receive a discount on the Switch 2 subscription.

Sources: [Square Enix launch announcement](https://www.square-enix-games.com/en_US/news/final-fantasy-xiv-nintendo-switch-2-is-out-now) · [Nintendo Life review](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/final-fantasy-xiv-online) · [RPG Site comparison](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/21052-final-fantasy-14-switch-2-port-review-vs-steam-deck-ps5-pro-xbox-pc-impressions) · [But Why Tho](https://butwhytho.net/2026/08/final-fantasy-xiv-switch-2-review/) · [Digitally Downloaded](https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/2026/08/review-final-fantasy-xiv-on-nintendo-switch-2-is-the-best-version-of-the-game.html)

## Dragon Quest XI S: Switch 2 Edition Reviewed — Great Game, Frustrating Upgrade Path

**Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age – Definitive Edition** launched for **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 24, 2026**, joining the same-day release of **Qliphah in Providence's Shadow**.

Reviews were broadly positive, with Nintendo World Report and Worthplaying both scoring it **8.5/10** and Gamestic giving it **88**. Critics praised the enhanced texture resolution, improved draw distance, and shadow quality versus the original Switch version, calling the Switch 2 an ideal platform for the game's epic adventure.

## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Announced — Full 3D Remake for February 2027

**Square Enix** announced **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** during the **Nintendo Direct on September 9, 2026**. The game is a full **3D remake** of the 1995 Super Famicom RPG *Romancing SaGa 3* and is being developed by **xeen Inc.**, the studio behind *Trials of Mana* and *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven*.

The remake launches **February 16, 2027** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S). It features eight protagonists — players choose one and follow their search for the Child of Destiny from that character's perspective. The game retains the non-linear structure of the original: players choose which lands to visit, which stories to pursue, and the order in which they confront the Sinistrals. New additions include 3D graphics, full voice acting in cutscenes, and newly arranged music. The game was among the **Japan Game Awards Future Division** winners at **Tokyo Game Show 2026**.

Sources: [RPGamer announcement](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced/) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-09-09/square-enix-announces-romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-remake-game/.241594) · [Nova Crystallis](https://novacrystallis.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-february-16-2027-release/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21322-romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-release-date) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc)

## Eternal Anima Revealed: Kazushige Nojima's Time-Travel JRPG Arrives March 2027

**Marvelous** unveiled **Eternal Anima** during the **Nintendo Direct on September 9, 2026**, confirming a **March 4, 2027** launch on **Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S). The game is the full reveal of **Project Life is RPG**, which Marvelous teased in 2023.

The development team includes some notable RPG veterans:
- **Director:** Katsuhisa Higuchi
- **Story concept:** Keisuke Makino
- **Scenario:** Kazushige Nojima — known for *Final Fantasy VII, VIII,* and *X*, and the *Kingdom Hearts* series

The story is set in the kingdom of **Urvis Ael**, where "wanting a better future" is a punishable crime. Protagonist **Wade** flees, gathers companions, and travels across different eras via **psychometry** — reading memories held in ancient artifacts to inherit the abilities and classes of past heroes. The turn-based battle system features **Future Sight**, which reveals enemy action plans each turn so players can counter, delay, or cancel them before execution.

Pricing: Standard Edition at $59.99 on Switch 2 (cartridge) and $49.99 on PC; Limited Edition at $89.99 on Switch 2 includes art book, two-disc soundtrack, and nine character art cards.

**Level-5** announced another delay for **DECAPOLICE** during its **Level-5 Vision 2026 II "Dream" presentation on September 10, 2026**, pushing the detective cyberpunk RPG from its 2026 window into **2027**. ## Xenoblade Chronicles 3 Switch 2 Edition — Heroes Vault Mode and New Hero Shimmer Detailed

The **September 9, 2026 Nintendo Direct** provided fresh details for **Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition**, still on track for **December 3, 2026**.

The headline exclusive addition is **Heroes Vault**, a new mode that lets players recruit and deploy a wider roster of the game's numerous hero characters in new optional challenge content.
