## Beast of Reincarnation Earns 35/40 from Famitsu Ahead of Tomorrow's Launch

**Game Freak** and publisher **Fictions** release **Beast of Reincarnation** tomorrow, August 4, on **PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S). The game is available Day One on PC Game Pass.

Famitsu has published the first scored review ahead of launch: **8/9/9/9 (35/40)**, a strong result for the studio's first new IP since Town (Little Town Hero). A hands-on preview published today on PlayStation Blog describes the combat as "parry-rich," with successful parries generating skill points used to queue tactical commands for canine companion **Koo** in a slow-motion tactical window — blending real-time swordplay with command-based elements.

The game is set in Japan in the year 4026. Protagonist **Emma** is an outcast "Sealer" fused with a parasitic corruption called the Blight, and travels with Koo across ruined landscapes to defeat giant bosses and cleanse the world of corruption. A Japan Times preview noted that the game shows Game Freak stretching creatively, though some rough edges remain.

## Blue Reflection Quartet Launch Reviews Are In

**Blue Reflection Quartet** launched July 30 on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5), and critic reviews are now live. The collection bundles the two mainline console games (*Blue Reflection* and *Blue Reflection: Second Light*), the *Blue Reflection Ray* anime series, and the mobile game *Blue Reflection SUN*.

Review scores so far:

- **Final Weapon** — 4/5: "a magical girl JRPG collection focused on friendship, personal growth, and heartfelt storytelling"
- **Shacknews** — 7/10: the two main games are compelling, but the anime and mobile adaptations are weaker inclusions
- **RPG Site** — positive overall; describes it as tremendous value for newcomers, with some structural shortcomings in the bundle presentation
- **Game8** — mixed; calls it "half-baked" for failing to elevate the series beyond what individual titles already offered

Sources: [Koei Tecmo America](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/news/relive-the-emotional-enchanted-world-of-blue-reflection-in-its-entirety-with-blue-reflection-quartet-launching-july-30-2026/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/07/30/blue-reflection-quartet-launch-trailer-illustration/) · [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150181/blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20992-blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [Game8](https://game8.co/reviews/blue-reflection-quartet/blue-reflection-quartet-review) · [Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/reviews/blue-reflection-quartet-review/)

## Crymelight Battle Demo Live Through August 9; New Boss Trailer Drops

**FuRyu**'s upcoming roguelike action RPG **Crymelight** has a limited-time **Battle Demo** running on Steam through **August 9** (approximately 7:59 PM PDT). The demo drops players directly into the game's combat without preamble.

Alongside the demo, FuRyu released a new trailer introducing boss characters **Dum** and **Dee** — a paired antagonist duo who appear to play contrasting roles in the game's encounters. Crymelight is the third entry in FuRyu's "Cry" series of dark bishoujo action RPGs, following *Crystar* (2019) and *Crymachina* (2023).

The game launches **November 5** on **Nintendo Switch 2 and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5).

## DragonSword: Awakening Crosses 200,000 Steam Sales; Switch 2 Port Confirmed

Korean developer **Hound13**'s anime action RPG **DragonSword: Awakening** has surpassed **200,000 units sold on Steam** in its first week following a July 23 launch — the game drew more than 450,000 wishlists before release and peaked at approximately 23,000 concurrent Steam players. The studio has confirmed that **PlayStation and Nintendo Switch 2 versions are in development**, though no release window has been announced.

The open-world Unreal Engine 5 RPG follows a young traveler named **Lute** across the continent of Orbis, where his journey becomes entwined with the reappearance of a dragon that nearly destroyed the region 60 years prior. Future expansions are also in planning.

## Darkest Dungeon: The Fire's Edge Announced — Launches August 18 on PC and Switch

**Red Hook Studios** has surprise-announced **The Fire's Edge**, the first new DLC for the original *Darkest Dungeon* in over six years. It launches **August 18** on **PC and Nintendo Switch** (Xbox One to follow later).

The DLC imports two heroes from *Darkest Dungeon II* into the original game: **The Duelist** and **The Runaway**. It also introduces **Burn** as a new combat mechanic (used by The Runaway), three new districts — the Academie Duello, the Craftworks, and the Archaeological Base Camp — along with new Trinkets, events, and writing. The Steam page is live.
