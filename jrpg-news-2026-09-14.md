## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure — PS5 & Switch 2 Ports Launch with 4K and Mouse Support

**Nihon Falcom**, **PH3**, and **NIS America** shipped the upgraded **PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2 versions of *Trails from Zero* and *Trails to Azure*** on **September 10**, offering the most technically capable versions of the Crossbell duology to date.

Both ports run at up to **4K resolution on PS5** (with full 120fps support on PS5 Pro), while the Switch 2 editions offer **five graphics presets**: three in handheld and two in docked mode, reaching 1080p handheld or 4K docked at 60fps. **Mouse-and-keyboard input** is supported on Switch 2 via the USB-C port — a notable feature for a pair of games with dense menus and a PC-CRPG-adjacent investigation system. PH3's **durante** handled the porting work and contributed to additional visual upgrades beyond resolution scaling.

Western digital editions are available **separately at $39.99 USD each** or as a combined **physical bundle ($69.99)**, with a **limited edition at $109.99** that includes physical soundtrack media and artwork. RPG Site praised both ports as the definitive way to experience the Crossbell arc, noting that the save-transfer system makes it easy to carry over a completed *Trails from Zero* file directly into *Trails to Azure*.

## Level-5 Vision 2026 II "Dream" — Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain Revealed, Lineup Updates

**Level-5** held its second showcase of 2026, **LEVEL-5 Vision 2026 II "Dream"**, on **September 10** at 21:00 JST, broadcast on the Level5Ch YouTube channel with English and Traditional Chinese subtitles.

The headline reveal is **Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain**, an enhanced remake of the 3DS original. The game is confirmed for **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam** worldwide. Building on the *Yo-kai Watch 2: Psychic Specters* base, *Haunted Domain* adds fully new story content, new Yo-kai, and modern graphical and control improvements. CEO **Akihiro Hino** presented the announcement personally; a release date was not announced.

The presentation also delivered status updates on several other titles in development:
- **Professor Layton and the Curious Village Remake** — new footage shown
- **Professor Layton and the New World of Steam** — updated progress shown
- **Holy Horror Mansion** — further details on the haunted-house adventure
- **Decapolice** — updated look at the investigative RPG
- **Snack World: Reloaded** — brief footage update

The event marks the first time Level-5 has held two full Vision showcases in a single calendar year. The "Dream" subtitle was widely read as signaling an emphasis on long-awaited or ambitious projects.

## Nintendo Direct 9.9.2026 — Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Announced

**Square Enix** used the **Nintendo Direct on September 9** to announce **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United**, a full 3D remake of the 1995 Super Famicom classic.

The remake retains the series' hallmark **Free Scenario System**, which lets players choose from **eight protagonists** and approach the game's main and side quests in virtually any order. The setting involves the cyclical catastrophe known as the **Rise of Morastrum**, which eclipses the sun every 300 years and destroys all newborns save one "Child of Destiny" — previous iterations produced an Archfiend and a Matriarch respectively, and the story opens with the Archfiend's Four Sinistrals re-emerging to crack open the Abyss Gates. The remake adds **fully voiced cutscenes**, **selectable difficulty**, and **3D visuals** that replace the original's 2D sprites while reportedly preserving the top-down overworld perspective.

**Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** is set for **February 16, 2027** on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**.

## Konami Announces Rhapsody in Scarlet — Magick Action-RPG Set in 1920s New York

**Konami** announced **Rhapsody in Scarlet** during the **September 2026 PlayStation State of Play**, with the Japanese showcase segment promising additional details. The game is an original **magick-themed action adventure** set in **1920s New York City**.

Players follow **Ellie Myers**, a young woman who performs as a singer on stage before venturing into the jazz-filled streets to battle supernatural threats alongside an otherworldly beast she calls **Skoll**. The reveal trailer showed Ellie launching large, screen-spanning magical attacks in tight urban environments, suggesting a high-spectacle action style. Konami framed the narrative around themes of fate, divine will, and human resolve. A release date has not been announced.

*Rhapsody in Scarlet* is confirmed for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**. No Nintendo platform was listed at announcement.

## Navinosuke: The Yo-kai Buster — Long-Lost GBA RPG Resurfaces for Switch and PC in 2027

**Kohachi Studio** has officially announced **Navinosuke: The Yo-kai Buster** for **Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam**, with **Happinet Corporation** serving as publisher. The game carries a **2027 release window** after its originally-targeted 2026 date slipped.

The project has a remarkable history: **Navinosuke** was originally developed in secret during the early 2000s by a team led by director Akio — including developers from **SNK's Metal Slug team** — for the **Game Boy Advance**, but was never released, sitting unreleased for more than two decades. Kohachi Studio rebuilt and reimagined it for modern audiences. The game is set in a **fictional version of ancient Japan** drawn from local folklore, following Navinosuke, a karakuri (mechanical puppet) **Onmyoji apprentice** created by the secretive Onmyo Bureau as humanity's last defense against an escalating Yo-kai threat.

Outlets have noted the game's creature-collecting and location-tagging mechanics were conceptualized before smartphones existed, predating titles like *Pokémon GO* by roughly 15 years. A playable build is scheduled for **Tokyo Game Show 2026** (September 17–21).

## Square Enix Reveals TGS 2026 Lineup — KH4 and FF7 Revelation Playable, DQ7 Reimagined Featured

**Square Enix** announced its booth lineup for **Tokyo Game Show 2026** (September 17–21, Makuhari Messe), which includes playable demos for two of its most-anticipated RPGs.

**Final Fantasy VII Revelation** will be playable on the show floor with a dedicated section of the build available to attendees, accompanied by three photo spots themed around the *FFVII Remake* trilogy. **Final Fantasy Resonance** will also be playable, and a large panel featuring pixel character artwork will anchor the *Resonance* area. **Kingdom Hearts IV** appears on the lineup; a *Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World* area will include a Fluffy-and-Slime meet-and-greet photo opportunity. **Dragon Quest VII Reimagined** (released February 2026) will be playable from the beginning in the Family Game Park zone, and **Dragon Quest X Online Ver. 8.0** content will be shown.

Square Enix's booth will also feature a moogle and Alpha for character meet-and-greets. The TGS period is expected to bring further announcements and trailers from the company during the show's business days (September 17–18) and public days (September 19–21).
