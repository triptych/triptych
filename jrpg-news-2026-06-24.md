## Deltarune Chapter 5: "Festival Day" — Now Live; Community Reacts

**Deltarune Chapter 5** launched today at **11:00 AM EDT / 8:00 AM PT** as a free update on **Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, PS5, and PC/Mac via Steam**, exactly as scheduled. The chapter's in-game subtitle is **"Festival Day"**, with the broader thematic umbrella Toby Fox has evoked being **"The Sunset of 7 Suns"** — a reference to an older Fox track and the idea that Chapter 5 represents a last bright, lighthearted adventure before the story's tone turns darker in later chapters. (Pre-launch coverage had circulated the phrase "The Field of Pink and Gold" for the chapter, likely sourced from draft promotional materials.)

Early community reception on **ResetEra** and the **Steam** store page is overwhelmingly enthusiastic. The ResetEra official topic thread is titled *"DELTARUNE Chapter 5 |OT| The Sunset of 7 Suns"* and was packed with activity within minutes of launch. Steam's cumulative review count (which includes all chapters) now sits above 104,000 reviews, maintaining the game's **Overwhelmingly Positive** status.

Toby Fox noted in pre-launch coverage that the Chapter 5 trailer deliberately avoided spoiling "the most exciting moments," and early player comments suggest the chapter delivers on that restraint — several players on ResetEra described it as "still thinking about it a lot" and praised the tone shift compared to Chapter 4. No professional outlet reviews had been published at the time of this writing (early in the launch window), but aggregates on OpenCritic and Metacritic are expected to populate within 24–48 hours.

## Monster Fantasy — AI-Powered Life Sim ARPG with Monster Taming Announced for PC

Chengdu-based indie studio **Jotoyo** revealed **Monster Fantasy**, an action RPG that blends creature hunting with cozy life-sim elements, for **PC via Steam**. No release date was announced; a **Kickstarter campaign launches July 15**.

The core loop casts players as an amnesiac hero exploring the world of **Eldoras**, hunting over **50 giant monsters** across biomes including rainforests, icy plains, and volcanoes. Players choose from four classes — **Warrior, Mage, Archer, and Martial Artist** — and can either specialize or gradually master all four. Defeated monsters can be **tamed as companions** that age and evolve new skills over time, and can also be ridden in the field. The entire campaign supports **online co-op multiplayer**.

The life-sim side features approximately **100 villagers**, each with distinct personalities and interests. The most technically unusual feature: Jotoyo is integrating **LLM and text-to-speech (TTS)** systems so players can hold freeform real-time conversations with any NPC rather than navigating fixed dialogue trees. Cozy activities include fishing, cooking, crafting, and mining.

The reveal drew quick comparison to both *Monster Hunter* (for its hunting structure) and *Stardew Valley* (for the life-sim layer), while the AI dialogue system drew both interest and skepticism in early community discussion.

## THQ Nordic Digital Showcase 2026 — August 7 Date Confirmed; Titan Quest II, Expeditions: Samurai, and More

**THQ Nordic** announced its **Digital Showcase 2026** will stream on **August 7, 2026 at 12:00 PM PDT / 3:00 PM EDT / 8:00 PM BST / 9:00 PM CEST** on YouTube and Twitch.

The publisher confirmed the following titles will appear, with a mix of updates, release dates, and potential world premieres:

- **Titan Quest II** (action RPG sequel, previously shown with "Wild Lands" expansion content)
- **Expeditions: Samurai** (tactical RPG set in feudal Japan)
- **Fatekeeper** (first-person atmospheric dungeon-crawling action RPG)
- **The Guild: Europa 1410** (medieval dynasty/trade sim — returning franchise, last entry released 24 years ago)
- **Wreckfest 2** (demolition racing sequel)
- **The Eternal Life of Goldman** (undead-protagonist adventure RPG)
- **Way of the Hunter 2** (hunting simulation)

THQ Nordic promised "world premieres, updates on previously announced games, and a handful of long-awaited release dates." For JRPG and RPG fans the most relevant entries are **Titan Quest II**, **Expeditions: Samurai**, and **Fatekeeper**. The return of **The Guild** franchise after 24 years is the headline surprise for longtime fans of the European-style dynasty sim genre.
