## Star Ocean: The Second Story R Launches Surprise Switch 2 Version; PC Versions Coming to GOG on July 28

**Square Enix** released a **Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** of **Star Ocean: The Second Story R** on **July 15, 2026**, with no advance warning — the title appeared on the eShop the same day. The Switch 2 version is priced at **$49.99** and delivers a significant technical improvement over the 2023 Switch release: it targets **1080p / 60 fps in both handheld and docked modes**, compared to 576p (handheld) / 864p (docked) at 30 fps on the original Switch version.

There is **no upgrade path** for owners of the Switch version, and save data cannot be transferred between the two versions. The Switch 2 edition also includes enhanced texture resolution across the board.

Separately, the PC version of *The Second Story R* and **Star Ocean: The Divine Force** will both arrive on **GOG on July 28**, marking their first time on the platform. Both titles will launch with steep discounts running through August 9: **Second Story R at 50% off** and **all editions of Divine Force at 60% off**.

## Digimon Story: Time Stranger Earns Strong Marks on Switch and Switch 2

**Digimon Story: Time Stranger** launched on **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 on July 10, 2026**, and the platform-specific reviews have come in warmly. The Switch 2 version holds a **Metacritic score of 79** with a user score of **9.0**, tracking well above the PS5/PC version's debut reception from 2025.

Reviewers consistently cite the game's narrative as one of the strongest in the franchise's history — describing it as the culmination of what the *Digimon Story* lineage has been building toward since *Cyber Sleuth*. Criticism focused on uneven pacing in the opening hours, with too much dialogue and exposition before the game's systems open up. The Switch 2 version targets **1080p / 60 fps** in docked mode, while the standard Switch version runs at 1080p / 30 fps docked.

Sources: [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/digimon-story-time-stranger) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/digimon-story-time-stranger-review-for-nintendo-switch-2-and-switch/) · [Crunchyroll](https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/features/2026/7/10/digimon-story-time-stranger-nintendo-switch-2-review)

## NTE: Neverness to Everness Launches on Steam July 8 with ¥100M Opening Day Revenue; AI Art Controversy Follows

**NTE: Neverness to Everness**, the open-world action RPG by Chinese developer Hotta Studio, launched on **Steam and Epic Games Store on July 8, 2026** — slightly ahead of its previously listed July 16 window. The game generated **CN¥100 million (roughly $13.7M USD) in global revenue on launch day** across all platforms including PC, iOS, Android, and Samsung Galaxy Store.

The Steam launch came bundled with **Version 1.2**, which debuted the new **999 Nights** challenge game mode. Player reception on Steam trended highly positive in the days following launch.

However, the release drew controversy when players alleged the game used **AI-generated art and AI video content** in promotional and in-game materials, leading VTuber **Ironmouse** to end her partnership with the studio. Hotta Studio has not issued a formal public response to the allegations.

## Ao Oni 2 Announced for Nintendo Switch and Steam — Launches August 6

**IPA** (publisher) has confirmed that **Ao Oni 2**, the sequel to the cult-classic horror RPG, will launch on **Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam on August 6, 2026**, priced at **¥1,200** (approximately $8–9 USD). The release includes a new **Seaside School** scenario not present in the mobile version.

A physical edition for Nintendo Switch is also planned, releasing **October 8**, with an early-purchase bonus: the **Ao Oni Horror Pencil Set**.

The original *Ao Oni* — a grid-based puzzle RPG in which players navigate a mansion pursued by a bulbous blue monster — became a cultural touchstone in Japan through NicoNico playthrough videos before finding international cult status. The sequel continues the horror RPG format with a new school setting.

## Prinny Party: Going Overboard! Confirmed for Global Release — Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PS5, PC

**Nippon Ichi Software** revealed on **July 16** that its Japan-announced party game **Prinny Sugoroku** is heading west under the title **Prinny Party: Going Overboard!** The game will release globally on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**, with **Japan's release set for November 12**; a western date is **to be announced**.

*Prinny Party: Going Overboard!* is a **4-player board game RPG hybrid**: players move around a board, earning experience and equipment, working together to take down spawning bosses. Core mechanics include picking up and throwing allies and enemies, building and upgrading facilities on "build spaces" (hospitals, armories), and leveling up gear to stay competitive when elite enemies appear. The Supreme Overboard and his Protago-Knights serve as the central antagonists across the board's stages.

## .hack//Z.E.R.O.: CyberConnect2 Details Self-Publishing Path and Modern Direction at AX 2026

**CyberConnect2 Vice President Taichiro Miyazaki** presented a panel at **Anime Expo 2026** titled *"The Tale of .hack//Z.E.R.O.: from Licensed IP to Unprecedented Self-Publishing"*, offering the most detailed public account yet of how the dormant franchise was revived.

Miyazaki described personally leading one-on-one negotiations with a **Bandai Namco** executive for the *.hack* IP, proposing that CyberConnect2 — on the occasion of its **30th anniversary** — take full ownership of planning, development, and publishing. Bandai Namco agreed, making **.hack//Z.E.R.O.** the first title CyberConnect2 has both developed and self-published, a significant shift for a studio that has primarily worked as a developer-for-hire throughout its history.

On the game's direction: *Z.E.R.O.* is set in a **near-future shaped by modern online culture** — a deliberate departure from the early-2000s internet aesthetic of the original *.hack* games. The duality of the in-game MMO **The World** and the real world remains central, but the real-world narrative thread is described as receiving greater emphasis than in prior entries. CyberConnect2 called *Z.E.R.O.* "a novel RPG experience" and a new representative title for the company.

No platforms or release window have been announced.

Sources: [CyberConnect2 AX 2026 site](https://www.cc2.co.jp/global-events-2026/ax/en/) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/interview/19928-a-brand-new-hack-that-will-be-new-representative-game-for-company-sitting-down-with-cyberconnect2-vice-president-taichiro-miyazaki-during-gdc-2026) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/hack-zero-interview-cyberconnect2-series-revival-modern-jrpg/) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-02-16/cyberconnect2-announces-new-.hack-z.e.r.o-action-rpg/.234241)
