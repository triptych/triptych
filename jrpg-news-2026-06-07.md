# JRPG News — June 7, 2026

## Top Story: Xbox Games Showcase 2026 Airs Today — Atlus Double-Feature Expected

The Xbox Games Showcase kicked off at 10 AM PT today (June 7), followed immediately by the Gears of War: E-Day Direct. This marks the first showcase for new Xbox CEO Asha Sharma, and Atlus is expected to be a centerpiece of the event.

### Persona 6 — World Premiere Expected
Multiple credible insiders — including NateTheHate and known Sega/Atlus leaker lolilolailo — stated with high confidence that **Persona 6** would receive its world premiere at today's showcase. This follows a well-established pattern: Persona 3 Reload, Persona 5 ports, Metaphor: ReFantazio, and Persona 4 Revival were all first revealed at Xbox showcase events. Atlus previously confirmed Persona 6 is in development after a security breach led to SEGA issuing DMCA takedowns for leaked concept art and early footage.

### Persona 4 Revival — Release Date Reveal
Famitsu published a hint earlier today suggesting **Persona 4 Revival** will appear at the showcase. Reliable insider lolilolailo has claimed a **February 2027** release date — which would mirror the launch timing of Persona 3 Reload. The game targets PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC, and Nintendo Switch 2. Sega Sammy's investor materials list it as shipping within their FY2027 window (April 2026 – March 2027), making February plausible.

## Persona 5 Royal Returns to Xbox Game Pass — June 9

**Persona 5 Royal** rejoins Xbox Game Pass on June 9 as part of the service's first June 2026 wave. It will be available on Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S, PC, and via cloud gaming on the Premium, Ultimate, and PC Game Pass tiers. The title originally left Game Pass in October 2023 after roughly a year on the service.

This is part of a broader batch of nine Game Pass additions for early June, which also includes the survival title **Starseeker** and several other titles.

## Coming Up: Steam Next Fest — June 15–22

Steam Next Fest runs **June 15–22**, with over 80 JRPG and RPG-adjacent demos available. Notable entries include:

- A **CrossCode spiritual successor** — top-down 2.5D action RPG from the CrossCode team featuring multi-weapon combos and environmental puzzles
- **Legends of Starkadia** — sci-fi turn-based RPG with quick-time inputs woven into attacks and defense
- A voxel-based action RPG following a boy named Lug with the power to shape the fate of humans and machines
- A narrative turn-based RPG where two sisters and their spirit-mech cook meals and fight corporations
