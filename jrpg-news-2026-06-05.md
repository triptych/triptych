# JRPG News — June 5, 2026

## Summer Game Fest Main Show — Live Today

**Summer Game Fest 2026** goes live today at **2 PM PT / 5 PM ET** from the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood, hosted by Geoff Keighley and Lucy James. Keighley has called it the biggest SGF show yet, with a two-hour runtime. Several major JRPG reveals are expected — see sections below. For live coverage, Tom's Guide and Esports.gg are running live update blogs.

---

## Final Fantasy VII Remake Part 3 — SGF Reveal "A Lock," Say Insiders

Multiple reliable insiders have converged on a strong claim: **Final Fantasy VII Remake Part 3** will be revealed at today's Summer Game Fest. The most prominent voice is NateTheHate, who says the reveal is effectively confirmed.

**What we know from Square Enix directly:** Director **Naoki Hamaguchi** told press in April that development is "on time and on schedule" and that "preparations toward the announcement are steadily underway." Square Enix issued a formal statement that a reveal is "being prepared." The timing fits — Rebirth was first revealed at SGF 2023, and **2027 would mark the 30th anniversary of the original Final Fantasy VII**, a milestone the studio is unlikely to let pass without a release.

A separate note: some outlets are reporting that **Final Fantasy IX Remake** remains in limbo internally, as Square Enix is concentrating resources on completing the FF7 trilogy first.

---

## Persona 6 — Xbox Games Showcase (June 7), Not SGF

Early speculation had Persona 6 landing at today's SGF show, but multiple insiders have since corrected the record: **NateTheHate** and **eXtas1stv** are both confident the reveal is set for the **Xbox Games Showcase on Sunday, June 7**, not today.

**Leaked details (since DMCA'd by SEGA):** Logo and branding images circulated briefly online before SEGA issued takedown notices — which many fans took as implicit confirmation the leak was real. According to circulating reports, Atlus is targeting a **September 2027 release window**. The same Xbox showcase slot is also reportedly where fans will get a **Persona 4 Revival** release date.

Persona 5 Royal returned to Xbox Game Pass on **June 9** (covered in yesterday's notes), which reads as deliberate franchise positioning ahead of the Persona 6 reveal.

---

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Artbook Leak and Possible Summer Window

New developments since yesterday's preview: an unofficial artbook has leaked and is circulating online, revealing a previously unannounced **Pugilist class**. The artbook leak has also shifted fan speculation on the release window — the game was widely expected as a **holiday 2026** title, but the detail density of the artbook (suggesting a near-complete production state) has players wondering if Nintendo could target **summer — July or August** — instead.

Nintendo has not officially responded to the leak. A **Nintendo Direct** is rumored for next week, which would be the likely vehicle for a release date announcement if Nintendo is ready to commit.

As a reminder from prior coverage: the game is a Switch 2 exclusive with tactical turn-based combat, and trailers feature **Sothis** in a supporting role, prompting ongoing speculation about a narrative link to *Three Houses*.

---

## Echoes of Aincrad — SAO Action RPG, Launches July 10

**Bandai Namco** has confirmed **July 10, 2026** as the launch date for **Echoes of Aincrad**, a new action RPG set in the *Sword Art Online* universe, for **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam)**.

**What it is:** Unlike the mainline SAO games, players create their own hero rather than playing as Kirito. The story is original, set across the **1st and 2nd Floors of Aincrad**, with familiar characters **Kirito, Asuna, and Argo** appearing as NPC partners. Each partner has distinct skills, and deepening your bond with them unlocks more powerful abilities — a social-link-adjacent progression layer. Combat is real-time with equipment, weapon, and attribute upgrading as you level.

Pre-orders are open now on all platforms.
