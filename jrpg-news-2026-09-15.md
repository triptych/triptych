## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Announced — February 16, 2027

**Square Enix** and **Xeen** (the studio behind *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven* and the *Trials of Mana* remake) announced **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** during the September Nintendo Direct. It launches **February 16, 2027** for **PS5, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC**.

The remake follows the same approach as *Revenge of the Seven*: the original 1995 SNES title receives a full visual overhaul to 3D, fully voiced cutscenes, and an overhauled combat system while preserving the non-linear open-world structure that defined the series. Romancing SaGa 3 had never received an official Western localization in its original form, making this the first time Western players will get the full story in English.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21322-romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-release-date) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/09/10/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-reveal/) · [Nova Crystallis](https://novacrystallis.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-february-16-2027-release/) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/romancing-saga-3-3d-remake-arrives-in-2027/)

## Eternal Anima Announced — Kazushige Nojima–Written Time-Travel RPG for March 4, 2027

**Marvelous** revealed **Eternal Anima** during the Nintendo Direct on September 9. The game is a "legacy-driven time-travel RPG" releasing worldwide on **March 4, 2027** for **PS5, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC**. It was previously teased in 2023 as *PROJECT LIFE is RPG*.

The game follows a time traveler named **Wade**, who uses a gift called **Psychometry** to read memories tied to objects and use those visions to rewrite events in the past and present. The combat system is turn-based and described as strategic, with players able to predict and "rewrite" future actions during battle.

The creative team is notably staffed by veterans of the late-90s and early-2000s JRPG golden era: director **Katsuhisa Higuchi**, story concept writer **Keisuke Makino**, and scenario writer **Kazushige Nojima** (Final Fantasy VII, Final Fantasy X, Final Fantasy XVI). Pricing is $49.99 for PS5 standard, $59.99 for Switch 2 full cartridge, and $79.99/$89.99 for the respective Limited Editions.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/marvelous-announces-rpg-eternal-anima-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-and-pc) · [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21337-eternal-anima-release-date-switch-2-physical) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/09/09/marvelous-eternal-anima-march-2027/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/marvelous-announces-eternal-anima/) · [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/64748-eternal-anima-coming-to-switch-2-on-march-4th-2027)

## Level-5 Vision 2026 II "Dream" Showcase — Yo-kai Watch 2 Remake, Professor Layton Remake, and AI Backlash

**Level-5** held its **LEVEL-5 Vision 2026 II "Dream"** online showcase on **September 10** at 21:00 JST. The event delivered two significant announcements alongside updates on previously announced titles.

**Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain** was confirmed as an enhanced remake of the 2014 3DS title (originally released in three versions: Bony Spirits, Fleshy Souls, and Psychic Specters). The Switch 2 version will feature revamped graphics, new features, additional story content, and new Yo-kai. Level-5 confirmed this will have a Western release — the first Yo-kai Watch title with confirmed Western localization in over seven years. No release date was given.

**Professor Layton and the Curious Village Remake** was the showcase's surprise final announcement. The remake of the original 2007 DS puzzle adventure is targeting a **2027 release** on **PS5, Switch 2, Switch, and PC via Steam**. Characters have been rebuilt in 3D with high-resolution widescreen animations, exploration has been enhanced, and the experience has been adapted for a single screen. Updates on **Professor Layton and the New World of Steam**, **Holy Horror Mansion**, **Decapolice**, and **Snack World: Reloaded** were also shown.

The showcase generated controversy: fans noted the apparent use of generative AI in several trailers. Level-5 CEO **Akihiro Hino** responded on social media, stating: *"out of the desire to create a clear and flashy presentation, we ended up using AI in a wide variety of ways."* The response did not satisfy critics who felt the AI use was undisclosed and undermined trust in the announcements.

## This Week in JRPGs — September 17 Releases

Three notable JRPGs launch on **September 17**:

**Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — Nihon Falcom's remake of *The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky SC* (originally 2006) arrives on **Steam, PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, and Nintendo Switch**. Following *1st Chapter* (2025), this entry continues Estelle Bright's journey after Joshua's disappearance with an overhauled combat system and new side content built into the remake. The game launches simultaneously worldwide.

**Another Eden Begins** — Developed by **Studio Prisma** and published by **WFS**, this standalone RPG arrives on **Steam, Nintendo Switch 2, and Nintendo Switch** with no free-to-play mechanics — it is a one-time purchase. The game is a time-spanning RPG adventure with turn-based command battles, more than 10 endings via New Game+, and full voice acting. Scenario and direction come from **Masato Kato** (Chrono Trigger, Chrono Cross, Xenogears), with a main theme and orchestral score composed by **Yasunori Mitsuda**.

**Kernel Hearts** — Developed by **Ephemera Games** and published by **Whitethorn Games**, this magical girl–themed **action roguelike** launches on **PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), PS5, Xbox Series, and Nintendo Switch 2**. Up to four players team up to climb the Tower of Babel and dethrone God, mixing and matching attacks, spells, and movement abilities across runs for the M.A.H.O.U Unit. The game features Japanese voice acting, and between runs players spend rewards to unlock chips and build bonds with hub characters. It was playable at **Tokyo Game Show** this past week ahead of launch.

## Psycho Casket — Striking Indie JRPG Demo Now on Steam

**Psycho Casket**, an indie JRPG developed by **ClockworkLink**, has a **playable demo on Steam** (an updated Version 2.0 is live). The game follows a vampire protagonist attempting to rescue her friends from a universe-devouring eldritch beast that has disguised itself as a sentient social messaging app — saving her friends means ending the world, putting players in the position of choosing between personal bonds and everyone else.

Gameplay combines conversation-driven investigation, environmental puzzle-solving, and turn-based combat. Additional party members beyond the main four can be recruited, each with distinct systems and mechanics.

Sources: [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/new-turn-based-rpgs-to-play-september-12-2026/) · [ClockworkLink itch.io](https://clockworklink.itch.io/psycho-casket-demo)
