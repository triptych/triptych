## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Near-Universal Acclaim

**Intelligent Systems** and **Nintendo**'s **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** released worldwide on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2**. Review scores place it among the best games of the year: the game is sitting at **89 on Metacritic** and approximately **91 on OpenCritic**, with over 90% of critics recommending it.

IGN awarded it a perfect 10, Eurogamer gave it 5/5, and Game Informer scored it 8.75/10. Critics consistently praise the four interconnected protagonist storylines — featuring **Cai**, a boy fighting to free his imprisoned father; **Dietrich**, a battle-seeking swordsman; **Theodora**, a queen defending her homeland; and **Leda**, a musician driven by revenge — as well as the enormous tactical campaign, flexible structure, and willingness to push beyond the series' established formula. Some reviewers noted the pace can be slow under the weight of its ambition, but no major outlet withheld a recommendation.

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches Simultaneously Worldwide

**Nihon Falcom**'s remake **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — a full-scale remake of the 2006 *Trails in the Sky SC* — launched **September 17, 2026** simultaneously on **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam**.

The game picks up immediately after the conclusion of the first chapter, continuing Estelle's pursuit of Joshua across the Liberl Kingdom. The release was handled by **GungHo** and **Falcom** for the Switch and Switch 2 versions, making this one of the biggest Kiseki same-day multi-platform launches to date.

## Another Eden Begins Out Now — Gacha Removed, Reviews Mixed-Positive

**Wright Flyer Studios**' standalone console adaptation **Another Eden Begins** released on **PC (September 16)** and **Nintendo Switch / Switch 2 (September 17)**. The game is based on the long-running mobile RPG *Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space* but strips out virtually all gacha mechanics — no banners, no pulls, no premium currency traps — making it a traditional premium release.

The script is by **Masato Kato** (writer of *Chrono Trigger*, *Chrono Cross*, and portions of *Xenogears*), and the time-traveling adventure spans 18 party members with a New Game+ system designed around multiple endings. Critics describe it as a "rediscovered PSP-era gem" in spirit, though the bobblehead sprite aesthetic and mobile-game origins show. Reviews land in the mixed-to-positive range, with Metacritic placing it around mid-70s and Steam user reviews at roughly 74% positive.

Sources: [Slant Magazine Review](https://www.slantmagazine.com/games/another-eden-begins-review-kato-masato/) · [Siliconera Review](https://www.siliconera.com/review-another-eden-begins-is-a-solid-adaptation-of-the-strongest-part/) · [Dual Shockers Review](https://www.dualshockers.com/another-eden-begins-review/) · [Final Weapon Review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/15/another-eden-begins-review-the-friends-weve-made-along-the-way/) · [Game8 Review](https://game8.co/reviews/another-eden-begins/another-eden-begins-review)

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Reviews: Metacritic 86, Combat Praised

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** launched **September 4, 2026** on **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC**. The game earned an **86 on Metacritic** (48 reviews) and an 85 on OpenCritic (63 outlets), with IGN awarding the franchise's first-ever perfect **10/10**.

Critics are nearly unanimous on the quality of the swordplay — calling it one of Capcom's best action games in years — but are divided on whether the 25-hour campaign earns its length, with some reviewers finding it stretched beyond its combat design. The game is the first mainline Onimusha entry in over two decades.

## Dragon Quest Heroes: Torneko's Mystery Dungeon Classic HD Shadow Drops — First Official English Release

**Square Enix** shadow-dropped **Dragon Quest Heroes: Torneko's Mystery Dungeon -Classic HD-** on **September 9, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam**.

The game is an HD remaster of *Torneko's Great Adventure: Mystery Dungeon*, a **Chunsoft** roguelike developed exclusively for the Super Famicom in September 1993. This marks the **first time the game has received an official English localization** in its 33-year history.

The remaster adds updated HD visuals, a new opening video, quality-of-life improvements, and interactive streaming features for the Steam version. A free demo for **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** also launched the same day, playable through the conclusion of the Witherwood story section; save data transfers to the full game at launch.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21336-dragon-quest-heroes-tornekos-mystery-dungeon-classic-hd-shadow-drops-on-switch-1-2-ps5-xbox-pc-stea) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/torneko-mystery-dungeon-classic-hd-pokemon-shiren-wanderer-roguelike-2000733006) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-09-09/dragon-quest-heroes-torneko-mystery-dungeon-game-hd-version-announced-for-same-day-release/.241564) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/square-enix-announces-tornekos-mystery-dungeon-classic-hd-for-nintendo-switch-2-and-switch-out-today/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/09/dragon-quest-heroes-tornekos-mystery-dungeon-classic-hd-now-available/)

## Xenoblade Chronicles 3 Switch 2 Edition Reveals Heroes' Vault and New Hero Shimmer

During the **Nintendo Direct on September 9**, **Nintendo** and **Monolith Soft** revealed full details for **Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition**, arriving **December 3, 2026** (digital and physical simultaneously; Switch 1 owners can buy an upgrade pack for **$9.99**).

The headline new content is **Heroes' Vault**, a roguelike survival mode in the vein of *Vampire Survivors*: players defeat waves of enemies, earn enhanced abilities or new party members between rounds, and work toward a powerful final boss. A new hero, **Shimmer**, is exclusive to the Switch 2 edition — an Attacker class built around rapid combos, heavy strikes, and a Foresight ability. Technical improvements bring the game to **4K docked / 1080p handheld** at 60 FPS.

## Fate/EXTRA Record Dated January 28, 2027

**Meteorise** and **TYPE-MOON**'s long-awaited remake **Fate/EXTRA Record** received a worldwide release date of **January 28, 2027**, announced on **September 3, 2026**. The game launches on **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. Physical editions are distributed by **XSEED Games** and **Marvelous Europe**.

The remake fully overhauls the original's rock-paper-scissors combat with a new strategic **deck-building Command Card system**. Published by **Aniplex**, physical pre-orders are now open. The game is a remake of the 2010 PlayStation Portable title set in a virtual arena where Masters battle for a wish-granting Holy Grail.
