## Granblue Fantasy: Relink — Endless Ragnarok Arrives July 9

**Cygames** is bringing an expanded edition of *Granblue Fantasy: Relink* to Switch 2 on **July 9, 2026**, alongside a paid expansion update for existing PS4, PS5, and Steam owners.

**What's included in Endless Ragnarok:**
- **Six new playable characters:** Beatrix, Eustace, Gallanza, Maglielle, Fraux, and Fediel — bringing the roster to 26 fighters.
- **New story content** continuing after the base game's ending.
- **Roguelite solo mode** — a self-contained endgame loop distinct from the co-op quest structure.
- **New bosses and combat systems.**

**Edition and pricing breakdown:**

| Version | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Switch 2 complete edition | TBA | Base game + Endless Ragnarok |
| Upgrade (PS4/PS5/Steam) | $29.99 | For existing Relink owners |

**Switch 2 exclusives:** Local co-op (never available on other platforms) and GameShare support. Cross-play is enabled across all platforms.

Nintendo Life's hands-on preview called it "right at home on Switch 2," noting the handheld performance targets favourably and the new roguelite mode as a natural fit for portable sessions.

## Phantom Blade Zero Delayed to October 29

**S-GAME** announced at PlayStation's **June 2026 State of Play** that *Phantom Blade Zero* will launch on **October 29, 2026**, shifted back 50 days from the previously confirmed September 9 date. Director **Liang "Soulframe" Qiwei** cited the extra time as dedicated to polish, writing the announcement personally on the game's social channels.

A new trailer was shown at the broadcast, and pre-orders are confirmed to open **this summer**. The game remains a PS5 console exclusive at launch; no additional platform announcements were made.

The delay puts it one week before a busy November window, though Phantom Blade Zero occupies a genre lane — wuxia-inspired character-action RPG — with few direct competitors at that time of year.

## Chained Echoes Physical Switch Edition — Pre-Orders Open

After a saga involving a lawsuit and a publisher change, **Chained Echoes** is finally getting a physical Nintendo Switch release. **Super Rare Games** and developer **Matthias Linda** (operating as **Umami Tiger**, co-published by Deck13) opened pre-orders on June 18 for two editions:

| Edition | Price (GBP) | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | £37.20 | Game + Ashes of Elrant DLC on one cartridge |
| Deluxe | £69.60 | Above + hardcover A5 art book, A2 full-colour poster, double-sided gold-plated coin, trading cards, clamshell box |

Editions are expected to ship in **September 2026**.

**Background:** Linda filed a lawsuit against **First Press Games** in early 2026 after more than two years without the physical edition promised to Kickstarter backers (only the standard PS4 copy had shipped). The Super Rare Games partnership is the resolution to that dispute — Kickstarter backers are expected to be compensated separately.

Sources: [Nintendo Life — New Physical Release](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/06/chained-echoes-is-finally-getting-its-physical-release-after-last-attempt-ended-in-a-lawsuit) · [RPG Site — Pre-order Details](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20656-chained-echoes-switch-new-physical-deluxe-complete-edition-preorder-super-rare-games) · [RPGamer — Confirmation](https://rpgamer.com/2026/06/chained-echoes-physical-switch-release-revealed/) · [Nintendo Everything — Overview](https://nintendoeverything.com/chained-echoes-announces-nintendo-switch-physical-release/)

## Steam Next Fest — Final Days, Entropy Gains Traction

Steam Next Fest runs through **June 22**, and with three days remaining a late-moving title is worth flagging:

**Entropy** — a PS1-style tactical JRPG with a dark fantasy tone — has steadily built word of mouth through the event's second half. Players guide a traveling theater troupe through a demon-haunted world; the demo's tone has drawn comparisons to early *Tactics Ogre* and *Vagrant Story*. No release date has been announced, and the developer is solo.

Previously noted Next Fest standouts (Echoes of Aincrad, Stories of Somnia, Lezarishia Stories, Legends of Starkadia) remain in their demo windows through June 22.
