# JRPG News — May 25, 2026

## Dragon Quest 40th Anniversary Livestream Set for May 27 — "Next Game" Reveal Teased

**Square Enix** series creator **Yuji Horii** confirmed today that a dedicated Dragon Quest livestream will air on **May 27**, timed to the franchise's 40th anniversary. Horii stated plainly: "I think we'll be able to make an announcement about the next game," adding that Square Enix will "have various other things" to show beyond that headline reveal.

The most anticipated piece is almost certainly **Dragon Quest XII: The Flames of Fate**, announced during the series' 35th anniversary in 2021 and almost entirely silent since. Industry speculation also points to potential reveals of **HD-2D remakes of the Zenithian Trilogy** (Dragon Quest IV, V, and VI), following the successful pattern set by prior Square Enix HD-2D projects. Horii's comments were made on the *KosoKoso* broadcast and quickly circulated across RPGFan, GamesRadar, Gematsu, and Nintendo Life. The livestream's exact air time has not been announced.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio and Persona 4 Golden Leaving Xbox Game Pass May 31 — Final Fantasy VI Arrives June 2

Five titles are exiting **Xbox Game Pass** on **May 31**, with the two biggest for JRPG fans being **Metaphor: ReFantazio** and **Persona 4 Golden**. Metaphor joined the service in May 2025 after roughly a year on the market; Persona 4 Golden returned in August 2025 following an earlier removal. Both are gone at month's end alongside Spray Paint Simulator, Against the Storm, and Crypt Custodian. Microsoft is offering a **20% discount** on permanent purchases for any departing title before the deadline.

The notable silver lining: **Final Fantasy VI** is confirmed to arrive on Game Pass on **June 2**, providing an immediate classic JRPG replacement. Persona 4 Revival — the next Persona 4 entry — has also been confirmed as a day-one Game Pass release, though no launch date has been set.

## Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered Launches Globally on May 27

**Aquaplus**'s **Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered** releases worldwide on **May 27** via **Steam**, with simultaneous **PS5** and **Nintendo Switch 2** launches in Japan. The game is a direct sequel to *Monochrome Mobius: Rights and Wrongs Forgotten* and a major continuation of the long-running Utawarerumono saga.

The story follows **Oshtor** and companions as a new shadow falls over Yamato, and is described as bringing the Utawarerumono legend to "a new climax." Combat uses the series' **Action Ring turn-based battle system** with the iconic Akuruka masks playable directly in fights. New characters, expanded town features, and additional battle mechanics have been detailed in preview coverage. The game supports Japanese voices with English, Japanese, and Traditional/Simplified Chinese text on Steam.

## Echo Generation 2 Arrives May 27 on PC and Xbox, Day One on Game Pass

**Cococucumber**'s **Echo Generation 2** releases on **May 27** for PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X|S, included in **Xbox Game Pass** at launch. The sequel to the studio's award-winning turn-based RPG shifts the formula toward **deckbuilding**, giving players a library of over **150 cards** to build devastating combos — from protective shields to timed summons.

The story follows **Jack**, an ordinary dad who tumbles into a dimension beyond his hometown of Maple Town and must battle extra-terrestrial threats alongside quirky allies to find his way home. The game picks up after the events of the original. A playable demo is currently available across storefronts ahead of launch.

## Chronosphere Early Access Now Live

**Chronosphere** — the turn-based RPG flagged in yesterday's roundup as entering Steam Early Access today — is now playable. The developers are targeting a full 1.0 release roughly six months after the Early Access launch.
