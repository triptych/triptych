## Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United — Full 3D Remake Revealed at Nintendo Direct September 2026

**Square Enix** and developer **xeen Inc.** (the studio behind *Trials of Mana* and *Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven*) announced **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United** during the **Nintendo Direct held in September 2026**.

The game is a full 3D remake of the **original 1995 Super Famicom RPG** and launches **February 16, 2027** on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Xbox Series).

Confirmed features include:

- **HD visuals** with fully voiced cutscenes
- **Freeform Scenario System** — the original's non-linear structure preserved and expanded
- **War Mode Minigame** and **Business Minigame** as side activities
- **Timeline Battles** and a new **United Attacks** system
- A roster of **formation options**: Free for All, Desert Lance, Whirlwind, Hunter's Gambit, and Seer's Focus
- The classic **Glimmers** skill acquisition system returns

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — April 8, 2027 Release Date Confirmed

**Square Enix** confirmed a worldwide release date of **April 8, 2027** for **Final Fantasy VII Revelation**, the final entry in the remake trilogy, during a **PlayStation State of Play** broadcast in early September 2026.

The game launches simultaneously on **Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam and Epic Games Store** (also PS5 and Xbox Series). Pre-orders opened at announcement.

New details from the reveal include:

- The **Highwind airship** serves as a core traversal system across the game's open world, described as the largest of the trilogy
- A **Trilogy Edition** is available that grants instant access to *Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade* and *Final Fantasy VII Rebirth*, unlocking *Revelation* at launch
- The game was also **playable at TGS 2026** alongside *Final Fantasy Resonance*

## Fate/EXTRA Record — January 28, 2027 Launch Date, ANIPLEX Takes Over as Publisher

**Fate/EXTRA Record**, the long-in-development remake of the **2010 PSP RPG**, has a confirmed worldwide launch date of **January 28, 2027**, announced during a September 2026 **State of Play Japan** broadcast. The game arrives on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** (also PS5 and PS4).

A notable development surrounding the release date: **Bandai Namco Entertainment** stepped down as publisher earlier this summer after missing its spring 2026 window, with **ANIPLEX** announced as the new publisher on **August 31, 2026**. Developer **TYPE-MOON studio BB** confirmed the game will still meet its original creative vision despite the publisher change.

The game is a remake of the PSP title with:

- Scenario revisions and updated dialogue
- Fully modernized visuals and audio
- **Deck-building turn-based combat** redesigned from the original
- A **playable demo** was available at TGS 2026

## Danganronpa 2×2 — January 14, 2027 Launch Date Confirmed, New Character Revealed

**Spike Chunsoft** confirmed **January 14, 2027** as the release date for **Danganronpa 2×2** during the September 2026 Nintendo Direct, along with a new trailer revealing a previously unannounced character.

The game launches on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Xbox Series). It offers two distinct modes available from the start:

- **Original Mode** — a retelling of *Danganronpa 2: Goodbye Despair* with enhanced HD visuals and modernized presentation
- **Slayhem Mode** — a new scenario set in the same summer resort location but with a completely different storyline

Nintendo Life and Kotaku noted that the new character revealed in the trailer is **Ryota Mitarai**, the animation student introduced in *Danganronpa 3: The End of Hope's Peak High School*, marking the first time a Danganronpa 3 character has been folded into a mainline game entry.

## Persona 4 Revival — Aika Nakamura Confirmed as Location Episode Character; Switch 2 Date Set for May 20, 2027

A **Persona Broadcast** held on **September 19** revealed that **Aika Nakamura** — daughter of the owners of the in-game Aiya Chinese restaurant and a character originally created for the **2011 Persona 4: The Animation anime** — will appear in **Persona 4 Revival**.

**Atlus** later clarified publicly that, despite early communications describing her as a new **Social Link**, Aika is actually a **Location Episode character** — a new content category for the remake that ties into specific in-game locations rather than the traditional Social Link bond progression system. Atlus apologized for the initial confusion in the English materials.

The broadcast also confirmed a **Nintendo Switch 2 release date of May 20, 2027**, separate from the previously announced **February 18, 2027** launch on PC and other platforms.

## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow — Reviews: Divisive but Distinct

**Qliphah in Providence's Shadow**, the horror-themed JRPG from developer **Chant** published by **Kemco**, launched on **September 24** for **Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store** (also PS5). The game had been announced earlier this summer with a counter-timeline combat system built around predicting and disrupting enemy actions.

Critical reception is sharply divided. Reviewers broadly agree the game is **genuinely unusual** — a horror-inflected visual novel with active RPG combat set in a distorted Tokyo, following delivery worker Soma Hoshigami as his transformation leaves him between human and monster.

- **Final Weapon** and **CogConnected** praised its originality and horror atmosphere, calling it "a brilliantly weird JRPG" and one of the year's strangest in a complimentary sense
- **DualShockers** found that initial intrigue collapsed under repetitive systems and a script more interested in terminology than storytelling, summarizing it as "untapped potential"
- **RPGFan** positioned it between the two camps — engaging combat design that struggles to sustain its ideas across a full runtime
- **Metacritic** aggregates reviews in the mid-60s range

Sources: [RPGFan Review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow/) · [Final Weapon Review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/24/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review-steam/) · [DualShockers Review](https://www.dualshockers.com/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [CogConnected Review](https://cogconnected.com/review/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/) · [MonsterVine Review](https://monstervine.com/2026/09/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-review/)
