## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave Launches to Near-Universal Acclaim

**Nintendo** and **Intelligent Systems** released **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** on **September 17, 2026** exclusively for **Nintendo Switch 2**, and critics have responded with some of the highest marks the franchise has ever received.

The game holds a **Metacritic score of 89** based on 53 scored reviews, with **OpenCritic placing it at 89 with 97% of critics recommending** the title. **13 outlets awarded perfect scores**. Notable review highlights include:

- **IGN — 10/10:** "builds upon its strategic arts in ways I couldn't have anticipated"
- **VGC — 10/10:** "an essential game for fans of Fire Emblem and SRPGs"
- **GameSpot — 9/10:** "a sprawling masterpiece of a strategy RPG"
- **Game Informer — 8.75/10:** "massive and worth the time investment"
- **Nintendo Life — 8/10:** "dense, deep, and really rather delightful"

The game features **four interconnected protagonist campaigns** across the Heroic Games, which players can tackle in different orders. Critical praise consistently targets the tactical grid combat, character progression, and the scale of its ambition. The main criticism is its length: full playthroughs reportedly range from **70 to over 100 hours** to see all routes, a scope that some reviewers found exhausting.

Sources: [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-reviews-scores/) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/review/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave/let-the-games-begin) · [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/fire-emblem-fortunes-weaves-fantastic-reviews-have-me-feeling-fomo/) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-are-in) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-review-round-up-switch-2-2000735072)

## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter Launches to Outstanding Reviews

**Nihon Falcom** and **NIS America**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** — the HD rebuild of *Trails in the Sky SC* — launched on **September 17, 2026** for **PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**, arriving just one year after the 1st Chapter remake.

The game earned a **Metacritic score of 91** and an **OpenCritic score of 92** with **97% critic recommendation**, making it one of the highest-reviewed games of 2026. Score highlights:

- **DualShockers — 10/10:** "a flawless JRPG experience"
- **RPG Site — 10/10**
- **PSX Brasil — 100/100**
- **Game Informer — 9.5/10:** "the Trails series' defining entry, and one of the best RPGs of the year"
- **IGN — 9/10**
- **Push Square — 9/10:** "one of the best RPG sequels ever made"
- **RPGFan — highly recommended**

Reviewers praised the outstanding storytelling, the fluid **hybrid action/turn-based combat** system, Falcom's music, and how effectively the game cuts the slow-burn pacing issues some found in the first chapter. Minor criticisms point to inconsistent voice acting, sparse environments, and a plain UI.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/21308-trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/a-superb-sequel) · [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/16/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review/)

## Another Eden Begins Launches as a Premium, Gacha-Free Standalone RPG

**Wright Flyer Studios** released **Another Eden Begins** on **September 17, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. The game is a full standalone premium release — no gacha, no live-service mechanics — built around the *Another Eden* universe.

The creative team is headlined by director **Masato Kato** (Chrono Trigger, Xenogears) and composer **Yasunori Mitsuda** (Xenogears, Chrono Cross). Players take on the role of **Aldo**, who crosses time periods searching for his sister **Feinne** while forging bonds with over **18 recruitable companions**. The combat system is command-based turn-based with party customization central to progression.

A demo covering the first six chapters was available before launch with full save data transfer to the final game. A Switch 2 physical edition was produced in Japan.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20042-another-eden-begins-release-date-steam-switch-2-physical-edition-serial-codes) · [GameDaily](https://gamedaily.com/games/another-eden-begins-release-date-september-2026) · [Bleeding Cool](https://bleedingcool.com/games/another-eden-begins-time-traveling-jrpg-launches-september-2026/) · [GameSpace](https://gamespace.com/all-articles/news/another-eden-begins-launched-worldwide/)

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword Launches to Strong Reviews, Sells 1 Million Day One

**Capcom**'s **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** — the first new Onimusha title in over two decades — launched **September 4, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series). The game sold **over 1 million copies worldwide on launch day**, pushing the total franchise lifetime sales past **10 million units**.

Critics responded positively, with the game earning a **Metacritic score of 86** and an **OpenCritic score of 85** with 92% positive reviews. **IGN awarded it a 10/10**, the first perfect score the outlet has given the franchise. Nintendo Life scored it **9/10**, calling it "a successful revival of Capcom's samurai franchise that pushes its gameplay in an exciting new direction while still retaining its core identity."

The most consistent criticisms are its **~20-hour campaign length**, linear structure, and limited post-game content. Praise consistently focuses on the swordplay depth, boss fight quality, and modernized dark-fantasy presentation.

Sources: [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-hits-1-million-sales-on-launch-day/) · [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-sales-top-one-million-on-launch-day) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/round-up-the-reviews-for-onimusha-way-of-the-sword-are-in) · [GameDaily](https://gamedaily.com/games/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review-roundup) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/games/2026/08/31/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-review-more-inagaki-than-kurosawa/)

## Trails from Zero and Trails to Azure Launch on PS5 and Switch 2

**NIS America** released upgraded PS5 and Switch 2 versions of **The Legend of Heroes: Trails from Zero** and **The Legend of Heroes: Trails to Azure** on **September 10, 2026**.

Both versions feature **4K resolution, 120 FPS, and mouse input support on Switch 2**. The games are available separately as digital downloads ($39.99 USD each) or as a physical bundle ($69.99 USD). A **limited edition** at $109.99 includes an art book, soundtrack CDs, holographic art cards, an acrylic stand, iron-on insignias, and a Mishelam Wonderland coaster set. Existing Switch and PC owners also received free patches adding Switch 2 compatibility and performance improvements.

## Nintendo Direct September 2026 — Key JRPG Reveals

**Nintendo** held a **Nintendo Direct on September 9, 2026** that included several significant JRPG announcements:

### Persona 4 Revival and Persona 6 Confirmed for Switch 2

**Atlus** confirmed both **Persona 4 Revival** and **Persona 6** are coming to **Nintendo Switch 2**. Persona 4 Revival's Switch 2 version is dated for **May 20, 2027** — a few months after its PS5/Xbox Series/PC launch on **February 18, 2027**. Persona 6's Switch 2 appearance was minimal — a brief logo reveal with no trailer or gameplay shown, and no release window.

### Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United — Full 3D Remake for 2027

**Square Enix** announced **Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United**, a full 3D remake of the **1995 Super Famicom RPG** *Romancing SaGa 3*. The game launches **February 16, 2027** on **PC, PS5, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2**.

The remake features HD visuals, fully voiced cutscenes, overhauled combat, and the series' signature **free-form scenario structure** with **eight playable protagonists** (Mikhail, Monika, Katarina, Khalid, Julian, Thomas, Sarah, Ellen) and **20 recruitable companions**. The **"Glimmer" system** for acquiring new abilities in battle returns from the original.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-09-09/square-enix-announces-romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-remake-game/.241594) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/09/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced/) · [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/romancing-saga-3-destinies-united-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2-switch)

### Eternal Anima — New Marvelous JRPG Announced

**Marvelous** announced **Eternal Anima**, a new original JRPG launching **March 4, 2027** for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam**. The game is directed by **Katsuhisa Higuchi**, with story concept by **Keisuke Makino** and scenario written by **Kazushige Nojima** (Final Fantasy VII Remake trilogy, Final Fantasy XV).

The game follows protagonist **Wade**, who travels through time saving past civilizations from catastrophes while unraveling the secrets of a lost history. Combat is tactical turn-based, with a **Psychometry mechanic** that lets players predict enemy actions and inherit the abilities and classes of heroes encountered in the past.

A **physical Limited Edition** at **$89.99** is planned for Switch 2, including the game, a two-disc soundtrack, nine character art cards with display stand, and an art book.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/marvelous-announces-rpg-eternal-anima-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-and-pc) · [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-09-09/marvelous-announces-eternal-anima-game-for-switch-2-ps5-xbox-x-s-pc/.241573) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/09/marvelous-reveals-new-turn-based-rpg-eternal-anima/) · [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/contents/64748-eternal-anima-coming-to-switch-2-on-march-4th-2027)
