## Edge of Memories Launches November 5 — Not Cancelled After All

When **Midgar Studio** was liquidated in July 2026, the fate of **Edge of Memories** looked bleak — Nacon had failed to find a buyer for the studio and cancellation seemed the likeliest outcome. That assessment turned out to be wrong.

At the **Xbox Tokyo Game Show 2026 Broadcast** in September, **Nacon** confirmed **Edge of Memories will release on November 5, 2026** for **PC via Steam**, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Early access for Deluxe Edition buyers opens **November 3**. The standard edition is priced at **$39.99** and the Deluxe at **$49.99**; all pre-orders include a Legacy Costume pack. Steam pre-orders are not available.

Nacon stated the launch plan is unaffected by the studio's closure — making Edge of Memories Midgar's **final game** and an 18-year studio's swan song. The action-JRPG features a soundtrack by **Yasunori Mitsuda** and Japanese voice acting.

## Octopath Traveler 1 & 2 Arrive on Switch 2 — No Upgrade Path for Existing Owners

**Square Enix** launched **Octopath Traveler** and **Octopath Traveler II** on **Nintendo Switch 2 on October 1, 2026**, coinciding with the original game's eighth anniversary.

Both Switch 2 editions offer **improved frame rates and resolutions** over the original Switch versions. Pricing is **$59.99 each**, with a digital bundle containing both games available for **$74.99**.

The significant catch: there is **no upgrade path** for existing owners. Save data is not compatible between the Switch and Switch 2 versions — players cannot transfer a save from the original edition, and anyone who wants the enhanced versions must repurchase and start over. No upgrade pack or discount for prior purchasers has been announced.

Sources: [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/octopath-traveler-and-its-sequel-are-coming-to-nintendo-switch-2-this-october/) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/07/octopath-traveler-1-and-2-announced-for-switch-2-with-improved-performance-and-visuals) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/07/13/the-first-two-octopath-traveler-games-are-coming-to-switch-2-in-october-but-theres-no) · [Full Cleared](https://fullcleared.com/news/octopath-traveler-switch-2-ports-skip-the-upgrade-path/)

## Decapolice Delayed Again — Now Targeting 2027

**Level-5**'s long-delayed detective RPG **Decapolice** has been pushed from its 2026 window to **sometime in 2027**. The game was originally announced in 2023 and has slipped multiple times since. It is currently targeting release on **Nintendo Switch 2, PS5, and PC**.

Level-5 CEO **Akihiro Hino** offered brief remarks: *"We know it's taking some time to get the game ready, but we're working to deliver a truly unique gameplay experience."* A playable demo was made available alongside the delay announcement.

The delay is the game's third (it was pushed from 2023 to 2024 to 2026 to now 2027), though Level-5 has not elaborated on specific reasons beyond the general statement.

Sources: [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/09/promising-detective-rpg-decapolice-officially-delayed-into-2027) · [Game Luster](https://gameluster.com/?p=140802) · [PSU](https://www.psu.com/news/decapolice-has-been-delayed-to-2027-confirms-level-5/)

## Gachiakuta: Breakout Officially Titled at TGS 2026 — 2027 for PC

**Com2uS Group** confirmed the final title of its *Gachiakuta* anime adaptation at the **Xbox Tokyo Game Show 2026 Broadcast**: previously known as *GACHIAKUTA: The Game*, it is now officially **GACHIAKUTA: BREAKOUT**, targeting a **2027 release on PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Xbox Series X|S).

The game is a **real-time action RPG** recreating the world of the anime in full 3D. The first gameplay trailer showed a boss fight against Jabber, with players controlling protagonist Zanka alongside key characters Rudo, Enjin, and Riyo, each with distinct weapons and fighting styles. Features include:

- Co-op multiplayer
- Expanding and decorating the Cleaners' headquarters using mission loot
- A graffiti collection system
- Character growth through battle experience and items

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21481-survival-action-rpg-gachiakuta-breakout-launches-in-2027-for-ps5-xbox-steam) · [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/gachiakuta-game-reveals-official-title-and-first-gameplay-trailer-2027-release/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/17/gachiakuta-the-game-is-now-titled-gachiakuta-breakout-launches-in-2027/)

## Stranger Than Heaven — RGG Studio's New Game Dated January 15, 2027; TGS Demo Live

**Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio**'s new IP **Stranger Than Heaven** received a confirmed release date of **January 15, 2027** at TGS 2026, where Sega dedicated a large portion of its booth to the title with a playable demo and a theater zone showcasing previously unannounced songs.

The game is an action-RPG from the team behind the Yakuza/Like a Dragon series. It launches on **PC via Steam** (also PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with day-one Game Pass). Physical and digital editions are available for pre-order, including a Collector's Edition.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21067-sega-will-feature-stranger-than-heaven-at-tokyo-game-show-2026) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/06/stranger-than-heaven-releasing-in-january/) · [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/stranger-than-heaven-reveals-new-gameplay-at-xbox-partner-preview/)
