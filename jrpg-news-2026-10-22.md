## Demon's Night Fever — Out Now on Switch and PC

**Arc System Works** and **SuperNiche**'s tactical RPG **Demon's Night Fever** launched today, **October 22**, on **Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam** (also PS5).

The game is produced and written by **Sohei Niikawa**, the creator of *Disgaea*, and developed by **Drecom**. The premise casts players as a devil-in-training tasked with corrupting souls on Earth, using a grid-based combat system built around sacrificing your own underlings. The more allies you deliberately destroy, the stronger you can reincarnate them — a mechanic the developers call the **Peon Exploitation System**. A dark comedy thread runs through the whole game, with God deliberately characterized as a scoundrel so players feel no guilt about doing evil things.

The Switch and Steam pages list English and Japanese voiceovers with subtitles in French, Korean, and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

## Final Fantasy Resonance — Launches Today on Switch, Switch 2, and PC

**Square Enix**'s **Final Fantasy Resonance** is out today, **October 22**, on **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC** (also PS5).

Described as the first HD-2D *Final Fantasy*, the game is a full single-player JRPG adaptation of the *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* story — rewritten from the ground up, with no gacha mechanics. Square Enix has been positioning it as a standalone entry accessible to players unfamiliar with the mobile original.

RPG Pursuit posted a review on October 20 ahead of launch.

## Vixen 357 — Classic Mecha SRPG Lands on Switch and PC Tomorrow

**Vixen 357**, the long-lost 1992 SEGA Mega Drive strategy RPG developed by **NCS/Masaya**, releases on **Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam on October 23** — exactly 34 years after its original Japanese launch.

The port is developed by **Shinyuden** and published by **Ratalaika Games**, and marks the first time the game has been legally available in English. (An English localization was planned in 2019 but was cancelled.) Switch and Steam store pages list English, Japanese, and Spanish text.

Gameplay is turn-based and grid-based with mechs; when two units clash, the view shifts to a side angle for the exchange. The game features permadeath. The eShop price is listed at **$5.99** for the Switch version.

The port includes save states, rewind, turbo speed, a cheat toggle, and screen filters.
