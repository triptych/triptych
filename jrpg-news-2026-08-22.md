## STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT Launches Globally — Switch 2 Western Version Arrives October 29

**5pb. / MAGES.** launched **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT** on **August 20, 2026** in Japan for **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch**, and simultaneously worldwide on **PC via Steam**. The Xbox Series X|S version also launched August 20 globally, though the **Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and Switch Western console releases** are set for **October 29, 2026**.

Re:Boot is a full remake of the landmark 2009 visual novel, rebuilt from the ground up with:

- **Fully redrawn character art** by original character designer **huke**
- **Fully re-recorded voice performances** for both the Japanese and English casts
- **Dynamic E-mote character animations** replacing the static sprite presentation of the original
- **New story routes** including a previously unreleasable ending
- **Full HD visual overhaul** of all environments and CGs

Standard physical and digital editions are priced at **$59.99**, with a physical SteelBook edition at **$69.99**. Physical editions of the Switch 2, PS5, and Switch versions are also available.

## Code Vein II: Mask of Idris DLC Dated September 9

**Bandai Namco** confirmed on **August 20, 2026** that **Code Vein II**'s **Mask of Idris** expansion releases **September 9, 2026**, along with a free **Version 2.0 update** for all owners of the base game.

The Mask of Idris story takes place after the events of the main game's **"Luna Fraterna"** ending. Players explore an **alternate world generated by the converged passions of vanished worlds**, encountering **Liv Voda**, former guardian of the Temporal Rift, as a central new character. To access the DLC, players must have completed the Luna Fraterna ending and finished the **"Valentin's Request"** sub-mission.

The DLC is included in the game's **Deluxe** and **Ultimate Editions**, or available for individual purchase. Details on the free Version 2.0 update content will be revealed separately ahead of launch.

## Brigandine: Abyss Launches This Week — Reviews Round-Up

**Happinet's** long-awaited **Brigandine: Abyss** hits **PC via Steam on August 26** and **Nintendo Switch 2** (alongside PS5 and Xbox Series) on **August 27**. The game is developed by **Adglobe and Happinet** and published in the West by **NIS America**; it is the first entry in the hex-strategy franchise since *The Legend of Runersia* in 2020.

Set in an entirely new world, *Abyss* features **six story campaigns** allowing the conflict to be seen from different factional perspectives. The strategic loop alternates between an **Organization phase** (resource management, unit recruitment, monster binding) and an **Attack phase** using hexagonal-grid combat with elemental attributes and skill interactions. A **Deluxe Edition** for Switch 2 bundles a mini art book and digital soundtrack.

Early reviews are mixed-to-positive, with critics calling it a **meaningful step forward for the series' formula**:

- The narrative receives more attention than previous entries, and the new battlefield verticality and positioning mechanics demand sharper thinking
- **Enemy AI** and **replayability** are the most common criticisms — the AI is easy to predict, and subsequent campaigns don't hold up as well as the first
- Most reviewers place first-playthrough length around **18 hours**

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/04/brigandine-abyss-launches-august-26-for-pc-august-27-for-ps5-xbox-series-and-switch-2) · [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/21120-brigandine-abyss-review) · [RPGFan Review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/brigandine-abyss/) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/04/22/brigandine-abyss-release-date-pc-ps5-switch-2-xbox-series-xs/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/04/brigandine-abyss-launching-in-august/)

## Neptunia Unlimited Launches in Japan This Week — Western Date TBA

**Compile Heart's** **Neptunia Unlimited** (Choushinjikuu Game Neptune ∞) launches **August 27, 2026 in Japan** for **PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch**, and **Nintendo Switch 2**. The PS4 version is digital-only; physical editions are available for the other platforms.

This is the **first new mainline Neptunia game in 11 years**, following Megadimension Neptunia VII (2015). The game introduces a **loop mechanic**: protagonist Neptune awakens alone after a catastrophic meteor event, gains the ability to "retry" — looping between endings and new beginnings — guided by an unknown voice.

Returning goddesses include **Neptune / Purple Heart, Noire / Black Heart, Blanc / White Heart**, and **Vert / Green Heart**, joined by three new goddesses: **Rouge / Red Heart, Ciel / Blue Heart**, and **Anna & Lot / Eclipse Heart**. Combat is action-based with an aerial focus distinct from the series' traditional turn-based systems.

A **Western release date has not yet been announced**, though NIS America is expected to localize the title given the series' history.

## Beast of Reincarnation Review Round-Up — Game Freak's Genre Debut Divides Critics

**Game Freak's** first multiplatform non-Pokémon action RPG, **Beast of Reincarnation**, launched **August 4, 2026** on PC (Steam), with console versions to follow. The Soulslike ARPG is published by **Fictions** and marks a significant genre departure for the developer.

Critical reception has landed in mixed territory — around **73 on Metacritic** (PC) / **76 on OpenCritic**. The major points of division:

- **Praised:** Inventive combat system with originality in a saturated subgenre; optimized PC performance; soul-searching thematic narrative; standout visual and creature design
- **Criticized:** Slow, dry story pacing; the open world feels empty; some frame-rate inconsistency on console; repetition in later areas

Notable individual scores include **9/10 from But Why Tho**, **7/10 from RPG Site**, **6/10 from IGN**, and **5/10 from Game Rant**, illustrating the wide range of opinions. Kotaku called the game "an RPG with good ideas but too much repetition."

Sources: [Final Weapon Review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/08/03/beast-of-reincarnation-review-pc-steam/) · [RPG Site Review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/21019-beast-reincarnation-review) · [But Why Tho Review](https://butwhytho.net/2026/08/beast-of-reincarnation-review/) · [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/beast-of-reincarnation-game-freak-soulslike-review-pokemon-2000721290) · [ResetEra Review Thread](https://www.resetera.com/threads/beast-of-reincarnation-review-thread.1594555/)

## Chronoscript: The Endless End Demo Arrives on Steam

**Shueisha Games** and **DeskWorks** released a free playable demo for **Chronoscript: The Endless End** on **Steam on August 20, 2026**, alongside a new trailer.

The game is an **exploration-based action-adventure RPG** set in a hand-drawn world built from pen-and-ink artwork, with 3D elements introduced for cinematic moments. The premise: you play as an **editor in 2026** who accepts a mysterious assignment at a mountain manor, blacks out, and awakens trapped inside a **cursed manuscript** — an unending tale that keeps writing itself beyond its end.

The demo covers the prologue and includes a boss encounter. The full game is targeting a **fall 2026 release** for **PC via Steam and PlayStation 5**.

## Square Enix Jazz Vol. 2 Announced — Cross-Franchise Arrangements Beyond Final Fantasy

**Square Enix** announced **Square Enix Jazz Vol. 2** on **August 21, 2026**, a 12-track jazz arrangement album spanning multiple franchises rather than focusing on a single series.

Unlike the first *Square Enix Jazz* album (which spotlighted *Final Fantasy*), Vol. 2 draws from a range of Square Enix properties including **Final Fantasy, NieR, Chrono Trigger, Legend of Mana**, and others. Arrangements are performed by world-class trombonist **Eijiro Nakagawa** and bassist **Ryu Kawamura**, with a full ensemble of jazz session players.

An RPGFan music review is among the early coverage published following the announcement.
