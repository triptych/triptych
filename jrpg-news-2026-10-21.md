## Vixen 357 Gets Its First-Ever English Release This Thursday

**Ratalaika Games** and developer **Shinyuden** are bringing the 1992 Sega Mega Drive strategy RPG **Vixen 357** to modern platforms on **October 23**, marking the game's first official English-language release after 34 years in Japan-only obscurity.

Vixen 357 is a mech-pilot SRPG released exclusively in Japan for the Mega Drive on October 23, 1992. The port adds save states, a rewind function, and screen filters, but otherwise keeps the original game intact — this is a clean port, not a remake or remaster. The game draws comparisons to the Fire Emblem series, including **permadeath for fallen mech pilots**.

The game launches digitally on **Nintendo Switch** for **$5.99**, with compatibility noted for Nintendo Switch 2. The Windows PC release via Steam is also confirmed for later this year. A separate English-language **physical Mega Drive cartridge** from Shinyuden is also available for purchase on Shinyuden's site (€69.50), sold independently of the digital port.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Launches to Mixed Reviews

**Bandai Namco**'s **Tales of Eternia Remastered** launched on **October 16** for Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam (along with PS4, PS5, and Xbox Series). The remaster arrives with sharply divided opinions from critics.

The remaster's highlights include a **brand-new English localization and script** and the reinstatement of **skits** — a Tales series staple that was cut from the original Western PlayStation release in 2001 due to memory constraints. Players can toggle between remastered and original visuals via a **Graphics Mode** switch, and between new and original sound effects via an **SFX Mode** switch.

Criticisms center on the visual overhaul: Shacknews called it "the bare minimum under the hood," faulting the upscaling and sprite filtering. CGM (reviewing the PS5 version) was more positive, praising the new localization and quality-of-life changes while acknowledging the game's dated presentation. Japan received the game one day earlier on **October 15**.

## Villion: Code Confirmed for 2027 Western Release

**Idea Factory International** announced in September that **Villion: Code** — **Compile Heart**'s action RPG that launched in Japan on June 25, 2026 — will arrive in the West in **2027** for **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PC via Steam**, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5.

The game's creative pedigree is notable: it is produced by **Kouji "Cozy" Okada**, co-founder of Atlus and creator of the Shin Megami Tensei and Persona franchises. Character design is by **Ilya Kuvshinov** (Ghost in the Shell: SAC_2045), the scenario is written by **Tadashi Satomi** (Persona 1 & 2), and the soundtrack is composed by **Tsukasa Masuko** (Shin Megami Tensei I & II).

Physical editions will be available for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5 in North America (PlayStation 5 only in Europe), with the console versions keeping **Japanese voice acting and English subtitles**. A specific release date has not been announced.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio Switch 2 — Strohl Trailer Released, Demo Available

**ATLUS** continues its character trailer series for **Metaphor: ReFantazio** on Nintendo Switch 2, releasing a spotlight trailer for **Strohl** on October 13. Strohl is a clemar tribe warrior who enlists in the army despite his noble background, motivated by a strong personal sense of justice. Earlier trailers in the series covered the game's protagonist (September) and fairy companion **Gallica** (September 23).

The Switch 2 version launches **November 12, 2026**, at **$39.99** for the standard edition. A Guidebook Edition ($49.99) and an Atlus 35th Anniversary Edition ($69.99) are also available, along with a limited Steelbook Edition. A **free Prologue demo** is available on the Nintendo Switch eShop now, with save data carrying over to the full game.

Metaphor: ReFantazio originally launched in October 2024 to a 94 Metacritic score, won Best Role-Playing Game, Best Narrative, and Best Art Direction at The Game Awards 2024, and sold one million copies on its launch day.

## Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom — February 25, 2027 Date Confirmed, Gameplay Detailed

**Koei Tecmo America** set a firm release date of **February 25, 2027** for **Atelier Karia: The Night Kingdom & the Guide of Memories** in September, alongside a new trailer showcasing the continent of **Aladiss** and a deep dive into gameplay systems. The game launches on Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, PC via Steam, and Xbox Series X|S.

Key gameplay details from the reveal:

- **Eating mechanic:** Karia can consume gathered ingredients while foraging, triggering immediate effects. Consuming animal-based components, for example, can cause Karia to sprout animal ears and gain special combat traits.
- **Combat:** Players can parry enemy attacks in real time, with a pause-and-select command menu for skills and items. **Style changes** let Karia shift her fighting style and appearance as the story progresses.
- **Synthesis:** Alchemy remains the series' core pillar, with a renewed cauldron system set in an underworld location.

The story follows amnesiac alchemist **Karia** and companions including **Servio Lektor**, **Fina Rozen**, and fellow alchemist **Yumia Liessfeldt** (protagonist of Atelier Yumia) as Karia searches for a way to recover her lost memories in the underworld.

Sources: [Koei Tecmo America](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/?p=3877) · [Gematsu](https://gematsu.com/2026/06/atelier-karia-the-night-kingdom-the-guide-of-memories-first-trailer-gameplay-details-and-screenshots) · [RPGSite](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20673-atelier-karia-the-night-kingdom-guide-memories-first-look-trailer-introduces-characters-gameplay) · [Full Cleared](https://fullcleared.com/news/atelier-karia-serves-up-story-combat-and-snack-details/)
