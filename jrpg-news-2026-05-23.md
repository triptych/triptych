# JRPG News — May 23, 2026

## STARBITES Reviews Are In — Mixed Reception for NIS America's Mecha JRPG

**IKINAGAMES** and **NIS America**'s sci-fi mecha JRPG **STARBITES** launched on May 21 for PC (Steam), PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch — though the Nintendo Switch 2 version was delayed to an unannounced window. The game arrived in the West after a Japan-only release in October 2025.

Set on **Bitter**, a desert planet left scarred by an interstellar war, STARBITES follows **Lukida**, a teenage scavenger whose encounter with a being called the Oracle drags her into a conflict involving hostile AI and buried truths. Each party member pilots a personal **Motorbot** that can be customized with weapons, armor, engines, and cores — crafted from materials scavenged during exploration. The machine designs are explicitly inspired by **Akira Toriyama**.

Review aggregation on OpenCritic currently sits at a **65 average** ("Weak") across 15 critics, with only 33% recommending it. The split is wide: Hardcore Gamer awarded 4/5 and praised the "engaging lead character, rewarding boss battles, and surprisingly rich worldbuilding," while Nintendo Life gave it 4/10, citing "repetitive dungeon design, shallow regular encounters, and dated presentation." The game appears to land squarely in "for fans of the genre only" territory.

## Tales of Arise — Beyond the Dawn Edition Lands on Nintendo Switch 2

**Bandai Namco**'s acclaimed 2021 action JRPG **Tales of Arise** made its Nintendo Switch 2 debut yesterday, **May 22**, in the form of the **Beyond the Dawn Edition** — a bundle that includes the base game and the full *Beyond the Dawn* expansion (20+ hours of additional story content). The package is priced at **$49.99** and ships as a **Game-Key Card** (no game data on the card itself). The download weighs in at **53.4 GB**.

Performance-wise, Nintendo Life's review notes that cutscenes run at a clean **1080p / 60fps**, while open-world exploration and battles drop to **30fps**. The port has been described as "solid" — nothing transformative, but a reliable way for Switch-first players to finally experience the title alongside its expansion. It's one of the higher-profile JRPG back-catalog releases the Switch 2 has received since launch.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio and Persona 4 Golden Leaving Game Pass May 31 — Final Fantasy VI Arrives June 2

A notable JRPG shuffle is coming to **Xbox Game Pass** at the end of the month. On **May 31**, both **Metaphor: ReFantazio** and **Persona 4 Golden** will be removed from the service. Metaphor — Atlus and Studio Zero's fantasy RPG that won multiple GOTY awards in 2024 — has been on the service since its launch. Persona 4 Golden had returned to Game Pass in August 2025 after a previous run from January 2023 to January 2024.

Subscribers looking to keep either title should note that departing games typically receive a purchase discount in the final days before removal.

The timing of the removals is softened slightly by the incoming addition: **Final Fantasy VI Pixel Remaster** arrives on **Xbox Game Pass Ultimate, Premium, and PC Game Pass on June 2**. The pixel remaster — which Square Enix released in 2022 — includes redrawn HD-2D-adjacent sprites, re-recorded music, and quality-of-life additions like auto-battle and a bestiary. It will be many subscribers' first time playing one of the most celebrated entries in the Final Fantasy series.
