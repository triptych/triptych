# JRPG News — May 21, 2026

## Persona 5: The Phantom X Ver. 4.1 — Persona 3 Reload Crossover Now Live

**Atlus** and **Black Wings Game Studio** launched **Ver. 4.1** of *Persona 5: The Phantom X* yesterday, bringing a full crossover story event with *Persona 3 Reload*. The update adds **Makoto Yuki** — the protagonist of *Persona 3 Reload* — as a new recruitable 5★ character via the time-limited contract gacha banner "When Moon Reaches Stars," running until **June 25, 2026**.

Makoto is classified as a Fire-attribute Assassin with an offensive kit built around **Moon Phase stacks**, which he builds through his own skills and ally support to unleash heavy burst damage while also providing party-wide buffs. The crossover story, titled **"Met by Moonlight Part 1,"** sends the Phantom Thieves into a space resembling Tartarus where they fight alongside S.E.E.S., with familiar locations from *Persona 3 Reload* — including Paulownia Mall — rendered in P5X's visual style.

The update also includes a P5X jazz remix of *Persona 3 Reload*'s battle theme "It's Going Down Now," available via the game's official YouTube channel. The global English and Japanese servers launched simultaneously.

## LumenTale: Memories of Trey — Creature Collector JRPG Launches May 26

**Team17**-backed indie **LumenTale: Memories of Trey** releases on **Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam on May 26**. The game follows **Trey**, a boy piecing together lost memories while traveling across **Talea** — a world shaped by old imperial divisions and protectors called the Lumen.

The creature-collecting core revolves around **Animon**, mystical beings tied to a life force called Anivis that can bond with partners and fight alongside them. Key features include:
- Roughly **140 Animon** across **13 elemental types** to collect
- Combat mixing **1v1 encounters** and **4v4 party battles**
- Side activities: trading, cooking and crafting at Fountains, and training in the **Anispace** hub

The game distinguishes itself from the Pokémon formula by leaning into a more traditional JRPG structure — Trey has a defined personality and goals, and the world's lore is front and center rather than backdrop. The project started as a Kickstarter in 2023, hit its €75,000 goal with nearly 3,000 backers, and later secured Team17 as publisher in 2025.

## Coffee Talk Tokyo Launches Today on All Platforms

**Chorus Worldwide Games** and **Toge Productions** release **Coffee Talk Tokyo** today on **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam**. The third entry in the *Coffee Talk* series moves the late-night café setting from Seattle's fictional multicultural city to **modern Tokyo**, where players brew drinks and hear the stories of a mix of humans and yōkai.

The game was briefly delayed from its original March 2025 date to allow more polish — the developer cited wanting the final details of story and character to "shine." Nintendo Life's review calls it "an enjoyable, bittersweet third blend," suggesting the shift in setting brings fresh energy while maintaining the series' signature slow, cozy rhythm. While not a JRPG in the traditional sense, the game sits squarely in the narrative RPG / visual novel space that has a strong overlap with the JRPG audience.

## Zero Parades: For Dead Spies — Disco Elysium-Adjacent RPG Out Now

Also launching today, **Zero Parades: For Dead Spies** is an espionage-themed RPG from a team that has cited *Disco Elysium* as a clear influence. Players step into the role of operative **Hershel Wilk**, navigating missions through **dice-roll checks**, branching dialogue, and a skill progression system that shapes how you approach problems.

Details remain sparse beyond the elevator pitch, but the dice-driven resolution system and emphasis on multiple viable approaches to each situation put it firmly in the skills-based narrative RPG space. It releases today on PC.
