## Blue Reflection Quartet — Critical Reception Divided at Launch

**Koei Tecmo** and **Gust**'s **Blue Reflection Quartet** launched on **July 30** for **Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam** (also PlayStation 5), collecting all four entries in the magical-girl RPG series into a single package: the original *Blue Reflection*, *Blue Reflection: Second Light*, the *Blue Reflection Ray* anime, and the previously mobile-only *Blue Reflection Sun*.

Critical reception landed on opposite ends of the spectrum. **PlayStation LifeStyle** called it "A JRPG Collection Done Right," praising the opportunity to experience the full arc of the series and highlighting that preserving *Blue Reflection Sun* — which would have been lost forever with its mobile service closure — is a genuine public service. **Cubed3** scored the Switch 2 version **8/10**, citing the series' emotionally resonant storytelling and the visual gains from the enhanced hardware. **CogConnected** framed it positively as "Four Emotional Adventures in One Package."

On the other end, **Noisy Pixel** published a sharp critique titled "A Hollow Compilation That Fails The Series Legacy," arguing that the original *Blue Reflection* has received only surface-level remaster treatment and still feels like the 2017 title it is, and that the bundle's pricing is difficult to justify for players who already own *Second Light*. **Digitally Downloaded**'s Switch 2 review echoed similar concerns about the lack of a meaningful upgrade for returning owners.

Sources: [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/review/934391-blue-reflection-quartet-ps5-review-jrpg-collection-done-right/) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/blue-reflection-quartet-review/) · [CogConnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/blue-reflection-quartet-review/) · [Digitally Downloaded](https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/2026/07/review-blue-reflection-quartet-nintendo-switch-2.html) · [Cubed3](https://www.cubed3.com/games/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/blue-reflection-quartet)

## Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Switch 2 Edition — Digital Version Now Live

**Xenoblade Chronicles 2 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition** went live digitally on **July 30** at $59.99. As detailed in last week's overview trailer, the edition adds Xenosaga's **Momo** as a new Rare Blade, new character outfits illustrated by **Masatsugu Saito**, an expanded Merc Assault system supporting up to six Blades per mission, and technical upgrades to **4K docked / 1080p handheld at 60 FPS**. All pre-existing owners of the original Switch version received the free **version 3.0.0 compatibility patch** on July 22.

A physical edition is slated for **October 1**.

## Verho: Curse of Faces — Console Port Launches with "Chaos" Update and RPGFan Review

**Kasur Games** and **CobraTekku Games** brought their King's Field-inspired dark action RPG **Verho: Curse of Faces** to **Nintendo Switch** (and PS5) on **July 30** at **$24.99**, alongside a simultaneous "Chaos" content update available on all platforms including the original PC release.

The game, which first launched on Steam and GOG in November 2025, is set in a world ravaged by the Curse of Faces — a scourge that kills anyone who exposes their bare face, leaving the survivors to live behind masks in the ruined land of Yariv. The console port carries the game's Steam "Very Positive" reputation over with a **15% launch discount** active for the first two weeks.

**RPGFan** published a [review on July 28](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/verho-curse-of-faces/) by Matt Wardell ahead of the console launch, calling it an experience that "truly delivers an authentic yet palatable" entry point for players curious about the King's Field legacy — effectively the successor *King's Field V* that FromSoftware never made.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/kings-field-inspired-dark-fantasy-action-rpg-verho-curse-of-faces-coming-to-ps5-xbox-series-and-switch-on-july-30-alongside-chaos-update) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/verho-curse-of-faces-console-release-date-july-2026/) · [RPGFan Review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/verho-curse-of-faces/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/07/verho-curse-of-faces-spreading-to-consoles-end-of-this-month/)

## Dungeon Antiqua — Retro Dungeon-Crawler Arrives on Nintendo Switch

**Shiromofu Factory**'s chiptune dungeon-crawler **Dungeon Antiqua** launched on **Nintendo Switch** on **July 30** for **$8.99** (also PS5 and Xbox Series), with a **15% launch discount** active for two weeks. The game draws on 1980s and 1990s Japanese RPG aesthetics — hand-drawn pixel art and nostalgic chiptune music — and has held a "Very Positive" rating on Steam since its October 2024 PC debut.

Publisher **AMATA K.K.** brought the game to consoles to expand the audience for what is described as a deliberately paced, exploration-focused crawler closer to classic Wizardry and early Dragon Quest dungeon design than modern action RPGs.

## Metaphor: ReFantazio Switch 2 — RPG Site Preview Flags Framerate Concerns

**RPG Site** published a hands-on preview of the **Nintendo Switch 2** port of **Atlus**'s award-winning fantasy RPG **Metaphor: ReFantazio** — due November 12 — following time with a demo build shown at **Anime Expo**. The assessment was cautiously mixed.

On the positive side, controls feel native, load times are fast, and the game's distinct visual style translates well to the hardware. However, the framerate "dips" noticeably within dungeons, and image quality is described as "just barely holding things back." The settings menu was locked in the demo build, leaving questions about whether the final release will include DLSS or resolution scaling options that could address the performance gaps. RPG Site concluded the port "doesn't quite feel up to par yet" but acknowledged that Atlus has until November to close the gap.

The Switch 2 version will also be available physically and at a lower price than the original release.

Sources: [RPG Site Preview](https://www.rpgsite.net/preview/20834-metaphor-refantazio-has-some-promise-on-switch-2-but-its-not-quite-there-yet) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/06/metaphor-refantazio-is-finally-getting-a-release-on-switch-2) · [SEGA Press Release](https://www.sega.co.jp/en/release/260610_1.html?year=2026&category=1&page=1)
