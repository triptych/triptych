## Kingdom Hearts IV — D23 2026 Confirms Late 2027 Window, Coco World Revealed

**Square Enix** and **Disney** brought **Kingdom Hearts IV** to the **D23 Expo 2026** Disney Entertainment Showcase on August 14, delivering the game's first new trailer since its initial reveal.

The headline news is a confirmed **late 2027 release window** across **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC (Steam)**. The new trailer introduces the **Land of the Dead** from Pixar's *Coco* as a playable world — Sora arrives in the vibrant afterlife city, meets **Miguel** and **Héctor**, and wields a *Coco*-styled Keyblade against the Heartless on the marigold-lit streets.

## Beast of Reincarnation — Game Freak's Action RPG Launches to Strong Reviews

**Game Freak**'s first non-Pokémon AAA title, **Beast of Reincarnation**, launched on **August 4** for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store**. Directed by **Kota Furushima**, the game blends real-time action with pseudo-turn-based commanding, built around a parry-focused combat loop.

The game holds a **73 on Metacritic** — Famitsu awarded it **35/40** — with reviewers praising the heart and combat while noting some rough edges. *Final Weapon* described it as "one of the best games of 2026."

Sources: [Final Weapon Review](https://finalweapon.net/2026/08/03/beast-of-reincarnation-review-pc-steam/) · [WCCFTech Review](https://wccftech.com/review/beast-of-reincarnation-review-turn-based-meets-action-in-one-of-the-years-best-action-rpgs/) · [PC Gamer Review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/beast-of-reincarnation-review/)

## Suikoden: STAR LEAP — Series Returns to Mobile in Japan

**Konami** launched **Suikoden: STAR LEAP** on **iOS and Android in Japan on August 7, 2026**. The game is the first all-new *Suikoden* title in 14 years and the franchise's first mobile-native entry. It is set in the same world as past Suikoden games, canonically positioned a few years before *Suikoden I* and slightly after *Suikoden V*, making it part of the main continuity rather than a spin-off.

The game pairs upgraded pixel art visuals with a timeline-based combat system. Konami confirmed global mobile and **PC via Steam** release details will be announced in future updates.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/08/suikoden-star-leap-launches-august-7-in-japan) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/suikoden-star-leap-japan-release-date-mobile/) · [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Suikoden-Star-Leap-gets-August-7-release-date-Steam-version-still-to-come.1358677.0.html) · [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/suikoden-star-leap-japanese-release-date-set/)

## Onimusha: Way of the Sword — "Severing Fates" Trailer Released

**Capcom** released a new trailer for **Onimusha: Way of the Sword** titled **"Severing Fates"** on August 6. The trailer takes protagonist **Musashi** to the **Yasui Konpiragu Shrine** — traditionally a place for severing unwanted ties — where he discovers a twisted presence preying on those who come to make wishes.

## Ledgerbound — Corporate-Satire Tactical RPG Launches to "Very Positive" Reviews

Indie studio **OmniMegaSuperCorp** launched **Ledgerbound** on **PC via Steam on August 13**, priced at **$29.99** (10% off through August 20). The strategy RPG — developed with creative leads from *Helldivers 2* — casts players as insurance adjuster **Rayna** navigating a bureaucratic fantasy kingdom while leading a grid-based tactical team against the Vacari.

After 80 user reviews the game sits at **93% positive** ("Very Positive") on Steam. *TechTimes* reviewed it as "Corporate Satire Meets Fire Emblem Tactics in Debut Worth Playing," calling out hundreds of customizable abilities and HR-approved romantic entanglements as standout systems.

Sources: [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/08/ledgerbound-out-now/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3315060/Ledgerbound/) · [TechTimes Review](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324528/20260814/ledgerbound-review-corporate-satire-meets-fire-emblem-tactics-debut-worth-playing.htm) · [Turn Based Lovers](https://turnbasedlovers.com/news/this-new-tactical-srpg-makes-you-fight-monsters-and-deny-insurance-claims/)

## Memoirium — Retro 3D Soulslike Launches August 13 on PC

**Memoirium**, a retro 3D soulslike from **GoldenGratus** (backed by *Slay the Spire* co-creator **Casey Yano** via Outersloth), launched on **PC via Steam on August 13** at **$14.99**. Players awaken in a dream realm where 21st-century classrooms neighbor coral reefs, wax castles, and far-future alien spaceships, and must escape eight interconnected dungeons while fighting phantasms.

Equipment alters both player abilities and the enemies encountered. The world is fully interconnected with multiple paths; players also have a decoratable personal safe room and full item replenishment at designated checkpoints.

Sources: [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/04/retro-soulslike-action-game-memoirium-launches-august-13) · [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/memoirium-new-trailer-dreamlike-souls-like/) · [RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/2026/08/new-release-round-up-august-13-2026/)

## Robot Rhapsody — Kickstarter Funds in 44 Hours

**Rapturous Studio** (*Beloved Rapture*) launched a **Kickstarter campaign for Robot Rhapsody**, a 90s-inspired turn-based JRPG set in a cyberpunk world involving techno-cults, rebels, and robotic bards. Inspired by *Neon Genesis Evangelion*, *Final Fantasy VII*, and *Xenogears*, the game hit its **$20,000 goal in 44 hours** with stretch goals already underway. The campaign ran through approximately August 14. An expected release window of **November 2027** for **PC** has been set.

## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Final Previews In; Manga Tie-In Announced

Nintendo published preview embargo lifts this week for **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave** (September 17, Switch 2), with **Nintendo Life** rounding up the final hands-on impressions. The tournament-based structure — where all four protagonists (**Cai, Theodora, Dietrich, and Leda**) compete in a tournament as a framing device for their individual routes — is praised by previews as a natural successor to *Three Houses*' academic setting.
