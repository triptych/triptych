# JRPG News — April 30, 2026

## Final Fantasy XIV Fan Fest 2026: Evercold Revealed

The North American Fan Festival took place in Anaheim on April 28, and producer/director Naoki Yoshida dropped the biggest FFXIV news in years:

**Evercold (Patch 8.0) announced** — The next full expansion takes place in the Fourth Reflection, a world described as on the verge of being consumed by cold. No release window yet, but the next two Fan Fests (Berlin on July 25–26) will reveal more.

**New Job Action system** — The most significant gameplay overhaul announced. Existing jobs will be split into two modes:
- **Reborn Mode** — retains current rotations and action sets
- **Evolved Mode** — redesigned actions with fewer required inputs and a stronger focus on job identity

Players can switch between modes outside of combat, letting veterans keep their rotations while giving new players a more streamlined experience.

**Two new jobs confirmed** — Both are original to FFXIV (not returning Final Fantasy jobs). One is a tank, one is a physical ranged DPS. The first job reveal is scheduled for Berlin Fan Fest.

**Neon Genesis Evangelion Alliance Raid** — A 24-man collaboration with *Neon Genesis Evangelion* was announced, continuing the game's tradition of high-profile crossover raids.

**FFXIV Switch 2 port** — Confirmed for August 2026.

**Shadowbringers joins the Free Trial** — The 2019 expansion (previously paid content) will be folded into the free trial, giving new players access to one of the game's most acclaimed arcs at no cost.

**Near-term patch roadmap:** Patch 7.51 arrives June 2, bringing Cosmic Exploration, Custom Deliveries, and the next Ultimate raid. Patch 7.55, featuring new Hildibrand and Occult Crescent content, follows shortly after Berlin.

## Tales of Eternia Remastered Leaked by PEGI

A PEGI rating listing — since deleted — revealed *Tales of Eternia Remastered* for Nintendo Switch, published by Bandai Namco Entertainment Europe with a PEGI 12 rating. No official announcement has been made.

*Tales of Eternia* originally launched on PlayStation in Japan in November 2000, reaching North America in September 2001. It has never appeared on a Nintendo platform. The leak fits neatly into Bandai Namco's ongoing **Tales Remaster Project**, which has already brought *Tales of Graces f Remastered*, *Tales of Xillia Remastered*, *Tales of Berseria Remastered*, and *Tales of Symphonia Remastered* to Switch. An official announcement is presumably imminent given the early PEGI slip.

## Ys Memoire: Revelations in Celceta — Launch Day Reviews

*Ys Memoire: Revelations in Celceta* launched on Nintendo Switch on April 28 as previewed in last week's coverage. Reviews have now landed, and the consensus is cautiously positive:

- **Nintendo Life** called it "a simple yet fun adventure," praising the fast combat and charming characters while noting the cumbersome menu system and NPC pathing issues keep it feeling dated.
- **MonsterVine** was more skeptical, headlining their review "Memories of Mediocrity" and citing the lack of meaningful changes over the existing PC version (aside from a $5 price increase).
- **Final Weapon** and **Hey Poor Player** were warmer, with the latter highlighting the booming soundtrack and explosive combat as enough to carry the game to credits.
- **LadiesGamers** praised it as a focused, well-designed action RPG that holds up well despite its age.
- The **Metacritic** page is now live and aggregating scores.

The broad picture: a competent port of a solid mid-tier Ys entry, best suited for Switch players who missed the Vita/PC releases.
