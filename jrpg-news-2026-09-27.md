## Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter — Review Scores Land Near Perfect

**Nihon Falcom**'s **Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter** launched September 17 to an exceptional critical reception, with a **Metacritic score of 91** and **OpenCritic at 92 / 97% recommendation** across the multi-platform release (Switch, Switch 2, PC via Steam).

The consensus across outlets: the remake faithfully rebuilds the original's dense narrative while sharpening its pacing, cutting the extended slow-burn stretches that tested patience in the first game. High marks went to the battle system, the depth of the cast, and the voice acting across the English dub — though several reviewers flagged **inconsistent voiceover coverage within individual scenes**, where some lines in the same exchange are fully acted and others sit silent. Major scores include:

- **DualShockers**: 10/10 — "A flawless JRPG experience"
- **RPG Site**: 10/10
- **Game Informer**: 9.5/10 — "The Trails series' defining entry, and one of the best RPGs of the year"
- **RPGFan**: Review published, strong positive
- **Analog Stick Gaming**: Positive ("A Warm, Welcome Return to Liberl")

The Falcom jdk BAND held a **special livestream celebrating the launch**, performing fan-favorite tracks from across the Trails in the Sky subseries following TGS 2026.

Sources: [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review/) · [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/review/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter/a-superb-sequel) · [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/09/16/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review/) · [GameDaily](https://gamedaily.com/games/trails-in-the-sky-2nd-chapter-review-roundup)

## Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age — Switch 2 Edition Out Now

**Square Enix** launched **Dragon Quest XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age — Definitive Edition** for **Nintendo Switch 2** on **September 24, 2026**, priced at **$39.99**.

The Switch 2 edition adds technical upgrades not present in the original Switch release:

- **Graphics Priority Mode**: 1440p / 30 fps
- **Performance Priority Mode**: 1080p / 60 fps
- All content from the Definitive Edition remains intact — new story chapters, orchestral soundtrack, 2D mode toggle, and Japanese voice option

Two important caveats for existing owners: **save data from the Switch version is not transferable** to the Switch 2 edition, and **no upgrade pack is available** for owners of the prior version. The physical release is a **Game-Key Card** rather than a traditional cartridge.

## Qliphah in Providence's Shadow Launches on Switch and PC

**Arc System Works**'s **Qliphah in Providence's Shadow** released worldwide on **September 24, 2026** for **Nintendo Switch, PC via Steam, and Epic Games Store** (also PlayStation 5).

The tactical counter-timeline RPG follows **Soma Hoshigami**, a delivery worker in a post-apocalyptic Tokyo who is attacked by a supernatural creature and becomes a "half-turned" human/supernatural hybrid. He's then taken into custody by **Rena Hizuki**, an investigator with the MPD's Public Safety Bureau. The game's central hook is its **time-stacking battle system**: while the game resembles an action RPG on the surface, it is built on command-based mechanics where players layer skills within a time-flow framework to amplify damage chains.

The Steam page and Arc System Works site have the full roster of characters and a gameplay breakdown trailer.

Sources: [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/20775-qliphah-in-providences-shadow-releases-worldwide-for-switch-ps5-pc-on-september-24) · [Arc System Works](https://www.arcsystemworks.com/game/qliphah-in-providences-shadow/) · [MonsterVine](https://monstervine.com/2026/09/qliphah-in-providences-shadow-gameplay-trailer/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3882410/Qliphah_in_Providences_Shadow/)

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — TGS 2026 Hands-On Showcases Vincent, Cid, and the Great Glacier

**Square Enix** featured a **playable demo of Final Fantasy VII Revelation** at **Tokyo Game Show 2026** (September 17–21, Makuhari Messe). Attendees and press previews have given the first in-depth look at the game's second half of Chapter 1.

Key details from the demo:

- The playable section begins in the **Great Glacier / Whirlwind Maze** — a frozen, desolate region at the base of the North Crater populated by zombies and enemy monsters
- **Vincent Valentine** and **Cid Highwind** are confirmed playable, joining the previously shown cast
- **Jenova Deathmourner**, a Synergy Ability shared between Cloud and Vincent, was shown for the first time
- **Vincent's Gates of Hell Limit Break** made its debut
- The FITS system, which outfits party members in enhanced costumes with new abilities, is shown on Cloud and Tifa in-battle

Square Enix's booth was notable for its scale: life-size statues, a rideable Cloud motorcycle, a holdable Buster Sword, and a descending life-size Sephiroth photo op. The game remains on track for **Spring 2027** on Switch 2 and PC (also PS5).

Sources: [DualShockers Preview](https://www.dualshockers.com/tgs-2026-final-fantasy-7-revelation-preview/) · [RPGFan Hands-On](https://www.rpgfan.com/2026/09/21/final-fantasy-vii-revelation-hands-on/) · [WCCFTech](https://wccftech.com/square-enix-impress-final-fantasy-vii-revelation-playable-demo-stuns/) · [GoSuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/79200-tokyo-game-show-2026-preview-final-fantasy-7-revelation-has-cloud-throw-a-fit-sephiroth-hug)

## Final Fantasy Resonance — October 22 Release Date Confirmed

**Square Enix** and **Lancarse** confirmed a firm release date of **October 22, 2026** for **Final Fantasy Resonance**, an HD-2D turn-based RPG, during TGS 2026. The game launches day-and-date across **Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam** (also PS5).

Final Fantasy Resonance is a collaboration between Square Enix's HD-2D visual team and Lancarse, the studio known for Etrian Odyssey. The game occupies the HD-2D visual language of Octopath Traveler and Triangle Strategy but builds its own original world and cast.
