# JRPG News — May 27, 2026

## Dragon Quest 40th Anniversary Livestream Airs Today

Today marks **Dragon Quest Day** — the 40th anniversary of the series' original Famicom launch on May 27, 1986 — and **Square Enix** held a special livestream on the official Dragon Quest YouTube channel to mark the occasion. Series creator **Yuji Horii** hosted the roughly 10-minute broadcast, which he personally teased earlier in the week by confirming it would include "an announcement about the next game" alongside other franchise news.

All eyes were on **Dragon Quest XII: The Flames of Fate**, the next mainline entry first announced at the series' 35th anniversary in 2021 and almost entirely silent since. Rumours circulating ahead of the stream suggested multiple major reveals were lined up. The event also celebrated Dragon Quest's enormous cultural legacy; a *Japan Times* feature published today noted that "it's impossible to ignore its influence," with dozens of modern developers crediting the series as a foundational inspiration. Full coverage of what was announced is expected across major outlets throughout the day.

## Echo Generation 2 Launches on PC, Xbox, and Game Pass

**Cococucumber**'s **Echo Generation 2** is out today on **PC**, **Xbox Series X|S**, and **Xbox Game Pass**. The sequel to the 2021 cult-favourite indie RPG substantially reworks its predecessor's turn-based combat by weaving in deckbuilding: players can build decks from over **100 cards** to chain combos, unleash attacks, and summon allies.

The story follows **Jack**, an ordinary dad who stumbles into a dimension beyond his home of Maple Town and must battle extra-terrestrial threats alongside an eccentric roster of allies while trying to find his way home. The game retains the original's distinctive 80s-vibe aesthetic and papercraft visual style. Early reviews published today describe it as a meaningful evolution of the formula rather than a simple retread, with Turn Based Lovers noting how much the combat system has changed.

## Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered Out Now on Steam

**DMM Games** and **Shiravune** released **Utawarerumono: Past and Present Rediscovered** on **PC via Steam** today, with the PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 versions launching in Japan tomorrow, May 28. Developed by **Aquaplus**, the game is a sprawling JRPG sequel to *Monochrome Mobius: Rights and Wrongs Forgotten* and is set prior to the events of *Utawarerumono: Mask of Deception*.

The story follows **Oshtor** and companions as a new shadow falls over Yamato, with the narrative hinting at a long slumber nearing its end. Combat follows the same turn-based system introduced in *Monochrome Mobius* rather than the tactical grid-based battles of the main *Utawarerumono* trilogy. The PC version ships with **English, Japanese, and Chinese** language support; the console releases are Japanese-only. RPGFan and RPGamer have both published coverage ahead of and alongside the launch.
