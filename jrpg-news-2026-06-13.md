## Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — Four Protagonists and Heroic Games Deep Dive

Following the June 9 Nintendo Direct reveal, Nintendo and Intelligent Systems have published a detailed breakdown of **Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave**'s story, cast, and systems ahead of its **September 17, 2026** launch (Switch 2).

**Story:** The game centers on the **Heroic Games**, a world-spanning tournament held in the city of **Dagsion** where warriors from every nation compete for a single wish. The four main protagonists each enter for their own reasons and cross paths inside the arena.

**Main Characters:**
- **Cai** — A kind-hearted boy from Ribeira Village whose father is imprisoned. He carries a red gem embedded in his left palm that lets him channel powerful magic blasts.
- **Dietrich** — A wandering swordsman seeking opponents worthy of his skill.
- **Theodora** — A queen fighting to reclaim and defend her homeland.
- **Leda** — A musician whose drive is revenge.

Each protagonist has unique personal techniques tied to their archetype, designed to reward learning each character's distinct playstyle.

**Systems:** A **free time** mechanic lets players explore Dagsion between battles — visiting training grounds, recruiting allies (highlighted recruit: a character named Ninae), sharing meals with party members, and venturing beyond the city walls. This appears to be Fortune's Weave's analogue to the social sim elements in Fire Emblem: Three Houses.

## Final Fantasy VII Revelation — FITS Job System Explained

Square Enix has detailed the **FITS (Function Integrated Tactical Suitware)** system coming to **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** (Spring 2027, PS5 / Switch 2 / Xbox Series / PC).

FITS is Revelation's take on the classic Final Fantasy job system, using in-game suits (outfits) to assign characters new roles and movesets in battle. Key details:

- All characters begin as **Freelancers** by default.
- New **Job FITS** are earned as the story progresses, not purchased — tying character progression to narrative milestones.
- Jobs evoke classic FF archetypes: confirmed examples include **Black Mage** and **Warrior**, with the trailer showing Cloud equipped as a Black Mage.
- The goal, per director Naoki Hamaguchi, is to let players customize characters "based on personal preferences" — a first for the Remake trilogy, where characters previously had fixed combat identities.
- The game is also confirmed to be **open world** with a fully controllable **Highwind airship**.
- Other confirmed playable characters in the reveal: **Vincent**, **Cid**, **Yuffie**, **Cait Sith**, and **Tifa** (in a new costume).

This is the first game in the trilogy launching **simultaneously across all platforms** on day one (the previous two were PS5 timed exclusives).

## Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales — Final Pre-Launch Preview Round-Up

With **five days to launch** (June 18), the final wave of previews for **The Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales** (Switch 2 / PS5 / Xbox Series / PC) has landed and the tone is strongly positive.

Key takeaways from critics:
- **Action-RPG feel** with responsive, rhythm-based combat — compared frequently to classic Zelda and the Mana series.
- **Companion puzzle design** built around Elliot and his fairy companion Faie recalling co-op adventure classics.
- **Time travel** gives the narrative scope beyond a typical HD-2D entry.
- Team Asano and Claytechworks incorporated player feedback from the earlier prologue demo: **Elliot's movement speed was increased** and several weapons and systems were retuned before final release.

The game is positioned as the most ambitious HD-2D release to date, moving the formula from traditional turn-based RPG into full action-RPG territory.

## Edge of Memories — Steam Next Fest Demo Spotlight (June 15)

**Edge of Memories**, the French-developed JRPG from **Midgar Studio** (published by NACON), launches a free **Steam demo on June 15** as part of Steam Next Fest — making it the most notable JRPG demo to try during the event.

Why it's worth watching:
- **Composer:** **Yasunori Mitsuda** (*Chrono Trigger*, *Xenogears*) and **Mariam Abounnasr** (*Xenoblade Chronicles 3*, *ONINAKI*) are handling the soundtrack.
- **Vocalist:** **Emi Evans** (*NieR*, *NieR: Automata*) provides vocal performances.
- **Character Design:** **Raita Kazama** (*Xenoblade Chronicles X*).
- **Writer:** **Sawako Natori** (*NieR*).
- **Setting:** The continent of **Avaris**, afflicted by a spreading corruption called the "Withering." Players guide protagonist **Eline** alongside allies **Kanta** and **Ysoris**.
- **Combat:** Real-time with combo chains alternating light and heavy attacks, built in Unreal Engine 5.
- The full game is targeting a 2026 release for PS5, Xbox Series, and PC (Steam).

The demo's opening chapter introduces Eline awakening on the Estfalian Coast and encountering the mysterious traveler Ysoris.

## Wo Long 2: Wings of Ember — Story and Scale Details

Following the June 7 Xbox Games Showcase reveal, Team NINJA has shared further details on **Wo Long 2: Wings of Ember** (Early 2027, PS5 / Xbox Series / Switch 2 / PC — day one on Game Pass).

**Story premise:** Set once again in a demon-infested version of China's Three Kingdoms era, a new nameless champion rises as a "**phoenix**" — the lore framing being that an eternal cycle of war is perpetuated by the desperate pursuit of an **elixir of immortality**, and the player must break that cycle.

**Scale:** Team NINJA has indicated the sequel operates at a significantly larger scale than *Wo Long: Fallen Dynasty*, though specifics (map size, number of missions) haven't been quantified yet.

**Combat:** Builds on the original's system of Chinese martial arts, parries, and deflection-based counterattacks, applied against both historical figures and supernatural enemies. New powers and abilities were visible in the reveal trailer.
